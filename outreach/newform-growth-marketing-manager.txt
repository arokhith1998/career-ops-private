Hi Hamza,

Just submitted for the Growth Marketing Manager role.

Agency-side performance marketing for consumer apps breaks at one specific seam: the gap between the creative refresh cadence and the channel diagnostics. Most teams have respectable ROAS in aggregate, a creative team shipping variants, and a platform specialist tweaking bids, and none of those three feedback loops are actually closed against each other. The agencies that win at $100M-of-spend scale are the ones with a per-client operating cadence where creative-fatigue diagnostics drive the next test, not the dashboard's blended numbers.

I have run that operating cadence at Pixis. 8-client B2B SaaS portfolio with per-client monthly media budgets ranging $15K to $90K. 8-person team across creative, account management, and platform specialists. Weekly client QBRs presenting ROI, iROAS, and roadmap recommendations to CFOs and growth leads. Replaced two underperforming channels mid-quarter when KPIs slipped. Cut churn 50% via per-client renewal-aligned playbooks documented in writing. Validated 3x ROAS lift for a fashion D2C partner via holdout incrementality testing. The shape of that book is the shape of Newform's account portfolio.

iOS app paid UA is direct, not transferable. I ran it via Appsflyer and Adjust at Pixis as part of the 8-client book, with SKAN / post-IDFA discipline on which conversion events to trust. On Plug Power I rebuilt the full paid stack on Google, Meta, LinkedIn, and Adobe-driven programmatic. UTM spec from scratch, GTM containers rebuilt, lead routing validated through Salesforce campaign attribution. CPA dropped 25%, conversion went up 20%.

On the AI-agent side, unusually well-aligned with WillBot. SwipeHire shipped solo on Python + OpenAI API + React. 4 Anthropic Claude certifications come from running prompt-based iteration as my actual workflow. At Pixis I trained 8 enterprise marketers on AI bidding tools.
