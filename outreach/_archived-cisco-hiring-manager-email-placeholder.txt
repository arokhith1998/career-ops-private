Most advocacy programs fail at that translation layer. Good ones build for it from day one.

I co-built case studies, decks, and battlecards with those champions, in their language, signed off by their compliance teams. 50+ reps used the assets. Partner win rates went up 30%. The work was less about writing and more about earning the right to publish.
