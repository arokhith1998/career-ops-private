## 1. Sensata Bleeders & Leakers — Margin Recovery

**Maps to:** pricing, margin analysis, cross-functional negotiation, BI/dashboards, executive reporting

- **S:** Sensata's Industrial BU was leaking margin across thousands of deals with no visibility into where.
- **T:** Build the visibility and recover margin without a blanket price increase that would hurt win rate.
- **A:** Built a 4-page Power BI dashboard tracking 1,200 pricing opportunities against a $627M target across 3 regions, 4 product families and 20 product lines; separated "bleeders" (margin erosion) from "leakers" (price leakage); led the negotiation program built on competitive gaps and customer willingness-to-pay data.
- **R:** Recovered $700K+ in margin in 2 quarters and raised distributor/channel-partner margins from 53% to 61%; surfaced $433M in margin gaps for leadership to prioritize by salesperson and product line.
- **Reflection:** The dashboard mattered less than the discipline behind it — tying every recovery action to a specific, defensible data point meant sales reps trusted the reprice instead of fighting it. Would build the salesperson-level view first next time; that's what got adoption, not the regional rollups.

## 2. Sensata GTM — New Sensing Products Launch

**Maps to:** GTM strategy, product marketing, positioning, launch management

- **S:** Sensata was launching new connected sensing products into North America and Europe with no established GTM motion.
- **T:** Own GTM end-to-end — positioning, pricing, channel, launch — for a product line with no existing playbook.
- **A:** Defined positioning and messaging that turned technical features into customer value propositions, built KPI frameworks in SQL + GA4 across 50K+ connected industrial devices to find adoption signals, and ran 30+ customer interviews with OEMs and distributors.
- **R:** $2M+ first-year revenue, +35% qualified leads, +18% feature adoption from the cohort work, +22% CSAT from the interview-driven product changes.
- **Reflection:** The customer interviews were the highest-leverage hour of the whole launch — most of the positioning came directly from language customers used unprompted. Would run them earlier in the cycle next time, before messaging was drafted, not after.

## 3. Sensata Sales Enablement + Roadmap Ownership

**Maps to:** sales enablement, roadmap/backlog management, cross-functional engineering partnership, win-rate improvement

- **S:** A 50-rep sales team was underselling new sensor solutions because collateral hadn't kept pace with a fast-moving product roadmap.
- **T:** Own the product roadmap for sensor solutions in automotive/industrial and build the enablement to match it.
- **A:** Defined and executed the roadmap, managed a 100+ feature backlog with engineering in Agile across 3 regions, and built sales enablement (decks, case studies) for the 50-rep team.
- **R:** Accelerated releases 25%, cut time-to-market 20%, and win rates rose 30% off the new enablement.
- **Reflection:** Backlog prioritization and enablement are usually treated as separate jobs; running them together is what let the collateral ship the same week as the feature instead of a quarter behind.

## 4. Plug Power — Paid Budget Ownership and Mar-Tech Automation

**Maps to:** paid media, budget ownership, experimentation, marketing automation

- **S:** Plug Power's paid budgets across business units were manually managed with no closed-loop reporting.
- **T:** Own $100K/month in budgets, present forecasts to leadership, and get spend efficiency up.
- **A:** Led Paid Search and Paid Social across Google, Microsoft, Meta and LinkedIn with bid optimization and A/B testing (15+ tests), automated the WordPress + Pardot lead flow, and automated the Mar-Tech stack (Salesforce, Adobe Analytics, GA, GTM) for closed-loop reporting.
- **R:** -25% CPA, -50% cost per lead, +35% organic search visibility, -15% wasted spend.
- **Reflection:** The automation work paid off more than any single campaign optimization — once closed-loop reporting existed, every subsequent test got faster to read and cheaper to run.

## 5. Pixis — Team Leadership and Client Churn Reduction

**Maps to:** people management, account strategy, retention, P&L ownership

- **S:** Pixis (AI adtech platform) was losing clients faster than the team could replace them.
- **T:** Turn around an 8-client, $15K-$90K/month-per-client portfolio while managing an 8-person team.
- **A:** Built proactive account strategy, channel diversification and vertical playbooks; used Amplitude and Tableau on user behavior across 30+ campaigns to catch churn signals earlier.
- **R:** Cut client churn 50%, lifted campaign ROAS 40%, delivered 3x ROAS in one month for a fashion-brand client via full-funnel restructuring.
- **Reflection:** Churn stopped being a fire drill once the team had a leading indicator (engagement drop-off) instead of a lagging one (the cancellation email). Would instrument that signal on day one at a new account next time.

## 7. SwipeHire — Zero-to-One Build

**Maps to:** product management, MVP scoping, PRD writing, growth/activation, founder range

- **S:** Job seekers were spending hours manually tailoring resumes to each posting with no visa-aware matching tool on the market.
- **T:** Ship an AI job-matching platform from zero to a working product with real users.
- **A:** Wrote the PRD, scoped the MVP, shipped auto-tailored resumes and visa-aware matching (Python, OpenAI API, React); owned the GTM and analytics stack (GA4, GTM, event tracking, funnel dashboards); ran 8+ A/B tests and 12+ user interviews.

## 8. GenY Medium — Catalog Feed Rebuild and SEO Audits

**Maps to:** e-commerce/D2C marketing, SEO, data hygiene, catalog/feed management, dashboards

- **S:** D2C e-commerce brand clients had underperforming Google Shopping and Meta catalog feeds dragging down paid and organic performance.
- **T:** Rebuild the feeds and fix the underlying data hygiene before touching creative or bidding.
- **A:** Rebuilt Google Merchant Center and Meta catalog feeds; ran database audits and built dashboards; ran SEO site audits (backlinks, keywords, internal linking, content).
- **R:** Delivered 16x ROAS for D2C e-commerce brands, lifted a B2B e-commerce startup's conversion rate 36% and revenue 25%, and improved data accessibility 30% from the dashboard work.
