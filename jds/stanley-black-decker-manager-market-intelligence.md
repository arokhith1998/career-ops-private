The Job: 

As Manager, Market Intelligence, you will help shape the future of our Tools & Outdoor business by delivering actionable market and competitive insights across the U.S., Europe, and key international markets. In this role, you will partner closely with cross-functional and regional teams to identify trends, assess market dynamics, and translate complex data into clear recommendations that inform strategic and commercial decisions.

You will:

- Deliver market and competitive insights: Monitor market trends, competitive activity, consumer behavior, and channel dynamics across the U.S., Europe, and other priority international markets to identify opportunities and risks. 
- Support strategic decision-making: Provide regular, actionable insights that inform business priorities, category strategies, and growth initiatives. 
- Translate data into business impact: Synthesize multiple data sources into clear, concise insights and recommendations for business leaders and cross-functional partners. 
- Develop compelling stories from data: Build presentations, dashboards, and visualizations that clearly communicate what is happening in the market, why it matters, and what actions should be considered. 
- Drive global perspective: Compare trends across regions and markets, highlighting key differences in consumer needs, competitive intensity, pricing, channel structure, and market maturity. 
- Partner across functions and regions: Collaborate with category management, brand insights, commercial teams, regional stakeholders, and other partners to ensure market intelligence is embedded in decision-making. 
- Manage projects with pace and discipline: Lead multiple workstreams simultaneously, balancing speed, quality, and attention to detail. 
- Support capability development: Help strengthen market intelligence tools, processes, and ways of working to improve the quality, consistency, and impact of insights. 
- Manage external partners: Support relationships with data, research, and analytics suppliers, helping evaluate outputs and identify opportunities to improve insight generation. 

The Person: 

You are intellectually curious, analytically strong, and energized by turning complex information into clear business direction. You are comfortable working across regions, functions, and data sources, and you bring a global mindset to understanding markets, consumers, and competitors.

You also have:

- Bachelor’s degree required; MBA or other advanced degree preferred. 
- 5+ years of experience in market intelligence, strategic insights, business analytics, strategy, consulting, or related fields. 
- Experience working with multiple data sources to generate actionable business insights, including sources such as Euromonitor, Circana/NPD, Numerator, YipitData, GfK/NIQ, retailer POS data, and other market or consumer data sources. 
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to synthesize large and complex data sets into clear takeaways. 
- Excellent communication skills, including the ability to translate technical or detailed analysis into simple, compelling messages for non-technical audiences. 
- Strong storytelling and presentation skills, with confidence engaging business leaders and cross-functional stakeholders. 
- Demonstrated project management skills, including the ability to manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced, matrixed environment. 
- Strong stakeholder management and collaboration skills, with the ability to build trust across functions, regions, and levels of the organization. 
- A proactive, self-directed approach and comfort operating with ambiguity. 
- A global mindset, with an appreciation for differences across geographies, channels, consumers, and competitive landscapes. 
- Experience mentoring junior team members or contributing to team capability building is a plus.
