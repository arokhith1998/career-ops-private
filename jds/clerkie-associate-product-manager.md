## Full JD text

About Clerkie

Clerkie is a fintech startup on a mission to democratize financial wellness using the power of artificial intelligence. Launched in 2018; aims to help the nearly 123 million underserved Americans in need of financial guidance. Expanded to pair consumer guidance with B2B software (the Clerkie network) for creditors managing loan losses while helping borrowers get out of debt. Remote-first, 40+ employees across 4 US time zones. Raised $41M total, including a $33M Series A in early 2023 (Citi Ventures, Left Lane Capital, Wellington Ventures, Flourish Ventures, Vestigo Ventures).

About the role

Looking for a driven, curious, collaborative Associate Product Manager to join the small-but-mighty Product team. Ideal for someone early in their product career -- first or second formal PM role -- ready for real ownership. Works closely with PMs, Designers, Engineers, and directly with the CEO/co-founder, supporting consumer-facing experiences and the B2B platform. Hands-on across discovery, delivery, and iteration. Meant as a launchpad for long-term growth in product at Clerkie or beyond.

Here's what you'll do:
- Partner with PMs, designers, engineers to explore problems and shape solutions
- Work directly on high-priority initiatives across SaaS products
- Support discovery, documentation, and delivery for key product work streams
- Join team rituals (sprint planning, retros, roadmap reviews)
- Help scope and ship features from research through release
- Keep the team organized with clear requirements, tracking, communication
- Bring fresh eyes, user empathy, curiosity

Recent Product team wins (context, not requirements): sandbox demos that helped win big deals; enterprise builds pressure-testing infrastructure; SLT scoping/prioritization; product strategy/roadmap input for Clerkie and Fiber; Coda knowledge-hub cleanup; onboarding new teammate; hackathon participation.

Must-haves:
- Early experience working in a product-focused role
- Ability to clearly communicate across technical teams, clients, and stakeholders
- Top-notch communication and organization skills
- Ability to think in systems, break down complex ideas, prioritize clearly
- Curiosity, humility, desire to learn from others
- Motivation to own work and grow fast
- Collaborative spirit, appreciation for feedback
- Comfort working independently in a remote environment

Nice-to-haves (not required): startup experience (Seed to pre-IPO), fintech or adjacent; familiarity with Agile, Jira, Coda, or Figma; background in CS, business, design, analytics, or related field; basic understanding of how software gets built; passion for financial wellness/tech-for-good/startup life.

Our salary and benefits: Salary range $90-110k; equity; remote-first role (US only); medical/dental/vision; 401(k) + retirement; PTO + annual company retreat; company laptop + home office stipend; collaborative, values-driven culture.

Our hiring process: Application (async) -> Intro Call w/ People Team (15-20 min) -> Take-Home Challenge (async, 1 week) -> Take-Home live Presentation (45 min) -> Technical Deep Dive w/ Product Leader (45 min). Standard background check plus reference check required before any offer.
