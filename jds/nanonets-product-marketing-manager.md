## Role

PMM owns how NanoNets shows up to market - turning product capabilities into clear messaging, high-converting assets, sales enablement driving pipeline. Works across both product pods, partnering with Product / Sales / Growth / Design. Reports to leadership.

## Core ownership areas

- **Landing pages (primary owner):** create + improve product/solution landing pages with messaging, positioning, structure, proof points, CTAs. Partner with design + growth to ship, test, iterate.
- **Sales collateral:** build + maintain sales decks, one-pagers, battlecards, case-study summaries, objection handling, ROI/value narratives used across the funnel.
- **Webinar collateral:** own end-to-end webinar assets - registration pages, email copy, slide decks, talk tracks, follow-up sequences, post-webinar recap materials.
- **Customer testimonials & stories:** source, shape, publish testimonials and case studies (written + video). Build a repeatable pipeline with sales/CS to capture proof.
- **Social media (PMM-led):** create product- and customer-led social content (launches, feature highlights, use-cases, short clips, carousels) aligned to campaigns + positioning.

## GTM & Messaging

- Define positioning + messaging for key products, use cases, verticals.
- Translate customer insights into crisp narratives + content themes.
- Coordinate launches with product, sales, growth (announce, enable, measure adoption/pipeline impact).
