## Job Description (extracted, verbatim from posting)

Who We Are: Serval is an AI-native automation platform transforming how enterprises operate. We build intelligent agents that understand real-world workflows and execute them end-to-end. Founded in early 2024, Serval is already trusted by companies like Fox, Notion, Perplexity, Vercel, and Brex. Backed by Sequoia Capital, Redpoint Ventures, Meritech, First Round, General Catalyst, Elad Gil, and others.

About The Role: We're launching a new business line in response to exceptional market demand. As our Business Operations Lead, you'll act like a GM and own the strategy, operations, reporting, and ultimately the P&L for this function. You'll partner with the Exec Team, Sales, Deployment, Engineering and Operations to establish our playbooks and then rapidly execute on them to turn this into a strong 8-figure business in the first year.

**What You'll Do:**
- Own the P&L for your line: build the operating plan and own the results, including revenue, margin, and profitability
- Set and defend pricing and packaging
- Partner with Deployment on unit economics
- Partner with Sales on deal strategy, sit in on calls, structure contracts
- Build the forecast and the operating cadence (weekly pipeline, monthly business review, quarterly plan)
- Scale from the 10k foot view down to the minutiae: office locations, recruiting, candidate criteria

**What You'll Need:**
- "5-8+ years across business operations, strategic finance, corporate strategy, consulting, or an operating role at a high-growth software company."
- Direct experience owning or materially shaping a P&L, a gross margin line, or a pricing model.
- Strong quantitative and modeling skills.
- Working fluency with Salesforce, a BI tool, and billing/usage data.
- Real command of software and AI unit economics (COGS, gross margin, payback, net revenue retention).

**Nice to Have:** Investment banking, management consulting, or private equity/VC experience; early-stage experience building the reporting/forecasting stack.
