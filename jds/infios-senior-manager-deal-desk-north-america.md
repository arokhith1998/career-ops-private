Role overview
This is a hands-on, player-coach role with no direct reports for now. The person would be the main Deal Desk partner for North America sales on complex and non-standard deals, helping set up the global Deal Desk model. The role also covers some APAC deals and may later lead regional Deal Desk staff.

Responsibilities
- Advise on deal structure, pricing, commercial terms, and financial analysis for strategic transactions.
- Coordinate with Sales, Finance, Legal, Accounting, Tax, Product, Professional Services, Security, and Operations to get deals approved and executed.
- Draft, negotiate, and manage commercial agreements, including booking, billing, and revenue recognition readiness.
- Oversee contract lifecycle management and alignment with CRM/CPQ systems.
- Improve processes, controls, reporting, and automation, and analyze deal trends.
- Provide coaching and cover multiple time zones, including quarter-end.

Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in Finance, Accounting, Business, Economics, or a related field, or equivalent experience.
- 8+ years in Deal Desk, commercial finance, revenue or sales operations, pricing, or a related function.
- Proven experience structuring complex enterprise software, SaaS, or technology transactions.
- Strong commercial, financial, and contractual skills, including pricing, profitability, and revenue recognition.
- Proficiency with Salesforce and CPQ platforms. CLM and ERP experience is a plus.
- Strong negotiation, communication, stakeholder management, and leadership skills.
