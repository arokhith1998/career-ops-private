## Full job description (verbatim)

Our client, an exciting fast-growing AI business building products that are changing how companies work, is looking for a Product Marketing Manager to join their team.

The business has built a strong product, attracted an impressive customer base and secured significant backing from leading investors. They're now looking for someone to take ownership of product marketing for an exciting new AI product.

This is a high-impact, hands-on role with significant room to build. You'll work directly with the CTO/Co-founder and Head of Marketing, alongside the wider commercial team, to shape how the product is positioned, brought to market and scaled. With the product still evolving rapidly, there is a real opportunity to experiment, develop new use cases and influence its direction.

The role is based in San Francisco, with three days per week in the office.

You'll:

- Own positioning, messaging and go-to-market strategy for a new AI product
- Develop and execute creative campaigns, product launches and new market initiatives
- Get hands-on with content, landing pages, launch videos, case studies and sales materials
- Identify and test new customer segments, use cases and opportunities to drive demand
- Work closely with Growth, Sales and Product to turn ideas into campaigns and measurable commercial outcomes
- Partner directly with the CTO/Co-founder to help shape the product's direction and go-to-market approach
- Equip commercial teams with compelling messaging and materials to communicate product value
- Measure campaign performance, learn quickly and continuously improve what works

You'll be a strong fit if you have:

- Experience in product marketing, creative growth or a broader marketing role within a B2B technology business
- Strong creative instincts, with the ability to develop compelling messaging, content and campaigns
- Experience taking products or campaigns to market, ideally in an early-stage or high-growth environment
- A strong understanding of how marketing contributes to demand, adoption and revenue
- Genuine enthusiasm for AI, with the curiosity to get into the product and understand how it works
- A hands-on approach, comfortable creating and executing rather than simply managing projects
- The confidence to work independently, challenge ideas and collaborate closely with senior stakeholders
- Adaptability, good judgement and a willingness to experiment in a fast-moving environment

We're open to candidates from different backgrounds who have the right combination of creativity, commercial thinking and execution.

Why join? Significant ownership, competitive compensation including equity, and the opportunity to help build and scale a new AI product within a well-funded, fast-growing business.
