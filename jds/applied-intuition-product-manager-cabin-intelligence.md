**About the role**
Applied is looking for a Product Manager who is passionate about building great in-vehicle experiences. As part of the Vehicle Intelligence team, you'll own Cabin Intelligence, covering everything from the software platform, tooling, and AI-powered cabin experiences. This is an important role where you will own products end-to-end and drive both strategy and execution with global OEMs across various industries.

**At Applied Intuition, you will:**
- Define the strategy and roadmap for the product platform, tooling, applications and more
- Ship production-grade in-vehicle experiences, balancing a scalable platform with OEM-specific customization
- Collaborate closely with engineering and design to prioritize impactful deliverables, from early POCs through SOP
- Own key technology partnerships (AI models, voice, content, SoC vendors), including build vs. buy vs. partner decisions
- Partner with business development and marketing on customer pitches, go-to-market positioning, and pricing
- Work directly with OEM customers to gather feedback and keep the product aligned with production needs

**We're looking for someone who has:**
- 2+ years of experience in Product Management at a top tech company or high-growth startup
- Experience at an automotive, trucking, or other OEM, or at a supplier building products for them
- A track record of shipping user-facing software, ideally on embedded or connected-device platforms
- Strong technical background with understanding of and/or hands-on experience in software development
- MS/BS degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or equivalent required
- Experience managing external partners or vendors
- Excellent written and verbal communication skills

**Nice to have:**
- Experience with Android Automotive OS (AAOS), digital cockpit, or HMI products
- Experience with ADAS visualization, 3D rendering, or graphics engines (e.g., Flutter, Unreal)
- Experience building voice assistants, conversational AI, or on-device ML products
- Experience building developer tools or design systems
- Previous founder or early startup experience
