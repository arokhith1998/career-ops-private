Who we areAbout StripeStripe is a financial infrastructure platform for businesses. Millions of companies — from the world's largest enterprises to the most ambitious startups — use Stripe to accept payments, grow their revenue, and accelerate new business opportunities. Our mission is to increase the GDP of the internet, and we have a staggering amount of work ahead. That means you have an unprecedented opportunity to put the global economy within everyone's reach while doing the most important work of your career.About The TeamEvery day, businesses across the internet need to answer one question with confidence: is this person real? Stripe Identity answers it millions of times a month for some of the world's largest artificial intelligence (AI) companies, marketplaces, and platforms. As fraudsters turn to generative AI to fake faces and forge documents, we're building the AI that catches them, while real people breeze through.Identity is a full-stack product, and we build every layer. That includes the verification experience consumers complete on web and mobile, the application programming interfaces (APIs) and Dashboard businesses use to run and review verifications, the software development kits (SDKs) that bring Identity into any app, and the machine learning (ML) foundations that tell a real face from a deepfake.
