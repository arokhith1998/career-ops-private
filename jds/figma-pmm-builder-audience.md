# Product Marketing Manager, Builder Audience — Figma

**URL:** https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/figma/jobs/5776152004
**Saved:** 2026-04-12
**Location:** San Francisco, CA / New York, NY / Remote US
**Salary:** $153,000-$317,000

## Job Description
Versatile marketer to support expanding product portfolio. Quickly understand complex products, engage technical audiences and translate capabilities into compelling narratives and growth strategies. Bridges product, sales, design, and marketing teams.

## Responsibilities
- Manage end-to-end product marketing for key Figma products serving designers, developers, and builders
- Create go-to-market strategies for launches, from initial concepts to feature enhancements
- Convert technical capabilities into clear positioning and messaging
- Execute beta programs including customer engagement and success story promotion
- Build adoption through educational content: tutorials, guides, events, and live sessions
- Coordinate cross-functional campaigns covering onboarding, activation, and retention
- Provide sales enablement and competitive positioning materials
- Identify emerging audiences and expand growth beyond core users
- Test and demonstrate features to understand limitations pre-launch

## Minimum Qualifications
- 7+ years in product marketing or related roles with net-new products or 0-to-1 initiatives experience
- Ability to rapidly learn and market technical products with strong communication skills
- Track record leading cross-functional go-to-market efforts from strategy through measurement
- Experience partnering with product teams and aligning executives
- Comfort with technical concepts like LLMs, software development, or no-code logic

## Preferred
- Experience marketing developer-focused or technical products
- B2B SaaS or Enterprise software background
- SQL familiarity or product usage data experience
- Entrepreneurial, product, or SaaS side-project experience
