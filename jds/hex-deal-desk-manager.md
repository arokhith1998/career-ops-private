About Hex

Hex is growing our team of builders on a mission to make everyone a data person. Our platform solves key pain points with today's data and analytics tooling, and empowers anyone to explore data using natural language, with or without code, on trusted context. Thousands of customers like Ramp, Figma, Stubhub, Anthropic, and Gamma love Hex for our beautiful UI, agentic superpowers, and boundless flexibility.

Hex has raised over $100M from leading investors like Sequoia, a16z, Snowflake and Amplify. With office hubs in San Francisco and New York, as well as remote team members throughout the US and UK, we're a high-agency team that ships fast and has fun doing it.

About the role:

Hex is scaling fast, and the way we quote, bill, collect, and provision has to scale with us. As Deal Desk Manager, you will own how Hex turns a signed deal into cash. You'll reimagine our quote-to-cash process for the next five years, choose and implement the systems behind it, and run the monetization committee that keeps our pricing and packaging flexible. You'll partner daily with Sales, Finance, Legal, Product, and Engineering, and your work will directly affect margin, sales velocity, and how quickly new reps ramp. This is a rare chance to design a Q2C engine from first principles at a company in hypergrowth, with real executive visibility.

What you will do:
- Design and implement a new order-to-cash system and own the business case, including moving billing off Stripe to improve gross margin.
- Chair the monetization committee: bring data on pricing, packaging, and discounting, and drive decisions that keep Hex flexible to what the market requires.
- Run deal desk day to day: review and approve non-standard deals, structure ramps, commits, and usage terms, and keep turnaround fast without giving up margin or compliance.
- Streamline CPQ to shorten sales cycles, cut quoting friction, and reduce time to ramp for new AEs.
- Partner with Finance to reduce the time it takes to close the books, including clean hand-offs on invoicing, revenue recognition, and collections.
- Improve the provisioning process so Hex can support 10x the customers and transactions.
- Build the approval matrix, deal guardrails, and playbooks that let reps close standard deals without deal desk involvement.
- Report on the health of the process: cycle time, discount and margin leakage, quote-to-cash errors, and days to close.

About You:
- 4+ years in deal desk, sales finance, or revenue operations at a B2B SaaS company, including ownership of a Q2C process end to end.
- Experience leading a CPQ and/or billing system implementation or migration.
- Strong grasp of SaaS contracts, revenue recognition, and the margin implications of pricing and billing structures.
- Familiarity with usage-based and hybrid pricing models.
- Proficiency in Salesforce and a CPQ and billing stack.
- Track record of aligning Sales, Finance, Legal, and Product on a hard decision.
- Clear written and verbal communicator who can explain trade-offs to executives and AEs alike.
- Bias to action, high ownership, and comfort with ambiguity.

The salary range for this role is: $164,000 - $205,000

In addition to our unique culture, Hex proudly offers a competitive total rewards package, including but not limited to, market-benched salary & equity, comprehensive health benefits, and flexible paid time off.
