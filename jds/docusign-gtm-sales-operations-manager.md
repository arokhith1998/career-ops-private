## Full Job Description

### Company Overview
Docusign brings agreements to life. Over 1.5 million customers and more than a billion people in over 180 countries use Docusign solutions.

### What You'll Do
As a GTM Sales Operations Manager for Contact Data Management, you are responsible for driving critical contact data quality and acquisition initiatives in Salesforce across the global GTM organization, leveraging agentic, autonomous, and analytical AI solutions to standardize, enrich, deduplicate, and acquire high-propensity contact records at scale.

### Responsibility
- Execute global contact standardization, enrichment, and deduplication initiatives.
- Develop and enforce contact data standards, governance policies, contact-to-account hierarchy rules aligned with global compliance/privacy regulations.
- Build and maintain contact data quality dashboards with monthly KPI tracking; establish SLAs and performance benchmarks.
- Design and deploy AI agents and automation workflows targeting 99%+ data accuracy and completeness.
- Architect intelligent contact data pipelines and validation frameworks.
- Drive contact acquisition strategy leveraging Clay, ZoomInfo, Lusha, Cognism and similar platforms.
- Lead vendor selection, integration, relationship management for contact coverage.
- Write BRDs and technical specs; lead UAT for new contact systems.
- Partner with Sales, Sales Operations, RevOps, Marketing Operations, and GTM teams.
- Lead offshore team performance management, training, QA, and process optimization.
- Establish contact data SLAs (98%+ compliance); improve accuracy to 95%+ and coverage to 90%+ within 12 months.

### Job Designation
"Hybrid: Employee divides their time between in-office and remote work. Access to an office location is required. (Frequency: Minimum 2 days per week; may vary by team but will be weekly in-office expectation)."

### What You Bring (Basic)
- 8+ years of experience in contact data quality, data governance, or data operations in a SaaS or high-growth tech environment.
- 2+ years hands-on experience managing contact data initiatives across multiple global regions.
- 2+ years hands-on experience with AI-driven contact solutions.
- Experience managing offshore/distributed data operations teams.
- Advanced Salesforce (contact models, hierarchies, CRM data governance).
- Experience with generative Agentic/AI solutions for contact data.
- Advanced SQL and Python.
- Experience with at least 2 contact enrichment platforms (Clay, ZoomInfo, or similar).
- Data privacy/compliance experience (GDPR, CCPA, LGPD).
- **5+ years of people management experience leading data, analytics, or operations teams including direct reports and offshore coordination.**

### Preferred
Strong executive-level communication; remote-first collaboration; vendor orchestration experience; **"Degree in Computer Science, Data Science, Engineering, Mathematics, or related analytical field (or equivalent professional experience)"**; GTM/RevOps/Sales Operations leadership background; CDMP/DGSP certification; MDM framework experience; Alteryx/Talend; Tableau/Claude Code.
