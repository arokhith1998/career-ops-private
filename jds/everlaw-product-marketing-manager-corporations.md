prioritization for our customers.This is a full-time, exempt position based in our Oakland, CA office with a hybrid work schedule: in office M/W/Th with the option to work from home on Tu/Fr.What you'll doOwn go-to-market strategy for selling Everlaw to legal teams, including messaging, positioning, battlecards, and sales materials (e.g. pitch decks, cheat sheets) that support the enterprise sales motion.Execute product launches for in-house corporate legal teams, developing launch campaigns and positioning, and managing sales enablement and go-live coordination.Partner with Demand Gen
