About Tekion

Positively disrupting an industry that has not seen any innovation in over 50 years, Tekion has challenged the paradigm with the first and fastest cloud-native automotive platform that includes the revolutionary Automotive Retail Cloud (ARC) for retailers, Automotive Enterprise Cloud (AEC) for manufacturers and other large automotive enterprises and Automotive Partner Cloud (APC) for technology and industry partners. Tekion connects the entire spectrum of the automotive retail ecosystem through one seamless platform. The transformative platform uses cutting-edge technology, big data, machine learning, and AI to seamlessly bring together OEMs, retailers/dealers and consumers. With its highly configurable integration and greater customer engagement capabilities, Tekion is enabling the best automotive retail experiences ever. Tekion employs close to 3,000 people across North America, Asia and Europe.

Job Introduction

Tekion's automotive retail platform, including payments and AI products, serves dealerships and dealer groups. Note: "Sales Operations Manager" is an individual-contributor title at Tekion; this role has no direct reports. On Deal Desk you apply and enforce product structure, pricing and discount policy on live deals, acting as the control point between Sales, Finance and Legal: deals are structured correctly, approved quickly and booked cleanly. You say yes fast on standard deals, find a workable structure on complex ones, and protect compliance.

Key Roles & Responsibilities

- Collaborate closely with Sales, Finance, Development/Product, and Implementation to address deal-related questions and challenges, develop solutions, and ensure alignment on deal structure.

- Review quotes, order forms and contracts for pricing, terms, SKU accuracy and policy fit; coach reps on structure requirements.

- Facilitate guidance on concise deal summaries covering economics, exceptions, risks, and recommendations for leadership review and approvals with clear rationale and an audit trail.

- Guide complex nonstandard commercial and enterprise transactions including multi-year commitments, phased deployments, ramp pricing, payment, product bundles, and negotiated commercial terms and route to Leadership, Finance, Legal or Product.

- Manage quote-to-cash coordination on all quote types with respective cross functional team members to ensure approved and signed terms translate accurately into bookings, billing, order fulfillment, and renewal records, reporting any process concerns to leaders timely.

- Maintain accuracy while managing multiple deals with monthly and quarterly deadlines

- Verify bookings meet company policy and partner with Finance on revenue-recognition compliance.

- Own the handoff to Finance, billing and order management; resolve discrepancies quickly.

- Improve quote-to-cash workflows, quoting rules and approval flows with Business Systems.

- Report on cycle time, approval volume and booking quality; document playbooks and train Sales.

- Support related work as needed, such as forecasting, cancellations, quota and compensation inputs, or ad hoc analysis.

Basic Qualifications

- 6-8 years in sales operations, deal desk, revenue operations or business operations, with direct exposure to deal review, quoting or contracts.

- Strong working knowledge of Salesforce, approval workflows and configuration concepts.

- Track record of improving deal quality through data, process and clear communication.

- Experience influencing stakeholders across Sales, Finance, Legal, and Product.

- Strong analytical skills (Excel or Sheets, BI reporting) with understanding of accounting and enforced commercial judgment.

- Bachelor's degree in Business, Operations, Finance or a related field, or equivalent in experience.

Preferred Qualifications

- CPQ tools (e.g., Salesforce CPQ, NUE CPQ).

- SaaS or technology experience.

- Revenue recognition basics and order-to-cash process.

- Automotive retail or dealer software, or payments, familiarity.

- MBA or advanced degree.
