Head of Growth
Stealth Startup - Deep Tech
Bengaluru, Karnataka, India
Seniority level: Director
Employment type: Full-time
Industries: Technology, Information and Internet

About Us
We're working on one of the most ambitious problems of our time: reversing biological age. We operate at the intersection of AI and life sciences, combining machine learning with cutting-edge biology to help people measure, understand, and reverse their biological age. We're building for the US market and looking for a growth leader who can scale the consumer application.

The Role
As Head of Growth, you'll own go-to-market strategy and growth engine end to end. You'll take what we've built and turn it into a multi-million-dollar business in the US, building the team, channels, and playbooks to get there. This is a high-ownership leadership role reporting directly to the founders.

What You'll Do
- Design and execute US go-to-market strategy, from positioning and pricing to channel mix and launch
- Own revenue and growth targets across acquisition, activation, retention, and referral
- Build and scale performance marketing, partnerships, lifecycle, and community channels
- Run a rigorous experimentation culture: test, measure, and double down on what works
- Translate complex science (longevity, biomarkers, AI-driven insights) into compelling, trustworthy messaging for consumers
- Develop strategic partnerships with clinics, wellness brands, employers, and health platforms
- Build and lead a high-performing growth team as we scale
- Work closely with product, science, and AI teams to shape the roadmap around customer insights and growth levers
- Set up the metrics, dashboards, and reporting that drive decisions across the company

What We're Looking For
- 5+ years in growth, marketing, or GTM roles, with significant time in a leadership position
- Proven track record of leading US go-to-market for a product or company
- Demonstrated success scaling a business to multi-million-dollar revenue
- Deep expertise in data-driven growth: CAC/LTV, funnel optimization, cohort analysis, and experimentation
- Hands-on experience across paid acquisition, lifecycle/CRM, content, and partnerships
- Ability to operate at both strategy and execution level in a fast-moving startup
- Strong storytelling skills and the ability to make complex science accessible

Bonus Points
- Experience in consumer health, longevity, diagnostics, wellness, or health-tech
- Familiarity with DTC subscription models
- Experience marketing in regulated categories (health claims, FDA/FTC guidelines)
- Prior experience at an early-stage or venture-backed startup

Why Us
- Work on a mission that could fundamentally change how humans age
- Join at a pivotal stage with real ownership and impact on company trajectory
- Collaborate with a team of scientists, AI researchers, and builders
- Competitive compensation and meaningful equity

If you've scaled a business in the US and want to apply that experience to one of the biggest frontiers in science, we'd love to hear from you.
