## Full JD Text

Job Summary:
We are looking for a sharp, multi-disciplinary Graduate Associate to work directly with the Founder. In the MEP industry, projects move fast and require a mix of technical precision and commercial savvy. You will act as the "right hand" to the leadership, helping transition the firm from engineering concepts to market-leading execution. This role is perfect for a fresh graduate who wants to see the "business side" of engineering early in their career.

Key Responsibilities:
- Market Research & MEP Trends: Research emerging technologies like BIM (Building Information Modeling), sustainable HVAC solutions, and IoT-integrated electrical systems to keep the firm competitive.
- Business Development & Tendering: Assist in preparing technical bids and commercial proposals. You will help identify new "Greenfield" and "Brownfield" project opportunities in the commercial and industrial sectors.
- Financial & Project Modeling: Support the founder in project cost estimation, resource allocation modeling, and tracking the profitability of ongoing MEP contracts.
- Graphic Design & Branding: Take ownership of the firm's visual identity. You will design brand decks, technical brochures, and high-impact project portfolios for client meetings.
- Data Analytics: Analyze project timelines and labor costs to identify bottlenecks, providing the founder with data-driven reports to improve operational efficiency.

The Profile we are looking for
- Educational Background: A recent degree in Mechanical or Electrical Engineering (preferred) or Business/Architecture with a strong interest in the built environment.
- The "Swiss Army Knife" Mindset: You are equally comfortable reading a technical floor plan as you are designing a logo or building a spreadsheet.
- Software Savvy:
  - Engineering: Awareness of AutoCAD or Revit (BIM) is a plus.
  - Design: Proficiency in Canva, Adobe Illustrator or Figma.
  - Data: Advanced Excel skills (Pivot tables, VLOOKUPs, basic macros).
  - Tech: Proficient in using AI technology to generate results.
- Communication: Exceptional writing skills to draft "Nearby Engineers" style documentation and professional correspondence.

What is in it for you?
- Direct Mentorship: Work daily with the Founder, gaining insights that usually take 10 years to acquire.
- Holistic Growth: Develop a rare profile that combines engineering logic with business development and creative design.
- Impact: Your research and designs will be the face of the firm in front of developers and architects.

This is a dynamic role, beyond the listed responsibilities, you will be expected to step in and own any high-priority tasks as needed.
