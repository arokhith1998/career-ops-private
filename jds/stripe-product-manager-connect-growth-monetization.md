## Full JD text

Stripe is a financial infrastructure platform for businesses. Millions of companies use Stripe to accept payments, grow their revenue, and accelerate new business opportunities. Our mission is to increase the GDP of the internet.

**About Connect:** Connect is Stripe's leading product for marketplaces, on-demand services, ecommerce site builders, invoicing platforms, professional SaaS products, fundraising platforms, rental management platforms, and more. By using Connect, users in each of these categories can link multiple parties to exchange goods or services, relying on Connect for the crucial function of routing payments reliably between these parties.

**Why this role:** This is a chance to define a foundational product area at Stripe. You will turn a fast-changing set of user and product needs into durable platform primitives that work across products, industries, and geographies. The role combines high-leverage strategy with hands-on product execution.

**What you'll do:** As a Product Manager on Stripe's Connect team, you will shape the strategy and oversee the execution of high-impact initiatives serving modern marketplaces and software platforms. You will work closely with engineering, design, analytics, marketing, sales, and customer-facing teams, engage directly with customers, and present your plans to Stripe's leadership team.

**Responsibilities:**
- Develop and advance a compelling product vision for Connect
- Devise the strategy for high-impact initiatives serving modern marketplaces and software platforms
- Translate product strategy and customer needs into clear product requirements
- Oversee execution across the teams involved in delivering these initiatives
- Collaborate with engineering, design, analytics, marketing, sales, and customer-facing teams
- Meet with customers to understand their needs and gather feedback
- Present product plans to Stripe's leadership team and incorporate feedback

**Who you are / Minimum Requirements:**
- More than three years of product management experience
- A computer science background or equivalent experience
- Strong product judgment
- The ability to lead products that require coordination across multiple teams
- Strong written and verbal communication skills
- Persistence and resourcefulness in driving successful outcomes for products and teams
