Role overview: Ottimate wants an experienced product marketer to shape the product roadmap, sharpen differentiation, and own go-to-market strategy as the company expands into procurement and other new product areas. The role works across marketing, product, sales, support, and partnerships. Prior hands-on experience with sourcing and procurement software and adoption strategies is preferred.

Key responsibilities:
1. Build and run go-to-market plans for new product areas, covering launch strategy, packaging, sales enablement, rollout, and post-launch measurement.
2. Work with product and sales to define target segments, buyer personas, and launch goals, and keep teams aligned on timelines and deliverables.
3. Lead the tailoring of marketing and sales assets (positioning, messaging, case studies, decks, web content) to specific industries.
4. Develop deep market intelligence on target buyers, especially procurement and finance teams, including how they handle the procure-to-pay process and how they buy.
5. Maintain expertise on competitors, including procure-to-pay and spend management vendors, and build battlecards and competitive insights for sales.
6. Create product positioning and messaging that differentiates Ottimate.
7. Produce content such as website pages, decks, one-sheeters, product training, case studies, and blog posts.
8. Feed customer and market insights into product roadmap and prioritization.
9. Build customer relationships through interviews, advisory boards, and industry events, and turn those findings into messaging, content, and product feedback.
10. Support retention and expansion through launch communications, adoption campaigns, and feature announcements.
11. Support sales channels by training them on the problems Ottimate solves and creating internal tools and external collateral to help them win.
12. Collaborate across teams to deliver collateral and strategic insights.

Requirements:
1. At least 4 years of product marketing experience, including owning go-to-market plans and launches end to end.
2. Hands-on experience marketing invoice automation, procurement, purchasing, or procure-to-pay software.
3. Experience adapting positioning and assets for different industries or customer segments.
4. Strong grasp of go-to-market strategy, positioning, messaging, and solution packaging.
5. Working knowledge of procurement workflows, including requisitions, purchase orders, approvals, receiving, three-way matching, and supplier management.
6. Solid understanding of market trends and product lifecycles.
7. B2B SaaS experience. Procurement, spend management, finance SaaS, or fintech experience is a strong plus.
8. Excellent written, verbal, and visual communication and storytelling.
9. Comfort working cross-functionally and juggling multiple projects in a fast-moving startup.

The posting also notes that candidates who don't meet every qualification are encouraged to apply.
