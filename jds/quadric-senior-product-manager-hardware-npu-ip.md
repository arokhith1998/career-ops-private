## Full JD text

Quadric is redefining edge AI with the industry's first General Purpose Neural Processing Unit (GPNPU), enabling developers to run both neural network inference and conventional C++ code on a single programmable architecture. Our technology powers intelligent edge devices across automotive, industrial, robotics, and embedded systems.

Founded in 2016 and based in downtown Burlingame, California, Quadric is building the world's first supercomputer designed for the real-time needs of edge devices. Quadric aims to empower developers in every industry with superpowers to create tomorrow's technology, today. The company was co-founded by technologists from MIT and Carnegie Mellon, who were previously the technical co-founders of the Bitcoin computing company 21.

The Opportunity

Quadric has created an innovative General-Purpose Neural Processing Unit (GPNPU) architecture, the Chimera GPNPU, which executes both neural network inference workloads and conventional C++ DSP code on edge and endpoint devices. As the Senior Product Manager, Hardware, you will own the Chimera GPNPU IP roadmap across five strategic tracks: Compute, Efficiency, Scalability, Safety, and Integration. Setting feature sets for upcoming IP generations, driving architecture freeze decisions, and partnering directly with anchor SoC customer teams worldwide, you'll ensure our silicon IP powers production devices shipping in 2027 and beyond.

What You'll Do

Hardware Roadmap & Architecture Freeze: Translate customer requirements, competitive pressures, and architectural constraints into track-by-track feature lists with explicit gating. Build the business and technical case for contested architecture decisions and lead trade-off reviews with architects to bring clear recommendations to the Product Steering Committee (PSC). Drive IP usability by identifying silent customer workarounds (API wrappers, custom RTL hooks, glue logic) and productizing proper architectural solutions.

Customer Engagement & Technical Ownership: Serve as the named hardware product owner for key anchor accounts, presenting new features and integrating formal architecture feedback into the product roadmap. Conduct architecture reviews with technical buyers, including Tier-1 automotive and OEM SoC design teams. Partner with international customers, particularly supporting key anchor accounts in the Japanese and global semiconductor markets.

Competitive Intelligence & SoC Integration: Analyze competitive architectures (e.g., Synopsys NPX6, Arm Ethos-U85, Ceva NeuPro-M, NVIDIA Jetson) to anticipate market needs and inform product requirements before gaps appear in customer evaluations. Define and position SoC integration features, specifying which knobs (AXI4/ACE-Lite, CoreSight, power domains, performance counters) are exposed and productized. Sequence functional safety positioning, lockstep configurations, FMEDA work, and safety-island architectures for automotive and critical application standards.

What Success Looks Like

Within your first 6-12 months, you'll: freeze and lock in next-generation GPNPU IP feature sets (QC/QD releases) aligned with target customer tape-out schedules; successfully lead technical architecture reviews with anchor Tier-1/OEM accounts and convert their feedback into actionable roadmap tracks; productize key integration and usability knobs to streamline customer IP integration cycles.

Required - What We're Looking For

5-8 years of Product Management experience focused on hardware or silicon products, with a senior IC background.

Direct experience in at least two of the following: NPU/AI accelerator architecture, SoC integration, DSP/vector compute, automotive silicon, or semiconductor IP licensing.

Proven experience leading technical architecture reviews with sophisticated buyers (Tier-1 automotive, OEM SoC teams).

Active adoption of agentic AI tools (e.g., Claude Code, Cursor, or equivalent) in daily workflows.

EE/CE/CS engineering degree (or equivalent depth), based in or willing to relocate to the Bay Area (Burlingame hybrid model), and willing to travel quarterly (domestic and international).

Preferred

Prior RTL or silicon-implementation background (e.g., shipped a block, went through tape-out, or owned a microarchitecture spec).

Experience working at a semiconductor IP vendor (e.g., Synopsys, Arm, Ceva, Cadence, Imagination, VeriSilicon).

Direct exposure to AI/ML compute stack architectures (dataflow, quantization, sparsity, mixed-precision trade-offs).

Experience engaging with Japanese-market customers and technical teams.
