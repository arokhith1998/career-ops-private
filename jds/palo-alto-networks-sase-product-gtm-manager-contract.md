SASE Product GTM Manager - Palo Alto Networks [Contract]

Role Description

This role is a contract assignment at Palo Alto Networks. Contractors will not be employed by Palo Alto Networks but through our trusted staffing partners. Palo Alto Networks is looking for a SASE Product GTM Manager - Contractor to connect product, field sales, and business data to drive growth and inform high-level decision-making for our SASE portfolio. Reporting within the SASE Go-to-Market organization, this position offers a great opportunity for a strategic, analytical professional to influence key initiatives in a fast-growing, international environment.

Location: Santa Clara, CA (Hybrid - 3 days in office per week)

Duration: 12 months

Your Impact

The SASE Product GTM Manager is a key driver in connecting strategy with execution across our SASE product line. Working in a fast-paced, matrixed environment, you will translate complex business data into actionable growth strategies, ensuring sales, product, marketing, and operations teams stay aligned to maximize revenue potential. This role demands a self-motivated leader who combines analytical rigor with strong business instincts, enabling you to navigate ambiguity, influence cross-functional partners, and deliver high-impact executive narratives.

Key Responsibilities

- Performance Analytics & Revenue Opportunities: Track GTM performance metrics (pipeline health, attach rates, win/loss) and analyze complex datasets to uncover whitespace and revenue opportunities.
- GTM Strategy & Planning: Lead annual GTM planning cycles, including territory sizing, market segmentation, capacity modeling, and resource allocation.
- Product Launch & Field Enablement: Partner with Product Management on launch readiness (Ideal Customer Profiles, field positioning) and build frameworks to inform launch and sales enablement strategies.
- Executive Presentations & Dashboarding: Build concise presentations, performance dashboards, and strategic memos for VP and C-level stakeholders.
- Cross-Functional Governance: Run cross-functional operating cadences to keep Product, Sales, Marketing, and Operations aligned, while mentoring junior analysts and contractors.

Your Experience

- Professional Experience: 6-8 years in product GTM, sales strategy, business operations, or management consulting, with proven experience in complex, multi-product enterprise SaaS or cybersecurity environments.
- Technical & Analytical Acumen: Proficiency in data analysis and familiarity with GTM and business metrics (e.g., pipeline conversion, bookings) in Salesforce (SFDC). Proficiency in SQL and Excel/Google Sheets to model large datasets (e.g., Salesforce, BigQuery) is a plus.
- Business Acumen & Leadership: Strong business acumen with a proven track record of driving cross-functional alignment and influencing senior leadership without direct authority.
- Communication Skills: Outstanding written and verbal communication skills, with a talent for distilling complex analytics into crisp, plain-language executive narratives.
- Mindset & Education: Highly organized, self-motivated, and comfortable navigating ambiguity in fast-paced environments. Bachelor's degree required.
