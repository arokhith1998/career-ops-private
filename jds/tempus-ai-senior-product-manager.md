## Full JD Text

Compensation

CHI: $110,000-$160,000

Duties And Responsibilities

- Drives the strategic direction and product roadmap for identified areas within the diagnostic product portfolio to ensure alignment with commercial and/ or corporate objectives
- Develop and implement strategies for the integration of Ambry’s software systems and applications with EMRs of various healthcare systems
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to design, develop, and test Ambry’s software and application
- Manage the implementation and ongoing maintenance of Ambry’s software systems and applications to ensure compliance with industry standards and regulatory requirements
- Identify and address technical issues related to Ambry’s software products.
- Drive innovation identifying short and long-term product and business opportunities while contributing to product and marketing strategies
- Creates and validates business cases for products in the healthcare IT space. This includes creation of product definition, market segmentation, collection of VOC, product requirements, and market analysis
- Liaises with internal and external stakeholders to better understand customer needs, coordinate feedback with commercial teams and customers around product lifecycle and identify process improvements along the value chain
- Understands market drivers, trends, regulatory, and test reimbursement implications
- Develop policies and procedures for the use and access of Ambry’s software systems and applications to ensure security and compliance with HIPAA regulations
- Travel up to 15%

JOB SPECIFICATIONS

Education And Knowledge

- Bachelor’s Degree or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Healthcare Informatics, Genomics or a related field
- 6+ years of experience in healthcare IT and software development. Ideally, SaaS or cloud-native application development.
- Knowledge of development leveraging FHIR, SMART on FHIR, client and server JSON/XML, HL7
- Familiarity with healthcare regulations and compliance standards (e.g., HIPAA, HITECH)

Technical Competencies

- Excellent written and verbal communication skills and interpersonal relationship skills including negotiating and relationship management skills with ability to drive achievement of objectives
- Proven problem-solving skills, and comfort in working in a dynamic environment with rapidly evolving business needs
- Ability to think creatively and develop, define, validate business cases for novel opportunities
- Ability to work independently and influence others around you
- Excellent organizational, analytical, and critical thinking skills including high discretion/judgment in decision making
- Experience managing software development projects and teams. Ideally with experience in Scaled Agile scrum-based development.
- Experience with phase-gated product development processes.
- Experience with SW design control best practices (SDLC). Fluent with tools such as JIRA for documentation of user needs, design inputs.
- Excellent business acumen
- Strict attention to detail while managing multiple projects and priorities simultaneously.

Experience

- 6+ years of experience in a product management or similar role within the healthcare IT, life sciences or diagnostics space
- Hands-on experience developing solutions which interface programmatically with one or more EMRs/EHRs
- Previous supervisory or people management experience is a plus but not required
- Experience in marketing or selling to oncologists or healthcare providers is a plus
