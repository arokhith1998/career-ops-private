Company Description DormCircle is India’s premier smart platform for student living, designed specifically for residential and boarding schools. Based in Guwahati, Assam, DormCircle offers a web dashboard for wardens and a secure mobile app for parents, enabling real-time visibility into student health, attendance, academics, and co-curricular activities. The platform focuses on reducing administrative workload, improving warden efficiency, and strengthening parental trust, especially for junior boarders in Classes 4–10. By addressing the “parent anxiety gap” and modernizing hostel management, DormCircle helps schools demonstrate their commitment to student safety and holistic well-being. DormCircle partners with leading institutions and provides a 1-month, no-risk free pilot program for schools seeking to optimize administration and parental engagement.
Role Description As Co-Founder — Head of Growth & Institutional Sales, the individual will lead DormCircle’s market expansion across premium residential and boarding schools, with a base in Kamrup Metropolitan in an on-site capacity. This full-time role involves identifying and qualifying institutional prospects, conducting discovery meetings with school leadership, and designing tailored growth and implementation plans. The person in this position will own the entire sales cycle—from outreach and product demos to negotiations, closures, and onboarding of new institutions—while building long-term relationships with stakeholders. Day-to-day responsibilities include collaborating with product and operations teams, refining DormCircle’s go-to-market strategy, tracking performance metrics, and representing the brand at conferences, events, and school visits. The role also includes developing strategic partnerships, gathering market intelligence, and continuously improving messaging to drive revenue and adoption.
