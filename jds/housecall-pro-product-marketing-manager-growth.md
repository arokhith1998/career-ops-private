You shape how Pros discover, experience, and adopt our products, from the first message they see on our website to the in-app guidance that helps them realize value quickly.

**What You Do Each Day**
- Own go-to-market strategies for free trial, onboarding, and product activation
- Develop compelling product messaging and positioning
- Plan, execute, and analyze A/B tests across onboarding flows, paywalls, and in-app experiences
- Guide how and when new features are introduced within the product
- Lead change management and communication strategies for platform-wide updates
- Partner with Product, Lifecycle Marketing, and Growth teams
- Analyze funnel performance, cohort data, and customer behavior
- Conduct customer research with new Pros and those who churn early
- Monitor and report on the effectiveness of product marketing initiatives

**Qualifications**
- Bachelor's degree in Marketing, Business, or a related field, or equivalent work experience
- 5+ years of experience in SaaS product marketing or growth, including at least 2 years focused on self-serve or product-led growth motions such as free trials, onboarding, or in-product adoption
- Proven experience developing and executing go-to-market strategies, product messaging, and customer adoption initiatives
- Hands-on experience designing, executing, and analyzing in-product experiments
- Proficiency in analyzing customer behavior, funnel performance, and cohort data (Amplitude, SQL, BI tools)
- Strong understanding of customer segmentation, market research, and product-led growth strategies
- Experience writing clear, compelling product messaging and in-app content
- Demonstrated ability to leverage AI tools
- Experience in SMB or vertical SaaS environments (home services, construction, field service) preferred
- Experience with in-app guidance or lifecycle marketing tools (Amplitude Guides, Pendo, Appcues, Iterable) preferred
