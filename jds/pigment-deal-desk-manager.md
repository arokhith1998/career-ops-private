## Full JD text

About the Role: Pigment seeks an experienced Deal Desk Manager to join the Revenue Operations team as part of the founding Deal Desk function in the US. This role sits at the center of the quote-to-cash motion, partnering closely with Sales, Legal, Finance, and GTM Systems to structure, review, and operationalize commercial deals - from pricing guidance and deal structure, to quote accuracy, approvals, and order form readiness. It's a hands-on role for solving complex commercial problems in a high-growth SaaS environment. As a founding member in the US, the successful candidate will have direct influence over Deal Desk operations, shaping policy, building operational rhythm, and setting strategic direction as Pigment scales. This is a hybrid role based in the New York City office.

What You'll Do: Own regional Deal Desk execution and quote-to-cash processes by supporting new business, renewals, upsells, amendments, and other commercial motions across the region while ensuring consistency. Help structure and review deals in Salesforce and CPQ, ensuring quotes are accurate, compliant, and ready for approval. Partner with Sales and Sales Leadership on pricing guidance, discounting questions, approval requirements, and non-standard deal structures. Review key deal details such as contract dates, pricing, products, quantities, billing terms, ramp structures, and special terms. Help ensure Salesforce quotes and customer-facing order forms are aligned before signature and closed-won processing. Support quarter-end execution by helping teams move quickly while maintaining accuracy and operational discipline.

Partner cross-functionally across Sales, Legal, Finance, and Systems: work closely with Legal on non-standard terms, special terms, and order form requirements. Partner with the broader Deal Desk team to share regional context, align on best practices, and support consistent commercial processes. Partner with Finance to ensure deals are clean for billing, reporting, and downstream operational processes. Collaborate with GTM Systems to identify recurring Salesforce or CPQ issues and translate them into process or system improvements. Act as a trusted operational partner to Account Executives, Sales Managers, and GTM leaders on deal execution and commercial process questions.

Automate Deal Desk processes by identifying recurring deal issues and proposing scalable ways to reduce manual work, rework, and approval friction. Contribute to improvements in Salesforce, CPQ, approval workflows, and quote validation processes. Support onboarding and training for Sales teams on Deal Desk processes, Salesforce hygiene, and quote creation best practices. Help build a more self-serve and scalable Deal Desk function through clear guidance, better workflows, and automation opportunities.

Who You Are: 4+ years of experience in Sales Operations, Revenue Operations, Deal Desk, Business Operations, Sales Strategy, or a similar role. Experience in B2B SaaS or enterprise software is strongly preferred. Strong working knowledge of Salesforce; experience with Salesforce CPQ or another quoting tool is a strong plus. Familiarity with quote-to-cash processes, pricing, approvals, order forms, or commercial deal workflows. Comfortable working with Sales, Legal, Finance, Systems, Strategy, leadership stakeholders, and distributed teams. Process-oriented and pragmatic, with the ability to balance speed, accuracy, and risk. Comfortable working in a fast-paced, high-growth environment. Curiosity and fluency with AI tools and automations, with an interest in applying them to CPQ and Deal Desk workflows.

What We Offer: Competitive compensation package including highly competitive base salary, equity package, and comprehensive benefits. Generous time off and parental leave policies. High-end equipment to do your work in the best conditions.
