# Product Manager, File and Remit

**Company:** Anrok
**Location:** San Francisco, CA
**URL:** https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/view/4475894540
**Track:** us

---

Anrok is the leading tax automation platform enabling businesses to expand globally without compliance complexity.

As the digital economy has grown 6x over the last decade, software businesses have gone from not worrying about sales tax to needing to monitor exposure, calculate rates, and file returns across 50 US jurisdictions and 100+ countries. This creates a critical bottleneck for companies that should be able to transact with customers everywhere.

Anrok eliminates this complexity by connecting with billing and payment systems to automate tax monitoring, calculations, and filing end-to-end. Our unified platform handles the ever-changing maze of tax laws at municipal, state, and federal levels—so companies can focus on growth, not compliance.

Our Customers Include

40% of Forbes Top 50 AI companies20% of Forbes Top 100 Cloud companiesTop companies like Notion, Anthropic, and Cursor

We're making compliant digital commerce a reality for companies big and small, backed by over $100M from leading investors including Sequoia, Spark, Index, and Khosla Ventures.

Every month, Anrok files more tax returns than ever before, and the next month beats it. Behind each return is a company counting on us to file it correctly, on time, and get the money to the right government. Nobody else is set up to do this at our scale.

As the File & Remit Product Manager, you'll own the last mile of tax compliance: from preparing a return until the money reaches the tax authority. Everything else Anrok does builds up to this. You'll be solving two problems that shape the company:

File every return in the world. Our filing volume keeps setting records. The systems that got us here won't carry us through the next 10x. You'll define the filing platform that can file every return for every company in every jurisdiction. It should run itself, with automation that monitors and repairs its own problems and humans involved only for edge cases.

Open Anrok to the world. Today, a company based outside the US has to open a US bank account before it can remit through Anrok. For great international companies, that's often the last thing standing between them and Anrok. You'll build the remittance infrastructure that lets them pay from their local bank account in top international markets.

Our vision: tax filing is handled like magic, and sellers never have to think about it.

In this role you will

Build a filing platform that scales to every return in the world, with most filings fully automated and zero human touchesPut AI at the center of filing, with agents that write filing automations, catch problems before a filing period opens, and repair them automaticallyUnlock international sellers by making remittance work from their local bank accounts, in local currencyOwn money movement end to end, from collecting seller funds to landing payments at thousands of tax authorities across currencies, banking partners, and jurisdictionsBuild tools for our in-house TaxOps team, your power users, so they can do more jurisdictions with less effortMake the returns experience that sellers trust completely: every filing on time and accurateDrive the metrics that matter: cost per filing, 100% on-time filings, and fewer amendmentsIdentify the next frontier, including whether Anrok's filing engine becomes a standalone platform of its own
