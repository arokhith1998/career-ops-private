## Full JD text (verbatim)

Upgrade helps customers move in the right direction with affordable and responsible financial products. Since 2017, we've helped over 7.5 million customers access over $42 billion in consumer credit. We're backed by some of the most prominent technology investors and were most recently valued at $7.3B.

Upgrade is a fintech company focused on providing affordable and responsible credit to everyday consumers. Our Auto business is growing, and demand generation is how we reach the customers and partners who power that growth. As a Demand Generation Manager, you will own the day-to-day strategy and execution that drive new business across Auto, spanning paid media, email, content, events, and sales-led campaigns.

You will work in close partnership with Sales, Partnerships, Sales Enablement, and Customer Success, and alongside Lifecycle, Product Marketing, Design, Legal & Compliance, and Data partners.

**What You'll Do:**
- Own demand generation strategy for Auto across PPC, email, content, events, and web
- Refine the GTM, messaging, and value proposition for each business, aligned to ICP and buyer personas
- Optimize lead acquisition, conversion, and pipeline velocity, and manage and improve MQL quality and performance
- Partner with Account Executives, Partnerships, and Sales leadership to brainstorm, align on, and launch campaigns
- Refine nurture journeys and build segmentation strategies in HubSpot, Salesforce, and Outreach
- Lead campaigns end to end (strategy, execution, measurement), defining success metrics and setting a clear reporting cadence
- Oversee website strategy and optimization, ensuring alignment with campaign messaging, ICP targeting, SEO best practices, and conversion rate optimization
- Build performance dashboards and attribution reporting tied to revenue outcomes
- Manage the demand generation budget and track program spend against results
- Use AI-enabled tools when helpful to streamline content development, summarize insights, and improve workflow efficiency

**What We Look For:**
- 5-7+ years of B2B or demand generation marketing experience, ideally within fintech, consumer lending, auto finance focused products
- Proven experience leading demand generation and pipeline growth, including paid digital channels such as Google Ads and LinkedIn Ads
- Proficiency in Salesforce and HubSpot
- Demonstrated skills or projects with claude, chatgpt, co-pilot or any other AI tool
- Data-driven mindset with strong analytical skills, including experience with attribution, funnel reporting, and dashboards in Tableau
- Track record of designing and analyzing test-and-learn experiments (e.g., A/B tests)
- Strong understanding of integrated marketing strategy
- Excellent written and verbal communication skills
- BA/BS degree in business or a closely related field, or equivalent practical experience
- Curiosity and adaptability with AI-enabled tools

**What We Offer You:** Competitive salary and stock option plan, 100% paid coverage of medical/dental/vision insurance, flexible PTO, opportunities for professional growth, paid parental leave, health & wellness initiatives. #BI-Hybrid
