Job Summary

We are seeking a Transfer Pricing Economist Manager to join our Tax team. In this role, you will lead strategic transfer pricing analysis, global compliance, and process automation initiatives. You will be a key player in supporting tax planning and global controversy matters within a fast-paced, collaborative, and high-performing environment. This position requires in-depth expertise in transfer pricing rules, a strong foundation in economic analysis, and the ability to monitor and adapt to global tax law developments.

This position is based in Santa Clara and reports to the Head of Global TP based in Santa Clara, and will continuously collaborate closely with the worldwide tax team. The Transfer Pricing Economist Manager will have in-depth expertise in transfer pricing rules and requirements, demonstrate knowledge of relevant tax rules and related application of economic analysis, and will monitor tax law and economic developments globally.

Key Responsibilities

- Lead strategic transfer pricing analysis for M&A activities, including IP valuations and supply chain restructuring.
- Manage the end-to-end global transfer pricing documentation and compliance cycle to ensure timely and accurate reporting.
- Support financial reporting by reviewing financial statements for transfer pricing implications and monitoring the execution of TP policies.
- Collaborate and partner with cross-functional teams, including legal, finance, and accounting, to support business needs and advise on special projects.
- Assist with global expansion by researching local country transfer pricing requirements and analyzing the tax consequences of entering new jurisdictions.
- Proactively identify transfer pricing issues, develop effective strategies, and contribute to the design of automated tools to drive process improvements.
- Prepare robust analyses and responses for transfer pricing topics during local country tax audits and provide support for Advance Pricing Agreement (APA) filings.
- Define criteria for, execute, and analyze benchmarking studies to identify valid comparable companies for our transfer pricing analyses.

Qualifications 

Required Qualifications

- Bachelor's degree in Economics, Accounting, Tax Law, or a related field.
- 8+ years of progressive transfer pricing experience, gained in public accounting and/or a multinational corporate environment.
- Strong technical knowledge of international transfer pricing principles and regulations.
- Demonstrated experience working with benchmarking tools and data to set requirements and analyze results.
- Proficiency with spreadsheet software (Excel, Google Sheets) for complex data analysis.

Preferred Qualifications

- Master's degree or PhD in Economics, Tax, or a related discipline.
- Experience with financial systems and data analysis tools such as SAP (S/4HANA, PaPM), Alteryx, or Access.
- Proven ability to work independently, manage multiple projects, and meet deadlines in a dynamic environment.
- Excellent oral and written communication skills with the ability to explain complex transfer pricing concepts to diverse stakeholders.
