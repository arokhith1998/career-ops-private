Chief of Staff, CEO's Office
InstaService | Remote, India | PST (US Pacific Time) working hours

Work directly with the Founder & CEO of a rapidly growing, AI-powered home services company that is live in all 50 U.S. states.

This is a hardworking role. You will work alongside the CEO for long hours. Apply only if you have a strong work ethic and plan to grow along with the organization.

ABOUT INSTASERVICE
InstaService is building the operating system for home services. Customers book trusted professionals for handyman, cleaning, and plumbing services, scheduled or instant (within 1 hour), and request quotes for larger projects from contractors, electricians, roofers, and other trades.
- Live in all 50 U.S. states
- 10,000+ vetted, background-checked providers, each with a reliability score
- AI operations layer, ServiceGPT, powering the platform
- National partnerships live

THE ROLE
You will operate as an extension of the CEO, owning execution across fundraising, investor outreach, LinkedIn posts, monthly investor newsletters, company social media, strategic research, and high-priority initiatives. This is not an assistant role.

WHAT YOU'LL DO
1. CEO Priorities & Execution -- own and track CEO-level priorities across fundraising, partnerships, growth, and strategy; manage follow-ups and timelines; prepare briefing notes for VC meetings and partner calls.
2. Fundraising & Investor Outreach -- build a targeted VC/angel pipeline (Seed to Series A/B) using LinkedIn, Crunchbase, PitchBook, and founder networks; draft outreach on behalf of the CEO; support pitch decks and data rooms.
3. LinkedIn Posts -- own the CEO's LinkedIn presence; write and publish posts on startup execution, growth, fundraising learnings, product launches, and milestones.
4. Monthly Investor Newsletters -- write and send the monthly investor update covering metrics, milestones, partnerships, launches, hiring, and asks.
5. Company Social Media -- plan and publish posts across InstaService's channels (LinkedIn, X, Instagram, Facebook); run a content calendar.
6. Strategic Research & Cross-Functional Execution -- research competitors, market trends, fundraising benchmarks, and investor theses; prepare strategy memos; drive CEO-led initiatives across Product, Engineering, Marketing, and Operations.

WHAT WE'RE LOOKING FOR
Must have:
- 2 to 6 years in Chief of Staff, Founder's Office, Strategy, Consulting, VC, Growth, or Operations roles
- Strong work ethic and willingness to work long hours alongside the CEO
- Long-term commitment to growing with the organization
- Exceptional writing skills, with experience writing LinkedIn posts, newsletters, and social media content
- Strong LinkedIn research and outreach experience
- Proven ability to work PST hours from India (regular late evenings IST)

Strong plus:
- Exposure to fundraising or investor relations
- Experience supporting US-based founders or C-suite leaders
- Familiarity with pitch decks, startup metrics, and VC workflows

FIRST 90 DAYS SUCCESS: high-quality VC pipeline with consistent outreach; active, credible CEO LinkedIn presence; monthly investor newsletter shipped on time; company social media calendar running; clean tracking of investor conversations; recognized as a trusted partner in the CEO's Office.

COMPENSATION: Competitive and aligned with experience, with performance-linked upside.
