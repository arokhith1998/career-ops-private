### About the Role
YipitData is the leading alternative data platform for institutional investors, giving hedge funds and asset managers the differentiated insights they need to make better investment decisions. We are a multi-modal platform, inclusive of data feeds, our Investor Portal, and AI-powered agents and MCPs, that are redefining how investors discover, analyze, and act on data.

Product Marketing sits at the intersection of Product and Revenue, ensuring what we build, how we position it, and how we sell it are tightly aligned. As we expand into AI-native workflows, this function plays a critical role in shaping how our platform is understood, launched, and sold to increasingly sophisticated buyers.

We're hiring a Senior Product Marketing Manager to own that work for our Public Investor segment. You'll define positioning, lead product launches, and enable our commercial teams to bring the next generation of investor workflows to market. This is a foundational role with real scope, building core PMM capabilities for one of our most critical business lines.

### Key Responsibilities Of The Senior Product Marketing Manager
- Own product positioning and go-to-market strategy for a new generation of AI and big data products, shaping how investors understand and adopt data feeds, the Investor Portal and AI-powered solutions like agents and MCPs
- Define ideal customer profiles (ICPs) and tailoring messaging to different investor segments (e.g., hedge funds vs. long-onlys)
- Partner with Product, Revenue, and Insights teams to influence roadmap decisions based on market demand and customer insights
- Lead competitive intelligence across both traditional data providers and emerging AI platforms to define clear differentiation
- Collaborate on pricing and packaging across a multi-product platform spanning data, software, and AI
- Develop and scale high-impact sales enablement and marketing assets that help commercial teams communicate complex products with clarity
- Establish and tracking success metrics for GTM efforts (e.g., adoption, pipeline impact, win rates) and iterating based on performance

### Qualifications & Skills

- Experience working closely with product teams and influencing decisions early, not just at launch
- Ability to operate both strategically and tactically, with ownership over outcomes end-to-end
- Strong written and verbal communication skills
