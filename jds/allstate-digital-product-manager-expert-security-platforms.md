## Full JD text

At Allstate, great things happen when our people work together to protect families and their belongings from life's uncertainties. 90+ years of history: seat belts, air bags, graduated driving laws, pricing sophistication, telematics, device and identity protection.

Job Description

Seeking an experienced Digital Product Manager to lead strategy, roadmap, and delivery of security-focused products protecting enterprise assets and enabling secure-by-default development practices for the Product Security team. Owns the lifecycle of critical security tools/platforms used by the enterprise. Combines product management expertise with security principles, DevSecOps practices, and modern engineering workflows; leverages AI-driven capabilities to optimize workflows and automate repetitive tasks.

Key responsibilities:
- Define Product Strategy & Vision: establish/communicate vision, roadmap, success metrics for security products aligned to enterprise risk posture/compliance.
- Set Clear Outcomes: define measurable KPIs per product.
- Plan for Scalability & Future Needs: anticipate evolving security threats/compliance; design scalable products.
- Incorporate AI for Efficiency: integrate AI into daily workflows to automate tasks, improve decisions, maximize efficiency.
- Manage Product Development: collaborate with engineering/security on secure-by-default capabilities (IDE, CI/CD pipelines); maintain backlog, write/groom user stories, drive iterative releases (Agile).
- Enable Data-Driven Decisions: define/monitor KPIs; use analytics/risk reporting to validate assumptions.
- Stakeholder Communication: lead product ceremonies; communicate progress/risks/trade-offs to engineering leadership, security teams, partners.

Essential Skills:
- Minimum 5 years' experience conducting product scoping, discovery, framing, and managing a backlog for a digital product team.
- Minimum 1 year's experience coaching/mentoring early-in-trade PM peers across a broader tech org.
- Strong understanding of security principles, secure SDLC, DevSecOps practices.
- Demonstrated ability to define success metrics (KPIs/OKRs), data-driven decisions, long-term strategic planning.
- Proven experience in Agile product delivery.
- Understanding of CI/CD pipelines, policy-as-code, automation in security workflows.

Desirable Skills: familiarity with application security tools (SAST, DAST, SCA), secrets management (Vault/KMS), IAM/PAM, vulnerability management, CSPM; cloud platforms (Azure/AWS/GCP) and PaaS; tools like Postman, Mural/Figma, Jira/Azure DevOps/VersionOne; relevant certifications (Security+, CSSLP, CISSP, CCSP); knowledge of AI-assisted dev tools (Copilot, Cursor).
