Founder's Office Generalist

Basil Health

Location: Bengaluru, India | On-site

Experience: 1-3 years

About Basil Health

Welcome to Basil Health, where Neuroscience meets Artificial Intelligence to redefine mental wellness.

We are a neurotechnology startup building EEG + AI-powered solutions to measure stress, focus, anxiety, and cognitive performance. Our mission is to make mental wellness measurable, personalized, and cost-efficient, helping individuals and organizations invest only in what truly works.

Backed by research collaborations with the Indian Army, IIT Kanpur, IIT Mandi, University of Maryland, and Michigan State University, Basil Health is building the future of neurotech-driven cognitive wellness.

We are at an early stage, moving rapidly toward product launch across consumer and enterprise markets. This is an opportunity to join while the company is still being built and have a direct impact on how it grows.

About the Role

We are looking for a high-ownership generalist to work directly with the Founder's Office across strategy, growth, partnerships, research, and operations.

This is not a coordination or support role. You will work on ambiguous, cross-functional problems, bring structure to them, and drive them through to execution.

The work can range from market research and strategic analysis to partnerships, GTM initiatives, data analysis, process building, and solving high-priority problems across the company.

What You'll Own

- Translate leadership priorities and ambiguous problems into structured plans, decisions, and execution.
- Drive initiatives across GTM, growth, partnerships, business development, and customer acquisition.
- Conduct market, customer, competitive, and technical research and turn findings into actionable insights.
- Analyse business and product data to support strategic decisions and identify opportunities.
- Build processes, trackers, and systems that improve execution across teams.
- Work cross-functionally to move high-priority initiatives forward and solve problems without waiting to be assigned.

What We're Looking For

- 1-3 years of experience in a startup, consulting, business development, strategy, analytics, product, or operations role.
- Strong analytical, problem-solving, research, and communication skills.
- Ability to structure ambiguous problems and work independently without step-by-step instructions.
- Strong ownership mindset and bias toward action.
- Comfortable moving across different functions and learning unfamiliar domains quickly.
- Comfortable with data, spreadsheets, presentations, and modern productivity tools.
- Strong familiarity with AI tools, LLMs, automation, and modern workflows.
- Tier 1 college background preferred.
- Prior startup or fast-paced environment experience is a strong plus.
- High discretion when working with confidential and pre-launch information.

You'll Thrive Here If You

- Take ownership of outcomes, not just tasks.
- Are naturally curious and enjoy figuring things out without a predefined playbook.
- Can move from research, insight, decision, execution.
- Communicate proactively and follow through.
- Enjoy working across functions and solving problems that don't have an obvious owner.

Why Join Basil Health?

- Work directly with leadership on high-priority strategic initiatives.
- Build processes, playbooks, and systems from the ground up.
- Gain cross-functional exposure across strategy, growth, partnerships, product, research, and operations.
- Work at the intersection of neuroscience, AI, and health-tech.
- Have high ownership and direct visibility into how an early-stage company is built.
