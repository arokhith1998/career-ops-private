Global Fluid Networks Lifecycle Services Product Manager
Vertiv | Global Services Business Unit
Role Overview
The Global Fluid Network Lifecycle Services Product Manager is responsible for defining, developing, and scaling Vertiv's global lifecycle service offerings for liquid cooling systems, fluid distribution networks, and thermal management infrastructure. This role owns the service portfolio strategy from concept through global deployment, ensuring offerings are serviceable, scalable, and digitally enabled.
The role partners closely with regional services teams, thermal product management, engineering, and digital teams to launch new service initiatives, establish standardized delivery models, and drive installed base penetration and recurring services revenue.
Key Responsibilities
Service Portfolio Strategy & Development
- Define and own the global lifecycle services roadmap for fluid networks and liquid cooling infrastructure
- Translate customer reliability, uptime, and efficiency needs into standardized service offerings
- Develop clear service definitions including scope, entitlements, and differentiation
Global Deployment & Regional Enablement
- Coordinate with regional services teams to deploy global offerings consistently
- Develop and maintain:
- Statements of Work (SOWs)
- Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)
- Service delivery playbooks
- Support regional adaptations while maintaining global standardization
New Service Launch & Innovation
- Lead market research and competitive analysis for new thermal and fluid services
- Launch new service portfolios including pilots, global rollout, and enablement
- Partner with engineering and product teams to influence design for serviceability and digital readiness
Commercialization & Value Pricing
- Define value-based pricing methodologies aligned to customer outcomes (uptime, efficiency, risk reduction)
- Partner with Sales and Finance to establish pricing guidance and margin targets
- Support sales enablement with value messaging and ROI narratives
Lifecycle Performance Management
- Monitor service adoption, execution quality, and customer outcomes
- Continuously refine offerings using field feedback and performance data
Qualifications
- 7-12+ years in lifecycle services, product management, or thermal infrastructure roles
- Experience with liquid cooling, CDUs, or fluid systems in critical environments
- Strong cross-functional and global collaboration skills

About Vertiv
Vertiv (NYSE: VRT) brings together hardware, software, analytics and ongoing services to enable its customers' vital applications to run continuously, perform optimally and grow with their business needs. Vertiv solves the most important challenges facing today's data centers, communication networks and commercial and industrial facilities with a portfolio of power, cooling and IT infrastructure solutions and services that extend from the cloud to the edge of the network.
