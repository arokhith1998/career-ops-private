## Full JD text (verbatim, source: LinkedIn jobs posting)

About Skyne Beauty

Skyne Beauty is a growing Korean skincare brand offering science-backed skincare solutions to consumers. As we expand our digital presence and product portfolio, we're looking for an experienced brand marketing professional who can strengthen our brand identity, develop engaging content strategies, and drive customer engagement across multiple channels.

About the Role

We're seeking a Senior Brand & Content Marketing Manager to lead brand positioning, content strategy, and creative marketing initiatives. You'll work closely with company leadership to develop compelling brand narratives, execute integrated marketing campaigns, and strengthen Skyne Beauty's presence across digital and consumer channels.

Key Responsibilities
- Develop and execute comprehensive brand marketing and content strategies.
- Lead brand positioning, messaging, storytelling, and creative direction.
- Plan and manage content calendars across Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, and other digital platforms.
- Develop engaging product education, skincare tutorials, and science-backed marketing content.
- Collaborate with designers, photographers, and content creators to produce high-quality marketing assets.
- Manage product launch campaigns, brand awareness initiatives, and promotional activities.
- Optimize website content, product descriptions, and landing pages to improve customer engagement and conversions.
- Develop SEO-driven blog content and educational marketing initiatives.
- Maintain consistent brand messaging across social media, email, e-commerce, and advertising channels.
- Analyze content performance, audience engagement, website traffic, and brand growth metrics.
- Monitor consumer trends, competitor positioning, and emerging opportunities in the beauty industry.
- Present campaign results, creative strategies, and growth recommendations to company leadership.

Qualifications
- 4+ years of professional experience in brand marketing, content marketing, or creative strategy.
- Proven experience developing and executing successful digital content campaigns.
- Strong understanding of brand positioning, consumer behavior, and storytelling.
- Hands-on experience managing social media content across Instagram, TikTok, or similar platforms.
- Experience with SEO, content optimization, and digital marketing analytics.
- Familiarity with Shopify, Google Analytics, Canva, Adobe Creative Suite, or comparable tools.
- Strong project management, creative direction, and communication skills.
- Experience collaborating with designers, photographers, and creative agencies.
- Prior experience in beauty, skincare, cosmetics, wellness, or consumer brands is preferred.
- Ability to independently manage creative initiatives and deliver measurable marketing results.

Compensation & Benefits
- Base Salary: $130,000 annually
- Additional performance-based bonuses tied to brand growth, marketing performance, and business outcomes.
- Opportunity to work directly with company leadership.
- Professional development and expanded leadership opportunities.

Why Join Skyne Beauty?

At Skyne Beauty, we believe strong brands are built through authentic storytelling, meaningful customer relationships, and exceptional product experiences. You'll have the opportunity to shape our brand identity, lead creative marketing initiatives, and influence how consumers discover and connect with our products. If you're passionate about brand building, content strategy, and creating meaningful consumer experiences, we'd love to hear from you.
