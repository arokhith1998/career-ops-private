Skydio is the leading US drone company and the world leader in autonomous flight, the key technology for the future of drones and aerial mobility. The Skydio team combines deep expertise in artificial intelligence, best-in-class hardware and software product development, operational excellence, and customer obsession to empower a broader, more diverse audience of drone users, from utility inspectors to first responders, soldiers in battlefield scenarios, and beyond.

About The Team

The GTM Strategy team is the muscle that connects product capabilities to market reality. Our job is to deeply understand the value of our products in the context of the market and our customers, and operationalize those insights to help Skydio refine our product portfolio and revenue model to scale efficiently.

About The Role

The Go-To-Market Strategy Analyst owns pricing strategy, implementation, and customer ROI modeling, shifting between strategic and deeply tactical work within the same day. This role reports to the Director of GTM Strategy and will work cross-functionally with the Sales, Product, Finance, and Operations teams. It is a high-visibility role suited for an individual excited to take on ambiguous challenges and own them through resolution.

Location: This position is based onsite 3+ days a week at our headquarters in San Mateo, CA.

How You'll Make An Impact

- Lead strategic workstreams across pricing architecture, value quantification models (ROI, TCO), business model development, and competitive positioning.
- Engage directly with customers to structure ambiguous problems into clear, compelling business cases backed by detailed research and analysis.
- Drive pricing implementations and maintain the related systems and documentation.
- Design AI-native workflows that reduce manual work, improve consistency, and accelerate decision-making.

Requirements

- 3+ years of experience in management consulting, investment banking, private equity/venture capital, or a strategic role at a high-growth startup.
- Ability to work onsite at our San Mateo, CA headquarters 3+ days a week.
- Obtaining FAA Part 107 certification within the first 60 days of employment is strongly encouraged for all Skydio employees and required for certain positions.

Experience & Skills

- High proficiency in data modeling and storytelling, with the ability to break down and solve problems through quantitative thinking and analysis.
- Expert fluency in Excel/Sheets.
- Detail-oriented with exceptional written and verbal communication skills; comfortable defending your model to the CFO and explaining pricing rationale to sales reps.
- Proactive problem-solver who moves easily between big-picture strategic planning and detailed tactical execution.
- Preferred experience with several of the following: Databricks, Python, SQL, LLM platforms (Anthropic, OpenAI, Gemini, or similar), Palantir Foundry.
- An advanced degree or MBA is a plus, but not required.
- Hardware experience is a plus, but not required.
