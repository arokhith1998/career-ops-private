- Present findings to executives and cross-functional teams

- 4+ years in product analytics, advanced BI, or similar with exposure to product-led growth, pricing, or platform analytics
- Expert-level SQL proficiency
- Strong data visualization tool expertise
- Python/R statistical modeling capability
- Ability to translate analysis into business and customer outcomes

## Preferred

- Strong measurement strategy and KPI development abilities
- Experimentation and digital analytics background
