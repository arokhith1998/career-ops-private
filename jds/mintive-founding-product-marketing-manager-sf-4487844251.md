## Full JD text (verbatim)

About Us: Mintive helps companies put agents to work safely. We build the infrastructure that makes a company's own work runnable by agents. Our first product is an MCP gateway that gives agents governed access to company data and tools.

We work in person in the San Francisco Bay Area. We are AI-native. Everyone uses Claude and Codex every day, and we have more agents than employees.

Since launching in February 2026, we have grown to 50+ paying customers, with strong inbound demand. Customers include Coursera, Arlo, and Braze. We are backed by Cowboy Ventures and Coatue, with angels including Frederic Kerrest (Okta), Michael Grinich (WorkOS), Andrej Karpathy, Jeff Dean, and Scott Belsky. Our founders are machine learning experts from Stanford, Berkeley, and CMU, with applied research at Google Brain and Waymo.

The Job: You own how Mintive shows up in the world. This is a founding role, so you own the whole arc: set messaging and narrative, drive launches end to end, turn customers like Coursera, Stability AI, and Modern Treasury into case studies, establish Mintive's point of view on agent governance, and build the sales content system.

You'll scale yourself with Mintive's team of coworker agents (our own product).

**About You**
- 5+ years in product marketing, ideally with technical or infrastructure products.
- A strong writer and storyteller who can turn deep technical capability into a clear narrative.
- Can sit with engineers and customers, absorb deep technical detail, and turn it into differentiated, jargon-free messaging.
- A strategic thinker -- sets positioning and priorities, makes sharp trade-offs.
- Agent-native -- uses Claude and agents to scale their own output.
- Well organized -- the scope is broad.
- Bias to ship -- can run a launch and move fast.
- A track record: a product or category positioned, a launch run, and the results that followed.

**Nice to Have**
- Familiarity with MCP, AI agents, or enterprise security.
- Knows the CIO, CISO, and platform/IT buyer.
- Fluent across the content and search stack -- SEO and AEO.
- Been an early marketing hire or built a function from scratch.

Details: In person, San Mateo and San Francisco. Competitive salary and meaningful early-stage equity.
