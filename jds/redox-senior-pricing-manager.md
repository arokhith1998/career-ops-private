## Full JD text

Opportunity & Impact: As Director of Pricing, you will own how pricing works operationally at Redox. Pricing and packaging strategy is set by Product; your job is to take that strategy and make it real - turning it into deal-level guardrails, tools, and decisions that Sales can use every day. You will run Redox's Deal Desk for bespoke, non-standard deals: working directly with reps and reviewing Gong call recordings to understand what a customer needs, and structuring the right combination of platform fee, connection pricing, and transaction pricing for that specific deal. You will also be the connective tissue between the field and Product: surfacing what's actually happening in negotiations - where reps get stuck, where customers push back, what packaging doesn't hold up in practice - so Product can continue to refine pricing strategy with real deal data. This is an execution and operations role, not a pricing-strategy-design role.

Job Responsibilities:
40% Deal Desk & Execution: serve as Redox's Deal Desk for bespoke, non-standard enterprise deals; pull deal context from AEs and Gong recordings; structure pricing across platform fee, connection-based pricing, and transaction-based pricing within Product's strategy; advise reps in real time on discounts, multi-year terms, minimum commitments, ramp schedules, MFN clauses; partner with Sales leadership, Finance, and Legal on contract-ready terms.
25% Pricing Operations & Governance: set and enforce discounting guardrails and approval thresholds; build and maintain self-serve pricing tools/models; own the operational workflow and systems (CPQ, Salesforce); maintain documentation of pricing rules, exceptions, approval paths.
20% Cross-Functional Collaboration & Insight: partner with Product on operational guardrails; track pricing/discounting patterns and feed findings back to Product; represent the voice of Sales and the field; partner with Finance on margin/revenue alignment.
15% Change Management & Enablement: lead rollout of pricing and packaging changes; partner with Sales Enablement and field leadership on tools/guidance/collateral; provide leadership updates on Deal Desk performance.

Required Skills and Experience: 8+ years in deal desk, sales finance, revenue operations, or pricing operations, ideally at a B2B SaaS company with usage-based or hybrid pricing. Proven track record structuring and negotiating complex, multi-element enterprise deals. Experience operationalizing pricing/packaging strategy set by others into guardrails, tools, and repeatable process. Comfortable working directly with reps under deal pressure. Strong financial modeling and analytical skills. Strong cross-functional collaboration skills. Healthcare, health tech, or healthcare interoperability experience is a plus, not required. Ability to effectively leverage AI tools in day-to-day work. Bachelor's degree in Business, Finance, Economics, or a related field (strongly preferred).

Tools: Salesforce, Gong, CPQ/quoting tool, Google Suite, AI tools (e.g. Claude).
