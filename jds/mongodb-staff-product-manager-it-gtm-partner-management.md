## Full JD Text

The IT Go-to-Market organization builds and operates technology platforms powering Sales, Marketing, and Revenue Operations, enabling scalable lead generation, pipeline management, deal execution, and revenue insights.

As a Staff Product Manager on the Partner Management team, you will own internal technology products supporting Partner capabilities including PRM platforms, AI capabilities, workflows, partner productivity, and ecosystem integration technologies.

You'll collaborate with GTM and Partner teams to define product strategy, prioritize initiatives, and deliver solutions improving seller productivity, accelerating deal execution, and enabling data-driven decision-making.

**Responsibilities include:**
- Leading product strategy for partner-first GTM and PRM capabilities
- Building scalable workflows for onboarding, deal registration, attribution, and partner operations
- Demonstrating deep expertise in partner tooling, programs, incentives, and information sharing
- Understanding engagement across all partner types (Resellers, Cloud Partners, Tech Partners, ISVs)
- Driving AI-enabled automation for partner administration
- Defining integrations between Salesforce, partner platforms, and internal systems
- Improving partner visibility, operational efficiency, and engagement processes

**Work Experience:** 12+ years of Product Management in enterprise SaaS/cloud platforms with PRM or partner operations experience.
