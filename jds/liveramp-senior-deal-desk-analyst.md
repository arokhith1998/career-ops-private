## Role summary
Provides deal structuring and pricing support across LiveRamp's sales organization, advising Sales, Legal, Finance, and Accounting, and helping scale the internal Deal Desk function.

## Responsibilities
- First-line Deal Desk support for new business, upsell, and renewal deals.
- Draft custom order forms for non-standard terms; maintain a clause library.
- Escalate non-standard deals; deliver published pricing guidance.
- Package final work orders after sign-offs; keep Salesforce/order-form data aligned.
- Review large end-of-quarter contracts; drive continuous improvement for SFDC, CLM, and CPQ.

## Qualifications
- 2-3 years of experience in contract, order, or deal desk management.
- Salesforce/CRM experience; CLM experience preferred; ERP proficiency.
- Strong Excel skills; strong communication and training ability; attention to detail.
