CoreWeave is seeking an exceptional Sr Analyst, RevOps - Field Planning and Performance. You'll work closely with Sales Leadership, Marketing, RevOps, Sales Enablement, Data Engineering and Finance to analyze sales performance, identify improvement opportunities, and support the execution of strategic initiatives across the funnel.

This is a pivotal moment for CoreWeave, as we scale our revenue operations infrastructure to support our trajectory as a newly public AI hyperscaler. Key Responsibilities

- Contribute to the development of GTM strategies for new product launches or market entries
- Monitor, analyze, and report on sales performance metrics across segments, verticals, and geographies
- Identify performance gaps at the rep, team, and regional level, and propose data-backed recommendations
- Partner with Sales Leadership and RevOps to build dashboards, scorecards, and KPIs that drive accountability
- Support territory and quota planning, coverage optimization, and segmentation strategies
- Conduct win/loss analysis, pipeline conversion studies, and sales cycle analysis to inform GTM motions
- Provide strategic insights for QBRs, forecast reviews, and executive reporting
- Collaborate with Sales Enablement to identify rep skill gaps and training opportunities based on performance data
- Use AI tools (LLMs, AI copilots, workflow automation) to accelerate analysis, reporting, and forecasting, and help build a next-generation, AI-driven RevOps function.
- Build and maintain predictive forecasting and long-range revenue models, not just review forecasts.

Who You Are

- 5+ years of experience in a GTM, strategy, business operations, or related analytics role.
- Strong analytical skills with experience in Excel, SQL, BI tools
- Experience working with CRM and sales enablement platforms (Salesforce, HighSpot, etc.).
- Ability to synthesize complex data into actionable insights and present them clearly.
- Strong communication and stakeholder management skills.
- Experience in management consulting, PE, B2B SaaS, Cloud, or a high-growth technology company is a plus.
- Bias to action in a fast-moving, hyper-growth environment.

- AI-first by default: you already reach for AI tools to work faster and smarter, and you are excited to help reinvent how a RevOps team operates with AI.
