## Job description (full text via LinkedIn guest API)

About Us

Established in 1996, Morphogenesis is globally recognized for its diverse work, encompassing a range of specialized practice areas such as Master Planning, Urban Design, Architecture, Interior Design, and Landscape Design.

About the role

You will be a part of the Founder's office to drive business planning, performance analysis and process automation. You will work across studios and support functions and drive strategic initiatives from idea to closure. The role suits someone who is analytically sharp, comfortable with ambiguity, and able to influence senior architects and partners without direct authority.

Key responsibilities

1. Automation, process & AI-led transformation
- Map core processes, identify manual and repetitive work, and build a prioritized automation roadmap.
- Lead automation projects end to end: requirements, tools, rollout, adoption and measurement of time saved.
- Pilot AI-enabled and agentic workflows with studios and support teams.
- Standardize SOPs and templates and contribute to the firm-wide Digital Delivery Manuals.
- Build and maintain leadership dashboards that track studio performance, billing, utilization, receivables and pipeline.

2. Business planning, financial performance & EBITDA
- Drive the annual operating plan: revenue, sales, billing, headcount and cost targets.
- Run the monthly and quarterly business reviews, with clear variance analysis and agreed actions.
- Set up project-level profitability tracking (fee versus effort, margin by project and studio) and flag overruns early.
- Identify EBITDA improvement levers and track agreed actions through to closure.

3. Governance & leadership institutionalization
- Run the leadership operating rhythm: agendas, presentations, pre-reads and minutes for leadership, partner and board meetings.
- Track decisions and action items across leadership forums through to closure.
- Help set up decision-making governance (decision rights, approval matrices, escalation paths) to reduce founder dependency.
- Support performance management and succession planning, including KPIs and scorecards for studio leaders.
- Act as the link between the COO, the Founders, studio leaders and support functions.
- Support firm-building initiatives such as knowledge management and leadership events.

4. Growth & strategic projects
- Build business cases, financial models and unit economics for new service lines, geographies, partnerships/JVs.
- Carry out market and competitor research to inform growth choices.

Requirements

Must have
- 3-5 years in consulting or business operations
- Strong analytical and financial skills: Excel modelling, financial statements and KPI design. Exposure to workflow automation or AI tools is a plus.
- Excellent written and verbal communication, with the confidence to present to senior leadership.
- Proven ability to work across functions and drive outcomes without formal authority.
- MBA / PGP in Strategy, Finance or Operations.
- Highly organized, discreet and trustworthy with confidential information.
- Comfortable with ambiguity and shifting priorities, with a strong bias for action.
- Takes initiative and ownership; builds strong relationships across teams.
