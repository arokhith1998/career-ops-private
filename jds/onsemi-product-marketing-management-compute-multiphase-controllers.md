Job Description
Sr. Product Marketing Manager - Multiphase Controllers Compute (Client, Cloud & AI)

The Sr. Product Marketing Manager is a senior leadership role at onsemi for a motivated, customer-focused, and self-driven leader to own and drive the multiphase VRM controller product portfolio for CPU, GPU, and high-performance compute applications across the Client, Cloud, and AI datacenter segments. This role provides a unique opportunity to influence next-generation platform architectures, drive design wins with leading customers, and establish market leadership in multiphase power control solutions.

Responsibilities
As the Sr. Product Marketing Manager, you will:
- Lead product strategy for multiphase controllers from definition through market release and drive profitable growth of the portfolio
- Develop and execute product roadmaps based on deep understanding of CPU/GPU power delivery architectures, VRM requirements, telemetry, and control systems
- Act as the key interface with Sales, FAE, and key customers to drive design-in opportunities and achieve design-win targets
- Provide thought leadership in multiphase controllers, system-level power optimization, and platform architecture, anticipating and shaping market needs
- Meet or exceed revenue, design-win, and margin goals for the business unit
- Collaborate with teams across Tech Dev, Design, Product & Test engineering, Project Management, Firmware, and Apps Engineering, to ensure product success
- Influence architecture decisions with OEMs, hyperscalers, and ecosystem partners
- Support customer engagements, technical discussions, and industry forums to build strong relationships and drive adoption
- Balance product differentiation, feature set, and cost structure to ensure competitive and scalable solutions

Qualifications
Qualifications that will allow you to be successful:
- 8+ years in the semiconductor industry and/or system-level power electronics
- Domain expertise in multiphase controllers, and voltage regulator architectures
- Understanding of CPU/GPU compute power delivery requirements and system-level challenges
- Proven experience in product management, product marketing, or product line leadership, including driving design wins
- Strong technical capability combined with a customer-focused mindset and ability to engage system architects and power engineers
- Demonstrated ability to develop business cases, pricing strategies, and market plans
- Experience working with global customers and cross-functional teams in a matrixed organization
- Strong problem-solving, strategic thinking, communication, and negotiation skills
- Excellent presentation and interpersonal skills to internal senior management and external customer leaders
