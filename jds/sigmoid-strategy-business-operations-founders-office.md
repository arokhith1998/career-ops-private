## Job description (full text via LinkedIn guest API)

Operational Excellence

Role Overview

We are seeking a dynamic, strategic, and execution-oriented leader to join the Founders' Office team. Working directly alongside Executive Leadership and Founders, you will serve as a core force multiplier in driving corporate strategy, scaling business operations, and executing high-priority strategic initiatives.

Key Responsibilities
- Strategic Planning & Execution: Work closely with the Leadership team to define, operationalize, and track enterprise OKRs, annual business priorities, and global growth strategies.
- Business Operations Excellence: Design, optimize, and implement scalable governance and operational frameworks across business functions to boost delivery efficiency and operating margins.
- Executive Decision Support: Conduct deep-dive financial and business analysis to provide actionable insights for critical leadership decisions, including new market expansion, strategic partnerships, and capital allocation.
- Cross-Functional Program Management: Program manage multi-stream strategic initiatives, ensuring cross-functional alignment, accountability, on-time delivery, and measurable ROI.
- Leadership Enablement & Governance: Own board deck preparations, executive business reviews (QBRs), investor updates, and town hall narratives. Serve as a trusted thought partner to founders during key strategic discussions.
- Special Initiatives & Incubations: Take full end-to-end ownership of unstructured, high-priority projects (e.g., global entity setup, strategic org design, M&A integration, strategic positioning).

Ideal Candidate Profile
- Education: MBA from a top-tier institution (IIT/IIM/ISB or equivalent tier-1 global business school).
- Experience: 8-11 years of progressive experience in management consulting (Top-tier firms preferred), corporate strategy, business operations, IT services, or analytics-driven enterprises.
- Problem-Solving & Rigor: Proven track record of breaking down complex, ambiguous business problems into structured, actionable frameworks.
- Stakeholder Management: Demonstrated capability to interface with, influence, and align founders, board members, and senior VP-level executives.
- Mindset: High ownership, entrepreneurial orientation, comfortable navigating fast-paced, matrixed, and rapidly evolving environments.

Why Join Us?
- Work hand-in-hand with Leaders and executives on top-priority corporate agenda items.
- High Visibility & Ownership: Lead enterprise-critical initiatives with high autonomy and board-level visibility.
- Global Scale & Impact: Influence the trajectory of a rapidly scaling analytics and technology services leader partnering with Fortune 500 enterprise clients.
- Accelerated Career Trajectory: Benefit from high-tempo mentorship, diverse project exposure, and clear pathways into business unit leadership or functional executive roles.
