## About Workato
Control and execution platform for enterprise AI, unifying data, applications, and processes so AI can run business processes in production. Cites customers including Nasdaq, Amazon, and Cisco.

## Role
Owns the customer-facing authoring, testing, and debugging experiences used to build automations. Focus: making complex orchestration easier to create, understand, test, and troubleshoot, while adding AI assistance that keeps user control, transparency, and reliability.

## Responsibilities
- Enable advanced integration patterns (scatter-gather, async processing, multiple triggers, conditional routing, error handling and compensation).
- Lead builder experience innovation; set product strategy and roadmap, including AI copilot features and advanced debugging.
- Develop AI-powered development assistance: code suggestions, smart error detection, contextual recommendations.
- Build development tools spanning macro-level asset management and micro-level step/loop debugging, plus design-time testing, undo/redo, drafts, autosave.
- Lead cross-functional execution with engineering, UX, architecture, Platform Experience PM, customer success, and support.

## Requirements
- 8+ years of product management experience in SaaS or B2B environments.
- Expertise in developer tools, IDE/visual programming design, and iPaaS or workflow platforms.
- Technical depth in integration patterns, APIs, data transformation, enterprise scalability.
- Experience with AI/ML in development tools; understanding of NLP and predictive analytics.
- Customer empathy across personas, from citizen developers to enterprise architects.
- Data-driven decision-making, including metrics, experimentation, and A/B testing.
- Bachelor's degree in computer science, engineering, HCI, or related field; advanced degree a plus.
