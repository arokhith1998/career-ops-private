## Full JD text (verbatim)

#TeamNextdoor. Nextdoor (NASDAQ: KIND) is the essential neighborhood network. Neighbors, public agencies, and businesses use Nextdoor to connect around local information that matters in more than 340,000 neighborhoods across 11 countries.

We offer a warm and inclusive work environment with a hybrid model that blends time in the office with working from home.

The Impact You'll Make: Small businesses are at the heart of every neighborhood. As our Product Marketing Manager for SMB, you will uncover what truly motivates a local business owner to show up on Nextdoor, earn the trust of their neighbors, and eventually advertise. You will own the SMB product marketing strategy, translating customer insights and long-range business goals into positioning, launches, and growth loops that fuel product-led growth.

You will be the voice of the small business owner in product decisions, including 0-to-1 product requirements. You will use AI as a daily partner to create, investigate, and collaborate.

**Your Responsibilities**
- Build first-hand understanding of small business owners: their goals, what success looks like to them, and what makes them engage in community and choose to advertise.
- Be the product expert, internally and externally, for our small business customers and partners.
- Develop impactful positioning and breakthrough messaging that makes Nextdoor's value to local businesses clear and differentiated.
- Identify and prioritize the most promising opportunities to grow SMB adoption; design and run low-cost or no-cost growth experiments.
- Lead product launches and feature releases across multiple channels to drive acquisition, engagement, and retention.
- Use AI daily to create, investigate, and collaborate: synthesize customer signal, prototype messaging and campaigns, analyze data, and build repeatable workflows.
- Identify insights from the competitive landscape, voice of customer, NPS, and market trends.
- Build analytical business cases and measurable KPIs for experiments and product marketing plans.
- Define priorities and execution for SMB product marketing and align leadership and cross-functional partners.

**What You'll Bring To The Team**
- 5+ years in product marketing or growth marketing.
- Deep, demonstrated understanding of small business owners, backed by specific examples.
- A track record of growing adoption or revenue with little or no paid spend.
- Strong PMM foundations: positioning and messaging, exceptional writing, and analytical rigor.
- Real AI fluency.
- Entrepreneurial instinct and genuine curiosity about helping small businesses succeed.
- Strategic ownership and independent judgment in an ambiguous, fast-moving environment.
- Exceptional written and verbal communication.
- A collaborator who builds strong relationships across functions and seniority levels.

Bonus points: Experience with advertising solutions, local or marketplace platforms, or community-led growth.

The salary range will be determined by the candidate's geographic location." Meaningful equity grant with quarterly vesting. Choice of health plans including a 100% covered employee-only option, plus OneMedical membership.
