## Job Description (full, verbatim from Weekday posting)
Position Title: Chief of Staff
Reporting to: CEO
Function: NSDC
Sub-Function: Advocacy

Purpose
The Chief of Staff is a critical member of the leadership support team, providing comprehensive and strategic support to the CEO. This role ensures the smooth and efficient operation of the CEO's office by facilitating cross-departmental communication and coordination, overseeing key projects and initiatives, and representing the CEO when needed. The position requires a proactive, adaptable professional who can excel in a fast-paced environment while managing a wide range of responsibilities with exceptional efficiency, judgment, and discretion.

Key Responsibilities

Business Analytics & Performance Monitoring
- Serve as the central repository for Key Result Areas (KRAs) of the organization.
- Track performance with monthly and weekly dashboards in a project-driven environment.
- Coordinate with business leaders to establish a regular cadence of reviews and performance monitoring.
- Identify flag risk areas and work with leaders to define mitigation measures.

Project Management
- Lead and support strategic projects and initiatives on behalf of the CEO and executive leadership.
- Organize key events, manage special assignments, and oversee cross-functional project teams to ensure timely and effective delivery.

Representation, Communication & Coordination
- Represent the CEO in internal and external meetings, including engagements with officials and key stakeholders.
- Ensure effective communication flow between the CEO and various departments.

Contingency & Issue Management
- Manage day-to-day contingencies, urgencies, and operational issues on behalf of the CEO.
- Act as a central point of contact for internal and external stakeholders, including board members, investors, partners, and senior leaders.
- Facilitate alignment, coordinate activities, and support smooth organizational functioning.

Strategic Planning & Execution
- Support the CEO in formulating and executing strategic plans and organizational objectives.
- Conduct research, analyze data, and prepare presentations and reports to inform decision-making.

Information & Confidentiality Management
- Handle sensitive and confidential information with high discretion and integrity.
- Maintain efficient systems for organizing, storing, and retrieving executive-level documents, reports, and materials.

Team & Cross-Functional Support
- Collaborate with the executive leadership team and other departments to ensure clarity, alignment, and timely execution of priorities.
- Act as a liaison to streamline communication and enhance cross-functional coordination.

Executive Office Leadership & Administrative Support
- Lead the Executive Support Team to ensure seamless delivery of administrative services.
- Provide high-level administrative assistance to the CEO, including calendar management, meeting scheduling, travel coordination, and preparation of presentations and briefing materials.

Knowledge and Experience
Education Qualifications: Bachelor's in administration or related domain. Master's in Administration or equivalent.
Experience: Minimum 11-15 years of experience in strategy, management consulting or advisory; experience in consulting preferred.
Language Skills: Must be fluent in English and Hindi.
Core Values and Attributes: Impact, Inclusion, Integrity, Innovation.
