**Saved:** 2026-04-12

**Salary:** $132,000-$147,000

## Job Description
Growth-focused marketing leader to accelerate self-serve advertising adoption among small-to-medium business merchants. Driving merchant engagement through website optimization, campaign strategy, and lifecycle marketing integrations.

## Responsibilities
- Oversee complete redesign of Uber Advertising website and integrate specialized ad content into merchant portal
- Develop and execute global paid media and webinar campaigns targeting unmanaged SMB restaurants
- Collaborate with product marketing and CRM teams on consistent messaging and data-triggered communications
- Expand evergreen educational programs to managed accounts for broader self-serve tool adoption
- Direct marketing strategy for new Learning Center focusing on small business onboarding and education
- Facilitate customer data migration and build B2B marketing infrastructure for scalable growth
- Monitor KPIs including SMB penetration, quarterly retention, and conversion metrics

## Minimum Qualifications
- 5-7+ years in Growth Marketing, SMB marketing, or Lifecycle Marketing
- Proven ability executing paid acquisition campaigns and managing B2B website strategy initiatives

## Preferred
- Expertise leveraging data segmentation and product analytics for personalized customer journey development
- Track record managing senior sales teams while aligning marketing with commercial objectives
- Strong presentation and communication skills for executive audiences
- AdTech or commerce media industry background
- Hands-on experience with Martech and CRM tools (specifically Marketo and Salesforce)
