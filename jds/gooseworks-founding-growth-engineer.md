## About Gooseworks

Goose is an OpenClaw-style AI coworker that does real GTM work - finds high-signal leads, runs SEO, runs outbound campaigns, tracks competitors, pulls marketing reports, manages CRM, and more. Each coworker has its own filesystem, memory, mailbox, and accounts. Users talk to Goose from Slack, Telegram, mail, or anywhere - like any other coworker.

Team of 3 - previously built Athina AI (LLM observability & evaluation platform used by Perplexity, You.com, Doximity, Meesho, and several public companies). Gooseworks is a few months old.

Traction so far:
- Built + shipped product with paying customers
- Thousands of signups, 50+ paying customers
- Skill library with 100+ GTM skills featured in TLDR newsletter
- Viral on X 5+ times with 1.5M impressions on socials
- Grew a subreddit to 30K monthly visitors and 2,000+ community members
- Large part of this done by Goose itself

## Role

Builder role for someone who lives in Claude Code / Codex / OpenClaw / Hermes and is obsessed with growth as a craft. NOT a typical sales/marketer role.

Half time = building, operating, tuning AI-powered growth engines for customers. Half time = R&D - turning what works into self-serve playbooks Goose agents can run autonomously.

Two business motions:
1. **The product** - workspace where AI coworkers run growth engines (PLG, self-serve).
2. **Growth-as-a-service** - bespoke growth engines configured + run for B2B startups (also the learning loop - every customer engine becomes a templatized playbook on product).

Wedge ICP: founders, GTM engineers, growth operators at fast growing startups.

## Ownership (50/50 split)

**1. Customer growth engines (50%)** - Own customer outcomes end-to-end. Outbound campaigns, SEO content, Reddit growth, AEO, influencer sourcing - whatever moves the customer's KPI. Build the engine (skills, automations, context, integrations), run it, iterate weekly with the customer until the numbers move. Bar: "customer hit their KPI", not "we shipped the engine".

**2. R&D for AI coworkers (50%)** - Every customer engine becomes a templatized playbook in Gooseworks. You're the loop that turns one-off agency work into self-serve product. Push boundaries of what Goose agents can do autonomously - new agent architectures, new skills, new playbooks for uncracked channels. Contribute to public skills repo, ship cornerstone agent systems, help define what "an AI coworker that runs growth" actually means.

## Timeline

- **First 7 days:** onboard + meet team. Create skills for public repository. Set up 1-2 useful growth engines end-to-end (for Gooseworks itself).
- **First 30 days:** build more growth engines end-to-end. Templatize into customer-configurable playbooks. Work with customers to tailor engines, identify issues, drive product feedback. R&D new playbooks.
- **3 months in:** orchestrating a fleet of AI coworkers for Gooseworks + customer growth. Helping unblock deals with larger customers.
