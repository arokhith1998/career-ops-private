## Full JD text

About E2M
E2M is a leading white-label growth partner for digital agencies worldwide, helping them scale through expert web development, digital marketing, and AI-powered solutions. With 13+ years of experience, 350+ professionals, and 400+ agency partners globally, E2M offers services across WordPress, Shopify, SEO, PPC, content marketing, and AI automation. Driven by an agency-first approach and our Black Label Standard, we work as an extension of our partners' teams, delivering quality, innovation, and reliable digital solutions that help them grow.

**Role Purpose**
We are looking for an exceptional Chief of Staff / Executive Operator to work directly with our CEO across multiple businesses and strategic initiatives. This is not a traditional administrative or project management role. We need a highly proactive, resourceful operator who can take ideas, priorities, and problems and turn them into completed outcomes with minimal oversight. You will serve as an extension of the CEO, helping drive execution, eliminate operational friction, and ensure important commitments don't get lost between meetings, teams, and business units. The ideal candidate is a builder and problem-solver who thrives in ambiguity, embraces technology and AI, and takes pride in making things happen. Your mission: Make sure more important things get done without the CEO having to manage them.

Location: Ahmedabad, India (Hybrid / Office-Based)
Reports To: Group CEO
Schedule: Full-time, with regular overlap with US Mountain Time

**Key Responsibilities**

*1. Executive Operations & CEO Leverage*
Own and maintain a centralized system for tracking the CEO's priorities, commitments, follow-ups, and outstanding decisions. Proactively manage executive workflows, meeting preparation, communications, and follow-through. Prepare concise daily executive briefings highlighting priorities, decisions, deadlines, and potential issues. Identify work that can be eliminated, delegated, automated, or independently completed. Protect the CEO's time by minimizing unnecessary meetings, interruptions, and administrative activities. Coordinate with internal and external stakeholders to move work forward without requiring constant direction.

*2. Independent Project Execution*
Take ambiguous ideas and business objectives and independently turn them into actionable projects and completed deliverables. Own special projects from initial concept through implementation. Coordinate resources across E2M, DOT, and other business units as appropriate. Anticipate roadblocks, solve problems, and proactively maintain momentum. Research opportunities, evaluate solutions, and make practical recommendations. Deliver working solutions, not just plans, presentations, or documentation.

*3. Process Improvement, AI & Automation*
Identify repetitive tasks, operational bottlenecks, and inefficient workflows. Design and implement simple, practical solutions that reduce manual work and complexity. Leverage AI tools, agents, and automations to improve the CEO's effectiveness. Work closely with E2M's AI and technical teams to implement more advanced workflows where needed. Develop systems for managing routine executive processes such as NDA requests, approvals, follow-ups, and information gathering. Prioritize simplicity, adoption, and measurable outcomes over unnecessary process documentation.

*4. Leadership Accountability & Business Execution*
Support Weekly Business Reviews (WBRs), Monthly Business Reviews (MBRs), and executive operating rhythms. Track strategic priorities, commitments, and cross-functional initiatives. Proactively follow up with stakeholders, identify delays, and help remove blockers. Synthesize business intelligence, KPIs, and financial information into concise, actionable insights. Ensure leadership discussions translate into clear ownership, action, and measurable progress. Escalate risks and decisions appropriately without becoming an unnecessary layer of management.

*5. Strategic Initiatives & CEO Representation*
Support growth initiatives, strategic partnerships, business development, and special projects. Independently advance opportunities between CEO interactions. Prepare business cases, research, recommendations, and decision-ready summaries. Represent the CEO's priorities in appropriate internal discussions. Build effective working relationships with leadership teams in India and North America. Exercise sound judgment, discretion, and appropriate decision-making authority.

**Outcomes & Measures of Success**
Success in this role will be measured primarily by outcomes, not activity, across: CEO Leverage (measurable reduction in time spent by the CEO on administrative work), Independent Execution (multiple initiatives completed with limited CEO intervention), Operational Improvement (recurring workflows simplified, automated, or eliminated), and Leadership Effectiveness (critical commitments consistently tracked and completed).

**Required Competencies**
Extreme Ownership, Proactive Execution, Bias for Action, Resourcefulness, Simplification, Business Judgment, Exceptional Follow-Through, AI & Technical Fluency, Executive Communication, Adaptability.

**Ideal Candidate Profile**
- 5+ years of relevant experience in business operations, executive operations, project execution, entrepreneurship, or a Chief of Staff capacity.
- Demonstrated experience independently delivering business projects and solving operational problems.
- Strong written and spoken English, with the ability to communicate effectively with US-based executives.
- Experience working with international teams, preferably US-based organizations.
- High proficiency with Google Workspace, Slack, and modern productivity tools.
- Hands-on experience using AI tools such as ChatGPT or Claude.
- Familiarity with workflow automation platforms such as Zapier or n8n is highly desirable.
- Strong analytical skills and financial/business literacy.
- High discretion and comfort handling confidential business information.
- Comfortable working with regular US Mountain Time overlap.
- Previous experience as a founder, operations leader, senior executive assistant, or Chief of Staff is valuable, but demonstrated independent execution is more important than job title.

**How We Work**
We value people who take initiative, solve problems, and deliver results: own the outcome not just the task, solve problems before escalating them, build the solution instead of just documenting the problem, keep things simple, close the loop on every commitment, protect the CEO's time and attention, move forward without waiting for perfect instructions.

Seniority level: Mid-Senior level. Employment type: Full-time. Industry: IT Services and IT Consulting.
