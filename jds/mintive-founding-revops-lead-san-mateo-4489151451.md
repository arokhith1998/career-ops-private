## Full description (verbatim via headless browser render of the LinkedIn guest posting page)
About Us
Mintive helps companies put agents to work safely. Our first product is an MCP gateway that gives agents governed access to company data and tools, scoping access down to exactly what an agent needs and capturing audit logs. We're hybrid, flat, AI-native (everyone uses Claude and Codex daily; more agents than employees). Since launching in February 2026, we have grown to 50+ paying customers, including Coursera, Arlo, and Braze. Backed by Cowboy Ventures and Coatue, with angels including Frederic Kerrest (Okta), Michael Grinich (WorkOS), Andrej Karpathy, Jeff Dean, and Scott Belsky. Founders are ML experts from Stanford, Berkeley, and CMU.

The Job
Mintive is growing fast and opening new go-to-market motions with a small, efficient team. You report to and work directly with the Head of Revenue. The role is hybrid in the Bay Area, out of San Francisco and/or San Mateo offices.

What you'll do
- Map every workflow across marketing, sales, support, finance, alliances; rank by revenue impact and automate.
- Build agents that do the work: account research/prep, lead routing/enrichment, follow-ups, deal-review assembly, renewal risk flags, partner co-sell kickoff.
- Engineer the data foundation (one source of truth across marketing/sales/support/finance/alliance partners).
- Launch every new motion with automation and instrumentation built in from day one, including partner attribution.
- Turn signal into action — leading indicators and pipeline shifts trigger plays automatically.

What success looks like
Year one: rep productive capacity and forecast accuracy >=95%. 90 days: three agents/automations live in production with measured impact, plus a ranked backlog. Tools: Attio, long-horizon agents, Claude, OpenAI, Slack, Google Workspace.

You'll likely thrive if
- You've shipped real systems (an agent in production, an automation that replaced a team's busywork, a messy dataset tamed into machine-actionable data).
- "You're a mid-level, exceptional builder with 4+ years in RevOps, sales ops, GTM engineering, or a similar role."
- You're agent-native (use Claude and agents to scale your own output).
- You move fast without much structure and add structure without bureaucracy.

Nice to have: MCP/AI agent/enterprise security familiarity; self-serve+PLG and sales-led motion experience; partner attribution/co-sell reporting; founding/early ops hire experience.

What we offer: Competitive base and significant equity; ownership of the revenue engine; direct access to Head of Revenue and founders; health benefits. "Prefer to have your agent apply? Point it at https://www.mintive.com/hiring/mcp"
