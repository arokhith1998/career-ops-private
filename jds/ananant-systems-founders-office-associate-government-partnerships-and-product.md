Founders' Office Associate - Government, Partnerships and Product
Ananant Systems | Noida | Full-time | Regular travel within India | Reports to the CEO

Ananant is hiring a Founders' Office Associate to own government programmes, partnerships, product input, contracts and marketing, working directly with the CEO and CTO.

About Ananant: Indian deep-tech semiconductor company designing chips for small-cell 4G/5G and camera applications; teams in Bengaluru, Noida, San Diego. Backed by venture capital and national semiconductor programmes; growing several-fold over the next 18 months.

The Role: Outside engineering, Ananant depends on three things: government programmes it wins, customers/partners who adopt its chips, and market standing. This role owns all three. Works directly with CEO and CTO; represents Ananant to government/partners/customers and brings learnings back into the company.

What You Will Own:
- Government programmes: identify/apply/deliver government semiconductor and R&D programmes (applications, milestones, reporting, compliance) and manage relationships with ministries/agencies.
- Partnerships and lead customers: build/manage the pipeline of strategic partners and lead customers; track every commitment.
- Product and market: bring customer requirements/market info/competitive analysis into product decisions; build business cases for new opportunities.
- Contracts: manage customer/partner/vendor/government agreements through review/approval/signature with the law firm; maintain contract register/renewals/obligations.
- Marketing: own positioning, collateral, the website, industry-event presence.

What You Will Deliver (year 1): government programmes won with every report/milestone delivered on time; partnerships signed and lead customers evaluating the chips; customer/market input feeding product decisions regularly; complete/current contract register with no missed renewals/obligations; consistent positioning/collateral in use with customers/partners/government.

Why This Role: broad scope (government, customers, product, contracts, marketing) each being set up for the first time; direct access to CEO/CTO; sector exposure to Indian semiconductor policy/customers/partners; path to leadership as the role grows into a function lead.

Who We Are Looking For: "1 to 5 years of experience." Comfortable in meetings with senior government officials/partners and building financial models. Clear writer, strong with numbers, able to run work end to end with limited supervision.

You bring: strong Excel modelling and grasp of business financials (P&L, cash flow, budgets, unit economics); working knowledge of compliance/audits; clear, persuasive writing/presentation for formal audiences; daily hands-on AI tool use; ability to represent the company with senior officials/partners/customers.

Helps if you have: an MBA, ideally with a consulting background; founder or early-stage startup experience; an engineering degree or experience in semiconductors/telecom/deep tech; experience with Indian government programmes, public-sector stakeholders, or commercial contracts.

How To Apply: send CV and a short note on something you built/ran end to end and what you'd do differently. Process: a conversation with the CEO, a short work sample on a problem from the role, and a review with the founders -- takes about two weeks.
