## Full Job Description

### About Vercel
Vercel is the agentic infrastructure company, freeing people and agents to ship what's next. For more than a decade helping builders move from idea to production with speed, security, and exceptional developer experience. Trusted by OpenAI, PayPal, Ramp, Supreme, and millions of developers worldwide.

### About the Role
Seeking a Senior Deal Desk Analyst to join the Deal Desk team within the Revenue Operations function. High-impact, high-visibility role at the center of how deals are structured, priced, and closed globally -- partnering with Sales, Finance, Legal, and Product. Lead the quote-to-contract execution cycle for the global sales team while helping build systems, processes, and playbooks that scale a world-class Deal Desk. This position reports to the Senior Manager, Global Deal Desk.

### What you will do
- **Deal Management** -- Own the day-to-day deal review and closing process end to end, including deal approvals, deal structuring, and quote configuration within Vercel CPQ.
- **Commercialization & Execution** -- Drive deal execution in close partnership with Finance, Legal, GTM, Partnerships, Product, and Pricing.
- **Trusted Advisor** -- Serve as the subject-matter expert for the Sales organization, providing deal guidance and operational support.
- **Revenue Integrity** -- Act as the gatekeeper of data hygiene and opportunity data, partnering with GTM and Finance on audits for revenue recognition, financial reporting, and forecasting.
- **Insights & Enablement** -- Analyze deal performance and market trends to develop new sales motions, deal levers, and training materials.
- **Operational Excellence** -- Identify opportunities to streamline, enhance, and automate processes, leveraging AI and automation to scale.

### About you
- 4+ years of experience in deal desk, pricing, quote-to-cash, contract management, or a related Revenue Operations or Sales Operations function within the technology sector.
- Proven record of partnering effectively with Sales and cross-functional teams to move complex deals forward.
- Demonstrated experience in project management and process improvement.
- Collaborative team member; resourceful self-starter.
- Thinks naturally about operational implications and downstream impact.
- Thrives in fast-paced, high-growth environments.

### Nice to have
Hands-on experience with Salesforce CPQ (or comparable); track record of using AI/automation to improve operational workflows.

### Bonus if you
Located within San Francisco Bay Area (HQ); primary focus on Pacific Standard Time (PST).
