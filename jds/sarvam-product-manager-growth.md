## Role: Product Manager, Growth

The PM Growth seat owns Sarvam's top-of-funnel and activation engine across the full product portfolio.

### Responsibilities (verbatim extract)

- **Onboarding and signup flow.** Own the charter of everything from the first touchpoint to an activated user. Design flows and identify activation moments across products.
- **Activation metrics.** Define activation per product, identify drop-offs, run experiments to improve conversion rates.
- **Marketing and attribution.** Manage channel strategy across paid, organic, referral, and partnerships while establishing attribution tracking to measure channel ROI.
- **User quality.** Segment acquired users by source, eliminate low-quality channels, and focus on repeat user acquisition.
- **Virality and referral.** Design in-product referral mechanics and social sharing features.
- **Lifecycle management.** Deploy nudges and touchpoints (email, WhatsApp, push) to drive repeat sessions and habit formation.

### Requirements

- 5+ years in product management with 2-3 years in growth, activation, or top-of-funnel roles
- Demonstrated success improving activation metrics with specific examples
- Deep expertise in activation, engagement, and retention funnel analysis
- Hands-on experience with marketing channels and attribution measurement
- Proficiency with analytics tools (Mixpanel, PostHog, Amplitude) and SQL
- Strong intuition for product friction and time-to-value optimization
- Fluency with key metrics: CAC, activation rates, D1/D7/D30 retention, cohort analysis

### Preferred Qualifications

- Referral program or viral loop building experience
- Background with developer products, AI products, or India-focused consumer applications
- Mobile growth expertise including store optimization
