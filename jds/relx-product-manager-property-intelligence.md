## Full JD Text

About the Business

LexisNexis Risk Solutions is the essential partner in the assessment of risk. Within our Insurance vertical, we provide customers with solutions and decision tools that combine public and industry specific content with advanced technology and analytics to assist them in evaluating and predicting risk and enhancing operational efficiency. Our insurance risk solutions help drive better data-driven decisions across the insurance policy lifecycle, all while reducing risk. You can learn more about LexisNexis Risk at https://risk.lexisnexis.com/insurance.

About the Team

The Property Intelligence product team consists of passionate product professionals who build solutions that help insurers better understand property, location, and risk. We balance current customer and market needs with innovative uses of technology that can shape the future of insurance workflows and industry standards.

About the Role

You will help shape the Property Intelligence platform by connecting customer needs, product insights, and measurable outcomes. You will work across customers, sales, and internal stakeholders to strengthen product adoption and engagement while using analytics, feedback, and agentic workflows to support effective product management.

Responsibilities

- Engage regularly with internal and external customers to gain a deep understanding of their use cases, businesses, and competitive landscapes.
- Build and maintain KPI reporting and analytics used to evaluate product adoption, customer engagement, operational performance, and progress against strategic goals.
- Facilitate feedback loops to quantify and report on the impact of platform initiatives.
- Enable sales, internal stakeholders, and customers to understand the capabilities, value, and direction of the Property Intelligence platform.
- Gather a wide range of information from internal and external sources to drive commercially viable decisions for the product and organization.
- Build and maintain a suite of agentic workflows to support the wider product management team.
Requirements

- Strong understanding of agile product development and iterative delivery practices.
- Experience gathering customer feedback and translating customer requests into actionable requirements and product insights.
- Strong analytical skills and comfort working with product metrics, KPIs, and data-driven decision making.
- Experience creating or maintaining dashboards, KPI reporting, or product analytics is preferred.
- Ability to work with technical concepts including APIs, SaaS applications, system integrations, and data structures.
- Excellent written and verbal communication skills.
- Strong organizational skills and attention to detail, with the ability to manage multiple priorities and operate effectively in ambiguous environments.
- Experience working with insurance, property technology, claims, risk intelligence, AI, or agentic workflows is a plus.
