## Full JD Text
Why join us

Brex is the intelligent finance platform that enables companies to spend smarter and move faster in more than 200 markets. By combining global corporate cards and banking with intuitive spend management, bill pay, and travel software, Brex enables founders and finance teams to accelerate operations, gain real-time visibility, and control spend effortlessly. Brex’s AI-native automation and world-class service eliminate manual expense and accounting tasks for customers so they can focus on what matters most. Tens of thousands of the world's best companies run on Brex, including DoorDash, Coinbase, Robinhood, Zoom, Plaid, Reddit, and SeatGeek.

Working at Brex allows you to push your limits, challenge the status quo, and collaborate with some of the brightest minds in the industry. We’re committed to building a diverse team and inclusive culture and believe your potential should only be limited by how big you can dream. We make this a reality by empowering you with the tools, resources, and support you need to grow your career.

Marketing at Brex

The Marketing team tells the Brex story, determines messaging and positioning, and translates our products and features into tangible customer benefits. Our marketers are responsible for acquiring and growing customers and have a deep understanding of both the business and our customers. We’re a highly cross-functional team and partner most closely with the Product, Sales, and Design teams to show our prospects and customers how we can help them reach their full potential.

What you’ll do

As a Customer Marketing Manager at Brex, you’ll turn happy customers into proof our GTM and Marketing teams can sell with. You’ll report to our Manager, Customer Marketing, and work with them to to keep our reference program running smoothly, produce a steady flow of customer stories, and grow a bench of customers who are glad to speak about their success with Brex.

This is a hands-on, high-ownership role. Scaling our customer proof points requires dedicated bandwidth for case study production, event execution, and third-party reviews. You’re organized and story-driven, and you can turn a 30-minute customer conversation into something compelling, while keeping many small moving parts on track.

Where you’ll work

This role will be based in our San Francisco, CA or New York, NY office. We are a hybrid environment that combines the energy and connections of being in the office with the benefits and flexibility of working from home. We currently require three days per week in office - Monday, Wednesday and Thursday. As a perk, we also have up to four weeks per year of fully remote work!

Responsibilities

- Run the customer reference program: triage requests, match them to advocates by segment, industry, product, and use case, and close the loop with Sales

- Recruit and onboard advocates, and protect them from burnout by tracking every ask and applying tier-based frequency guidelines

- Deeply understand our customers through interviews and direct conversations to inform stories and content

- Create case studies, quotes, video scripts, and social-ready stories across startup, midsize, and enterprise segments, from sourcing to customer sign-off to publishing

- Manage cross-functional content requests from GTM, Marketing, PR, and Product, and align customer content to CY’27 priorities and launches

- Partner with Product Marketing, Sales Enablement, and Events to keep a searchable HighSpot library of proof points and to support customer speakers for webinars, panels, and conferences

- Lead our Gartner Peer Insights and G2 review strategy as an always-on program, and prioritize AEO/GEO visibility of reviews to drive AI citations and influence buyers

- Manage logo approvals and work with Legal and GTM to secure permissions for customer names, quotes, and stats

- Monitor and report on program health (references fulfilled, content produced and used, advocate engagement), using data to optimize the program

Requirements

- 5+ years in marketing, communications, content, or customer-facing work (ideally at a B2B SaaS or fintech company)

- Strong writing and interviewing skills, with the ability to turn a 30-minute conversation into a compelling story

- Exceptional organization and follow-through across many moving parts and deadlines

- Proven ability to build trusted relationships with customers and cross-functional partners across GTM, Legal, and Marketing

- Customer-obsessed with deep empathy for the people you’re marketing to

- Data-minded, with comfort in spreadsheets and a CRM

- Good judgment and discretion with confidential customer information

- Adaptable and composed in a fast-paced environment with shifting priorities

- Proficiency with AI tools for productivity and content creation

Bonus points

- Hands-on experience with a customer advocacy or reference management tool, or with Salesforce or similar CRM tooling

- Experience supporting webinars, panels, user conferences, or customer events

- Familiarity with finance, fintech, or B2B SaaS buyers

- A track record of using AI tools to draft, repurpose, and organize content more efficiently while keeping a strong editorial eye
