About the Role
We are looking for a highly driven Senior Associate - CEO's Office to work directly with the CEO on strategic initiatives, business planning, operational excellence, and special projects. This is a unique opportunity to gain broad business exposure and contribute directly to the company's growth journey.

Strategic Initiatives
Partner with the CEO to drive strategic business initiatives and high-impact cross-functional projects.
Support annual operating plans, business reviews, organizational priorities, and strategic planning.
Conduct market research, competitive intelligence, and industry analysis to identify growth opportunities.
Build business cases and evaluate new products, markets, partnerships, and investment opportunities.

CEO Office & Business Operations
Prepare executive dashboards, business reports, and KPI reviews for leadership meetings.
Track strategic initiatives, monitor execution, identify risks, and ensure timely closure of action items.
Coordinate with Business, Technology, Sales, Finance, HR, and Delivery teams to drive alignment.

Business Analytics
Analyze operational, financial, and commercial data to generate actionable insights.
Build presentations and executive-ready reports for Board meetings, investors, customers, and leadership.

Special Projects

Support mergers & acquisitions, strategic partnerships, due diligence, and organizational transformation initiatives when required.
Represent the CEO's Office in cross-functional meetings and ensure effective follow-through.
