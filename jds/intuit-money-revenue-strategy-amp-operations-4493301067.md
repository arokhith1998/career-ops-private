Overview

Accelerate growth for Intuit’s Money business. This high-impact role partners closely with senior sales leaders to drive strategy, operational excellence, and revenue performance. We’re looking for a strategic, data-driven operator with strong executive presence and a passion for enabling sales success in financial products or payments.

Responsibilities

 Partner with the VP of Money Sales to define strategic priorities and drive execution.
 Lead key revenue initiatives from design to delivery, ensuring alignment across functions.
 Build and manage the operating cadence, including planning, forecasting, and performance reviews.
 Collaborate with analytics and GTM teams to deliver actionable insights through Salesforce and other tools.
 Identify and resolve business challenges that impact growth and operational efficiency.

Qualifications

4+ years in Management Consulting, Strategy, or Revenue Operations roles with customer-facing teams.

Experience With Payments, Banking, Or Financial Products Strongly Preferred.

Strong analytical, problem-solving, and financial modeling skills.

Proven success leading cross-functional initiatives in fast-paced environments.

Excellent communicator with executive presence and influence across all levels.

Comfortable operating with ambiguity and driving clarity through structure and action.
