Job Summary:

We are looking for a Senior Product Manager to own a portion of our Payouts product portfolio, helping businesses and platforms send money to workers, sellers, creators, partners, and customers quickly, securely, and at a global scale. This is an individual contributor role. You will use data and customer insight to shape product requirements, align with a shared product vision, and partner closely with engineering, design, data science, research, risk, compliance, and finance to deliver outcomes that matter for both

users and the business.

Job Description:

Essential Responsibilities: 

- Uses data to build insights on product or platform requirements consistent with the shared vision for the product.
- Gathers insights from the customer experience and customer needs to input to product requirements
- Analyzes research, market analysis and usability studies, research and market analysis to support data-driven decision making
- Monitors product profitability measures, including budget.
- Lead sprint planning, daily standups and retrospectives to drive execution. Interfaces with product and technology leadership as needed.
- Partners with content developers, data scientists, product designers and user experience researchers to identify new opportunities.

Minimum Qualifications:

- 3+ years relevant experience and a Bachelor’s degree OR Any equivalent combination of education and experience.

Additional Responsibilities & Preferred Qualifications:

What You Will Do

- Use data to build insights on product and platform requirements that fit the shared vision for the Payouts product portfolio.
- Gather insights from the payee and payer experience, including onboarding, payout method selection, delivery speed, fees, and support needs, and turn them into clear product requirements.
- Analyze research, market analysis, and usability studies to support data-driven decisions on features such as payout rails, currencies, and disbursement controls.
- Monitor product profitability, including budget, unit economics, and cost drivers such as processing and network fees.
- Lead sprint planning, daily standups, and retrospectives to drive execution, and interface with product and technology leadership as needed.
- Partner with content developers, data scientists, product designers, and user experience researchers to identify new opportunities.
- Work with risk, compliance, legal, and finance partners so that payout products meet regulatory, fraud, and reporting expectations across markets.

What You Bring

- 5+ years of relevant experience and a bachelor's degree, or any equivalent combination of education and experience.
- You anticipate needs, follow through on commitments, and rally others to move work forward without waiting to be asked.
- You deliver value through agile, iterative methods and make smart trade-offs while holding a high bar under pressure.
- You form hypotheses, run lightweight experiments or prototypes, and use the results to discover, de-risk, and evolve product direction.
- You build trusted relationships and influence decisions through clarity, context, and collaboration.
- You break down ambiguous problems with structured thinking and data to find root causes and evaluate options.
- You understand technical systems well enough to communicate clearly with engineers and make credible trade-offs.
- You understand core AI and ML concepts and responsible practices and can apply them to AI-powered products.

Nice to Have

- Experience with payments, money movement, or disbursement products, such as bank transfers, wallets, cards, or cross-border payouts.
- Familiarity with payments APIs, developer platforms, or marketplace and platform business models.
- Working knowledge of fraud, KYC, AML, or sanctions requirements that affect payouts.
- Experience using experimentation and analytics tools to guide product decisions.
- Interest in applying AI to improve pay
