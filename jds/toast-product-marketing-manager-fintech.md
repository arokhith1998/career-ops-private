## Full JD Text

Toast creates technology to help restaurants and local businesses succeed in a digital world, helping business owners operate, increase sales, engage customers, and keep employees happy. The Toast Product Marketing team is responsible for connecting the Sales, Product, and Marketing teams to inform product development and bring new products to market through customer segmentation, value-based messaging, sales knowledge transfer, and cross-functional marketing activation. In this high-impact team, you will gain substantial senior leadership exposure and the opportunity to drive long-term customer and shareholder value at a high-growth company.

As the Product Marketing Manager for Toast Embedded Finance, you will help shape the go-to-market (GTM) strategy for a fast-growing portfolio of financial products, including Toast Capital, designed to help businesses take control of their cash flow. You will partner closely with Product, Marketing, Finance, Sales, and Customer Success teams to bring new offerings to market, support cross-product launch strategies, and build messaging that positions Toast Capital (and other fintech products/services) as a key differentiator in competitive deals. You will lead and contribute to initiatives spanning product positioning, customer research, campaign planning, and sales enablement, with a focus on driving awareness, adoption, and retention across the full lifecycle.

**A day in the life (Responsibilities):**
- Lead go-to-market strategy, launch processes, and stakeholder management, and influence product development and positioning for new embedded fintech products and features
- Advocate as the voice of our customer, and partner with Product and Customer Marketing to develop key initiatives that drive efficient product adoption
- Become a market and industry expert on key financial products, and use customer and market research to inform both product and GTM strategy
- Take ownership of conversion and adoption metrics and GTM performance, and implement cross-functional plans to drive improvements
- Collaborate with Sales, Sales Enablement, and Customer Success to drive sales productivity through product knowledge transfer, seller collateral, and value-based talk tracks

**Requirements:**
- 5+ years of experience in product marketing, ideally in B2B banking, lending and/or fintech
- Experience developing messaging, positioning, and sales enablement materials
- History of creating highly engaging and creative life-cycle engagement strategies for financial services products
- Strong ability to navigate complex and ambiguous situations across a fast-growing organization
- Demonstrated proficiency in leading cross-functional partnerships
- Excellent written and verbal communication skills
- Data-minded with strong analytical capabilities
- Fluent with AI tools in day-to-day work
- Proven ability to uncover and translate customer needs into product/GTM strategies

**Nice to Haves:** Inbound/outbound PMM experience, B2B sales enablement, thought leadership blog content, Salesforce/SQL, previous restaurant industry experience.
