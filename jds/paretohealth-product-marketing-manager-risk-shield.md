## Role summary
Executes go-to-market programs for Pareto's Risk Shield portfolio, including
messaging, sales content, customer communications, enablement, and growth and
retention programs. Reports to the Director of Product Marketing — Risk
Shield; partners closely with Sales and Customer Management.

## Key Responsibilities (paraphrased)
- Support go-to-market initiatives across the Risk Shield portfolio
- Develop and maintain sales decks, playbooks, battlecards, one-pagers, case
  studies, and customer materials
- Create customer communications for onboarding, implementation, renewal, and
  retention
- Build proof points, success stories, and value narratives
- Support sales enablement and training, including content in Seismic and
  other platforms
- Run Consultant First, National, and Member engagement programs
- Conduct competitive and market research
- Gather field feedback to improve messaging and customer experience
- Support product launches, campaigns, and GTM programs with Marketing,
  Product, and Commercial teams
- Track program performance and recommend improvements
- Use AI tools to speed up content, research, and program execution while
  maintaining quality

## Minimum Requirements
- 5-8 years of experience in product marketing, marketing, sales enablement,
  communications, product management, or a related field
- Bachelor's degree required
- Preferred: B2B sales experience, customer-facing content experience, and
  experience in healthcare, employee benefits, insurance, financial services,
  or other highly regulated industries
