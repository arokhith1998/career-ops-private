About the Role
The Chief of Staff will serve as a strategic advisor and right hand to the CEO, ensuring the CEO's office operates efficiently and effectively. This role is ideal for a highly organized, analytical, and forward-thinking individual with strong leadership, communication, and problem-solving skills. The Chief of Staff will help drive strategic initiatives, manage cross-functional projects, and facilitate decision-making across the organization.
