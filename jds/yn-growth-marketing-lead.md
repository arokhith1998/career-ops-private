## Full description

About the role
 About Y/n

 Y/n is creating a new form of entertainment: interactive stories. Y/n lets people play a character in a book, finally letting anyone meddle with the universes and characters they care about.

 Our mission is to build a superhuman A.I. storyteller and distribute it to billions of people. We're giving the technology to creators, so they can turn their most ingenious ideas into stories others can live in.

 To date, people have sent over one billion messages on Y/n. Teenagers and twenty-somethings are going to imaginary schools, falling in love with fictional people, and living out entire lives in A.I.-generated stories made for them.

 Soon, billions will play stories too, across every genre, in text, in video, alone or with their friends. By 2030, playing stories will become the dominant form of entertainment.

 Our Culture

 Our culture is simple:

- Story quality determines Y/n's success, so we hire people obsessed with making stories great.

- We are commercially minded. We want people who use their abilities to make numbers go up.

- We run Y/n like a hedge fund: a small group of people making very big bets on model training and network effects. We hire people who think rigorously and own their domain.

 We've raised $14M from top investors, and we're hiring for several roles.

 If you want to live in pursuit of superhuman storytelling, join us.

 The Role

 TL;DR: Come up with good ideas for marketing channels, test them, and own them end to end.

 This can mean running ads and creating creative assets: commissioning ads, learning what works, doubling down, and scaling spend across channels.

 Qualifications

- You've owned a meaningful paid budget across Meta, TikTok, or Google.

- You get our audience: teenagers and twenty-somethings who live on the internet and love stories and fandoms.

- You are smart as hell, hungry to win, and believe in our values.

- You can read charts well, want to measure everything, and kill what doesn't work.

- You have good takes, proactively.

- You are comfortable with open-ended work like "figure out the good marketing channels."

 Some experience as an early member of a project or company is a nice-to-have. Holistic growth experience is good too.

 Hiring Process

 We aim to make hiring simple. You'll have:

- Two interviews with the team.

- A day-long (paid) work trial.

 About Y/n
 Y/n is an app where you play a character in your story. Be a boarding school student and get the boys to fall in love with you, or plan a jailbreak with Sherlock Holmes.

 Stories are made for you. Your texts change the plot and how characters feel about you, so no two playthroughs are the same (but you can rewind).

 Can't find the one you want? Write and publish your own.
