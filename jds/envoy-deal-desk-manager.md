**About The Role**
As a Deal Desk Manager at Envoy, you'll be the go-to partner for our Sales team on structuring, pricing, and processing deals. This is a hands-on individual contributor role for someone with critical thinking skills and attention to detail, who has the ability to connect individual transactions to broader business needs and independently lead improvements from planning through rollout. You'll start in Salesforce and Salesforce CPQ, supporting quotes, approvals, and order booking. Over time you'll cross-train into provisioning and billing in Chargebee, and gain a full view of the quote-to-cash lifecycle.

This is an onsite position that requires at least 4 days a week (Monday - Thursday) in our San Francisco HQ.

**You will**
- Partner with Account Executives and Sales leadership to structure deals, including pricing, discounting, multi-year terms, ramps, and non-standard requests
- Build, review, and validate quotes and order forms in Salesforce CPQ
- Oversee the deal approval process
- Book closed-won orders and hand them off cleanly for provisioning and billing; cross-train into Chargebee
- Serve as a subject-matter expert on pricing, packaging, and deal policy
- Find gaps and friction in the quote-to-cash process, then propose, document, and implement improvements
- Help maintain and improve deal desk documentation
- Work with SalesOps, Finance, Legal, and Customer Success on cross-functional projects
- Build and manage project plans
- Evaluate and implement practical uses of AI and automation, such as checking quote completeness, identifying contract discrepancies, and drafting process documentation

**You have**
- 3+ years of experience in Deal Desk, Sales Operations, or a similar quote-to-cash role, ideally at a B2B SaaS company
- Hands-on experience with Salesforce. Salesforce CPQ experience is strongly preferred
- A solid understanding of SaaS deal structures, pricing models, and contract terms
- Strong critical thinking skills and sound judgment within your scope of authority
- Strong attention to detail
- Strong organizational and time-management skills
- Proficiency in Excel or Google Sheets
- Demonstrated project management experience
- Bonus: experience with Chargebee or another subscription billing platform
- Bonus: familiarity with Jira, Intercom, or similar tools
- Bachelor's degree, or equivalent experience

**You are**
An exceptional writer and spoken communicator; highly organized and autonomous; comfortable in a fast moving, changing organization; confident conducting your own product demos; passionate about the product and the workplace or commercial real estate industry; excited about new logo prospecting and self-sourcing deals; consultative, intellectually curious and ambitious; enthusiastic about learning and growing at Envoy.
