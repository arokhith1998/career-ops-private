## Description
Group Product Marketing Manager owning the global go-to-market story and commercial outcomes for the Reality Capture & Robotics portfolio, including DroneDeploy (robotics and visual intelligence platform: drones, ground robots, mobile phones, fixed/wearable cameras capturing construction sites and operational assets in 3D). Player-coach seat, building a team of 2 to 5 product marketers over time. Reports to the VP, Product, Reality Capture & Robotics. Product Marketing sits inside Product as a peer to Product Management and Design.

## What You'll Do
- Own positioning, messaging, and packaging for Reality Capture & Robotics across Procore's markets, differentiated against named competitors.
- Accountable for commercial outcomes for the portfolio: new ARR, adoption, win rate.
- Run a continuous view of why Procore wins and loses; bring that insight to Product, Sales, executive leadership.
- Partner with Sales Enablement to equip Sales/Solutions Engineering with messaging, competitive positioning, proof points for live deals.
- Partner with Product Management on roadmap, betas, launches; lead go-to-market for launches that change what's sold.
- Build deep customer understanding through live conversations and call reviews.
- Recruit, coach, grow a team of product marketers.
- Use AI to work faster and see more of the market, while holding a high quality bar.

## What We're Looking For
- 8+ years in product marketing or related roles at B2B software companies, including 5+ years in SaaS.
- Experience leading or mentoring product marketers, desire to build a team.
- Track record owning positioning, packaging, launches with measurable impact on win rate, pipeline, or adoption.
- Deep competitive expertise, turning market insight into roadmap and pricing decisions.
- Strong product fluency, ideally hardware-enabled, data-capture, computer vision, or AI products.
- Construction, infrastructure, drones, robotics, or con-tech experience is a plus.
- Proven ability to influence Product, Sales, executive stakeholders.
- Outstanding writing/storytelling; comfort presenting to customers and senior audiences.
- Hands-on use of AI in daily work.
- "Bachelor's degree in business, marketing, a technical field, or equivalent experience. MBA is a plus."
