## Full JD text

Amphenol Communications Solutions (ACS), a division of Amphenol Corporation, is a world leader in interconnect solutions for Communications, Mobile, RF, Optics, and Commercial electronics markets. Amphenol Corporation is one of the world's largest designers/manufacturers of electrical, electronic and fiber optic connectors/interconnect systems, antennas, sensors, and coaxial/high-speed specialty cable.

Power Solutions Group, a business unit of Amphenol, is the market leader for power electrical connectors and cables for the Telecom/Datacom Market (Storage, Servers, Networks, etc.). Seeking a motivated and experienced Product Marketing Manager to join the team in the U.S. (preferably California, Texas), reporting to the North American Regional Product Marketing Manager.

Responsibilities:
- Work with cross-functional teams marketing next-generation power products within the Datacom Market (Rack & Power, Storage, Servers, Networks) to OEM/ODMs.
- Promote products to the market, win key new design opportunities, develop/execute product strategies to grow top and bottom line.
- Proactively present/promote the Power Solutions portfolio to help global sales teams understand positioning, benefits, target customers.
- Work with global sales teams to identify requirements/market trends for new design wins; maximize share at key accounts by coordinating pricing/market strategy with sales.
- Drive the new business opportunity funnel; align sales and business unit on strategy; develop sales/marketing tools as needed.
- Prepare/support monthly and quarterly revenue projections; forecast analysis and book-to-ship estimates.
- Serve as liaison between customer requests and internal functions (Operations, Engineering, Customer service, Quality); engage with customer commodity/engineering teams daily.
- Work across internal teams (marketing, engineering, business development, operations) to identify product/solution gaps and define market requirements.
- Manage project timelines, product roadmaps, technical deliverables.
- Travel domestically/internationally up to 50% for customer visits, field engagements, trainings, factory visits.
- Attendance in meetings outside standard working hours expected (global team).

Qualifications:
- Bachelor's degree in business, finance, or a technical engineering discipline.
- Fluent in English; bilingual Mandarin a plus.
- 4+ years of product management, sales, and/or technical business development experience, preferably for an electronics product line in a related market.
- Technical knowledge of the interconnect industry and/or familiarity with datacenter architecture, AI computing, server/storage hardware.
- Exceptional organization, creativity, adaptability, project management in a rapidly changing, multi-tasking environment.
- Good communication skills, proficiency with MS Office (Word, Excel, Outlook, PowerPoint, GANTT charts), ability to integrate with global cross-functional teams, willingness to "roll up your sleeves."

Amphenol is an "Equal Opportunity Employer" - Minority/Female/Disabled/Veteran/Sexual Orientation/Gender Identity/National Origin.
