Job summary

Trust infrastructure has to work when customers need it at scale, under load, across regions, and when things don’t go according to plan.

DigiCert operates critical digital trust infrastructure used by enterprises around the world. As our platform and product portfolio grow, reliability, availability, scalability, and performance must be designed into the product - not treated simply as engineering concerns.

We are looking for a highly technical Senior Product Manager for Reliability, Availability & Scalability (RAS) to own the product strategy and requirements for these capabilities across DigiCert’s platform and products.

This is not a traditional feature PM role. You will work horizontally across Product, Engineering, Architecture, SRE, Cloud Operations, Support, and customer-facing teams to identify the reliability and scale requirements that matter most to our customers, translate them into measurable product requirements, and drive a prioritized roadmap that makes DigiCert’s products increasingly resilient, scalable, observable, and operationally excellent.

The ideal candidate combines the mindset of a product manager, systems thinker, and technical operator. You understand distributed systems well enough to engage deeply with architects and SREs, but you approach reliability through the lens of customer experience and business outcomes.

 

What you will do

1. Define the RAS Product Strategy

- Establish a clear product strategy for Reliability, Availability and Scalability across DigiCert. Define what enterprise-grade means for DigiCert products across dimensions including:- Availability and service continuity
- Performance and latency
- Scalability and capacity
- Resilience and fault tolerance
- Disaster recovery and business continuity
- Observability and service health
- Deployment and upgrade reliability
- Operational readiness
- Customer-visible service health and transparency

- Translate these dimensions into measurable product requirements and investment priorities.
2. Turn Customer Expectations into Engineering Requirements

- Understand the operational requirements of DigiCert’s largest and most demanding enterprise customers.
- Work with customers, Sales, Support, Customer Success, and Engineering to understand:- Where customers experience reliability or performance challenges
- What scale they need DigiCert to support
- What availability and recovery expectations their businesses require
- What operational visibility enterprise administrators need
- What requirements emerge during large deployments and expansions

- Convert those insights into clearly defined product requirements, SLOs, success metrics, and roadmap priorities.
3. Make Reliability Measurable

- Create a product-level framework for measuring the reliability and performance of DigiCert products.
- Partner with Engineering and SRE to establish and continuously improve metrics such as:- Availability: uptime, SLO/SLA attainment and error budgets.
- Performance: API latency, transaction latency and response-time objectives.
- Scalability: certificates, identities, agents, devices, integrations, transactions and other product-specific scale dimensions.
- Resilience: failure isolation, redundancy, recovery capabilities and graceful degradation.
- Recovery: RTO, RPO, failover and disaster-recovery readiness.
- Quality: escaped defects, regression rates and automated test coverage.
- Operations: incident frequency, severity, MTTR and recurrence.

- Ensure that RAS investments are evaluated based on measurable improvements in customer experience and platform health.
4. Drive the RAS Roadmap

- Build and manage a prioritized roadmap of RAS investments across the DigiCert portfolio.
- Work with product leaders and engineering teams to balance:- New customer-facing capabilities
- Reliability improvements
- Scalability investments
- Performance improvements
- Architectural modernization
- Operational improvements
- Technical debt
- Compliance and resilience requirements

- Develop a rigorous framework for prioritizing investments based on customer impact, business risk, growth requirements, operational cost, and engineering investment.
5. Partner Deeply with Engineering and SRE

- Operate as the product counterpart to Engineering, Architecture, SRE, and Cloud Operations. You should be comfortable discussing topics such as:- Distributed systems
- Cloud-native architectures
- Kubernetes and containers
- Multi-region architectures
- Databases and data stores
- Queues and asynchronous processing
- Caching
- APIs and high-volume transaction systems
- Horizontal and vertical scaling
- Rate limiting and throttling
- Observability and telemetry
- Failure modes and dependency management
- Capacity planning
- Disaster recovery

- You don’t need to be the person designing every technical solution. You do need enough technical depth to understand the architecture, ask difficult questions, evaluate tradeoffs, and translate technical investments into customer and business outcomes.
6. Bring the Customer into Reliability Decisions

- Develop a deep understanding of how infrastructure behavior translates into customer experience.
- Partner with Support and Customer Success to identify recurring operational pain points and use incident, escalation, telemetry, and support data to identify systemic product improvements.
- Participate in major customer escalations when product or architectural decisions are required.
- Help turn lessons from incidents into durable improvements rather than one-time fixes.
7. Raise the Bar for Operational Readiness

- Create product standards and readiness criteria that ensure new capabilities are prepared for production at enterprise scale.
- Partner with Product and Engineering teams to incorporate RAS requirements into the product lifecycle, including:- Scale and performance testing
- Capacity planning
- Failure-mode analysis
- Monitoring and alerting
- Operational runbooks
- Rollback and recovery
- Disaster recovery
- Support readiness
- Customer migration
- Production launch criteria

- Ensure reliability is considered before launch rather than after incidents occur.
 

What you will have

- Significant product management experience with technically complex enterprise software, cloud platforms, security infrastructure, developer platforms, or distributed systems.
- Strong understanding of reliability, availability, scalability, and performance concepts.
- Experience working closely with SRE, infrastructure, platform engineering, or cloud operations teams.
- Demonstrated ability to translate technical infrastructure challenges into clear product requirements and business priorities.
- Strong understanding of SaaS and cloud architectures and how modern distributed systems operate at scale.
- Experience defining and working with SLAs, SLOs, SLIs, error budgets, performance targets, and operational metrics.
- Experience using data to identify systemic product issues and prioritize investments.
- Strong customer orientation and the ability to understand how technical system behavior affects enterprise customers.
- Ability to influence multiple engineering and product organizations without directly managing them.
- Excellent communication skills and the ability to explain highly technical issues clearly to executives, customers, and non-technical stakeholders.
- Comfort operating in an environment where the right answer requires balancing customer experience, architectural quality, engineering capacity, business growth, and risk.
 

Nice to have

- Cloud infrastructure; cybersecurity; identity and access management; PKI; DNS; certificate lifecycle management; high-volume transaction platforms; distributed systems; Kubernetes; developer infrastructure; observability; SRE; multi-region SaaS platforms; or other mission-critical enterprise infrastructure. 
- Experience working on products with demanding enterprise SLAs or extremely high transaction volumes would be particularly relevant.
