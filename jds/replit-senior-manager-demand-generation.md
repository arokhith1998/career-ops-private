## Job Description (full, verbatim, via Ashby posting-api JSON descriptionHtml)
Replit is the agentic software creation platform that enables anyone to build applications using natural language. With millions of users worldwide, Replit is democratizing software development by removing traditional barriers to application creation.
Enterprise Marketing is responsible for driving B2B pipeline and revenue at Replit. The team partners closely with Sales, RevOps, Product Marketing, and Growth to engage enterprise buyers and turn demand into qualified opportunities. With millions of individual users already in the product, Replit has a unique opportunity to connect product-led adoption with enterprise growth.

About the Role
We're seeking a strategic, execution-oriented Senior Manager, Demand Generation to lead integrated campaigns that accelerate enterprise pipeline and revenue growth at Replit. You'll own the strategy, execution, and optimization of full-funnel programs that win new customers and expand adoption within existing accounts.
Reporting to the Director / Sr. Director of Demand and Digital, you'll partner with Product Marketing to translate Replit's AI capabilities, positioning, and customer use cases into compelling campaigns for technical and business audiences. You'll build account-based programs, launch targeted campaigns, and work closely with Sales to turn engagement into qualified opportunities.
You'll bring deep channel expertise and partner with channel owners to optimize spend, improve conversion, and allocate budget toward programs that deliver the strongest pipeline and revenue impact. This is a hands-on role that combines strategic planning with detailed execution.

What You'll Do
- Own campaign strategy and pipeline results. Build and execute enterprise demand generation programs with clear audiences, offers, pipeline goals, and measures of success.
- Build enterprise campaign playbooks across account-based marketing, webinars, content syndication, email nurture, and integrated field marketing.
- Develop account-based programs with Sales: select high-value accounts, identify buying groups, coordinate multi-touch campaigns with sales outreach.
- Own land-and-expand campaigns across new customer acquisition and account expansion.
- Shape campaign messaging and creative with Product Marketing, Content, and Design.
- Test and improve performance: experiment with audiences, messaging, creative, CTAs, landing pages.
- Connect product-led growth with enterprise sales; nurture high-potential users/accounts toward enterprise adoption.
- Strengthen lead management and sales handoff with Sales, SDRs, RevOps, Product, and Analytics.
- Measure and communicate business impact: campaign contribution, funnel conversion, program efficiency.
- Deliver high-quality execution: manage campaign calendars, budgets, cross-functional dependencies.

What You'll Bring
- A strong desire to learn and apply AI tools to scale campaign execution, automate repetitive work, and improve marketing performance.
- 10+ years of demand generation experience, including 5+ years in B2B SaaS focused on enterprise customers.
- A proven track record of building and scaling demand generation programs with measurable pipeline contribution and ROI.
- Expertise with account-based marketing.
- Experience developing lead scoring, qualification, and funnel optimization approaches with Sales and RevOps.
- Strong analytical skills and disciplined experimentation.
- Strong cross-functional communication and project management skills.
This is a full-time role based in the Foster City, CA office, with an in-office requirement of Monday, Wednesday, and Friday.
