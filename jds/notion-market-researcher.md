We're building one place where your knowledge, projects, meetings, and AI tools live side by side, so work is faster, clearer, and less fragmented. Notinos (our employees) are customer zero in bringing this future of work to life. We care about craft, building things that last, and the belief that great work is still fundamentally human. Our goal isn’t to ship the next feature. Each and every team of Notinos is working to set the standard for how humans work together in the AI era. From building a business’s system of record to making and managing AI agents to automating away the busy work, we care deeply about giving our customers more time for their life’s work.

About The RoleTurn market signals into GTM strategy for Marketing. Notion is a collaborative AI workspace for teams and companies, operating in a market where AI is changing how teams evaluate software and get work done. This role will help Marketing understand how customers, competitors, and the broader market are responding, including shifts in brand perception, then turn that understanding into sharper messaging, growth strategy, and audience strategy. You’ll own the market research relationship with GTM partners across global markets and regional teams, and expand Notion’s thought-leadership research capability through original work on topics like AI transformation and ROI.

This role is based in New York City or San Francisco. We work from our offices on Mondays, Tuesdays and Thursdays (our Anchor Days) because we do our best thinking and building together in person. We’re looking for someone who’s excited to work alongside the team during those days.

What You’ll Achieve- Build a trusted market research practice and become a standing strategic partner for GTM planning, brought into launch planning early enough to shape the work.

- Own brand tracking as a clear input into how Notion understands awareness, consideration, and perception, and translate those signals into sharper messaging and GTM priorities.

- Connect customer, competitive, product, and top-of-funnel research across global markets to inform growth strategy and audience strategy.

- Raise the bar for Notion’s research capabilities across decision support and thought leadership, building original studies on topics like AI transformation and ROI that give Marketing and Sales a credible point of view in the AI landscape.

- Use AI as a research collaborator and workflow layer to connect data, scale analysis, surface patterns, and make insights easier for stakeholders to use, while improving the quality of decisions, not just the speed of delivery.

 
Skills You’ll Bring- Strong quantitative research craft, including the ability to design and run research sprints, surveys, and MaxDiff studies, and to choose the right method for the decision at hand.

- Deep understanding of brand tracking and top-of-funnel research, with the judgment to place those signals in the broader customer, competitive, and product context.

- Strategic judgment: you can challenge the brief, connect market and product data, and turn ambiguous evidence into a clear recommendation that changes GTM priorities.

- Experience working across global markets and regional teams, flexing across Product, Creative, and GTM while distinguishing local context from patterns that should shape broader strategy.

- AI fluency and systems thinking: you use AI and data workflows thoughtfully to connect research infrastructure, scale insight delivery, and make the broader team more effective
