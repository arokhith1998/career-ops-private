You'll help support Public Sector overall, with a clear focus on Healthcare and SLED, and you'll collaborate across adjacent verticals where motions overlap (including Life Sciences events). We are looking for a high-energy, revenue-minded marketing strategist who can alternate between strategic pipeline and account planning with vertical sales and flawless execution of industry experiences for customer executives.
