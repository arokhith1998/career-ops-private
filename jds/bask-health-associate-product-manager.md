About Bask Health

Bask Health provides a full-service SaaS platform that enables doctors, entrepreneurs, and healthcare businesses to build, manage, and grow direct-to-consumer telehealth businesses.

We're an entrepreneurial, product-focused team that values ownership, speed, and exceptional execution. We work in small teams, tackle complex problems, and expect everyone to take an active role in building and improving our products.

About the Role

We're looking for an Associate Product Manager to help build, launch, and improve products across Bask's telehealth platform. You'll work closely with product managers, engineers, designers, and customer-facing teams to understand user problems, define product requirements, and deliver improvements. This is an early-career technical product management role for someone who has experience building software, contributing to product development, or taking ownership of technical projects. You'll support larger product initiatives while independently managing smaller features and improvements from discovery through launch.

What You'll Do

Own smaller product features and improvements from initial discovery through development, launch, and iteration. Support product managers in defining and executing product roadmaps. Work directly with merchants, patients, internal teams, and other stakeholders to understand user needs. Translate user feedback and business requirements into clear product specifications, user stories, and acceptance criteria. Partner with engineers and designers to evaluate solutions and understand technical tradeoffs. Investigate product issues, reproduce bugs, and collaborate with engineers on solutions. Support product discovery, user research, competitive analysis, and feature prioritization. Help define success metrics and evaluate feature performance using product analytics and customer feedback.

Requirements

Hands-on experience contributing to software products through internships, professional work, personal projects, or other practical experience. Foundational understanding of software development, web applications, and modern technology products. Hands-on coding experience with JavaScript, TypeScript, Python, or another programming language. Ability to read code, understand basic technical concepts, and communicate effectively with engineers. Experience taking ownership of a project, feature, or technical initiative from planning through completion. Strong analytical thinking and problem-solving skills. Familiarity with APIs, integrations, or modern web application development. Excellent written and verbal communication skills.

Nice to Have: previous experience in an associate product manager, product management intern, software engineering, or related technical role. Familiarity with JavaScript/TypeScript frameworks such as React or Next.js. Familiarity with product management tools such as ClickUp, Jira, Linear. Experience with user research, usability testing, product analytics, or experimentation. Interest in SaaS, e-commerce, consumer-facing applications, or telehealth/digital health.
