## Job Description (extracted, verbatim from LinkedIn posting)

Position Summary...

What you'll do...

As a Staff Product Manager at Walmart, you will lead strategy and execution for complex products, driving end-to-end outcomes from discovery through value realization. This role requires deep customer and technical insight, strong stakeholder leadership, and the ability to define multi-quarter product vision and roadmaps.

You will collaborate cross-functionally to prioritize initiatives, leverage data-driven decision making, and incorporate emerging technologies. Acting as a recognized authority, you will influence product direction, mentor peers, and ensure alignment with broader business objectives to deliver impactful solutions that enhance customer and operational success.

About the team:

The team leads strategy and execution for Offsite Ads, Partnerships, and CTV products, delivering impactful outcomes through data-driven decision making and stakeholder collaboration. By integrating customer insights with emerging technology expertise, the team defines product vision and roadmaps. Working cross-functionally, it develops innovative solutions that enhance business value and customer experience. Emphasizing rigorous discovery, prioritization, and performance measurement, team members mentor peers to advance product management practices. Focused on operational excellence and strategic growth, the team plays a key role in expanding Walmart’s product portfolio and market presence.

What you'll do:

- Shape and drive multi-quarter product strategy across multiple related products, leveraging deep domain expertise and data-driven insights.
- Own complex customer problems and product requirements, ensuring alignment with business goals and technical feasibility.
- Develop and manage comprehensive product roadmaps, thru MVP creation, launch planning, and long-term evolution.
- Build deep understanding of customer and business needs using data analysis, journey mapping, and feedback synthesis.
- Collaborate cross-functionally with Engineering, Design, Analytics, and Business partners to deliver prioritized features, manage risks and influence technical direction.
- Use data-driven decision making and experimentation to validate assumptions.
- Promote best practices in product management and mentor peers to elevate organizational capability

What you'll bring:

- Proven experience leading product strategy and managing roadmaps for complex product suits in ad platform, social or media space.
- Strong stakeholder management skills with the ability to influence cross-functional teams without direct authority.
- Expertise in data-driven decision making, including advanced analytics and experimentation to guide product direction.
- Deep understanding of ad tech technologies and their applications within product development.
- Ability to translate customer and market insights into prioritized, measurable problem statements and product outcomes.
- Ability to lead product lifecycle from MVP to scalable launch and ongoing optimization.
- Excellent communication skills to align product vision with broader business strategies.
