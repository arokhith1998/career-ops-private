## Job Description (extracted, verbatim from posting)

Lyft is building a centralized Pricing, Yield & Compensation function to bring rigor and visibility to how it prices inventory, manages programmatic performance, and compensates Ads and Lyft Business sellers. High-visibility, cross-functional role sitting within the MBA StratOps function, touching Finance, Analytics, Sales, and Accounting daily. The Manager will own three interconnected functions: setting/managing CPM floors, rate cards, and margin targets for direct-sold advertising inventory; overseeing performance/economics of the Audience Extension managed service; and partnering with Analytics/Accounting on commissions and compensation for Lyft Ads/Lyft Business sellers.

**Responsibilities:**
- Direct Inventory Pricing & Yield Management: own the Lyft Ads rate card strategy, enforce pricing discipline through Boostr (OMS), set/manage CPM floors, evaluate custom deal structures against margin thresholds
- Managed Service Performance Oversight: internal owner of vendor-led Audience Extension managed service, hold vendor accountable to CPM targets/margin commitments, conduct performance reviews
- Sales Compensation & Commission Administration: design and administer commission/incentive plans for Lyft Ads and Lyft Business sellers, own end-to-end compensation calculation process
- AdTech Systems Coordination: cross-functional connector on system-level pricing/revenue recognition questions

**Experience:**
- "4-7 years of experience in yield management, pricing strategy, revenue operations, media operations, or sales compensation."
- Strong financial modeling skills; fluency in Excel/Google Sheets
- Experience owning or contributing to sales compensation design/commission calculation at scale
- Familiarity with programmatic advertising economics (CPMs, floor pricing, managed service fee structures)

Preferred: DSP managed service vendor experience (MiQ, DV360, Xaxis); OMS/CRM platform familiarity (Operative, Salesforce); buy-side and sell-side media ops background, two-sided marketplace/rideshare/mobility advertising experience.

Benefits: medical/dental/vision, mental health, family building, child care/pet benefits, 401(k) match, 12 observed holidays + discretionary PTO (salaried) / 15 days PTO (hourly), 18 weeks paid parental leave, subsidized commuter benefits, monthly Lyft credits, Lyft Pink membership.
