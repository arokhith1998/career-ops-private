## Job Description (extracted; detailed paraphrase - source blocked verbatim reproduction past ~125 characters per quote)

**Company overview:** Impiricus is an AI-driven healthcare provider engagement platform, named to Deloitte's 2025 Fast 500 (fastest-growing company in North America). Founded by a practicing physician and pharma industry executive. Platform connects healthcare providers with pharmaceutical resources, advised by a council of 2,000+ healthcare professionals.

**Deal Support Functions:** Manages day-to-day pricing support for individual transactions, including investigation into previous deal terms. Owns quotation approaches and customer requests; communicates with sales, deal desk, and senior management. Supports non-standard contract provisions and evaluates strategic/high-value transactions under the Pricing Lead.

**Dynamic and Inventory-Based Pricing:** Examines supply-and-demand dynamics across therapeutic categories and product launch phases to set/modify pricing. Builds and maintains inventory-driven dynamic pricing models.

**DealHub and Pricing Systems Leadership:** Primary expert on DealHub (CPQ platform); controls system management, tracks adoption across sales, converts new business-model requirements into DealHub setup, trains sales personnel, diagnoses/fixes technical issues.

**Cross-Product Pricing Analysis and Reporting:** Executes pricing evaluations supporting the Pricing Lead and Pricing Strategy Manager; builds/maintains dashboards on price reductions, revenue realization, profitability by segment, approval timeframes. Runs recurring evaluations across the Ascend, Pulse, and Spark product lines.

**Pricing Operations and Data Quality:** Maintains CRM data cleanliness for pricing/deal information; collaborates with finance on reporting.

**Pricing Governance Assistance:** Helps ensure pricing compliance across quotes, service agreements, and legal contracts; supports approval sequences and discount limitations.

**Requirements:** Bachelor's degree in quantitative disciplines, business administration, or comparable field (graduate degree a plus). 5+ years hands-on pricing/revenue optimization experience, ideally in enterprise transaction pricing/quotation in a deal desk or front-line pricing setting (or strong analytical background with sales-collaboration track record). Demonstrated management of a CPQ system or pricing tech at a multi-million-revenue business. Strong quantitative competency, supply-and-demand or stock-based dynamic pricing modeling experience. Advanced spreadsheet skills, CRM data stewardship experience. Strong written/oral communication.

**Benefits:** Medical/dental/vision, HSA/FSA/dependent care FSA with employer HSA contributions, 401(k) with match, short/long-term disability, life/AD&D, flexible PTO, paid family leave after 6 months, remote-friendly with home office equipment.
