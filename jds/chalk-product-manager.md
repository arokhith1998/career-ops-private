About Chalk
Chalk (chalk.ai) is the platform for production AI. Trusted by enterprises including Whatnot, Grindr, Mission Lane, Turo, and Apartment List, Chalk brings together context, runtime, and learning for agents and models. Chalk’s Context Engine delivers live context by querying data across warehouses, databases, streams, and APIs, straight from the source. Chalk’s runtime powers agents and models with infrastructure and governed access to LLMs and tools. Together, these capabilities help teams build AI that understands their business, continuously improves, and runs reliably in production. We've recently raised a $50 million Series A, led by Felicis. Learn more at chalk.ai.

About the role
We are hiring a Product Manager in San Francisco to join Chalk as an early employee and make a large impact. You will work with a high degree of autonomy and ownership, working closely with our VP of Product, co-founders, and engineering team to ship product in a fast-moving environment.
We are looking for someone who is scrappy, low ego, and very technical — someone who will dive into details and drive outcomes. The role includes both product management and project management responsibilities.
We’re in the office 5 days a week. When unavoidable conflicts come up, we’re flexible. This is not a hybrid role.

What you will do
Work directly with Chalk’s VP of Product and co-founders to define and execute product prioritiesDevelop a clear understanding of user needs across data and AI/ML teams; translate customer feedback into product requirementsDrive end-to-end delivery for product initiatives, including scoping, milestones, project management, and release coordinationPartner deeply with engineering on technically complex areas (developer experience, performance, reliability, integrations)Write product specifications and make tradeoffs aligned with business outcomes and customer impactDefine success metrics, evaluate results using data, and iterate quicklySupport strategic initiatives such as pricing analysis, packaging, and feature scoping as the company scales

What we’re looking for
5+ years of product management experience on technical products (developer-facing tools, platforms, infrastructure, data systems)Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a quantitative disciplineStrong technical acumen and the ability to engage in-depth with engineering teams on architecture, tradeoffs, and system constraintsDemonstrated ability to ship complex products in a fast-paced environment with ambiguityWillingness to take on both product management and project management responsibilities to ensure deliveryLow ego, high ownership, and a bias toward executionStrong written and verbal communication skills; able to articulate product strategy to technical and non-technical audiences

Bonus points
Ability to write Python for analysis, prototypes, or debuggingExperience with data science, analytics, experimentation, or performance/benchmark-driven productsFamiliarity with distributed systems, query engines, compilers, or data infrastructureExperience building for enterprise customers and balancing platform integrity with customer needs
