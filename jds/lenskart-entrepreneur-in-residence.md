## Description (full text)

About the Role: Lenskart is looking for an Entrepreneur in Residence (EIR) - someone who
has built something from scratch, felt the weight of ownership, and knows what it takes to
go from zero to one. This isn't a traditional corporate hire. We want ex-founders who bring
first-principles thinking, bias for action, and comfort with ambiguity into a high-growth,
execution-heavy environment. The EIR will work directly with leadership on 0-to-1
initiatives, new business lines, or high-priority strategic projects - with the mandate and
autonomy of a founder, backed by Lenskart's scale, data, and distribution.

What You'll Do:
- Own a defined charter (new initiative, business line, or strategic project) end-to-end,
  from problem definition to execution to scale-up
- Operate with founder-like ownership: make decisions fast, build lean, iterate based on
  real feedback rather than process
- Work cross-functionally with category, ops, tech, and leadership teams to unblock
  execution
- Build and pitch business cases, structure ambiguous problems into workable plans, and
  drive them to measurable outcomes
- Act as a thinking partner to leadership on emerging opportunities, market shifts, and
  internal innovation bets
- Potentially build and lead a small team as the initiative matures

What Makes This Role Different:
- Direct access to leadership and real business mandates, not a rotational or shadow
  program
- Autonomy to define how a problem gets solved, not just execute someone else's plan
- A path to owning a P&L or a full business unit based on performance
- Exposure to Lenskart's scale (retail, tech, supply chain, consumer data)

Who We're Looking For:
- Minimum 2 years as a founder or co-founder of a venture (startup experience a plus, not
  mandatory)
- 5-15 years total experience, combining founder time with operating/consulting/
  high-growth company experience
- D2C background strongly preferred (retail, fashion, F&B, beauty, consumer tech, or
  similar) with firsthand ownership of customer acquisition, unit economics, or category
  growth
- Track record of building something from the ground up (raising capital, acquiring
  customers, hiring a team, navigating a shutdown/pivot all count)
- Strong first-principles problem solving; comfortable operating without a playbook
- High ownership mindset
- Excellent stakeholder management
- Willingness to be based out of Delhi NCR or Bangalore
