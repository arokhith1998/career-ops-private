## Full JD text (verbatim, via headless browser past the LinkedIn sign-in wall)

About The Opportunity: Digital Brew Marketing (DBM) is a small, founder-led performance and growth marketing agency with 15 years of experience across Google, Samsung, and Amazon. The agency serves global brands in e-commerce, autonomous vehicles, AI, luxury real estate, and B2B sectors. As a founding team member, you will shape how DBM operates and share in its future success. This is not an account manager role; you are building a growth engine from the ground up.

Role & Responsibilities:
- Own client results: full accountability for performance across all DBM accounts including strategy, channel plans, reporting, and renewals. Be the primary point of contact for clients.
- Drive DBM growth: build the agency's pipeline through outbound efforts, referrals, partnerships, content, and case studies.
- Build scalable systems: turn successful processes into SOPs, templates, dashboards, and playbooks.
- Lead team development: hire, train, and manage junior staff and contractors.
- Manage other ventures: select 1-2 of the founder's other projects with the most upside and own them end-to-end.
- Handle commercials: scope work, price projects, protect margins, and make strategic decisions on client retention or termination.

Skills & Qualifications - Must-Have:
- Minimum 5 to 7 years in marketing, with at least 3 years at an agency working directly with US clients.
- Proven ownership of key metrics such as pipeline, revenue, CAC, or ROAS with demonstrable before-and-after results.
- Strong expertise in at least two of: paid media, SEO and content, lifecycle and email marketing, or B2B demand generation.
- Comfortable working with numbers: able to interpret P&L statements, GA4 reports, and ad account data independently.
- Sales experience: have closed clients or led pitches.
- Daily AI user.

Benefits & Culture Highlights: Direct access to a founder with experience at Google, Samsung, and Amazon; real ownership with a visible P&L; variable pay directly tied to the growth you build; founding member status with ownership upside in DBM and new ventures (structure to be defined at offer stage).
