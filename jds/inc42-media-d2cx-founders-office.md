## Full JD Text

The Founder's Office - Inc42 Courses owns the growth engine behind Inc42's executive education vertical, D2CX, D2CX Foundations, RetailX and D2CX.AI.

This role sits inside the Founder's Office and works across two mandates. First, new initiatives: identifying what Inc42 Courses should build next, new programs, formats, cohorts, pricing constructs, and revenue lines, and taking them from idea to live product. Second, operating efficiency: acting as the connective tissue between content, editorial, design, marketing, growth, product, and operations so that every cohort runs tighter, faster, and more profitably than the last.

Inc42 Courses has already worked with 400+ D2C founders across eight cohorts. The next phase is about scale: more programs, more learners, better unit economics, and a repeatable operating system that doesn't depend on heroics.

This is a high-ownership role for someone who can think like a product owner, execute like a program manager, and read a funnel like a growth marketer.

Your Impact in This Role:
- Build What's Next: You'll research, design, and launch new programs, formats, and revenue lines across the Inc42 Courses portfolio, from curriculum architecture to pricing to go-to-market.
- Own The Operating System: You'll design the end-to-end operating model for course delivery, admissions, onboarding, session flow, mentor management, community, and completion, and make it repeatable across programs.
- Drive Cross-Functional Efficiency: You'll align content, design, marketing, growth, product, and operations around a single cohort calendar, removing dependencies and duplicated effort.
- Make The Numbers Visible: You'll build the dashboards and reporting cadence that give founders real-time visibility into enrolments, funnel health, revenue, engagement, and learner outcomes.
- Raise The Bar On Experience: You'll own learner experience end-to-end, ensuring every cohort reflects the standard associated with the Inc42 brand.

Inc42 is India's #1 startup media & intelligence platform that informs, educates and empowers startup & business leaders through its incisive reporting, insightful reports, curated Events, Conferences & communities, and industry-leading executive courses & programs.

Since launching in January 2015 and having risen to become the authoritative voice on India's startup economy, we've moved markets, gotten the early scoop on billions of dollars of transactions, and told you what's happening deep inside some of the fastest-growing startups & industries alike. Our stories have been followed by the Wall Street Journal, Bloomberg, Reuters, TechCrunch, and other major outlets hundreds of times. We also track over 10,000+ startups and provide market research and intelligence through our in-depth research reports, and we organise some of the most sought-after conferences and Events for startup & business leaders across India.

The Inc42 Courses Portfolio

- D2CX.AI: Focused paid program on AI-led brand & content building, for D2C operators building an AI-native marketing stack

Primary Responsibilities

1. New Initiatives & Program Development
- Identify, evaluate, and build new initiatives across the Inc42 Courses vertical, new programs, cohort formats, certifications, workshops, sprints, and community layers.
- Run market, competitor, and demand research to validate what founders will pay for, and translate findings into concept notes, business cases, and launch plans.
- Own curriculum architecture in partnership with editorial and faculty, module structure, session outcomes, assignments, tooling, and assessment.
- Define pricing, cohort sizing, seat economics, and revenue targets for each program; test and iterate on offers.
- Take new programs from concept to live, landing page, curriculum, faculty, funnel, launch campaign, and first cohort, with clear ownership at every step.

2. Program Operations & Cohort Delivery
- Own the end-to-end cohort lifecycle across D2CX, D2CX Foundations, and D2CX.AI, from admissions and onboarding to sessions, assignments, community, and convocation.
- Build and run the cohort calendar: session scheduling, speaker confirmations, content readiness, platform setup, and communication timelines.
- Manage the live + self-paced delivery mix, ensuring recordings, resources, playbooks, and templates reach fellows on time and in the right format.
- Track attendance, engagement, drop-offs, and completion; intervene early on at-risk fellows.
- Troubleshoot in real time during live sessions and cohort weeks, keeping delivery quality uncompromised.

3. Cross-Functional Efficiency & Systems
- Act as the single point of alignment across content, design, marketing, growth, product/tech, partnerships, and operations for everything Courses.
- Map current workflows, identify duplicated effort and bottlenecks, and rebuild them into leaner, documented processes with clear owners and turnaround times.
- Run a weekly operating rhythm, review calls, blockers, dependencies, and next actions, with complete visibility for the Founder's Office.
- Drive automation and tooling decisions across LMS, CRM, payments, community platforms, and internal trackers to reduce manual load per cohort.
- Ensure every recurring activity has an SOP, a template, and a named owner, so cohort N+1 costs less effort than cohort N.

4. Funnel, Revenue & Performance Reporting
- Own the admissions funnel end-to-end, applications, screening, conversion, payments, and onboarding, in partnership with marketing and growth.
- Work with marketing on acquisition campaigns, landing pages, content, referral loops, and alumni-led demand for each program.
- Track and report on enrolment, revenue, CAC, conversion rates, refunds, completion, NPS, and alumni outcomes.
- Build weekly and monthly dashboards and MIS for founders, with clear reads on what's working, what's at risk, and what needs a decision.
- Manage program P&L inputs, faculty costs, platform costs, production, marketing spend, and drive cost efficiency without diluting experience.

5. Mentor & Alumni Ecosystem
- Identify, onboard, and manage faculty, mentors, and session speakers from India's top operator and investor pool.
- Own speaker briefs, session outlines, moderation documents, and pre-session prep to ensure every session delivers a sharp, tactical takeaway.
- Build and activate the alumni network across cohorts, community programming, referrals, testimonials, and case studies.
- Manage partner and sponsor integrations within programs, ensuring deliverables are met without compromising learner experience.

6. Documentation, Playbooks & Quality Control
- Document program playbooks, SOPs, checklists, and templates for every recurring workflow across the vertical.
- Run structured post-cohort reviews, capturing feedback, learnings, content gaps, and operational misses, and convert them into changes for the next cohort.
- Act as the quality check on outward-facing course material: curriculum decks, landing pages, emailers, session collateral, and fellow communication.
- Maintain a single source of truth for cohort data, content libraries, and program documentation.
