At Palo Alto Networks®, we’re united by a shared mission—to protect our digital way of life. If you’re ready to do the most meaningful work of your career alongside people who are just as passionate as you are, you’re in the right place.

Who We Are

In order to be the cybersecurity partner of choice, we must trailblaze the path and shape the future of our industry. This is something our employees work at each day and is defined by our values: Disruption, Collaboration, Execution, Integrity, and Inclusion. We weave AI into the fabric of everything we do and use it to augment the impact every individual can have. If you are passionate about solving real-world problems and ideating beside the best and the brightest, we invite you to join us!

We believe collaboration thrives in person. That’s why most of our teams work from the office full time, with flexibility when it’s needed. This model supports real-time problem-solving, stronger relationships, and the kind of precision that drives great outcomes.

Job Summary

As the Senior Product Manager for SaaS Agent Security, you will lead our mission to enable safe and secure adoption of AI capabilities offered by top SaaS vendors like Microsoft, Salesforce, ServiceNow, Google, etc. You will tackle complex cybersecurity challenges, leveraging AI to deliver a simple, intuitive user experience. This role is central to shaping the future of SaaS Security by protecting the world's most critical digital assets on a market-leading, rapidly growing platform.

Key Responsibilities

- Drive feature development through the entire product lifecycle, from customer feedback collection and feature prioritization to writing product requirements and ensuring engineering delivery.
- Proactively master new AI capabilities launched by SaaS vendors and their intersection with cybersecurity.
- Collaborate and exchange information with cross-functional teams globally to deliver enterprise-grade, high-reliability features.
- Work closely with product enablement, marketing, and sales teams to evangelize products and conduct demos for partners and customers.
- Solve complex problems requiring cross-company alignment to deliver foundational functionality.
- Define and analyze metrics that measure product success, simplifying complex data to inform future decisions.
- Challenge the status quo by questioning existing processes to identify opportunities for innovation in product strategy and execution.
- Ensure alignment across teams by clearly communicating product roadmaps, dependencies, and trade-offs to all stakeholders.

Qualifications

Required Qualifications

- 5+ years of product management experience in a cross-functional, enterprise environment.
- BSc/MS in Computer Science, another engineering/technical degree, or equivalent military experience.
- Demonstrated experience in building enterprise-scale, high-performing, globally available SaaS products.

Preferred Qualifications

- Excellent analytical, written, and verbal communication skills with a proven ability to influence stakeholders.
- Self-motivated with effective skills in managing multiple tasks, projects, priorities, and deadlines.
- Strong team player with a 'can-do' approach, excellent people skills, and a positive mentality.
- Proven ability to manage product planning with multiple stakeholders within large, matrixed organizations.
