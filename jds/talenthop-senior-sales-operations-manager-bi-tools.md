About Our Client:

The organization operates within the sales enablement and operations sector, focusing on transforming commercial strategies into actionable field execution. The Americas Business & Sales Operations group acts as a center of excellence for cross-business planning and sales operations transformation, developing programs and systems that enable thousands of sales professionals across the Americas to perform at scale and with precision.

About the Opportunity:

The SENIOR SALES OPERATIONS MANAGER, BI & TOOLS is responsible for understanding the needs of sales leaders and teams in the Americas, collaborating with engineering teams to deliver scalable reporting and AI-enabled solutions. This role drives improvements in data confidence and accelerates decision-making processes by translating complex business needs into actionable insights, playing a critical role at the intersection of sales, strategy, data, engineering, and operations.

Responsibilities:
- Gather and document complex reporting requirements from stakeholders across sales, finance, engineering, and operations.
- Translate business questions into clear specifications for data models and dashboards.
- Identify and implement AI, automation, and advanced analytics solutions to enhance sales operations workflows.
- Manage intake, prioritization, and resolution of business intelligence issues and reporting enhancements.
- Establish service level agreements and communication rhythms to ensure timely delivery of solutions.
- Oversee reporting and tooling initiatives, driving adoption and ensuring actionable insights for sales teams.

Requirements:
- Bachelor's Degree in Business Administration, Marketing, Finance, Sales, Accounting, Information Systems, Social Sciences, or related field with 5+ years relevant experience, or equivalent.
- Minimum 2 years experience with business intelligence software or tools.
