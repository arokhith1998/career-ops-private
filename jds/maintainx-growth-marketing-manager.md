## Full JD Text

MaintainX is a leading mobile-first work execution platform for industrial and frontline teams, used by 13,000+ customers including Duracell, McDonald's, Shell, DHL and Volvo. In August 2026 MaintainX became part of Autodesk, joining Autodesk Operations Solutions.

Role: sits on the Growth Marketing team, which runs the experimentation engine for customer acquisition. Tests new channels, manages the test budget, and scales what works. Core paid search is owned by another team, so this role covers everything beyond it.

Responsibilities:
- Design and run structured tests across paid social, streaming audio, direct mail, programmatic, and other emerging channels, aiming for CAC at or below target this year.
- Manage a growing test budget, shift spend toward top performers, and take full ownership of 1-2 channels within 6-12 months.
- Analyze quantitative and qualitative data to scale, pivot, or stop experiments, and present recommendations to Marketing and Sales leaders.
- Use AI tools for creative testing, automation, and optimization while keeping human judgment on key decisions.
- Build testing frameworks and documentation to speed up launches.
- Partner with Creative, Sales, and Product Marketing.

Success at 12 months: owning 1-2 channels end-to-end, documenting new channel performance, and scaling at least one channel beyond its initial test budget.

Must-have qualifications:
- Hands-on experience running performance campaigns and budgets on paid social, programmatic, content syndication, or similar demand channels.
- Experience co-managing $250K-$500K+ in annual performance marketing spend.
- A track record of structuring clean tests, measuring impact, and making clear recommendations.
- Time in B2B growth or performance marketing, ideally SaaS.
- Working fluency in analytics tools and a CRM (Salesforce, HubSpot, Google Analytics, Tableau).
- Practical experience using AI tools in campaign or creative workflows.

Nice-to-haves: experience with offline channels such as direct mail or streaming audio; paid search experience (noted as not part of the role).

Compensation and benefits: compensation may include commission, annual bonus, and equity depending on role; benefits differ by country; US roles follow Autodesk's benefits (benefits.autodesk.com).
