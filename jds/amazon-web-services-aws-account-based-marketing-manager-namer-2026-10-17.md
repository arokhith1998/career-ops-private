## Job Description (extracted, verbatim from posting)

Amazon Web Services is seeking a results-driven Account-Based Marketing (ABM) Manager to design and execute high-impact ABM programs for enterprise customers. This role combines strategic thinking with hands-on execution to drive measurable business outcomes through targeted marketing initiatives. The Account-Based Marketing Manager will own the ABM strategy and end-to-end execution of territory ABM programs, working backwards from customer needs to create data-driven, multi-channel campaigns that accelerate pipeline and revenue growth.

**Key job responsibilities:** Develop account-based marketing strategies for priority enterprise customers; serve as a strategic marketing partner to Sales; build integrated customer engagement initiatives connecting account insights, messaging, content, executive engagement, digital marketing, events; own programs from strategy through execution; develop account-specific content and messaging; use data and customer intelligence to inform strategy; partner across AWS marketing organizations (Global CoE, Industry, Partner, Digital, Events); measure business impact; build for scale.

**A day in the life:** The role sits within the NAMER Strategic Customer and Partner Marketing organization, accelerating adoption, growth and revenue across AWS's largest and fastest-growing enterprise customers.

**Basic Qualifications:**
- 5+ years of professional non-internship marketing experience
- 5+ years of B2B marketing experience, including experience developing and executing integrated marketing strategies for enterprise customers or complex B2B audiences
- Demonstrated ability to translate business objectives into marketing strategies and programs
- Experience building and executing cross-functional marketing initiatives from strategy/concept through execution, measurement and optimization
- Strong track record partnering with Sales and cross-functional stakeholders
- Experience using data and analytics to inform marketing strategy
- Strong written and verbal communication skills

**Preferred Qualifications:** Experience marketing technology, cloud, SaaS or other complex B2B solutions; account-based marketing experience; MBA.
