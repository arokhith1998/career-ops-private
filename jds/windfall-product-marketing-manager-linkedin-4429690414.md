## Role Summary
Helps define how Windfall positions its people-intelligence and AI capabilities, and how
revenue teams tell that story. Reports to VP of Marketing; works with Sales, Customer
Success, Product.

## Responsibilities
- Maintain core messaging/positioning, aligned with customer needs and competitors.
- Build/update sales collateral (decks, one-pagers, battlecards, vertical sheets,
  objection-handling guides).
- Work with Sales/CS to find where deals stall; create assets to unblock them.
- Support product launches with messaging, launch briefs, internal comms, basic
  measurement.
- Contribute POV, messaging, story arcs for content/campaigns (webinars, whitepapers,
  events).
- Light competitive research/market scanning; turn findings into guidance for revenue
  teams.
- Use AI tools to speed up drafting, research, asset iteration while keeping quality and
  narrative control.
