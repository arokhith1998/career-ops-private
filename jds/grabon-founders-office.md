What You'll Do: own an initiative end-to-end; validate ideas quickly with real users/merchants/creators/customers; build/launch MVPs rather than relying on presentations; use AI tools/automation/agents to improve processes; work closely with Product/Engineering/Marketing/Growth/Business teams; define success metrics and review regularly; take independent decisions; identify what's working/not and decide to scale/change/discontinue; drive projects across stakeholders even without direct reporting lines; eventually take ownership of a product/business line/strategic capability.

The Three Tracks:

Potential growth: ownership of a product line with P&L responsibility.
- Track 2 -- AI, Automation & Operations: identify repetitive manual processes, redesign with AI/agents/automation; build AI-powered workflows/agent-based systems; reduce manual effort while improving speed/accuracy; coordinate execution across teams. - Track 3 -- Growth, Partnerships & Distribution: help new products acquire brands/partners/creators/audiences; identify/engage decision-makers; build partnerships/commercial relationships; develop scalable acquisition/distribution strategies. Potential growth: ownership of supply/demand/distribution for new business lines.

What We're Looking For: approximately 2-5 years of relevant professional experience (1-2 years considered with exceptional ownership/execution); strong problem-solving/decision-making; ability to take ownership without constant supervision; comfortable in ambiguous/fast-moving environments; strong communication/stakeholder management; bias toward execution over prolonged analysis; ability to work with numbers/validate assumptions/measure outcomes; willingness to experiment/learn from failures/iterate quickly; interest in AI tools/automation/tech-driven businesses; experience building/launching/owning something end-to-end strongly preferred.

What Success Looks Like (first few months): take ownership of a clearly defined initiative; put an early version in front of real users/customers; establish measurable success metrics; build/automate required processes; launch something generating measurable usage/revenue/efficiency/impact; make clear scale/change/stop decisions based on evidence.

About InspireLabs: bootstrapped product company building/operating multiple digital businesses. Portfolio includes GrabOn, GrabCash, GrabShare and other emerging initiatives across commerce, affiliate technology, creator ecosystems, AI-led products. Strong focus on ownership, experimentation, automation, measurable outcomes.

Who Should Apply: built/owned something independently; enjoy solving unclear problems; prefer executing/experimenting over long presentation prep; naturally take ownership; comfortable being accountable for measurable results; want to eventually run a product/business line/high-impact function.
