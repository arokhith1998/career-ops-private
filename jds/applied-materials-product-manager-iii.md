Salary: $124,000.00 - $171,000.00

This role is responsible for all business aspects of Applied Materials Metal Deposition Products (MDP)/Atomic Layer Deposition (ALD) Service Business Unit (SBU) - Service Product Segment supplying the semiconductor industry. This person will engage cross-functionally to drive all aspects of the business and product lifecycle to deliver to customer and business expectations. This is a high growth business opportunity within Applied Materials Global Service Division.

Key Responsibilities
- Supports regional business and customer strategy, planning and execution for regional service business
- Builds understanding of regional and customer specific market requirements to provide inputs on customer and market requirements for product development needs
- Creates and maintains cost models to support service proposals
- Support SBU Operations with MDP and ALD segment market analysis and forecasting
- Responsible for service product marketing activities, developing opportunity value proposition, business sizing, sales opportunity analysis, Go-To-Market strategy including 7Ps of service marketing
- Develops product marketing strategy, Service Business Unit financial plans, business case assessment and analytics for the service opportunities
- Engages directly with customers, business development, service sales, field service and other value chain partners to deliver to business and stakeholder expectations
- Sponsors/drives inorganic growth activities and partnership development to support business execution and delivery
- Drive program and business outcomes utilizing applied and industry best practices/processes (CRM, SF, ERP etc.)
- Deeply engages with technical application and product development execution to ensure program success (Performance/Capability, Cost, Timing)
- Supports product lifecycle ownership facilitating PDP and end of life support programs
- Drives activities for the Service Business Unit through strong cross-functional engagement across multiple geographies & external partners in Sales, Marketing, Engineering, Operations, Finance, Legal etc. to deliver to release schedules and business financials
- Makes customer and management presentations on Service Business Unit business to support sales opportunity development, strategic and business planning, and service opportunity penetration
- Communicates business progress and execution to senior executive and stakeholders to successfully secure necessary support and resources to deliver to growth expectations
- Uses business analytics tools to support internal business analysis and reporting

Desired Skills, Competencies & Experience
- Higher Degree or equivalent experience with minimum 5+ years of industry experience
- Demonstrates depth and/or breadth of expertise in Wafer Fabrication Equipment and Metal and Atomic Layer Deposition Products operation and business
- Demonstrates understanding of service business principles
- Customer focused - Strong business and commercial acumen with previous regional ownership, or marketing/sales experience
- Program management and technical acumen with past product marketing experience
- High financial acumen and analytical/data-oriented decision-making style
- Strong analytical skills and experience using Microsoft Excel
- Positive can-do entrepreneurial attitude that builds support
- Strong communication skills and interpersonal presence that extends confidence and humility
- Experience building and delivering presentations
- Ability to innovate and solve unique and complex problems with broad impact on the business
- Critical thinking and ability to influence and communicate effectively with multiple internal stakeholders (technologists, engineering, Ops, finance, marketing, strategy)
- Ability to anticipate customer needs, business and regulatory issues; recommend product, process or service improvements
- Excellent time management and project management skills
