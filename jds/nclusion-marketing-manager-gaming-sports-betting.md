The role drives customer acquisition, engagement, and retention for online sports betting and iGaming platforms in emerging and frontier markets. The manager builds the vertical's digital marketing strategy, measurement framework, and operating standards, which local market teams use. It reports to the Head of Group Marketing, with a dotted-line relationship to local Marketing Managers, and works closely with senior operations, product, BI, finance, legal, and compliance.

Responsibilities:
- Developing digital strategies across paid media, SEO, social, affiliates, and CRM
- Running player lifecycle programs across email, SMS, push, WhatsApp, and in-app
- Managing promotions and setting bonus standards with finance
- Owning KPI frameworks, dashboards, and the paid media and affiliate structures, including fraud controls
- Writing playbooks and running tests that create cross-market benchmarks

Requirements: 8+ years of digital marketing experience in sports betting, online gambling, or iGaming; experience in emerging or frontier markets (Africa, Latin America, or the Caribbean preferred); familiarity with gambling advertising regulation; travel flexibility. Fluency in French and English is required, Spanish highly valued.
