## Full JD text

NVIDIA's AI Software Platforms team is building the next generation of agentic AI infrastructure that lets coding agents synthesize, optimize, and deploy GPU kernels automatically. This job focuses on crafting AI kernels that connect data pipelines, evaluation suites, and GPU-accelerated runtimes.

As Product Managers at NVIDIA, we enable developers to be successful on the NVIDIA platform. In this role, you will act as the internal champion for AI agents and LLM-based coding workflows that generate optimized kernels, partnering with engineering, research, and customers to define strategy, develop roadmaps, and build products spanning the entire agent lifecycle.

What You'll Be Doing:
- Architect agent-focused products that let coding agents generate, refactor, and optimize CUDA kernels and graph-level execution plans across GPU architectures.
- Define the end-to-end data lifecycle for agent training/evaluation: dataset curation, synthetic data creation, benchmark suites for correctness/latency/adaptability.
- Partner with CUDA, kernel, and compiler engineering teams to integrate agents with compilers, profilers, execution sandboxes, and runtimes safely/observably.
- Collaborate with internal/external developers, NVIDIA leaders, and ecosystem partners to drive multi-agent orchestration, prioritize features, deliver launches/messaging.

What We Need To See:
- 7+ years of technical product management or closely related experience shipping developer or platform products in AI, ML infrastructure, or HPC.
- Proven experience in the AI agent or LLM space, including developing/productizing coding agents; multi-agent orchestration and self-healing/code loops required; experience connecting agents to compilers/execution environments.
- Proven record of crafting/releasing automated testing or evaluation suites (correctness, performance, latency).
- BS or MS in Computer Engineering, Computer Science, or related technical field, or equivalent experience in parallel computing architectures/systems.

Ways To Stand Out: PhD or equivalent; track record building/launching coding-agent platforms/copilots at scale, contributions to performance-critical open-source projects (Triton, TVM, FlashAttention, kernel libraries, agent frameworks); research experience in GPU kernel optimization, collective communication algorithms, multi-agent systems, or ML model serving/inference; cost-per-inference or cost-per-token modeling experience.

Base salary range 168,000 USD - 258,750 USD for Level 4, and 208,000 USD - 327,750 USD for Level 5. Eligible for equity and benefits.
