## Description
About the Revenue team: drives Affirm's top-line growth through strategic partnerships, growth initiatives, go-to-market execution; acquires/retains/expands merchant relationships across eCommerce, in-store, telesales, and the Affirm app.

About the Role: GTM Strategy & Operations takes product and pricing initiatives and creates commercially viable, operationally sound go-to-market motions, partnering with Product, Product Marketing, Legal, Rev Ops, and Pricing to design rollout groups, coordinate implementation, and equip the field (CSMs, AEs, TAMs). As an IC, the Lead owns go-to-market operations for a specific product line or segment end to end: building target lists, assigning account actions, segmenting customers by contractual/pricing eligibility, tracking adoption.

## What You'll Do
- Own go-to-market operations for field teams executing on strategic initiatives, segmenting potential customers and managing legal and pricing operations.
- Turn inputs from Product, Legal, Risk, and Pricing into an executable process.
- Manage Affirm's "first-in-class AI contract dashboard" for field teams to understand legal and pricing eligibility.
- Build/maintain tools and playbooks for strategic initiatives: contract renewal planning, pricing standardization, new product GTM.
- Navigate different perspectives on the same launch (exec, customer, field-facing).
- Sit close to the field, turn feedback into recommendations for cross-functional teams.
- Analyze performance data to find where a launch is stalling, flag risks early.

## What We Look For
- 6+ years of experience in business operations, revenue or sales operations, ideally FinTech/SaaS/high-growth.
- Track record owning a go-to-market motion or customer-facing initiative end to end.
- Experience working cross-functionally with Product, PMM, Client Success, Sales, Analytics, Legal, or Pricing.
- Familiarity with merchant, payments, or SaaS sales motions is a "must-have."
- Experience using SQL.
- Demonstrated experience leveraging AI tools.
