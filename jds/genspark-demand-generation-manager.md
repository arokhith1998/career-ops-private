Genspark is seeking an ambitious Senior Demand Generation Manager to join our GTM team. This is an opportunity to play a foundational role in bringing one of the fastest-growing AI platforms to businesses around the world. You'll own global demand generation programs that attract new business customers, convert existing product adoption into qualified sales opportunities, and drive measurable pipeline and revenue growth.

Reporting to the Head of B2B Marketing, you will partner closely with Sales, Product Marketing, Performance Marketing, Revenue Operations and our regional marketing teams to develop scalable global campaigns that can be adapted to local market needs.

If you're a revenue marketer who loves to execute, and prioritizes experimentation, speed, and measurable business impact, this is the role for you

What You'll Do:

Build and scale global B2B demand generation
- Develop and execute integrated, multi-channel campaigns that drive qualified pipeline and revenue across SMB, mid-market, and enterprise segments.
- Own B2B paid media strategy and execution across LinkedIn, industry publications, demand generation partners, and other targeted channels.
- Partner with the Performance Marketing team to optimize shared acquisition channels, including Google and Meta, for B2B targeting, tracking, and measurement.
- Lead integrated campaigns across paid media, email, webinars, content, retargeting, and other high-impact channels. Work with regional marketing managers to adapt and localize global campaigns, balancing global consistency with local market needs.
- Develop account-based marketing (ABM) programs targeting high-value accounts and drive co-marketing initiatives with strategic partners to expand reach, generate demand, and accelerate pipeline.
- Test new channels and approaches, optimize campaign investments, and develop repeatable programs that scale across regions.

Connect product-led growth with sales-led growth
- Develop programs that convert Genspark's existing global user base into business opportunities, team adoption, and enterprise pipeline.
- Build targeted lifecycle and nurture programs based on product usage, customer behavior, account characteristics, and buying intent.
- Partner with Revenue Operations and Sales to identify high-value accounts, establish qualification signals, and improve conversion from product engagement to sales opportunities.

Drive performance and revenue impact
- Establish and monitor KPIs across the marketing funnel, including conversion rates, qualified pipeline, acquisition efficiency, and revenue contribution.
- Partner with Revenue Operations to improve attribution, segmentation, lead management, and reporting.
- Analyze campaign performance across regions to identify opportunities, share best practices, and optimize global marketing investments.
- Use performance data and experimentation to continuously improve targeting, messaging, conversion, and marketing ROI.
- Continuously identify opportunities in which our own Genspark AI tools can speed up campaign planning, production, and reporting, and standardize repeatable plays.

Who You Are:
- Experienced B2B revenue marketer: 5+ years in demand generation or growth marketing, ideally in high-growth SaaS or AI companies, with a track record of driving qualified pipeline and revenue.
- Fluent in PLG and SLG: Experience building demand generation programs across product-led and sales-led motions, including converting product engagement into business opportunities.
- Hands-on, multi-channel expert: Proven ability to develop, execute, and optimize integrated campaigns across paid media, email, webinars, content, and lifecycle marketing. Hands-on experience with modern GTM tools (e.g., HubSpot, Clay, Common Room) and adept at building AI agents, automations, and workflows to accelerate campaign execution, improve efficiency, and scale marketing operations.
- Data-driven and commercially minded: Strong analytical skills, proficiency with CRM and marketing automation platforms, and a focus on funnel performance, attribution, and measurable business outcomes.
- Collaborative builder with a global mindset: Experience developing scalable global programs with regional teams, strong cross-functional leadership skills, and the resourcefulness to thrive in a fast-moving environment.

Location: Remote-friendly across the US. Preference for NYC or Palo Alto.
