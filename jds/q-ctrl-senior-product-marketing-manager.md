About The Team

At Q-CTRL, marketing plays a central role in advancing our mission: making quantum technology useful for our customers worldwide. The Marketing team is a globally facing group of experts in marketing and communications with a passion for deep technologies and dual-use industries, and experience promoting both software and full-stack products. The team’s work spans content marketing, PR and external communications, product marketing, field marketing, performance marketing (digital and web), and marketing strategy. Closely partnered with product, sales, design, operations, and executive teams, the team is critical to promoting our unique capabilities in AI-powered software and quantum navigation.

About The Role

Q-CTRL is looking for a hands-on Senior Product Marketing Manager based in SF or LA to develop and deploy product, growth and GTM campaigns in Quantum Computing. The role will report to our Head of Marketing.

As a Senior product marketing manager, our ideal candidate has deep experience in product-led marketing within a high growth / cutting edge technology environment. You will have developed a strong set of skills to analyze market opportunities, create impactful product-marketing material and deploy go-to-market plans and activities that drive growth and brand outcomes. Your product marketing capabilities will span inbound audience development (research, product definition and testing) to GTM execution (narrative, content and campaign execution) and finally outbound customer engagement (sales enablement, customer success and communications). These should be grounded in a strong understanding of how product marketing works with other teams (Product, Sales, Engineering, Design and more) to deliver on an ambitious product marketing roadmap. Storytelling will be at the heart of what you do, telling a compelling narrative across our audiences and products, simplifying complex concepts and creating content that brings our world-changing technology to life.

We are looking for a proven Senior Product Marketing Manager who loves rolling up their sleeves, making things happen, and helping emerging and fast growing companies like us stand out in a crowded, rapidly growing early market like Quantum Computing and Quantum Sensing.

What you'll be doing:

Product Marketing

Manage Product Marketing efforts at Q-CTRL. Partner with Sales and Product teams in aligning roadmaps, conducting market research and implementing new strategies based on findings

Tell our product story and formulate a product marketing roadmap that outlines engagement and develop collateral that facilitate the sales process and communications objectives

Go-To-Market

Develop GTM launch strategies that establish value proposition, launch narrative, collateral, channel plan and media mix

Deploy campaigns focused on new and existing products, determining ideal customer segments, creating high impact assets and measuring success.

Analyze and continuously improve campaign performance in order to meet strategic business objectives.

Execution

Translate high-level strategies into actionable, ambitious, yet attainable plans. Develop, plan, and manage marketing activity inline with campaign budgets

Create advertising material including PR, print, digital, social media, videos, posts and interactive assets

Lead, design and develop conference campaigns that showcase new products to potential customers, key stakeholders and business partners

Ideally you'll have:

7+ years of work experience in product marketing or technology marketing with specific experience managing key product marketing functions: Customer Research, GTM, Digital Marketing, Social Media and Events/PR

Demonstrable experience designing and implementing successful marketing campaigns. Solid knowledge of SEO, CRM (Salesforce), web analytics, and digital marketing best practices

Strategic and biased toward action, a great collaborator who never stops pushing towards clarity and delivery of our products’ value.

Someone who perseveres and never hesitates to roll up their sleeves and tackle something hands-on

Detail-oriented with a focus on always delivering the highest-quality content.

Outcome-focused, Marketing helps to drive sales - the Senior Product Marketing Manager has a crucial role to play in enabling the sales team to succeed
