## Job Description (extracted, verbatim from posting)

The Senior Manager is an integral part of Latham's Global Finance team, responsible for providing revenue and financial analysis support to all US practice offices within their region, building relationships with and supporting practice office Revenue Managers, supervisors, and staff.

**Responsibilities:**
- Assist the Director of Revenue Operations with setting up/supporting new or expanding practice offices (including travel)
- Create tip sheets, billing manual updates, training materials; train practice office revenue staff
- Ensure "Inventory Management Gold Standard" is executed effectively
- Continuing review of revenue-related procedures for efficiency improvements
- Develop and manage a bill auditing function
- Protect highly sensitive, confidential, privileged, financial, and/or proprietary information

**Qualifications:**
- "A bachelor's degree in Accounting, or a related field"
- "A minimum of six (6) years increasingly complex accounting experience, with at least two (2) years of relevant experience in client billing"
- "A minimum of two (2) years' of experience in a management capacity"
- Experience at a professional services organization
- Experience with Elite accounting system or comparable enterprise financial software

Benefits: healthcare/life/disability insurance, generous 401k, 11+ paid holidays, PTO accruing 23 days in year one (grows with tenure), well-being programs, professional development, employee discounts, affinity groups.
