## Job Description (full, verbatim)
About the Company
AIRES (AI Research & Entrepreneurship Scholars) provides year-round, hands-on mentorship in artificial intelligence, engineering, and entrepreneurship for middle and high school students. Its programs include residential summer experiences hosted on university campuses such as Yale and UC Berkeley.

Students work with experienced mentors to identify meaningful problems and develop original research and innovation projects, taking them from initial concepts through testing and presentation.

About the Role
AIRES is seeking a Digital Marketing Manager to lead its digital marketing strategy and manage paid advertising campaigns across Meta Ads, Google Ads, and ChatGPT Ads.

The primary focus of this role is paid acquisition, with an emphasis on generating qualified inquiries from parents, program applications, and student enrollments. The manager will also oversee organic social media content and collaborate with the enrollment team to understand campaign performance and improve the quality of prospective family inquiries.

Key Responsibilities
- Develop digital marketing strategies for AIRES programs, defining campaign goals, budgets, target audiences, and timelines around key application and enrollment periods.
- Build, launch, manage, and optimize paid advertising campaigns across Meta Ads, Google Ads, and ChatGPT Ads.
- Test ad copy, creative assets, and landing pages to improve campaign performance.
- Monitor campaign results and adjust advertising budgets based on performance.
- Set up conversion tracking and report on advertising spend, cost per qualified inquiry, and, where measurable, applications and enrollments generated through paid campaigns.
- Work with the enrollment team to gather feedback and improve lead quality.
- Manage the organic social media calendar, create and publish content, and maintain current program information across AIRES channels.
- Use campaign data to identify effective channels, messaging, and offers and guide future advertising decisions.

Required Qualifications
- Hands-on experience managing campaigns on Meta Ads, Google Ads, and ChatGPT Ads, including campaign setup, budget management, performance monitoring, and optimization.
- Experience developing digital marketing strategies and using campaign data to determine which campaigns to scale, revise, or pause.
- Ability to independently manage campaigns while collaborating with a remote team.
- Experience creating marketing materials such as flyers, brochures, marketing emails, landing pages, or advertising creative.

Preferred Qualifications
- Experience marketing education programs or other services to parents and families.
- Experience in consultative sales or enrollment advising.
- Willingness to communicate directly with parents, follow up with prospective families, and support enrollment closures when needed.

What Success Looks Like
- Paid campaigns consistently generate qualified inquiries within the allocated budget.
- Campaign performance clearly shows which channels, messages, and offers contribute to applications and enrollments.
- Marketing decisions and future advertising budgets are guided by measurable campaign results and feedback from the enrollment team.
