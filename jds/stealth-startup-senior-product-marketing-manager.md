ABOUT THE ROLE

We are looking for an experienced Senior Product Marketing Manager to lead market positioning, product marketing strategy, and go-to-market initiatives for an innovative AI-powered voice technology platform serving enterprise customers across multiple industries.

This role will focus on identifying growth opportunities, translating product capabilities into compelling market narratives, and building marketing strategies that drive customer adoption and business growth.

You will own product positioning, messaging, product launches, sales enablement, and customer-facing marketing initiatives. Additionally, you will oversee creative brand and social media campaigns to strengthen market presence and engagement.

Approximately 80% of the role will focus on core product marketing, with the remaining 20% dedicated to creative campaign direction and social media initiatives.

This is a high-ownership position that involves working closely with executive leadership, product, sales, and creative teams.

KEY RESPONSIBILITIES

Product Positioning & Messaging

Develop and manage product positioning and messaging across different customer segments and industries.

Create industry-specific marketing narratives for healthcare, legal, automotive, insurance, utilities, and logistics.

Establish clear competitive differentiation and communicate the product's value proposition.

Product Launches & Go-to-Market Strategy

Lead end-to-end product launches, including strategy, messaging, planning, and execution.

Coordinate with cross-functional teams to ensure successful product announcements and market adoption.

Collaborate with technical teams to develop product demonstrations and supporting content.

Sales Enablement

Develop sales enablement materials, presentations, and messaging frameworks.

Conduct internal training sessions to ensure sales teams are equipped with relevant product information.

Support sales teams with effective positioning and competitive insights.

Customer & Market Research

Conduct ongoing customer research and market discovery.

Translate customer feedback and market trends into actionable marketing strategies.

Develop customer case studies, success stories, and industry-specific proof points.

Brand & Social Media Campaigns

Develop creative campaign concepts and provide direction to creative teams.

Plan and execute campaigns around product launches, industry events, and emerging AI trends.

Drive brand awareness and organic engagement through compelling social media initiatives.

WHAT WE'RE LOOKING FOR

Must-Have Qualifications

5+ years of experience in product marketing within B2B SaaS, developer tools, or AI infrastructure.

Proven experience managing end-to-end product launches.

Strong expertise in product positioning, messaging, and go-to-market strategy.

Experience developing sales enablement programs and marketing resources.

Ability to understand technical products and translate complex concepts into clear business value.

Excellent communication, strategic thinking, and cross-functional collaboration skills.

Good-to-Have Qualifications

Experience in AI infrastructure, CCaaS, or contact center technology.

Experience marketing to both technical and business audiences.

Background in managing social media campaigns with strong organic reach.

Creative mindset with a strong understanding of brand development and content strategy.

Previous experience working in a fast-paced startup environment.

SUCCESS CRITERIA

The ideal candidate will demonstrate:

Proven ownership of product positioning and messaging strategies.

Ability to develop impactful marketing campaigns that generate genuine audience engagement.

Strong collaboration skills across product, sales, design, and leadership teams.

Ability to work independently, prioritize effectively, and make informed decisions in ambiguous situations.

A hands-on approach to executing marketing strategies and delivering measurable results.
