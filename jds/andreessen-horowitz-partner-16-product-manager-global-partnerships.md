The Role

a16z's Frontiers organization operates like an internal startup within the firm, building AI-native software that helps a16z discover, evaluate, and support the world's most ambitious founders. The Global Partnerships (GPX) team is dedicated to crafting an outstanding client experience for our trusted Limited Partners ("LPs"), and Frontiers is building the product foundation that makes that possible.

We're looking for a Product Manager to embed with GPX and own the product roadmap for the systems that power it, including LP reporting and communications, fundraising workflows, relationship and access management, and the data infrastructure that ties it all together. You'll work directly with GPX stakeholders and Frontiers engineering and design to turn LP needs into prioritized, shippable work. You'll be hands-on: scoping problems, shaping requirements, and shipping alongside engineers rather than handing off a spec and waiting.

This is a high-trust role. You'll work with sensitive LP data, partnering closely with GPX, Finance, Legal, and Frontiers leadership. You'll have real ownership from day one and a clear chance to shape how a best-in-class product function supports an investor relations team.

This role requires an in-office presence 2-3 days a week in our San Francisco, CA office.

To join our team, you should be excited to:

Partner with GPX stakeholders to translate LP and firm needs into a clear, prioritized product roadmapExecute end to end on GPX products, from discovery and requirements through shipping and iteration alongside Frontiers engineersImprove the systems behind LP reporting, capital activity communications, and firm performance updatesShape workflows and tooling that support fundraising, LP relationship management, and access to firm content and eventsHandle sensitive LP and performance data with rigor and discretion, and bring a strong point of view on data governanceRun user discovery and drive adoption within GPX, turning feedback into product decisions that hold up under scrutinyRepresent GPX's needs in Frontiers's broader roadmap, and bring Frontiers's product practice to a relationship-driven, non-technical team

Minimum Qualifications

4+ years of experience in product management or product engineering, ideally including AI-powered or data-intensive productsExperience building internal tools or workflow software for a relationship-driven, non-technical stakeholder groupComfort working with sensitive or confidential data, with strong instincts for data governance and access controlStrong product judgment and the ability to turn ambiguous workflows into clear requirementsTrack record of shipping product end to end in a fast-paced, resource-constrained environmentExcellent written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to build trust quickly across investing, GPX, and engineeringLow ego, high empathy, and the capacity to collaborate effectively with diverse teams
