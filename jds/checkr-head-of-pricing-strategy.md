## Role summary
Build Checkr's pricing strategy function, described in the posting as "one of
the highest-leverage decisions a company makes."

## Responsibilities (summarized from source)
- Own pricing and packaging strategy across all products and go-to-market
  channels, from enterprise to self-serve.
- Build pricing models, including consumption-based and outcome-based
  structures.
- Run pricing research such as conjoint analysis, Van Westendorp studies, and
  willingness-to-pay interviews.
- Design and run pricing experiments and analyze elasticity and purchasing
  behavior.
- Build a function that uses AI-powered research and automated analysis.
- Maintain coherent pricing architecture as Checkr launches products, acquires
  companies, and scales.
- Partner with Product, Sales, Marketing, Deal Desk, and GTM leadership, and
  own pricing governance.
- Track competitive pricing and market trends.
- Define pricing KPIs and report performance to senior leadership.

## Requirements (summarized from source)
- 10+ years of experience, including 6+ years in B2B pricing and packaging.
  Consumption models or real COGS experience is a plus.
- Hands-on experience with pricing research methods.
- A track record of launching pricing models with measurable results.
- Strong financial modeling skills and comfort with large datasets. SQL is a
  plus.
- Ability to align Product, Sales, Finance, and executives.
- Ability to turn analysis into executive-ready recommendations.
- Strong bias for action, comfort with ambiguity, and full ownership of
  outcomes.
