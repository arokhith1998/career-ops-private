About Salesforce

Salesforce is the #1 AI CRM, where humans with agents drive customer success together. Here, ambition meets action. Tech meets trust. And innovation isn’t a buzzword — it’s a way of life. The world of work as we know it is changing and we're looking for Trailblazers who are passionate about bettering business and the world through AI, driving innovation, and keeping Salesforce's core values at the heart of it all.

Ready to level-up your career at the company leading workforce transformation in the agentic era? You’re in the right place! Agentforce is the future of AI, and you are the future of Salesforce.

The Experience

The MeshMesh team is building the next-generation mesh networking platform for Salesforce customers. As the Agentic Product Manager, you will develop the strategy and own the product roadmap and vision for product, providing insights that fuel critical decisions across the company, including executive leadership.

Responsibilities

- Develop the strategy and own the roadmap and vision for product.
- Work with collaborators to understand internal customer use cases.
- Partner with engineering architects to implement the roadmap.
- Work through ambiguity and lean on key internal partners to align on priorities.
- Provide prioritized use cases for engineers.
- Define technical specifications, write high-level documents, and build roadmap presentations.
- Lead small cross-functional teams as well as large programs.

Required Skills/Experience

- You have 5+ years of proven experience in the Product
- You have 3+ years in Product Management.
- You have experience with the SaaS business model.
- You work effectively with technical and business leaders.
- You can translate business requirements into product and technical requirements and a practical roadmap.
- You navigate decision-making and communicate effectively across teams.
- You are an Agentic Engineer - you build with Claude and similar AI coding tools, using agent loops, tool use, and well-grounded context engineering to ship code fast.
- You have a related technical degree.

Preferred Skills/Experience

- You love data and analytics, using quantitative and qualitative metrics to build a roadmap.
- You have deep empathy for customers and can work with tech and product, billing, legal, sales, and data platform teams.
- You have excellent written communication skills.
- You have experience with the Salesforce Platform and agile methodologies.
- You can lead through influence across a complex organization.
