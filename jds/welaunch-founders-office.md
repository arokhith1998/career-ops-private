## Full JD Text (verbatim)

Company Description

WeLaunch engineers businesses for the AI era by building and operating a portfolio of vertical AI systems, each proven inside real companies before becoming standalone assets. Rather than acting as an agency or traditional software vendor, WeLaunch installs and launches AI-native business engines that move beyond data dashboards to automated, end-to-end execution. Our systems run the full operational loop - from acquiring customers and booking jobs to routing technicians, servicing work, collecting reviews, invoicing, and payments - then optimize for density and growth. We have already transformed operations for organizations in fire protection, pest control, and law, delivering measurable change in under thirty days. Having built the core AI "brain," WeLaunch is now focused on expanding its portfolio and deepening impact across industries.

Role Description

This is a full-time, remote Founder's Office role that sits at the intersection of strategy, execution, and operations. The individual in this role will work closely with the founders to structure and drive high-impact initiatives, including new vertical launches, product experiments, and operational improvements across the portfolio. Day-to-day responsibilities include conducting market and competitive research, synthesizing insights into clear recommendations, preparing strategic briefs and investor or partner materials, and coordinating cross-functional projects from idea to implementation. The role also involves building lightweight processes, tracking key performance metrics, and ensuring follow-through on decisions and priorities set by the leadership team. This position is ideal for someone who thrives in ambiguous environments, is comfortable switching between strategic thinking and hands-on execution, and wants direct exposure to building AI-native businesses.

Qualifications
- Strategic and analytical skills: ability to conduct structured research, analyze data, build clear narratives, and translate findings into actionable plans.
- Operational and project management skills: experience organizing complex initiatives, defining milestones, managing stakeholders, and driving execution to deadlines.
- Communication and collaboration skills: strong written and verbal communication, experience preparing decks or memos, and ability to work with technical and non-technical teams.
- Product and business sense: familiarity with technology or AI-driven products, comfort with experimentation, and understanding of how operations, product, and growth interact.
- Self-management and adaptability: ability to work independently in a remote setting, prioritize across competing demands, and operate effectively in a fast-paced, evolving environment.
- Bachelor's degree in a relevant field (e.g., business, economics, engineering, computer science) or equivalent practical experience.
- Experience in startups, consulting, operations, or founder-associate roles is highly beneficial.
- Interest in AI, automation, and building systems that run entire business workflows is a strong asset.
