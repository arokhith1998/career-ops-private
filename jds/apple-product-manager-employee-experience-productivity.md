## Full JD text (verbatim, via headless browser extraction of jobs.apple.com)

Imagine what you could do here. At Apple, great ideas have a way of becoming great products, services, and experiences very quickly. Apple's Employee Experience and Productivity team are seeking a Senior Product Manager who will be a crucial leader of a unique team responsible for defining, researching, and delivering Collaboration and Productivity tools to all business units at Apple. Some of the tools we deliver provide features to enhance large segments of our daily workflow; this includes tools built internally and sourced externally, and focus areas such as Meeting Experience, File Sync & Share, Chat, Content Collaboration, Work Management, and Artificial Intelligence.

Employee Experience and Productivity (EEP) is part of IS&T and delivers some of the most widely used communication and productivity technology and services across Apple, from tools that enable collaboration to the support teams that keep Apple's workforce running. EEP also leads user experience design and research, and manages the systems behind Apple's original content studios.

**Description:** In this role, you will be leading the creation and management of tools to support and reshape how employees work together. As new business requirements evolve, you will craft the story, explore alternative solutions and define the vision, strategy, and roadmap for your portfolio of products. A key part of this role is the ongoing rationalization of your tool portfolio, continuously evaluating the landscape to identify opportunities to consolidate redundant solutions, surface synergies across tools, and simplify the overall experience for employees.

**Responsibilities**
- Define and own the vision, strategy, and roadmap for a portfolio of employee productivity and collaboration tools, balancing short-term delivery with long term strategic bets
- Lead cross-functional teams through full product development cycles from discovery and requirements through design, engineering, and launch
- Continuously evaluate the tool portfolio for consolidation and synergy opportunities
- Partner with vendors to align external roadmaps and capabilities to Apple's internal priorities and employee needs
- Use data and employee insights to measure product impact, identify gaps, and drive continuous improvement across the portfolio

**Minimum Qualifications**
- 4+ years of experience in a product management role leading product development cycles from concept to launch, with a demonstrated track record of shipping products at scale
- 4+ years of experience working cross-functionally with peer teams such as software engineering and UX Design
- "BS or MS degree in computer science, information management, or a technical field, or equivalent experience."

**Preferred Qualifications**
- Expert in identifying complex employee requirements, aligning them to business objectives, and building functional engineering requirements
- Strong prioritization skills
- Advanced skills in analyzing product data
- Hands-on experience designing, shipping, or integrating AI-powered tools and experiences
- Proficient with visual design and diagramming tools
- Experience working with vendor-managed products and roadmaps
- Experience managing and rationalizing a portfolio of tools
- Keen ability to rally teams and shepherd product development from discovery to launch
- Highly confident leading teams and driving cross-functional alignment in ambiguous problem spaces
- Excellent communication and interpersonal skills
- Experience mentoring product managers
