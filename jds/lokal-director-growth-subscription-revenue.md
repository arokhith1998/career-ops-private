## Role Summary
Lokal is a family of apps for Tier-2/3+ India. This role owns total subscription revenue
across four products (GyanTV, Sahi English, Poster Maker, Matrimony) as a leadership P&L
seat parallel to product heads. Quoted: "one owner who can see all four at once."

## Responsibilities
- Core metric: total subscription revenue across the portfolio (revenue scale, LTV/CAC).
- Acquisition: performance marketing across Meta, Google, emerging channels (reduce
  single-platform dependence); ASO/organic growth; influencer/channel mix; vernacular
  creative testing at volume; ongoing CAC optimization.
- Funnel/experimentation: own full funnel (awareness through win-back); structured
  experiments (pricing, paywalls, onboarding, creative-to-landing-page match); new growth
  plays.
- Monetization: pricing, packaging, trials, paywall placement, autopay mandate economics,
  win-back, keeping ad promises aligned with in-app delivery.
- Budget/people: allocate marketing budget across four businesses with different LTV
  curves; build team spanning performance marketing, lifecycle/CRM, creative, ASO, growth
  analytics.
- Measurement: blended/channel ROAS, M0-to-payback visibility, CAC/payback targets by
  product, incrementality/attribution (MMM + platform data), compliant account structures
  for scrutinized categories (matrimony, personal-finance-adjacent traffic).

## Requirements
- 5+ years growth marketing experience, detailed/verifiable track record.
- Prior ownership of a revenue P&L, ideally subscription business.
- Experience scaling to ₹200-300 Cr+ annual revenue or ₹8-10 Cr+ monthly revenue.
- Hands-on CAC optimization and performance marketing expertise.
- End-to-end funnel building with experimentation bias.
- Experience running growth across multiple product lines sharing a budget.
- Knowledge of Indian consumer subscription mechanics (UPI autopay/e-mandate economics,
  vernacular creative at scale).
- Fluency in ad-platform policy/account health, ideally in a regulated/scrutinized
  category.
- Ability to discuss unit economics (CAC, payback, cohort LTV, ROAS, MMM/attribution) in
  depth.
- Marketing org-building experience (hiring/managing performance, CRM, creative, ASO,
  analytics).
- Existing network across ad platforms, agencies, creative supply.
