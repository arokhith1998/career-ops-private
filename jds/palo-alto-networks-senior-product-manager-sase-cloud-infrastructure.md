## Job Description (extracted, verbatim from posting)

As a Principal Product Manager for the SASE solution, you will shape the strategy and roadmap for Palo Alto Networks' core Prisma Access cloud infrastructure, managing all stages of the feature lifecycle from conception to post-release.

**Key Responsibilities:**
- Spearhead creation/deployment of a new public cloud security platform (enhancing/expanding Prisma Access)
- Manage entire product lifecycle (conception through post-release)
- Collaborate with Product Marketing on sales tools/marketing content
- Evangelize product strategy internally (sales) and externally (customers/partners)
- Work with legal, InfoSec, engineering on privacy/data protection regulation compliance
- Define/prioritize product requirements, make feature trade-off decisions

**Required Qualifications:**
- "Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Business, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience."
- "10+ years of product management or a minimum of 8 years of technical marketing experience in the enterprise networking and/or cloud security space."
- Deep understanding of Layer-2/3/4 networking protocols, VPN/tunneling, cloud compute/network solutions
- Practical experience in Generative AI (GenAI) and Agentic AI systems

Preferred: MBA or advanced technical degree (MS/PhD); customer engagement/executive briefing experience.
