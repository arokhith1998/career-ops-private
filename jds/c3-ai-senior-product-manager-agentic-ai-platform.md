C3 AI (NYSE: AI), is the Enterprise AI application software company. - Strong technical foundation in data management/infrastructure, data science/AI/ML workflows, or developer tooling/application environments.
- Track record of driving measurable adoption, revenue, or commercial outcomes for a platform product.
- Strong data fluency -- comfortable using metrics and querying data (SQL) to inform decisions.
- Proven ability to develop and communicate product vision, strategy, roadmap.
- Excellent communication/collaboration across engineering, data science, UX, GTM functions.
- Bachelor of Science in a STEM field; MBA or Master of Science preferred.
