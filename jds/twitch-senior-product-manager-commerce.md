 About UsTwitch is the world’s biggest live streaming service, with global communities built around gaming, entertainment, music, sports, cooking, and more. It is where thousands of communities come together for whatever, every day.We’re about community, inside and out. You’ll find coworkers who are eager to team up, collaborate, and smash (or elegantly solve) problems together. We’re on a quest to empower live communities, so if this sounds good to you, see what we’re up to on LinkedIn and X, and discover the projects we’re solving on our Blog. Be sure to explore our Interviewing Guide to learn how to ace our interview process.About The RoleTwitch's Commerce organization drives over $1B+ in annual payouts to creators through innovative monetization products. As we continue to evolve how streamers earn and how communities engage, we're looking for a visionary Sr. Product Manager to own the roadmap for supporting streamers through interactive, fun, and mobile-native commerce products.In this role, you will define and execute the strategy for helping streamers build deeper connections with their communities through interactive features and engaging monetization tools. You'll build solutions for products that help creators monetize (like Bits, Subscriptions, etc) while exploring new ways to help creators earn, engage their audiences, and grow their channels. By focusing on streamer success and community connection, you'll help shape how millions of viewers support their favorite streamers across all devices.You can work from San Francisco, CA; Los Angeles, CA; Irvine, CA; or Seattle, WA.You Will:Lead the product vision and roadmap for high impact Twitch commerce capabilities, balancing near-term customer and business outcomes with longer-term product investmentsOwn the one-to-two year vision & drive prioritization for features that help streamers connect and monetize with viewers, including future innovations in community engagement and creator monetizationOwn core streamer success metrics including interaction rates, creator earnings, viewer engagement, mobile conversion, and retention. Continuously run experiments to test new features and improve outcomes for streamers and their communitiesDrive multi-screen product strategy, ensuring streamers can engage and monetize their communities seamlessly across devicesPartner cross-functionally with Engineering, Design, Data Science, Research, and Community teams to deliver products that help streamers succeed and grow their channelsChampion streamer-first thinking, ensuring all products enhance creator earnings, deepen community bonds, and support channel growthYou Have:5+ years of work experience as a Product Manager Experience with live streaming, gaming, or the broader creator economyExperience with monetization products and understanding how to balance creator earnings with a consumer experienceExperience building consumer-facing products at scale, particularly mobile applications or creator/community toolsDeep understanding of creator ecosystems: their needs, community dynamics, and what helps them succeed on digital platformsExperience leading cross-functional initiatives and driving alignment across diverse stakeholder groupsExperience defining 1-2 year product strategies and executing against ambitious roadmapsBonus PointsFamiliarity with virtual goods, digital currencies, or in-app purchase systemsAnalytically self-serve: able to write basic SQL, use experimentation/analytics tools and AI tools for product analysisBackground in social features, chat systems, or real-time interactive experiencesPersonal experience as a content creator or deep engagement with creator communitiesPerksMedical, Dental, Vision & Disability Insurance401(k)Maternity & Parental LeaveFlexible PTOAmazon Employee DiscountPursuant to the San Francisco Fair Chance Ordinance, we will consider for employment qualified applicants with arrest and conviction records.Pursuant to the Los Angeles Fair Chance Ordinance, we will consider for employment qualified applicants with arrest and conviction records.
