## Full JD text

About Feathery

Feathery is the AI operating system for financial institutions. With hundreds of clients and over $30 million raised, Feathery is the fast-growing leader in financial agentic automation. Industry leaders like Tokio Marine, Sequoia Financial, and Cerity Partners run their client-facing workflows on Feathery, from onboarding and risk assessment to reconciling systems and generating proposals and reports.

At the center of it all is Robin, our AI operations assistant. Robin scans client documents, configures and deploys workflows, assists users in their day-to-day work, takes notes during meetings, and supports a growing range of operational tasks across the client lifecycle.

We're building a future where financial operations shift from paperwork, manual data entry, and disconnected systems to intelligent, automated workflows that let firms spend their time on clients instead of process. We're proud to be backed by world-class investors who share that vision, including Portage Ventures, Allstate Ventures, and Index Ventures, among others.

We're driven by a shared commitment to excellence and velocity. Our values - Own the Problem, Grit Beats Gloss, Say It Straight - shape how we work and grow as a team.

Why We're Hiring

As Feathery scales across financial services, we're investing in a world-class go-to-market motion to keep pace. With growing enterprise demand and new product capabilities rolling out quickly, we need strong product marketing to sharpen our positioning, accelerate adoption, and enable our Sales and Customer Success teams to win.

We're hiring a Product Marketing Manager to own strategic positioning, competitive differentiation, product launches, and the voice of the customer. You'll help shape how Feathery tells its story internally and externally, translating complex product capabilities into clear narratives that resonate with enterprise buyers.

You'll collaborate closely with Product, Sales, Customer Success, and Leadership to refine our market strategy, influence product direction, and build the messaging and enablement that help win enterprise deals.

About The Role

What makes this an exciting role to step into? You'll have significant ownership, with a few things that make this opportunity stand out:
- Driving an AI-native marketing organization, shaping marketing in the AI era
- We have lots of fun making videos for launches: see examples for our Robin launch and Proposals
- Ability to build and hire a team, and grow into a marketing leader

You'll play a key role in shaping how Feathery tells its product story and brings it to market, with direct impact on customer adoption and growth.
