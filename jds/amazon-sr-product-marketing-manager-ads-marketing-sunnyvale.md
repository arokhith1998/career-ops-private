About The Team

The Ads Product Marketing team within Amazon Ads is the strategic connective tissue between product innovation and market impact. We don't just launch products, we shape how advertisers discover, understand, and derive value from Amazon's advertising solutions across the full funnel.

The Commerce pod sits at the heart of this mission, owning product marketing for some of Amazon Ads' core solutions: Sponsored Products, Sponsored Brands, and Brand Stores. Together, these products serve millions of advertisers globally from small businesses running their first campaign to the large brands.

This role is part of the Sponsored Products & Sponsored Brands (SPB) product marketing team. We are evolving the PMM function beyond traditional launch communications to earning a seat at the table to directly influence the product roadmap through voice-of-customer research, competitive intelligence, and deep advertiser insights. Our work spans the full marketing lifecycle: from shaping what gets built, to how it's positioned in market, to driving adoption and measuring impact post-launch.

You'll join a collaborative, fast-paced team that partners daily with Product Management, Go-to-Market, Sales Enablement, and cross-functional leadership. We operate with a builder mentality by testing, learning, and iterating to deliver solutions that move the needle for advertisers and for the business.

Responsibilities include running customer research and competitive analysis to inform the product roadmap and prioritization, supporting effective Alpha/Beta testing, and refining the strategic roadmap. Align GTM strategy to the product roadmap, translate complex product information into simple and clear language, and continue to gather customer feedback post-launch to inform future iterations. Develop single-source-of-truth documentation to enable sales and marketing with positioning, messaging, product availability, best practices, implementation guides, and FAQs.

Basic Qualifications
6+ years of professional non-internship marketing experience
Experience using data and metrics to drive improvements
Experience communicating results to senior leadership
Experience building, executing and scaling cross-functional marketing programs
Experience leading go-to-market for consumer software or hardware product launches

Preferred Qualifications
Experience using any of SQL or other analytical tools for conducting data analysis
Experience with customer segmentation, profiling, and targeting
