## Job Description (full text via Ashby posting API)

### Why Harvey
At Harvey, we're transforming how legal and professional services operate. By combining frontier agentic AI, an enterprise-grade platform, and deep domain expertise, we're reshaping how critical knowledge work gets done for decades to come. (Standard company-mission boilerplate follows in the original listing.)

### Role Overview
"We are hiring a Partner Marketing Manager to own and execute joint go-to-market with the technology and data partners our customers already run their practices on. You'll build the integration narrative, run co-marketing campaigns, launch moments, and partner events, and enable both sales teams to tell a shared story."

"This is a high-ownership individual contributor role for someone who can set priorities across partners at very different stages of maturity, work through influence across two organizations at once, and turn ambiguous alliance conversations into programs that produce pipeline and product adoption."

### What You'll Do
- Own the joint go-to-market strategy for a portfolio of technology and data partners.
- Develop positioning and messaging for integrated solutions and joint value propositions.
- Plan and execute co-marketing programs (campaigns, webinars, joint customer stories, executive events, thought leadership).
- Partner with Product and Product Marketing to shape integration launches.
- Partner with Partnerships, Demand Generation, and Sales to move plans from strategy through execution.
- Enable Harvey sellers and partner-facing teams with messaging frameworks, integration collateral, proof points, co-sell materials.
- Track and report on partner-sourced/influenced pipeline, integration adoption, program performance.

### What You Have (verbatim)
- "6-8+ years of experience in partner marketing, product marketing, or a related go-to-market role in B2B SaaS."
- Proven experience building and executing joint go-to-market programs with strategic technology or data partners in an enterprise sales motion.
- Strong understanding of ecosystem business models.
- Demonstrated ability to translate technical integrations into clear customer value.
- Proven ability to operate cross-functionally and influence stakeholders across multiple organizations without direct authority.
- Experience marketing to legal, financial services, consulting, or other high-touch professional audiences is a plus.
