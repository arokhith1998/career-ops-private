The Deal Desk Analyst supports the EMEA and East Coast sales teams, providing expert guidance on deal structuring, pricing, quote creation, negotiation, and contract review. Serves as a strategic advisor to sales, collaborating across departments, working closely with senior leadership.

**Responsibilities:**
- Primary contact and advisor to sales on pricing, deal economics, structure
- Collaborate with finance, legal, sales, operations on complex transactions
- Manage daily deal processes (quoting, commercial terms approval)
- Deliver regular training to sales on deal structure/pricing
- Define and monitor key metrics to evaluate deal performance

**Requirements:**
- "Bachelor's degree in finance, economics, accounting, or related field preferred"
- "3-5 years of experience in pricing, finance, deal desk, or analytics"
- Proficiency with Excel, Google Sheets, Salesforce, Salesforce CPQ
- Knowledge of SaaS subscription models and revenue recognition
