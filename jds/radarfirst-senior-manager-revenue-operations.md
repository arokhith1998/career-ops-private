Senior Manager of Revenue Operations We are seeking a commercially minded, analytical, and hands-on Senior Revenue Operations Manager to strengthen our go-to-market operations. This role combines deal strategy and execution, annual sales planning, revenue systems, forecasting, and practical AI-powered automation. Reporting to the CFO, this individual will partner closely with Sales, Finance, Legal, Marketing, and Customer Success to improve commercial outcomes, build reliable operating processes, and turn revenue data into actionable decisions. The ideal candidate can move comfortably from negotiating an enterprise deal to modeling quotas, improving Salesforce workflows, and building an AI agent that eliminates manual work. Essential Responsibilities & Duties Revenue Operations Leadership: Own cross-functional operating initiatives across the revenue lifecycle, establishing scalable processes, clear ownership, and reliable execution across GTM teams. Deal Desk & Commercial Negotiations: Serve as a commercial negotiation focal for new business, renewals, and expansions; partner with Sales and Legal to structure workable terms, resolve exceptions, and accelerate signature. Pricing & Deal Strategy: Develop creative, financially sound pricing and packaging proposals; assess discounting, multi-year structures, uplifts, and commercial tradeoffs while protecting revenue and customer relationships. Order & Approval Governance: Manage quote-to-cash and order approval workflows, including CPQ controls, nonstandard terms, approval routing, and coordination with Finance and Legal. Territory, Quota & Capacity Planning: Design and deploy territories, coverage models, headcount scenarios, annual quotas, and performance targets in partnership with Sales leadership and Finance. Sales Compensation: Develop, implement, and evaluate incentive compensation plans, commission mechanics, attainment reporting, and plan communications. Forecasting & Revenue Planning: Lead pipeline and bookings forecasting, forecast inspection, scenario modeling, and quarterly business planning; partner with Finance on annual revenue budgets and company-wide bookings and quota targets. Reporting & Revenue Intelligence: Build executive dashboards and recurring operational reporting on pipeline generation, conversion, velocity, attainment, retention, and forecast accuracy; translate findings into decisions. GTM Systems & Salesforce: Oversee the Salesforce instance, administration, data governance, reporting, CPQ, user enablement, and systems roadmap; coordinate integrations and external technical partners. GTM Technology & Integrations: Optimize connected platforms such as Apollo, ZoomInfo, Marketo, and other revenue tools; improve data flow, reliability, adoption, and end-to-end workflows. AI Agents & Workflow Automation: Identify high-value use cases and design, build, deploy, and refine practical AI agents and automations for research, enrichment, signal detection, reporting, and revenue processes using tools such as Claude and Prismatic. AI Operational Infrastructure: Establish repeatable agent workflows, access and data controls, integration handoffs, documentation, testing, monitoring, and human review so automations remain dependable and useful. Operating Cadence & Collaboration: Support weekly sales leadership reviews, metrics decks, QBRs, and executive planning; coordinate priorities across Sales, Finance, Legal, Marketing, and Customer Success. Continuous Improvement: Identify process bottlenecks, simplify manual work, and lead implementation of sustainable improvements across the commercial organization. Management of Sales Operations Analyst Support and mentor team member to ensure personal growth Ensure data integrity and actionable reporting and metric insight. Qualifications 5+ years in Revenue Operations, Sales Operations, Commercial Operations, or related B2B SaaS roles; experience owning complex cross-functional initiatives. Strong commercial judgment and hands-on experience with enterprise Deal Desk, SaaS pricing, renewals, and negotiation support. Experience designing territories, quotas, sales capacity models, and incentive compensation plans. Advanced skills in forecasting, pipeline analytics, revenue budgeting, and Excel or Google Sheets financial modeling. Practical Salesforce and CPQ expertise, including administration, reporting, process design, data quality, and connected GTM systems. Experience building workflow automations or AI-enabled processes, or demonstrated ability to learn and implement tools such as Claude, Prismatic, and integrations.
