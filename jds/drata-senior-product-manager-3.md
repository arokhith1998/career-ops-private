## Job Description

Drata is building the trust layer between great companies - automating compliance, managing risk, and helping organizations prove trust continuously as they scale. We're Dratanauts: a global crew of 600+ professionals united by a culture that rewards integrity, ownership, and raising the bar, no matter where in the world we're working from.

Why Join the Drata Team?
At Drata, you're not maintaining legacy compliance software - you're building the agentic AI platform defining what trust looks like for the next generation of companies. Here's what makes the work itself worth showing up for:

- Problems without a playbook: You'll work at the edge of AI and security, building agentic governance, continuous compliance, and real-time trust verification to solve problems that don't have an established answer yet. You're writing it as you go.
- Real ownership, not just process: Our values center on owning outcomes and raising the bar, not checking boxes. You're expected to have opinions and back them.
- A seat at the table: Your perspective is unique and valued. Open debate and diverse viewpoints are built into how decisions actually get made here, at every level.
- Growth at rocketship speed: Drata is scaling fast, which means scope grows fast too. High performers get more ownership, visibility, and experience.
- A crew, not just coworkers: Dratanauts consistently describe a "come as you are" culture with sharp, curious people, the kind of team that makes hard problems genuinely fun to solve.

Job Summary:

The Senior Product Manager is a key position to support our growth, and we are looking for an experienced product manager to join us! As the Senior Product Manager, you will be working cross-functionally to design, build, and roll-out products and features that align with Drata's strategy and vision. A top-notch senior product manager will have worked on numerous projects and have a firm grasp of how to satisfy consumers' needs. Ideal candidates will be equally analytical and creative, and able to justify decisions to a range of stakeholders. This is an ideal position for someone who thrives in changing environments and can build the roadmap to create innovative product offerings.

What you'll do:

- Define roadmap and requirements to solve big customers problems in the Enterprise space
- Define an MVP experience and quickly learn and iterate based on feedback
- Form hypotheses and run experiments using lean methodologies
- Partner closely with engineering and design teams to define, build, and maintain products/features which are critical to growth and retention
- Partner closely with cross-functional teams on release plans and documentation to deliver value to our customers
- Proactively partner across teams to anticipate and address risks and dependencies
- Communicate progress and plan to leadership, peers, etc.

What you'll bring:

- 5+ years designing and delivering world-class software products in a Product Management role
- Deep customer empathy
- Passion for identifying and solving problems with the ability to conduct effective customer interviews
- Capable of breaking down a big vision into small deliverables
- Ruthless prioritization of the backlog
- Self-starter with an entrepreneurial mindset and the drive to pursue opportunities beyond current constraints
- Exceptional collaboration skills working with PMs, engineers, designers, marketers, and customer success teams
- Growth mindset with a desire for continuous improvement
- Strong verbal and written communication skills
- Strong analytical skills paired with a data-informed mindset
- Experience launching a new product offering from 0-1 strongly preferred
- Experience building complex products for Enterprise customers strongly preferred
- Experience in the Procurement, Vendors, Third-Party Risk or Risk space strongly preferred

How we support you:
At Drata, our people are our strongest advantage, and we prove it with support that exceeds industry standards.

- Shared Success: We provide stock equity to ensure that as the company grows, you share directly in that success.
- Health & Wellness: Up to 100% employer-paid premiums for medical, dental, and vision coverage for employees and their dependents, along with comprehensive wellness benefits and healthcare concierge services.
- Financial Well-being: A comprehensive suite of financial benefits, including a 401(k) plan, company-paid life and disability insurance, tax-advantaged spending accounts, and discounted voluntary offerings.
- Family Support: Paid Parental Leave policy after six months of employment, Kindbody fertility and family-building benefits, and dedicated leave specialists.
- Growth & Development: Generous annual stipends for both professional and personal development, plus internal learning opportunities.
- Time Off & Flexibility: Flexible vacation policy, paid holidays, and other perks to recharge.
