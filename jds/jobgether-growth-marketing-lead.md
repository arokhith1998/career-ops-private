## Job Description (full, verbatim)
This position is listed on behalf of a partner company, who manages all applications and next steps. Our partner is looking for a Growth Marketing Lead based in United States.

We are looking for a strategic and hands-on Growth Marketing Lead to build and scale a high-performing marketing engine that transforms market awareness into measurable business growth. In this senior role, you will own the full demand generation strategy, driving qualified pipeline through inbound and outbound campaigns, growth initiatives, SEO/SEM, social media, and marketing automation. You will identify high-impact opportunities, experiment with new acquisition channels, and continuously optimize performance using data-driven insights. Working closely with marketing, sales, product, and operations teams, you will ensure consistent messaging and effective campaign execution across every touchpoint. This is an opportunity to establish scalable marketing infrastructure, strengthen market presence, and directly influence revenue growth within a fast-moving, early-stage technology environment.

Accountabilities
You will lead the development and execution of comprehensive growth marketing strategies, ensuring that every initiative contributes to predictable, measurable, and sustainable pipeline growth.
- Own demand generation strategy and execution across inbound and outbound programs, maximizing qualified pipeline contribution.
- Develop and scale growth marketing initiatives by identifying high-potential acquisition channels, running experiments, and expanding successful campaigns.
- Lead SEO and SEM strategies, including keyword research, organic search optimization, paid search campaigns, landing page improvements, and performance reporting.
- Drive inbound marketing through content-led campaigns, gated resources, webinars, and lead nurturing programs.
- Build and strengthen social media and community engagement, particularly on LinkedIn and relevant industry platforms, converting audience engagement into qualified prospects.
- Manage marketing automation through HubSpot, developing workflows, lead scoring models, and nurturing sequences that accelerate prospects through the funnel.
- Collaborate with Marketing Operations to improve attribution models, funnel reporting, and pipeline dashboards.
- Partner with Product Marketing to develop compelling campaign messaging and maintain consistent positioning across all marketing channels.
- Monitor campaign performance, identify optimization opportunities, and ensure marketing activities deliver measurable business results.

Requirements
The ideal candidate combines strong B2B demand generation expertise with a growth-oriented mindset, excellent analytical capabilities, and the ability to execute independently in a fast-paced startup environment.
- At least 6 years of B2B marketing experience, including 3-4 years in demand generation, growth marketing, or a comparable position.
- Previous experience in cybersecurity, identity security, or technical SaaS environments is highly desirable.
- Proven ability to build and scale demand generation programs that deliver measurable pipeline and revenue impact.
- Hands-on expertise across SEO/SEM, paid social, email marketing, inbound content, and marketing automation.
- Advanced HubSpot knowledge, including the ability to build complex workflows, lead scoring models, and automated nurturing programs from scratch.
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to interpret funnel metrics, evaluate campaign performance, and identify conversion bottlenecks.
