## Role overview
Owns product strategy and delivery for software that runs Natera's CGP labs, covering extraction, library prep, sequencing, and assay QC. Works closely with Lab Operations, R&D, Automation, Bioinformatics, and Engineering.

## Key responsibilities
- Set product strategy and roadmap for lab execution software, tied to Oncology goals (launch timelines, volume, turnaround time, COGS).
- Build reusable, configurable platform capabilities across assays and labs.
- Drive AI-forward features that reduce manual touchpoints and surface exceptions early.
- Own end-to-end delivery of CGP assay launches, from requirements through hypercare.
- Write epics, user stories, and acceptance criteria; prioritize backlogs with engineering leaders.
- Define and track KPIs such as turnaround time, rework rates, and adoption.
- Work with Quality and Regulatory on CLIA/CAP, 21 CFR Part 11, design control, and LDT/IVD requirements.
- Spend time on the lab floor and coordinate with adjacent product areas.

## Required qualifications
- Experience shipping complex, workflow-heavy software for high-complexity labs (LIMS/LES/LIS), healthcare, or other regulated domains.
- Experience taking products from zero to one and scaling them.
- Understanding of NGS lab workflows; oncology or CGP experience strongly preferred.
- Data-driven, comfortable with SQL and dashboards.
- Agile (Scrum/Kanban) fluency with Jira and Confluence.
- Strong stakeholder management and communication skills.

## Nice to have
Tissue or liquid CGP assay experience, histology or IHC workflows, lab automation and instrument integration, AI/ML product experience.
