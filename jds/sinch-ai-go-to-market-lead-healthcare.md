## Full JD text (verbatim)

About Sinch
Sinch is pioneering how the world communicates. More than 150,000 businesses, including many of the world's largest technology companies, rely on Sinch's Customer Communications Cloud to engage customers across messaging, voice, email, and identity verification. As artificial intelligence transforms healthcare, providers, payers, digital health companies, and healthcare technology organizations are increasingly deploying AI agents to improve patient engagement, streamline administrative workflows, enhance access to care, and support operational efficiency.

The Opportunity
We are seeking an AI Go-to-Market Lead, Healthcare to help define and scale Sinch's position at the intersection of AI, healthcare, and customer communications. This is a highly strategic and customer-facing role responsible for developing healthcare industry expertise, shaping go-to-market initiatives, supporting complex enterprise opportunities, and building strategic partnerships across the AI and healthcare ecosystem. This role sits alongside Sinch's regional sales organizations as a specialist overlay function. You will not own a direct sales quota or carry end-to-end account management responsibility. This is a commercial and advisory role, not an engineering position. You will not be expected to build AI models or write software code.

**What You'll Do**
- Develop and articulate Sinch's AI-focused healthcare strategy and industry point of view.
- Identify and prioritize AI-driven patient engagement, member engagement, and healthcare communications use cases.
- Partner with regional sales teams to support strategic healthcare opportunities involving AI-enabled experiences.
- Build industry-specific sales plays, messaging frameworks, customer success stories, and enablement content.
- Engage healthcare executives responsible for digital transformation, patient experience, innovation, revenue cycle, member services, operations, contact center transformation, and AI initiatives.
- Collaborate with product, marketing, and sales leadership to align healthcare market opportunities with Sinch's platform capabilities.
- Establish and grow relationships with healthcare technology providers, AI vendors, cloud providers, consulting firms, system integrators, and industry influencers.
- Represent Sinch at healthcare, AI, and digital transformation conferences and events.
- Monitor market trends, regulatory developments, competitive activity, and AI adoption patterns across healthcare.
- Help position and differentiate Sinch's Conversation API, Verification API, Voice API, and Email API within healthcare engagement and workflow ecosystems.

**Basic Qualifications**
- Bachelor's degree in Business, Healthcare Administration, Marketing, Technology, Life Sciences, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience.
- 7+ years of experience in healthcare, health technology, healthcare SaaS, enterprise software, cloud technology, digital health, or related commercial roles.
- Experience working with or selling into healthcare providers, health systems, payers, healthcare technology companies, life sciences organizations, or digital health companies.
- Experience supporting complex enterprise sales cycles involving multiple stakeholders and executive buyers.
- Demonstrated success developing go-to-market strategies, vertical market initiatives, sales plays, solution positioning, or business development programs.
- Strong communication, presentation, and executive engagement skills.
- Willingness to travel as needed.

**Preferred Qualifications**
- 10+ years of experience in healthcare technology, digital health, healthcare consulting, enterprise software, cloud platforms, or AI solutions.
- Deep understanding of AI applications within healthcare, patient engagement, member services, and operational automation.
- Knowledge of healthcare industry regulations and compliance requirements, including HIPAA.
- Experience with healthcare technology platforms including Epic, Cerner/Oracle Health, athenahealth, Salesforce Health Cloud, Microsoft Cloud for Healthcare, AWS for Healthcare, or Google Cloud Healthcare solutions.
- Existing network of relationships within healthcare providers, payers, health technology companies, digital health organizations, or industry associations.

**Benefits:** comprehensive market competitive medical/dental/vision plans with telehealth access; free virtual counselling (EAP); Roth and Pre-tax 401(k) with employer match; generous paid time off; paid parental leave and family planning support; flexible remote work; paid volunteer time.

This role is primarily remote, but candidates must reside near one of our hub locations for occasional collaboration: Atlanta GA, Chicago IL, Nashville TN, Denver CO, Seattle WA, New York NY or San Francisco/Bay Area CA.
