About The Role
This is not a traditional Executive Assistant role focused only on calendar management. We are looking for someone who can function as an extension of the Founder's Office by ensuring smooth coordination across teams, tracking priorities, managing follow-ups, organizing meetings, and supporting strategic execution.

The ideal candidate should be detail-oriented, highly organized, trustworthy, proactive, and capable of handling multiple priorities in a dynamic business environment.

Key Roles & Responsibilities

1. Calendar & Meeting Management

- Manage leadership calendars, appointments, meetings, and scheduling priorities.
- Coordinate internal and external meetings with clients, partners, vendors, and stakeholders.
- Ensure meeting agendas, documents, presentations, and discussion points are prepared in advance.
- Avoid scheduling conflicts and ensure smooth coordination of leadership commitments.
- Send reminders and maintain meeting timelines.

2. Minutes of Meeting (MoM) & Follow-Up Tracking

- Attend meetings and prepare detailed Minutes of Meeting (MoM).
- Capture action items, timelines, responsibilities, and key decisions.
- Maintain trackers for pending and completed action items.
- Follow up with internal teams and stakeholders to ensure timely execution.
- Escalate delays or blockers to leadership when required.

3. Founder's Office Coordination

- Coordinate with departments including HR, Finance, Operations, Marketing, Technology, and Business teams.
- Track strategic priorities and ensure timely execution of leadership initiatives.
- Prepare daily, weekly, and monthly status reports for leadership reviews.
- Maintain visibility on important deadlines and deliverables.

4. Communication & Documentation Management

- Draft professional emails, meeting summaries, business communications, and follow-up notes.
- Organize and maintain confidential documents, reports, presentations, and business records.
- Maintain structured folders and documentation systems for easy access.
- Coordinate communication between leadership and internal/external stakeholders.

5. Travel & Event Coordination

- Manage travel bookings including flights, hotels, transportation, and itineraries.
- Coordinate logistics for conferences, meetings, workshops, and business events.
- Support event planning, guest coordination, and vendor communication.
- Ensure smooth execution of leadership travel and event schedules.

6. HR & Recruitment Coordination

- Coordinate interview scheduling and candidate communication.
- Maintain hiring trackers and recruitment updates.
- Support onboarding coordination for leadership-driven hiring requirements.
- Follow up with HR teams regarding offers, joining dates, and documentation.

7. Finance & Compliance Coordination

- Coordinate with finance teams, auditors, consultants, and vendors for documentation.
- Track invoices, approvals, reimbursements, and compliance-related activities.
- Maintain trackers for important submissions and deadlines.
- Ensure timely coordination for leadership approvals.

8. Strategic Execution Support

- Assist leadership in tracking business priorities and strategic initiatives.
- Coordinate cross-functional execution and ensure accountability.
- Support presentations, reports, dashboards, and business reviews.
- Help streamline workflows and improve operational efficiency.
