## Full JD text

Our Internal Data team is on a mission to power decisions and applications across MongoDB, oriented around the business areas of GTM, Product & Technology, Finance and HR. With this role, we are focusing on delivery of scalable, insightful, and impactful data products for our Product and Technology (P&T) teams - the org that builds MongoDB products for our external customers. These products drive smarter decisions, automate workflows, enable AI agents and deliver actionable insights that accelerate growth.

As a Senior Data Product Manager on our Data team -- which includes Data Pipeline/Platform Engineering, Data Architecture, and Data Governance -- you will lead efforts to build, enhance, and scale the data products MongoDB employees use every day across a growing, high-impact company. You'll deliver data products like data pipelines, datasets, reports/dashboards, APIs, ML models, playbooks, and frameworks while enhancing more traditional data management tools/services to power both AI and BI.

In this role you will also have the opportunity to drive the vision and strategy for internal data as a product, with a focus on telemetry and product adoption/engagement/churn signals. By developing tools and systems that provide deep insights into how customers use or don't use MongoDB's products, you will empower teams to understand customer behaviors and improve the end-user experience.

This role can be based out of our Palo Alto, San Francisco, or New York City office or remotely in the East Coast region.

Responsibilities

Lead Product Strategy and Ownership
- Define product vision, strategy, and roadmap for MongoDB's Data teams and products
- Evangelize the importance of data-as-a-product by creating repeatable and scalable data solutions tailored to business needs
- Balance quick wins for immediate needs with longer-term innovation aligned with strategic goals, particularly integrating modern data frameworks such as lakehouses and medallion data architecture (bronze, silver, gold sets)

Partner with Product and Technology (P&T) Teams
- Collaborate deeply with P&T teams to ensure data products address their unique challenges and workflows
- Work closely with diverse personas -- analysts, engineers, P&T teams, and senior leadership -- to capture detailed business processes, user stories, and pain points
- Serve as a trusted partner and advocate for P&T stakeholders by prioritizing solutions that have direct, measurable business impact

Performance and Market Analysis
- Define metrics and KPIs to measure success and iterate on continuous improvement
- Track innovation and emerging trends in modern data platforms, scalable data engineering, and advanced analytics tooling

Stakeholder Enablement
- Build user-friendly documentation, guides, and onboarding materials for data products to drive adoption
- Partner with training teams and change management functions

Adoption and Evangelization
- Announce new product features, datasets, and tools with tailored product marketing strategies
- Champion the value of data products in solving business questions

Agile Product Management
- Own and drive agile processes, including sprint planning, backlog grooming, and retrospectives
- Balance innovation with tackling tech debt

Requirements

- 5+ years of hands-on product management experience, with at least two years focused on data products or platforms at a modern, fast-paced, high-growth company
- Experience with stakeholders in advanced engineering organizations is critical; strong understanding of software development lifecycle processes
- Strong technical foundation in data engineering, analytics, and data products
- Deep familiarity with modern data platforms, scalable architecture (e.g., lakehouses, medallion data)
- Proven ability to prioritize roadmaps in line with P&T operational needs and long-term goals
- Adaptable and resourceful problem-solver
- Expertise in identifying measurable outcomes, tracking success metrics, and iteratively improving product impact
- Outstanding communication skills

What Sets You Apart
- Experience uncovering and driving insights with data science, BI, analytics, or data engineering tools
- Proven track record of creating engaging product enablement materials
- Knowledge around metadata management
- Up-to-date awareness in data market trends

About MongoDB: MongoDB is built for change, empowering our customers and our people to innovate at the speed of the market. We have redefined the data platform for the AI era.
