Overview

Due to growth, we are adding a Manager, Pricing and Underwriting to our team .

The Manager, Pricing and Underwriting supports and manages core functions across the full scope of pricing, underwriting, and risk analysis. This position reports to the Director, Pricing and Underwriting and will provide leadership to Supervisors and Analysts. The Manager is responsible for developing pricing recommendations, evaluating financial risk, maintaining underwriting models, and supporting pricing decisions for new business, renewal business, client retention, and RFP opportunities.

Is this you? Find out more below!
