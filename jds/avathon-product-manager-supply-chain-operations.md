Who We Are & Why Join Us

Avathon is the leading Industrial AI autonomy platform, helping customers across heavy industries -- energy, mining, manufacturing, aerospace, defense, and logistics -- accelerate the journey toward autonomous operations. Our platform is built on a Computational Knowledge Graph foundation that contextualizes and connects operational data across siloed systems, bringing together time series, structured, unstructured, and machine vision data to power AI-driven applications in asset performance management, supply chain intelligence, visual AI, and global trade management. With capabilities spanning digital twins, normal behavior modeling, natural language processing, and computer vision, Avathon delivers real-time predictive intelligence and agentic decision-making at industrial scale.

Cutting-Edge AI Innovation -- Join a team at the forefront of AI, developing groundbreaking solutions that shape the future. High-Growth Environment -- Thrive in a fast-scaling startup where agility, collaboration, and rapid professional growth are the norm. Meaningful Impact -- Work on AI-driven projects that drive real change across industries and improve lives.

Learn more at: avathon.com

We are looking for a Product Manager, Supply Chain & Operations to help define and shape Avathon’s next generation of Physical AI products for global supply chains, logistics, transportation, and industrial operations. You will own product strategy for cutting-edge capabilities spanning autonomous planning, multi-objective optimization, transportation and network intelligence, inventory and production optimization, decision automation, and agentic AI. Working alongside AI scientists, product engineers, forward-deployed teams, and industry leaders, you will translate complex real-world challenges into scalable AI products that continuously reason, replan, optimize, and act transforming how the world’s most complex supply chains and logistics networks operate.

What You'll Work On

- Define product vision, strategy, roadmap, and priorities for Avathon’s supply chain & operations portfolio
- Define product requirements, user journeys, APIs, workflows, agents, and success metrics
- Work directly with customers and industry experts to understand operational challenges, validate product concepts, and prioritize product capabilities
- Translate customer and business requirements into clear product requirements, workflows, user stories, KPIs, and acceptance criteria
- Track industry trends, competitive offerings, customer priorities, and emerging technologies to identify new product opportunities
- Define and monitor product KPIs and use customer and performance data to continuously improve the product.
- Demonstrate new products to customers, partners, and executives
- Work with applied science, Product and Forward-Deployed Engineering teams to take products from idea → prototype → deployment → scale
- Partner with Forward Deployed Engineering to turn successful customer deployments into repeatable platform and product capabilities

What We’re Looking For

- MBA required from top-tier universities with a globally recognized supply chain, logistics and operations program
- 4+ years of relevant experience in product management, supply chain strategy, operations, or management consulting
- Strong understanding of supply chain planning and operational processes, including demand planning, supply planning, inventory, logistics and manufacturing
- Experience defining and deploying products incorporating AI, machine learning, optimization, or advanced analytics preferred
- Strong ability to translate complex business problems into clear product requirements
- Excellent analytical, communication, and stakeholder-management skills
- Experience working directly with enterprise customers and senior business stakeholders
- Strong understanding of industrial and B2B operating environments preferred
- Ability to operate with significant ownership in a fast-moving technology company

What You'll Learn

Within your first few years at Avathon, our goal is for you to develop an unusually powerful combination of skills: Deep technical fluency in AI, exceptional product judgment, understanding of complex industrial systems, and the ability to take ambiguous problems from zero to production. That combination can be difficult to acquire anywhere else early in a career - If you want to help build the intelligence layer for the physical world, come build it with us.

Compensation & Benefits

- California base salary range: $150,000 – $190,000 USD (based on experience)
- Equity in the form of stock options
- Comprehensive health, dental, and vision coverage
- 401(k) participation

- Ongoing learning and development opportunities
