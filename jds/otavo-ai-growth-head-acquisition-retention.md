Title: Growth Head - Acquisition & Retention

About the Hiring Company
(Name will be revealed to shortlisted candidates only after evaluating fit)
A corporate-backed media and entertainment company - one of India's leading FM networks - operating a broadcast radio network, digital podcasting and a content studio, and growing its digital entertainment business across India and international markets.

The Role
We are looking for a Growth Head to lead user acquisition and retention for the digital entertainment business across India and international markets.
You will own growth end-to-end, working closely with Product and Content, and build the playbook for international expansion.

Key Responsibilities:
- Own growth across paid acquisition, organic channels, partnerships, influencers and referrals.
- Drive retention and engagement through lifecycle campaigns, notifications, personalization and community features.
- Manage unit economics, experimentation and forecasting.
- Collaborate closely with Product and Content teams.
- Develop international expansion playbooks and adapt growth strategies for different markets.

What We're Looking For
- Owned full-funnel user acquisition across paid channels, organic, partnerships, influencers and referrals.
- Drove user retention and engagement through lifecycle campaigns, notifications or personalization.
- Proven track record managing growth metrics such as CAC, LTV, retention cohorts and payback period.
- Built or executed growth strategies for international markets.

Must Haves:
- Experience in a digital entertainment, content or consumer app business.
- Experience running growth experimentation and forecasting.
- Experience building community features as a retention lever.

Why This Opportunity?
This is an opportunity to own growth for a digital entertainment business backed by an established media brand, and to build its expansion into international markets.

About the Hiring Agency
Otavo.ai is a global AI-driven platform focused on senior recruitment for roles ranging from 40L to 2 Cr in compensation. Founded by serial entrepreneur Mahak Garg, a Stanford MBA and Forbes Under 30 honoree, the company builds on her prior experience scaling Udayy with leading global investors. Otavo.ai currently partners with 30+ fast growing companies worldwide to address their senior talent hiring needs. The platform maintains a curated network of 10,000+ high-performing candidates with 5-20 years of experience, including alumni from top global schools and leaders across marketing, product, and strategy in major firms and startups. For each open role, Otavo.ai provides a small set of pre-vetted, well-matched candidates rather than long lists of low-fit profiles, enhancing hiring efficiency and quality.
