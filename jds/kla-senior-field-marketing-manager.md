## Job Description (full, verbatim)
The Senior Field Marketing Manager is responsible for driving customer penetration, product adoption, and market share growth across strategic accounts, regions, and technology segments. The role serves as the primary link between customers and the division, translating customer requirements, competitive dynamics, and market opportunities into actionable strategies that accelerate business growth.

This individual partners closely with Product Marketing, Sales, Applications Engineering, Service, and Business Unit teams to develop and execute customer engagement strategies, remove barriers to adoption, and ensure successful commercialization of new and existing products. The role combines deep technical credibility, business acumen, and customer-facing leadership to influence both customer decisions and internal investment priorities.

Key Responsibilities (per posting)
- Develop and execute customer penetration and adoption strategies for assigned strategic accounts or regions.
- Drive successful adoption of new products, technologies, and workflows (Product Adoption & Commercialization).
- Track adoption milestones and proactively address barriers that may delay customer deployment.
- Escalate and resolve issues that impact customer satisfaction, product adoption, or revenue growth.
- Develop business cases to support customer investments and adoption programs.
- Track progress against penetration, adoption, and market share objectives.

Qualifications
- 8-15 years of semiconductor industry experience.
- Experience managing strategic customer engagements and product adoption programs.
