## Full JD text

Vercel seeks a Product Manager to own the compute platform including Fluid compute, Vercel Functions, Sandbox, and related services. The role involves setting direction for how developers configure, scale, and run code on Vercel across single functions, multi-service architectures, and agent workloads. The position reports to the Product Lead and requires San Francisco presence with in-office anchor days on Monday, Tuesday, and Friday if based within commuting distance of company offices (SF, NY, London, or Berlin); otherwise fully remote.

Responsibilities include: owning product vision, strategy, and multi-year roadmap; defining AI and agent workload support; shaping developer experience regarding resource models and APIs; partnering with Engineering on performance and reliability; defining pricing; setting compute primitives; working with Enterprise customers on infrastructure requirements.

Required qualifications: infrastructure or developer platform product shipping experience; technical depth in concurrency, cold starts, isolation models, cost/performance tradeoffs; platform ownership experience; AI/agent workload experience; Enterprise customer engagement; analytics-driven decision-making.

Bonus qualifications: cloud provider or serverless/container/edge compute platform experience; engineering background; multi-runtime platform experience; sandboxing and secure code execution expertise.
