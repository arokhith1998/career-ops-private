The opportunity

We’re seeking a Product Manager with strong technical fluency who can operate at the intersection of client needs, business strategy, and engineering execution. This role is responsible for defining the why and what of our products while also shaping the how by partnering closely with engineering, architecture, and design; someone who can both define market-driven roadmaps and collaborate deeply with engineering on technical specs, data flows, and architecture discussions.

You’ll own the product roadmap end-to-end: from discovery, to translating requirements into detailed specifications, to guiding teams through delivery and adoption.

This role requires a balance of strategic thinking, user empathy, and hands-on execution.

Your Key Responsibilities

- Define product vision, success metrics, and roadmap in collaboration with stakeholders.

- Own the product backlog; translate business requirements into detailed epics, user stories, and technical specifications.

- Collaborate with engineers and architects on APIs, integrations, data flows, and system scalability.

- Validate concepts and features through user feedback and iterative testing.

- Prioritize features based on client value, business impact, and technical feasibility.

- Work closely with engineering, design, and architecture to ensure timely and high-quality delivery.

- Partner with cross-functional teams on go-to-market plans and adoption strategies.

- Communicate progress, risks, and decisions with clarity across levels.

To qualify you must have

- Bachelor’s Degree in a related field.

- 6-8+ years of product management work experience with a proven track record of successfully launching and managing products throughout their lifecycle.

- Demonstrated experience in a product management role, preferably with a focus on AI solutions or technology-driven products.

- Strong understanding of software development lifecycle, system design, and modern tech stacks (APIs, cloud platforms, data pipelines).

- Foundational AI knowledge and experience building AI-enabled products

Ideally, you'll also have

- Experience in AI/ML, LLMs, agentic strategies, SaaS, and product management tools such as ADO, JIRA, Trello, or similar systems.

- Familiarity with product management frameworks (JTBD, RICE, OKRs, Lean, Agile, ShapeUp).
