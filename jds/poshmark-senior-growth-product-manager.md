## Full Job Description

### About Poshmark
Poshmark is the leading fashion marketplace where style comes alive through discovery, self-expression, and human connection. Powered by a vibrant community of 165 million members.

Poshmark is seeking a Senior Growth Product Manager to drive high-impact, data-informed growth across the marketplace. This role will initially focus on new user onboarding and early lifecycle experiences, with the opportunity to expand into broader growth opportunities over time, including engagement, retention, and monetization.

You will be responsible for identifying and scaling the highest leverage growth opportunities across the user journey. This includes optimizing first touch experiences, accelerating activation, and improving long-term retention.

### Responsibilities
- Own and improve key growth funnels across the user lifecycle, with an initial focus on new user onboarding and early activation for buyers and sellers
- Identify and prioritize high-impact growth opportunities using quantitative analysis, user research, and experimentation
- Lead a high-velocity experimentation program across onboarding, discovery, engagement, and retention surfaces
- Design and analyze experiments with strong statistical rigor, and translate results into clear product decisions and roadmap direction
- Conduct and synthesize user research to understand motivations, behaviors, and drop-off drivers across the funnel
- Apply principles of customer psychology, habit formation, and behavioral economics to inform product design and optimization
- Define and maintain core growth metrics, dashboards, and KPI frameworks
- Partner closely with design, engineering, data, and marketing
- Communicate strategy, insights, and trade-offs clearly to senior leadership

### 6-Month Accomplishments
Audit the new user journey; establish activation framework/metrics/dashboards; launch and iterate on onboarding/activation experiments; define activation milestones and roadmap.

### 12+ Month Accomplishments
Deliver measurable improvements in activation, retention, engagement; expand ownership beyond onboarding; establish experimentation engine; improve time to first listing/purchase/offer; develop and socialize a growth playbook.

### Required Qualifications
- 6-10 years of experience in product management, with a significant portion dedicated to growth-specific functions.
- Proven track record of driving product-led growth in a consumer or marketplace environment
- Strong analytical skills with experience in experimentation, funnel analysis, and data-driven decision making
- Ability to operate across a wide range of problem spaces, from onboarding to retention and beyond
- Experience applying user research and behavioral insights to product design
- Excellent communication skills
- High ownership mindset in ambiguous, fast-paced environments
