About The Role

The Developer Platform is an embedded platform-as-a-service (PaaS) built on highly scalable cloud backend infrastructure and AI capabilities, enabling enterprise applications, AI agents, and core integrations to run securely and reliably.

As a Senior Product Manager (P4) on the Developer Core Services team, you will serve as the strategic product lead responsible for building, scaling, and advancing our platform telemetry, logging, and observability foundation. You will lead cross-functional execution across backend engineering teams, drive architectural evolutions, and deliver high-throughput observability pipelines, OpenTelemetry (OTEL) integrations, enterprise logging systems (e.g., Splunk), and infrastructure telemetry APIs that underpin the entire developer experience.

Key Responsibilities -

- Strategic Observability Roadmap & Telemetry Ownership: Define, own, and maintain a multi-quarter product roadmap for platform observability, centralized logging, and metrics gathering—treating internal telemetry architecture as a product to drive system visibility, real-time monitoring, performance analysis, and platform resilience.
- Scalable Telemetry & Logging Pipelines: Architect resilient, high-availability telemetry collection pipelines and standards using OpenTelemetry (OTEL) to handle high-volume logs, distributed traces, and metrics to seamlessly power enterprise logging (Splunk) and downstream developer analysis.
- Enterprise Monitoring & Dashboarding: Establish and operationalize measurable telemetry KPIs, SLIs/SLOs, and interactive monitoring dashboards in tools like Splunk and cloud-native monitoring tooling to track service reliability, identify performance bottlenecks, and optimize platform adoption.
- Cross-Functional Orchestration: Partner deeply with development teams, system integrators, site reliability engineers (SREs), and internal backend engineering groups to align technical dependencies and unlock platform velocity.
- Multi-Team Delivery Ownership: Operate as a Senior PM lead guiding multiple scrum teams. Drive end-to-end product delivery from technical design and OTEL spikes through testing, rollout, and post-launch optimization.
- Architectural & Operational Governance: Lead technical and business trade-off discussions around legacy logging decommissioning, tenant lifecycle management, security/compliance, and zero-trust telemetry patterns.
- Community & Evangelism: Serve as the primary product advocate across developer events, customer advisory boards, and internal forums, effectively translating complex observability capabilities into clear value for software engineers and platform builders.

About You

- 4+ years of hands-on experience building, scaling, or managing observability platforms, log management systems (e.g., Splunk, ELK), monitoring tooling, or OpenTelemetry (OTEL) telemetry infrastructure.
- 4+ years of experience working cross-functionally across complex technical dependencies with multiple engineering, SRE, and architecture teams.

Preferred Qualifications:

- Deep technical expertise with OpenTelemetry (OTEL) standards, high-volume enterprise logging architectures (e.g., Splunk, Datadog, ELK), and cloud-native observability stacks.
- Strong background in defining telemetry metrics, conducting usability spikes for developer tools, and using operational monitoring data to drive product decisions.
- Experience leveraging AI-powered tools and platforms to automate observability workflows, analyze logs, or enhance developer troubleshooting.
- Demonstrated track record of taking platform observability products from initial concept to commercial availability and continuous post-GA iteration.
