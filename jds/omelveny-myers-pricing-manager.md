The Pricing Manager is responsible for analyzing the economics of both traditional and alternative pricing arrangements, helping to create matter budgets, facilitating the approval of non-standard fee arrangements and working with attorneys and the Client Development team to develop pricing and financial responses to client RFP's.

The salary range in CA for this role is $150,000 - $180,000 and represents the firm’s good faith minimum and maximum range for this role at the time of posting. The actual compensation offered to a candidate will be dependent on a variety of factors including, but not limited to, the candidate’s experience, qualifications, and location.

Responsibilities Include

- Analyze the profitability of both traditional hourly and alternative fee arrangements.

- Partner with attorneys to create matter budgets, including the modeling of representative matters.

- Facilitate and streamline the approval of alternative fee arrangements, matter budgets and rate discounts, including and as part of the new business intake process.

- Work closely with attorneys in the development of pricing and fee arrangements for client proposals.

- Partner with attorneys and Client Development on pricing narratives, value messaging, and negotiation strategy; respond to pricing sections of RFPs and panel submissions.

- Work with Practice Management to educate partners on matter management with a goal of improved profitability.

- Drive, oversee, and optimize the annual rate setting process; perform ad hoc projects and data-driven analysis as directed.

- Performs other duties and responsibilities as assigned.

Qualifications:

- Have a minimum of five years of pricing experience in legal industry.

- Hold a Bachelor’s degree, preferably in Business, Economics, Finance, or Accounting; MBA preferred.

- Possess excellent analytic and problem solving skills, with the ability to think on his/her feet in a fast paced environment, anticipate requirements, and consistently deliver exceptional customer service.

- Have excellent oral and written communication skills with the ability to effectively collaborate with Partners, Practice Management, Finance and Client Development.

- Be organized, able to multi-task, manage time well, and work independently.

- Exhibit superior attention to detail and pride in high quality deliverables.

- Have strong technology skills especially with Microsoft Excel, but including PowerPoint, Word and Outlook. Elite/3E experience is beneficial.
