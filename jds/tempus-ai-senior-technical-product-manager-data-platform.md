## Role summary
Leads on-premises and real-time FHIR and HL7 integrations on the Data Platform team, focusing on secure data exchange infrastructure. Owns the Edge Appliance (on-premises virtual appliance) and Edge API (real-time FHIR/HL7 integration) products.

## Responsibilities
- Define product strategy for secure, scalable, interoperable data exchange; improve ingestion, processing, and delivery for DICOM, HL7, FHIR, and other data types.
- Advocate for integrators, data engineers, and clinical operations users across on-premises and cloud-to-cloud deployments.
- Explore generative AI for integration setup, data mapping, anomaly detection, and troubleshooting.
- Define KPIs for throughput, latency, reliability, adoption, and successful data product creation.
- Own the Edge roadmap, define requirements and launch plans, analyze product data, manage stakeholder expectations, lead change management for production changes affecting integration partners.

## Qualifications
- 5+ years of technical product management experience, at least 2 years in enterprise integration, data platforms, or cloud infrastructure.
- Experience with API products (FHIR or REST), data transfer services (SFTP or S3), or on-premises software like virtual appliances.
- Experience with agile software teams.
- Strong written and verbal communication, able to bridge scientists, clinicians, engineers, and external partners.
- Pluses: healthcare data standards (DICOM, HL7, FHIR), cloud integration (AWS, GCP, Azure), on-premises deployment and connectivity, federated compute.
- Highly desirable: data governance, security, and compliance in regulated industries like healthcare.
