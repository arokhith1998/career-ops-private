## Role summary
Leads and scales paid media for Together AI's Demand Generation and Growth Marketing team, targeting AI engineers.

## Responsibilities
- Own paid media strategy and performance across search, paid social, retargeting, display, and emerging channels.
- Run hands-on campaign execution/optimization (especially Google Ads and paid social) with agency support.
- Plan media investments: forecasting, budget allocation, pacing.
- Measure paid media's impact on acquisition, pipeline, inbound performance, working with RevOps/Analytics.
- Run ongoing experiments across audiences, messaging, creative, bidding, channel mix.
- Coordinate with ABM, Growth, and Field Marketing on integrated campaigns/events.

## Requirements
- 6+ years in B2B paid media, digital, or performance marketing, ideally at a high-growth SaaS or tech company.
- Deep hands-on Google Ads experience.
- Strong paid social experience, particularly LinkedIn.
- Experience managing large budgets and forecasting performance.
- Strong analytics skills, including attribution and incrementality.
