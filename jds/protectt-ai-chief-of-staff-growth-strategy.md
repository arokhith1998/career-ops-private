Position Overview

The Chief of Staff will serve as a strategic partner to the CEO, driving execution of the company's highest-priority initiatives and ensuring alignment across the organization. Combines strategy, operations, governance, and leadership enablement.

Key Responsibilities
- Strategic Planning & CEO's Office: partner with CEO to define/prioritize/execute strategic agenda; drive key initiatives; act as extension of the CEO.
- Business Operations & Governance: own operating cadence (leadership reviews, MBRs, quarterly planning, annual operating plans); establish governance for OKRs/KPIs; identify and resolve risks/bottlenecks.
- Board & Investor Management: lead board materials, investor updates, fundraising presentations; translate performance/market trends into executive narratives; support fundraising, due diligence, strategic partnerships.
- Cross-functional Leadership: drive alignment across functions, resolve dependencies.
- Growth & Revenue Enablement: support revenue growth via business insights, pipeline reviews, pricing strategy, forecasting; partner with GTM teams on scalable processes.
- Data, Insights & Executive Reporting: develop executive dashboards covering revenue, pipeline, customer adoption, retention, product performance, org health.
- Talent & Organizational Effectiveness: support org design, critical hiring, succession planning, culture-building.
- Executive Representation: represent CEO in internal/external meetings, customer engagements, partnerships, industry forums.

Required Skills & Experience
- MBA from a premier institution such as ISB, IIMs (A/B/C/L), or a globally recognized business school.
- Bachelor's degree in Engineering, Technology, Computer Science, or a related discipline.
- 8-12 years of experience across strategy, business operations, consulting, product, revenue operations, growth, or CEO/Founder Office roles.
- Business acumen across commercial, operational, product, and technology priorities.
- Exceptional analytical/problem-solving skills; proficiency in Excel, BI tools, data-driven decision-making.
- Demonstrated ability to lead complex cross-functional initiatives in high-growth, ambiguous environments.
- Outstanding stakeholder management, able to influence senior leaders without direct authority.
- Exceptional written/verbal communication; experience preparing executive presentations, board papers, strategic documents.
- High degree of ownership, intellectual curiosity, bias for action.

Preferred: prior CoS/Founder-Office/BizOps/Strategy/Consulting experience; high-growth SaaS, cybersecurity, enterprise tech, or AI-focused org experience; B2B sales motion/recurring revenue familiarity; fundraising/investor relations/M&A exposure; experience supporting leadership through rapid growth/scaling.

Why Join: Great Place To Work (R) certified; category-defining opportunity (India's first Cybersecurity unicorn aspiration); front-row seat with founder/CEO; meaningful equity with significant stock options; deep-tech + enterprise SaaS combination; high-growth trajectory.
