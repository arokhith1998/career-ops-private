Job Description

Manager – CEO's Office (Growth & Operations)

Mumbai | Leadership Track Role

Reporting to: Founder & CEO

CTC -  24 - 30 lakhs

YANGPOO IS THE MOST TRUSTED EDUCATION MARKETING & CONSULTING COMPANY BASED IN INDIA.

Yangpoo represents and works with institutions and brands including:

- Harvard Business Impact
- Harvard Business School Online
- Harvard Medical School Online
- NMIMS University
- Welingkar Institute of Management
- Leading universities and higher education institutions across India and the GCC

As Yangpoo enters its next phase of growth, international expansion, and operational scale, we are seeking an ambitious and highly capable professional to join the CEO's Office and work directly with the Founder & CEO on strategic growth, execution, business operations, and organizational effectiveness.

The Opportunity

This is not an Executive Assistant role.

This is not a traditional Operations role.

This is a high-visibility, high-impact leadership track position for an individual who wants to work closely with the Founder & CEO and gain exposure to business strategy, growth initiatives, sales execution, partnerships, operational excellence, and leadership decision-making.

The role is ideal for someone who enjoys solving business problems, driving execution, improving processes, and ensuring that strategic priorities get translated into measurable outcomes.

Key Responsibilities

CEO's Office & Strategic Execution

- Work directly with the Founder & CEO on strategic initiatives and business priorities
- Drive execution of key organizational projects and growth initiatives
- Monitor progress of critical business objectives and ensure timely completion
- Prepare leadership briefs, review documents, business updates, and presentations
- Coordinate cross-functional execution across teams

Growth & Business Operations

- Support business growth initiatives across institutional partnerships, executive education, and strategic business verticals
- Track revenue performance, business metrics, and growth opportunities
- Support new market, product, and partnership initiatives
- Identify operational bottlenecks and recommend improvements

Revenue Operations & Business Intelligence

- Build and maintain leadership dashboards and business performance reports
- Track targets, pipeline movement, revenue forecasts, and key business metrics
- Generate actionable insights through business analytics and performance reviews
- Support leadership in business planning and decision-making

Cross-Functional Leadership Support

- Work closely with Sales, Marketing, Finance, Client Success, and Academic Partnership teams
- Drive accountability, follow-ups, and action-point closures
- Ensure smooth execution of key business priorities across functions
- Improve organizational communication and coordination

Process Excellence & Scalability

- Develop and improve business processes, systems, and operating mechanisms
- Support CRM governance and reporting discipline
- Build scalable frameworks to support future growth
- Drive operational excellence across the organization

Ideal Candidate Profile

We are looking for professionals who possess:

- MBA from a reputed institution mandatory
- 8–15 years of overall experience
- Strong business acumen and commercial orientation
- Experience in Growth, Business Operations, Founder’s Office, Strategy, Consulting, Revenue Operations, or Business Excellence roles
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills
- Excellent communication and stakeholder management capabilities
- High ownership mindset and execution discipline
- Ability to thrive in a fast-paced entrepreneurial environment

What Success Looks Like

Within 12–24 months, the successful candidate will become a trusted execution partner to the Founder & CEO, helping drive growth, improve organizational effectiveness, enhance operational discipline, and support Yangpoo's expansion across India and international markets.

Compensation

Competitive and aligned with the candidate's experience, capability, and growth potential.
