## Full job description

The Chief of Staff will act as a strategic partner to the Founder & CEO, helping translate vision into execution across the organization. High-impact, cross-functional role for someone who thrives in ambiguity, can move seamlessly between strategy and operations, and brings rigor, clarity, and momentum to the CEO's priorities.

**Key responsibilities:** Partner with CEO to prioritize, structure, and execute strategic initiatives; translate high-level goals into clear plans, milestones, and ownership across teams; prepare briefing materials, memos, dashboards, and decision frameworks; own the company's operating cadence and track progress against goals; drive alignment between Product, Sales & Operations; lead and unblock critical initiatives and projects; act as an extension of the CEO in internal communications; bring structure to complex problems and conduct deep analysis.

**Success metrics (12-18 months):** CEO time focused on highest-leverage priorities; faster strategic initiative execution; leadership team alignment and accountability; scaled organization with minimal friction.

**Experience:** Up to 8 years in management consulting, high-growth startups, strategy/operations roles, or founder's office. Prior exposure to senior leadership/founders preferred.
