Job Summary

The Team

Our Strategy and Business Operations is a focused team that works on our most critical business challenges to drive and support our industry-leading growth. Your efforts will directly affect the overall strategy and success of a world-leading cybersecurity business. You will collaborate on complex business and technology problems that are top of mind for our executives. Job Summary

As the Lead for NetSec Go-To-Market Strategy, you will be a senior leader in our business strategy organization, directly contributing to our overall success. You will work on a case team to solve complex, high-priority problems for our executives, focusing on the Network Security platform, our largest line-of-business. This role is pivotal in driving CEO priorities and making a measurable impact across product and go-to-market strategies as we rapidly scale the business.

Key Responsibilities

- Structure and deconstruct complex business problems, develop hypotheses, drive rigorous analysis, and deliver actionable recommendations to senior leadership.
- Proactively collaborate with cross-functional stakeholders to gather critical information, pressure-test hypotheses, and co-create deliverables to accelerate execution and unlock value.
- Develop and present high-quality, executive-level presentations and documents that clearly articulate strategic recommendations and findings.
- Analytical Rigor:Expert-level proficiency in modeling and data visualization to turn pipeline raw data into actionable executive insights.
- Support the annual and quarterly strategic and business planning processes, contributing to the development of strategic capabilities across the organization.
- AI Automation & Efficiency - design and implement AI-driven automation workflows to streamline the GTM lifecycle activities, from identifying high-propensity customers to automating pipeline management and proposal generation.
- AI Aptitude: Familiarity with how AI/ML can be applied to business process automation and a conceptual understanding of security solutions.
- Drive organizational change by leading strategic projects, managing timelines, and ensuring execution to 'move the needle' on key initiatives.
- Provide coaching and mentorship to associates and other leads on the team, taking a personal interest in their growth and development.

Qualifications

- Minimum 7 years of relevant work experience, with at least 3 years in management consulting, private equity, or a corporate strategy/bizops function at a top-tier technology company.
- MBA from a top-tier business school, a 'direct promote' in consulting, or equivalent military experience.
- Expertise in the Enterprise technology industry.
- Exceptional verbal and written communication skills, with the ability to comfortably lead both business and technical discussions with senior audiences.

Preferred Qualifications

- Experience in the cybersecurity industry.
- Demonstrated experience building relationships and leading through influence in a matrixed organization.
- Proven track record of managing complex projects and large-scale transformations.
- A self-starter who thrives in fast-paced, high-growth environments with minimal supervision.
