## Job Description
Digital Marketing Manager for J&J Heart Recovery. Craft and execute impactful commercial marketing strategies for Heart Recovery's therapies and products throughout the US. Work collaboratively with product marketing, professional education, sales training, reimbursement and conference leads.

## Responsibilities
- Drive and manage development of Heart Recovery's digital channel strategy in the US, primarily via website content
- Work collaboratively on omni-channel strategy and messaging campaigns
- Establish performance metrics and KPIs to evaluate marketing campaign effectiveness
- Review all external-facing content from marketing for brand consistency

## Requirements
- Bachelor degree required, MBA preferred
- 3-5 years in cardiovascular med tech marketing or agency experience
- Expertise in developing and implementing product-specific campaigns in an omni-channel approach
- Ability to review editorial content in language that will resonate with relevant physician customer audience
- Proven track record leveraging analytics, customer insights, and field feedback to develop marketing strategies
- Excellent interpersonal, communication and negotiation skills
