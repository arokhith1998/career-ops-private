**About the team:** The role sits in AWS Cross-Category Product Marketing and owns AWS's top-level portfolio marketing messaging, including how AWS's product narratives reach customers across web, email, paid, and social channels, especially around major launch moments. The team works with the Integrated Demand Center on the customer journey across major launch events, maintains the editorial calendar and prioritization process for AWS's surfaces, and owns how AWS's 250+ products are organized and presented on AWS.com (including the homepage and global navigation). It coordinates with demand generation and product marketing to keep the portfolio story consistent, and also acts as an incubator for new storytelling approaches and AI-enabled marketing practices.

**Basic qualifications:**
- 6+ years of professional, non-internship marketing experience
- Experience building and scaling cross-functional marketing programs
- Experience aligning cross-functional teams
- Experience in product marketing, brand strategy, or portfolio marketing for technology companies
- Experience writing positioning and messaging for complex, multi-product portfolios
- Strong writing and editorial skills

**Preferred qualifications:**
- Leading large cross-functional teams and agency partners
- Balancing conflicting priorities in ambiguous situations
- B2B cloud or enterprise software marketing
- Leading marketing for major launch events at scale
- Editorial judgment and trade-off decisions
- Working with Director-level and above stakeholders
- Using data and metrics (engagement analytics, A/B testing, customer research)
