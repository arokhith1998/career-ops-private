About Argyle

Argyle is a fast-growing, remote-first, Series C startup solving a consumer data problem. For decades, financial service providers and other businesses have collected and verified consumer income, employment, and asset data the same way: by hand or via verification databases. These methods are expensive, time consuming, high friction, and/or unreliable. In turn, experiences like applying for a loan or completing a background check are challenging for service providers and consumers alike.

The solution is Argyle. We're the leading provider of direct-source, consumer-permissioned income, employment, and asset verifications. We make it fast and easy to gain secure and reliable access to the most complete real-time datasets stored in consumers' payroll and banking accounts, so service providers can automate verification workflows, save time, reduce risks, lower costs, and build better product experiences.

Named one of America's Best Startup Employers of 2026 by Forbes, Argyle largely serves the mortgage, government benefits, tenant screening, lending, and background check industries as well as the gig economy.

Founded in 2018, Argyle's commitment to innovation is backed by investors including Bain Capital Ventures, Checkr, Mastercard, Rockefeller Asset Management and SignalFire.

About The Team

Revenue Operations runs the critical business operations that keep Argyle's sales motion moving fast, from deal approvals and contracting to billing, commissions, and the CRM. We're a small team of experienced operators, and we lean heavily on automation and AI tools to do more with less.

This is a hands-on role for someone early in their career who wants to learn from an experienced RevOps team and own real processes within a few months. You'll work daily with account executives, customer success managers, marketing, and finance.

What You'll Do
- Be the first stop for RevOps requests. Own the request queue for Sales, Marketing, and Customer Success. Handle the routine work yourself, from contract amendments and exports to pricing reviews, SKU setup, and Salesforce cleanup. On every request, engage stakeholders and pressure-test their asks until you land on a solution or a clear escalation path.
- Own monthly billing preparation. Run most of the pre-invoice process each month, from custom billing processes, credits tracking, and discrepancies review to finalizing amounts each customer owes.
- Build controls that scale. Run recurring checks that signed contracts, billing setup, and platform access line up, and turn repeat requests into documented processes or automation, including AI-assisted workflows.
- Handle routine deal desk approvals. Review opportunities in Salesforce for completeness, pricing setup, and contract dates before approval, and resolve setup issues that slow deals down.
- Manage the customer account lifecycle. Handle activations, terminations, reinstatements, and access changes, and keep Salesforce and our billing platform in sync.
- Keep CRM data clean. Maintain accounts, contacts, ownership, and pipeline hygiene. Monitor the health of the HubSpot-Salesforce sync to ensure a smooth handoff from Marketing to Sales.
- Support partner and government reporting. Help prepare monthly and quarterly revenue share reports for channel and integration partners, plus recurring reporting on our government contracts.
- Co-own sales commissions. Learn our monthly commission process hands-on, then share ownership of running it each month, from calculating payouts to keeping the process accurate and on time.

What Success Looks Like
- Month 1: You've worked through a full billing cycle and the request queue alongside the team, and you've shadowed the monthly commissions process.
- Month 2: You own most of billing preparation and routine requests on your own, and you've used AI tools to automate your first repetitive steps.
- Month 3: You own all billing preparation, serve as the front line for the RevOps request queue, and co-own the monthly commission process.
- Ongoing: You're the first response to GTM requests and resolve most of them yourself. You run the monthly pre-invoice process and own key communications between GTM and Finance throughout. With the time AI automation frees up, you spend more of your month on higher-level business analysis.

What We're Looking For
- 1-3 years in revenue operations or sales operations, ideally at a B2B SaaS business
- Comfort working in Salesforce day to day, from building reports and updating records in bulk to troubleshooting validation rules
- Experience building reports in Looker or another BI tool, and turning that data into analyses that solve real business problems
- Strong spreadsheet skills, including lookups, pivots, and reconciling data across sources
- A high bar for accuracy and attention to detail, since you'll work with the contract, billing, and commissions data behind our revenue
- Interest in using AI tools to automate repetitive work
- Clear written communication with internal teams and customers
- Nice to have: Salesforce Administrator certification; familiarity with HubSpot; exposure to mortgage, lending, or fintech
