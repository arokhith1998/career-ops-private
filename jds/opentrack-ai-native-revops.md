# AI-Native RevOps

**Company:** OpenTrack
**Location:** San Francisco Bay Area
**URL:** https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/view/4483265702
**Track:** us

---

At OpenTrack, we're tackling the fragmented data and workflows of the trillion dollar logistics industry. We spent nearly a decade stitching together the world’s ocean, rail, vessel, port, and trucking data sources into a consistent feed that exceeds the Autonomy Threshold for the largest companies in the world.  Now, we’re deploying AI workflows and solutions to transform these companies into Autonomous Enterprises.

The OpenTrack platform includes an AI workflow platform, enterprise web application, API service, and customer white label portal which gives companies unprecedented access to end-to-end ocean container visibility, performance metrics and predictive insights.

Our customers are some of the most iconic brands and logistics companies in the world, and we have already transformed the way they work.

We are an international team (Nashville, Chicago, Europe) with a growing presence at our Nashville HQ.

We value autonomy, curiosity, and having a good time. We’re seeking a qualified candidate to join our team in the AI-Native RevOps role.

Responsibilities:
Own and evolve our GTM systems to support sales, marketing, and customer success in an AI-first environmentDesign and build custom agents, workflows, and data pipelines from scratch, not just configure existing tools. Own the technical implementation end-to-endIncrease the productivity of our customer-facing teams through automation, AI, and process improvementServe as an expert user of HubSpot, ensuring its effective utilizationAnalyze and optimize sales processes to maximize efficiency, productivity, and revenue generation.Analyze and improve sales and marketing workflows to increase conversion rates, reduce friction, and enhance productivityProactively identify gaps and areas of improvement in the revenue lifecycle and implement scalable solutions to drive growth and operational excellence

Qualifications:
3+ years of experience in Revenue Operations, Sales Operations, or a similar analytical/strategic role, preferably in a B2B tech environmentGenuine interest in using and building with AI in practical, high-leverage ways to improve workflows, analysis, and decision-makingStrong proficiency in HubSpot CRM, HubSpot certification(s) is a plusDeep understanding of sales funnels, marketing attribution, customer lifecycle stages, and sales processDetail-oriented and passionate about data quality, process optimization, and team enablementFamiliarity with marketing operations and customer success processesExcellent communication and interpersonal skills with a demonstrated ability to collaborate effectively across teams.Excellent organizational and project management abilities, able to juggle multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment.
