About the Opportunity:

The PRICING ANALYST role focuses on enhancing deal-level margin performance and enabling smarter pricing decisions to improve seller experiences. This position involves hands-on analysis of deals, pricing data, and profitability to offer actionable recommendations that support competitive and profitable customer solutions. The analyst acts as a trusted advisor to sellers, driving improved deal outcomes and accelerating execution.

Responsibilities:

- Provide pricing analysis and commercial recommendations to enhance competitiveness and profitability.
- Review business data and deal outcomes to identify margin improvement opportunities.
- Support evaluation of complex or non-standard sales opportunities.
- Translate data analysis into actionable insights and leadership reports.
- Promote adoption of pricing tools and best practices within the sales organization.
- Document key outcomes to improve pricing consistency.
- Collaborate with Sales, Finance, Operations, Procurement, and other teams on pricing initiatives.
- Identify and implement improvements to pricing processes, tools, and reporting.
- Maintain documentation and standards related to pricing operations.
- Support testing and implementation of enhancements in pricing functions.
- Contribute to the development of a scalable pricing operations function.

Requirements:

- 3-5 years of experience in pricing, deal desk, sales operations, commercial finance, or related analytical roles.
- Ability to analyze pricing, profitability, discounting, cost structures, and margin performance.
- Strong analytical skills to translate data into business recommendations.
- Knowledge of gross profit, margins, pricing strategy, discounting practices, rebates, incentives, and deal economics.
- Advanced proficiency in Microsoft Excel for analysis and reporting.
- Attention to detail and commitment to data accuracy.
- Excellent communication skills.
- Ability to manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced sales environment.
- Experience collaborating across multiple business functions.
- Strong problem-solving skills and sound business judgment.
- Consultative approach focused on seller support and customer outcomes.
- Ability to adapt in a growing organization with evolving processes.
- Bachelor’s degree in Business, Finance, Economics, Accounting, Data Analytics, or related field, or equivalent experience.
