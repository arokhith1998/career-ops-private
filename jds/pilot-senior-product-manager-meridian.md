## Full job description

Meridian is the AI operating system for accounting firms. It runs the month-end close across a firm's entire client portfolio, and the firm's accountants review finished books and sign off before anything reaches the client. Each accountant carries a far bigger book and spends more time on advisory and client relationships.

Pilot is hiring a Senior Product Manager to own Firm Experience: everything a firm's team sees and does in Meridian once a client is live, including the firm dashboard, the close checklist, the review layer, and Meridian AI (an accounting super agent for one-off work outside the close). This is the second PM on Meridian, working alongside the PM who owns activation (kickoff to first close), inheriting a dedicated engineering and design team, a roadmap in flight, and firms using the product every month.

**What success looks like:** Increase the number of clients an accountant can confidently oversee; reduce the time required to review and approve a close; increase the percentage of closes that agents can manage without human intervention; make Meridian the system a firm uses to understand the state of its entire book of business.

**Key responsibilities:** Own the Firm Experience roadmap across the dashboard, close checklist, review layer, and Meridian AI; build a review layer firms trust; grow Meridian AI and firm customization; take Meridian beyond the close (firm-to-colleague and firm-to-client collaboration); be the voice of the accounting firm (spend time with partners, CPAs, bookkeepers); support sales and expansion.

**About you:** 4+ years as a Product Manager owning a product line or major product area, with a track record of shipping features that moved customer or business outcomes; B2B SaaS experience, ideally with workflow products used by teams; strong product and design judgment for complex, information-dense interfaces; comfort in customer-facing and sales settings; clear writing; fluency with AI tools and curiosity about agent-driven products.

**Strongly preferred:** Working knowledge of accounting or bookkeeping. **Bonus:** Practice Management or client collaboration product experience; productizing internal tools; shipping AI/agentic features; QuickBooks Online or Xero familiarity; founder or operator experience.
