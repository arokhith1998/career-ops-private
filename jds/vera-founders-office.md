Company Description
VERA is a sustainable packaging company partnering with FMCG and beauty and personal care (BPC) brands to reduce packaging-related emissions while preserving product performance and scalability. The team focuses on practical, climate-conscious solutions that can be deployed across large and complex supply chains. VERA's Returnable Transport Packaging (RTP) offers durable, reusable systems that cut single-use waste and logistics costs. Its bamboo biopolymer packaging provides low-carbon, compostable alternatives to conventional plastics for consumer-facing applications. Applicants join a mission-driven organization working at the intersection of sustainability, materials innovation, and operational excellence.

Role Description
This is a full-time, hybrid Founders Office role based in New Delhi, with flexibility for partial work from home. The role supports the founders across strategic projects, including market research, competitive analysis, and preparation of investor and client presentations. Day-to-day responsibilities include tracking key business metrics, coordinating cross-functional initiatives, and preparing concise briefs to support decision-making on product, operations, and partnerships. The person in this role will draft and refine internal documents, meeting notes, and project plans, ensuring follow-through on action items with relevant stakeholders. Additional tasks may include assisting with fundraising materials, managing special projects, and representing the Founders Office in internal and external meetings as needed.

Qualifications
- Strong analytical and research skills to handle market studies, data interpretation, and competitive benchmarking.
- Excellent written and verbal communication skills for creating presentations, reports, and concise strategic briefs.
- Organizational and project management abilities to coordinate cross-functional work, manage timelines, and ensure execution.
- Comfort with business tools and software such as spreadsheets, presentation tools, and collaboration platforms.
- Demonstrated interest in sustainability, packaging, climate-tech, or operations within FMCG or BPC sectors is an advantage.
- Bachelor's degree in business, engineering, economics, or a related field; advanced degrees or prior startup experience are a plus.
- Ability to work independently, handle ambiguity, and collaborate effectively with founders and small, agile teams.

Seniority level: Executive. Employment type: Full-time. Industry: Packaging and Containers Manufacturing.
