Manager, Sales Operations & Order Administration

The Manager, Sales Operations & Order Administration, is a critical role up your sleeve’s leadership role at the intersection of our commercial, supply chain, and finance organizations. You will own the end-to-end order-to-cash process—from CRM hygiene and pipeline forecasting through customer order entry, fulfillment coordination, downstream logistics execution and post-shipment invoicing handoff. As Lyten transitions from pilot programs to volume production, you will build the scalable systems, processes, and team that allow us to onboard new customers, ship on time, and recognize revenue cleanly.

This is a player-coach role for a hands-on operator who is energized by ambiguity, comfortable in a fast-moving manufacturing startup environment, and ready to build something durable.

Key Responsibilities

Sales Operations

Own the full Salesforce instance: data quality, opportunity stages, account hierarchy, dashboards, and reporting cadence for the commercial team.Drive the weekly, monthly, and quarterly forecast process; partner with sales and operations leadership to deliver accurate pipeline calls and variance analysis to executives and the board.Build commercial KPIs (win rate, sales cycle, ASP, design-win conversion) and the operating cadence to act on them.

Order Administration & Order-to-Cash

Lead the order administration function: customer onboarding, order entry, order acknowledgment, change orders, and shipment coordination across Lyten's North American.Own the order-to-cash workflow end-to-end—pricing approvals, deal desk, contract review handoffs, PO validation, ERP order release, and revenue recognition handoff to Finance.Maintain working knowledge of outbound logistics — carrier coordination, freight terms, and shipment documentation — sufficient to troubleshoot fulfillment issues without escalating every exception.Serve as the single point of accountability for on-time, accurate customer shipments; resolve cross-functional blockers between Sales, Supply Chain, Quality, and Logistics.Manage customer master data, pricing master data, and contract terms in the ERP; ensure SOX-aligned controls are in place as we scale.

Systems, Process & Team

Define and continuously improve SOPs for the commercial operations function; document, train, and audit for adherence.Lead system integration projects between CRM (Salesforce), ERP, CPQ, and downstream finance/inventory tools; partner with IT on roadmap and implementation.Build and lead a small high-performing team (initially 2–4 order administrators and sales ops analysts); coach, hire, and develop talent as the function scales.Partner with Legal on standard T&Cs, NDA workflows, and customer contract intake; with Finance on credit review, AR aging, and revenue recognition; with Supply Chain on demand signals and allocation.
