## Role description

The position is centered on growth strategy, where the analyst converts unclear business questions into data-driven insights that influence customer acquisition, activation, and retention approaches. The role emphasizes measurable impact through shipped experiments and observable results.

## Key responsibilities

- Create and sustain dashboards displaying performance metrics, cohort patterns, and leading indicators.
- Manage analysis spanning the entire growth funnel, pinpointing high-impact improvement opportunities.
- Work with cross-functional teams on experiment design, measurement, and interpretation.
- Translate data findings into narratives explaining outcomes and recommending actions.
- Evaluate channel effectiveness, messaging variations, and audience segments for optimization.

## Required qualifications

- Demonstrated background analyzing growth or marketing metrics in experimentation-focused environments.
- Advanced SQL and Python capabilities with hands-on data warehouse and event-level data experience.
- Competency in experiment design and statistical analysis (A/B testing, cohort studies, funnel metrics).
- Strong ability to present data findings and convert analysis into strategic recommendations.

## Preferred qualifications

- Experience with product-led growth or self-serve sales funnels.
- Familiarity with analytics platforms: Amplitude, GA4, Looker, Hex, Tableau.
