About the Role
SoulAce Consulting is looking for a highly motivated, curious, and execution-focused professional to join the CEO's Office. Strategic & Business Support

- Support in preparing business reviews, dashboards, presentations, and internal reports.

- Maintain trackers, meeting notes, action logs, and follow-up mechanisms.

Research & Market Intelligence

- Track trends in CSR, ESG, sustainability, consulting, AI, and social impact sectors.
- Prepare research notes, summaries, briefing documents, and business insights for leadership.

Executive Documentation & Communication
- Assist in creating presentations, proposal decks, concept notes, meeting briefs, and executive reports.
- Draft professional emails, communication notes, MOMs, and stakeholder correspondence.
- Support preparation of client presentations and leadership meetings.

Cross-functional Coordination
- Work closely with inter teams.
- Support execution of special projects assigned by the CEO's Office.
- Track deadlines, dependencies, and project milestones.

Data & Reporting
- Compile business data, analyze trends, and prepare MIS reports.
- Build simple dashboards, trackers, and data summaries for decision-making.
- Support documentation of organizational processes and performance metrics.

- Strong proficiency in Microsoft PowerPoint, Excel, Google Workspace, and AI productivity tools.
