## Full JD text

As a Product Manager on the AI & Data Foundations team, you will build the shared platforms that power Plaid's AI and data capabilities through early-stage, hands-on work partnering with data, ML, and platform engineers to take capabilities from 0->1 and scale them into infrastructure that is accessible, reliable, and easy to use across Plaid.

Scope: spans Plaid's AI platform (e.g., LLM gateway, eval infrastructure), data platform (e.g., Databricks, AI-powered data tooling), and the systems connecting the two.

### Key Responsibilities

- Define the vision, roadmap, and success metrics for the shared platforms that power data and AI development across Plaid
- Partner with data, ML, and platform engineers to design and ship foundational capabilities spanning AI infrastructure, developer tooling, data platforms, pipelines, quality, and governance
- Build platforms with strong internal developer experience and adoption
- Balance immediate product team needs with long-term platform investments
- Lead cross-functional partnerships with product, engineering, data, research, and security teams
- Translate complex technical decisions into clear narratives for stakeholders

### Required Qualifications

- 8+ years product management experience including AI/ML platform systems, applied intelligence products, or data infrastructure
- Deep technical fluency in modern data and AI/ML systems
- Success in ambiguous, early-stage environments
- Platform mindset with experience building developer or data platforms
- Strategic and executional range across vision-setting and near-term delivery
- Strong communication and cross-functional leadership
- Commitment to responsible AI and transparency

### Nice-to-Have

- Hands-on LLM, embeddings, or agentic systems production experience
- AI developer tooling production experience
- Applied AI experience in regulated/high-trust domains (fintech, identity, risk)
