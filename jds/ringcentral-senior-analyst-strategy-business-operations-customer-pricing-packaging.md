With $2.5B+ in ARR and $250M invested in R&D annually, we’re building the future of AI-powered business communications.

This is where you and your skills come in. We are looking for: a Senior Analyst to join our Strategy & Business Operations team. In this role, you will build automated customer analytics products and data pipelines using Python and AI tools to inform pricing and packaging decisions.

Job Responsibilities

- Build and maintain live customer journey analytics and Voice of Customer products that combine product usage, support, survey, and CRM data.
- Support pricing and packaging decisions with analysis of plan mix, discounting, and customer behavior, and turn the results into clear recommendations for executives.
- Replace manual data pulls and slide-based reporting with automated pipelines and tools.
- Analyze large datasets with Python and SQL, and document your methods so others can reuse and check them.
- Work with Product, Finance, Sales, and Customer Success to define metrics and keep them consistent across teams.
- Present findings to senior leaders, including the VP and C-suite.

What Success Looks Like In This Role

- 3 Months: Master customer data source locations, align on metric definitions with cross-functional partners, ship your first automated data refresh, and deliver an initial pricing analysis.
- 6 Months: Launch automated Voice of Customer and customer journey dashboards, present pricing recommendations to leadership, and publish documented code to a shared repository.
- 12 Months: Fully own end-to-end customer and pricing analytics, retire manual reporting, and deliver strategic analysis that directly shapes executive decision-making.

To succeed in this role you must have experience in:

- 4+ years in strategy, business operations, analytics, or management consulting.
- Hands-on experience with Python (e.g., pandas), SQL, and cloud data warehouses (e.g., Snowflake, BigQuery) to build, test, and maintain automated data pipelines and tools.
- Proficiency leveraging and reviewing AI coding tools (e.g., Claude Code, Cursor, GitHub Copilot).
- Strong communication skills to translate complex data into concise executive recommendations.
- Ability to work in-office in Belmont, CA four days a week.

Preferred qualifications:

- Experience at a management consulting firm, including a consulting firm's capability center or global delivery center.
- An advanced degree (MBA, master's, or PhD), ideally in a quantitative field.
- Experience with pricing and packaging, customer analytics, or Voice of Customer programs at a software company.
