Responsibilities:
- Lead end-to-end pricing for customer RFQs and RFPs, meeting accuracy and deadline requirements.
- Build pricing models covering materials, labor, overhead, logistics, duties, tooling, engineering, financing, warranty, and other costs.
- Work with Sales, Finance, Engineering, Supply Chain, Operations, Legal, and Program Management to validate assumptions.
- Own pricing governance and approvals, confirming margin, cash flow, and return requirements are met.
- Assess customer terms, volume forecasts, payment terms, and contractual exposure, and recommend pricing protections.
- Run scenario, sensitivity, benchmark, and profitability analyses.
- Find cost gaps and unsupported assumptions, and drive resolution across functions.
- Prepare executive briefings with recommendations for senior leadership.
- Maintain pricing files, approval records, and decision history.
- Improve deal desk tools and processes, using AI and automation where possible.

Qualifications:
- Demonstrated experience pricing complex RFQs/RFPs in a high-volume setting. Manufacturing, technology, server, or rack experience is preferred.
- Advanced financial modeling and cost analysis skills.
- Ability to influence senior stakeholders and lead cross-functional reviews without direct authority.
- Strong written and verbal communication.
- Proficiency with Windows and advanced Excel.
- Some travel and occasional work outside normal hours may be required.
- Bachelor's degree with a minimum of 5 years of relevant deal desk, pricing, or finance experience, or an equivalent combination of education and experience.
