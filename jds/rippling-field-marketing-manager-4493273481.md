About The Role

We are hiring a Field Marketing Manager to own a segment for field marketing end-to-end: strategy, budget, and hands-on execution.

You will set the vision for how we activate and nurture national and regional partners, reach their HR and finance clients and prospects, design repeatable playbooks, and personally run the programs that turn partners into advocates and advocates into revenue. You will partner closely with Sales, Revenue Ops, and Corporate Events to ensure every dollar and every activation ties to measurable outcomes.

What You Will Do

Own field marketing strategy and investment end-to-end. Build annual, quarterly, and monthly plans, balance the activity mix against budget and team capacity, and allocate resources to achieve business goals. 
Execute events and integrated campaigns end-to-end. Lead audience strategy, vendor management, program delivery, sales follow-up, and reporting to turn engagement into measurable outcomes. 
Align Sales and Marketing around shared priorities. Set program goals, clarify ownership, and coordinate follow-through to connect marketing activity with sales outcomes. 
Forecast, measure, and optimize program performance. Use historical results and clear assumptions to forecast outcomes, track pipeline contribution, conversion, and ROI, and refine investments as results emerge. 
Build differentiated programs and repeatable playbooks. Test creative formats and experiences that stand out in a crowded market, then scale successful approaches through practical playbooks. 
Manage budgets, timelines, and stakeholder communication. Maintain financial and operational rigor, communicate progress and risks proactively, and keep programs on track with a high degree of autonomy. 
Expand reach and investment through co-marketing. Identify shared audiences and co-funded initiatives that extend available budget, increase reach, and create mutual value. 
Develop strategic industry and partner relationships. Build trusted connections with partners, industry organizations, and event producers to secure high-value opportunities and optimize investment terms. 
Turn market insight and product knowledge into new opportunities. Monitor industry trends, deepen understanding of the product and customer needs, and identify new ways to engage the market. 

What You Will Need

4-6 years in field, channel, or demand gen marketing, owning strategy, budget, and hands-on execution in high-growth SaaS. 
Proven ability to build and scale repeatable partner playbooks that drive measurable pipeline and revenue. 
Financial and operational rigor, including vendor management and budget optimization
Salesforce and ideally Outreach and Sendoso experience, with the ability to architect campaigns that drive revenue. 
Executive communication skills, strong stakeholder management, and comfort operating with high autonomy. 
Creative problem solver who ships quickly, learns fast, and continuously raises the bar. 
Work ethic that matches our pace – sleeves rolled up, high throughput, and clear prioritization.
