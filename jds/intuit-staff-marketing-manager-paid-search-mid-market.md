## Job Description (extracted, verbatim from posting)

Part of Intuit's Global Business Solutions Group investing to serve mid-market businesses. Search is one of the highest-intent acquisition channels for mid-market buyers. The Staff Marketing Manager (IC) will own paid search strategy and performance across the mid-market portfolio, including Intuit Enterprise Suite. Reports to the Head of Mid-Market Media, dotted line to the lead for Holistic Search.

**Responsibilities:**
- Own the channel: set/execute paid search strategy across Google Ads and Microsoft Advertising for the mid-market portfolio
- Optimize the pipeline: partner with Analytics to connect search spend to MQAs, qualified opportunities, revenue
- Capture and generate new pipeline demand (brand defense, competitive conquesting, category/solution demand)
- Run testing agendas across keyword campaigns, ABM account-match lists, automated bidding, Performance Max
- Lead the agency; influence with data (channel reporting, business reviews); capitalize on search trends

**Minimum Qualifications:**
- "6+ years of hands-on paid search or performance marketing experience for B2B brands with ABM or MQA practice, with a track record of driving profitable growth at meaningful scale"
- Deep expertise in Google Ads and Microsoft Advertising campaign architecture, Smart Bidding, audience layering, conversion tracking
- Strong analytical skills and comfort with data and AI
- "Bachelor's degree or equivalent practical experience"

**Preferred Qualifications:** mid-market/B2B marketing experience; sales-assisted/hybrid GTM familiarity; value-based bidding/LTV modeling; SaaS/subscription business model experience with ABM tools like Demand Base; AI-assisted workflow fluency.
