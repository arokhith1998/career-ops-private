## Full JD text

Requirements: As the first product lead for commercial systems at Zoox, own the vision/priorities for marketplace optimization in the ride-hail business -- pricing, promotion, fixed-fleet deployment across hours/zones -- turning them into requirements for the teams building those systems. Supply is fixed short-run; these levers trade growth against profit weekly. Includes:
- Planned pricing -- rate card and shaping by time of day/geography, per-zone fees, operator controls.
- Dynamic pricing -- real-time engine balancing fixed fleet against live demand, growth vs profit market-by-market.
- Promotions and incentives -- mechanics, budget planning, targeted/personalized incentives.
- Fleet supply -- vehicles in service each hour, staging/charging, so fleet and pricing optimize against shared objectives.

Hands-on role: write PRDs defining what each system optimizes for, specify models, prove they worked. Sits in Commercial org, partners with Data Science, Fleet Operations, Finance, product/engineering teams.

In This Role You Will:
- Set objectives that pricing/promotions/fleet deployment optimize against, from weekly supply plan to real-time dispatch.
- Partner with Data Science on elasticity estimation, price effects modeling, demand/supply forecasting, supply planning, marketplace simulation.
- Set vertical priorities: size business impact/effort with science/engineering leads, stack rank, re-rank quarterly.
- Drive delivery across teams, manage dependencies, hold schedule, escalate early.
- Design experiments/simulation studies gating launches; set ship criteria/guardrail metrics; make ship call; write readout. Run weekly operating cadence with Commercial Operations, Fleet Operations, Finance.

Qualifications:
- 8+ years in product management, technical program management, or marketplace strategy roles, including significant time on marketplace products (ride-hail, delivery, travel, or similar) where pricing/incentives/matching/supply planning was the product.
- Demonstrated ability to specify an optimization problem in writing (objective, constraints, data needs, guardrails, validating experiment).
- Track record driving a roadmap through teams that don't report to you, changing priorities with evidence.
- Quantitative foundation: degree in economics, operations research, statistics, or similar, with practical modeling/experiment experience.
- Clear writing/presentation skills (PRDs, readouts).
- Judgment on which decisions need a model vs a defensible heuristic shipped quickly.

Bonus Qualifications: pricing/revenue management for capacity-constrained businesses (airlines, hotels, freight, logistics, energy); taking a pricing/incentives/fleet system from zero to production; graduate study in economics/OR/statistics/ML; experiments with interference (geo/time splits, switchbacks, holdouts) and simulation.

Base Salary Range: $165,000 - $221,000 a year. Compensation also includes Amazon Restricted Stock Units (RSUs) and Zoox Stock Appreciation Rights; sign-on bonus may be offered. Comprehensive benefits (PTO, health insurance, long-term care/disability/life insurance).
