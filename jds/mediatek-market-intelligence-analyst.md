Job Description

MediaTek is a global fabless semiconductor industry leader, providing solutions from the edge to the cloud and powering over 2 billion connected devices every year. Established in 1997, our leading-edge technologies keep the world connected and enhance everyday life. At the forefront of innovation, MediaTek drives advancements in transformative technologies such as AI, 5G/6G, and Wi-Fi 8. Our high-performance, power-efficient solutions form the foundation for a smarter, more connected world, enabling devices from smartphones, smart homes, and AI PCs to high-performance computing, automotive, and AI data centers. As a trusted partner to the world's leading brands, we are committed to ensuring access to world-class technology for everyone. Our dedication to accelerating AI underscores our mission to enrich the future of humanity.

- Track, evaluate, and forecast market trends, technology adoption cycles, and ecosystem dynamics across the global Data Center sector.
- Select, engage, and manage relationships with premier external market research agencies and analyst firms (e.g., Gartner, IDC, Omdia, Dell'Oro, Linley Group/TechInsights).
- Utilize primary and secondary market research databases and tools to synthesize quantitative TAM/SAM/SOM modeling, market share distributions, and competitive intelligence.
- Analyze workload trends (e.g., AI training/inference, hyperscale cloud, edge computing) to identify requirements for next-generation SoC architectures and full-system integrations.
- Map competitive semiconductor offerings, chiplet architectures, interconnect standards (e.g., PCIe, CXL, NVLink, Ethernet), and system-level reference designs against market demand.
- Track the broader platform ecosystem - including software stacks, hardware partners, ODMs/OEMs, and hyperscalers - to identify strategic opportunities for our SoC products.
- Present market intelligence and technology outlooks to internal cross-functional engineering teams, product management, and senior leadership.

Main Requirements and Qualifications
- Bachelor's Degree in Electrical Engineering (EE) or a closely related technical field (Master's degree or relevant business/analytics coursework is a plus).
- 8+ years of experience in market research, product marketing, competitive intelligence, or strategic technical analyst roles within the semiconductor or data center technology sectors.
- Solid knowledge of Data Center architecture and silicon technology, with a clear understanding of how SoC platforms integrate into full-system data center applications.
- Proven track record of leveraging third-party research tools and managing external analyst agency relationships to generate high-value strategic reports.
- Proficiency with market analytics tools, data visualization platforms (e.g., Tableau, Power BI), and financial/TAM modeling in Excel.
- Exceptional written, verbal, and visual presentation skills with the ability to articulate complex technical and commercial concepts to diverse stakeholders.

Desired Additional Skills
- Prior experience working within semiconductor vendors, hyperscalers, or data center hardware OEMs/ODMs in the San Francisco Bay Area / Silicon Valley ecosystem.
- Experience with SoC platform positioning, chiplet integration trends, and developer ecosystem dynamics.
