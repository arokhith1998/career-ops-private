## Job Description (extracted, verbatim from posting)

About Us: At You.com, we are building the AI Search Infrastructure that powers modern AI systems. Our goal is to create the trusted knowledge layer that agents, applications, and enterprises rely on to retrieve real-time, accurate, and citation-backed information. Our platform combines proprietary vertical indexes with LLM-optimized retrieval systems to power AI agents, applications, and enterprise workflows.

About the Role: You will sit at the intersection of Product, Engineering, DevRel, Sales, and Marketing, turning deeply technical product capabilities into clear positioning, compelling launches, credible proof points, and developer experiences that drive adoption. You will own the technical product narrative: understanding our products at a deep level, understanding the competitive landscape just as deeply, and translating that knowledge into how You.com shows up everywhere from a product launch and benchmark to our website, documentation, demos, sales conversations, and developer community.

**Responsibilities:**
- Own positioning and messaging for You.com's developer products and APIs
- Develop a deep understanding of Search, Contents, Research, Answer, and emerging API capabilities
- Lead go-to-market strategy for major product launches, working with Product and Engineering
- Turn technical innovation into compelling developer narratives
- Own competitive intelligence across the AI search, retrieval, grounding, and agent infrastructure landscape
- Build technical evidence that supports positioning (benchmark narratives, competitive comparisons, demos)
- Partner with Product/Engineering and DevRel; equip Sales and Partnerships with technically credible messaging
- Instrument and analyze the developer journey from discovery through production usage

**Qualifications:**
- Significant experience in technical product marketing, product management, developer relations, solutions engineering, engineering, or another deeply technical role
- Strong understanding of APIs and modern AI application architecture (LLMs, agents, retrieval, grounding, RAG, inference, evaluation)
- Experience marketing or building developer infrastructure, APIs, AI products, developer tools, data infrastructure
- Personally shipped technical artifacts (benchmark analyses, technical launch posts, integration guides, demos)
- Technical degree or previous experience as an engineer, technical PM, DevRel engineer, solutions engineer, or similar role is a plus

Company perks include hubs in SF and NYC, flexible PTO, health insurance, 401k 3% match, WFH/tech stipends.
