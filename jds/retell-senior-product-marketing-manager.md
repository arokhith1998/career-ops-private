About The Role

Retell AI transforms customer experience with voice AI for enterprises, including customers like CVS/Aetna, American Airlines, Lenovo, and Grab. We have more customer stories than we can tell!

Retell is hiring a Senior PMM to own how we show up to the market. This role is responsible for identifying breakout opportunities and turning early product momentum into scalable, market-defining growth.

You'll lead positioning, run product launches, build the sales enablement toolkit, and concept brand campaigns that earn real attention. 80% of the role is core PMM. 20% is directing creative campaigns for social.

This is a high-ownership role. You won't just maintain messaging, you'll define it. You report to the CEO and work closely with the founding team.

Key Responsibilities

- Own positioning and messaging across all buyer types and verticals

Develop vertical-specific narratives for healthcare, legal, automotive, insurance, utilities, and logistics

Keep competitive differentiation sharp as the Voice AI landscape evolves

- Lead product launches end-to-end

Own strategy, narrative, and cross-functional coordination from brief to announcement

Partner with DevEvangelist on the demo and technical content layer

- Build sales enablement from scratch

Plan campaign bursts around launches, events, and AI industry moments
