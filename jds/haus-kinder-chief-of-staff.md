## Full JD text (verbatim)

Chief of Staff to the CEO

ABOUT HAUS & KINDER: Haus & Kinder is a fast-growing D2C home-textiles brand offering bedsheets, dohars, comforters, curtains and more. We sell through our own website hausandkinder.com, leading marketplaces (Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, Meesho) and quick-commerce platforms (Blinkit, Zepto, Swiggy Instamart).

ABOUT THE ROLE: The Chief of Staff is the CEO's strategic partner and the company's central point of execution. You will turn priorities into plans, drive the most important cross-functional programmes to completion, and give the CEO the analysis needed for fast, well-informed decisions as we scale.

**WHAT YOU WILL DO**
1. Strategy and business planning: planning cadence (annual, quarterly, monthly priorities, operating rhythm); execution plans with clear owners/milestones/KPIs; strategic analysis (market research, competitive benchmarking, business cases).
2. Cross-functional execution: programme leadership across Product, Sourcing, Content and Marketing, Operations, Finance and Sales; alignment between CEO and functional heads.
3. Business performance and channel economics: performance tracking (CEO dashboard covering revenue, contribution margin, CAC, repeat rate, inventory health); channel P&L analysis (D2C, marketplaces, quick commerce, B2B).
4. Growth initiatives: new business launches and GTM for new categories/NPD cycles, new brands, B2B partnerships, export markets; transformation (operational improvements, channel transitions).
5. Investors and board: fundraising and reporting (board/investor materials, due-diligence requests, post-investment commitments).

**WHAT WE ARE LOOKING FOR**
Must have:
- 5-10 years of experience in top-tier management consulting, business strategy or a strategy / CEO's-office role.
- Experience in D2C, e-commerce, consumer brands or new-age businesses.
- Strong financial acumen, with exposure to business planning, P&L management or unit economics.
- Structured problem-solving and comfort with data-driven decisions (advanced Excel or SQL).
- A track record of leading complex cross-functional programmes.
- Excellent communication and stakeholder management, from the shop floor to the boardroom.
- Comfort with ambiguity and the ability to work independently at startup pace.

Preferred:
- MBA from a top-tier business school, or an engineering degree from a top-tier college.
- Exposure to marketplace or quick-commerce operations, home and lifestyle categories.
