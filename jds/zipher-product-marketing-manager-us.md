## Full job description (verbatim)

Zipher is building the Autonomous Execution Layer for cloud data and AI, a new category we are defining: $50M raised and live in Fortune 500 environments. Our agents run production data and AI workloads on Databricks, Spark, Kubernetes, and AWS, and every customer measures us on the same thing: cost, performance, and reliability.

We are looking for a Product Marketing Manager to define how the market understands Zipher. You will own positioning, messaging, and the category story of autonomous data platforms, and turn them into launches, content, and the tools our sales team wins with.

This role is fully remote, open to candidates living in the US, and includes occasional travel to customers and industry events.

What You’ll Do

- Own positioning and messaging: the category narrative for autonomous data platforms, by persona, from the data engineer to the CTO
- Run launches end to end: from product to market, with clear goals and measured results
- Equip the sales team: decks, battlecards, objection handling, business cases, and customer stories
- Partner closely with the founders, product, and sales: bring customer and competitive insight into the roadmap
- Create technical content that data and engineering leaders actually read: blogs, whitepapers, webinars, and demos

What You’ll Bring

- Based in the US, plus 5+ years of product marketing for B2B data platform products
- The technical depth to speak with data engineers in their language: Databricks, Spark, Snowflake, and the cloud
- A track record of category creation or launching early-stage products
- Strong writing and storytelling for both practitioners and executives
- Hands-on experience building sales enablement that sales teams actually use
- A high-agency, hands-on mindset: you write it, ship it, and measure it yourself

Nice to Have

- Experience marketing data or AI platforms to enterprise buyers
- A background as an engineer, Solution Engineer, or Product Manager
- Experience with analyst relations and industry events
- Fluency with AI tools for content and research

What We Offer

- A chance to define a new category of autonomous data platform for the largest data and AI organizations in the US
- A story of your own to build: you set the positioning, the launches, and the voice of Zipher in the market
- A seat next to the founders: direct work with the CEO and CTO on how Zipher goes to market
- Highly rewarding compensation: competitive base plus performance-based bonuses
