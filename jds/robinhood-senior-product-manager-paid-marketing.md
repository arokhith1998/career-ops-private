### About the team + role
We are building an elite team, applying frontier technologies to the world's biggest financial problems. We're looking for bold thinkers, sharp problem-solvers, builders who are wired to make an impact. The Growth team's mission is to drive the expansion of Robinhood's premium and high-value user segments, focusing on optimizing web presence, funnel experiences, and custom landing pages to acquire and retain highly active customers.

As Senior Product Manager, you will lead critical growth initiatives spanning funnel optimization, subscription services, and custom landing page experiences. You will collaborate closely with engineering, design, and marketing teams to implement high-performing user acquisition journeys. You will analyze complex funnel metrics, direct user attribution tracking, and launch targeted cross-sell campaigns to increase user retention.

**This role is based in our New York, NY and Menlo Park, CA offices, with in-person attendance expected at least 3 days per week.**

### What you'll do
- Define and execute the product strategy for web funnels, SEO landing pages, and customer acquisition experiences.

- Leverage user attribution tracking and data analytics to identify drop-off points and implement measurable conversion improvements.
- Design and launch cross-sell programs and campaigns that drive adoption of premium services and subscription offerings.
