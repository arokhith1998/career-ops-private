Location: Andheri- Saki Naka Reporting to: Founder & CEO Experience: Approximately 2–5 years; exceptional early-career candidates may also be considered. This is an entrepreneurial role offering broad exposure to the workings of a growing consumer brand. The candidate will work closely with the founder and various functional teams, gaining first-hand experience in building and scaling a business. Working days: 5 days; 2nd and 4th Sat off. On other Saturdays leave around 4-5PM Work timings: 10am - 6.30/7 pm Role Overview The Founder’s Office professional will act as an analytical and execution partner to the founder, helping drive key business priorities, improve decision-making and ensure effective execution across functions. The role requires strong numerical and analytical capabilities, structured thinking, high ownership and a willingness to learn the business from the ground up. The candidate should be comfortable moving between financial analysis, business problem-solving, operational reviews and strategic projects. This is not a conventional executive-assistant role. It is intended for someone who wants to understand how a consumer business operates and contribute directly to its growth. Key Responsibilities Business Analysis & Management Reporting Analyse business performance across revenue, profitability, categories, SKUs and sales channels. Work with Finance and Operations to consolidate data and develop meaningful management dashboards. Track key business metrics, identify trends and investigate deviations from targets. Analyse product-level and channel-level economics, including margins, advertising costs, inventory and working capital. Prepare structured business reviews, financial analyses and decision-support reports for the founder. Translate data into actionable insights and recommendations rather than simply compiling reports. Strategic Planning & Business Problem-Solving Work with the founder on business planning, growth initiatives, market opportunities and expansion strategies. Conduct research and analysis on new categories, distribution channels, competitors and potential partnerships. Evaluate business cases for new initiatives, including revenue potential, costs, investment requirements and expected returns. Support annual and quarterly planning, budgeting and performance reviews. Develop structured recommendations for business problems and support the founder in evaluating alternative courses of action. Cross-functional Execution & Accountability Track key priorities and initiatives across Finance, E-commerce, Operations, Marketing and Sales. Coordinate with functional owners to monitor progress against agreed timelines and deliverables. Identify bottlenecks, unresolved issues and dependencies that require management attention. Prepare agendas, action trackers and follow-up reports for key business reviews. Ensure important decisions translate into clear action plans with accountable owners and deadlines. Escalate delays, risks and deviations to the founder with relevant context and proposed solutions. Financial Analysis & Fundraising Support Support the preparation and analysis of budgets, forecasts, cash flows and business performance reports. Assist in evaluating unit economics, contribution margins, customer acquisition costs and other relevant business metrics. Support fundraising activities through preparation of investor materials, financial analyses, data-room coordination and investor updates. Assist with valuation analysis, financial modelling and business-plan preparation. Coordinate with Finance and external advisers to gather accurate information for investor discussions and strategic decisions. Special Projects & New Initiatives Take ownership of selected projects assigned by the founder, from problem definition through implementation. Support the evaluation and launch of new categories, products, channels and business initiatives. Work with internal teams and external partners to drive projects to completion. Identify opportunities to improve processes, reduce costs and enhance organisational productivity. Undertake research, analysis and execution of time-sensitive business priorities. Business Intelligence & Process Improvement Help establish a reliable and consistent reporting framework across the organisation. Identify gaps in business data, reporting processes and performance tracking. Work with Finance and Operations to improve data accuracy, consistency and availability. Develop standard templates for business reviews, project tracking and management reporting. Help the founder distinguish between issues requiring strategic intervention and those that can be resolved by functional owners. Candidate Profile Chartered Accountant (CA) or MBA from a premier management institute. Approximately 2–5 years of relevant experience in management consulting, investment banking, corporate strategy, business finance, business operations or a similar analytical role. Exceptional early-career candidates with strong analytical aptitude may also be considered. Strong numerical ability, financial understanding and proficiency in Excel. Ability to structure ambiguous business problems, analyse information and develop clear recommendations. Strong presentation and communication skills, including the ability to prepare concise management reports and presentations. High ownership, attention to detail and ability to manage multiple priorities. Comfortable working directly with the founder and interacting with different functions. Willingness to learn all aspects of the business, including products, marketplaces, supply chain, marketing, finance and customer behaviour. Prior experience in a D2C, consumer brand, e-commerce or start-up environment will be a significant advantage. Personal Attributes Entrepreneurial mindset and intellectual curiosity. Strong work ethic and willingness to get hands-on. Structured, analytical and solution-oriented thinking. High integrity and discretion when handling sensitive business and financial information. Ability to challenge assumptions constructively and communicate independently. Comfort with ambiguity and changing priorities in a growing organisation. What Success Looks Like The founder has reliable, decision-ready information on business performance. Key initiatives are tracked effectively and executed within agreed timelines. Business problems are identified early, analysed rigorously and presented with practical recommendations. Cross-functional coordination improves without creating unnecessary bureaucracy. The founder spends less time chasing updates and compiling information, and more time on strategy, growth, fundraising and product development. The candidate develops a comprehensive understanding of the business and progressively takes independent ownership of important initiatives.
