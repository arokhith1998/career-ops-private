About The Role

OpusClip is the world's #1 AI video agent for social media, used by 20M+ creators and businesses to turn raw footage into viral-ready clips.

We're hiring an AI Product Manager, based in Mountain View, to lead the product direction of our AI team. You'll work at the intersection of frontier AI, model innovation, and platform capabilities, turning rapid advances in AI into meaningful product and business impact.

This role has two dimensions. Internally, you'll partner closely with our AI research and engineering teams to drive model strategy, build scalable evaluation frameworks, and continuously improve our AI capabilities. Externally, you'll explore how our AI models and capabilities can create value beyond our existing products, identifying new market opportunities and shaping how we bring them to customers.

What You'll Do

Turn Frontier AI Into Product Opportunities: Stay hands-on with emerging models and AI capabilities. Understand what new models can and cannot do, rapidly prototype promising ideas, and evaluate their potential product and business impact. Partner closely with AI researchers and engineers to identify which model capabilities and improvements are worth investing in.

Build a Scalable AI Evaluation Platform: Define and drive a shared evaluation framework that enables product teams to measure, compare, and continuously improve AI quality. Establish meaningful evaluation rubrics, benchmarks, and quality standards, making evaluation easy to integrate into development workflows so teams can experiment faster and ship with confidence.

Build the AI Data Flywheel: Develop the product strategy for continuously improving our AI capabilities through training data, feedback, and evaluation. Work closely with AI engineers to identify high-impact opportunities for model training and improvement, prioritize investments, and turn technical advancements into measurable product advantages.

Drive AI Capability Strategy: Make informed decisions about where to adopt external technologies, where to invest in differentiated model capabilities, and how to balance quality, performance, cost, and speed. Partner with AI research and engineering to prioritize model development based on technical feasibility, user value, and business impact. Focus on building advantages that matter to users and compound over time.

Explore New Commercial Opportunities: Identify opportunities to bring our AI models and capabilities to new customers and markets through APIs, services, or standalone offerings. Develop a deep understanding of customer needs, competitive positioning, pricing, and go-to-market strategies. Drive opportunities from early validation toward commercialization, determining what makes an AI offering compelling and commercially viable.

Own Outcomes, Not Just Projects: Define clear success metrics for model quality, internal platform adoption, and external business opportunities. Measure success by how much faster teams can innovate, how much better our AI experiences become, and what new customer value and revenue opportunities we create.

Bridge AI, Product, and Engineering: Work closely with AI researchers, engineers, product managers, and business teams. Translate technical breakthroughs into product and business opportunities, and turn product needs into clear priorities for the AI team. Drive cross-functional alignment and make thoughtful trade-offs between technical investments and business outcomes.

What We're Looking For

Technical AI/ML and Product Management Experience: 5+ years of combined relevant experience in technical AI/ML and product management, including meaningful hands-on exposure to model evaluation, training workflows, or AI platform development.
Deep, Hands-On Fluency With AI: You actively experiment with emerging models, understand their strengths and limitations, and can quickly identify where new capabilities create real product value. You have a strong understanding of model development, training, and improvement workflows.
Strong Experience With AI Evaluation and Model Quality: You have hands-on experience with AI evaluation systems, model quality measurement, or data-driven model improvement. You know how to define what good looks like, design meaningful evaluation frameworks, and make decisions when traditional metrics don't tell the full story.
AI/ML Platform or Infrastructure Background: Experience working on AI/ML infrastructure, developer platforms, model-serving systems, or API-based products. You understand how to build reusable capabilities that make multiple teams more effective.
Technical Depth to Work Closely With AI Engineers: You're comfortable discussing model capabilities, training workflows, data pipelines, inference, and the trade-offs involved in building and improving production AI systems.
Strong Platform Product Thinking: You understand how to develop internal AI platforms, shared evaluation frameworks, and reusable capabilities that help product and engineering teams innovate faster, rather than solving the same problem repeatedly.
Commercial Instincts and Market Awareness: You can evaluate competitive landscapes, identify differentiated value propositions, and think critically about positioning, pricing, and monetization. You understand how technical capabilities can translate into sustainable business value.
Bias Toward Action: You move quickly from ideas to prototypes to real-world validation, stay curious about emerging AI technologies, and know when to double down or move on.
Ownership, Prioritization, and Communication: You can balance immediate product opportunities with longer-term strategic investments, make clear tradeoffs, and effectively communicate across AI research, engineering, product, and business teams.
Based in the Bay Area and able to work on-site in Mountain View; comfortable partnering with our Shanghai and Vancouver teams across time zones.
