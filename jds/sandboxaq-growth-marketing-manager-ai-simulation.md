**About SandboxAQ**

SandboxAQ delivers AI solutions addressing global challenges through Large Quantitative Models (LQMs) powering advances in life sciences, financial services, navigation, cybersecurity, and other sectors. The company emerged from Alphabet Inc. as an independent, growth capital-backed company in 2022. The organization values creativity, collaboration, and impact.

**The Opportunity**

The AI Simulation (AISim) division builds Large Quantitative Models grounded in physics, chemistry, and biology to solve complex problems. The marketing team works with technical, scientific audiences including computational chemists, drug discovery leaders, and materials R&D professionals. This Growth Marketing Manager role centers on building a full-funnel growth engine from awareness through revenue generation.

**Key Responsibilities**

- Own full-funnel demand generation across paid media, SEO, email, webinars, and content syndication, measured through sourced and influenced pipeline
- Build growth engine from ground up, establishing campaigns, workflows, reporting, and processes prioritized by performance data
- Execute programs end-to-end including webinars, audience development, campaign execution, and performance analysis
- Develop and report on funnel metrics, channel efficiency, cost per pipeline dollar, campaign ROI, and attribution
- Partner cross-functionally with Sales, Product Marketing, Marketing Operations, and RevOps

**Essential Skills & Experience**

- 4-7 years B2B demand generation or growth marketing with demonstrated pipeline growth track record
- Bachelor's degree in Marketing, Communications, Business, or equivalent practical experience
- "Strong quantitative and analytical skills, including comfort with funnel metrics, attribution, performance analysis"
- Hands-on demand generation program building and execution capability
- Proven cross-functional collaboration ability

**Highly Desired Skills**

- AI fluency and builder's mindset with hands-on AI tool usage
- Experience marketing to technical, scientific, or R&D audiences
- Background in deep tech, AI/ML, life sciences, or complex B2B categories
- Hands-on experience with marketing automation, CRM, and BI/analytics platforms
