**About the role**
The Pricing & Monetization team designs pricing, packaging, and commercial strategy for ChatGPT and Codex, including model pricing. The role reports to the Head of Product Pricing for Chat & Codex and works with Product, GTM, and Finance leaders.

Responsibilities include:
- Developing pricing and packaging across plan usage limits, credits, overages, and new monetization models.
- Analyzing customer usage, building financial models, and evaluating pricing experiments.
- Aligning pricing with product value, roadmap, and growth plans.
- Setting up lightweight pricing governance, such as decision frameworks, approval processes, and performance tracking.

**Qualifications**
- 6+ years in a strategy role, such as consulting or business operations.
- 3+ years of hands-on pricing and monetization experience, ideally launching self-serve subscription or usage-based offerings. Sales-led experience is a plus.
- Familiarity with seat-based and usage-based pricing models.
- Strong analytical and quantitative skills.
- Ability to influence senior cross-functional stakeholders in ambiguous situations.
- Self-starter comfortable in a fast-paced environment.

**Compensation Range:** $234,000 - $260,000 per year.
