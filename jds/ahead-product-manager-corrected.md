## Full JD text (verbatim, from Lever posting API)

AHEAD builds platforms for digital business. By weaving together advances in cloud infrastructure, automation and analytics, and software delivery, we help enterprises deliver on the promise of digital transformation.

At AHEAD, we prioritize creating a culture of belonging, where all perspectives and voices are represented, valued, respected, and heard. We create spaces to empower everyone to speak up, make change, and drive the culture at AHEAD.

We are an equal opportunity employer, and do not discriminate based on an individual's race, national origin, color, gender, gender identity, gender expression, sexual orientation, religion, age, disability, marital status, or any other protected characteristic under applicable law, whether actual or perceived.

We embrace all candidates that will contribute to the diversification and enrichment of ideas and perspectives at AHEAD.

**What Will You Be Doing?**
- Own one of our product modules end to end: its outcomes, roadmap, and full lifecycle from concept to launch to measuring results
- Contribute to overall product strategy and own communicating your module's roadmap to internal and external audiences
- Define product requirements grounded in real user problems and validated feedback
- Collaborate with UI/UX designers to bring intuitive, user-centered experiences to life
- Work closely with stakeholders across various teams, such as platform success, marketing, operations, to ensure launches are well-coordinated and successful
- Identify new ways to leverage AI to enhance the product experience
- Stay deeply engaged with clients and end users: run feedback sessions, iterate quickly, and proactively surface improvement opportunities

**What Does the Ideal Candidate Look Like?**
- Focus on driving outcomes over output: problems solved, metrics moved, user behavior changed, revenue influenced
- Strong product discovery skills that turn ambiguity into insight and a strong opinion on what to do next
- Proven user empathy: starts with the user's problem, not the feature request
- Builder mindset: quick to make ideas tangible, quicker to throw them away when they miss
- Works fluently with designers and engineers: translates vision into prototypes and understands technical feasibility firsthand
- Excellent judgment balancing user value and business needs
- Clear, concise communicator comfortable with executives, engineers, and everyone in between
- Fluent with current AI tools, with an aptitude for using them to research, prototype, and communicate intent

**Why You'll Love It Here:**
- Join a client-centric culture where your ideas shape the tools our clients rely on every day
- Be a builder -- launch meaningful features from the ground up and influence the evolution of our platforms
- Thrive in a collaborative environment where delivery, engineering, design, and operations work together as partners
- Competitive salary, comprehensive benefits, and a healthy respect for work-life balance

**Make Your Voice Heard:**
We know impactful product managers bring more than just requirements, they bring perspective. Whether you've led internal tools for complex orgs or helped ship customer-facing features in tight timelines, your experience matters. Share what you've built, how you think, and what product leadership means to you.
Please include links to any product documentation, project briefs, launch materials, or artifacts that showcase your work and ownership.

**Why AHEAD (from additional/benefits section):**
Through our daily work and internal groups like Moving Women AHEAD and RISE AHEAD, we value and benefit from diversity of people, ideas, experience, and everything in between. We fuel growth by stacking our office with top-notch technologies in a multi-million-dollar lab, by encouraging cross department training and development, sponsoring certifications and credentials for continued learning.
