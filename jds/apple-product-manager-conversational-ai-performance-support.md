Summary

The WW Channel Strategy & Operations (CSO) team focuses on developing and deploying global sales platforms and processes to deliver exceptional customer experiences and drive business outcomes. We are seeking a Product Manager to own the roadmap for real-time, conversational AI guidance that enables seller performance and skill development, and is accountable for driving seller performance outcomes, not just usage. This is a global role, serving Apple's channel sellers across 100+ countries and many languages.

Description

1. Product Strategy & Vision
- Define and drive the product vision, strategy, and roadmap for real-time, in-the-moment seller performance support powered by conversational AI
- Own the Perform pillar of Prepare, Perform, Grow - the live, with-customer moment - while ensuring real-time guidance also strengthens how sellers prepare and continue to grow
- Partner with the Product Managers owning seller learning and practice experiences to ensure a connected path from preparation through live performance

2. Conversational AI & Guidance Experience
- Own the end-to-end experience of how sellers access guidance in real time, including conversational AI interactions
- Define product requirements that balance speed, accuracy, and trust in AI-generated guidance
- Own personalization of the guidance experience itself, partnering with the backend platform PM to leverage the underlying signal and profile infrastructure

3. Performance Impact & Outcomes
- Define what "effective in-the-moment support" looks like and build the mechanisms to measure it
- Center success criteria on seller performance and confidence in live selling moments, not just usage or engagement
- Use outcome data to continuously evolve the guidance experience

4. Cross-Functional Collaboration
- Partner with the PM owning seller practice and simulation experiences to ensure skills built in practice carry into live performance support
- Partner with the PM owning personalization and coaching intelligence, and the backend platform PM, to ensure guidance is personalized and powered by the right signals
- Partner with engineering, design, data & analytics, and EPMs to drive execution from concept to launch
- Partner with Content, Product Marketing, Brand, and Legal to define and govern the voice, tone, and disclaimers of AI-generated guidance

5. Metrics & Performance
- Define and track success metrics centered on seller performance and guidance effectiveness
- Use data to drive continuous improvement and prioritization
- Maintain a well-structured, prioritized backlog for the guidance experience

Responsibilities
- Own the roadmap for real-time, in-the-moment sales guidance
- Define the product strategy for growing real-time guidance into a full performance-support experience
- Ensure clean handoff and alignment with the PMs owning practice, personalization, and backend platform
- Define and track performance-based success metrics
- Partner with engineering and data science to translate guidance strategy into scalable systems

Minimum Qualifications
- 8+ years of product management experience, ideally including learner-facing, seller-facing, or performance-support products
- Experience with LLM-based or GenAI product development
- Experience building products that span both B2B and B2C selling motions, at global scale, including multi-language or multi-region deployment
- Strong ability to translate complex seller- or customer-facing needs into scalable product requirements
- Proven track record defining success metrics centered on performance outcomes, not just usage or engagement
- Experience partnering across multiple PMs/surfaces to drive a cross-cutting capability
- Experience working with cross-functional teams in Agile environments
- Bachelor's degree or equivalent experience

Preferred Qualifications
- Experience in B2B/B2C sales enablement or learning technologies
- Experience implementing and scaling workflow learning or learning-in-the-flow-of-work approaches
