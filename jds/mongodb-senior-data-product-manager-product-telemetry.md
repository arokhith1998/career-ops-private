MongoDB's Internal Data team seeks a Senior Data Product Manager to lead data product development for Product and Technology teams. This role focuses on building scalable data solutions including pipelines, datasets, dashboards, APIs, ML models, and frameworks.

Key Responsibilities - Lead Product Strategy and Ownership: define vision, strategy, and roadmap for data teams and products; evangelize data-as-a-product approaches; balance quick wins with innovation using lakehouse and medallion data patterns. Partner with P&T Teams: collaborate on data product solutions; engage analysts, engineers, and leadership; serve as trusted advocate. Performance and Market Analysis: define success metrics and KPIs; track emerging trends. Stakeholder Enablement & Adoption: build documentation, guides, onboarding materials; drive adoption. Agile Product Management: own sprint planning, backlog grooming, retrospectives; balance innovation with technical debt.

Requirements: "5+ years of hands-on product management experience, with at least two years focused on data products or platforms." Strong stakeholder experience in advanced engineering organizations. Technical foundation in data engineering, analytics, and data products. Familiarity with modern data platforms and scalable architecture. Strategic roadmap prioritization and trade-off management. "Outstanding Communication Skills" articulating complex concepts across audiences. Differentiators: experience with data science, BI, analytics, or data engineering tools; track record creating product enablement materials; knowledge of metadata management.
