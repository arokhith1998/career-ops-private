## Full JD Text

Cohesity is a leader in AI-powered data security and management. Aided by an extensive ecosystem of partners, Cohesity makes it easy to secure, protect, manage, and get value from data — across the data center, edge, and cloud. Cohesity helps organizations defend against cybersecurity threats with comprehensive data security and management capabilities, including immutable backup snapshots, AI-based threat detection, monitoring for malicious behavior, and rapid recovery at scale.

We’ve been named a Leader by multiple analyst firms and have been globally recognized for Innovation, Product Strength, and Simplicity in Design.

Join us on our mission to shape the future of our industry.

Cohesity is looking for a Senior Product Manager to drive execution for our Filesystem & Storage portfolio. This is a hands-on, high-ownership role. You'll own execution for a defined area of our filesystem and storage platform spanning customer-managed and cloud SaaS working in tight partnership with engineering, UX, and go-to-market teams, directly with customers across the globe.

HOW YOU'LL SPEND YOUR TIME HERE:

-  Own the roadmap and PRDs for your area within Filesystem & Storage driving innovation and adoption across - Enterprise file systems and storage protocols (NFS, SMB, S3, etc.), Scale-out and distributed storage architecture, Data mobility, interoperability, and platform performance, Storage efficiency, resiliency, and scalability at petabyte scale, Cloud/hybrid-cloud extensions, and adjacent agentic AI-driven data management.
-  Go deep with engineering, daily — understand system internals well enough to drive real design conversations, not just requirements hand-offs.
-  Work through disagreements with engineering using data and trade-off framing — not by pulling rank or escalating by default.
-  Own release trade-off calls when scope, quality, and timeline conflict, and communicate the “why” clearly.
-  Stay engaged from PRD through design review, build, GA, and beyond. Shipped is the starting line, not the finish.
-  Define success metrics before you ship and track them after: adoption, usage depth, retention, support tickets, NPS/CSAT.
-  Be the voice of the customer — keep a constant pulse on markets, competitors, and technology trends to spot opportunities and risks early.
-  Partner with Marketing on packaging, pricing input, blogs, and launch events for your area.
-  Track your product after it ships — revisit adoption and quality data at 30/60/90 days and feed learnings back into the roadmap.

You'll partner closely with:

-  A Principal PM or Head of Product for Filesystem & Storage, aligning your execution with the broader roadmap.
-  Peer PMs across adjacent areas of the Cohesity platform.
-  GTM teams — Marketing, Ecosystem, Support, Enablement, Sales, and Sales Engineering.
-  A progressive UX team, and a world-class engineering team you'll work alongside daily — not just at the start and end.

WE'D LOVE TO TALK TO YOU IF YOU HAVE MANY OF THE FOLLOWING:

-  7–10 years of PM experience owning and shipping complex, technical B2B/infrastructure products end to end — not just writing requirements and moving on.
-  Deep, hands-on experience with enterprise filesystems and storage (block/file/object); exposure to cloud (AWS/Azure/GCP) is a plus, but not required.
-  Strong grasp of distributed systems, storage protocols, and performance/scalability trade-offs — you can read an architecture diagram and hold your own in a design review.
-  A track record of delivery you can walk through in detail: what you shipped, why, the impact, and how you measured it.
-  A customer-obsessed, ownership-driven philosophy — “shipped” is the starting line, not the finish line.
-  Experience partnering with Marketing on launches, packaging/pricing, and blogs — and a habit of tracking a product after it ships.
-  MBA, cloud certifications, or exposure to agentic AI/LLM system design are a plus, not a requirement.
