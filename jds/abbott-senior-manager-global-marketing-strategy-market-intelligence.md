The Opportunity

Structural Heart Business Mission: why we exist
Our business purpose is to restore health and improve quality of life through the design and provision of device and management solutions for the treatment of structural heart disease.

The Global Strategy Manager will be part of the Global Strategy team in the Structural Heart headquarters in Santa Clara, California and will develop qualitative and quantitative market insights in support of strategic business decisions.

The candidate will be expected to quickly develop an understanding of the Structural Heart space, Abbott's product offerings, key market dynamics and internal strategic processes. Essential success factors for this role include strong business acumen, attention to details, expertise in PowerPoint & Excel, ability to work independently under tight deadlines and strong interpersonal skills to liaise with cross-functional stakeholders.
