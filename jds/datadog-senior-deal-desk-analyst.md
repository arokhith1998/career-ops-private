## Full JD text

This is a growth position supporting Datadog's expanding business through cross-functional collaboration with Sales Operations, Billing, Contracts, and Legal teams on quoting and deal structuring.

### Key Responsibilities
- Develop expertise in Datadog's licensing and pricing models for deal structuring support
- Solve customer satisfaction issues post-deal execution
- Negotiate directly with customers as needed
- Work cross-functionally with legal, contracts, sales operations, order management, revenue, finance, and product management
- Serve as subject matter expert for sales on pricing matters
- Drive adherence to internal controls and business rules

### Requirements
- 4+ years of Deal Desk experience
- Expertise in structuring/quoting complex SaaS transactions
- Knowledge of revenue recognition principles
- Strong attention to detail and communication skills
- Ability to work independently in fast-paced environments
- Data-driven with strong quantitative skills

**Bonus Skills:** Salesforce, CPQ, advanced MS Excel proficiency
