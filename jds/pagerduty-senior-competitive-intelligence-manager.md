## Full Job Description

### About PagerDuty
PagerDuty, Inc. (NYSE: PD) is the global leader in AI-first digital operations. By automatically detecting, diagnosing, and remediating issues, the PagerDuty Platform orchestrates AI agents and automated workflows with context from over 750 integrations. Trusted by approximately two-thirds of the Fortune 100 and nearly half of the Fortune 500.

### Position Overview
The Senior Competitive Intelligence Manager will build and run PagerDuty's competitive intelligence program that serves as the internal authority on competitive dynamics, equipping sales teams with sharp differentiation, timely insights and effective strategies to win. Reports directly to the Senior Director of PMM Operations; partners with product marketing, product management, marketing, field enablement and sellers.

### Key Responsibilities
- Define, execute, optimize the competitive intelligence program through feedback and engagement with cross-functional teams, stakeholders, and the field
- Equip sales team with key positioning and messaging via battlecards, bulletins, internal training and enablement
- Uncover competitive differentiation and maintain thorough knowledge of Win/Loss and competitor capabilities
- Monitor and assess competitor activity; report timely updates to stakeholders and field
- Train field teams regularly on latest competitor intelligence and winning strategies
- Work with product and PMM team to articulate messaging and GTM approaches
- Provide independent view of win/loss via external interview program
- Speed and improve research and content delivery with AI and automation tools

### Required Skills & Experience
- Deep fluency in IT and Digital Operations
- Track record of at least 5+ years leading competitive intelligence for product lines; SaaS B2B strongly preferred
- Field-focused, obsessed with equipping them with best messages, tools and differentiation approaches
- Excel at framing problems, balancing trade-offs, synthesizing clear solutions
- Plan work, drive through ambiguity, deliver timely high-quality content
- Organize complex information, simplify, articulate essentials, inspire the field
- Desire to work with AI tools to automate and improve intelligence delivery

### Preferred Qualifications
Deep fluency in IT and Digital Operations; fluency in SRE and DevOps domain; technical aptitude for deeper conversations with engineers.
