## Description Summary
Role: second person for Hover's new account-based marketing (ABM) function in the enterprise marketing team, supporting sales in construction and insurance; builds much of the ABM foundation (account tiering, vertical playbooks, co-marketing program). Reports to the Senior Director, Enterprise Marketing; works with sales, customer success, product marketing, enablement, and partner managers.

Responsibilities: run/maintain vertical ABM playbooks for insurance and construction; build account tiering frameworks and account-specific plans; run ABM campaigns with channel owners; evaluate and rebuild the co-marketing program; track marketing-influenced revenue and campaign performance; align with sales, customer success, and partner managers on account priorities; create account-specific materials with product marketing and enablement; use AI tools to scale the function without adding headcount.

Requirements: 8-12+ years of B2B, enterprise, or related marketing experience; experience working directly with sales on enterprise accounts; B2B marketing experience, ideally with long enterprise sales cycles; a record of building programs from scratch; comfort with marketing/sales stack data including fixing data hygiene issues; genuine interest in AI tools and their effect on marketing.

Nice-to-haves: construction, building products, or property insurance experience; startup experience; partner/channel co-marketing experience; familiarity with ABM platforms and multi-touch attribution.

Benefits: competitive salary and equity; medical/dental/vision; unlimited flexible vacation; paid family leave; monthly self-care day; remote wellbeing resources; learning support for management training, conferences, workshops, or certifications.
