## Role Summary

Runway builds AI world models for robotics, scientific discovery, and storytelling. This is a builder role: create new demand-generation programs from scratch, ship quickly, and scale what works, partnering with Sales, Product Marketing, Content, Design, and RevOps.

## You Will

- Plan, launch, and optimize paid campaigns on LinkedIn, Google, Meta, and content syndication; own budget pacing, testing, and CAC/pipeline/ROI reporting.
- Run the webinar program end to end (topics, speakers, promotion, production, follow-up through pipeline conversion).
- Work with Sales on target accounts and tiered ABM plays (1:1, 1:few, 1:many).
- Use paid and organic social as a demand channel with Content and Creative.
- Define KPIs per program with RevOps dashboards; launch new channels and scale what works.

## You Have

- 3+ years running paid campaigns on LinkedIn, Google, or Meta with a record of hitting pipeline targets.
- Experience building and scaling a webinar program that converts to pipeline.
- Experience running ABM with Sales; track record using paid/organic social for demand.
- Strong analytics (attribution, funnel metrics); working knowledge of marketing automation/CRM (HubSpot/Marketo, Salesforce).
- Experience marketing to large, complex enterprises with long sales cycles; strong project management.
