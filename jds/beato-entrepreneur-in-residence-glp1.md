## Full JD text (verbatim from Workable .md endpoint)

BeatO (Health Arx Technologies Pvt. Ltd.) is India's most trusted digital platform for diabetes and metabolic health, built by founders Gautam Chopra, Yash Sehgal and Kunal Kinalekar to close a very real gap: over 200 million Indians live with or are at risk of diabetes, and most have no continuous, affordable way to manage it.

BeatO solved this through a full-stack model combining smart connected devices (glucometers, CGMs, BP monitors), doctor- and coach-led care programs, and a range of food and supplements, all integrated into a single app experience.

The scale speaks for itself:
- Over 2 million patients
- 10 lakh+ device users
- Network of 100+ doctors and 200+ coaches
- Published clinical outcomes with an average HbA1c reduction of 2.16%
- More than 70% of users come from Tier 2 cities and beyond

BeatO has raised over $33 million in Series B funding from Lightrock India, HealthQuad, Flipkart Ventures, Orios Venture Partners, W Health Ventures, and Blume Ventures.

BeatO's newest and fastest-growing business is its GLP-1-led weight management program, a doctor-supervised offering that combines medication, endocrinologist support, and IIHMR-certified diet coaching. The business is growing rapidly and now requires a dedicated business owner.

THE OPPORTUNITY: BeatO is hiring an Entrepreneur in Residence (EIR) to take complete ownership of its GLP-1 Weight Management Business, currently generating approximately Rs 1 crore in monthly revenue. This is not a strategy or support role. It is a business leadership mandate where you'll run the business like your own, with full ownership of the P&L, growth targets, and business performance.

Key Responsibilities:
- Own the complete P&L for the GLP-1 business, including revenue, margins, unit economics, and burn.
- Drive business growth across acquisition, conversion, retention, pricing, and expansion beyond the current Rs 1 crore monthly run rate.
- Ensure quality of patient care by working closely with clinical, coaching, and operations teams.
- Design and execute experiments across new channels, partnerships, and program formats, while quickly shutting down ineffective initiatives.
- Work directly with the founders and leadership team.

WHO WE'RE LOOKING FOR: This role is ideal for someone who has already run a business or owned a business unit, rather than simply contributing to one. Experience in GLP-1, weight management, consumer health, or healthtech is a strong advantage.

Ideal Candidate:
- 5+ years of experience with ownership of a P&L or business unit
- Comfortable balancing both strategy and execution
- Strong understanding of financials and business metrics
- Experience building or scaling businesses in consumer health, D2C, healthtech, or other high-growth sectors
- Seeks ownership, accountability, and impact over hierarchy or large teams

WHY THIS ROLE: GLP-1-based weight management is one of the fastest-growing healthcare categories globally, and BeatO is already an established, well-funded player in this space.
