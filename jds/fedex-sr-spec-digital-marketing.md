# Sr Spec-Digital Marketing — FedEx Freight

**URL:** https://careers.fedex.com/sr-spec-digital-marketing/job/P25-329882-1
**Saved:** 2026-04-10
**Location:** Memphis, TN (hybrid, several times weekly in-office)
**Salary:** Not specified

## Job Description
Develop and execute digital marketing strategies to support FedEx Freight's product and service portfolio while driving customer engagement initiatives.

## Responsibilities
- Create business cases for marketing initiatives with defined objectives, success metrics, and resource requirements
- Plan and manage multiple marketing campaigns with alignment to organizational priorities
- Identify and analyze target audiences using customer insights and data segmentation
- Monitor campaign performance and optimize content, messaging, and channel effectiveness
- Contribute to digital marketing strategy supporting brand awareness and conversion goals
- Collaborate cross-functionally on storytelling and content alignment
- Support marketing channel planning with Performance Marketing and Operations teams
- Stay current with digital marketing tools, platforms, and industry best practices
- Ensure compliance with applicable laws, regulations, and company policies

## Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in Marketing, Finance, Computer Science, Engineering, or related field
- 3+ years in digital marketing or digital product management
- Strong understanding of business/customer needs and user experience management
- Technical acumen and product analysis capabilities
- Excellent collaboration, communication, and analytical skills
