## Description
Glean seeks a strategic PM to define and build the Growth product area, focusing on product-led growth, platform activation, and engagement strategies. The role involves designing loops and experiences that drive activation, retention, and expansion across users and teams. The PM will work cross-functionally with engineering, design, data, and go-to-market teams to identify high-leverage growth opportunities and translate concepts into measurable product experiences.

## Key Responsibilities
- Define growth product strategy and roadmap across activation, retention, and expansion
- Own PLG motion end-to-end from acquisition through habit formation
- Build platform-level capabilities like referrals, invitations, notifications, and lifecycle messaging
- Drive off-surface growth across browser extensions, email, Slack, Teams, and mobile
- Partner with teams to scope products and experiments with clear metrics
- Build experimentation and measurement culture
- Ensure high product craft standards in onboarding and adoption flows
