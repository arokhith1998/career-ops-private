Role Overview
We are looking for a driven and analytical professional to join the Founder's Office - Business Planning & Analysis team at a fast-growing organisation. The ideal candidate should have strong analytical and financial modelling skills, a solid understanding of business strategy, and the ability to translate financial insights into actionable business outcomes.

Key Responsibilities
- Drive business planning, forecasting, and budgeting for new and existing business initiatives.
- Analyse business performance and prepare management reports (MIS) to support leadership decisions.
- Build financial models and conduct scenario analyses to evaluate business opportunities.
- Identify key business drivers, growth opportunities, and areas for operational and financial improvement.
- Evaluate the financial viability and potential returns of strategic initiatives.
- Collaborate cross-functionally with different teams to gather business inputs and assess performance.
- Track key business metrics and provide actionable insights to improve business outcomes.
- Support strategic planning and decision-making through financial analysis and data-driven recommendations.

Must-Have Requirements
- 3-6 years of relevant experience in business finance, FP&A, business planning, financial analysis, consulting, or strategic finance.
- Strong analytical, quantitative, and problem-solving skills.
- Understanding of financial planning, budgeting, forecasting, and business performance analysis.
- Hands-on experience in financial modelling and scenario analysis.
- Strong proficiency in Excel and the ability to analyse complex business data.
- Ability to interpret financial and business metrics and generate actionable insights.
- Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills.
- Willingness to work 5 days a week from the Gurugram office.

Good to Have
- MBA or Master's degree in Finance or a related field.
- Experience working closely with senior leadership or in a Founder's Office.
- Exposure to strategic initiatives, business expansion, and financial viability assessments.
- Experience preparing MIS reports, dashboards, and management presentations.

Candidate Profile
- Analytical and detail-oriented, with a strong business mindset.
- Comfortable working with financial data, business metrics, and management reports.
- Able to connect financial performance with business strategy and operational outcomes.
- Proactive, commercially aware, and comfortable working in a fast-paced environment.
- Strong ownership and cross-functional collaboration skills.
