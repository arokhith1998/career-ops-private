About Vercel: Vercel is the agentic infrastructure company, freeing people and agents to ship what's next. About the Role: Build and run Vercel's Product Strategy & Operations function from the ground up. This senior, cross-functional role sits at the heart of the EPD organization and works in close partnership with the CPO, EPD Leads, GTM leadership, and Finance. Own three core things: the rigor that makes high-velocity shipping sustainable, measurement infrastructure to determine product success post-launch, and planning systems that keep roadmap and revenue plan sharp, funded, and aligned.

What You Will Do: Own EPD rhythm: weekly operating reviews, monthly business reviews, planning cycles, roadmap processes. Run ShipOps function for Launch Calendar reviews and high-velocity "You Can Just Ship Things" approach. Establish "product landing" framework for post-launch adoption, business outcome verification, and iteration learnings. Partner with Sales and Marketing on targets, plans, and tactics for pipeline creation and user launches. Develop competitive intelligence systems for continuous market landscape updates. Partner with Data Engineering/Science on analytical infrastructure supporting analysis speed. Partner with EPD leads on team health, capacity visibility, and resource allocation.

About You: 8+ years in product operations, business operations, strategy, or adjacent function in high-growth tech. Understand complex technical products; articulate value to non-technical audiences. Deep familiarity with full software development lifecycle. Passion for structuring and solving ambiguous strategic problems. Experience leading complex cross-functional projects. Background working at intersection of product and go-to-market teams. Strong oral and written communication skills.

Bonus Qualifications: Background in developer tooling, cloud infrastructure, or platform products. Experience building agents for workflows and data analyses.
