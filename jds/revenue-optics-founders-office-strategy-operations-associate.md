We're Hiring: Founder's Office – Strategy & Operations Associate📍 100% Remote | India🌎 US-Based Company | US Business Hours💼 Full-Time | Early Career🚀 Work with Senior Leadership (Director Level & Above)Want to learn how a growing US company operates from the inside while helping shape how it scales?Revenue Optics is looking for an ambitious, analytical, and highly organized Founder's Office – Strategy & Operations Associate to join our growing team.This isn't a traditional administrative role. You'll work alongside senior leaders across business strategy, commercial consulting, pricing, AI products, talent solutions, and growth marketing.You'll turn complex information into executive-ready presentations, research companies and industries, coordinate strategic initiatives, and use AI to make business operations smarter and more efficient. We're looking for someone who combines the analytical thinking of a consultant, the execution discipline of a project manager, and the curiosity of someone who wants to build a business.ABOUT REVENUE OPTICSRevenue Optics is a US-based commercial growth company headquartered in Memphis, Tennessee.We help B2B distributors, industrial manufacturers, and private equity-backed businesses accelerate revenue growth, improve pricing, build high-performing sales organizations, and implement AI-enabled solutions.We're building a company where strategy, technology, and execution come together to deliver measurable business results.You'll have an opportunity to see how decisions are made, how commercial opportunities are evaluated, and how senior leaders turn strategy into results.WHAT YOU'LL DO📊 Executive Research & Strategic AnalysisResearch companies, industries, competitors, and emerging market opportunities.Analyze complex information and translate findings into actionable insights.Develop executive-ready PowerPoint presentations, strategic briefs, and leadership reports.Turn lengthy documents, meeting transcripts, and research into concise, compelling business stories.🎯 Strategic Initiatives & Business OperationsSupport senior leadership on cross-functional business priorities.Coordinate initiatives spanning commercial consulting, pricing, talent solutions, AI, and marketing.Maintain project trackers, milestones, action items, and executive dashboards.Prepare leadership meeting agendas, materials, and follow-up communications.Ensure commitments have clear owners, deadlines, and documented outcomes.🤖 AI, Automation & Operational ExcellenceUse tools such as ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and other AI platforms to accelerate research, analysis, and content creation.Identify repetitive workflows that can be automated.Build practical AI-assisted processes that improve productivity and execution.Use sound judgment to validate AI outputs for accuracy and quality.📈 Executive Communication & Project OwnershipWork with senior leaders to turn ambiguous requests into structured deliverables.Communicate progress, risks, and deadlines proactively.Develop professional presentations, summaries, reports, and internal communications.Own assigned deliverables from initial request through completion.WHO WE'RE LOOKING FORRequired Qualifications✅ Recent graduate or early-career professional with strong academic or practical accomplishments.✅ Exceptional written and spoken English communication skills.✅ Strong PowerPoint presentation and business storytelling abilities.✅ Ability to research unfamiliar topics, synthesize information, and develop logical recommendations.✅ Highly organized, detail-oriented, and comfortable managing multiple priorities.✅ At least one year of practical experience using AI tools such as ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini.✅ Proactive mindset with the ability to work independently and take ownership.✅ Comfortable communicating with senior leadership and following through on commitments.✅ Availability to work primarily US business hours from India.Preferred BackgroundBusiness Administration, Management, Economics, Engineering, Analytics, or related disciplines.Exposure to business strategy, consulting, program management, business operations, or research.Experience developing professional PowerPoint presentations or executive reports.Familiarity with project management platforms, spreadsheets, and automation tools.Interest in a future career in strategy, operations leadership, or a business management function.We value demonstrated ability, curiosity, and initiative more than a particular degree or prestigious employer.WHY JOIN REVENUE OPTICS?🌟 Executive Exposure: Work with Directors and senior leaders across multiple business functions.🚀 Accelerated Learning: Understand how a growing US company makes decisions, wins clients, develops new offerings, and scales operations.🧠 Meaningful Responsibilities: Go beyond routine tasks by contributing to research, strategy, presentations, and operational improvements.🤖 AI-First Environment: Work in a business that actively uses artificial intelligence to improve how teams operate.🌍 Global Experience: Collaborate with professionals supporting US industrial manufacturers, distributors, and private equity-backed companies.📈 Career Development: Build a strong foundation for future opportunities in strategy, business operations, program management, and executive leadership support.OUR SELECTION PROCESSWe're looking for people who can demonstrate their capabilities, not simply describe them on a résumé.Shortlisted candidates will be invited to submit a five-minute video walkthrough you used AI or technology to improve efficiency or the quality of your work.Project Management & Ownership: How you manage competing priorities, track deadlines, and follow through on commitments.Problem-Solving: An example of turning an unclear assignment into a completed deliverable.Communication: Your ability to present ideas clearly, confidently, and professionally.Examples may come from professional experience, internships, academic projects, or independent initiatives.READY TO BUILD YOUR CAREER?If you're someone who enjoys solving problems, learning quickly, creating
