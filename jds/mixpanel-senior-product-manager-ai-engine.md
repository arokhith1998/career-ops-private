<div class="content-intro"><p><strong>About Mixpanel</strong></p>
<p>Mixpanel is the leading product intelligence and analytics platform, trusted by more than 29,000 companies to help understand how people use the products they build. By combining powerful analytics with AI that knows your business, Mixpanel helps teams see what’s working, diagnose what’s not, and decide what to build next. Learn more at <a href="https://mixpanel.com/home/">mixpanel.com</a>.</p></div><p><strong>About the Team</strong></p>
<p>The mission of the AI Engine team is to ground Mixpanel in AI-native foundations, enabling internal teams to deploy capabilities with accelerating speed while providing customers with intelligent, highly context-aware product insights.. The team owns the full MCP stack (server, client, and dynamic client registration), the context engine, Mixpanel's AI quality and evals framework, prompt infrastructure, and AI compliance.</p>
<p>AI Engine serves three customers at once.&nbsp;</p>
<ul>
<li>Product builders — the PMs, engineers, and analysts at companies using Mixpanel — experience the platform indirectly, through whether Mixpanel AI gives them trustworthy answers.&nbsp;</li>
<li>Every internal Mixpanel team shipping an AI feature is a direct customer: when the platform is there, they build with speed and confidence instead of rebuilding foundations from scratch.&nbsp;</li>
<li>And increasingly, the “user” isn't human at all — it's an agent that depends on the team's context engine, MCP coverage, and quality guarantees to act on a person's behalf.</li>
</ul>
<p>The team is also its own best customer: every AI feature inside Mixpanel runs on the same substrate shipped to the rest of the company, so the foundation gets tested by Mixpanel's own product teams before anyone else touches it.</p>
<p><strong>About the Role</strong></p>
<p>As Senior Product Manager for AI Engine, you'll own the roadmap for the infrastructure every AI surface at Mixpanel is built on — from MCP and the context engine to the evals framework that defines what “good” means for an AI agent. Your stakeholders are mostly other builders: engineers, PMs on other teams, and increasingly the agents themselves. Your product's success shows up in what it lets other teams ship, not in a single customer-facing feature.</p>
<p>You'll work closely with engineering and design to take the team from MCP parity today toward trusted, autonomous agents — establishing quality baselines, driving adoption of shared infrastructure across the rest of Mixpanel's product org, and making the case, in writing, for where the platform should invest next.</p>
<p><strong>What You'll Do</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Set and evolve the strategy for Mixpanel's AI platform — using adoption data from internal teams, agent usage patterns, and customer signal to decide where the platform should invest next</li>
<li>Align engineering and design on the highest-leverage substrate work — MCP parity, the context engine, the evals framework, prompt infrastructure — with clear, written plans that hold up under scrutiny</li>
<li>Work closely with engineering to experiment with and ship new platform capabilities, from MCP tooling to model routing to context memory, without sacrificing the reliability every downstream team depends on</li>
<li>Define what “quality” means for an AI agent and build the evals, baselines, and dashboards that make it measurable — then track whether what shipped actually moved it</li>
<li>Drive adoption of shared infrastructure across other product teams — decide the right model (opt-in, mandated standard, or incentive-based) and own the motion that gets teams to build on the platform instead of rebuilding it themselves</li>
<li>Tell a clear story about platform investments — to engineering leadership, to other PM teams, and eventually to customers — that connects infrastructure work to the business outcomes it unlocks</li>
<li>Prioritize ruthlessly across a long list of platform bets, balancing unblocking internal teams today against the long-term bet on an external, customer-facing intelligence layer</li>
<li>Help define the AI compliance and trust standards every team shipping an AI feature at Mixpanel has to meet</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>What We're Looking For</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>5+ years of hands-on product management experience, including time as a platform, infrastructure, or developer-tools PM</li>
<li>Comfort being the PM for engineers and other internal teams rather than only end customers — you're energized by a product whose success is measured in what it lets other people build</li>
<li>An ability to synthesize usage data, agent behavior, and qualitative feedback from internal stakeholders to drive clarity on what the platform should do next</li>
<li>A track record of developing, articulating, and executing a product strategy that other teams had to align behind, not just your own</li>
<li>Experience using AI tools in product management workflows (e.g., competitive research, prototyping)</li>
<li>Direct experience working with LLMs, agents, or AI quality/evals — you understand why performing well on a handful of examples isn't the same as being reliable in production</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Bonus Points For</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Experience with MCP (Model Context Protocol), agent frameworks, or building on top of LLM APIs</li>
<li>Experience as a PM for a developer platform, API product, or internal infrastructure team</li>
<li>Experience using Mixpanel as an end customer</li>
<li>Experience writing production-grade software</li>
<li>Experience designing products using tools like Figma, Sketch, or Abstract</li>
