# Product Manager - Mixpanel

## Responsibilities

Use insight from customer conversations and Mixpanel's own product data to
shape strategy and direction for their area. Key activities include aligning
teams on important problems through clear communication, experimenting with
and shipping new capabilities, and telling compelling stories about shipped
features that center customer problems.

Additional duties encompass ruthless prioritization across potential
initiatives, defining success metrics, cross-functional alignment with
engineering, design, sales, marketing and support, competitive landscape
monitoring, and helping shape the product function's evolution.

## Required Qualifications

Hands-on product management experience with demonstrated user research skills
and customer hypothesis testing capabilities. Strong ability to synthesize
quantitative and qualitative signals, a track record developing product
strategies, and experience using AI tools for product management workflows
like competitive research and prototyping.

## Nice-to-Have Skills

Prior Mixpanel customer usage, production-grade software writing experience,
and proficiency with design tools such as Figma, Sketch, or Abstract.

## Compensation & Location

$175,000 - $210,000 USD total target cash compensation with equity
eligibility and comprehensive benefits. Based in San Francisco (hybrid
arrangement).
