Job Responsibilities:

Outbound Pipe Generation (50%)

- Campaign Execution: Ship and evaluate 20+ high-intent outbound campaigns per quarter.

- Integrations & Scoring: Implement low-code cross-system integrations and build custom account scoring models based on intent, firmographics, and engagement.
- Experimentation: Continuously test and iterate on outbound plays to improve conversion rates.

Inbound Funnel Orchestration (30%)

- Inbound Lifecycle & Routing: Automate capture, scoring, and SLA routing of inbound leads (form, chat, visitor intent) to reps or AI agents.
- Marketing Alignment: Align campaign timing/targeting with scoring models to convert air-cover spend into qualified pipeline.
- Tracking & Reporting: Monitor speed-to-lead cadences, build stage-by-stage conversion reporting (MQL to SQL to opp), and resolve handoff stalls.
- Feedback Loops: Connect inbound conversion data with outbound play design for bi-directional optimization.

Reporting & Business Impact (20%)

- Analytics & Dashboards: Build reporting infrastructure to track performance metrics (conversion, pipeline contribution, efficiency) and show incremental value.
- Experiments & Insights: Run tooling/process experiments and provide data-driven recommendations to leadership on resource allocation.
