## Full JD text

For our client, we are seeking a Senior Manager, Pricing, Contracting & Revenue Analytics to join the team of a leader in the Pharmaceuticals & Biotech space.

This role will lead pricing, gross-to-net, and revenue analytics in support of commercial decision-making across a vaccine portfolio. The ideal candidate will translate complex market, channel, and contract data into clear insights that strengthen pricing strategy, improve margin performance, and reduce revenue leakage. This position will also partner closely with pricing leadership and governance stakeholders to monitor performance and support disciplined commercial execution.

Location: Remote - US based candidates only, no visa sponsorship available

Compensation: $150,000 – $195,000 annually

Responsibilities

- Develop and maintain pricing, net revenue, and gross-to-net analytical models
- Analyze realized price, discount effectiveness, and margin performance by channel
- Monitor pricing performance relative to approved price corridors
- Identify revenue leakage and pricing performance variances
- Evaluate channel inventory movement and purchase behavior
- Produce structured analysis of market performance and sales trends
- Provide analytical support for pricing committee and governance processes

Qualifications

- Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Economics, Business, Data Analytics, or a related field
- 7+ years of experience in commercial analytics, pricing, or commercial finance
- Experience with large commercial data sets and revenue performance analysis
- Strong financial modeling capability, including pricing, margin, gross-to-net, and revenue analysis
- Proficiency in Excel; experience with SQL and/or Python preferred
- Ability to synthesize complex datasets into structured insights
- Strong communication skills for presenting findings to stakeholders

Benefits

- Remote work flexibility
- Opportunity to influence pricing strategy across a vaccine portfolio
- Engagement with multi-channel vaccine commercialization
- Access to advanced analytical tools and frameworks
- Collaboration with pricing and contracting leadership

Our client is an equal opportunity employer. We encourage you to apply even if you don’t meet every qualification—your background could be exactly what this team needs.
