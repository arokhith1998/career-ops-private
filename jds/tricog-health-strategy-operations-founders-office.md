Role Title -Strategy & Operations - Founder's Office

About Company
Tricog is a healthcare technology company headquartered in Bengaluru, focused on transforming cardiac care through innovative AI-powered solutions and connected healthcare technology. Our products enable hospitals, clinics, and healthcare professionals to deliver faster, more accurate diagnoses and improve patient outcomes across the globe.As the Executive Assistant, you will play a critical role in enabling the CEO to operate efficiently by managing priorities, coordinating strategic activities, and ensuring seamless communication across internal and external stakeholders. This role requires exceptional organizational skills, sound judgment, discretion, and the ability to thrive in a fast-paced, high-growth environment.

Role Overview
We are looking for a highly organized, proactive, and tech-savvy Executive Assistant to provide end-to-end executive support to the CEO. This role goes beyond calendar management and administrative coordination. It requires someone who can anticipate needs, manage multiple priorities, leverage modern productivity and AI tools, and independently drive follow-ups to completion.The ideal candidate is detail-oriented, resourceful, comfortable working with senior leadership, and capable of handling confidential information with the highest level of professionalism.

Key Responsibilities
- Manage the CEO's calendar, prioritise meetings and resolve scheduling conflicts.
- Coordinate internal and external meetings across multiple time zones.
- Manage domestic and international travel, including visas, accommodation, transport and itineraries.
- Act as the point of coordination for leadership, board members, investors, customers, and partners.
- Prepare agendas, meeting briefs, presentations, reports and relevant documents.
- Hands-on agentic tools and up to date with technology.
- Record key decisions and follow up on action items until closure.
- Manage expenses, reimbursements, invoices, signatures and important documentation.
- Maintain strict confidentiality of business, employee and personal information.
- Anticipate requirements and independently act on short instructions with minimal follow-up.
- Work full-time from the Bengaluru office and remain flexible for critical requirements.

Required Skills
- Excellent verbal and written communication skills.
- Exceptional organizational and time management abilities.
- Strong stakeholder management and interpersonal skills.
- Ability to manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment.
- High attention to detail with excellent execution capabilities.
- Strong problem-solving skills and the ability to make sound decisions independently.
- High level of integrity, professionalism, and discretion while handling confidential information.
- Proficiency in Google Workspace (Docs, Sheets, Slides, Calendar, Gmail).
- Comfortable using AI-powered productivity tools and modern workplace technologies.
- Ability to work independently with minimal supervision and proactively anticipate requirements.

Good to Have
- Experience supporting CXOs, founders, or senior leadership.
- Familiarity with project management and collaboration tools such as Jira, Zoho or similar platforms.
- Experience coordinating international travel, visa processes, and executive events.
- Knowledge of expense management and documentation processes.

Key Deliverables
- Efficient management of the CEO's calendar and daily priorities.
- Seamless coordination of meetings across internal and external stakeholders.
- Well-planned domestic and international travel arrangements with complete itineraries.
- Timely preparation of meeting materials, presentations, and executive documents.
- Accurate tracking and closure of action items arising from meetings.
- Timely processing of executive expenses, reimbursements, invoices, and documentation.
- Effective stakeholder communication and coordination.
- Secure handling of confidential business information.
- Continuous improvement in executive productivity through the adoption of AI and automation tools.

Experience
- 4-6 years of experience as an Executive Assistant, Executive Coordinator, Administration, or a similar executive support role.

Work Location
- Bengaluru (Work From Office - Full Time).
- Flexibility to support critical business requirements beyond regular working hours when necessary.

What We Look For
- Highly proactive with a strong sense of ownership.
- Excellent planning and execution skills.
- Strong business acumen and professional maturity.
- Ability to think ahead and anticipate executive needs.
- Calm under pressure and capable of managing changing priorities.
- Tech-savvy with a passion for leveraging AI and automation to improve productivity.
- Reliable, trustworthy, and committed to maintaining confidentiality.
- Collaborative mindset with exceptional stakeholder management skills.
