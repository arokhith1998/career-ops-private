About Sova Health

Sova Health is a leading D2C preventive healthcare company focused on transforming personalized wellness through science, data, and AI. Our mission is to build India’s most trusted precision health platform by delivering tailored health solutions that drive measurable outcomes.

Role Overview

The Founder’s Office is the engine room of Sova. In this role, you’ll work directly with the founding team on high-priority initiatives across strategy, operations, growth, and execution. The ideal candidate is a problem-solver, project owner, and strategic extension of the founders to bring clarity, structure, and momentum to fast-moving business needs.

This is a high-ownership role for someone who enjoys ambiguity, learns fast, and thrives in a fast-paced environment where every decision leaves a visible impact.

Key Responsibilities

- Work closely with founders to execute mission-critical strategic and operational projects.
- Lead cross-functional initiatives from scoping to execution to measurable outcomes.
- Conduct market and competitive research to generate actionable insights for business decisions.
- Build dashboards, reports, and documentation to improve visibility and alignment across teams.
- Collaborate with Growth, Sales, Operations, Marketing, Product, and HR to drive seamless execution.
- Identify bottlenecks, streamline workflows, and implement process improvements across business units.
- Own special projects such as new product/feature launches, business experiments, or GTM initiatives.
- Prepare internal and external communication materials, including founder decks, investor updates, and leadership review documents.

Qualifications & Experience

- 2–5 years of experience in startups, consulting, strategy, VC/PE, operations, or similar high-performance roles.
- Strong analytical skills paired with strategic thinking.
- Exceptional communication that is clear, structured, and crisp.
- Ability to work under ambiguity and build structure in unstructured environments.
- High ownership mindset and comfort with rapid execution cycles.
- Proficiency in Excel, PowerPoint/Google Slides, Notion, and Google Workspace.
- Bonus: Exposure to automation tools or no-code workflows.
