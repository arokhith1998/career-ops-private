## Job Description (full, verbatim)
About
Arceus is building the AI-native law firm for the next generation of category-defining companies.

We're replacing the slow, expensive, traditional legal experience with AI agents that make world-class legal expertise fast, affordable, and accessible to every business.

We believe the biggest opportunity in the $1T legal industry isn't selling software to law firms, but delivering legal outcomes and services directly to businesses and displacing traditional law firms.

Arceus was founded by Mac Liu, who led engineering at Anduril as one of its first 30 engineers and led banking and internationalization as an early team member at Robinhood. He also founded Vultron, which raised $22M before being acquired.

We're looking for a Founding Growth Marketing Lead to help define a new category, build an unmistakable brand, and turn attention into qualified pipeline alongside our GTM team.

This is an in-person role based in San Francisco.

THE OPPORTUNITY
We announced $17M in funding in October and are growing tremendously. Marketing is already in motion across events, content, creators, paid media, and partnerships; now we're looking for a Founding Growth Marketing Lead to turn that momentum into a marketing function that builds our brand and consistently generates revenue.

You'll work directly with Mac to shape our narrative, create campaigns people remember, and turn attention into customers.

What You'll Own
- Identify, launch, and scale growth experiments that drive revenue; examples include: events, conferences, brand moments, product launches, gifting, paid media, etc.
- Create systems to continuously optimize and measure growth channels
- Own large budgets and autonomy to scale, maintain or deprioritize growth channels
- Control brand, narrative, and positioning across every touchpoint
- Write content across LinkedIn, X, blog, and newsletter, including founder and team voice
- Manage creators and influencers, from sourcing to briefs to measuring impact
- Manage relationships with referral and event partners
- Own onboarding, activation, nurture, reactivation, and other campaigns that convert on-demand customers to annualized plans

Experience
- You have 4+ years at a high-growth B2B startup as a founder or leading marketing or growth
- Own marketing-generated qualified pipeline and customer acquisition
- You've personally built campaigns that generated measurable pipeline and revenue.
- You write exceptionally well and have sharp taste in design, voice, and storytelling
- You think in systems, not one-off campaigns, and can build automations when needed
- (Bonus) You've helped an early-stage startup grow from first customers to scale
- (Bonus) You are hands-on with AI and automation tools

Compensation & Benefits
- Base salary: $160,000 - $220,000
- Competitive equity and ownership in the company
- Comprehensive health, dental, and vision coverage
- Catered lunches and dinners at the office
- Wellness and learning stipends
- Option to contribute to a 401(k)
- Unlimited PTO

Compensation Range: $160K - $220K
