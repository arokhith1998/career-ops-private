From Fivetran's founding until now, our mission has remained the same: to make access to data as simple and reliable as electricity.

About Us: Fivetran and dbt Labs are bringing together two industry-leading companies with a shared mission: helping organizations unlock the full value of their data. During this transition period, you may see references to both Fivetran and dbt Labs throughout our recruiting process.

About The Role: Fivetran is building data pipelines to power the modern data stack for thousands of companies. The Senior Marketing Manager is responsible for driving brand awareness, demand, and building pipeline for Fivetran in the Commercial business segment. This role will support sales teams in North America through impactful, multi-touch marketing programs and execution. The ideal candidate is a results-driven professional with expertise in B2B lead generation within the small-to-medium business segment.

This is a full-time hybrid position based in our Denver, Austin, Philadelphia, Oakland or New York City office.

Technologies You'll Use: DemandBase, Webflow, Marketo, Sendoso, SFDC, Chat GPT, Claude, and Looker.

**What You'll Do**
- Develop and execute a comprehensive regional marketing strategy to drive brand awareness, demand, and pipeline.
- Work closely with teams across marketing to formulate and execute targeted, multi-channel demand generation programs.
- Manage program execution for influencer marketing, email marketing, events, sales plays and more.
- Partner with sales to prioritize top prospect accounts and develop targeted 1:few and 1:1 ABM campaigns.
- Plan and execute 1:few regional events, including executive dinners, networking events, and partner programs.
- Work closely with sales to ensure a seamless handoff of qualified leads.
- Closely monitor KPIs and metrics to measure program effectiveness and progress to targets.
- Collaborate with product marketing to develop relevant messaging, positioning, and segment-specific content plans.
- Build and maintain relationships with cross-functional teams, vendors, agencies, and strategic partners.

**Skills We're Looking For**
- 5+ years experience in B2B marketing with a focus on the commercial business market
- Proven track record of success in developing and executing lead generation programs within the commercial market
- Event management experience
- Excellent communication and collaboration skills
- Ability to manage multiple projects simultaneously and meet deadlines
- Data-driven approach with a knack for translating data insights into actionable marketing strategies
