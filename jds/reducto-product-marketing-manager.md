The Opportunity

As our Founding PMM, you’ll play a key role in driving Reducto’s self-service PLG and sales-led enterprise go-to-market motions. You’ll collaborate closely with product, engineering and GTM teams to drive new signups, product usage and enterprise expansion opportunities. This is a high-impact role where you’ll shape GTM strategy and campaign execution as we grow.

What You’ll Do

- Own the product marketing function and lay the foundation for scaling our GTM team.

- Drive GTM campaigns and product launches, coordinating across product, design, growth, and sales to drive awareness and self-service adoption.

- Craft messaging and positioning for Reducto products that resonates with both technical personas (AI/ML engineers, software developers, data engineers, etc.) and enterprise buyer personas (executive buyers, product managers, line of business owners, etc.).

- Partner with product and growth to optimize the self-serve funnel, from activation through expansion, using product data to identify friction and opportunity.

- Develop ICP and persona intelligence, mapping buyer journeys for both self-service users and enterprise customers.

- Create compelling and effective technical marketing content including web, blog, video, and social media to fuel marketing campaigns and demand generation efforts.

- Work closely with GTM ops team to measure our impact to the sales & marketing funnel (web traffic, leads, sales opportunities, etc.) and implement improvements to accelerate conversion rates at each stage of the funnel.

- Support sales enablement initiative that equip our teams to win: pitch decks, customer stories, competitive intelligence, and objection handling.
