## Full Job Description

### Innovate with purpose
At BILL, we believe in empowering the businesses that drive our economy. By replacing outdated financial processes with innovative tools, we help businesses -- from startups to established brands -- make smarter decisions and gain control of their operations. We're remote-first, with office spaces in San Jose, CA and Draper, UT -- and opportunities to connect in person when it counts.

We're looking for a Senior Sales Operations Analyst to join our Revenue Operations team. This is a hands-on, execution-focused individual-contributor role for someone who thrives on operating within our sales systems and keeping day-to-day GTM operations running smoothly. You'll own operational workflows end-to-end, work on problems of diverse scope, and exercise strong judgment in selecting the best approach to a solution.

Your day-to-day is operational execution within our sales stack -- administering and improving tooling, safeguarding data quality, and running the processes that keep Sales moving. You'll also produce the analysis that supports operational decision-making and the presentations that keep Sales leadership informed.

### What you'll do
- Own operational workflows end-to-end for a defined business area -- reporting, data governance, and process execution -- with limited oversight.
- Operate and improve GTM tooling: hands-on administrator for tools in the sales stack (Salesforce, LeanData, Outreach, and more); partner with BizTech on larger enhancements and enforce CRM data governance and process adherence.
- Build reporting and dashboards Sales relies on across pipeline, conversion, bookings, and attainment.
- Create analysis to support decision-making: extract, reconcile, and analyze operational data across CRM and connected systems.
- Build presentations for Sales leadership: package findings and operational updates into clear, executive-ready presentations.
- Execute and improve processes: identify patterns and improvement opportunities, recommend changes, implement more efficient, scalable ways of working.
- Partner cross-functionally with Sales Leadership, Marketing, Strategy, Data, Pricing, and BizTech.

### What you'll bring
- 3-5 years in revenue, sales, or business operations, with proven ownership of operational workflows and the ability to work independently.
- Salesforce experience -- hands-on building reports, dashboards, list views, workflows, and administration; strong understanding of CRM data governance and hygiene.
- Process design -- demonstrated ability to design, document, and improve scalable operational processes.
- Cross-functional partnership track record across Sales, Marketing, Finance, and BizTech.
- Analytical and data-driven; structured problem solver.
- Strong reporting skills (advanced Excel/Sheets) and ability to build polished presentations.
- LeanData experience (lead-to-account matching and routing).
- Outreach experience (sales engagement / sales automation platforms).
- Sales stack fluency (CPQ, sales intelligence, forecasting, enablement platforms).
