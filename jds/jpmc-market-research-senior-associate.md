## Role
Senior Associate on the Marketing Research team within JPMC's Insights Center of Excellence. Conduct multi-method consumer research, manage research vendors, synthesize findings, and present to senior leadership. Champion voice of customer across functions.

## Responsibilities
- Quickly understand the goals and needs of project stakeholders, while centering on the goals and needs of end-users.
- Select and manage research vendors to deliver research on time and on budget.
- Manage multiple projects and workstreams concurrently.
- Synthesize research findings and create relevant, revealing, and inspiring reporting.
- Prepare presentation materials for senior management.
- Deliver recommendations with confidence and consideration for diverse stakeholders.
- Champion the voice of the customer within the business and across functions.
- Play an active role, contribute to strategy discussions.
- Escalate project issues and risks appropriately, drive resolution proactively.
- Identify and document best practices, drive implementation.

## Required Qualifications
- 4+ years of experience in market/consumer research.
- Independent and self-motivated, able to create structure and direction out of ambiguity.
- Experience managing projects, project teams, working with stakeholders and research vendors.
- Strong communication, interpersonal, and project management skills.
- Analytical and conceptual thinking; ability to understand systems and processes.
- Proactively seeks guidance, clarification, and feedback.
- BA/BS degree required.

## Preferred Qualifications
- Master's degree in Marketing Research or related field.
- Experience with Financial Services.
