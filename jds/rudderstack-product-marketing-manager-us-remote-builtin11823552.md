About RudderStack: RudderStack helps companies build on top of their own data via a warehouse-native Customer Data Platform that lets engineering, data, and AI teams collect, unify, govern, and activate customer data while keeping full ownership and control, running directly on the customer's cloud data warehouse or lakehouse. The company is moving from a warehouse-native CDP toward a fully "Agentic CDP" built for the AI era.

About the role: a full-stack Product Marketing Manager covering positioning and messaging, go-to-market strategy and launches, competitive programs, sales enablement, and content; translating product and company strategy into campaigns and buyer-facing materials. Remote-first, can be based anywhere in the US.

What you'll do:
- Own positioning and messaging for RudderStack's category.
- Plan and run go-to-market for product launches end to end, including campaigns, webinars, sponsorships, and partner/analyst work.
- Own the competitive program: track the landscape, keep competitive materials current.
- Build sales enablement (decks, talk tracks, one-pagers, objection handling).
- Create content: announcement blogs, thought leadership, web/ad copy, social.
- Stay close to the product roadmap and company priorities.
- Use AI to streamline core marketing work where it adds leverage.

Qualifications:
- 4+ years in product marketing at a fast-paced software company, owning GTM strategy and major product launches, OR
- 4+ years in growth, performance, or lifecycle marketing, owning data-driven campaigns end to end.
- Strong grounding in the customer data tooling ecosystem (collection, unification, activation).
- Strong writing skills paired with execution ability.
- Comfort with ambiguity and a bias to action.
- Comfort with technical detail; ability to learn a deep product quickly.
- Low-ego, high-ownership working style across product, sales, and marketing.

Nice to have: experience marketing to technical/developer-adjacent buyers; background at a data infrastructure, martech, or developer-tooling company.

Candidates who don't check every box are still encouraged to apply.

Compensation and benefits: salary range $140,000-$160,000 for US-based candidates, set by job-related factors (experience, skills, location); eligible for equity/stock options; medical, dental, vision insurance; 401(k); PTO; paid parental leave; learning and home-office stipends; wellness benefits/perks.
