4. Conducts broad and comprehensive competitive analysis for specific products or product lines.
5. Applies broad and comprehensive knowledge to establish pricing strategies to build and protect a leadership position in market share while enhancing profit margins and developing marketing tools for successful product introductions.

7. Demonstrates broad and comprehensive understanding of technical and business environment. Develops and articulates strategies to meet business objectives.
8. Manages the Field support to ensure synergistic account approaches and optimization of all opportunities for account partnership and penetration.
