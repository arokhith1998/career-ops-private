## About Baseten
Powers mission-critical inference for AI companies (Cursor, Notion, OpenEvidence, Abridge, Clay, Gamma, Writer). Recently raised $1.5B Series F led by Altimeter Capital, Conviction Partners, Spark Capital.

## The Role
Make AI inference faster, smarter, and more efficient at scale - work with inference engineering/research to turn performance advances into product capabilities customers can adopt confidently.

## Responsibilities
- Work with inference engineering/research teams to bring new performance capabilities to customers.
- Translate advances in batching, caching, quantization, speculative decoding, serving architectures into product features.
- Define benchmarks/evaluations demonstrating improvements in latency, throughput, quality, cost.
- Understand customer workloads; help prioritize highest-impact optimizations.
- Make performance features easier to configure, evaluate, adopt in production.
- Help customers understand tradeoffs across hardware, model configs, autoscaling, routing.
- Partner with engineering on release readiness, compatibility, regression testing.

## Requirements
- Experience building/shipping AI/ML products or infrastructure where performance/scalability/production behavior mattered.
- Technical background in software engineering, ML engineering, research, or equivalent hands-on experience.
- Strong understanding of model serving architectures, GPU economics, latency/throughput/quality/cost tradeoffs.
- Experience working closely with researchers/infra engineers bringing complex capabilities to production.
- Rigorous approach to benchmarking, experimentation, performance measurement.
- Strong product judgment; ability to translate technical work into clear customer value.

## Nice to have
Hands-on experience with PyTorch, vLLM, TensorRT-LLM, SGLang, NVIDIA Dynamo, or similar; open-source inference/model optimization/ML systems research experience.
