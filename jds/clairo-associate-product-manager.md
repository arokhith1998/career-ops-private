## About Clario

At Clario, part of Thermo Fisher Scientific, we develop regulated technology that helps unlock better evidence and improve patient outcomes worldwide. Our software platforms support clinical trials, diagnostics, and medical decision-making in highly controlled regulatory environments.

## About the Role

Join our Technical Product Development team as an Associate Product Manager and help shape innovative solutions that support clinical research and improve the customer experience. You will collaborate with cross-functional teams to translate market needs into product requirements, support product development, and coordinate successful launches across our portfolio of software, devices, and services.

## What You Offer / Will Be Doing

- Support product strategy, roadmap development, and lifecycle management for new and existing software, devices, and services, from initial planning through launch, maintenance, and retirement.
- Conduct market research, analyze competitive trends, and gather customer feedback to identify opportunities, define product requirements, and prioritize features that address business and customer needs.
- Collaborate with Product Development, Engineering, Operations, Marketing, Sales, and Quality and Regulatory Affairs teams to refine user stories, coordinate releases, manage risks, and ensure alignment with quality and regulatory requirements.
- Coordinate product development timelines, documentation, stakeholder communications, and cross-functional launch readiness, including training materials, product demonstrations, and sales enablement resources.
- Monitor product performance, customer satisfaction, serviceability, and business outcomes while supporting continuous improvement, financial planning, and effective management of product-related risks.

## What We Look For

- Bachelor's degree or equivalent experience, preferably in business, science, or a related field.
- 0-3+ years of relevant experience in product management, operations, commercial functions, or related roles.
- Experience supporting product planning, market research, customer requirements gathering, or software and technology product development.
- Strong project coordination, analytical, and organizational skills.
- Understanding of product lifecycle processes.
- Excellent written, verbal, and presentation skills in English.
- Familiarity with regulated healthcare technologies, clinical data capture, medical devices, or relevant quality/regulatory standards is preferred.
- Ability to work remotely from the United States or Canada with preference for Eastern Time Zone alignment; and travel occasionally as business needs require.
