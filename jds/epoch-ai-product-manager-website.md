## Description

The website serves as Epoch AI's main product. The Product Manager will determine
what gets built, establish specifications and goals for pages/features with
researchers and designers, prioritize across the site, set design standards,
manage projects from conception to launch, provide design feedback, understand
the audience through analytics and user feedback, and conduct quality assurance
before publication.

Key responsibilities: setting goals and specs for new pages, prioritizing
requests, establishing site standards, running projects end-to-end, giving
design feedback, knowing the audience, owning QA.

Required qualifications: strong product taste, clear writing ability, comfort
with data and charts, understanding of the audience (policymakers, journalists,
researchers, AI lab staff), experience shipping web products with designers and
developers, people skills, organizational ability, ability to travel to three
annual team retreats.

Nice-to-have: familiarity with Epoch's research or AI broadly, prior product
management experience, front-end knowledge, comfort with AI coding tools.
