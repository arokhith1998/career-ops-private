Finance & Business Operations Manager, WhatsApp Responsibilities:

- Become an integral part of the org's strategy and planning processes
- Own end-to-end partnerships with a broad range of Product leads
- Drive nuanced ROI and impact frameworks across headcount, opex and capacity investments
- Own day-to-day operational tasks related to budget management
- Perform strategic / ad-hoc business analysis as required
- Champion independent viewpoints across WhatsApp and Meta Finance leadership
- Collaborate with horizontal Financial Planning & Analysis teams to improve and operationalize processes related to headcount planning, budgeting, forecasting, and long-range planning

Minimum Qualifications:

- 10+ years of experience in finance / strategic finance, business strategy, product analytics, or related professional experience managing strategic investments and strategy
- Financial leadership across headcount planning, budgeting, forecasting, and long-range planning
- Experience synthesizing strategic insights and problem solving individually or in a group setting
- Experience communicating, influencing, and relationship building with business leaders
- Experience independently managing projects from scoping through delivery, including tracking details and maintaining organized documentation, and experience prioritizing competing workstreams based on impact, deadlines, and stakeholder needs

Preferred Qualifications:

- Demonstrated ongoing AI skill development (e.g., prompt/context engineering, agent orchestration) and staying current with emerging AI technologies
- Demonstrated ability to integrate AI tools to optimize/redesign workflows and drive measurable impact (e.g., efficiency gains, quality improvements)
- Experience in data analytics, developing code, and/or forecasting (SQL, Python, R)
- Experience working at a leading technology, internet or high growth company
- Experience supporting financial planning during periods of rapid organizational or product growth
- Experience adhering to and implementing responsible, ethical AI practices (e.g., risk assessment, bias mitigation, quality and accuracy reviews)
