About Kiteworks
Kiteworks empowers organizations by ensuring secure and compliant data exchanges across all channels, including email, file sharing, APIs, and A.I. Agents. The platform controls, monitors, and protects every data interaction between people, machines, and systems across user collaboration, automated workflows, and enterprise AI. 

Job description
The Strategic Alliances Marketing Manager (Product & Partner) is responsible for building and scaling Kiteworks’ joint marketing strategy, including the product marketing narrative behind it, with some of the company’s most strategic technology and Global Systems Integrator (GSI) partners, including ServiceNow, Crowdstrike, Palo Alto, Nvidia, Google, AWS, Salesforce, Anthropic, Accenture, Deloitte, Leidos, and Booz Allen. Reporting into the Marketing organization and partnering closely with the Strategic Partnerships team, this person is both the marketing lead behind Kiteworks’ partnership-driven growth strategy and the product marketer responsible for how each partnership is positioned, messaged, and differentiated in the market. 
We are looking for an inventive, entrepreneurial marketer who brings fresh ideas, challenges conventional approaches, and enjoys building from the ground up. There is no established playbook for this role. This person will be expected to ideate and develop creative, differentiated marketing approaches that strengthen both Kiteworks and its strategic partners in the market. 
This is a highly strategic, cross-functional role that combines partner marketing, product marketing, demand generation, executive engagement, strategic partner events, and AI/cybersecurity storytelling. The ideal candidate is both strategic and execution-oriented, with experience building co-marketing programs and product-level positioning that strengthen partner relationships, increase market visibility, and drive measurable pipeline impact. 
This is intentionally a hybrid mandate, and a rare one. Most partner marketing roles hand positioning and messaging off to a separate product marketing function; this one owns both. Kiteworks is looking for a candidate who brings genuine, hands-on depth in product marketing (positioning, messaging frameworks, competitive differentiation) alongside genuine, hands-on depth in partner and channel marketing (co-marketing execution, event presence, partner enablement), not a partner marketer with light exposure to messaging, and not a product marketer unfamiliar with channel dynamics. That combination is what makes this role hard to fill and high-impact once filled. 
This role demands a marketer who uses AI tools fluently and instinctively to accelerate their work, from content creation and campaign planning to research, analysis, and execution. 

Hybrid role 
This is a hybrid role based out of our San Mateo office. Employees are expected to be in office on Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday.
