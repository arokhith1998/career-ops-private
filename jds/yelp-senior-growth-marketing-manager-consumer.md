Summary Yelp's mission is to connect people with great local businesses. As a trusted source of information on local business, millions of people turn to Yelp every day to make decisions about where to spend their money — from where to eat, shop or stay, to which home or local service provider to use, and beyond. Yelp's Growth Marketing team drives user acquisition, activation and long-term engagement across the full consumer lifecycle. As a Senior Growth Marketing Manager, you will lead strategy and hands-on execution to deepen engagement with our existing users across our Services and Restaurants, Food & Nightlife (RFN) verticals, and to build awareness of Yelp as the go-to destination for home and local services. You will own campaigns across Yelp's owned channels (i.e., email, push, in-product placements) as well as paid and organic social, with the goal of inspiring consumers, capturing intent and converting it to action. We are looking for a growth marketer who is also a strong creative leader: someone who keeps a pulse on culture, brings timely trends into our marketing, and turns Yelp's authentic user-generated content into scroll-stopping creative that delivers measurable results. In this highly cross-functional role, you will partner closely with Creative, Social, Paid Media, Product Marketing, Marketing Operations and Technology, Growth Science, Product and Engineering to plan, launch and optimize campaigns that drive engagement, reach and revenue growth. Our team is passionate about data, experimentation and the power of new technology, including AI, to drive innovation. We are especially interested in candidates who bring direct experience with engagement and growth marketing at scale, and who bring curiosity and enthusiasm for using AI and emerging technologies to advance marketing effectiveness. We’d love to have you apply, even if you don’t feel you meet every single requirement in this posting. At Yelp, we’re looking for great people, not just those who simply check off all the boxes. This is a full-time remote role based in the United States. What You'll Do Lead consumer engagement and growth strategy to inspire ongoing engagement with existing users across Services and RFN, while building awareness and consideration of Yelp as the go-to destination for home and local services. Own the campaign calendar across owned channels (i.e., email, push, in-product) for Services and RFN, including seasonal, thematic and always-on programs, and experiment with new messaging, creative and targeting to drive engagement and conversion. Drive Services awareness through paid and organic social, partnering with the Social and Paid Media teams to develop channel-native content and campaigns across platforms such as Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Facebook, Pinterest and Reddit, and evaluate new upper-funnel channels as opportunities emerge. Serve as a creative leader for consumer growth campaigns, setting the creative vision and partnering with in-house design and production teams to build thumb-stopping creative and reusable creative systems the broader team can scale. Move quickly on cultural moments by tracking emerging trends and platform-native formats, and turning them into timely, on-brand campaigns that drive user engagement. Turn Yelp's user-generated content into marketing, using reviews, photos, videos and community voices to build authentic, trust-driving creative across channels. Partner with Product and Product Marketing to bring new product launches, brand positioning and personalized discovery experiences to life. Lead the development, execution, measurement and optimization of growth experiments using A/B testing, creative testing, segmentation and analytics. Use AI tools and emerging technologies to enhance consumer insights, accelerate creative development, improve marketing workflows and boost campaign effectiveness. What It Takes To Succeed 7-10 years of experience in growth or lifecycle marketing with a focus on driving engagement among existing users, plus experience driving awareness and consideration. Proven experience building and scaling growth campaigns across owned channels (i.e., email, push, in-product placements) that drive measurable business impact. Hands-on experience with marketing platforms (i.e., Braze) for campaign execution and audience segmentation. Hands-on experience with paid and organic social marketing, including channel-native content strategy, creator or UGC-driven creative and platform best practices. A strong creative eye and demonstrated experience leading creative development from brief to launch, with a portfolio of campaigns that broke through. Deep understanding of internet culture, social trends and consumer behavior, with the ability to act quickly on cultural moments while protecting brand trust. Experience integrating user-generated content and community voices into marketing campaigns. Strong analytical skills, including measuring the business impact of engagement and upper-funnel marketing, analyzing performance across a portfolio of campaigns and turning insights into continuous optimization. Excellent organizational, project management, cross-functional collaboration and prioritization skills, with the ability to work independently in a fast-paced, changing environment. Strong communication and storytelling skills, with the ability to summarize complex concepts for an executive audience. Curiosity and enthusiasm for applying AI in growth marketing and creative development. Bachelor's degree in Marketing, Business, Communications or a related field, or equivalent practical experience. Nice To Have Ability to self-generate high-quality creative (i.e., using design or AI tools) without relying on Creative teams for every asset. Hands-on experience applying AI solutions in growth marketing workflows. Experience in a two-sided marketplace, consumer tech or local services category.
