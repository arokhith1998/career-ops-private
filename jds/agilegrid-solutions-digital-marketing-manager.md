## Job Description (full, verbatim)
About The Company
Cintas Corporation helps more than one million businesses of all types and sizes get Ready to open their doors with confidence every day. The company provides products and services that help keep customers' facilities and employees clean, safe, and looking their best. Its offerings include uniforms, mats, mops, towels, restroom supplies, workplace water services, first aid and safety products, eyewash stations, safety training, fire extinguishers, sprinkler systems, and alarm services.

This is a regular, full-time position in the Information Technology job category and Operations organization. The schedule is first shift. Requisition Number: 240334.

About The Role
Cintas Corporation is seeking a highly skilled and experienced Digital Marketing Manager to lead strategies that strengthen brand visibility across search channels and support demand generation initiatives. The manager will oversee organic search across search engine optimization (SEO), answer engine optimization (AEO), generative engine optimization (GEO), and local listings management.

This role calls for deep expertise in technical SEO, on-page optimization, and content-driven growth. You will help embed search best practices throughout the company's website, content ecosystem, and broader digital initiatives. Working closely with web, content, analytics, and marketing technology teams, you will identify opportunities, address risks, and continuously improve search performance.

Qualifications
Required qualifications include at least six years of digital marketing experience. Candidates should bring proven experience with SEO and AEO, content optimization, performance analysis, and local listings management. Familiarity with GEO and current developments in search marketing will support success in this evolving role.

Proficiency with digital marketing and measurement platforms such as Google Analytics 4 (GA4), Google Search Console, Moz, SEMrush, and Microsoft Clarity is preferred. The role also requires strong project management skills, including the ability to lead multiple high-priority initiatives simultaneously and deliver work on time and within scope.

Responsibilities
Develop and execute comprehensive SEO, AEO, and GEO strategies to increase visibility and organic search performance across markets and platforms. Manage and optimize Google Business Profile and other online listings, maintaining accuracy and consistency while strengthening local search presence.

Oversee technical, on-page, and off-page SEO best practices, including content optimization and link-building initiatives. Collaborate with cross-functional teams to integrate search strategies into broader marketing campaigns. Manage vendor and agency relationships for search-related services and tools. Conduct regular campaign and performance audits, identify opportunities, and recommend optimizations. Prepare detailed reports and presentations that communicate key findings and practical recommendations to stakeholders.

Benefits
Cintas offers comprehensive, competitive medical, dental, and vision coverage, with premiums below the national average. Additional benefits include competitive pay; a 401(k) with company match, profit sharing, and an Employee Stock Ownership Plan (ESOP); and company-paid disability, life, and AD&D insurance.

Equal Opportunity
Cintas Corporation is proud to be an Equal Opportunity Employer. This job posting will remain open for at least five days.
