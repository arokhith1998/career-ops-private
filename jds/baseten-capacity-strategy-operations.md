## Full Job Description

### About Baseten
Baseten powers mission-critical inference for the world's most dynamic AI companies, like Cursor, Notion, OpenEvidence, Abridge, Clay, Gamma, and Writer. Recently raised a $1.5B Series F led by Altimeter Capital, Conviction Partners, and Spark Capital.

### The Role
As a member of the Capacity Strategy & Operations team, you sit at the intersection of supply intelligence, demand forecasting, and cross-functional execution, turning a complex, fast-moving hardware market into a predictable, reliable foundation for customers and internal engineering teams.

This is not a purely analytical role -- own the end-to-end capacity planning process: translating customer commitments and growth forecasts into concrete supply requirements, coordinating fulfillment across vendors, finance, and infrastructure, and building the systems that make this repeatable and scalable.

### Example Initiatives
- Demand-Supply Alignment Framework
- Constrained Allocation Playbook
- Capacity War Room Ownership
- Strategic Briefings for Leadership

### Responsibilities
- Own the capacity planning process end to end: intake, triage, prioritization, fulfillment tracking, post-mortem learnings
- Translate customer commitments, sales pipeline, and infrastructure growth targets into concrete GPU demand forecasts across regions, GPU types, timelines
- Partner with Finance to model capacity scenarios, evaluate supply opportunities, support CapEx decisions
- Work with Capacity Acquisition team to align supply procurement with forward demand
- Coordinate cross-functionally across Sales, SRE, Infrastructure to resolve capacity conflicts
- Build and maintain operational cadence for structured, visible capacity planning
- Develop reporting and dashboards for capacity health, utilization trends, risk exposure
- Identify recurring operational gaps and drive process improvements

### Requirements
- 5+ years of experience in strategy, operations, consulting, finance, or a similarly analytical and execution-oriented role
- Demonstrated ability to own complex, cross-functional processes
- Strong quantitative and modeling skills
- Excellent communication skills
- High ownership mindset
- Operates well under pressure and in ambiguous, fast-moving environments

### Nice to Have
Familiarity with GPU infrastructure, cloud platforms, or AI/ML ecosystem; experience in a high-growth startup or Series B+ environment; exposure to vendor/partner management in hardware, cloud, or infrastructure contexts.

### Benefits
Competitive compensation with meaningful equity; (U.S. only) 100% coverage of medical/dental/vision for employee and dependents; flexible PTO with company-wide Winter Break; paid parental leave; fertility/family-building stipend (Carrot); (U.S. only) company-facilitated 401(k).
