## Job description (full text via LinkedIn guest API)

Company Description

Unicel Greens is building India's circular battery economy by recovering critical materials from lithium-ion batteries through advanced recycling technology. The company's proprietary processes reclaim lithium, cobalt, nickel, and graphite, making battery materials reusable and supporting the rapid growth of the EV and renewable energy sectors.

Role Description

The Entrepreneur in Residence (EiR) is a full-time, on-site role based in Gurugram, focused on identifying, designing, and building new business opportunities within the battery recycling and clean energy ecosystem. The EiR will conduct market research, validate business models, and develop go-to-market strategies for new products, services, or partnerships aligned with Unicel Greens' mission. Day-to-day responsibilities include collaborating with cross-functional teams on pilot projects, preparing financial and operational plans, and presenting insights and proposals to senior leadership. The role also involves establishing external partnerships with industry players, investors, and technology providers, while closely tracking regulatory, technical, and market trends in energy storage and recycling.

"Hard Task Follow up & Doer can only survive! Apply only if you are looking for stable & long term perspective!"

You will learn at high speed directly from an industry veteran. You will take on real responsibility and high ownership early. And you will build a strong professional network across the industry.

Compensation

You will start as an intern for the first two months with a monthly stipend of ~50,000 per month. Perform well and you will receive a full-time offer with higher compensation based on your performance.

Qualifications
- Strong skills in business strategy, market research, and opportunity assessment within sustainability, clean energy, or advanced manufacturing sectors.
- Experience in venture building, startup incubation, or entrepreneurial roles, including business model design and go-to-market planning.
- Ability to develop financial projections, unit economics, and investment cases for new initiatives or ventures.
- Comfort collaborating with technical teams and understanding high-level concepts in battery technology, recycling, or materials science.
- Excellent communication, stakeholder management, and presentation skills, with the ability to influence decision-making.
- Demonstrated capacity to work independently in an ambiguous, fast-paced environment and drive projects to completion.
- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Business, Engineering, Chemical Engineering, or a related field; prior experience in EV, energy storage, or circular economy is an advantage.
