**Lab Summary:** Samsung Research America is seeking an experienced, highly motivated individual to join our Digital Health team as a product management leader and be a core part of a passionate team charged with developing, incubating, and launching innovative product concepts that will transform the future of Samsung Health. **Position Summary:** You will be responsible for driving GenAI Coaching product for the digital health team. This will involve planning and developing new features for our exercise product. As a product leader, you will develop innovative product concepts, articulate the product vision and competitive positioning, define product requirements, and play a critical role in influencing both internal and external stakeholders.

**Position Responsibilities:**
- Lead product management including market opportunity and user need identification, product vision & strategy formation, roadmap, product definition & development, business case development, competitive positioning, goal/KPI setting & monitoring

- Collaborate with the design team to translate complex problems into simple, elegant and minimal solutions
- Prioritize feature development to create maximum possible impact based on available resources
- Drive product development working closely with technical program managers and development leads
- Own product requirement document (PRD), user guide, and product performance reporting; be the single point of contact for key stakeholders including executives, partners, business, strategy, engineering, design, QA, operations, and legal

**Required Skills:**
- BS degree in EE, CS, Math, Statistics or similar or equivalent combination of education, training, and experience
- 5+ years of product management experience with a minimum of 5+ years in the building health/wellness apps
- Excellent communication to simplify problems into insights and requirements
- Ability to understand user data and synthesize user research into product requirements
- Demonstrated business agility and ability to work in a fast-paced, ambiguous, and frequently changing environment while prioritizing and managing multiple sets of responsibilities

**Special Attributes:**
- MBA preferred
- Good knowledge of fitness coaching
- Understanding of AI/ML product development process and knowledge of AI evals
- A passion for fitness/running/biking/swimming to be able to connect with the target user community
