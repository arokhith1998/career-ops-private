## Description (as extracted)

Mid-Senior level, Full-time, Financial Services. Hands-on B2B performance marketer
connecting upper-funnel media (programmatic display and video, CTV, YouTube,
LinkedIn, Meta) to qualified pipeline and revenue. Covers campaign operations,
audience development, measurement infrastructure, and data flows between ad
platforms, Marketo, Salesforce, and analytics.

**Key Responsibilities:**
- Manage full-funnel B2B campaigns and media operations, including budgets, pacing, frequency, and creative optimization
- Build and test first-party, account-based, retargeting, and prospecting audiences
- Own measurement setup in Google Tag Manager, DV360, Floodlight, pixels, and conversion tracking, and troubleshoot data-quality issues
- Work with Marketing Operations and RevOps to tie media signals to CRM lifecycle stages and offline conversions
- Design lift tests, geo experiments, and holdouts, and develop leading indicators of lead quality

**Minimum Qualifications:**
- 5+ years in B2B performance marketing, programmatic, demand generation, or a related field
- Proven record linking upper-funnel media to leads, pipeline, and revenue
- Hands-on DV360 and programmatic experience
- Working knowledge of CTV and digital measurement and attribution
- Advanced Google Tag Manager and measurement implementation skills
- Strong experience with Marketo and Salesforce
- Strong analytical skills and cross-functional communication

**Preferred Qualifications:**
- First-party audience activation, lead enrichment, and CRM-to-media integrations
- Incrementality or causal measurement experience
- AI, machine learning, or predictive scoring experience
- Measurement framework and tagging governance experience
- Experience in complex B2B SaaS, fintech, payments, or enterprise tech
