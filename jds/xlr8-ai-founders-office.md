Founder's Office | XLR8 AI

About XLR8 AI

XLR8 AI is a San Francisco-based Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) company. We help brands gain visibility in AI search engines like ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Google AI Overviews and Claude. We are bootstrapped and profitable, growing 20-30% MOM.

The role

You will work in the Founder's Office as the CEO's right hand. You take ownership of the admin and operational work that currently sits with the CEO so that he can focus only on company growth. You will not wait to be told what to do. You identify bottlenecks, make decisions on the CEO's behalf, and run things end to end.

This is a demanding, high-ownership role with a lot to do, and it pays accordingly.

What you will own

Client contracts
- Send contracts (our internal agreement or Common Paper) based on what was agreed over email
- Handle redlines from client legal teams, accept acceptable changes yourself, escalate the rest to our legal team, and coordinate until signed

Invoicing and collections
- Send invoices the day a contract is signed, and automate this where possible
- Keep invoice status updated in Notion
- Make sure our AR provider (Monk) works correctly and resolve issues flagged for human review
- Follow up with clients directly and loop in our collections specialist on late payments

Taxes, payroll and compliance
- Work with our bookkeeping team so quarterly taxes are paid on time
- Handle one-off tax cases, including coordinating with our former accounting team on a stuck refund
- Share compliance notices with our payroll provider (Warp) and drive next steps to resolution

Hiring and onboarding
- Send contracts to hires in India and the US
- Coordinate onboarding across Slack, our platform and our payroll system
- Post job descriptions on LinkedIn

One-time setups
- Research vendors, run vendor demo calls, negotiate pricing and integrate with our systems (QuickBooks Online, Stripe, etc.)
- Example: sales tax. Vendor selection, exposure analysis, and setup for future invoices

CEO support
- Manage the CEO's tasks in Notion, track updates and help prioritize based on growth impact
- Remind the CEO of commitments and deadlines from our weekly prioritization
- Coordinate meeting times with stakeholders
- Coordinate with the India team
- Handle miscellaneous operational tasks as they come up (vendor payments, India team invoices, holiday calendars)

How you will work
- 15-minute daily sync with the CEO: updates and blockers only
- Available during US Pacific hours, 8 AM to 4 PM PST (will be relaxed once working arrangement is established)

What we are looking for
- 4+ years of experience in operations, founder's office, chief of staff, operations or a similar role
- Strong academic background
- Excellent written and verbal communication; able to represent the CEO with clients, vendors and legal counsel
- Sharp judgment and high intelligence; you think on the CEO's behalf and add value in conversations
- Comfortable handling contracts, payments and invoices with accuracy
- Proactive and self-directed: you spot problems and fix them without being asked
- Experience with Notion, Slack, QuickBooks Online and Stripe is a plus

Compensation
INR 1.2 - 1.5 lacs per month

Location
Remote, with availability during US Pacific hours
