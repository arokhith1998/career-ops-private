## Job Description (extracted, verbatim from posting)

Autodesk is shaping the future of the design and make industries by helping customers navigate a rapidly evolving AI-driven landscape. This role supports Autodesk AI initiatives, understanding how AI and new business models are reshaping the competitive landscape across Manufacturing, AECO, and Media and Entertainment. "A successful candidate builds with AI by default, not as a tool added on top."

**Responsibilities:**
- Build and maintain automated systems that produce competitive intelligence (source monitoring, refresh cadences)
- Track how AI is changing markets (competitors, startups, funding, product releases, partnerships, research, talent movement, regulatory shifts)
- Work with business functions, product management, engineering, technical benchmarking
- Synthesize qualitative/quantitative information into clear points of view
- Coordinate research and intelligence projects
- Produce recurring competitive intelligence and fast-turn analysis for leaders (including C-suite)

**Minimum Qualifications:**
- "You've built workflows, agents, or pipelines that run without you. Bring one to the interview and walk us through the choices you made"
- "5+ years of experience in one or more of the following, or a combination: competitive intelligence, market research, business intelligence, strategy, consulting, product management, software or data engineering"
- "Bachelor's degree in business, economics, data science, computer science, engineering, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience"

**Preferred Qualifications:** demonstrated interest in physical AI across Autodesk industries; Python/SQL at a working level; familiarity with PitchBook/CB Insights/Crunchbase; Excel/Power BI/Tableau; MBA or equivalent.
