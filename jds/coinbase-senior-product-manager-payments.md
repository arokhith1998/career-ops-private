## Role
Senior PM on the Payments Platform team (Platform product group): owns how customers add and use bank accounts, cards, and digital wallets. Payments Platform builds/runs the systems moving customer money in/out of Coinbase (bank transfers, card payments, wires, real-time rails).

## Responsibilities
- Own multi-year strategy/roadmap for adding/using bank accounts, cards, digital
  wallets, full funnel from first screen through linking, verification, failure recovery - Drive intelligent routing of payment onboarding across payment service providers and banks (cheapest/most-likely-to-succeed path; avoid single-provider outages) - Shape approach for confirming a payment method belongs to the customer, with Risk and Compliance, balancing fraud loss vs. conversion - Partner with Business Development and external bank/processor partners on commercial terms, integrations, next-wave payment capabilities - Lead payment method cost/performance reporting end to end; build reusable platform capabilities for other product teams

## Requirements
7+ years PM experience with depth in payments, money movement, or financial platforms; track record owning a payment/money-movement funnel end to end at scale; working knowledge of card payments/bank transfers (declines, retries, verification, tokenization, routing); strong analytical instincts; responsible generative-AI use with human oversight.
