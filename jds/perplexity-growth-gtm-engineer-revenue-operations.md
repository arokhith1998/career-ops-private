In 2026, we launched Computer, the defining product for the new era of agentic AI. Millions of people and teams adopt Perplexity and Computer on their own, and our marketing programs bring in thousands more leads. To turn that demand into pipeline, every lead has to be enriched, scored, and routed to the right next step within minutes.

We're hiring a Growth GTM Engineer on the Revenue Operations team to own the lead-to-engagement engine. You'll orchestrate enrichment across marketing and product leads. You'll build the scoring models that combine firmographic and technographic fit with demonstrated intent and product behavior. You'll own the routing that sends each lead back to Marketing for nurture or to BDRs and Sales for engagement. And you'll help build the custom internal apps our reps use to research, prioritize and engage prospects.

You'll report to the Head of Marketing Operations, who sets the priorities for this work and owns our marketing automation platform, campaigns and attribution. You'll write production code, own the data and logic between our warehouse and Salesforce, and measure everything you build against meetings, pipeline and revenue.

**Tech stack:** Salesforce | Snowflake | dbt | Python | TypeScript | Clay | Reverse ETL (Hightouch or similar) | Routing and scheduling (Default, Chili Piper or similar) | LLM APIs

### What you'll do
- Own enrichment orchestration (waterfall enrichment across third-party data providers, AI research agents, first-party data; dedup, field governance, data freshness, provider cost)
- Own and iterate on lead and account scoring (firmographic/technographic fit models combined with intent/behavior signals; calibrate against closed-won/closed-lost; version and explain to Sales)
- Own lead routing (decision logic sending leads to Marketing nurture or BDR/AE/Account Manager; co-define lifecycle stages, SLAs, recycle rules)
- Close the feedback loop (instrument funnel from lead creation to MQL, meeting, opportunity, closed-won)
- Help build custom internal apps for prospect engagement (account/prospect research briefs, signal-triggered alerts, LLM-powered agents for personalized outreach)
- Be the technical owner of the lead data layer (dbt models, reverse ETL syncs, APIs, webhooks)
- Partner across the business (Marketing, Growth, BDR, Sales leadership, Data Science, Sales Systems)

### What success looks like
30 days: audit current enrichment coverage, scoring logic, routing rules, lead SLAs.
90 days: ship enrichment waterfall with measurable fill-rate/accuracy gains; ship unified scoring model with published rubric; ship routing with SLA dashboards.
6 months: ship at least one rep-facing engagement app/agent with sustained weekly BDR/AE use; show measurable lift in MQL-to-meeting and meeting-to-opportunity conversion.
