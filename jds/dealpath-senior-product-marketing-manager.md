Senior Product Marketing Manager, San Francisco, CA

Dealpath is looking for a Senior Product Marketing Manager to help shape how we position, launch, and grow our platform as we expand the role of AI, data, and intelligent workflows across institutional commercial real estate.

Sitting at the intersection of Product, Marketing, Sales, Revenue and Customer Success, this role will turn customer insight, market dynamics, competitive intelligence, and product innovation into differentiated positioning and go-to-market strategies that drive adoption and growth.

What You'll Do: Own positioning and market narrative. Develop and evolve positioning, messaging, and value propositions across Dealpath's platform, products, audiences, and priority use cases. Lead go-to-market strategy and product launches. Own GTM strategy for major launches and new capabilities. Own customer, market, and competitive intelligence. Lead customer research, competitive intelligence, and win/loss analysis. Shape ICP, segmentation, pricing, and growth strategy. Enable Sales and influence Product.

Qualifications: 7+ years of B2B product marketing experience, ideally in enterprise SaaS, fintech, proptech, data, financial services, or another complex technology category. BA/BS in Marketing, Business Administration, or a related field. Demonstrated success owning positioning, messaging, product launches, and go-to-market strategy for sophisticated products and enterprise buyers. Strong analytical orientation. Experience with customer and market research, competitive intelligence, win/loss analysis, ICP and segmentation strategy, and pricing and packaging. Strong understanding of commercial real estate, institutional investing, private markets, or capital markets preferred. Strong cross-functional leadership. Exceptional written and verbal communication skills. Strong fluency with AI tools.

The Perks & Culture: Medical, dental, & vision insurance coverage. Hybrid 3-days in office policy. Flexible Spending Account. Paid Parental Leave. 401(k). Company-sponsored commuter benefits. Flexible time off policy. Catered lunches and snacks. Monthly wellness reimbursement.

The estimated pay range for California candidates for this position is $150,000 - $165,000 base, & variable compensation of $20,000. This position is also eligible for Dealpath's equity plan.

About Dealpath: Dealpath is the world's largest and most trusted AI-powered operating system for real estate investing. To date, the platform has powered more than $10 trillion in transactions, partnering with hundreds of firms, including Blackstone, Nuveen, LaSalle, CBRE IM, MetLife, Newmark, Oxford Properties, New York Life, UBS, Manulife, DWS, and Principal.

Interview Process Notice: Dealpath conducts interviews via video conference. To support our interviewers with accurate note-taking, we may use AI-assisted transcription tools during your interview sessions.

Seniority level: Mid-Senior level. Employment type: Full-time. Industries: Software Development.
