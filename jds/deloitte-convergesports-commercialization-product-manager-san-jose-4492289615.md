**The Team:** ConvergeSPORTS is a product-driven business within Deloitte that combines sports industry experience, proprietary data, AI-driven decision intelligence, and reusable product capabilities. Its Audience Extension Network helps teams and leagues reach fans beyond the arena using first-party data, partner inventory, and publisher reach, at the crossover of sports media, retail media, adtech, and sponsorship.

**Position Summary:** A commercially focused product manager will set product vision and roadmap for the Audience Extension Network, turn market insights and large datasets into narratives and commercial opportunities, and work with product, engineering, data science, and sales so the product can be sold and delivered repeatably.

**Responsibilities:**
- Own and maintain the roadmap across audience curation, measurement, targeting, activation, and monetization.
- Turn fan, media, campaign, and partner data into insights and opportunity sizing.
- Work with internal and external partners to set requirements and priorities.
- Support pitches, demos, executive presentations, and client workshops.
- Create collateral such as one-pagers, decks, case studies, and demo scripts.
- Monitor market, competitor, and category trends across retail media, sports media, sponsorship, and adtech.
- Use client, sponsor, publisher, and seller feedback to improve the product.
- Help shape pricing, packaging, and commercialization plans.
- Own backlog prioritization with delivery teams.
- Support marketing, conferences, networking, and brand-building.

**Required qualifications:**
- Bachelor's degree or equivalent experience.
- 5+ years in product management, strategy, product marketing, commercial strategy, media, consulting, adtech, or adjacent digital businesses.
- Experience with large datasets and executive-ready narratives.
- Experience supporting pitches, sales, or client workshops.
- Understanding of retail media, audience monetization, or digital media economics.
- Travel required (exact percentage shown as a placeholder, "X-X%," in the source posting).
- U.S. work authorization without employer sponsorship.

**Preferred qualifications:** Sports media, sponsorship, activation, retail media, or adtech experience; Excel, PowerPoint, Tableau, or SQL skills; experience building product narratives, case studies, pricing materials, or sales enablement content.
