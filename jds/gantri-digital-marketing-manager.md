## Job Description (extracted, verbatim from posting)

Runs Gantri's digital marketing engine across every channel: paid social, paid search, email, SMS, affiliate, on-site conversion. Owns cross-channel reporting system built on multi-touch attribution. "This is a role for a builder of systems, not a firefighter." Manages two agency relationships (paid media and email) directly.

**What You'll Do:**
- Build and own cross-channel performance reporting using multi-touch attribution
- Plan, budget, forecast DTC marketing spend; set CAC/ROAS/MER/contribution margin targets
- Own paid media agency relationship (briefs, budgets, creative requests, testing roadmaps)
- Own email/SMS agency relationship (campaign calendar, flows, segmentation, deliverability)
- Run campaign calendar across channels coordinated with product launches
- Run structured testing programs (audiences, creative, offers, landing pages)
- Maintain clean tracking (pixels, UTMs, server-side events)

**Required Experience:**
- "5+ years in digital marketing or growth for a DTC brand, with a proven record of growing DTC revenue."
- Experience in a design-led brand (home, lifestyle, beauty, fashion, or adjacent)
- Exceptional quantitative skills (attribution models, cohort analyses, LTV/CAC)
- Deep hands-on knowledge of ecommerce stack (Klaviyo, Meta Ads, Google Ads, GA4, Northbeam/Triple Whale/Rockerbox)

Preferred: SQL/BI tool experience (Looker, Metabase, Mode); B2B/trade marketing exposure alongside DTC; made-to-order fulfillment company experience.

Benefits: competitive salary + equity, health/dental/vision, flexible PTO, 401(k) match, professional development stipend, access to Gantri-designed products.
