## Full Job Description

### Who We Are
Cobalt was founded on the belief of a fundamental human aspiration: the desire to live better and safer. It all started in 2013, when our founders realized that pentesting can be better. Today our diverse, fully remote team is committed to helping organizations of all sizes with seamless, effective and collaborative Offensive Security Testing.

Our customers can start a pentest in as little as 24 hours and integrate with advanced development cycles thanks to the powerful combination of our SaaS platform and an exclusive community of testers known as the Cobalt Core.

### Description
Cobalt is looking for a Senior Growth Marketing Manager to join our marketing team. This isn't a traditional demand gen role. We are building a sophisticated Growth Marketing framework focused on automation and orchestration to identify accounts with the highest propensity to buy, and nurture them into turning purchase ready.

In this role, you will develop and execute integrated marketing playbooks and measure the efficacy of the programs on regular cadences, generating and accelerating pipeline across North America and EMEA. You will utilize Salesforce, UserGems, HubSpot to identify intent and track buyer movement.

### What You'll Do
- Full-Lifecycle Account Progression: integrated marketing plans for new logo acquisition and expansion within existing customer accounts.
- Multi-Touch Program Engagement: drive multi-channel initiatives (email, webinars, social, direct mail, digital advertising) through every phase of the buyer journey.
- Automation & AI Agents: design and oversee automated agents to streamline routine workflows.
- Intent & Movement Orchestration: use UserGems intent signals and tracking to trigger automated playbooks.
- Customer Marketing & Advocacy: nurture existing customers, leveraging success stories for whitespace expansion and cross-sell.
- Content Strategy & Execution: co-own content calendar with the content team.
- Sales & Success Enablement: primary marketing contact for sales and BDR teams.
- Analytics & Optimization: track active program metrics continuously.

### You Must Have
- 6+ years of marketing experience with a focus on growth, demand generation, or regional marketing, ideally within a B2B technology or cybersecurity environment.
- Demonstrable expertise in account-based marketing strategies, tactics, and modern growth technologies.
- Expertise with Salesforce, HubSpot, and intent tools (UserGems or 6Sense), plus hands-on experience developing MCP agents.
- Analytical skills, exceptional written/verbal communication.
- Strong sense of ownership; ability to manage multiple projects and meet deadlines.

### Why You Should Join Us
Growing industry, mentorship, competitive comp + equity, 401(k)/pension, medical/dental/vision or statutory healthcare, stipends (wellness, WFH equipment, L&D), flexible PTO and paid parental leave.
