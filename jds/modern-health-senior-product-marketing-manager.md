## Job Description (extracted, verbatim from posting)

The Senior Product Marketing Manager will play a critical role in helping Modern Health win, retain, and grow business by shaping how it positions and brings products/services to market. High-ownership individual contributor role with visibility to leadership.

**What You'll Do:**
- Shape messaging and positioning using customer/market/competitive insight
- Lead go-to-market strategy and execution for product launches, feature enhancements
- Partner with Demand Generation, Brand, Content, Events, Client Marketing on integrated campaigns
- Equip Sales and customer-facing teams (pitch content, battlecards, objection handling, RFP support)
- Be a visible strategic partner across Product, Sales, Client Management, Partnerships, Clinical, Marketing, leadership

**Requirements:**
- "5+ years of progressive product marketing experience, demonstrated by increasing scope and ownership, in healthcare or employee benefits."
- Prior experience owning core PMM work (positioning, messaging, go-to-market execution, campaigns, sales enablement)
- AI-savvy, uses AI tools thoughtfully

Benefits: medical/dental/vision/disability/life, HSA/FSA, coaches/therapists via platform, generous PTO + Collective Pause Days, parental leave, Carrot family-forming benefit, UrbanSitter family assistance, professional development stipend, 401k + financial planning via Origin, wellness stipend, WFH stipend, monthly cell phone reimbursement.
