## Full JD Text (verbatim, as extracted)
Senior Market Researcher, AI & Research Ecosystem 

Location: San Jose, California / NYC, NY
 
About LatentView
LatentView Analytics is a global data analytics and AI solutions company that partners with Fortune 500 and high-growth clients to turn data into competitive advantage. Our teams combine deep technical expertise with business context to ship measurable outcomes — not just models and dashboards — across industries.
 
Role Overview
LatentView is hiring a Senior Market Researcher to join a flagship engagement with the research organization of a globally leading technology company. You will be the research-intelligence lead on the global scientific, technological and academic ecosystem around that organization. You will map macro research trends, track advances by competitors, academic labs and venture-backed AI entities, and turn complex signals into executive-ready memos and decks.

This is research intelligence, not traditional market segmentation. Your subject matter is foundation models, breakthrough papers, top-tier conferences, open-source communities and applied sciences such as health, education, climate and weather, sustainability and quantum computing.

What you will do

- Own end-to-end intelligence programs: define research questions, plan the work and deliver with minimal supervision (1–2 check-ins per week).
- Monitor the AI/ML scientific landscape, including novel research labs, key technical innovations and market shifts.
- Benchmark strategic positioning against advances at NeurIPS, ICML, CVPR and similar venues, across open-source ecosystems and among venture-funded AI labs.
- Collect signals creatively: custom web scraping, bibliometric analysis, OSINT, alternative data and automated tracking.
- Rapidly synthesize large volumes of external information into executive briefings, landscape maps and ecosystem deep-dives on tight timelines.
- Write concise analytical memos and narrative documents, and build visually refined decks for research leadership.
- Represent the team independently in internal and external meetings.

What you bring (required)

- 8–10 years in competitive intelligence, market research, technology strategy or research analysis, with a substantial focus on AI/ML.
- Deep technical fluency in AI/ML: you can explain why a paper matters, what it does and does not show, and who it affects.
- A track record of analyzing scientific research trends, academic labs, conferences, open-source communities or AI startups, rather than general sales, marketing or UX research.
- Exceptional executive writing: short memos and narrative briefs that lead with the answer.
- Strong slide design and storytelling for Director- and VP-level audiences.
- Ability to turn ambiguous ecosystem questions into structured, rigorous analysis.
- A self-directed, fast-moving working style with full ownership of deliverables.
- Based in, or willing to work from, the Bay Area, New York City or London, with the right to work there.

Nice to have

- Hands-on data collection skills: Python scraping, APIs (arXiv, OpenAlex, Semantic Scholar, GitHub, Hugging Face), or automated tracking tools.
- Bibliometric, citation or patent analysis experience.
- Experience at an AI research lab, deep-tech strategy group, venture-intelligence firm or technology research and advisory firm.
- An advanced degree in a technical or scientific field.
- Familiarity with applied-science AI domains such as climate and weather, health, materials or quantum.

Base pay range: USD $139,500 – USD $ 166,500 annually. This range reflects base salary only — it excludes Incentive and benefits — and is a good-faith estimate for this level and location that may vary based on experience, skills, and where the role is performed.

Communication: This role requires fluent spoken and written English to communicate directly with client stakeholders — a job-related requirement of the client-facing work itself, not of any candidate's background or origin.
