## Job description (full text via LinkedIn guest API)

About The Role

We are looking for a high-ownership, business-oriented professional to join our Founder's Office and work closely with the founders and leadership team on driving business growth, strategy, and revenue.

This role is designed for someone with a strong entrepreneurial mindset and hands-on experience across business strategy, P&L management, growth, digital marketing, organic marketing, and sales.

We are looking for someone who understands the complete business lifecycle - from identifying growth opportunities and developing marketing strategies to driving sales, managing revenue, and ensuring profitability.

The ideal candidate should have previously owned business outcomes, managed revenue targets, contributed to P&L decisions, and driven initiatives that have had a measurable impact on business growth.

This is a high-impact role that requires someone who can think strategically, execute independently, navigate ambiguity, and take complete ownership of business-critical initiatives.

What You'll Do

Business Strategy & P&L Management
- Work closely with founders to develop and execute business strategies and growth plans.
- Take ownership of business performance, including revenue, costs, margins, and profitability.
- Drive P&L management and identify opportunities to improve business efficiency and financial performance.
- Analyze business metrics, identify gaps, and recommend data-backed strategic decisions.
- Evaluate new business opportunities, revenue streams, and scalable business models.

Growth & Revenue Ownership
- Drive business growth initiatives with direct accountability for revenue outcomes.
- Develop strategies to improve customer acquisition, conversion, retention, and revenue generation.
- Identify growth opportunities across existing and new business verticals.
- Build scalable growth frameworks and drive execution to achieve business targets.
- Work with cross-functional teams to improve revenue performance and business outcomes.

Marketing & Customer Acquisition
- Develop and execute digital marketing strategies across paid and organic channels.
- Drive organic growth through SEO, content marketing, social media, brand positioning, and other relevant channels.
- Understand performance marketing metrics, CAC, ROI, lead generation, and conversion funnels.
- Identify opportunities to optimize marketing spends and improve acquisition efficiency.
- Build sustainable marketing strategies that directly contribute to revenue growth.

Sales & Business Development
- Develop and execute sales strategies to achieve business and revenue targets.
- Understand and drive the complete sales funnel, from lead generation to conversion and deal closure.
- Identify opportunities to improve sales productivity, team performance, and conversion rates.
- Work closely with sales teams to build predictable revenue pipelines.
- Explore new business opportunities, partnerships, and revenue channels.

Founder's Office & Strategic Initiatives
- Partner directly with founders and leadership on high-priority business challenges and strategic initiatives.
- Identify operational and business gaps and independently drive solutions.
- Lead cross-functional projects across sales, marketing, product, and operations.
- Drive 0-to-1 initiatives, from ideation and strategy to execution and scaling.
- Build scalable processes, systems, and frameworks to improve business performance.
- Bring structure to ambiguous problems and ensure initiatives deliver measurable outcomes.

What We Are Looking For
- 3-5 years of experience in startups, Founder's Office, business strategy, growth, business operations, or entrepreneurship.
- Proven experience in P&L ownership and business performance management.
- Strong understanding of revenue generation, cost management, margins, and profitability.
- Hands-on experience in growth strategy, digital marketing, performance marketing, and organic marketing.
- Experience in driving sales, business development, customer acquisition, and revenue growth.
- Strong business acumen with the ability to connect marketing, sales, and business strategy.
- Demonstrated ability to take ownership of business-critical initiatives and deliver measurable results.
- Strong analytical, strategic thinking, and problem-solving capabilities.
- Excellent communication, stakeholder management, and execution skills.
- Ability to thrive in a fast-paced startup environment with high levels of ambiguity and ownership.

Preferred Experience
- Experience working directly with founders, CEOs, or senior leadership teams.
- Experience managing a business vertical, category, or independent P&L.
- Prior experience scaling a business, launching new initiatives, or building processes from scratch.
- Experience in EdTech, Consumer Tech, D2C, SaaS, or other high-growth startup environments.
- A track record of driving revenue growth, improving profitability, and building scalable business models.
