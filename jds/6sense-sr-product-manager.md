## Role summary
Owns strategy, roadmap, and execution for audience building, account/contact discovery, and handoff to Activation channels.

## Responsibilities
- Define product vision, strategy, roadmap, and success metrics for Audience & Discovery.
- Build workflows to create/organize/act on audiences and lists at scale; design search/filter/exploration experiences for accounts and contacts.
- Enable reliable handoff to Activation channels (Advertising, Intelligent Workflows, AI Email, Sales Intelligence, Agents at scale).
- Define how agents, RevvyAI, and APIs interact with audience and account/contact data.
- Turn customer needs into requirements, user journeys, acceptance criteria, measurable outcomes.
- Track adoption, audience reuse, discovery engagement, activation rate, and business impact.
