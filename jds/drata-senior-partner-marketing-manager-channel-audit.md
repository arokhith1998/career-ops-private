## Full description (verbatim via Ashby API, HTML stripped, condensed)
Drata is building the trust layer between great companies — automating compliance, managing risk, and helping organizations prove trust continuously as they scale.

### Job Summary
Lead partner marketing strategy and execution across Drata's priority Channel and Audit partners. In close partnership with Revenue Marketing and Business Development (BD), build and adapt joint go-to-market strategies that generate demand, create and progress qualified opportunities, and deliver measurable pipeline. This is a hands-on senior role spanning partner strategy, campaign delivery, investment decisions, and performance measurement. The focus is AMER, with occasional support for APAC or EMEA as needed.

### What you'll do
- Develop joint go-to-market strategies with priority partners and BD
- Build trusted relationships with Channel partner marketing and sales leaders, including strategic VARs
- Design and execute partner campaigns, events, webinars, demand generation, content, sponsorships, and MDF-backed programs
- Manage MDF and co-marketing investments for priority Channel and Audit partners
- Use campaign, partner, funnel, and investment performance to identify opportunities and optimize programs
- Build scalable Channel marketing processes and resources
- Partner across BD, Sales, Field Marketing, Demand Generation, Corporate and Product Marketing, and Marketing Operations
- Own a broad portfolio autonomously

### What you'll bring
- 5+ years of B2B marketing experience with significant Channel or partner marketing ownership; experience with VARs, resellers, MSPs, or systems integrators
- Track record of developing and executing partner go-to-market strategies and full-funnel programs
- Strong analytical judgment
- Ability to build trusted partner relationships and lead work across teams without direct authority
- An AI-forward mindset and practical use of tools such as Glean and Claude
- A self-directed builder's mindset

### Preferred Qualifications
Pre-existing marketing relationships with strategic Channel partners; B2B SaaS, cybersecurity, compliance experience; experience with tiering/MDF/co-marketing/channel sales motions; experience across regions.

### Benefits (summarized)
Stock equity; up to 100% employer-paid premiums for medical/dental/vision (employee + dependents); 401(k); paid parental leave after 6 months; fertility/family-building benefits; learning stipends; flexible vacation and paid holidays.
