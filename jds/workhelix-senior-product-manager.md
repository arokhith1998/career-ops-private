Who We Are

Workhelix is an enterprise SaaS company with a single goal: helping organizations get the most out of their AI investments.

Our people and software combine to give leaders answers to the three critical questions about the business value of AI:

- Where are our top opportunities to create value with AI?

- Are our current efforts actually delivering? What’s their ROI?

- How can we accelerate and increase the benefits of our AI?

To find answers for each customer, Workhelix applies our unmatched expertise in economics, AI, and data science. We have pioneered a task-scoring method to identify top GenAI opportunities, ensuring clear, data-driven deployment strategies. Our ROI monitoring suite applies Nobel Prize-winning economics to provide accurate, ongoing insights into the impact of GenAI implementations. Renowned AI and Economics thinkers including Andrew Ng, and cofounders Andy McAfee and Erik Brynjolfsson stand proudly behind our mission.

We maintain a culture established on five key principles. For more information on our leadership principles, please visit the careers page of our website.

### Role

Workhelix is looking for a Senior Product Manager based in the Bay Area who thrives at the intersection of complex data and elegant UX. This role is ideal for someone who has built products in experimentation platforms, analytics tools, or fintech environments — places where clarity, statistical rigor, and usability are non-negotiable.

You'll work directly with engineering, data science, and forward-deployed teams to translate noisy data into intuitive interfaces that help Fortune 500 customers understand and unlock productivity with GenAI. You'll own products that power core business value — particularly experimentation frameworks, metrics pipelines, dashboards, and AI-augmented insights.

We're looking for someone with exceptional product craft who's comfortable thinking from first principles and who thrives in a fast-moving, intellectually demanding, customer-obsessed environment.

### You'll Be Successful in This Role If You:

- Drive roadmap alignment — manage and evolve roadmaps across Workhelix's flagship product, ensuring they reflect top customer pain points and deliver measurable value.

- Execute with rigor — keep cross-functional teams focused and accountable, consistently shipping high-quality features on time.

- Right-size solutions — scope features effectively, shipping fast without overengineering while still fully addressing the customer problem.

- Structure chaos — thrive in fast-paced, ambiguous environments and bring clarity, process, and structure so nothing falls through the cracks.

- Close the feedback loop — proactively establish customer feedback channels and integrate insights into the development cycle to guide feature evolution.

- Stay fluent in AI tooling — bring strong working knowledge of AI tools, both conceptually and practically, and apply them in your daily product work.

### Key Responsibilities:

What You’ll Do

Product Ownership & Strategy

- Own the product roadmap for data-intensive, insight-delivering features.

- Prioritize product investments that balance short-term speed with long-term architectural and strategic integrity.

- Partner with the Head of Product and CTO to define milestones, align on KPIs, and deliver measurable business value.

Platform Design

- Lead development of Workhelix products used to measure GenAI impact.

- Partner with data science to turn statistical models and economic insights into user-facing features.

- Ensure statistical rigor in product designs — confidence intervals, control groups, etc. — without over-complicating the UX.

- Partner with the Tech Lead to build out the platform integrations roadmap, enabling customer data ingestion.

User & Customer Discovery

- Conduct deep discovery with technical end-users (e.g., engineering leaders, data scientists) to identify latent needs.

- Translate abstract customer goals into structured problem statements, delightful solutions, and product specs.

- Develop and maintain detailed product requirements, user stories, and journey maps.

Cross-Functional Collaboration

- Collaborate with engineering and design on solutions that balance feasibility, usability, and complexity.

- Drive sprint planning, backlog prioritization, and execution quality.

- Partner with forward-deployed teams to ensure successful delivery and feedback integration.

Product Craft & Quality

- Define intuitive ways to present complex metrics, statistical models, and benchmarks to senior leadership end users (C-Suite, SVP/VP, Directors, Heads of).

- Drive consistency in interaction patterns across complex user workflows.

- Establish and monitor product metrics and dashboards to track adoption and impact.
