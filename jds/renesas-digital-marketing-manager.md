## Job Description
Marketing Data Analytics Manager to lead analyst teams in driving data-informed decisions. Partner with marketing leadership on strategy, campaign optimization, website decisions, and measurement best practices while mentoring analytics staff.

## Responsibilities
- Lead development, governance, and evolution of marketing dashboards and performance monitoring frameworks
- Transform complex marketing and customer data into actionable insights that influence strategy
- Establish measurement strategies and define success metrics for key initiatives
- Present insights and recommendations to senior stakeholders and marketing leadership
- Enhance data quality and tracking methodologies across platforms
- Monitor analytics tools and industry trends; recommend improvements
- Mentor junior analysts and establish analytics standards

## Requirements
- Bachelor's in Marketing, Statistics, Business, Engineering, Data Science, or related field
- 5-7 years marketing analytics or data analysis experience with demonstrated strategic impact
- Strong analytical capabilities with ability to synthesize large datasets
- Hands-on experience with marketing analytics and data visualization tools (Google Analytics, Looker Studio, Power BI)
- Advanced Excel proficiency
- Project management and cross-functional collaboration skills
- Strong presentation and communication abilities

## Preferred
- Prior leadership experience
- Additional analytics or business intelligence platform experience
- Semiconductor industry or high-tech manufacturing background
