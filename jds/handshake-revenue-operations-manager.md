About The Role
Handshake is hiring a Revenue Operations Manager reporting to the Director of Strategy & Operations who leads our Revenue Operations team. You will be the day-to-day operating partner for our new business sales teams (Mid-Market, Strategic Corporate and Public Sector), owning the pipeline, forecast and productivity insights that help us win new logos. This is a hands-on individual contributor role with real ownership. You will build the analysis yourself, run the operating cadence, and use AI and automation to take manual work off reps and managers so they spend more time selling.

In This Role, You Will
- Partner with new business sales leaders as their go-to operator, turning bookings goals into pipeline, coverage and productivity targets, and helping managers act on them
- Run the new business operating cadence: weekly pipeline reviews, forecast calls, and monthly and quarterly business reviews, bringing an independent, data-backed view of the forecast
- Own new business reporting on pipeline health, stage conversion, sales cycle, coverage, attainment and rep productivity, with one clear definition for every metric
- Find and fix funnel friction: diagnose where deals stall, and partner with Marketing, Sales Development and Enablement on programs that improve conversion and velocity
- Support annual and quarterly planning, including capacity models, territory and account assignment, and quota inputs, in partnership with Finance and the RevOps leader
- Keep the sales process clean: stage definitions, rules of engagement, lead and account routing, and Salesforce data quality
- Model and monitor new business comp plans with Finance and Commissions, and track whether plans drive the behavior we intend
- Build AI and automation programs that drive pipeline: partner with GTM Engineering to launch and scale programs that generate new business leads, testing in short build, test, learn sprints
- Automate the manual work: use AI to automate reporting and deal inspection, and build internal tools that give managers and reps time back

You Have

- The ability to influence without authority across Sales, Finance, Marketing and Product
