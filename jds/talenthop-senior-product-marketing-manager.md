## Full job description text

**This is a Fully Remote Job**

  

**About Our Client:**

The organization operates in the construction technology industry, addressing challenges related to customer understanding and adoption of complex B2B software solutions. It focuses on serving conservative, process-heavy industries with software tailored for large businesses operating in fast-paced sales environments. The company targets construction customers and integrates advanced technologies such as AI and machine learning to enhance product offerings and customer engagement.

  

**About the Opportunity:**

The SENIOR PRODUCT MARKETING MANAGER will build and lead the product marketing function, shaping how diverse customer segments perceive and adopt software products. This role is responsible for developing positioning and messaging, leading go-to-market strategies across multiple markets, and driving customer engagement to support product discovery and adoption. The position significantly contributes to the organization's growth by establishing effective marketing practices and aligning cross-functional teams to market needs.

  

**Responsibilities:**

-   Own positioning and messaging for different customer segments, buyer personas, and regions
-   Develop the product marketing playbook and improve existing materials and workflows
-   Lead go-to-market strategy and execution for product, partner, and feature launches
-   Create and maintain sales enablement resources and coach teams on active deals
-   Collaborate with cross-functional leaders to enhance product discovery and adoption
-   Design AI-enabled workflows for competitive monitoring, research synthesis, content drafting, and sales support
-   Direct positioning and messaging strategy for website redevelopment
-   Use customer insights and market data to guide product and marketing decisions
-   Develop customer proof points and advocacy materials for references and events
-   Advise regional marketing managers on positioning for demand generation

  

**Requirements:**

-   Five to eight years of B2B SaaS product marketing experience, including marketing to conservative, process-heavy industries
-   Experience building a product marketing function from scratch and contributing within established teams
-   Proven ability to manage product marketing for software with fast-paced sales cycles across multiple regions
-   Strong self-direction and adaptability in evolving, ambiguous environments
-   Experience in construction technology, property technology, or marketing to technical and field-based buyers preferred
-   Familiarity with construction software (e.g., Procore, Autodesk Construction Cloud), AI/ML tools, CRM platforms, and marketing automation
-   Authorization to work in the United States without visa sponsorship

  

**Pay Range and Compensation Package:**

-   Base salary: $130,000 to $160,000 annually
-   Visa sponsorship is not available
