## Job Description (extracted, verbatim from posting)

About The Role: Every enterprise adoption of dbt begins with questions that have nothing to do with SQL: can we prove who's logging in, can we control what they touch, can we run this inside our own network. You own the trust and access layer of the dbt platform: identity/auth primitives (OAuth, account discovery), enterprise security surface (SSO, SCIM, RBAC, PrivateLink), the first five minutes of every account (login, sign-up, invites, users, groups), and the deployment footprint (new regions, cloud providers).

dbt is used by more than 100,000 open-source community members and tens of thousands of customers. Works across two engineering teams (IAM and Transport), partners with security, infrastructure, compliance, field teams.

**In this role you will:**
- Own the roadmap for identity, access, and deployment
- Make enterprise security a reason we win: SSO, SCIM, RBAC, PrivateLink
- Lead the single-tenant to multi-tenant migration
- Unlock new geographies and clouds
- Own the front door: login, sign-up, account discovery
- Give admins leverage over users, groups, invites, permissions
- Be the product voice in enterprise security reviews

**You're a good fit if you have:**
- "5+ years of product management experience, with meaningful time on identity, access management, security, or infrastructure products."
- Direct experience with authentication/authorization: SSO, SCIM, OAuth/OIDC, RBAC
- Experience with multi-tenant SaaS deployment models

**Edge:** worked at an identity provider (Okta, Auth0, Entra) or hyperscaler identity products; shipped PrivateLink/VPC peering; compliance frameworks (SOC 2, ISO 27001, FedRAMP, HIPAA, GDPR).
