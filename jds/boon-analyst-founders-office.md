About Boon
Boon makes water purifiers for modern Indian homes. "Water For Every You." Founders personally involved in growth, product, operations, service.

The role: work directly with the founder on Boon's most important problems at any given time (e.g. why sales dropped in a city, launching on a new marketplace, fixing installation process). Turn messy questions into clear answers, then make them happen.

What you'll do
- Run the numbers: weekly business review covering sales, marketing spend, CAC, margins, inventory, service metrics.
- Answer hard questions ("Which channels are actually profitable?", "Why are returns up?", "What's our real margin per product?").
- Own special projects end-to-end (new city launches, Amazon/Flipkart expansion, pricing tests, filter subscription plans, partnerships).
- Prepare founder/investor material (monthly MIS, board/investor updates, decks, financial summaries).
- Research the market (track Kent, Aquaguard, Livpure, Pureit, newer D2C brands on pricing/offers/products/service).
- Drive follow-through on action trackers from founder meetings.
- Fix processes: write/improve SOPs (installation, service, returns, customer support).
- Go to the ground: visit installation sites, call customers, sit with support team.

Must-haves
- "1–3 years in consulting, investment banking, VC, a startup strategy/ops role, or business analytics. Exceptional freshers are welcome."
- Strong Excel/Sheets (pivots, XLOOKUP/INDEX-MATCH, simple financial/unit-economics model).
- Comfortable with business numbers (revenue, gross margin, contribution margin, CAC, LTV).
- Clear structured writing; high ownership; happy to do unglamorous work; full-time from Gurgaon office.

Nice to have: D2C/consumer products/hardware experience; SQL/Python/dashboard tools; AI tools for research/analysis; Hindi or regional language.

What you get: direct work with founder; exposure across every function; path to own a function/business line; competitive salary based on experience.
