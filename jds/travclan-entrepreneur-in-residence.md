Your Mission
Work with TravClan’s founders to identify, validate and build high-priority opportunities. Turn an ambiguous customer or business problem into a testable proposition, launch it quickly and build evidence of customer adoption, revenue or improved economics.
Roles & Responsibilities-
• Own one or more 0-to-1 opportunities from customer discovery and market sizing through pilot and early scale.
• Convert broad questions into a clear hypothesis, business case, success metric and execution plan.
• Speak directly with customers, launch minimum viable solutions and use evidence to iterate, scale or stop.
• Own defined adoption, revenue, volume or contribution targets for the initiative.
• Work with Product and Technology to translate customer insight into requirements and drive product adoption.
• Test segments, channels, pricing, propositions and partnerships to create repeatable growth loops.
• Coordinate across Product, Marketing, Sales, Operations, Analytics and Finance while remaining the single owner of outcomes.
