## Full JD Text

MongoDB's Query Team seeks a Senior Staff Product Manager to lead division-wide product strategy for their distributed query engine. As the most senior PM contributor, you'll shape multi-year strategy, architecture, and market direction for the Query business unit, handling complex trade-offs and serving as primary product authority. You'll collaborate with engineers, debate strategy rigorously, advise executives on business options, and own pricing/monetization strategy.

**Key Responsibilities:**
- Spearhead strategic unity across product areas over multi-year horizons
- Originate and de-risk consequential strategic bets with rigorous business cases
- Engage strategically significant customers at senior levels
- Mentor Staff and Senior PMs, pioneering data-driven playbooks
- Partner with Directors and VPs on investment allocation
- Serve as primary external face for the product line

**Requirements:**
- 10+ years professional experience with significant product leadership in platform, infrastructure, or database domains
- Exceptional communication of complex technical issues to diverse audiences
- Proven cross-functional project delivery and multi-year business case execution
- B.Sc. in a technical discipline, with Computer Science preferred, or equivalent relevant experience
- Deep technical fluency in database query engines, query languages (SQL, MQL), distributed systems, and AI platform tooling

**Nice-to-Have:** MBA/M.Sc./Ph.D., MongoDB experience, developer-focused product leadership, open-source/cloud-native familiarity
