## Position Overview
The Design and Manufacturing Strategic Partnerships team at Autodesk plays a pivotal role in driving growth for the product design and manufacturing product portfolio through strategic external collaborations. The Partnerships Marketing Manager role has a dotted-line relationship to this team, which oversees the negotiation and relationship management with external partners.

The Partnership Marketing Manager for Autodesk Design and Manufacturing will support the team by developing and executing marketing strategies with strategic partners that generate demand, engage the community of Autodesk Fusion users, and uplevel the perception of Autodesk Fusion in the market. A successful Partnership Marketing Manager deeply understands the goals and priorities of their assigned partners, while aligning these efforts with the team's overarching growth and marketing objectives. Requires fostering meaningful internal and external relationships.

## Responsibilities
- Own and support the marketing relationships with assigned strategic partners to develop and implement creative and high-impact marketing campaigns that help all parties achieve their goals.
- Apply a win-win approach to the development of creative marketing tactics and strategies with strategic partners.
- Develop, manage, and communicate comprehensive partnership plans for assigned partners, ensuring their successful execution.
- Manage the end-to-end execution of marketing tactics aligned with defined partnership strategies, including tradeshows, campaigns, communications, content, events, and more.
- Create, manage, and communicate partnership performance data and insights for assigned partners, ensuring alignment with strategic goals.

## Minimum Qualifications
- 3–5 years' experience in marketing, business development, or partner engagement within a B2B software setting.
- Proven track record in campaign execution, project management, and/or partner relationship management.
- Strong writing, communication, and presentation skills. The ability to communicate and sell others on a vision is a must.
- Analytical, experimental mindset to conduct structured experiments/test hypotheses, identify relevant metrics to measure performance, and translate insights into action.
- Self-starter with strong organizational skills and attention to detail.

## Preferred Qualifications
- Experience with product design agencies, manufacturing environments, engineering firms, or other audiences relevant to the product design and manufacturing industry.
- Expertise in paid social media advertising.
- Basic graphic design skills (Canva, Adobe Suite) and an aesthetic sense when creating marketing materials or presentations.
- Experience managing, attending, and marketing at trade shows, conferences, and open houses. Comfort with occasional travel (10-20%).
