Innovate with purpose

At BILL, we believe in empowering the businesses that drive our economy. By replacing outdated financial processes with innovative tools, we help businesses, from startups to established brands, make smarter decisions and gain control of their operations. And we don't stop there: we're creating the future of financial automation so businesses can spend more time on what matters.

Working here means you become part of a vision-driven team that's ready to tackle challenges and build cutting-edge solutions. We value purpose, drive, and curiosity, and we thrive in a fast-paced, ever-changing environment. We're remote-first, with office spaces in San Jose, CA and Draper, UT, and opportunities to connect in person when it counts. BILLders collaborate to deliver real impact for businesses that need more time in their busy weeks.

Make your impact within a rapidly growing Fintech Company

- BILL is looking for a Senior Customer Marketing Manager to lead customer marketing programs for accountant firms and SMB/MM customers. This role will help customers learn, adopt, engage with, and grow through BILL's products and services.
- This is a highly cross-functional role with a particularly close partnership with Product Marketing. You'll translate product strategy and launch priorities into compelling customer programs while aligning with Product, Community, Marketing Operations, Analytics, Creative, Sales, and Events.

Responsibilities:
- Product launches and customer education: Lead Customer Marketing strategy for Tier 1 product launches, monthly product showcases, and related education programs. Partner with Product Marketing on positioning, messaging, audience strategy, promotion, and measurement. Expand product showcases to Direct SMB/MM customers while continuing to serve the accountant firm audience.
- Webinar and virtual programs: Develop strategy for webinars and virtual lunch-and-learns that support education, retention, adoption, and cross-sell. Own audience targeting, programming, promotion, follow-up, and measurement. Serve as the Customer Marketing team's webinar strategy and logistics expert.
- Customer event strategy: Develop the strategy for in-person customer experiences, including audiences, objectives, formats, programming, promotion, and success measures. Provide Events with clear briefs, audience plans, and promotional support; the Events team owns live execution and logistics.
- Community and communications: Partner with Community on customer engagement, education, advocacy, and feedback. Develop and coordinate communications across email, in-product, web, webinars, community, and other channels.
- Cross-functional leadership: Align stakeholders, manage dependencies, establish clear operating rhythms, and ensure the customer perspective is represented in planning and decision-making.

We'd love to chat if you have:
- 5+ years of experience in customer marketing, lifecycle marketing, growth marketing, integrated marketing, or a related discipline.
- Experience leading product launches, customer education programs, webinars, or customer events from strategy through measurement.
- Demonstrated ability to build trusted partnerships with Product Marketing and other senior cross-functional stakeholders.
- Strong project management skills and the ability to manage multiple complex priorities in a fast-paced environment.
- Strong analytical skills, including defining KPIs, evaluating performance, and turning insights into action.
- Excellent written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to influence and align partners.
- Experience with marketing automation, customer communication, or webinar platforms (Inflection, Pendo, Goldcast, SFDC)
- Customer-first mindset, strong attention to detail, and a bias toward ownership.

Desired Qualifications:
- Experience in B2B, SaaS, fintech, payments, or product-led growth. Bonus: experience with Accountant firms.
- Bachelor's degree or equivalent practical experience.

What's in it for you? Redefining how businesses automate their work is a fast-paced, exciting, and fun environment. Benefits include: 100% paid employee health, dental, and vision plans (HMO, PPO, or HDHP); HSA & FSA accounts; Life Insurance, Long & Short-term disability coverage; Employee Assistance Program (EAP); 11+ Observed holidays and wellness days and flexible time off; Employee Stock Purchase Program with employee discounts; Wellness & Fitness initiatives; Employee recognition and referral programs.
