## Role Summary
Works with the founding team to build Soul Space from the ground up - business model
development, commercialization, scaling. Initial market is schools and educational
institutions. Explicitly "neither a conventional strategy, business development, nor
operations management position."

## Key Responsibilities (condensed)
- **Business model/commercialization:** Refine value proposition, develop pricing/revenue
  models (institutional subscriptions, per-student pricing), validate market, build
  financial projections/unit economics.
- **GTM and business development:** Design go-to-market strategy, build relationships with
  school leaders/counsellors, run pilot programs, own institutional sales cycle.
- **Product/service ecosystem:** Align tech platform and clinical teams with customer
  needs, build institutional wellbeing offering, explore higher-ed expansion.
- **Partnerships:** Establish partnerships with schools, school networks, mental health
  professionals; build professional support network with clinical team.
- **Operations:** Set up service-delivery processes (safeguarding, referrals,
  confidentiality, escalation procedures with clinical experts); build teams as business
  grows.
- **Financial performance:** Develop budgets, revenue forecasts, performance dashboards;
  monitor acquisition costs, retention, profitability.

## Ideal Candidate Profile (condensed)
- Has founded or significantly helped build an early-stage business, ideally from concept
  to commercialization.
- Strong commercial acumen in revenue generation, pricing, financial planning.
- Proven ability to acquire customers, build partnerships, negotiate commercial agreements.
- Comfortable executing with limited resources and ambiguous priorities.
- Cross-functional experience across strategy, sales, marketing, operations, technology,
  finance.
- Able to lead independently, build teams, take accountability.

## Preferred Experience
EdTech, HealthTech, mental wellbeing, or institutional B2B background; experience selling
to schools/colleges/universities or other educational institutions; experience launching
products, entering new markets, or scaling early-stage ventures; familiarity with the
Indian education ecosystem and its decision-making processes.
