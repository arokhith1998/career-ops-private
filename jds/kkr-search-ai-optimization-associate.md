## Job Description
Performance and growth marketing Associate with deep expertise in search, content strategy, and conversion optimization. Lead acquisition across paid and organic search while advancing KKR's visibility in emerging AI-driven discovery environments. Partner with Marketing, Content, Web Product, and Analytics teams on SEO, paid search, AEO, and GEO.

## Responsibilities
- Develop and execute integrated digital acquisition strategies across paid search, organic search, and AI-driven discovery platforms
- Own paid search (SEM/PPC) programs — Google Ads, campaign development, budget management, performance optimization
- Lead SEO strategy — keyword research, technical optimization, on-page improvements, content gap analysis
- Advance Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) — featured snippets, knowledge panels, zero-click search
- Develop Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) strategies — Google AI Overviews, Gemini, ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Copilot
- Partner with content/editorial teams on high-quality, authoritative content aligned to search intent and AI retrieval
- Lead CRO initiatives — A/B and multivariate testing of landing pages, forms, user journeys
- Collaborate with UX/UI and product teams on digital experience and conversion
- Define KPIs and performance benchmarks across acquisition channels
- Partner with external agencies and vendors

## Requirements
- Bachelor's in marketing, business, communications, or related
- 5+ years in digital acquisition, performance marketing, or growth marketing
- Deep expertise in paid search (Google Ads, Microsoft Ads) and performance optimization
- Strong SEO, technical SEO, and content-driven organic growth
- CRO, experimentation, landing page optimization
- Familiarity with AEO and emerging GEO/AI-driven search optimization
- Adobe Analytics, Google Search Console, SEMrush, attribution platforms
- Strong analytical mindset — data to actionable insights
- Budget management and ROI optimization
- Cross-functional collaboration and stakeholder management

## Preferred
- MBA or advanced degree
- AI-powered search platforms and LLM optimization strategies
- Financial services, asset management, or B2B marketing background
- Regulated environments with compliance requirements
- Google Ads or related certifications
