**In this role you will:**
- Own the SMB Ads web strategy and roadmap to grow qualified discovery, signup, and advertiser activation.
- Grow organic discovery through search, content, and GEO.
- Test new ways to help businesses find and evaluate OpenAI's advertising offering.
- Analyze journeys, funnels, and cohorts to identify conversion barriers and prioritize high-impact improvements.
- Run experiments across landing pages, messaging, navigation, personalization, offers, and conversion flows.
- Improve the journey from website visit to signup, onboarding, and first campaign with Product and Ads Engineering.
- Define instrumentation, test design, attribution, and reporting with Data Science and marketing systems teams.
- Launch useful web experiences with Content, Product Marketing, Design, Engineering, and specialist agencies.
- Use AI to accelerate research, prototyping, content, analysis, and experimentation.

**You might thrive in this role if you bring:**
- 4-7 years of experience in growth marketing, web strategy, organic acquisition, conversion optimization, or product growth, with measurable acquisition, activation, or revenue impact.
- Apply strong analytical judgment across funnels, cohorts, customer behavior, and incremental impact to prioritize growth opportunities.
- Demonstrate ownership of web experiences and experimentation programs that improve conversion from discovery to activation.
- Connect SEO, content-led discovery, and GEO with customer insight, web strategy, and product judgment.
- Translate business opportunities into clear roadmaps and launched experiences with Product, Design, Engineering, Data Science, and Marketing.
- Relevant backgrounds include scaled technology businesses, startups, or consulting followed by operating experience in startup growth.
- Understand SMB customers and bring experience in self-serve products, advertising, SaaS, marketplaces, fintech, commerce, or other high-volume digital businesses.

**Preferred Qualifications:** Experience building web and organic growth programs for SMBs or self-serve advertising platforms; track record improving conversion from website discovery through signup and first campaign activation; experience growing qualified traffic through SEO, content, and GEO; hands-on experience testing landing pages, personalization, and onboarding flows; experience implementing web instrumentation, attribution, and experimentation tools with technical partners; experience using AI to accelerate research, prototyping, content development, and analysis.
