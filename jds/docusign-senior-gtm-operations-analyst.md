Company Overview

Docusign brings agreements to life. More than 1.8 million customers and more than a billion people in over 180 countries use Docusign solutions to accelerate the process of doing business and simplify people's lives.

What you'll do

As a Senior GTM Operations Analyst, you will leverage data, analytical tools, and AI to investigate complex GTM operational issues, uncover insights, and recommend resolutions that improve sales execution and support annual planning. A key focus of this role is resolving complex GTM and Sales Operations exceptions by analyzing sales, post-sales, opportunity, quota, and compensation data.

This position is an individual contributor role reporting to the Sr. Director, Sales Operations.

**Responsibility**
- Investigate complex GTM and Sales Operations exceptions involving opportunity credit, quota, compensation, and related operational scenarios.
- Leverage data from sources such as Snowflake, Tableau, Salesforce, and Oracle, along with analytical tools such as spreadsheets, Python, and AI solutions.
- Apply AI and emerging technologies to enhance analytical workflows, accelerate investigations, synthesize complex information, and reduce repetitive manual effort.
- Apply bookings frameworks, quota structures, and sales compensation metrics to develop analyses and capacity models that support annual planning.
- Investigate cross-system operational challenges, identify recurring issues and underlying causes, and recommend scalable solutions.
- Partner with GTM leadership and stakeholders across FP&A, Revenue Operations, HR, Sales Compensation, Field Leadership, and Strategy.

Job Designation: Hybrid: Employee divides their time between in-office and remote work. Access to an office location is required. (Frequency: Minimum 2 days per week; may vary by team but will be weekly in-office expectation)

**What you bring -- Basic**
- 8+ years of experience in Sales Operations, GTM Operations, Sales Strategy, Consulting, or related Strategy & Operations roles
- Bachelor's degree or equivalent practical experience
- Experience investigating and resolving complex GTM operational exceptions
- Experience with analytical tools such as Excel or Google Sheets, with experience working with data from Snowflake, Salesforce, or Oracle
- Experience with SaaS business models, GTM principles, bookings frameworks, quota structures, and sales compensation
- Experience leveraging AI or emerging technologies to analyze data

**Preferred**
- Experience in B2B SaaS within a global or complex, matrixed organization
- Experience using SQL, Python, or similar analytical tools
- Experience with Tableau or other BI/data visualization tools
- Experience supporting annual GTM planning, quota and capacity modeling, sales compensation analysis, or opportunity crediting
