## Description
Glean seeks a strategic product manager to define the Enterprise Intelligence product area. The role involves translating company vision into specific product bets, spending significant time with customers to understand use cases, and building products that turn Glean's enterprise context layer into actionable workflows and dashboards for teams and leaders. The PM will partner with engineering, design, and data teams to scope products and drive customer-focused prioritization.

## Key Responsibilities
- Define and drive product strategy/roadmap for Enterprise Intelligence
- Conduct customer research on high-value intelligence use cases
- Build products converting enterprise context into actionable workflows
- Partner with cross-functional teams on product scoping and quality standards
- Collaborate with go-to-market and customer-facing teams
- Establish product foundations for the emerging category
