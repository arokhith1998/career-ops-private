## Full JD text (verbatim, as extracted)

We're hiring for two roles inside the Founders' Office at Binge Labs .

Both sit close to the founders. Both are high-trust. Both are for people who take ownership without being told twice.

1. Founders' Office: New Initiatives

This one is hard to put in a box. And that's exactly the point.

You'll hold the founders accountable for their own personal brands. Own Binge Labs' marketing and branding as a company. Find creative ways to delight clients and the team. Spot what's broken across the business and fix it. And represent Binge Labs at events and on stages.

One day it's brand. The next it's a hard conversation with a founder. The day after that it's an event.

If you need a narrow lane, this isn't for you. If you thrive on variety and want to shape something from scratch, this might be the best seat you'll find at this stage of your career.

1-3 years of experience in a founder's office, brand, marketing, or generalist role.

2. Founders' Office: Admin

This one is about making sure the commercial backbone of Binge Labs never breaks.

Invoicing. Collections. Contracts. Vendor payments. Client gifting. Travel logistics.

You're not here to process paperwork. You're here to make sure money comes in on time, vendors get paid without friction, contracts don't stall, and the founders never have to follow up on the same thing twice.

High trust. High ownership. Close to the founders.

1-3 years of experience in operations, admin, or finance support.

Both roles are fully remote. Delhi preferred.

If either of these sound like you, email people@bingelabs.in with the role name in the subject line.
