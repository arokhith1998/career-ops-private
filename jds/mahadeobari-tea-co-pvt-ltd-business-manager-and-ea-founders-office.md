Company Description Mahadeobari Tea Co. Pvt. Ltd. is a tea manufacturing company specializing in Assam Black Tea, including both CTC and Orthodox varieties. The company operates tea estates in Upper Assam, focusing on quality production and consistent supply. With deep roots in the region’s tea-growing tradition, Mahadeobari combines estate-level expertise with modern manufacturing practices. The organization offers opportunities for professionals interested in the tea industry, end-to-end operations, and long-term growth within a stable, product-focused company.
Role Description The Business Manager and EA – Founder’s Office is a full-time, on-site role based in Kolkata. This position supports the founder in managing day-to-day business operations, including coordinating tea production and supply activities, tracking performance metrics, and preparing business reports, summaries, and presentations. The role involves managing the founder’s calendar, travel, meetings, and communication, as well as handling confidential information and follow-ups with internal teams and external partners. The Business Manager and EA will assist in strategic initiatives, vendor and stakeholder coordination, and cross-functional project execution to ensure smooth operations between the Kolkata office and the tea estates in Upper Assam.

- Excellent executive assistance capabilities, such as calendar and travel management, meeting coordination, documentation, and stakeholder communication.
- Solid analytical and reporting skills, with the ability to prepare business reviews, performance dashboards, and concise presentations for leadership.
- Effective communication and interpersonal skills, with the ability to interact professionally with internal teams, vendors, and external partners.
- High degree of integrity, discretion, and organizational skills, with the ability to manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment.
- Proficiency in productivity tools (e.g., MS Office or similar), email and document management, and basic data analysis.
- Bachelor’s degree in Business, Management, Commerce, or a related field; prior experience in an EA or business management role is preferred.
