## Full JD text

We're looking for a Chief of Staff to the CMO to provide business leadership and establish operational rigor for the Databricks Marketing organization and support our mission to be the leading enterprise Data + AI company. You will develop an understanding of strategic and operational challenges faced by teams and define appropriate solutions to address such challenges. As a member of the leadership team, directly reporting to the CMO, you will extend the reach of the CMO by acting as a strategic advisor to the Marketing leadership team and managing all the critical business operations of the CMO office, including:

- AI transformation
- Investment stewardship
- Annual strategic planning
- Company priority initiatives
- Marketing priorities
- Cross-functional initiatives
- Executive presentations
- Marketing performance (ROI) management
- Organizational strategy
- Internal marketing communication

### Key Responsibilities

- Translate Databricks' company strategy into Marketing annual plans, OKRs, and strategic initiatives
- Drive marketing alignment and execution across a 250-person Marketing team
- Lead cross-functional process improvements for efficiency and profitability goals
- Accelerate decision-making velocity by owning critical CMO forums
- Increase planning and analytic rigor to improve Marketing ROI
- Drive marketing-wide and cross-functional alignment through CMO communications

### Requirements

- 10+ years of marketing experience, ideally including recent Director-level experience
- B2B experience at a dynamic, high-growth software company
- Proficiency in AI and experience transforming marketing functions with AI
- Proficiency in analytics and data-driven decision-making
- Significant experience leading strategic and annual planning
- Strong project management and OKR leadership skills
- Excellent written and verbal communication with Senior Leadership and C-Level
- Experience in organizational structure improvement and process optimization
- Analytics consulting firm experience preferred
- Chief of Staff experience nice to have, not essential
