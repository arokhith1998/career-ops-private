- Excellent communication and presentation skills.
- Highly organised, with the ability to multitask and work in an ambiguous, fast-paced environment.
- Hunger to learn, take ownership and work closely with leadership.
- Very strong with data: Excel or Google Sheets modelling, dashboards, and comfort working from raw numbers.
- A proven record of building or fixing processes in a team, with results to show.

Good to have
- Experience in D2C, consumer brands or e-commerce.
