About the Company
Millions of people need legal help but never hire an attorney because
traditional legal services are too expensive, confusing, or difficult to
access.

Access Legal is working to change that.

We help law firms build more accessible and profitable practices while
making it easier for everyday people to hire an attorney. We combine
marketing, technology, intelligent intake, flexible service models, and
business support to modernize how legal services are delivered.

We are looking for a senior, hands-on marketer to help us bring that model
to a much larger audience.

The Role
You will work directly with the Chief Marketing Officer to develop, build,
launch, and improve integrated marketing campaigns.

We need someone who can develop the campaign idea, write the messaging,
build the campaign, work with creative, review the landing page, and
analyze the results.

Our team already includes skilled specialists across paid media, design,
development, analytics, and RevOps. Your role will be to connect those
capabilities and help turn strong ideas into campaigns that actually launch.

What You Will Do
- Develop campaigns for both consumer and attorney audiences
- Personally build and optimize campaigns across Google, Microsoft, Meta,
  LinkedIn, and YouTube
- Write ad copy, landing pages, emails, video scripts, and creative briefs
- Develop campaign messaging, offers, audiences, and testing plans
- Lead landing-page strategy and conversion testing
- Create video advertising concepts and scripts
- Promote webinars and selected events
- Work directly with creative, paid media, web, and RevOps specialists
- Review performance and determine what should change next
- Take campaigns from initial idea through launch and iteration

What We Are Looking For
- Significant experience in integrated marketing, growth marketing, demand
  generation, or campaign strategy
- A strong record of personally building and launching campaigns
- Excellent writing, positioning, and messaging skills
- Hands-on experience with major advertising platforms
- Experience with landing pages, conversion strategy, video, and analytics
- The ability to move comfortably between strategy and execution
- Strong written and verbal English communication
- Recent examples of work you personally created, built, launched, or
  optimized

Why Join Us
- Work on a meaningful problem affecting millions of people
- Help modernize an industry that is ready for change
- Work with substantial advertising budgets and experienced specialists
- Have direct access to leadership and meaningful influence over strategy
- Help shape a growing marketing organization and its future playbook

This Role Is Not for Someone Who
- Wants to operate primarily as a manager or delegator
- Has not personally built a campaign recently
- Prefers strategy presentations over execution
- Believes hands-on work is beneath a senior role
