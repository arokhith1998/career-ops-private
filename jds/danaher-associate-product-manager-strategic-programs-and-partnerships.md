This position reports to the Product Manager, Product Lifecycle Management and is part of the Product Management organization and will be fully remote.

In this role, you will have the opportunity to:
- Execute and support strategic partnership initiatives and cross-functional programs that advance business objectives and expand organizational capabilities.
- Partner with stakeholders across Product Management, Marketing, Commercial, Operations, Quality, Regulatory Affairs, Digital Product, IT, and external organizations to drive project execution and achieve key deliverables.
- Lead the coordination, documentation, and tracking of business requirements, project plans, milestones, risks, dependencies, and decisions to ensure successful program delivery.
- Support product and partnership launches by driving launch readiness activities, stakeholder communications, training coordination, and operational transition planning.
- Analyze customer, partner, and operational insights to identify opportunities for continuous improvement and contribute to the scalability and effectiveness of product and partnership programs.

The essential requirements of the job include:
- Bachelor's degree in Business, Marketing, Life Sciences, Engineering, or a related field, or equivalent experience.
- 1+ years of experience in Product Management, Project Management, Business Operations, Consulting, or a related discipline.
- Experience applying established methods to analyze information and develop findings and recommendations.

It would be a plus if you also possess previous experience in:
- Supporting strategic partnerships or external collaborations, including working cross-functionally with globally distributed teams and without direct authority.
- Product launch planning, commercialization, or launch-readiness activities within life sciences, biotechnology, diagnostics, or a related scientific industry.
- Using project management tools and methodologies to coordinate projects and cross-functional activities.
