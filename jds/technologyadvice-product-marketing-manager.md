**The opportunity**
We're looking for a passionate B2B Marketer to take on a key role in our product marketing, thought leadership, sales enablement programs here at TechnologyAdvice.

The Product Marketing Manager will play a pivotal role within our core marketing team that is responsible for building awareness and generating demand from B2B marketing and demand generation leaders at the largest technology companies in the world. That's right, you get to market to other B2B marketers!

Reporting directly to the Chief Marketing Officer, this role will be responsible for planning and delivering our product messaging and positioning, customer and market intelligence, client-facing content and collateral, and related sales enablement activities. This role will also support our content marketing and thought leadership efforts, helping us craft compelling content that aligns with our core positioning strategy and market insights.

As a key part of a small and collaborative team, you'll have countless opportunities to impact the business, grow your product marketing and content strategy skills, and expand your career as a B2B marketer.

Location: United States (Remote)

**What You'll Do**
- Lead the strategy and execution of core product marketing functions including customer research, market intelligence, product positioning, and customer-facing collateral
- Drive our content and thought leadership strategy based on customer and market insights, industry trends, and core positioning priorities
- Partner closely with our sales team to provide up-to-date collateral, presentations, research, videos, and other tools to be used throughout the sales process
- Partner with our Product and Operations teams to support product strategy, new product and service introductions, and related product collateral and messaging
- Collaborate with others in our marketing team including Demand Gen and Sales Development to support key campaigns, refine messaging, and ensure our key programs are leveraging the latest thought leadership and market research
- Act as a subject matter expert on B2B marketing as a content creator and publisher, with potential to participate in podcasts, videos and more (if of interest!)

**Who You Are**
- 5+ years of experience in B2B Marketing, Product Marketing, Demand Generation, Content Marketing, or similar roles
- Genuine passion for understanding buyers, researching markets, and developing stand-out messaging and creative assets that elevate brands in the market
- Strong ability to work collaboratively with cross-functional teams
- Detail-oriented, organized, and able to manage multiple projects simultaneously
- Desire to go beyond 'core product marketing' by contributing to content marketing, sales enablement, demand generation, and other priorities across the team
- Able to thrive in a high-velocity, agile, and rapidly changing environment
- Self-starter who is comfortable working independently and experimenting
- A genuine desire to learn, grow, and build your career as a B2B marketing professional
- Agile professional who excels in a fast-paced environment and thrives on continuously pivoting strategies to drive business needs forward
