## Job Description (extracted, verbatim from posting)

At Google, we put our users first. In this role, you will work cross-functionally to guide products from conception to launch by connecting the technical and business worlds. You will work closely with product teams to innovate, incubate and prototype how to evolve and transform Google Cloud Platform (GCP) products to provide Generative AI driven experiences and solutions.

**Minimum qualifications:**
- Bachelor's degree or equivalent practical experience.
- 8 years of experience in product management or related technical role.
- 3 years of experience taking technical products from conception to launch (ideation to execution, end-to-end, 0 to 1, etc).
- Experience with the domain area of Generative AI or Large Language Models.

**Preferred qualifications:**
- Master's degree in a technology or business related field.
- 5 years of experience working cross-functionally with engineering, UX/UI, sales finance, and other stakeholders.
- 5 years of experience in a business function or role (e.g., strategic marketing, business operations, consulting).
- 4 years of experience in a role preparing and delivering technical presentations to senior leadership.
- 3 years of experience in software development or engineering.
- 3 years of people management experience with direct reports, and in technical leadership.

**Responsibilities:**
- Lead PRDs by collaborating with partner teams (engineers, PgMs, UX) to turn PRDs into OKRs for multiple teams
- Own and define project roadmaps
- Drive development and secure buy-in for a product vision
- Manage validation of market size and opportunity
- Lead and harmonize processes to define/identify/collect/track product or business metrics
