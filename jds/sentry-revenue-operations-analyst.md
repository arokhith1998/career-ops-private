About the Role: Sentry's Revenue Operations team is built around three pillars - Analytics, GTM Technology, and Deal Desk - and we're hiring a Revenue Operations Analyst who will work across all three. Roughly half your time will be deal desk ownership while the other half is the broader rev ops surface area - process design, systems and tooling, reporting and insights, and cross-functional projects.

In This Role You Will: Own Deal Desk - end-to-end processing of deals (quote creation, discount review, order form generation, approval routing). Become an expert in the CPQ system and Salesforce integration. Help design and implement self-service guardrails and automation. Build and maintain deal desk playbooks, approval matrices, and process documentation. Support pricing updates in CPQ. Identify patterns across deal data and surface insights.

Qualifications: 5+ years of experience in Deal Desk, Sales Operations, Revenue Operations, or a closely related function at a B2B SaaS company. Hands-on experience with CPQ tools (Dealhub, Salesforce CPQ, MonetizeNow or similar). Comfortable in Salesforce. Experience handling the full deal lifecycle: quoting, redlines, order forms, and booking. Strong analytical instincts. Experience using AI tooling (Copilot, ChatGPT, or purpose-built RevOps tools) to accelerate deal review.

Nice to Have: Familiarity with SaaS pricing models including usage-based, ramped, or multi-product structures. Experience supporting both new business and renewal motions.
