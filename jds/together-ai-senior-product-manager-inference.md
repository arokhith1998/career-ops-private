Inference is a critical part of Together AI's vision and growth strategy. Developers and enterprises use Together to run leading open models across coding agents, AI-native applications, enterprise workflows, and large-scale production workloads.

We're looking for a Senior Product Manager to shape how customers consume and build on Together inference. As inference becomes a larger part of our business, the product surface is becoming more complex. Workloads span different tenancy, capacity, performance, and modality needs; agentic applications are changing how inference is consumed; and customers increasingly expect deep infrastructure capabilities to translate into predictable, easy-to-use products.

This role owns the product layer between Together's inference infrastructure and the developers and enterprises consuming it. This is a hands-on technical product role. You'll use our APIs and SDKs, study real workloads, test integrations, and work closely with inference platform engineers and researchers to turn infrastructure and model capabilities into clear product decisions and customer experiences.

**Responsibilities**
- Shape the inference product strategy
- Own the developer experience around inference (APIs, SDKs, compatibility, integrations)
- Build for emerging workloads and modalities (agentic, voice, multimodal, batch)
- Turn infrastructure and model capabilities into customer-facing products
- Connect product decisions to business outcomes (pricing and packaging alongside Finance and GTM)
- Stay close to customers and the product
- Drive products from idea to production

**Requirements**
- Strong product judgment and the ability to take an ambiguous technical problem, identify what matters, make tradeoffs, and develop a clear point of view
- Meaningful experience building or owning APIs, developer tools, infrastructure, cloud platforms, data systems, AI products, or another technically deep product
- Strong technical depth and curiosity
- A hands-on operating style
- Strong developer and customer empathy
- A track record of making clear product decisions and shipping in fast-moving environments
- Strong communication and cross-functional leadership
