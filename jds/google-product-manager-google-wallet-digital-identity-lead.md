## Role Summary
Owns end-to-end strategy/execution for trusted, real-world digital identity credentials in
Google Wallet - create/store/share digital IDs with third parties, privacy-conscious,
standards-based, globally interoperable. Works with governments, privacy experts, issuers;
defines hero use cases for digital IDs.

## Responsibilities
- Lead product strategy/execution for high-trust digital identity credentials.
- Work with design/engineering on UX, feature prioritization, credential
  management/sharing.
- Drive privacy/policy reviews for handling sensitive user data.
- Work with business development/technical partners on a globally interoperable ecosystem
  (in-person and online use).
- Serve as digital identity SME; engage public sector partners and government affairs on
  standards/use cases.
