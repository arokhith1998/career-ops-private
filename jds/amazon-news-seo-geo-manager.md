## Role overview

Amazon seeks an experienced SEO/GEO Manager to own search and AI visibility strategy for Amazon News (AboutAmazon.com). The role develops SEO and GEO plans, expands organic traffic, and partners with editorial, comms, and engineering teams across a global organization. The seat pairs traditional SEO (keyword strategy, technical audits, structured data, site architecture, SEMrush + GSC reporting) with GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) - i.e., shaping how AboutAmazon.com content surfaces and is cited by ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, and Google's AI Overviews.

## Key responsibilities

- Develop comprehensive organic SEO and GEO strategy with growth initiatives.
- Monitor and optimize content visibility in AI chatbots like ChatGPT and Perplexity.
- Build SEO/GEO reporting processes for leadership and stakeholders.
- Manage SEO tools including Google Search Console and SEMRush.
- Conduct technical SEO audits addressing site architecture and indexation.
- Partner with engineering teams on URL structure and schema optimization.
- Work with editorial teams to maximize organic engagement.
- Identify keyword opportunities and provide editorial guidance.
- Develop link and traffic-building strategies.
- Stay current on industry trends and competitor techniques.

## Basic qualifications

- 6+ years professional marketing experience.
- Excel or Tableau proficiency.
- 5+ years SEO experience with organic traffic growth track record.
- 4+ years marketing, journalism, or related field experience.
- 3+ years building SEO reports and communicating to stakeholders.
- 3+ years with SEO tools and content management systems.
- Understanding of LLM content surfacing and AI discoverability strategies.

## Preferred qualifications

- SEO leadership in journalism/media organizations.
- GEO tool experience (Profound, Scrunch, Writesonic).
- 2+ years leading content marketing programs.
- International, multi-language project management experience.
- Product management background.
- Strong storytelling judgment and communication skills.
- Self-directed project ownership and independence.
