## Role overview
Faire is the largest online marketplace for independent retail. The Growth Marketing team scales acquisition, retention, and reengagement across paid + organic channels. This Paid Social seat owns Meta-led retargeting + reengagement for the retailer side of the marketplace, with explicit room to lead AI/automation pilots inside the paid program.

## Responsibilities
- Execute and scale retargeting campaigns to drive repeat retailer purchases and improve retention.
- Manage day-to-day paid social operations - analyze campaign performance, surface optimization opportunities, and act on them.
- Lead execution of high-impact pilot growth projects requiring quick implementation.
- Identify opportunities to leverage AI and automation tools for campaign processes and workflows.
- Collaborate cross-functionally on tracking issues, creative coordination, and data-driven solutions.

## Required qualifications
- 3-5 years direct experience managing medium-scale campaigns on Meta and other paid social platforms.
- Strong understanding of paid marketing metrics (CPM, CTR, CVR) and the underlying optimization levers.
- Proficiency with marketing automation tools (named in JD: Smartly, Hunch, Omneky).
- Highly analytical with a structured problem-solving approach; comfortable in Excel, SQL, and data-visualization tools.
- Experience using AI and automation tools to improve workflows.
- Fast learner who can move between creative and technical solutions.
- Familiarity with A/B testing and incrementality testing.

## Preferred qualifications
- Experience with retargeting and reengagement campaigns.
- Background in two-sided marketplaces or small-business products.
- Cross-border program management experience.
