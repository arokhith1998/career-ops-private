Direct message the job poster from Veersa Technologies: Kavita Madan SHRM-CP - Chief of Staff to the COO, Neurealm

Role summary: The Chief of Staff is the COO's right hand: they turn priorities into an operating rhythm, keep commitments on track across delivery, practices and business units, and free the COO to focus on customers, growth and transformation. Neurealm is an AI-first technology services company that helps enterprises become AI-first through Engineering, IT Operations and Data Engineering, with NeuGAIN as its AI delivery platform.

Title: Chief of Staff, Office of the COO. Reports to: Chief Operating Officer. Works with: ExCom, CBOs, Global Delivery Heads, Practice Heads, Finance, HR, TSC/WFM, Marketing. Location: Bengaluru or Chennai, India (hybrid; travel as needed). Level: Director/Associate Director (based on experience). Experience: 8-14 years. Qualification: MBA from premier B Schools.

Key responsibilities:
1. Operating rhythm and governance: run the COO's operating cadence (weekly leadership reviews, monthly BU and practice reviews, quarterly business reviews); own agendas, pre-reads, decisions and minutes; maintain the COO's action and commitments tracker.
2. Strategy, planning and budgeting: coordinate the annual planning cycle; turn strategy into measurable objectives; run special projects for the COO (org design, partnerships, cost programmes, M&A integration e.g. Veersa).
3. Performance management and insight: own the COO dashboard (revenue, margin, billable headcount and utilisation, pipeline conversion, fulfilment, client satisfaction, AIMI and AIQ); prepare board, chairman and ExCom material.
4. Customer and delivery escalations: track critical accounts and escalations; coordinate recovery plans; prepare the COO for customer meetings.
5. AI-first transformation: drive internal adoption of NeuGAIN, AIMI process-maturity targets and AIQ people-maturity goals; use AI tools to run the COO office itself.
6. Communication and stakeholder management: draft communications from the COO; act as a trusted link between the COO and leaders across BUs.
7. People and talent initiatives: support talent reviews, leadership hiring, succession planning and key-talent retention.

Qualifications and experience - Must have: 5-14 years of experience, including at least 3 years in strategy, business operations, PMO or delivery leadership in IT services or a technology company; hands-on understanding of the IT services business model; track record of running governance or planning processes across senior leaders; strong financial and analytical skills, advanced Excel and PowerPoint; MBA or equivalent from a reputed institute, or an engineering degree with a strong business record.

Good to have: Prior Chief of Staff, CEO/COO office, or management consulting experience; exposure to US healthcare clients; experience with AI tools and automation; experience supporting M&A integration or organisation design.
