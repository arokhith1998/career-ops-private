## Summary
DigiCert wants a technical, execution-focused product manager to advance its Trust Lifecycle Manager platform across cloud, Kubernetes, and identity-security ecosystems. Hands-on work with cloud services, validating workflows, partnering with engineers, turning early concepts into customer-facing features.

## Responsibilities
- **Cloud and Kubernetes:** Build/iterate integrations with AWS, Azure, GCP key and certificate services; work with engineering on certificate automation for Kubernetes and mTLS workflows; prototype in cloud-native environments.
- **Roadmap delivery:** Turn customer needs into specs, user stories, acceptance criteria; manage Agile backlogs; build POCs; help with release planning/rollout.
- **Customer discovery:** Interview DevOps, security, and platform engineers; present prototypes for feedback; turn insights into roadmap proposals; support customers adopting new integrations.
- **Cross-functional work:** Align with architecture and engineering on design/security controls; work with solutions architects, sales engineers, and marketing.

## Qualifications
- 3+ years in product management, technical program management, DevOps, cloud engineering, or similar
- Practical experience with AWS, Azure, or GCP, ideally in security or identity workloads
- Working knowledge of Kubernetes, certificate issuance, mTLS, cloud-native security
- Familiarity with CI/CD, container orchestration, PKI, cryptography, API design, Agile, data-driven decision-making
- Experience building prototypes or working with engineers on POCs
- Strong communication skills for technical and non-technical audiences
