## Description (summarized from live posting; condensed due to fetch-tool quoting limits)

Manages end-to-end Deal Desk functions for new business, expansions, and renewals, working with Sales, Customer Success, and Partnerships. Acts as main escalation point for deal issues.

Key responsibilities:
- Building and reviewing quotes; advising on packaging, multi-year and ramp structures
- Approving discounts within authority; maintaining price book and quote templates
- Tracking turnaround times and SLAs
- Preparing order forms, renewals, and amendments
- Managing redlines with Legal and Finance
- Navigating public-sector procurement (RFPs, purchase orders, budget cycles)
- Auditing Closed Won bookings; maintaining contract repository in Salesforce
- Supporting invoicing and month-end close with Finance

Requirements:
- 5+ years managing Deal Desk or order management in SaaS
- Admin-level Salesforce skills; CPQ or similar quoting tools
- Advanced Excel or Google Sheets
- Familiarity with ARR, NRR, GRR, co-terming, uplifts, and renewals
- Ability to build processes, documentation, and SLA-managed request queues
