## Description

Are you passionate about solving complex business problems, diving deep into data, driving process improvement, and leading change throughout an organization? The AWS Strategic Accounts Business Operations team is seeking an experienced Sales Operations Lead to partner with our account management teams to define, land, and execute against our strategic priorities. You will partner with Sales Leadership and cross-functional teams to develop standard metrics that measure the productivity and success of programs, automate and optimize operational processes, and invent new ways of diving deep with data to uncover actionable insights.

Strategic Accounts is one of AWS's most important and complex businesses, a portfolio of recognized industry leaders unlike any other segment.

**Key job responsibilities**
- Own revenue analysis and forecast accuracy for your accounts, surfacing the "why" behind weekly and monthly trends and variance drivers in partnership with Finance and ProServe.
- Drive measurable improvement in pipeline quality and hygiene.
- Build and run the operating cadence for your account teams, including Weekly and Monthly Business Reviews.
- Lead strategic and annual planning: territory planning, quota setting, goal setting, and account planning.
- Design and manage goals and metrics mechanisms that drive accountability.
- Develop actionable, automated, self-service dashboards and reports.
- Drive process improvement and change management, leading and delivering training.
- Define tooling requirements and influence central teams' roadmaps.
- Lead adoption of AI tooling for operational use cases, quantifying the impact.
- Prepare ad hoc analysis and participate in projects as needed.

**Basic Qualifications**
- Experience conducting sophisticated and creative analysis of complex data and translating results into actionable deliverables, messages, and presentations.
- Bachelor's degree or equivalent.
- Experience defining, refining and implementing sales processes, procedures and policies or equivalent.
- 6+ years of sales operations or equivalent experience.
- Experience presenting to senior leadership.

**Preferred Qualifications**
- Experience using Salesforce (or other CRM tool) or BI tools.
- Experience in business-process design or equivalent.
