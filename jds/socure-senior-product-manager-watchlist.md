## Full Job Description

### Why Socure?
Socure is building the identity trust infrastructure for the digital economy -- verifying 100% of good identities in real time and stopping fraud before it starts.

### About the Role
We are looking for a Senior Product Manager to own Socure's Watchlist screening and monitoring platform -- a foundational product in our compliance suite serving a rapidly growing customer base.

You will own the roadmap and strategy for our market-leading Watchlist Screening and Monitoring product, which enables banks, fintechs, gaming companies, and more around the world manage their sanctions and AML risk. This is a high-ownership, externally-facing role requiring strong product judgment, AML domain curiosity, and the ability to operate across complex partner and customer relationships.

### What You'll Do

**Product Strategy & Roadmap**
- Own the Watchlist product roadmap, balancing compliance accuracy, customer needs, and engineering capacity.
- Translate market trends, regulatory changes, and customer feedback into product priorities and requirements.
- Define and track key product metrics: screening accuracy, false positive rates, match coverage, and customer outcomes.

**Screening, Monitoring & AI Development**
- Drive improvements to Watchlist's core individual screening and monitoring capabilities, including name match accuracy and data coverage.
- Work with Engineering to evolve the platform's scalability, reliability, and integration with Socure's broader product suite.
- Work with data science to build next generation data ingestion technology to improve accuracy of watchlist data.
- Develop and maintain machine learning systems that keep the patented real-time matching engine at the highest levels of recall and precision.

**Cross-Functional Leadership**
- Collaborate with Engineering, Data Science, and Compliance Ops to translate requirements into executable plans.
- Partner with Sales, Customer Success, and GTM to support deal cycles and retain and grow Watchlist's enterprise customer base.
- Represent product externally with compliance buyers, at industry events, and in customer conversations.

**Data & Performance Analysis**
- Use SQL and analytics to evaluate product performance and derive insights that shape prioritization.
- Conduct competitive analysis and deep dives on emerging compliance requirements and market opportunities.

### What You Bring
- Experience: 3-5 years in product management, preferably in compliance technology, AML/sanctions screening, identity verification, or ML-driven products.
- Domain Knowledge: Familiarity with AML/BSA regulations, sanctions screening workflows, or financial crime compliance is strongly preferred.
- Technical Expertise: Strong understanding of APIs, data pipelines, and ML model behavior; comfortable reviewing technical specs and writing SQL.
- Analytical Skills: Ability to evaluate screening performance metrics (recall, precision, hit rate) and translate them into product decisions.
- Customer Focus: Experience working directly with compliance buyers, financial institutions, or enterprise customers.
- Communication and cross-functional collaboration skills.
