## The Opportunity

A high-impact role focused on defining monetization strategy within Adobe's Creativity and Productivity business segment (historically Digital Media: Creative Cloud, Acrobat, Photoshop, Illustrator, Firefly). The team leads development and refinement of Adobe's monetization strategy, including monetization of GenAI and emerging agentic capabilities across segments and routes-to-market.

## What You'll Do

- Define and optimize pricing and packaging strategy for offers within the Creativity and Productivity segment.
- Collect, synthesize and analyze industry and competitor data.
- Develop and conduct qualitative customer research; design quantitative market research (Build-Your-Own Conjoint, Discrete Choice Conjoint, Gabor-Granger).
- Perform statistical analysis on empirical data (sales, usage, etc.).
- Develop financial models that simulate customer behavior and project costs; scenario analysis estimating impact on market penetration, revenue and margin.
- Develop and present arguments that articulate strategy and ROI to acquire executive approval.
- Engage with engineering, finance, and operational teams on implementation within existing quote-to-cash systems.
- Collaborate with Product Management and Product Marketing on roadmaps; set guidelines for direct sales and sales plays/training; complete post-mortems.

## What You'll Need to Succeed

- Proven experience developing pricing and packaging strategies and bringing them to market in a complex matrixed organization.
- Understanding of emerging GenAI capabilities and their practical application.
- Ability to simplify the complex and frame solutions; attention to detail with comfort in ambiguity.
- Excellent deck-writing, communication, and upward-influencing skills; strong EQ and leadership ability.
- Experience driving consensus in a large matrixed organization.
- Background in strategy consulting, product marketing, and/or change management (software/technology) is a plus.
- Familiarity with creativity and document management solutions/workflows is a plus.
- MBA or equivalent experience.
