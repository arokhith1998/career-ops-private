## Description

Mercury seeks a Senior PM to build their first consumer credit card product. The role encompasses the complete credit account experience, including acquisition and application funnels, approval processes, underwriting decisions, line management, rewards structures, and day-to-day customer experience. Key responsibilities include launching the product, optimizing the approval experience, managing customer line experiences, owning the value proposition, ensuring accuracy and trust, driving growth, and aligning cross-functional teams.

Key Requirements:
- 6+ years building consumer products, with at least one involving financial transactions (where money moved and errors cost significantly)
- Track record launching products people actively choose to use, with strong decision-making
- Credit knowledge or ability to quickly develop it (approval rates, line assignment, and related metrics)
- Exceptional cross-functional collaboration across engineering, design, data, risk, compliance, and partners
- Strong craft standards and customer-centric, data-driven approach
- AI product shipping experience, with realistic perspective on capabilities and limitations
- Sound regulatory judgment in regulated environments
- Nice-to-have: card issuing, lending, payments experience; Reg Z and CARD Act familiarity

Compensation: US $200,700-$250,900 USD base salary; Canada $189,700-$237,100 CAD base salary. Package includes equity (stock options/RSUs) and benefits.
