## Company

Prolific is "the architects of the human data infrastructure that's reshaping the landscape of AI development." Connects researchers + AI developers with ~120K vetted global participants for ethically sourced human behavioral data used in RLHF, SFT, model evaluation, and annotation.

- Series A: GBP 25M (~$32M) co-led by Partech + Oxford Science Enterprises, July 2023
- Total raised to date: ~$33.4M (seed + Series A)
- Customers include Google, Stanford, Oxford, King's College London, European Commission
- >50% of customers in the US; company expanding US presence

## Role framing

Growth Marketing Manager, AI. Drives growth through technical credibility rather than traditional marketing. Owns revenue motion across full funnel (documentation -> enterprise data access deals). Reports into growth leadership.

## Responsibilities

- Organize technical roundtables, sponsor hackathons, engage AI communities
- Translate platform capabilities into researcher-focused language (e.g., "reducing hallucination rates via high-quality RLHF")
- Manage complete sales funnel from documentation engagement to enterprise data access deals
- Operate as a "laboratory" across multiple channels with emphasis on performance metrics and ROI
- Build signal-based GTM systems leveraging technical indicators (GitHub activity, funding announcements)
- Implement AI-augmented workflows to automate data analysis, personalization, and content generation

## Required qualifications

- Understanding of AI lifecycle and technical concepts including SFT and RLHF
- Proficiency with automation tools: Clay, Zapier, Cowork, Skills, or custom GPTs
- Demonstrated ability to learn rapidly in a fast-evolving AI landscape
- Revenue-focused mentality with emphasis on pipeline and CAC efficiency
- Proven execution bias and self-directed project completion
