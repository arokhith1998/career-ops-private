## Description

**About Moloco:** Moloco is an AI-native performance advertising company, bringing the power of AI advertising to the open internet. Founded in 2013 by machine learning engineers who had built AI advertising systems inside the world's largest technology companies. Today, Moloco is one company with two businesses: Moloco Ads (AI-native performance advertising platform) and Moloco Commerce Media (AI-native retail media platform), both powered by CARA (Compound Ad Recommendation Architecture).

**The Impact You'll Be Contributing to Moloco:** We are looking for a GTM Sr. Strategy Ops Manager to drive the AMER business. As a strategic thought partner for regional leaders, this role plays an instrumental role in shaping the commercial strategy and operations for Moloco's AMER business.

Success for this leader will come in four core areas:
- Developing AMER commercial strategy grounded in market insights, product roadmaps, competitive dynamics, and customer behaviors.
- Acting as a trusted thought partner to AMER sales leadership, proactively surfacing risks and opportunities.
- Leading complex, cross-functional initiatives end-to-end, coordinating across Sales, GTM, Product, and other stakeholders.
- Building AI-enabled systems and workflows that allow the AMER sales and GTM teams to operate with greater leverage.
- Owning and continuously improving the AMER rhythm of business (WBRs, MBRs, QBRs).

**The Opportunity:** The best candidate is a strategic thinker and trusted advisor first, who also knows how to drive execution. This is not a reporting or process management role. This leader will:
- Develop market insights and competitive intelligence.
- Scope, own, and lead complex GTM initiatives end-to-end.
- Identify operational gaps across the commercial team, define improvement initiatives.
- Own the regional operating rhythm and alignment with global GTM policy.
- Lead regional planning processes including target setting, headcount planning, and annual/half-year strategic planning cycles.
- Design and deploy AI-enabled workflows and repeatable systems across the AMER commercial team, defaulting to AI as the implementation layer.
- Manage and develop 1 direct report, providing coaching, clear direction, and building a culture of ownership and accountability.

**How Do I Know if the Role is Right For Me?**
- 10+ years of experience in Strategy Consulting, Sales Operations, Revenue Strategy, or Commercial Leadership.
- Keen understanding of B2B sales motions.
- Proven track record of driving top line growth via strategic initiatives in a global, fast-paced, B2B sales setting.
- Experience directly managing or developing team members.
- Demonstrated AI fluency; actively uses AI tools in their own work; strongest candidates will have built or deployed at least one AI-assisted workflow or agent in a professional setting with measurable results.
- Bonus: performance advertising language (ROAS, incrementality, attribution, audience targeting, measurement); familiarity with programmatic and walled garden advertising ecosystem; work experience in a fast-paced startup or high-growth environment.

**Compensation & Benefits:** U.S.-based employees have access to medical, dental, and vision insurance, a 401(k) plan with company match, short-term and long-term disability coverage, basic life insurance, well-being benefits and perks, up to 12 scheduled paid holidays per calendar year, one Thrive Day off per quarter, and Flexible Time Off (FTO).
