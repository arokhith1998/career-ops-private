## Full JD text (verbatim from Ashby posting-api descriptionHtml)

Perplexity is revolutionizing how companies get work done, from the Fortune 500 to one-person ecommerce shops. We are looking for a Customer Marketing Manager to find the best of those stories and tell them well. This is a 0->1 role with executive exposure from day one. You'll set the strategy for and build our customer storytelling and advocacy program, working closely with Sales, Customer Success, Product Marketing, Comms, Brand, and Legal. You'll run the interviews and write most of the stories yourself. If you have a reporter's instinct for a good story and like building the playbook rather than following one, this role is for you.

RESPONSIBILITIES: Find the stories (partner with Sales, spot customers getting real results); tell them well (interview customers from global brand executives to solo founders); cover the full range of customers (enterprise, startups, small businesses, solo operators); make every story travel (case studies, films, quotes, social posts, event talks, launches, press, keynotes); build an advocacy program (recruit and look after customer advocates); build the operating system (own the ops behind the program, intake, prioritization, production, approvals); measure what they influence.

QUALIFICATIONS: 7+ years in customer marketing, customer advocacy, content, journalism, or product marketing, ideally at a high-growth B2B or AI technology company. Strong command of written and spoken language. Track record building and leading customer story and advocacy programs, including high-profile stories. Equally credible with Fortune 500 executives and founders with no marketing team. Comfortable with technical products. Works across teams without formal authority, thrives in ambiguity, operates with urgency.

NICE TO HAVE: You've led marketing at a startup or small business. You use AI tools in your own work, and you use Perplexity regularly.
