→ Ship an MVP in weeks, not quarters
→ Get your first users, pilots or revenue
→ Build toward a pre-seed raise

→ Strong command of AI coding and LLM tooling. This is a must.
→ Real curiosity about a specific industry or problem
→ Something you've built: a project, a tool, a side hustle, or an internship where you owned the outcome
→ Comfort with ambiguity and a bias toward action
→ Willingness to relocate to Bangalore full-time

Bonus: AI/ML experience, product thinking, or domain depth in one of our tracks.

→ Full residency at StudioK HackHouse: housing, meals and tools
