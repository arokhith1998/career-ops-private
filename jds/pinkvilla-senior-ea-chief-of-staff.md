## Full JD text

About the Role
We are looking for a highly organised, proactive, and business-oriented Senior Executive Assistant/Chief of Staff to provide end-to-end support to the CEO and CXO leadership team.

This role goes beyond traditional executive assistance and requires a professional who can manage executive schedules and travel, coordinate leadership priorities, prepare strategic presentations, drive cross-functional projects, analyse business performance, and support business planning and operational excellence.

The ideal candidate will act as a trusted partner to the CXO team, ensuring seamless executive operations while contributing to strategic initiatives, business decisions, and organisational growth.

**Key Responsibilities**

*1. Executive & CXO Support*
Manage and optimise the CEO/CXO calendar, including meetings, appointments, leadership reviews, and business commitments. Coordinate internal and external meetings, prepare agendas, meeting briefs, and minutes of meetings. Track key decisions, action items, and follow-ups to ensure timely execution. Act as a central point of coordination between CXOs, department heads, internal teams, and external stakeholders. Handle confidential information, sensitive communications, and executive-level matters with utmost discretion.

*2. Travel & Logistics Management*
Manage end-to-end domestic and international travel arrangements for CXOs, including flights, hotels, ground transportation, and itineraries. Coordinate visa applications, travel documentation, and travel-related requirements. Plan and manage executive travel schedules, ensuring seamless coordination and time efficiency. Handle last-minute travel changes, cancellations, and logistical challenges. Manage travel expenses, bills, and reimbursements.

*3. Strategic Planning & Business Support*
Work closely with the CEO/CXOs on strategic priorities, business planning, and organisational initiatives. Support the development of business plans, strategic presentations, and management reports. Conduct research, competitor analysis, and business performance assessments. Translate leadership discussions and business objectives into actionable plans. Assist in tracking strategic goals, organisational priorities, and business outcomes.

*4. Presentation & MIS Management*
Create high-quality PowerPoint presentations, business reviews, management reports, and leadership decks. Consolidate business information and data from various departments for CXO-level presentations. Prepare MIS reports, dashboards, and performance trackers. Convert complex business data into meaningful insights and actionable recommendations. Ensure accuracy, quality, and timely delivery of executive reports and presentations.

*5. Project & Program Management*
Coordinate and drive strategic projects and cross-functional business initiatives. Monitor project timelines, milestones, deliverables, and dependencies. Work with different department heads to ensure smooth project execution. Track project progress and highlight delays, challenges, and improvement opportunities. Ensure accountability and timely closure of leadership-driven initiatives.

*6. Business Analytics & Planning*
Analyse business performance, operational metrics, and key performance indicators (KPIs). Support budgeting, forecasting, resource planning, and business performance reviews. Identify business trends, performance gaps, and opportunities for growth. Prepare analytical reports to support data-driven decision-making. Track business objectives against actual performance and highlight deviations.

*7. Operations & Process Improvement*
Identify opportunities to streamline business processes and improve operational efficiency. Develop and implement SOPs, workflows, and process improvement initiatives. Coordinate with teams to improve productivity, communication, and operational effectiveness. Support the implementation and monitoring of new business processes. Drive operational excellence through structured planning and follow-through.

**Skills & Qualifications**
- 7-10 years of experience as a Senior Executive Assistant, Executive Assistant to CEO/CXO, Chief of Staff support, or in business operations.
- Proven experience managing senior leadership calendars and end-to-end domestic and international travel.
- Strong expertise in Microsoft PowerPoint, Excel, and business reporting.
- Experience in preparing CXO-level presentations, MIS reports, and business dashboards.
- Understanding of business strategy, project management, business planning, and operations.
- Strong analytical, organisational, and problem-solving abilities.
- Excellent verbal and written communication skills.
- Ability to coordinate with senior stakeholders and manage multiple priorities.
- High level of ownership, confidentiality, attention to detail, and execution skills.
