## Full JD Text (verbatim, via Greenhouse boards-api)
SeatGeek believes live events are powerful experiences that unite humans. With our technological savvy and fan-first attitude we're simplifying and modernizing the ticketing industry.

As a Product Marketing Lead, reporting to the Head of Product Marketing, you will be responsible for core positioning, messaging and go-to-market of key products, articulating their unique benefits to clients and their fans. You will partner cross-functionally with product, marketing, comms and more to bring products to market in uniquely SeatGeek ways, and help ensure future products have a strong market fit. You will also partner with sales and customer success to drive understanding of SeatGeek's differentiated value.

What you'll do
- Craft differentiated, unified positioning, messaging, RTBs and go-to-market plans for our product solutions, with a prioritized focus on our enterprise audiences and their fans
- Develop a comprehensive understanding of product offerings for both clients and fans, a deep understanding of our client base and their core needs, our differentiators v. key competitors, and the biggest opportunities for us to grow
- Create programs and content that help our clients and their fans get the most value out of SeatGeek products
- Contribute to core sales and marketing assets such as pitch decks, case studies, one-pagers, and more
- Maintain strong relationships with XFN partners including Product, Marketing, Comms, Sales, Client Success, Analytics, and Finance
- Leverage competitive and customer insights to drive pricing and packaging for new and existing products

What you have
- 7+ years of product marketing experience, ideally with experience working in unique industries with complex business models, major competition, or interesting regulatory environments
- Proven track record of taking technical, high-profile products and features to market for growth companies with business and consumer audiences
- Ability to articulate the essence of complex products and the value they create. Can develop a strong messaging hierarchy and effective content strategy plan.
- Willingness and know-how to quickly, and deeply, understand the market (live event technology), our customers (teams, leagues, artists and fans) and competition
- Excellent written and verbal communication skills. Must be comfortable presenting and sharing work with company and leadership
- Proactive self-starter. Solutions over problems, north-star over specific roadmap, and balancing analytical skills with gut decisions

- A WFH stipend to support your home office setup

- Health, vision, dental, and life insurance
- Up to $25k towards family building, reproductive health services and Gender-affirming care
- $500 per year for wellness expenses
- Subscriptions to Headspace (meditation), Headspace Care (therapy), and One Medical
- $360 per quarter to spend on tickets to live events
- Annual subscription to Spotify, Apple Music, or Amazon music
