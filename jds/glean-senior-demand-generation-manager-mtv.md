## Description
Glean seeks a demand generation manager to develop and execute marketing campaigns driving business growth. This cross-functional role reports to the Head of Demand Generation & Integrated Campaigns and involves close collaboration with sales and marketing teams. The position focuses on end-to-end program execution for lead generation and opportunity creation within target market segments, combining wide-reaching campaigns with account-based marketing strategies.
