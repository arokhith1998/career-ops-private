Flashpoint is the pioneering leader in threat data and intelligence. We empower commercial enterprises and government agencies to decisively confront complex security challenges, reduce risk, and improve operational resilience amid fast-evolving threats. Through the Flashpoint Ignite platform, we deliver unparalleled depth, breadth and speed of data from highly relevant sources, enriched by human insights. Our solutions span cyber threat intelligence, vulnerability intelligence, geopolitical risk, physical security, fraud and brand protection. The result: our customers safeguard critical assets, avoid financial loss, and protect lives. Discover more at flashpoint.ioAre you an experienced product marketer who thrives at the intersection of positioning, storytelling, and go-to-market strategy? Flashpoint is looking for a Senior Product Marketing Manager to join our Product Marketing team, remote in the United States. You will sit at the intersection of Product, Sales, Customer Success, and Marketing, working closely with your manager to translate technical capability into a narrative that resonates with CISOs and security practitioners around the world.
