## Description
Glean seeks a demand generation leader to develop and execute marketing campaigns driving business growth. Reporting to the Head of Demand Generation & Integrated Campaigns, this cross-functional role partners with sales and marketing to generate net new leads and influence opportunity creation through both broad campaigns and targeted account-based marketing.

## Key Responsibilities
- Design and execute multi-channel demand generation campaigns including content syndication, paid newsletters, email nurtures, direct mail, referral programs, and demo programs
- Manage webinar programs from conception through promotion and follow-up
- Collaborate with BDRs and sales teams on campaign operationalization and enablement
- Partner with product marketing and creative teams on messaging and landing page optimization
- Identify net new lead generation opportunities by monitoring industry trends
- Leverage analytics to refine strategies and optimize conversion rates and cost-effectiveness
- Execute targeted ABM strategies for high-value accounts
- Manage timelines, budgets, and resources across campaign lifecycle

## Requirements/Qualifications
- 5+ years in fast-paced B2B SaaS environment
- Bachelor's degree in Business, Marketing, or related field
- Proficiency with CRM and marketing automation (Salesforce/Marketo preferred)
- Experience with Sendoso and Apollo preferred
- Proven success partnering with sales teams on lead generation
- Strong communication and creative instincts
- Ability to thrive in dynamic, changing environments
