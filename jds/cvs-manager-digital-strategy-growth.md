## Job Description
Execute Store Brands digital initiatives across eCommerce and marketplace channels. Translate strategy into day-to-day operations, ensuring quality digital content, strong search performance, and accurate reporting while partnering cross-functionally.

## Responsibilities
- Manage content updates, audits, and accuracy checks across platforms
- Ensure compliance with quality and accuracy standards before launch
- Execute digital initiatives across search, merchandising, and promotions
- Execute workflows using Syndigo, STIBO, AEM, Palantir, and IMDASH systems
- Monitor reporting and escalate systemic issues
- Support automation implementation and process documentation
- Prepare performance reports on digital sales, search visibility, and content quality
- Identify trends and opportunities through data analysis
- Support test-and-learn initiatives and compile results

## Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in Marketing, Digital Marketing, Communications
- Hands-on PIM/DAM/CMS platform experience (Syndigo, STIBO, AEM)
- Multi-workstream management with strong attention to detail
- Performance reporting and dashboard experience with large datasets
- Executive presentation skills (PowerPoint/Slides)
- Cross-functional collaboration and independent operation abilities

## Preferred
- Digital marketing or eCommerce initiative experience
- Digital content best practices knowledge
- SEO and optimization fundamentals understanding
- Test-and-learn program exposure
