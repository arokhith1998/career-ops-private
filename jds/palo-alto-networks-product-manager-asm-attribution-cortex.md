Job Summary
The mission of the Product Manager is to own a high-visibility piece of the Xpanse roadmap related to our attribution capabilities. This role is highly collaborative, working hands-on with Xpanse engineering teams, attack surface analytics, sales and customer success, and Cortex teams based in Israel.
Key Responsibilities
Build and maintain a roadmap for attribution that is aligned with our overall product strategy.
Work closely with engineering and design teams to prioritize work and ensure the team is building optimal solutions.
Gather market requirements, collect feedback from users, prospects, and Sales/Post-Sales, and prioritize Engineering resources accordingly.
Drive the feature and product release process, including researching and collaborating with other teams to prepare GTM strategies.
