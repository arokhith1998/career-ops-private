## Job Description (full, verbatim)
Company Description
TransferX is a growing, technology-driven company focused on simplifying and improving how people and businesses move money across borders. The organization is committed to building secure, transparent, and user-friendly financial solutions that help customers manage international transactions with confidence. As a remote-first workplace, TransferX values collaboration, ownership, and problem-solving across distributed teams.

Role Description
As a Founders Office Associate at TransferX, you will work closely with the founding team on strategic and operational initiatives across the company. This full-time remote role involves supporting planning, execution, and follow-up for key projects, including market research, internal process improvements, and special cross-functional initiatives. You will prepare presentations, dashboards, and reports for leadership, manage information flow, and ensure that decisions are documented and acted upon. On a day-to-day basis, you may coordinate meetings, synthesize data and insights, track project timelines, and collaborate with teams such as product, operations, finance, and marketing.

Qualifications
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to interpret data, structure information, and derive actionable insights.
- Excellent written and verbal communication skills, including the ability to create clear presentations, concise reports, and well-organized documentation.
- Project coordination experience, with capability to prioritise tasks, manage timelines, and follow through on multiple initiatives simultaneously.
- Comfort working in a remote, fast-paced environment, with high levels of ownership, accountability, and adaptability.
- Proficiency with productivity and collaboration tools (e.g., Google Workspace or Microsoft 365, project management tools, and communication platforms).
- Demonstrated interest in fintech, financial services, startups, or technology-driven businesses is highly beneficial.
- Bachelor's degree in Business, Economics, Engineering, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience.
- Prior experience in consulting, strategy, founder's office, business operations, or an early-stage startup is a plus.
