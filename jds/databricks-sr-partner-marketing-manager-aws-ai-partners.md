## Description (summarized; full text exceeds quoting limits)

Turns cloud/AI technology partnerships (especially AWS) into measurable pipeline, demand,
and customer adoption: partner strategy, joint messaging, campaigns, events, field
execution. Responsibilities:

  stories/joint announcements per year

cloud, or SaaS companies; experience running joint programs with AWS or comparable
partners with measurable pipeline/revenue impact; experience managing MDF/co-op budgets;
ability to translate data/AI/ML/analytics capabilities into customer-facing messaging;
cross-functional leadership; experience activating audiences via conferences/webinars/
executive briefings.
