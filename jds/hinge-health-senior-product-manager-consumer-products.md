**About The Role**
Hinge Health is looking for an experienced, mission-driven Senior Product Manager to join our consumer product team. In this role, you will lead the strategic vision and execution of a high-impact consumer product surface, focusing on creating deeply personalized and engaging experiences for our members.

As a Senior PM, you will be a leader within the product organization, working closely with cross-functional partners in engineering, design, and clinical teams to deliver innovative solutions at scale. This role requires a "product crafter" mindset - someone who can balance experimentation with long-term strategic thinking.

**What Makes You a Great Fit**
- Product Intuition: You notice subtle things about products and people, anticipating non-obvious user problems and identifying opportunities for delight.
- Strategic Ownership: You operate with a CEO-like sense of ownership, taking complete accountability for your product area's performance and outcomes.
- Ambiguity Navigator: You thrive in entrepreneurial environments, developing high-quality hypotheses and moving work forward even when success is not yet well-defined.
- Collaborative Leader: You serve as an advocate for your engineering and design counterparts, providing clear direction and context to drive productive collaboration.
- Metrics & Experimentation: You have a strong aptitude for A/B experimentation and a track record of optimizing key product metrics, using data to drive informed decisions and measurable outcomes.

**What You'll Accomplish**
- Define Product Vision: Take a major role in defining the product vision, strategy, and objectives for your product area.
- Drive Engagement: Own the roadmap for consumer-facing features that drive user engagement, retention, and clinical outcomes.
- Ship at Scale: Ship high-quality features within a cross-functional pod, working directly with executives as the voice of your team.
- Metric Accountability: Establish success metrics and use both quantitative and qualitative data to adjust strategy mid-flight.

**Basic Qualifications**
- Experience: 5-7+ years of product management experience, specifically in consumer-facing features and personalized user experiences.
- Data Rigor: Demonstrated ability to make data-driven decisions and optimize lifecycle metrics (engagement, retention); experience in single hypothesis-driven testing and experimental design.
- Communication: Excellent written and verbal skills, with a proven ability to influence senior leaders and align cross-functional partners.

**Preferred Qualifications**
- Healthcare experience: Experience in digital product development within the healthcare, fitness, or mind-body therapy space

**About Hinge Health**
At Hinge Health, we're using technology to scale and automate the delivery of healthcare - starting with musculoskeletal (MSK) conditions, which affect over 1.7 billion people worldwide. With an AI-powered human-centered care model, Hinge Health leverages cutting-edge technology to improve outcomes, experiences and costs to help people move beyond their pain. The platform addresses a broad spectrum of MSK care - from acute injury, to chronic pain, to post-surgical rehabilitation - through personalized, evidence-based care.

As the preferred partner to 50+ health plans, PBMs and other ecosystem partners, Hinge Health is available to over 20 million people across more than 2,550 employers. The company is headquartered in San Francisco with additional offices in Montreal and Bangalore. Learn more at http://www.hingehealth.com
