This is a Fully Remote Job

About Our Client:

This organization operates in the omni-channel furniture retail industry, addressing the challenges of pricing strategy and execution across multiple sales channels. It focuses on maintaining competitive pricing structures, promotional strategies, and markdown governance to ensure accurate pricing at every customer touchpoint. The company is experiencing fast growth and emphasizes delivering value through disciplined pricing management.

About the Opportunity:

The PRICING LEAD role is responsible for owning and executing the enterprise-wide pricing strategy. This position drives price architecture, promotional strategies, and markdown governance, ensuring these decisions are accurately and timely activated across all sales channels and customer interactions. The role leads a team of analysts and collaborates with various departments to maintain pricing integrity and improve margin outcomes.

Responsibilities:

- Develop and maintain price architecture including price point structures and competitive gap targets
- Lead the annual and in-season pricing calendar, aligning pricing actions with market and inventory conditions
- Define and govern promotional strategy including offer design and approval of event economics
- Set markdown policies balancing inventory sell-through and margin recovery
- Direct competitive intelligence efforts to inform pricing decisions
- Ensure precise and timely activation of price and promotional changes across systems and channels
- Manage workflows and dependencies in the pricing activation calendar
- Maintain pricing controls, approvals, and conduct audits to prevent errors
- Collaborate with Marketing and E-Commerce to align messaging and merchandising with approved pricing
- Optimize pricing change processes to reduce time to market without losing control
- Lead and develop pricing analysts, building analytical capabilities within the team
- Oversee pricing analytics including elasticity, promotional impact, and markdown effectiveness
- Present pricing recommendations and impacts to leadership forums
- Implement tools and process improvements to enhance pricing decision speed and quality

Requirements:

- Bachelor's degree in Business, Finance, Economics, Analytics, Mathematics, or related field
- 6 to 9 years experience in pricing, merchandising, merchandise planning, or finance with direct pricing responsibility
- Experience managing or leading analysts or teams
- Proven track record in multi-channel retail pricing or promotional execution
- Strong understanding of retail margin mechanics, promotions, and markdown strategies
- Advanced Excel and SQL or similar data tools proficiency
- Ability to influence senior stakeholders and maintain defensible commercial positions
- Strong organizational skills managing complex calendars and cross-functional dependencies
