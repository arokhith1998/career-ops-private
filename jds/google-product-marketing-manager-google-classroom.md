## About the Job

As a Product Marketing Manager for Google Classroom, drive the global product marketing strategy, positioning, and cross-functional go-to-market execution for Google's flagship learning platform. Google Classroom has evolved from a foundational workflow tool into an advanced, premium learning platform that uses generative AI.

## Responsibilities

- Lead creation of global positioning, value propositions, and core messaging frameworks.
- Own end-to-end GTM strategies for new features including AI capabilities, managing activations across channels, Edu marketing, keynotes, sales enablement, product demos.
- Partner with Product Management and UX to incorporate educator/student/admin insights into early-stage product definitions.
- Collaborate with PR, Communications, and Legal on trust-centered messaging around sensitive topics (student data privacy, pedagogical efficacy, administrative AI controls).
- Direct external creative agencies and production partners.
