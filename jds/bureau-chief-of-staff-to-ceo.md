About The Role
As Chief of Staff to the CEO, you'll be the strategic and operational force multiplier who ensures our boldest priorities don't just get set, they get executed. You'll own the operating system: quarterly planning, strategic initiatives (international expansion, org design, GTM strategy), and internal alignment. You'll work across every function -- Sales, Product, Engineering, Finance -- to turn ambiguity into structure and strategy into results. This isn't a note-taking role. You'll drive outcomes, unblock execution, and build the infrastructure that powers a global company.

Build the Operating System

- Design operating cadences that keep teams aligned and moving fast
- Run executive offsites, board prep, and company-wide forums
- Accelerate decision-making across functions

Lead Strategic Projects
- International expansion (market entry, GTM execution, partnerships)
- Organizational design (team restructuring, performance management, leadership transitions)
- GTM acceleration (sales playbooks, channel strategy, revenue operations)
- Product launches and fundraising prep

Drive Clarity and Alignment
- Write strategic memos, decision docs, and company updates
- Prep materials for board meetings, all-hands, and investor updates
- Ensure everyone knows what we're building, why it matters, and what's expected

Enable the CEO
- Help prioritize what gets done now vs. later
- Prepare decision briefs, frame tradeoffs, drive follow-through
- Anticipate bottlenecks and unblock execution

Operational Oversight
- Monitor KPIs across Sales, Product, Marketing, and Finance
- Identify bottlenecks and scaling gaps
- Build dashboards and drive data-informed decisions
