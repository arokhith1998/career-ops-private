## Role framing

As the Generative AI Product Manager for Shopping & Content Search, lead the development and integration of AI-powered capabilities that transform how customers, dealers, and internal users discover parts, products, content, and solutions across Caterpillar's digital ecosystem.

Focus: intelligent, contextual, and personalized search and shopping experiences including part identification, content retrieval, AI-assisted discovery, and conversational commerce across platforms like AI Assistant, cat.com, catdealer.com, and parts shopping experiences.

Work closely with engineering, search relevance, content, commerce, and UX teams to design, test, and scale GenAI-driven shopping and search solutions that improve findability, reduce friction, increase conversion, and elevate end-to-end customer experience.
