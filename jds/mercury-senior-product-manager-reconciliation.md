## Full Job Description (as extracted)

The Reconciliation team develops products, processes, and controls that protect the accuracy and integrity of Mercury's core financial systems and money movement.

Leads the evolution of reconciliation at Mercury, including key financial controls enabling operational excellence, strong governance, and end-to-end auditability. Partners with Product, Engineering, Finance, Operations, and Accounting to automate complex workflows, strengthen control coverage, and ensure issues are detected, investigated, and resolved quickly.

**Responsibilities:**
- Own the vision, strategy, and roadmap for Mercury's reconciliation platform, including work required to support Mercury's bank charter
- Partner deeply with engineers to understand how financial data moves through Mercury's systems
- Work closely with other Product teams, Accounting, Finance, and Reconciliation Operations to define priorities and deliverables
- Evolve Mercury's reconciliation platform to handle long term scale
- Build tools that help internal teams identify, understand, and resolve discrepancies

**Required Qualifications:**
- 7+ years of product management experience working on technical platforms, infrastructure, data products, or similarly complex systems
- Strong systems thinking, bias toward reusable primitives and durable platform solutions
- Track record independently driving ambiguous work from problem definition through delivery
- Exceptional cross-functional partnership skills

**Preferred:** Experience with platform products involving audits, financial controls, or regulatory requirements; systems related to payments, ledgers, accounting, financial operations, or data infrastructure.
