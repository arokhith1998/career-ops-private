This role owns the vision, strategy, and roadmap for the home page, working closely with design, engineering, marketing, and analytics to deliver a best-in-class shopping experience across desktop, mobile, and App. The Product Manager - Ecommerce Home Page delivers business and fan impact through BOLD leadership and execution excellence, leveraging data, automation, and AI-enabled insights.
