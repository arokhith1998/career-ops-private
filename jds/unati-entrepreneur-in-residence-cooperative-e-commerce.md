## Full Job Description

UAMMCL is hiring an Entrepreneur in Residence to build and lead its cooperative e-commerce business from zero. You will launch it across seven Indian states and run it as your own venture, with the backing of one of the country's top-rated cooperatives.

This is a founder's job without the founder's cold start. The products, the cooperative infrastructure and the mandate already exist. What is missing is the person who turns them into a business that customers return to and that women across rural and urban India earn a living from.

About UAMMCL and the business

UAMMCL is a multi-state cooperative rated among the top cooperatives in India. Its new e-commerce business will bring products made by cooperatives to households, using infrastructure the cooperative movement already owns.

The business has two purposes that carry equal weight:

•	Livelihoods. Create steady, dignified income for a large number of women in rural and urban areas, as producers, sellers and community-level partners.

•	A market for cooperative products. Give cooperative producers a reliable, branded route to customers, with fair terms and fast, dependable delivery.

It is a commercial business and it must stand on its own numbers. It is also owned by its members, so growth that does not reach the women in the network does not count as success.

More about the organisation: https://unatibharat.coop/

What you will do

You own the business end to end: strategy, launch, operations and the P&L.

•	Build the business plan. Define the customer, the catalogue, pricing, unit economics and the launch sequence across the seven states.

•	Launch and iterate. Take the first markets live, learn from real orders, and fix what does not work before scaling.

•	Set up supply. Onboard cooperative suppliers, agree quality standards and terms, and build a catalogue customers trust.

•	Run fulfilment. Design warehousing, inventory, replenishment and last-mile delivery with operational partners, to quick-commerce standards of speed and reliability.

•	Grow the customer base. Build customer acquisition and retention through the women-led community network, alongside digital channels.

•	Shape the technology. Work with technology partners on the ordering, catalogue, payments and transaction systems the business runs on.

•	Build the team. Hire, train and lead the core team in Chandigarh and the operating teams in each state.

•	Track impact. Measure and report women's earnings and supplier outcomes with the same rigour as revenue and margins.

•	Work with leadership. Report to UAMMCL's leadership on progress, risks and what the business needs next.

Who we are looking for

We want someone who has seen e-commerce or quick commerce from the inside and now wants to lead a business of their own. We care more about what you have built than about years on a CV.

You are likely a fit if you:

•	Have worked in a quick-commerce or e-commerce brand, in operations, category, growth, supply chain or a general management role

•	Have built something from scratch: a new city, a new category, a new business line or your own venture

•	Are comfortable with numbers and can own a P&L, unit economics and a budget

•	Can work across very different worlds in one day: cooperative members, suppliers, technology partners and customers

•	Want your work to create livelihoods, and see that as a reason to run a tighter business, not a looser one

•	Are ready to be based in Chandigarh and to travel regularly across the launch states

It helps if you also have:

•	Experience with FMCG, food, agri or rural supply chains

•	Experience with cooperatives, producer organisations, self-help groups or social enterprises

•	Working Hindi; Punjabi or other languages of the launch states are an advantage
