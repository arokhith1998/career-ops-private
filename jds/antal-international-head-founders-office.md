## Full JD Text

Head - Founder's Office
Location: Gurugram, with domestic and occasional international travel
Experience: 4-7 years
Qualification: MBA from a leading business school preferred

Role Overview: We are seeking a high-potential professional to work directly with the promoters of a diversified business and investment portfolio. The role combines strategic analysis, financial evaluation, transaction execution and cross-business coordination, offering direct exposure to key business decisions and investments.

Key Responsibilities
- Portfolio MIS & Analysis: Consolidate promoter-level reporting on business performance, investments, liquidity, capital commitments, risks and key decisions.
- Investment & Transaction Support: Evaluate new investments, acquisitions, partnerships and expansion opportunities through financial modelling, scenario analysis and due diligence coordination.
- Strategic Execution: Track business priorities, strategic projects, management deliverables and partner commitments across the portfolio.
- Promoter Support: Prepare decision briefs, meeting materials and investment notes; maintain action trackers and ensure timely follow-through.
- Governance & Coordination: Coordinate with business leaders, finance teams, legal, tax and other professional advisers on transactions, ownership structures and governance matters.
- Portfolio Monitoring: Track investment performance against expectations and flag emerging risks, deviations and additional capital requirements.

Candidate Profile
- 4-7 years of experience in investment banking, private equity, strategy consulting, corporate development, CEO's Office or a sophisticated family office.
- Strong financial modelling, analytical, commercial and problem-solving skills.
- Excellent communication, stakeholder management and project execution capabilities.
- High levels of integrity, discretion, initiative and intellectual curiosity.
- Comfortable working independently in a fast-paced, evolving environment, balancing strategic thinking with hands-on execution.

Opportunity
A high-visibility role offering direct promoter exposure, involvement in significant business and investment decisions, and the opportunity to grow into senior Founder's Office or investment leadership responsibilities.

Note: The role carries significant coordination and analytical responsibility. Capital approvals and authority to act on behalf of business entities will remain subject to specific delegation.
