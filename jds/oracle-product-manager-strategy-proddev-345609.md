## Job Description (verbatim, via Oracle Recruiting Cloud recruitingCEJobRequisitionDetails API, ExternalDescriptionStr field)
Help define how enterprises build AI workloads on their most valuable data and operate them at industrial scale.
You will own the strategy and execution for a major area of Oracle Database AI platform. You will work directly with developers and enterprise customers to identify the problems that prevent AI applications from reaching production, then turn those needs into products customers can adopt and operate at scale.
You will shape how developers connect agents to enterprise data, use models and tools, evaluate behavior, diagnose failures, and apply security and governance controls. You will translate these workflows into APIs, SDKs, visual tools, and operational experiences.
You will work closely with engineering, design, applied AI, and go-to-market teams. You will make product and technical decisions involving developer experience, architecture, quality, latency, reliability, security, scalability, and cost. As a senior individual contributor, you will influence the direction of Oracle Database's AI platform.
