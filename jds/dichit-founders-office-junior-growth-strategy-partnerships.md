Kochi Onsite Full-time
About The Role
The Founder’s Office – Junior: Growth, Strategy & Partnerships role is designed for an ambitious early-career professional who wants direct exposure to startup strategy, partnerships, customer acquisition and business execution. You will work closely with the Founder & CEO and collaborate with the product, technology, creative, marketing, customer-success and operations teams. This is a hands-on execution role. You will help convert ideas, discussions and partnership opportunities into structured plans, coordinated actions and measurable outcomes.
Key Responsibilities
Growth and Strategy
 Conduct market, customer, and competitor research.
 Support the preparation of business plans, growth experiments, and go-to-market initiatives.
 Collect and analyse data related to leads, customer onboarding, activation, and campaign performance.
 Maintain business dashboards, trackers, and weekly performance reports.
 Identify operational gaps and suggest practical improvements.
 Assist in preparing management updates, reports, and strategic documents.
Partnerships
 Research and identify potential B2B partners and business opportunities.
 Prepare partnership presentations, proposals, meeting notes, and follow-up documents.
 Coordinate meetings, demonstrations, and partner communications.
 Maintain the partnership pipeline and ensure timely follow-ups.
 Track documents, approvals, integrations, and pending action items during partner onboarding.
 Coordinate with internal teams to support successful partner launches.
Project Execution
 Convert founder-level decisions into clear tasks, owners, and timelines.
 Follow up with internal and external stakeholders to close pending actions.
 Coordinate cross-functional projects involving marketing, product, technology, and operations.
 Maintain project trackers and highlight delays or execution risks.
 Support customer-acquisition campaigns, branch activations, and launch events.
 Document processes, decisions, and standard operating procedures.
Candidate Profile
 Graduate in business, management, marketing, finance, economics, engineering, or a related discipline.
 Fresh graduates and candidates with up to two years of relevant experience may apply.
 Strong interest in startups, fintech, financial services, growth, or partnerships.
 Good research, analytical, and problem-solving ability.
 Strong written and verbal communication skills.
 Comfortable working with spreadsheets, presentations, and business reports.
 Able to manage multiple tasks and follow through without repeated supervision.
 Comfortable working in a fast-moving and evolving startup environment.
 Fluency in English is required; Malayalam communication skills will be an advantage.
