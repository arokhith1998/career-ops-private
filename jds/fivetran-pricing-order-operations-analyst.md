## Job Description (extracted, near-verbatim)

Fivetran and dbt Labs are bringing together two industry-leading companies with a shared mission: helping organizations unlock the full value of their data. Fivetran helps organizations automate data movement across the systems, clouds, engines, and tools they rely on. dbt Labs pioneered analytics engineering, helping teams transform data into reliable, governed insights.

We're looking for a Pricing & Order Operations Analyst to join our Order Operations & Monetization team. This role restores dedicated capacity for pricing and order operations testing, quote-to-cash (QTC) release validation, and order execution support. You'll help ensure system changes are tested, validated, and operationally ready before they go live, providing Pricing and Order Operations subject-matter expertise across signature packets, bookings, marketplace offers, and booking-to-order management handoffs. Partners closely with Order Operations & Monetization, IT's business systems team, Pricing Operations, Sales, and Deal Desk. Reports to the Order Operations & Monetization lead.

This is a full-time remote position.

**Technologies:** Salesforce CPQ, Jira, marketplace UIs (AWS, Azure, GCP, Snowflake), Notion, Metronome, Hex.

**What You'll Do:**
- Pricing Operations & QTC Support: validate pricing-related QTC changes, confirm pricing changes are operationally ready before launch, maintain pricing/order system documentation.
- UAT & Testing: participate as SME in UAT, execute assigned test cases, maintain the regression test suite for recurring QTC changes.
- Order Operations: validate execution across signature packet generation/e-signature routing, booking rules, marketplace offer configuration, and booking-to-order-management handoff.

**Skills We're Looking For:**
- "2-3 years of experience in pricing operations, order operations, business systems analysis, or quality assurance within an agile environment, ideally involving quote-to-cash processes."
- Working knowledge of Salesforce CPQ and order management or billing systems.
- Comfort using conversational AI tools (Claude, ChatGPT, internal chatbots).

**Bonus Skills:** Exposure to Revenue Recognition or Professional Services systems.

**Perks:** 100% employer-paid medical insurance, generous PTO, RSU stock grants, monthly cell phone stipend, mental health support platform.
