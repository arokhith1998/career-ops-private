## Full job description

As a Sr. GTM Strategy & Operations Manager, you'll design and execute business-level initiatives to accelerate global revenue growth and optimize GTM processes across the entire sales cycle. This highly-visible, transformational individual contributor role reports to the Sr. Director, GTM Strategy & Programs.

**Key responsibilities:** Construct overarching GTM vision and strategic roadmap aligned with global growth targets; identify and eliminate operational friction within sales and GTM functions; incorporate AI, automation, and advanced analytics into GTM processes; partner cross-functionally with Sales, Marketing, Post-Sales, Product, Operations, HR, Legal, and Finance; analyze quantitative and qualitative data to generate actionable insights; provide C-suite and GTM leadership with deep insights on business performance drivers and investment priorities; build executive-level presentations supported by data-driven analysis; drive accountability and operational strategy execution.

**Required qualifications:** 12+ years progressive experience in tech strategy/operations, management consulting, private equity, or fast-paced growth environments; 4+ years with SaaS companies; 4+ years designing and scaling GTM Strategy, Sales Strategy, and/or GTM operating models; experience operating in complex matrixed environments; project and cross-functional initiative management experience; Bachelor's degree or equivalent.

**Preferred:** MBA, enterprise SaaS experience, deep SaaS business model knowledge, full sales lifecycle knowledge, strong analytical track record.

**Benefits:** PTO, paid parental leave (up to 6 months), health plans, retirement programs, learning/development, compassionate care leave.
