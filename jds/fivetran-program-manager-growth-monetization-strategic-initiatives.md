## Full JD text

From Fivetran's founding until now, our mission has remained the same: to make access to data as simple and reliable as electricity. With Fivetran, customer data arrives in their warehouses, canonical and ready to query, with no engineering or maintenance required.

### About Us
Fivetran and dbt Labs are bringing together two industry-leading companies with a shared mission: helping organizations unlock the full value of their data. Together, we're delivering the data infrastructure layer that helps organizations move, transform, and trust their data. Fivetran helps organizations automate data movement across the systems, clouds, engines, and tools they rely on. dbt Labs pioneered analytics engineering, helping teams transform data into reliable, governed insights. During this transition period, you may see references to both Fivetran and dbt Labs throughout our recruiting process as we integrate our teams, systems, and career sites.

### About the Role
Fivetran and dbt Labs are building data pipelines to power the modern data stack for thousands of companies.

We're looking for a Program Manager to serve as the operational backbone of our pricing and monetization strategy in addition to supporting strategic initiatives, as needed. Sitting within Product Program Management and partnering closely with Analytics, Product, Sales, Product Marketing, RevOps, Finance, and Engineering, you'll own the cross-functional execution that turns pricing strategy into well-governed, shipped programs. You'll coordinate launches end to end, track commitments, resolve escalations, and expand the playbook that makes growth at Fivetran repeatable and scalable. This is a high-visibility role for someone who thrives at the intersection of program management, GTM, and product strategy.

This is a full-time, hybrid position based out of our Oakland, CA office. Our hybrid work model offers a blend of remote flexibility and in-person collaboration, including two days in the office each week to connect and build as a team.

**Technologies You'll Use:** Google Workspace (sheets)/Excel, Jira, Looker/Sigma/Hex, Notion, Salesforce and Slack.

### What You'll Do
- Drive program governance, including running various meetings and decision-making cadences.
- Lead Growth & Monetization planning in close partnership with Sales & Engineering leadership.
- Track program deliverables and commitments end to end.
- Coordinate engineering timelines, dependencies, and launch readiness with the ePMO.
- Partner with Product Marketing and RevOps on enablement plans and rollout execution.
- Manage sales escalations and the cross-functional coordination required to resolve them.
- Partner with Finance and Analytics on analyses and ad hoc business metric requests.
- Report on outcomes, such as closed-lost-due-to-pricing and overall pricing impact.
- Expand upon and further operationalize Fivetran's pricing and program management playbooks.

### Skills We're Looking For
- Proven experience in program management or product operations, ideally supporting pricing, monetization, or GTM initiative in B2B SaaS and with consumption based pricing.
- A track record of driving cross-functional execution across Product, Sales, Marketing, and Engineering.
- Excellent stakeholder management and executive communication.
- Strong analytical skills, with the ability to interpret pricing and revenue data.
- Experience establishing operating rhythms, governance processes, and repeatable playbooks.
- Familiarity with GTM and RevOps tooling and processes (e.g., Salesforce, CPQ, and BI tools such as Looker).
- Highly organized and self-directed, able to manage multiple concurrent programs in a fast-paced environment.
