Job Summary

 Define and drive the IAM product vision, strategy, and multi-quarter roadmap, including RBAC, fine-grained authorization, identity federation (SAML/OIDC/SCIM), and MFA/SSO.

 Own the cross-platform access control model across SCM, ensuring consistent policy enforcement for operators, customers, and service-to-service interactions in a multi-tenant environment.

 Partner with downstream product teams (NGFW, Prisma, Cortex) to define IAM integration requirements and ensure platform capabilities meet their go-to-market needs.

 Engage directly with enterprise customers, field teams, and security architects to validate requirements and translate feedback into prioritized product decisions.

 Drive alignment with compliance and regulatory requirements (SOC 2, FedRAMP, GDPR) as they relate to identity, access control, and audit logging.

 Collaborate with Product Marketing to build messaging, technical documentation, and competitive positioning for IAM capabilities.

 Define and track success metrics: adoption, time-to-provision, MTTR for access incidents—and use data to drive continuous improvement.

 Contribute to platform-level decisions on API security, service authorization, and developer-facing identity primitives (tokens, scopes, API keys).

Qualifications

Required Qualifications

 Education & Experience: BS/MS in Computer Science, Information Security, or a related technical field, or equivalent practical experience.

 Product Management Track Record: 5+ years of product management experience, with a demonstrated history of shipping enterprise SaaS or cloud platform products.

 Identity & Access Management Expertise: Deep understanding of IAM concepts and protocols, including RBAC, ABAC, SAML 2.0, OIDC/OAuth 2.0, SCIM, MFA, SSO, and directory services.

 Platform Thinking: Experience building shared platform capabilities consumed by multiple products or teams, with the ability to balance diverse stakeholder needs against a cohesive platform architecture.

 Enterprise Security Context: Familiarity with zero trust security architecture principles, enterprise compliance requirements (FedRAMP, SOC 2, ISO 27001), and how IAM intersects with audit, governance, and risk

management.

Preferred Qualifications

 Multi-Tenancy Experience: Hands-on experience defining access control models for multi-tenant SaaS platforms, including operator/reseller/customer hierarchy patterns.

 Developer Platform Exposure: Experience defining IAM capabilities for developer-facing APIs, scopes, service accounts, API key management, and machine-to-machine authentication.

 Cross-Functional Leadership: Proven ability to align engineering, security architecture, legal, and GTM teams around a shared identity strategy without direct authority.

 Executive Communication: Strong written and verbal communication skills, with experience presenting technical identity tradeoffs to platform architects, and leadership.

 Data-Driven Ownership: Track record of defining success metrics for platform capabilities and using data to drive roadmap decisions and stakeholder confidence.
