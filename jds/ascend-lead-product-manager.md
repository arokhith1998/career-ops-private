## Full JD text

What We Do

Insurance is a $10T industry still running on PDFs, email threads, and manual reconciliation. Ascend is changing that. We built the first and only complete financial operations platform purpose-built for insurance — combining accounting automation, payments, and premium financing in a single vertically integrated solution. In five years, we've become the market leader: over 4,000 businesses, including more than half of the 50 largest brokers in the country, trust Ascend to bring clarity to complex accounting operations from collection to close.

How We Do It

Ascend automates the financial operations that insurers, MGAs, and agencies run every day and embeds directly into the transactions where money actually moves. Point solutions are easy to build and even easier to copy. The companies that win won't just sell tools — they'll capture value where the money actually moves. Ascend is built for exactly that moment. Our customers don't just adopt software, they unlock measurable efficiency with a proven 5-7x return on investment.

Our team is onsite in San Francisco. We bring deep expertise in insurance, fintech, and financial infrastructure to build what the industry has never had.

Why We Do It

The insurance industry intermediates trillions of dollars — and almost none of that infrastructure was built for the modern era. Legacy systems aren't just inefficient; they're a ceiling on what the industry can become.

Ascend's mission is to become the leading financial automation platform for insurance by 2030 — powering the financial operations of the entire industry and transforming the way trillions of dollars move. We're not building another point solution, we're building the financial backbone of insurance.

Your Role

Ascend is looking for a Lead Product Manager to define the vision and strategy for our core money movement products. You'll start by partnering with an existing product manager and grow into owning this product area overall.

These products span integrations, invoicing, payments, receivables, financing, payouts, funds flow, and the full payment lifecycle. They're closely connected. You'll own vision, strategy, and execution for a defined set of products and also align priorities and sequencing across all of them. Enterprise and strategic customers rely on these products to run increasingly complex financial operations. A big part of the job is using AI to automate those operations. You'll measure where automation is working, find where it isn't, and keep cutting the manual work it takes for customers to close their books.

This role mixes strategy with hands-on technical product work. You'll work directly with customers, write product and technical specs, and work closely with engineering to define how the system behaves and to make tradeoffs. Many of these workflows have never been automated before, so you'll often need to define the problem before you can solve it. You'll be accountable for measurable gains in adoption, operational efficiency, automation, and revenue.

Responsibilities Will Include

Objective #1: In your first 30 days:

Learn Ascend's platform, how customers work today, and how money moves through our system

Talk with customers about their financial operations: where they get stuck, where they still work by hand, and what they'll need as they grow

Work with Engineering to understand the APIs, data models, system behavior, and dependencies between products

Review the AI and automation already in place: what's working, where exceptions and manual touches still happen, and what drives them

Present an initial assessment of the biggest opportunities, backed by customer feedback and data

Objective #2: In your first 60 days:

Define a product vision and strategy based on customer needs and Ascend's business goals

Set a baseline for operational efficiency, such as manual touches, exception rates, and turnaround time, and set targets for reducing it

Turn the strategy into a quarterly roadmap with clear priorities, tradeoffs, and expected outcomes

Write product and technical specs for the top initiatives, covering workflows, system requirements, edge cases, and acceptance criteria

Align product, engineering, and leadership on priorities, sequencing, and how success will be measured

Objective #3: In your first 90 days and beyond:

Own and evolve the product vision, and take on more of the product area over time

Build AI into the core of product workflows to automate manual work and exception handling, with the goal of reducing the time and effort customers spend on their financial operations

Track how well automation performs, find where it falls short, and keep improving it

Build capabilities and tooling that scale with enterprise complexity and growth

Lead initiatives from discovery and spec through launch and adoption, and be accountable for the results

Work with Engineering on API requirements, data flows, failure scenarios, and system tradeoffs

Test ideas with prototypes and early releases, and make clear calls on scope and sequencing

Work across Design, Engineering, GTM, and Operations to simplify financial workflows and anticipate how changes affect other teams and products

Must Have

6+ years of product management experience, including 3+ years owning the vision, strategy, and outcomes for a major product area

A track record of translating product strategy into successful launches and measurable customer and business results

Experience building for enterprise customers with complex workflows and integration requirements

Hands-on experience writing product and technical specifications, with clear requirements, edge cases, and acceptance criteria

Technical and analytical fluency to work with engineers on APIs, data models, and interconnected systems, and use data to guide decisions

Clear communication and the ability to articulate a compelling vision, make tradeoffs, and align cross-functional teams

Nice to Have

Experience in insurance, premium finance, lending, or loan management systems

Experience building public APIs, platform integrations, or financial infrastructure at scale

Experience building AI products or applying AI to financial workflows
