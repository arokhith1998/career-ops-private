About This Role:

We are seeking a strategic and experienced Actuary to support our pricing strategy across our portfolio of third-party P&C programs. This role will support program price monitoring, planning, and reinsurance placement, helping to inform underwriting decisions, strengthen cross-functional alignment, and deliver insights that directly impact growth and risk management. The ideal candidate combines technical excellence with insurance aptitude and the ability to influence stakeholders across the organization.

About You:

You're an ACAS-credentialed pricing actuary who has worked across several P&C lines and is comfortable building from the ground up. You make sound pricing calls on thin experience data, using industry benchmarks, judgment and a clear statement of your assumptions. You build frameworks, monitoring tools and governance that make pricing consistent across a growing portfolio, and you keep them simple enough to maintain. You explain actuarial conclusions in plain terms to executives, underwriters and program partners, and you work closely with Underwriting, Claims, Finance and Reinsurance. You use SQL, AI tools and code to work faster, and you help develop the people around you.

What You'll Do:

- Support actuarial pricing for program portfolios across multiple business lines including Property and Casualty lines
- Build the foundations to support accelerating program business growth
- Leverage Casualty expertise to design, refine, and maintain price monitoring frameworks that support sound underwriting decisions and sustainable profitability
- For property lines, collaborate on enterprise views of CAT, capital, and reinsurance costs
- Work closely with Underwriting, Claims, Finance, and Reinsurance teams to provide actuarial insights in program evaluation, treaty structuring, and risk assessment
- Implement governance protocols for pricing reviews, model usage, and program onboarding to promote consistency, transparency, and control
- Assist in defining data model requirements that enable team members to effectively and efficiently manage program performance

Must Haves:

- Bachelor's degree in Actuarial Science, Mathematics, Statistics, Economics, or a related field
- ACAS with robust pricing experience
- 4 - 7 years of actuarial experience with broad exposure to pricing across multiple lines;
- Experience operating with limited experience data
- Demonstrated success in applying tools, frameworks, and best practices that drive pricing excellence
- Ability to clearly communicate actuarial concepts to executive stakeholders, underwriting teams, and external partners
- Actively supported team collaboration and development
- Proficiency with Excel, SQL, AI and possibly programming languages (e.g., R, Python)

Nice to Haves:

- Casualty pricing experience is preferred
- Prior program experience
