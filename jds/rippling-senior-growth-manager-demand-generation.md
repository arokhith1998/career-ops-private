About Rippling

Rippling gives businesses one place to run HR, IT, and Finance. It brings together all of the workforce systems that are normally scattered across a company, like payroll, expenses, benefits, and computers. For the first time ever, you can manage and automate every part of the employee lifecycle in a single system.

Take onboarding, for example. With Rippling, you can hire a new employee anywhere in the world and set up their payroll, corporate card, computer, benefits, and even third-party apps like Slack and Microsoft 365—all within 90 seconds.

Based in San Francisco, CA, Rippling has raised $1.4B+ from the world’s top investors—including Kleiner Perkins, Founders Fund, Sequoia, Greenoaks, and Bedrock—and was named one of America's best startup employers by Forbes.

We prioritize candidate safety. Please be aware that all official communication will only be sent from @Rippling.com addresses.

About The Role

Rippling has scaled well beyond $1B, which means expanding our engine upmarket and rapidly scaling to more channels. That’s where you come in.

As the Senior Demand Generation Manager on the Growth Marketing team, you’ll be responsible for driving pipeline throughout the customer journey with lead capture and nurture initiatives. You’ll conceptualize, execute, and optimize marketing campaigns across channels (lead capture hacks, email, ads, webinars, direct mail, content syndication, events etc.) that educate prospects and deepen their engagement with Rippling.

This is a highly cross-functional role. You’ll work closely with growth, product marketing, brand, content, sales, growth engineering and product to build, test, and scale programs that impact pipeline and revenue.

Rippling’s marketing engine has always been a growth lever - Kleiner Perkins partner Mamoon Hamid calls Rippling “the 1% of the 1%” - and keeping the gas pedal on our demand gen & growth motion is central to unlocking our next phase of scale.

What You Will Do

- Own & execute demand generation campaigns from concept to analysis, including copywriting, audience targeting, content creation, sales enablement, and performance tracking.
- Write email nurture drips, run lead capture campaigns and manage paid advertising (LI, Meta, Reddit, Content Syndication, etc) to educate prospects around products and thematic topics.
- Partner with product, growth, and data teams to identify lead firmographic and behavioral triggers, map journeys, and build personalized nurture streams that increase activation and retention.
- Collaborate cross-functionally with other teams, including sales, growth, marketing operations, analytics, product marketing, content marketing and brand marketing to execute your campaigns.
- Analyze performance across touchpoints and identify areas to optimize. Shift resources to what’s working, test new ideas quickly, and scale what moves the needle.
- Serve as the connective tissue across marketing, growth-engineering, and sales — ensuring messaging, timing, and experience are aligned across the prospect lifecycle.

What You Will Need

- 6+ years of experience in lifecycle, growth, or demand marketing roles at high-growth B2B SaaS companies.
- Proven track record of using demand generation / growth marketing to drive lead nurture, acquisition, and conversion.
- Customer obsession that you translate into punchy, specific copy and out-of-the-box lead gen campaigns.
- Deep expertise in marketing automation, journey building, A/B testing, and customer segmentation.
- Clear, concise communicator with the ability to work across teams and influence stakeholders at all levels. Previous close collaboration with sales teams is imperative.
- Analytical mindset: you can parse complex data, spot opportunities, and act on them quickly. Data-driven decision-making—experience analyzing datasets in Excel or SQL to draw insights and measure impact.
- Strong project management abilities, balancing multiple initiatives while collaborating across teams like growth, sales, marketing ops, and brand.
- Comfortable operating in a fast-paced environment: you're energized by experimentation and rapid iteration. You are biased to action and consistently ask “What’s our crawl-walk-run?” to get results.
