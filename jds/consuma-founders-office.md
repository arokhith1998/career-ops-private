## Full JD Text

Founder's Office
Bengaluru - In-person - Full-time

Why this role exists
Consuma is a high-growth startup and we're scaling fast. We run live research engagements across multiple geographies for some of India's biggest consumer brands - Coca-Cola, Britannia, Foxtale - and partner with agencies like WPP. Everything we do touches brands people know, so the work you do here shows up in real decisions.
As we grow, more is happening across the company every week, and we need someone that can take point on critical, strategic initiatives and trust to close them.

Why it's exciting
This is a seat where you can make a visible impact, early, on a company that's moving quickly. You'll work close to the founder on problems with real stakes, and own them end to end.
We're hiring a Founder's Office member to own operations across the business: picking up what's undefined, driving it to done, and making sure nothing falls through the cracks.

What you'll actually do
- Own cross-functional priorities for the founders - client work, hiring, finance and reporting, events, ops, admin.
- Turn undefined problems into clear plans, trackers, and finished output, without waiting to be told how.
- Run follow-ups with internal teams, vendors, and partners and keep work unblocked.
- Build simple processes and systems where none exist, and improve the ones that do.
- Manage multiple priorities at once and take full ownership of outcomes.
- Be a trusted thought partner to the founders, flagging risks and making recommendations, not just executing.

Who we think this is for
You have 3+ years of experience in a founder's office, chief of staff, strategy, operations, or a similar generalist role, ideally at a startup. We care more about what you've owned and delivered than where you studied.
You probably:
- Are high-energy and self-motivated, and follow through on what you start.
- Have owned projects end to end with real business impact.
- Learn quickly and adapt easily.
- Are comfortable with ambiguity and can figure things out independently.
- Are organized, detail-oriented, and sound in your judgement.
- Communicate clearly with senior stakeholders, clients, and external partners.

This role is probably not the right fit if:
- You prefer a clearly defined task list to stay productive.
- You'd rather be told what to do than take initiative.
- You're looking for a narrow, single-function role.

What you get
- Real responsibility from week one.
- Hands-on exposure to how a startup operates and scales.
- Ownership over projects and outcomes, not just routine tasks.

About Consuma
We're an AI-powered consumer research platform helping brands go from brief to report in minutes, making research faster, simpler, and more human. We've raised $1.3M, led by Equirus, with backing from Abhishek Goyal (Tracxn), Varun Mayya (Aeos Labs), Biswa Kalyan Rath, and other operators from India's tech ecosystem.
We're an in-person team in Bengaluru. We value clarity, craft, and initiative, and the kind of teammate who treats Consuma like their own company.
