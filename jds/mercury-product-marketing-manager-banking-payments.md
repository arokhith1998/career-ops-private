## Full JD Text

Mercury is a fintech company building products that help ambitious businesses manage their money, including banking, payments, international money movement, and agentic payments. This role sits across Product, Marketing, and Revenue. The Product Marketing Manager will own go-to-market strategy and launches for banking and payments products, including agentic payment features.

Responsibilities:
- Lead launches, align cross-functional teams, and prepare customer-facing partners.
- Research founders' and finance teams' needs and turn insights into positioning and messaging.
- Use AI to build workflows and automations for research, launch execution, and competitive intelligence.
- Drive product adoption with Product, Lifecycle, Activation, and Revenue teams.
- Track the competitive landscape, including fintechs and traditional financial institutions, and build battlecards and training for Revenue teams.
- Work with Brand and Creative on campaign briefs and channel strategies.
- Measure launch impact against business goals and manage multiple projects at once.

Requirements:
- 2-3 years of B2B product marketing experience, including owning launches and GTM initiatives.
- Experience bringing products or features to market and explaining their business impact.
- Ability to build a complete GTM plan, from customer insight through post-launch measurement.
- Hands-on, advanced use of AI in day-to-day work.
- Strong customer curiosity and the ability to write clear, compelling messaging.
- Comfort using data and experimentation to evaluate marketing performance.
- Fintech, banking, or payments experience is a plus but not required.
