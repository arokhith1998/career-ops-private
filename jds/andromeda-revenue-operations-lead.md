## Description
Andromeda is a market and infrastructure platform to buy, sell, and operate compute (GPU capacity marketplace: hyperscalers, neoclouds, independent data centers). First Revenue Operations Lead - strategic partner to Sales, Customer Success, and leadership as the company scales a GPU compute market. Owns the operating system behind the revenue number: pipeline hygiene, forecasting, deal desk process, supply administration, connective tissue between sales, supply, legal, finance. "There's no playbook to inherit here."

## What You'll Do
- GTM tech stack: own/optimize HubSpot (current CRM) - field standardization, data hygiene, integrations.
- Forecasting & reporting infrastructure: build/maintain dashboards and forecast rollups for weekly deal reviews.
- Deal desk & process design: routing logic between strategic and velocity deals, own the RACI, close sequencing gaps in contracting.
- Planning & compensation: partner with sales leadership/finance on capacity planning, quota design, comp modeling.
- AI-driven efficiency: identify where AI tools can cut manual work from reporting/forecasting/deal ops.
- Cross-functional glue across sales, supply/ops, legal, finance.

## What We're Looking For
- 5+ years in Revenue Operations, Sales Operations, or Business Operations at a high-growth B2B company, ideally usage-based/capacity-based/infrastructure pricing (compute, cloud, hosting).
- Hands-on CRM ownership at scale (built field architecture, workflows, hygiene process).
- Systems thinking with a long-term view; builder not maintainer; high agency/ambiguity tolerance.
- Strong candidates may have: hyperscaler/GPU cloud/high-performance compute domain experience; scaling experience (~$200M to $700M+ run-rate); SQL/scripting fluency.
