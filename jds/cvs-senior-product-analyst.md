## Job Description
Analytics & Behavior Change division. Transform clinical and claims data into actionable insights using AI technology. Bridge strategy and execution within agile teams, supporting data scientists and engineers across healthcare initiatives.

## Responsibilities
- Define and document product requirements, user stories, and acceptance criteria
- Maintain and refine product backlogs in partnership with leadership and operations teams
- Monitor feature KPIs aligned with program objectives
- Translate strategic directives into functional specifications and workflows
- Ensure requirements remain clear, feasible, and testable for technical teams
- Collaborate with clinicians to validate clinical AI model results
- Facilitate sprint planning, backlog refinement, and retrospectives
- Develop go-live timelines and coordinate stakeholder alignment
- Lead problem-solving efforts balancing speed, scalability, accuracy, and quality
- Analyze performance metrics to identify optimization opportunities
- Coordinate dependencies across product teams proactively

## Requirements
- 1-2+ years professional experience
- Background in product management, product analytics, data analysis, or related fields
- Experience with digital products, analytics platforms, or data products
- Demonstrated stakeholder management and consensus-building abilities
- Proficiency with data metrics and reporting
- Strong Excel and data visualization skills
- Organizational capability to manage multiple concurrent workstreams

## Preferred
- Healthcare data experience (claims, EHR, clinical datasets)
- SQL proficiency and large dataset handling experience
- Familiarity with visualization tools (Tableau, Looker, Power BI)
- Executive communication and data storytelling abilities
