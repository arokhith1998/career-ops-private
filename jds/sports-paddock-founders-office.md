Company Description

Sports Paddock is a sports-tech company on a mission to build a more structured, connected, and commercially sustainable sports ecosystem in India.

Since 2014, we have worked at the intersection of sports, technology, and business, connecting grassroots athletes, amateur and semi-professional players, sports federations, institutions, and global brands.

Our core verticals include The Amateur League (TAL), Corporate Sports & Engagement, Elite Sports Management, and Sports Technology Solutions, offering end-to-end services spanning league and tournament management, strategic consulting, technology infrastructure, data analytics, and brand partnerships.

At the heart of our vision is Sports Paddock's integrated digital platform — a one-stop sports ecosystem designed to connect stakeholders, streamline operations, unlock commercial opportunities, and drive sustainable growth across Indian sports.

We aren't just organising sports. We're building the infrastructure to power its future.

Role Description

The Founder's Office is a role that's simple in its objective but ambitious in its scope: work directly with the founders to shape, execute, and accelerate the company's growth strategy.

This is a cross-functional, high-ownership role that offers exposure to virtually every aspect of building and scaling a business — from strategy, business development, and partnerships to operations, technology, and organisational growth.

This is not a conventional desk job. It is an opportunity to work at the centre of an evolving sports-tech business, contribute to critical decisions, and play a meaningful role in shaping the future of Sports Paddock.

Who should apply?

We're looking for individuals who bring an entrepreneurial mindset, relentless curiosity, a strong sense of ownership, and a genuine passion for sports.

The ideal candidate is someone who thrives in a fast-paced environment, is comfortable wearing multiple hats, and is equally willing to think strategically and get their hands dirty executing.

You should be someone who sees challenges as opportunities, takes initiative without waiting for instructions, and is motivated by the prospect of building something with lasting impact.

Above all, we're looking for people who believe in the potential of Indian sports and are willing to fully immerse themselves in helping unlock it.

If you're looking for more than just a job — an opportunity to build, learn, lead, and make a tangible impact on the sports ecosystem — we'd love to hear from you.
