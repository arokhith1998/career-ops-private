## Full JD Text

About The Opportunity

-  Dodo Payments is a global Merchant of Record platform for SaaS, AI, and digital businesses, handling payments, local taxes, compliance, fraud prevention, and invoicing so founders can sell anywhere without operational overhead.
-  The company serves 25,000+ businesses across 220+ countries and territories, supports 30+ local payment methods including Apple Pay, Klarna, Affirm, CashApp, and UPI, and is backed by Antler, 9Unicorns, and leading angels with $1.5M raised in seed funding.
-  The goal is to power 50,000+ businesses over the next two years. The team is small and high-ownership, where every hire shapes the product and the company.

Role & Responsibilities

-  Take CEO priorities and convert them into clear execution plans with owners, milestones, deadlines, and success metrics.
-  Run the cadence: conduct weekly reviews with function heads, maintain a tracker of every CEO initiative, and flag early when something slips.
-  Drive cross-functional projects end to end, especially those that sit between teams and have no natural owner.
-  Close the loop: confirm each initiative is complete, measure results against original goals, and report back to the CEO.
-  Map how key workflows run today, especially across compliance, finance, and risk, and identify bottlenecks.
-  Redesign and document processes as simple SOPs, checklists, and RACI charts that teams actually follow.
-  Track before-and-after metrics such as turnaround time, error rate, and hours saved.
-  Own meeting hygiene: agendas, decision logs, and action items, followed up until closed.
-  Handle special projects as they come: new market or payment-method launch, pricing change, partner rollout, audit or licence readiness, fundraise data room.

Skills & Qualifications

Must-Have

-  3–5 years in operations, program/project management, business excellence, consulting, or a Founder's Office / Chief of Staff team, ideally at a startup or fast-growing company.
-  Track record of taking messy, cross-functional projects from idea to done; bring two or three concrete examples with results.
-  Hands-on process improvement experience: mapped a workflow, cut steps out of it, and made the change stick.
-  Clear writer and communicator; able to turn a 40-minute CEO brain-dump into a one-page plan.
-  Able to influence without authority, including holding senior people accountable politely and firmly.
-  High ownership, discretion with confidential information, and comfort with ambiguity and shifting priorities.
-  Comfortable working alongside compliance, legal, and finance teams in a regulated business.
-  Graduate in any discipline; an MBA is a plus, not a requirement.

Preferred

-  Exposure to Lean or Six Sigma (Green Belt or above).
-  Prior work directly with a founder or CXO.
-  Familiarity with basic P&L, unit economics and MIS reporting, or exposure to payments, fintech or SaaS.

Benefits & Culture Highlights

-  Direct, daily exposure to the CEO and how the company is run.
-  Visibility across every function, making this a strong launchpad into leadership roles in ops, strategy or a business unit.
-  Real ownership from week one, with room to shape how the company works.
-  Health and accidental insurance cover.
