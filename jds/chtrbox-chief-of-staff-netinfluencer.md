About Us

Chtrbox is India's leading influencer marketing and creator-led growth company, partnering with brands to build meaningful consumer connections through authentic, high-impact storytelling. Blending creative excellence with cutting-edge technology and data intelligence, we deliver award-winning campaigns powered by one of the country's strongest networks of influencers, content creators, and digital talent. With a rapidly expanding footprint including the Middle East, we are redefining how modern brands engage audiences - shaping the future of digital advertising through creator-powered innovation at scale.

Role Brief

We're hiring a Chief of Staff to work directly with the Founder & CEO at Chtrbox. This is a high-impact role focused on driving strategic priorities, solving critical gaps, and accelerating growth. You'll act as an extension of the founder-combining strategy with hands-on execution.

This role reports to the MD and CEO, Mr. Raj Mishra (Ex Country Head, TikTok)

Roles and Responsibilities

- Partner closely with the Founder on key decisions, priorities, and strategy
- Drive high-impact, cross-functional projects focused on growth and expansion
- Work with internal and external stakeholders to drive alignment and execution
- Identify organizational and execution gaps, and step in to solve them
- Bring structure to ambiguous problems and ensure strong follow through
- Lead special projects and 0->1 initiatives from concept to execution.
- Define KPIs, OKRs, and performance frameworks

What We're Looking For

- 5-7 years in consulting, startups, or strategy roles
- Strong problem solver with a bias for action
- Able to operate in ambiguity and drive outcomes independently
- Excellent communicator with strong stakeholder management skills
- High ownership, discretion, and execution focus

What Makes You Stand Out

- You think like an owner and act without waiting for direction
- You can simplify complex problems and drive clear outcome
- You're equally comfortable in strategy discussions and execution trenches
- You have a strong business instinct and sound judgment under pressure
- You build trust quickly with founders, teams, and external partners
