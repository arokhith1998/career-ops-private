Role summary: Exotel is hiring a Chief of Staff to work directly with the CTO. The posting says this is not a coordination role. It calls for a builder who has started something, runs it, and can still build things personally. The person is expected to ship work, not only organize it, and to take on whatever matters most to the CTO and engineering organization at the time.

Responsibilities:
- Take on problems the CTO would otherwise own and drive them to an outcome.
- Run the CTO's operating rhythm, including staff meetings, quarterly reviews, and OKR planning.
- Build dashboards and engineering metrics, such as DORA, for visibility into delivery and reliability.
- Build working tools, prototypes, and analyses directly instead of handing them off.
- Lead cross-team engineering initiatives from start to finish with engineering and product leaders.
- Serve as a thinking partner to the CTO on technical strategy, priorities, and decisions.
- Turn technical proposals into clear decision options and one-page briefs.
- Shape the technical narrative for board, investor, and customer materials.
- Work with Finance on the engineering budget, infrastructure, and cloud spend.
- Coordinate communication during major incidents or critical infrastructure issues.
- Help evaluate vendors and tools.

Qualifications:
- Has founded or built something and run it personally. A failed company counts, and the posting says the candidate's lessons from it are valued.
- More technical than a typical Chief of Staff, able to build things and not only coordinate them.
- Builds systems and processes that others adopt and trust.
- Comfortable reading system architecture, technical documentation, and engineering roadmaps.
- Able to explain complex systems end to end to non-technical audiences.
- Works well with ambiguity and proposes a first solution rather than waiting for direction.

About the company (paraphrase): Exotel describes itself as one of Asia's leading customer communication platforms, combining CPaaS, CCaaS, and Voice AI. It states that its platform supports billions of customer interactions and that it operates across India, APAC, the Middle East, and Africa.
