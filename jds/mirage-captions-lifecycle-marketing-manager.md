## Role Summary
Lifecycle Marketing Manager owns end-to-end customer journey across Captions products. Hands-on building, launching, and optimizing campaigns across email, push, in-app, SMS. "In the tools" role focused on activation, conversion, retention, winback with AI tooling integrated throughout.

## Key Responsibilities
- Build, launch, manage lifecycle campaigns: email + push + in-app + SMS
- Execute onboarding, activation, conversion, retention, winback programs
- Own campaign setup + QA in **Iterable**: journeys, segmentation, personalization
- Monitor campaign performance; continuously optimize on data

- Maintain campaign documentation + workflows
- Use AI tools to accelerate production, copywriting, testing
