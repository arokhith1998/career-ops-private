## Description
We're not here to blend in -- we're here to redefine what observability means and how it's delivered. groundcover has built a radically different approach to cloud-native observability that competitors can't match. As a part of our marketing team, you won't just market a product, you'll help reshape the narrative of the category so the world understands why groundcover is the clear, modern choice for engineering and operations teams.

**About groundcover:** groundcover is a fast-growing Series C startup delivering the industry's most modern observability platform for cloud-native environments. Unlike traditional tools that are brittle, costly, and complex, our approach is lightweight, instantly deployable, and built for today's AI-driven, cloud-native developer-first world. We're not chasing category leaders -- we're redefining the category. As our Technical Product Marketing Manager, you'll play a critical role in shaping and executing a go-to-market (GTM) strategy that challenges industry norms and outpaces legacy players like Datadog and Dynatrace. You'll help build a modern, agile marketing engine designed for speed, innovation, and impact. This is a hybrid role with 3 days a week from the office in SF.

**What You'll Do:**
- Own the narrative and positioning: develop differentiated positioning and messaging across groundcover's platform, translating technical architecture and product capabilities into clear advantages for developers, SREs, platform teams, engineering leaders, and buyers
- Become a technical product expert: use groundcover yourself, understand how the platform works across Kubernetes, eBPF, OpenTelemetry, logs, metrics, traces, APM, AI workloads, and agentic workflows
- Build the proof: create and deliver technical demos, test product capabilities, work with APIs and integrations, and build the technical proof points behind the messaging
- Lead next-generation GTM: own GTM initiatives from positioning through launch, adoption, and field enablement; define the audience, use cases, competitive alternatives, value proposition, proof points, and measures of success
- Launch products as a system: coordinate readiness across Product, Engineering, DevRel, Sales, and Marketing so messaging, demos, content, enablement, and proof points are ready when the product launches; measure impact through adoption, pipeline, deal velocity, and win rates
- Create deeply technical content: technical blogs, demos, diagrams, webinars, videos, solution guides, customer stories, and thought leadership
- Enable Sales and Solutions Engineering: give the field the messaging, talk tracks, competitive intelligence, battlecards, technical proof points, and demo environments they need to win complex observability deals
- Own competitive and market intelligence: maintain a strong point of view on observability, cloud-native infrastructure, OpenTelemetry, AI infrastructure, and emerging agentic development and operations workflows
- Showcase customer proof: work directly with customers and Sales to understand how groundcover is being used in production and turn those outcomes into credible technical and business stories
- Partner deeply across the company: work directly with Product and Engineering to understand what's being built and why

**Requirements:**
- Experience: 5+ years in product marketing, product management, solutions engineering, developer relations, technical consulting, or a similar role working with developer infrastructure, observability, cloud platforms, AI infrastructure, or data platforms
- Technical practitioner: comfortable getting hands-on with technical products; can work in an IDE, interact with APIs, navigate a Kubernetes environment, understand logs, metrics, and traces
- Cloud-native depth: strong understanding of Kubernetes, containers, cloud infrastructure, distributed systems, observability, and modern application architectures; experience with OpenTelemetry, Prometheus, Grafana, eBPF, AWS, GCP, or Azure is highly valuable
- AI-native mindset: practical understanding of how AI is changing software development and operations, including LLM applications, AI agents, MCP, RAG, and agentic workflows
- Technical storytelling, product curiosity, proven positioning skills, content and communication, commercial mindset, independent execution
- Education: Bachelor's degree or equivalent relevant experience
- Ability to come in 3 days a week to the SF office

**Why groundcover:** Define the GTM playbook for one of the most important layers of the cloud-native stack, setting a new industry standard for how observability solutions are brought to market. Join a high-growth team at the forefront of Observability. Enjoy rapid career growth, high visibility, and a collaborative culture.

**Benefits:** Competitive compensation (cash + equity), medical/dental/vision insurance, paid time off including vacation and sick leave, 401(k) matching.
