About the role:
We're looking for a Product Marketing Manager to join a small, high-leverage marketing team as a true generalist. You'll run product launches end-to-end, own positioning and messaging for parts of our product suite, and support the rest of the PMM engine - sales enablement, competitive intelligence, customer proof, website copy, and campaigns. AssemblyAI ships fast: new models, new products, and new capabilities land every quarter, and each one needs a story, a launch, and the enablement to sell it. You'll partner closely with product, research, sales, growth, and brand to make sure every release lands with the right buyers - developers and product teams building voice agents, medical scribes, AI note takers, and customer-call intelligence. This is a hands-on role. You'll write the copy, build the deck, ship the landing page, and run the launch yourself, using modern AI tools to move at the pace the market demands.

What You'll Do:
Product Launches
- Run product and model launches end-to-end using our launch playbook - landing pages, announcement blog, email, social, Product Hunt, in-product modules, and handoffs to paid and influencer partners
- Own the launch plan: timeline, tiering, stakeholder alignment, asset production, and the internal and external communications that go with it
- Align on success metrics with product and marketing leads before each launch, then report honestly on what worked and what didn't
- Build evergreen campaigns that keep shipped products growing between launches - refreshing assets, retargeting stalled signups, and cultivating verticals long after launch day

Positioning & Messaging
- Own positioning and messaging for assigned products across our suite - speech-to-text, streaming, text-to-speech, and new product lines as they ship
- Translate technical capabilities (accuracy, latency, model improvements) into positioning that ties to business outcomes for developers and enterprise buyers
- Develop strategic narratives for our top use cases - voice agents, medical scribes and dictation, AI note takers, and customer calls - including the buyers, competitors, and market dynamics in each
- Keep the website accurate and consistent as the product and positioning evolve - product pages, solution pages, and landing pages - owning narrative and copy while partnering with brand and web on build and publish

Sales Enablement
- Build and maintain the core enablement library: pitch decks, one-pagers, battlecards, FAQs, demo scripts, and objection handling
- Run enablement sessions for sales and CS on new launches, positioning changes, and competitive updates
- Gather feedback from reps on what's landing and what's missing, and close the loop with product and marketing

Competitive Intelligence
- Track competitor moves - model releases, pricing changes, positioning, and campaigns - and keep internal teams informed
- Maintain competitive benchmarks, battlecards, and comparison pages, and turn competitive shifts into messaging and campaign responses
- Partner with research and product on benchmark claims so our comparisons are accurate, defensible, and current

Customer Marketing
- Source and produce customer proof points - case studies, quotes, logos, and video - from identifying the customer to shipping the asset
- Partner with growth and CS on expansion and upsell campaigns
- Feed customer insight back into positioning: what buyers actually say, why they chose us, and where they struggle

Growth & Market Insight
- Collaborate with lifecycle, demand gen, and paid partners on campaigns - email nurtures, onboarding flows, audience building, and targeting based on signup behavior
- Spot emerging use cases and verticals from customer feedback, signup trends, and market signals - and flag where we should lean in next

What You'll Need:
- 3-5 years in B2B product marketing, ideally with experience owning launches and positioning at a developer-focused, API-first, or AI company. We value non-traditional paths (former sales engineers, content or growth backgrounds) as much as classic PMM resumes.
- Proven experience running product launches end-to-end - you've owned the plan, the assets, the cross-functional coordination, and the results
- Ability to translate technical capabilities into positioning that ties to business outcomes for developers and enterprise buyers
- Experience building enablement materials sales actually uses, and a point of view on how to tell whether it's working
- Strong writing: you can produce clear, specific copy for a landing page, a launch email, a battlecard, and an exec deck - and know how each should differ
- A hands-on builder: comfortable shipping website edits, landing pages, and emails yourself with modern AI tools (Claude, Claude Code, Cursor) rather than only briefing others
- Comfort with ambiguity and speed - our campaigns ship in weeks, not quarters, and priorities shift as the market does
- Excellent cross-functional project management and communication skills
- Fluent with AI and LLMs in your daily workflow - you use them to move faster on research, drafting, analysis, and iteration, and have a stance on where they help vs. where they get in the way
- Deeply curious about Voice AI and excited to market infrastructure that helps developers build the next generation of voice-powered products

Nice to Have:
- Experience in AI/ML, speech, conversation intelligence, or voice tech
- Background marketing to developers and product teams
- Experience running competitive intelligence programs or maintaining benchmarks
- Experience with growth or activation metrics - you've owned a number, not just a content calendar
- Familiarity with developer tooling ecosystems and modern GTM strategies
