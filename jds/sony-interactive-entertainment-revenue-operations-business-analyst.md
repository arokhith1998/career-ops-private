An individual-contributor role that turns data into insights, improves operational efficiency, and administers revenue systems across PlayStation Media Solutions. Works with Revenue Operations, Sales, Campaign Management, Finance, and Product.

Responsibilities:
- Analyze revenue and operational data, build dashboards and executive reports, and define reporting requirements.
- Support forecasting and business reviews, and ensure data accuracy across CRM, order management, finance, and reporting systems.
- Manage small to medium operational projects and system enhancements from requirements through implementation.
- Act as a Salesforce administrator (configuration, users, permissions, troubleshooting).
- Handle revenue operations support tickets and coordinate escalations, plus integrations, UAT, documentation, and training.

Required qualifications:
- 4+ years in business analysis, revenue or sales operations, ad operations, or a similar analytical role.
- Salesforce Administrator certification or equivalent hands-on experience.
- SQL proficiency with enterprise data and relational schemas.
- Experience with BI tools such as Domo, Tableau, or Looker.
- Experience with ticketing systems and change management.

Preferred qualifications: advertising, media, gaming, entertainment, or tech industry experience; Snowflake or similar warehouses; ETL or workflow automation; Python or similar scripting; knowledge of advertising sales processes.
