## Full description (verbatim via headless browser render of the LinkedIn guest posting page)
As Sr. Deal Desk Manager, you will lead the development and execution of commercial operations processes that support complex B2B technology and SaaS transactions. You will serve as a strategic partner to Sales and cross-functional leadership, ensuring deals are structured effectively while protecting revenue, profitability, and long-term business value. This role combines commercial strategy, financial analysis, pricing governance, and technology optimization. You will oversee deal approval frameworks, pricing policies, CPQ systems, and commercial reporting, and drive automation/AI-powered initiatives.

Accountabilities (selected)
- Lead the end-to-end Deal Desk operating model (intake, commercial reviews, approvals, contracting, customer handoffs).
- Partner with Sales on complex/non-standard transactions; evaluate deal structures (pricing, discounts, contract duration, payment terms).
- Develop financial/analytical models connecting discounting, deal size, pipeline coverage, win rates to revenue, margins, profitability.
- Establish and maintain commercial approval matrices and pricing policies.
- Administer/optimize CPQ platforms (DealHub or equivalent); maintain Salesforce opportunity/pricebook/quote/contract data governance.
- Leverage AI-powered tools for commercial analysis and operational productivity.

Requirements
- At least 4 years of professional experience in Deal Desk, Commercial Operations, Revenue Operations, Sales Operations, or a related commercial function within a B2B technology or SaaS organization.
- Strong AI proficiency; substantial hands-on experience structuring complex/non-standard commercial transactions.
- Strong commercial judgment, recurring-revenue/unit-economics fluency, advanced analytical/financial modeling skills.
- Strong Salesforce expertise; practical CPQ/Deal Desk platform administration experience (DealHub, Salesforce CPQ, or equivalent).
- Experience within identity verification, fraud prevention, cybersecurity, or financial services technology is an additional advantage (listed under "Additional Advantages").

Benefits: remote flexibility to work from the United States; stated salary range above.
