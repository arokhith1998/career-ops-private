## Full JD text

MongoDB seeks a strategic Senior Product Manager to own the strategy, roadmap, and delivery of Revenue and Order to Cash (OTC) capabilities. This role works with the Group Product Manager for Quote to Cash, partnering across Finance, Revenue Operations, Sales, Engineering, and Program Management to improve the end-to-end order-to-cash lifecycle and help the business scale with reliable revenue systems.

Key Responsibilities: Define Revenue/OTC product vision, strategy, and roadmap. Partner with Group Product Manager on OTC and revenue processes. Build expertise in NetSuite and revenue systems (billing, AR, revenue recognition, close processes). Elicit and document business requirements; translate into epics and user stories. Lead prioritization across Finance, Revenue Operations, and Sales needs. Evaluate and integrate first- and third-party OTC solutions. Define success metrics and KPIs (billing accuracy, DSO, close cycle time). Own end-to-end revenue workflows. Improve revenue accuracy, accelerate invoicing, reduce manual entries, strengthen SOX compliance.

Required Experience: "8+ years of relevant product management experience, with a proven track record of delivering enterprise business systems." Strong OTC, Quote to Cash, or revenue process experience. "Hands-on understanding of NetSuite-based billing and revenue operations." Agile/Scrum delivery team experience. "Strong data analysis and metrics skills." Complex integrations and enterprise revenue platform management. Bachelor's degree or equivalent; advanced degree is a plus.
