## Full Job Description

### About Poshmark
Poshmark is the leading fashion marketplace where style comes alive through discovery, self-expression, and human connection. Powered by a vibrant community of 165 million members.

### About the Role
The Sr. Manager, Product Marketing (Posh Live) sits at the intersection of Product, Growth, Community, and Content, working closely with the Director of Product Marketing and the GM of Posh Live. This role defines how Posh Live shows up to buyers and sellers -- shaping positioning, messaging, and go-to-market strategies that drive adoption, engagement, and growth for Poshmark's live shopping product.

This role starts from the buyer: build a deep, first-hand understanding of why and how buyers discover, watch, and purchase through live shows, then use that to define what it takes for a brand or enterprise seller to succeed on Show.

### Responsibilities

**Buyer Insight as the Foundation for Seller Success**
- Build a data-informed understanding of buyer behavior on Show (discovery, viewing, watch time, purchase drivers, price sensitivity, trust/loyalty).
- Translate buyer insight into a point of view on what makes a show/host/category successful.
- Feed buyer-side learnings back into seller-facing strategy, content, and product requirements.

**Brand and Enterprise Seller Value Proposition and Playbooks**
- Define value proposition for brand/enterprise sellers hosting on Show, anchored in demonstrated buyer demand.
- Build and maintain playbooks for brand/enterprise hosts.
- Partner with Supply/Partnerships and Merchandising on onboarding/enablement materials.
- Establish seller educational programs and trust guidelines for compliance during live broadcasts.

**Product Narrative, Positioning, and Messaging**
- Define value propositions and messaging frameworks for buyers and sellers across the Show experience.
- Develop clear narratives translating live shopping mechanics into user-centric benefits.

**GTM Strategy for Launches and Enhancements**
- Lead GTM strategy for new Show features: positioning/differentiation, segmentation, rollout plans, launch KPIs.
- Partner with Product, Growth, Analytics on launches.

### Requirements
- 5-8 years of experience in product marketing, growth marketing, or lifecycle marketing, ideally at a marketplace, social commerce, or live/video commerce company.
- Demonstrated ability to build consumer/buyer insight and translate it into seller/partner-facing strategy and enablement.
- Experience building playbooks/enablement materials for B2B2C partners, brands, or enterprise accounts is a strong plus.
- Track record of driving adoption and engagement for a product in an early or scaling phase.
- Comfort with data/analytics to define success and measure impact.
- Excellent written/verbal storytelling skills.
- Genuine interest in resale, social commerce, and/or live shopping formats is a strong plus.
