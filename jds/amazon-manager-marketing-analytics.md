## Role
Lead the Marketing Analytics team across Whole Foods Market and Amazon Fresh. Define and execute the analytics strategy that enables data-driven decisions across the growing portfolio of grocery businesses.

## Responsibilities
- Deliver automated reporting, dashboards, and insights for marketing optimization.
- Measure campaign performance across email, push, paid media, organic search, and onsite channels.
- Automate manual reporting processes in partnership with Business Intelligence Engineers.
- Build dashboards in QuickSight, Tableau, or similar.
- Manage team recruitment, development, and performance.
- Present findings to Director and VP leadership.
- Establish measurement frameworks and analytical standards.
- Navigate ambiguity to define problems and strategic priorities.

## Basic Qualifications
- Bachelor's in Engineering, Math, Business, or related quantitative field.
- 5+ years as a business/data analyst or equivalent.
- Direct management experience.

## Preferred Qualifications
- Master's in Business, Economics, Statistics, Engineering, or related field.
- Proficiency with SQL, MS Power Query, or Python.
- Experience in retail, CPG, or grocery.
- Analytics team leadership experience.
- Expertise in marketing measurement: attribution modeling, incrementality testing, segmentation.
- Marketing mix modeling (MMM) or multi-touch attribution (MTA) experience.
