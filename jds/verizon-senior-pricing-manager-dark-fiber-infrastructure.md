What You'll Be Doing...

The Senior Pricing Manager - Dark Fiber & Infrastructure will lead pricing strategy, deal support and commercial analysis for fiber infrastructure offerings with a primary focus on hyperscale customers, neocloud companies and large strategic enterprise opportunities. Operating as a senior individual contributor, this role acts as the lead financial architect for complex bids, custom builds, and hyperscale pursuits. This role is responsible for developing market-competitive, financially sound pricing for long-haul and metro dark fiber solutions, In-Line Amplification (ILA) Service and other AI-driven connectivity solutions.

This position partners closely with Sales, Finance, Network Planning, Product, Legal, and Executive leadership to structure complex opportunities, support customer negotiations, and drive disciplined commercial outcomes. The ideal candidate combines strong financial and analytical skills with an understanding of fiber infrastructure economics and hyperscale buying behaviors.

- Develop pricing strategies and financial models for dark fiber, fiber infrastructure and related network solutions with emphasis on hyperscale and large enterprise opportunities.
- Lead commercial pricing support for complex bids, custom builds, overbuilds and strategic customer pursuits.
- Model and analyze deal economics including revenue, margin, capital requirements, payback period, and NPV.
- Structure and price IRU, lease and other long-term commercial arrangements for metro and long-haul fiber opportunities.
- Partner with Sales and business leadership to create customer-specific pricing recommendations that align market competitiveness with Verizon’s return thresholds.
- Support negotiation strategy by evaluating impacts of pricing and other contractual concessions.
- Build and maintain pricing tools, scenario models, and approval packages for executive review.
- Collaborate with Network Engineering to incorporate route design, build costs and service delivery considerations into commercial models.
- Prepare clear, executive-level summaries of deal economics, risks, assumptions, and recommended pricing positions.
- Partner with Finance, Sales and Product on forecast assumptions, revenue planning and portfolio performance tracking metrics.
- Conduct post-deal analysis to improve pricing discipline, win-rate effectiveness and return performance over time.
