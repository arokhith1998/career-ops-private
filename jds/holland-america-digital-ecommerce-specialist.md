# Digital E-Commerce Specialist — Holland America Line (Carnival Corp)

**URL:** https://jobs.carnivalcorp.com/job/-/-/28021/93766970704
**Saved:** 2026-04-11
**Location:** Seattle, WA (remote, regular travel to Seattle HQ)
**Salary:** $28.46-$38.41/hr (~$59K-$80K)

## Job Description
Analytics professional to drive e-commerce success by analyzing digital data and providing insights to support online sales optimization across Holland America and Seabourn brands.

## Responsibilities
- Analyze web and mobile data using analytics tools to identify trends supporting e-commerce effectiveness
- Create dashboards and visualizations presenting findings to leadership weekly and quarterly
- Collaborate with product and A/B testing teams to identify and prioritize improvements
- Support marketing campaigns with performance measurement and reporting
- Participate in strategic planning and forecasting initiatives

## Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in technical data analysis or quantitative discipline
- 3+ years analytics experience
- 3+ years hands-on B2C e-commerce environment experience
- Expert SQL or comparable programming proficiency
- Advanced proficiency in tools like Adobe Analytics, Google Analytics, PowerBI, Alteryx or similar
- Strong problem-solving, communication, and project management abilities
- Experience with A/B testing and data-driven goal-setting
