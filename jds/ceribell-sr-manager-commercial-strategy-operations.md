**About Ceribell:** Ceribell is a medical technology company focused on transforming the diagnosis and management of patients with serious neurological conditions. The Ceribell System is a novel, point-of-care electroencephalography ("EEG") platform specifically designed to address the unmet needs of patients in the acute care setting, and is being used in hundreds of community hospitals, large academic facilities and major IDN's across the country.

The Sr. Manager of Commercial Strategy & Operations is an individual contributor who is a core strategic, operational, and analytical partner to Ceribell's commercial leadership team. Partnering directly with VPs, Regional Directors, Territory Managers, and Health System leaders across territories, this individual will drive forecasting, territory design, and capacity models, while establishing operational KPIs, dashboards, and strategic performance metrics to guide executive decision-making and accelerate commercial execution.
