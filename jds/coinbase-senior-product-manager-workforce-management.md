## Full JD text (verbatim from page, via headless browser)

Ready to do the most impactful work of your career? Coinbase is building the future of finance for people, institutions and their agents, with one place to trade crypto, equities, derivatives and prediction markets, use stablecoins, and connect AI agents with clear controls. Coinbase is a remote-first, but not remote-only company. Expect to get together quarterly for in-person working sessions.

Shared Platforms builds the technology that CX and Global Financial Crimes Operations run on, and we are hiring a Senior Product Manager to own workforce management. You will own one real-time system for demand and capacity across agentic and human workforces, so the business can optimize for cost and quality as conditions change. You will partner closely with Operations leadership on where to invest and how to land change, and you will carry the roadmap from problem framing through adoption.

What you'll do:
- Own the multi-year workforce management roadmap, with one trusted view of cost, quality, and partner performance
- Turn operational processes into product requirements, acceptance criteria, and measurable outcomes, and build the loops that correct drift in forecasts, schedules, and quality scoring
- Automate partner billing end to end, from governed source data to a monthly close that Finance and Operations can both audit
- Partner with Engineering on architecture, data quality, and reliability, and own the path to launch through phased rollout, monitoring, and named support owners
- Decide where automation and AI earn their place, including evaluation before scale, human review, auditability, and fallback
- Manage third-party vendor relationships across evaluation, statements of work, service levels, renewals, and escalations

Required Skills and Experience:
- 6+ years of product management experience, including ownership of an internal or enterprise platform used by Operations teams
- A track record shipping complex, cross-functional operational products end to end
- Experience making buy-versus-build decisions on systems of real consequence
- Fluency with data: defining baselines, instrumenting adoption, and challenging a forecast or savings estimate that does not hold up
- Experience owning workforce management, quality management, or billing systems in a regulated environment with privacy, audit, and oversight requirements
- Proven influence across Operations, Engineering, Analytics, Compliance, Finance, and Procurement without direct authority
- Utilizes generative AI responsibly, with human-in-the-loop practices
