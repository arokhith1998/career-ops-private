To build this emergent technology, we are looking for innovative, motivated, and experienced leaders to join us and move this field forward. If you like to build, tinker, and create with a team of trusted and passionate colleagues, then Cyngn is the place for you. Key reasons to join Cyngn:

About The Role

- Cyngn is expanding its Product Management organization and is seeking a Senior Product Manager to lead the strategy and execution of Cyngn Insight and Fleet Management, the company’s core software platform for operating, monitoring, and scaling autonomous vehicle fleets in production environments.
- This role owns the end-to-end product lifecycle for Cyngn’s customer-facing autonomy platform—covering fleet operations, telemetry, analytics, safety monitoring, workflow orchestration, and enterprise integrations. Cyngn Insight is central to how customers deploy, operate, and extract value from autonomous vehicles at scale.
- You will be the senior product owner responsible for defining what operational excellence looks like for autonomy in real-world industrial settings—and ensuring the platform delivers it. This role sits at the intersection of autonomy software, cloud infrastructure, and enterprise operations, requiring strong systems thinking, customer empathy, and cross-functional leadership.
- We are looking for a seasoned product leader who can operate at both the strategic and execution layers: setting multi-quarter platform direction while driving day-to-day prioritization and delivery across multiple engineering and stakeholder teams. You will partner closely with Engineering, Program Management, Sales, and Customer Success to scale autonomy from early deployments to large, multi-site fleets.

What You'll Do

- Own the product vision, strategy, and roadmap for Cyngn Insight and Fleet Management, including fleet operations, monitoring, analytics, safety workflows, and enterprise integrations.
- Define and maintain a multi-quarter roadmap that balances customer needs, operational scalability, system reliability, security, and commercial objectives.
- Act as the senior product owner for fleet and operations initiatives, driving clear prioritization, trade-off decisions, and execution across concurrent programs.
- Partner with Engineering and Program Management to translate platform strategy into actionable requirements, milestones, and delivery plans.
- Translate customer workflows, operational data, and deployment feedback into product requirements that improve fleet uptime, efficiency, safety visibility, and ease of use.
- Collaborate with Sales, Business Development, Marketing, and Customer Success on go-to-market strategy, customer onboarding, and long-term account success.
- Define, track, and communicate success metrics (KPIs) for fleet performance and platform adoption, including utilization, uptime, incident response, and customer value realization.
- Engage directly with customers and strategic partners to validate product direction and ensure alignment with real-world operational needs.Serve as a product leader within the organization—modeling clear communication, strong ownership, and a data-driven, collaborative product culture.

Qualifications

- BS/BA in a technical field or equivalent practical experience. 5+ years of product management experience, including at least 2 years in a senior or lead PM role.
- Demonstrated success delivering complex, multi-tenant software platforms or enterprise products operating at scale.
- Strong technical aptitude with experience working closely with engineering teams on distributed systems, data platforms, or cloud-based products.
- Excellent communication and leadership skills, with comfort engaging executives, customers, and deeply technical stakeholders.
- Proven ability to drive alignment and execution across multiple teams in fast-paced, ambiguous environments.Strategic mindset with a track record of translating technical capabilities into clear customer and business value.

Bonus Qualifications

- Experience with fleet management systems, enterprise SaaS platforms, or operational analytics products.
- Background in software development, data platforms, or systems engineering (e.g., Python, SQL, cloud infrastructure).
- Familiarity with autonomous vehicles, robotics operations, industrial automation, or large-scale deployments in logistics, manufacturing, or warehousing.
