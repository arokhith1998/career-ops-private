Job Description

Support the growth of the Contact Center business by partnering with Product, Sales, Marketing, and Enablement to bring Contact Center solutions to market. Shape positioning and messaging, support product launches, develop sales and customer-facing content, and surface market and customer insights to inform product strategy. Temporary role providing coverage for a member of the Product Marketing team.

Responsibilities

- Develop and refine clear, differentiated positioning and messaging for the Contact Center solutions.
- Partner with Product, Sales, Marketing, Enablement, and other teams to develop and execute go-to-market plans for new products, capabilities, and strategic initiatives.
- Support product launches from strategy through execution, including messaging, launch materials, internal communications, and sales readiness.
- Create product marketing content, including pitch decks, solution briefs, web copy, customer presentations, and sales enablement materials.
- Equip Sales and customer-facing teams with messaging, tools, and competitive context to engage customers and prospects.
- Build understanding of Contact Center buyers, customer needs, market trends, and competitive landscape; translate insights into actionable recommendations.
- Partner closely with Product teams to bring market and customer perspectives into product strategy and translate product capabilities into clear customer value.
- Support competitive positioning and differentiation to articulate why customers should choose the company.
- Use qualitative and quantitative insights to evaluate go-to-market effectiveness and identify opportunities to improve.

Qualifications

- 4-7 years of relevant experience, including product marketing, preferably in B2B SaaS or enterprise technology.
- Experience developing positioning and messaging and supporting product launches and go-to-market programs.
- Ability to understand complex or technical products and translate them into clear customer value.
- Excellent written, verbal, and presentation skills.
- Experience creating high-quality customer-facing and sales enablement content.
- Strong cross-functional collaboration experience with Product, Sales, Marketing, and Enablement teams.
- Balance of strategic thinking and hands-on execution; ability to navigate ambiguity and prioritize in a fast-paced environment.
- Bachelor's degree in Marketing, Business, Communications, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience.

Preferred Qualifications

- Experience in Contact Center, CCaaS, customer service, conversational AI, voice, workforce engagement, or adjacent technologies.
- Familiarity with Contact Center and customer experience buyers.
- Experience marketing AI-powered SaaS products or bringing AI capabilities to market.
- Experience supporting enterprise sales motions.
