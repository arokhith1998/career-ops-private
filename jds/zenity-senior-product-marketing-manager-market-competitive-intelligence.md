About The Role: Most product marketing roles start with the product and work outward. This one starts with the market and works inward. You'll own the evidence behind our go-to-market decisions: who we sell to, who we compete with, how big the opportunity is, and why we win or lose. Your work feeds positioning, launches, sales plays, and campaign targeting. AI agent security is a young category that changes quickly.

About Zenity: Zenity is the leader in AI Agent Security and the first company to bring an agent-centric security platform to market. We deliver full-lifecycle visibility, governance, detection, prevention, and response for AI agents from build time to runtime, across SaaS, home-grown platforms, and end-user devices. Backed by $180M+ in total funding, including a $125M Series C led by Norwest, with participation from SoftBank Vision Fund 2 and Microsoft's M12.

What You'll Do:
- Market Intelligence: build and maintain the market model (sizing, segmentation, trends); track category movement; work with analyst relations.
- Competitive: run the competitive program end to end (monitoring, analysis, battlecards, rapid response); give sellers honest guidance; partner with Revenue Enablement.
- ICP and buyer insight: define and maintain ICP and buying committee personas; run the win/loss program.
- Turning insight into decisions: bring market and competitive evidence into positioning, messaging, pricing and packaging; publish a regular market and competitive readout for leadership; build repeatable systems including AI-assisted workflows.

What You've Done (Requirements):
- 5+ years in product marketing, competitive intelligence, market research, or strategy at a B2B software company.
- Experience in cybersecurity, or the technical depth to quickly become credible with security practitioners and CISOs.
- Comfort with both quantitative work (CRM and pipeline analysis, market sizing) and qualitative work (buyer interviews, call analysis).
- A record of competitive or market work that changed a decision.
- Clear, concise writing. Good judgment about evidence.

Nice to Have: Background in AI security, application security, identity, or cloud security; experience running a formal win/loss program; experience building workflows with AI tools.

Interview Process: Recruiter Screen, Hiring Manager Interview, Peer Interview, People Team Interview, VP Interview.
