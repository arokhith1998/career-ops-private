## Full JD text

Description: Amazon Ads is dedicated to driving measurable outcomes for brand advertisers, agencies, authors, publishers, and entrepreneurs. Ad solutions include retail media, display, video, DSP, measurement, and ad tech.

Seeking a strategic, customer-obsessed Product Marketing Manager for the Ads Product Marketing team -- bridge between product development and go-to-market execution, translating complex advertising solutions into narratives that drive product adoption at scale. Partner with Product Management, Go-to-Market, and cross-functional stakeholders; own the end-to-end marketing lifecycle from informing the roadmap through voice-of-customer research to positioning/messaging.

Key job responsibilities:
- Collaborate with Product Managers to align on customer research priorities informing the roadmap.
- Conduct in-depth research and competitive analysis to identify opportunities for Amazon Ads products.
- Communicate product vision/direction to internal/external stakeholders; establish a regular cadence of engagement.
- Partner with PMs to recommend customer feedback prioritization, support Alpha/Beta testing, refine the roadmap.
- Align GTM strategy to the roadmap; translate complex product info into simple language; gather post-launch feedback.
- Develop single-source-of-truth documentation enabling sales/marketing: positioning, messaging, availability, best practices, implementation guides, FAQs.

About The Team: Ads Product Marketing team is the strategic connective tissue between product innovation and market impact. The Commerce pod owns PMM for Sponsored Products, Sponsored Brands, and Brand Stores. This role is part of the Sponsored Products & Sponsored Brands (SPB) PMM team, evolving PMM beyond launch comms to influencing the roadmap via voice-of-customer research and competitive intelligence.

Basic Qualifications:
- 6+ years of professional non-internship marketing experience
- Experience using data and metrics to drive improvements
- Experience communicating results to senior leadership
- Experience building, executing, and scaling cross-functional marketing programs
- Experience in marketing or marketing research

Preferred Qualifications: experience using SQL or other analytical tools for data analysis; experience with customer segmentation/profiling/targeting; experience with Excel or Tableau.
