## Full JD text

Coinbase is hiring a Group Product Manager to lead its Customer Engagement & Experience (CEE) team, owning the retail support ecosystem for 100M+ customers. The role drives AI-powered self-service and automation across chat, voice, and social channels, and leads the Concierge program, guiding a distributed team of product managers, technical program managers, and designers toward an ambitious goal of automating more than 90% of customer contacts while maintaining greater than 95% CSAT across automated and human-handled support contacts.

### Team & Collaboration

Reports to a product leader who leads all Customer Experience (Agent and Customer-facing) tooling. Collaborates closely with Engineering, Data Science, and CX teams to align on objectives, define strategy, and drive execution for the customer-facing support technology ecosystem, with team members across North America and India.

### Responsibilities

- Own multi-year vision, strategy, and roadmap for retail customer support experiences
- Drive AI-powered self-service and automation across product surfaces, Help Center, and communication channels
- Own strategic direction for the Concierge program
- Build proactive support capabilities
- Develop and mentor a globally distributed product group

### Requirements

- 10+ years product management experience; 5+ years shipping customer-facing support/self-service/automation in fintech or regulated domains
- 5+ years leading and developing PM teams
- Demonstrated experience delivering AI-powered products with appropriate controls and governance
- Ability to define measurable outcomes and use data for roadmap prioritization
- Leverage AI to accelerate product group output and efficiency
