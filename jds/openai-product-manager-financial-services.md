## Description

About the Team: OpenAI is building AI products for serious professional work. Going deep on finance with a committed team focused on unlocking the potential of AI in this vertical: working closely with industry experts and users to build bespoke solutions that help finance professionals do real work. OpenAI's Financial Services team works closely with customers to understand where AI can improve the economics and quality of financial work: faster diligence, broader research coverage, sharper client materials, more accurate models, and better investment analysis. Work spans investment banking, equity research, private equity, asset management, wealth management, insurance, and other parts of finance.

About the Role: Looking for a Product Manager to help define and build OpenAI's financial services products. Reporting to the GM of Financial Services, will own product strategy, roadmap, and execution for key experiences, taking them from customer discovery and early prototypes through launch, adoption, and iteration. Hands-on role: spend time with bankers, investors, analysts, and other finance professionals; build and test early product experiences; work closely with Engineering, Design, Research, and customer-facing teams to ship.

In this role:
- Own product strategy and priorities with the GM
- Go deep on financial workflows (sit with customers, observe how they work)
- Build and ship: create working prototypes, demos, example outputs, and product requirements using Codex and other OpenAI tools
- Raise the bar for quality and trust (evaluations, datasets, model feedback)
- Make products work inside financial institutions (integration, data-access, privacy, security, review requirements)
- Measure results and drive adoption

You might thrive if you:
- Have a track record of taking complex products from discovery through launch and sustained adoption, as a PM, founder, or builder with substantial product ownership
- Understand financial work well enough to earn credibility with demanding practitioners (built products for financial institutions or worked directly in investment banking, investing, equity research, corporate development, or related fields)
- Combine strong product taste with attention to detail
- Work comfortably with engineers and researchers on model behavior, data quality, integrations, and evaluations
- Use AI deeply in your own work and build prototypes to test ideas
- Know how to choose useful goals and metrics, test assumptions, and change priorities when evidence changes
- Bring sound judgment about privacy, trust, and accuracy in consequential professional work
- Take ownership in ambiguous situations, drive decisions across teams not managed, communicate clearly
