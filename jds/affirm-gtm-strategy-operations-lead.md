## Description

The position holder owns end-to-end go-to-market strategy for a product line or segment, including rollout design, enablement, and adoption tracking.

Key Responsibilities:

- Transform cross-functional inputs into coherent GTM strategy and track performance

- Build materials, processes, and playbooks for field teams
- Gather field insights and recommend improvements to Product Marketing
- Analyze performance data to identify launch obstacles and flag risks early
