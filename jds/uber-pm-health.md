- Understand competitive positioning to deliver industry-leading products
- Optimize efficiency in dynamic environments
- Build alignment across internal and external stakeholders

- 3+ years product management experience
- Track record in metrics and experimentation-driven organizations
- Strong customer empathy and ability to blend research with data
- Passion for Uber's mission and hybrid technology/operations model
- Bachelor's degree in computer science or quantitative field
- Exceptional communication skills
