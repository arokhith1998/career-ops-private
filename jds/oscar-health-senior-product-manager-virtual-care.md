## Job Description (extracted, verbatim from posting)

Leads product development for Oscar Virtual Care (OVC), improving how members access care and how clinicians deliver it, spanning intake/scheduling through clinical documentation, treatment, follow-up.

**Responsibilities:**
- Define/execute product strategy and roadmap for Virtual Care
- Lead discovery with members, clinicians, operations teams
- Improve member journey (intake, scheduling, visit completion, care coordination)
- Build tools for clinicians (medical history review, documentation, care gaps, test/treatment ordering)
- Evaluate AI/automation opportunities to reduce administrative work
- Define success metrics; measure clinical/financial/operational impact
- Lead alignment across clinical, operations, engineering, design, data science
- Mentor more junior team members

**Requirements:**
- "6+ years of experience collaborating cross-functionally among engineers, designers, operations experts, and others in a Product Management or Product Management-adjacent role."
- "4+ years of experience in driving rigorous prioritization processes."
- "4+ years of experience with tech systems and understanding basic technology infrastructure such as backend services, database relationships, and leveraging database tools such as SQL."
- "2+ years of experience leading complex, cross-domain initiatives."
- "2+ years of experience root-causing ambiguous problems, building roadmaps towards a solution, and successfully executing against that roadmap."

Bonus: 1+ years building clinician-facing experiences.

Benefits: medical/dental/vision, 11 paid holidays, paid sick time, paid parental leave, 401(k), life/disability insurance, paid wellness time.
