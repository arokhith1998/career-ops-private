## Description (full text as rendered by LinkedIn)

At Mondrio we’re on a mission to build the future of pricing: agentic pricing management. We see many brilliant companies that deliver great products, but forget a key part: How to monetize the value they are delivering.
We believe that monetization should be data-driven and that it should not be a one-time exercise. It should be something that evolves as products, markets, or sales motions change. Our AI-native platform equips companies to bring that pricing intelligence into strategy and deal decisions. The highest-stakes pricing decisions still need an expert in the room that foster a long-term relationships with our clients. That expert is you.
About MondrioMondrio is a fast-growing startup built on deep pricing experience from our founding team who have run 150+ pricing engagements. We’ve seen firsthand how companies struggle with pricing and leave money on the table. We’ve also seen firsthand how companies that make pricing a continuous capability thrive.
Our team is designed to deliver that capability. Today our team of eight has pricing experts and engineers. Our main hubs are San Francisco and Amsterdam, which means we cover North American and European market. We believe that those markets are key to building a category-defining company.
We're backed by investors who've helped build leading tech companies and expect to grow the team 2-3x in the coming 12 months. Joining now means you’ll actively shape the product, the team, and the way we run engagements. If that kind of ownership and entrepreneurship sounds energizing rather than daunting, then this role is for you.
