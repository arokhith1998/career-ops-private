## About Joveo
Joveo is an AI-first recruitment advertising platform using machine learning, real-time bidding, and predictive analytics to help the world's largest employers hire faster and fairer. Recruitment adtech SaaS.

## Role Overview
Growth Manager owns the end-to-end growth engine at Joveo -- identifies the highest-leverage levers across acquisition, activation, and retention, and runs the experiments that move them. Works at the intersection of product, data, and marketing to compound growth systematically.

## Key Responsibilities
- Identify, prioritize, and execute growth experiments across the funnel (acquisition to retention)
- Design and run A/B and multivariate tests with rigorous statistical methodology
- Analyze user behavior, funnel data, and cohort metrics to surface growth opportunities
- Collaborate with product, engineering, and marketing teams to implement growth initiatives
- Build growth reporting frameworks; maintain a clear view of funnel performance
- Develop and scale growth channels (SEO, referral, lifecycle email, product-led growth)

## Required Skills & Qualifications
- Proven experience in growth, product growth, or growth marketing at a tech company
- Strong analytical skills: SQL, GA4, Amplitude, or Mixpanel proficiency
- Experience designing, running, and interpreting growth experiments at scale
- Familiarity with both product-led and marketing-led growth strategies
- Cross-functional work with product, engineering, and data teams
- Creative problem solver, bias to action, rapid iteration
