## Full job description (verbatim)

Who we are

OneCrew is building the dominant platform in paving and asphalt — a rapidly digitizing and consolidating industry that's been too long overlooked. Our product spans the heart of our customers' operations: mapping, estimating, contracting, scheduling, invoicing, job costing, and integrations.

At OneCrew we're building something special. If you want to transform a massive, underserved industry — and you love to grind toward simple, powerful solutions — we want to hear from you.

The role

We're hiring a Marketing Manager to work directly with our CMO and help build OneCrew's marketing function. This is a broad, hands-on role spanning content, customer marketing, social and field marketing. You'll own projects end-to-end, learn across every part of the marketing mix, and grow quickly as the team does.

What you'll do

- Own customer marketing end to end: interview customers and turn each conversation into case studies, short-form video, social posts and website testimonials. 
- Write and edit content across channels, including blog posts, emails, web copy and sales collateral. 
- Plan and execute field marketing, including industry events, trade shows and sponsorships. 
- Build and manage OneCrew’s online presence including social and peer review sites
- Partner with sales on collateral and help keep the website current. 

Who you are

- Early-to-mid career and motivated to grow fast. You hold a high standard for your work and treat feedback as a way to get better. 
- Strong writer and editor with a clear voice and holds the final draft to a high bar for clarity, accuracy and tone. You know what is and what isn’t appropriate ai content. 
- You aspire to be a full-stack marketer and are looking to go deep and learn new skills. 
- Audience-first in how you approach channels. You focus on where customers actually spend their time, not on chasing trends. 
- Self-directed and energized by building something new. You take ownership of your work, close loops and finish what you start. 
- You are comfortable using martech systems and tools, and are hyper-focused on measurement of programs

Qualifications

- 3+ years of hands-on marketing experience, ideally at a B2B saas startup. 
- A portfolio or writing samples that show your work. Please include a link with your application. 
- Plus: product marketing experience. 
- Plus: experience running demand gen or lead acquisition programs. 
- Plus: experience with basic video and image editing tools such as Descript, CapCut, Canva or Figma. 
- Plus: familiarity with marketing systems including Google Ads, GA4, etc and martech systems such as HubSpot, Marketo, Clay. 
- Plus: a background in marketing to construction, field service or the trades. 
- Authorized to work in the US without employer sponsorship, now or in the future. 

What we offer

- A great environment to grow, learn, and do your best work at a fun, fast-paced startup. 
- Unlimited PTO. 
- Full medical, dental, and vision coverage (100%-covered options available). 
- Office perks: breakfast, weekly lunch, monthly team events.
