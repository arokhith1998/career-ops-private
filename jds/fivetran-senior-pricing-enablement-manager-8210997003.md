About the Role
Pricing at our company is evolving quickly, and sellers need to keep pace with each change. We are seeking a Senior Pricing Enablement Manager who can translate packaging, curve, consumption, and ELA updates into clear enablement that provides guidance, covering what changed, why it changed, what sellers can offer, and when approval is required. You will own the enablement timeline across Pricing, Product, Deal Desk, and Product Marketing, and you will use field feedback to help shape future pricing decisions.
This is a full-time remote position with a preference for candidates to be near one of our offices in New York, Austin, or Denver. 
Technologies You’ll Use
Thought Industries, Salesforce, Gong, Notion, Claude
What You’ll Do

Partner with Pricing and Product Operations to provide enablement around net-new packaging, curve, discounts, consumption, bundles, and ELA changes. Build compelling and thorough and multi-modal enablement that explains what changed, why it changed, how to price it in a live deal, and deliver them through live sessions, leader briefings, and LMS content so the message lands consistently across segments.
Translate approved pricing policies into clear, digestible enablement so sellers understand what they can offer, when approval is needed, and how to follow the process. Work with Strat Ops, Pricing, and Deal Desk to confirm that quoting, approval, and CPQ workflows match the policy, and train the field on system changes as they ship.
Support with post launch reinforcement by identifying gaps in how the field is applying each pricing change, using deal reviews, Deal Desk escalations, call recordings, and sales input. Attend follow-up launches and pricing meetings to pull in sales feedback, and keep iterating on reinforcement until seller behavior changes.
Partner with Deal Desk, Pricing, Product, and Product Marketing to maintain a single enablement timeline for all pricing and product updates, from launch through follow-up. Run a regular cadence with these teams to surface upcoming changes early, agree on owners and dates, and prevent conflicting or late communications to the field.
Gather structured input from Sales, Customer Success, and field leaders on pricing effectiveness, customer sentiment, and operational challenges, through surveys, office hours, and deal-level conversations. Analyze recurring themes and pain points, and synthesize them into specific recommendations for pricing policy improvements.
Bring what you see in the field to Pricing Strategy, Product, and Sales leadership to inform future pricing and packaging decisions.

Skills We’re Looking For

5+ years of experience in sales enablement, product marketing, pricing, deal desk, or sales strategy at a B2B software company 
Experience enabling sellers on complex pricing, packaging, and consumption-based or usage-based models
Understanding of how pricing and packaging decisions flow into GTM systems, such as CPQ and Salesforce, and the ability to write clear business requirements for systems teams
Ability to build trusted relationships across Deal Desk, Pricing, Product, and Product Marketing, and to coordinate their input into a clear training timeline
Skill designing and delivering learning across formats, including live sessions, LMS content, and follow-up coaching
An analytical approach to field feedback, with the ability to find recurring themes and turn them into clear recommendations for leadership
Strong written and verbal communication, with the confidence to present to sellers and to Pricing Strategy, Product, and Sales leaders
Ownership, sound judgment, and the ability to manage multiple priorities across a fast-moving launch cadence
