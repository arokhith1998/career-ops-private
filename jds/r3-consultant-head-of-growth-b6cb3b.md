## Full JD Text

Job Description: Head of Growth

Location: Hyderabad
Work Mode: On-site
Reporting To: VP Sales

Role Summary

The Head of Growth owns demand for our client: build new traction channels and brand awareness, grow the channels that work, and increase the admissions.
Growth owns everything up to a qualified lead handed to the Admissions/Sales team; Sales owns conversion.
The target audience includes final-year engineering students and recent graduates, currently in Andhra Pradesh and Telangana and open to pan-India, along with the parents, relatives, and friends who influence their decision.

Key Responsibilities

1. Diagnose & Plan (First 30 Days)

- Audit every current channel: real admissions, CAC, lead quality, and attribution gaps.

- Agree on handoff SLAs with Sales against the existing qualified-lead definition.

- Set up one source-of-truth dashboard: source → lead → QL → admission, by channel and program.

- Deliver a written 7-month channel plan: admissions, qualified leads, spend, and CAC per channel per month to May 2027, offline and online.

2. Build & Scale Traction Channels

- Launch new channels as 4–6 week pilots, each with a hypothesis, budget, target CAC, and target volume.

- Scale proven channels in budget steps (~2x a month) while CAC stays within cap.

- Stop or redesign pilots that miss CAC by 30%+ after 6 weeks.

- Channels include:

- Paid digital

- SEO/AEO/GEO and content

- Student/alumni/parent/employee referrals

- Telugu and English creators

- College tie-ups and campus ambassadors

- Bootcamps, hackathons, and webinars

- Keep 10–15% of monthly spend for new experiments and at least 2 pilots running at all times.

3. Paid Digital

- Optimise creatives, landing pages, funnels, and retargeting on Meta, Google Search, and YouTube.

- Lower CAC before raising spend.

4. Lead Quality & Sales Handoff

- Generate and qualify leads.

- Hand every qualified lead to Sales within 24 hours.

- Keep the QL-to-admission rate within the band agreed with Sales.

5. Brand & Pipeline

- Build brand awareness among final-year students and recent graduates, so more students come to Intensive on their own.

- Build and track an engaged pool of final-year students through bootcamps, communities, campus programs, and content.

6. Team & Reviews

- Lead Marketing & Communication, the Social Media Manager, the Digital Marketing Consultant, and SEO/AEO (2 interns).

- Hire channel leads only as channels prove out.

- Run a weekly channel dashboard.

- Conduct a monthly review with Aniketh (feeds the Intensive MBR).

- Conduct a quarterly channel strategy reset.

- Document a playbook for each channel so it runs without the Head of Growth personally driving it.

Required Qualifications & Experience

- 8–12 years in Growth or Marketing, with 3+ years owning a lead or admissions number end-to-end.

- Has built at least two channels from zero to meaningful scale beyond paid ads (referrals, campus, creators, SEO, or events).

- Strong paid digital fundamentals: Meta and Google, funnels, attribution, CAC, and payback math.

- Runs growth as experiments: hypotheses, pilots, kill decisions, and written learnings.

- Data fluent: builds and reads funnel dashboards; comfortable with sheets, CRM data, and attribution.

- Has led a team of 5+, including first-time managers and freshers.

- Hyderabad-based or open to relocating to Hyderabad; on-site role.

Preferred Qualifications

- Edtech or upskilling experience, ideally a student or career-transition audience in India.

- Experience with Tier-2 and Tier-3 markets and offline demand: centres, walk-ins, campus, and events.

- Telugu speaker, or deep familiarity with AP/Telangana students and families; experience scaling acquisition across several Indian states.

- Experience with SEO/AEO, creator programs, or referral mechanics at scale.

Key Skills & Competencies

- Multi-channel growth building

- Experiment design and kill discipline

- CAC and payback math

- Funnel analytics and attribution

- Ownership: commits to a number and reports misses early

- Team building and coaching first-time managers

- Working with Sales on lead quality and handoff
