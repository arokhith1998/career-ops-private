## Full JD Text

Company Overview

Docusign brings agreements to life. More than 1.8 million customers and more than a billion people in over 180 countries use Docusign solutions to accelerate the process of doing business and simplify people’s lives. With intelligent agreement management, Docusign unleashes business-critical data that is trapped inside of documents. Until now, these were disconnected from business systems of record, costing businesses time, money, and opportunity. Using the Docusign AI-native IAM platform, companies can create, commit, and manage agreements with solutions created by the #1 company in e-signature and contract lifecycle management (CLM).

What you'll do

The Commerce Application Product Management group owns the systems that power how Docusign transacts, grows, and retains its customer base spanning quoting, billing, provisioning, and entitlements through account lifecycle at enterprise scale. We sit at the intersection of product, engineering, finance, and go-to-market, building the infrastructure that connects customer intent to revenue outcomes. Our work is deep, cross-functional, and consequential to how Docusign operates. You will own commerce capabilities end to end connecting how Docusign's products are defined, priced, quoted, billed, and provisioned into a coherent, reliable system that scales with the business. This role sits across the full commerce stack, requiring someone who can hold the whole arc in mind: from the data models and governance that underpin product and pricing constructs, through the quoting and ordering controls that govern how customers and sellers transact, to the downstream fulfillment and revenue systems where commitments become outcomes. This is a solution architect's role as much as a product manager's. You will be expected to map how decisions in one layer ripple into others, identify where fragmentation creates systemic risk, and drive the integrations and standards that make the commerce stack coherent rather than merely functional. Domain depth in any part of the stack is valuable; the ability to reason across all of it is essential. Areas you will be responsible for include quoting experience and controls, product and pricing enablement (including the introduction of new monetization models and catalog governance), and the cross-system integrations that connect these capabilities to billing, provisioning, and revenue operations. A defining expectation for this role is an AI-first product mindset: approaching commerce experience design not by asking "where can we add AI?" but by asking "what should this experience look like when AI is native to it?" That means rethinking quoting flows, configuration guidance, and pricing recommendations from first principles designing for outcomes that are only possible when intelligence is embedded throughout, not bolted on. This role is right for someone who combines deep commerce domain expertise with a systems mindset, thrives in cross-functional complexity, and is energized by building the infrastructure that every Docusign transaction depends on.

This position is an individual contributor role reporting to the Director, Applications Product Management - Commerce.

What you bring

Basic

- 12+ years of product management experience in commerce, quote-to-cash, or order-to-revenue domains within SaaS or enterprise software
- Experience reasoning end to end across commerce systems, understanding how product definitions, pricing constructs, quoting rules, billing logic, and provisioning outcomes connect, and how a change in one area creates downstream consequences in others
- Experience with one or more layers of the commerce stack (CPQ, catalog/pricing systems, billing platforms, provisioning, or revenue operations) and the ability to quickly build fluency in adjacent layers
- Experience with quoting controls: pricing rules, discount governance, configuration validation, approval hierarchies, and policy enforcement across direct and partner channels
- Experience enabling new products or monetization models to reach market, including familiarity with the data standards, workflow governance, and cross-functional coordination (e.g., NPI processes and product catalog management) that make launches reliable and scalable
- Experience working with enterprise commerce platforms (e.g., CPQ tools, subscription management systems such as Zuora, ERP/CRM systems such as Oracle or Salesforce) with an understanding of how data and state flow across system boundaries
- Experience identifying systemic issues in commerce or operational workflows and drive corrective action that prevents recurrence, not just resolves the immediate incident
- Experience with AI-first products: a track record of designing or shipping AI-powered capabilities in an enterprise context, not just integrating AI tools, but owning the product decisions around what the AI does, when it acts autonomously versus defers to humans, how its outputs are evaluated, and how it improves over time
- Experience with the building blocks of applied AI in enterprise workflows: LLM-based assistants, agentic task execution, retrieval-augmented generation, structured output and tool use, and the evaluation and feedback loops that make AI systems production-reliable
- Experience working fluently with engineering and data science teams on AI feature development - including translating business outcomes into model objectives, defining evals, and shaping rollout criteria for AI-assisted or AI-driven experiences
- Experience with data pipelines and analytics tools (e.g., Snowflake, Looker, Tableau) to monitor, analyze, and report on commerce platform performance and operational health
- Experience influencing priorities, shaping cross-functional strategies, and aligning multiple teams toward shared objectives
- Experience leading complex, high-impact cross-functional initiatives and communicate outcomes clearly to senior leadership
- Experience with Agile/Scrum and tooling such as Jira and Confluence
- Related technical degree (Computer Science, Engineering) or equivalent practical experience

Preferred

- Masters in Business, Computer Applications, or other advanced degree
- Experience with self-service or partner-led quoting and ordering models in a high-volume SaaS environment
- Experience building or shipping copilots, agents, or AI-native products in a commerce, sales, or revenue operations context, especially where the AI is taking action, not just surfacing information
- Certification in Lean Six Sigma, Agile, or Product Management
