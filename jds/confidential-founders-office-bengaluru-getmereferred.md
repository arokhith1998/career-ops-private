Job Description

About the Role
We are looking for a strategic generalist to join the Founders Office as a Chief of Staff - a high-impact role that sits at the intersection of strategy, operations, finance, and execution. You'll work directly with the Founder/CEO to drive key company priorities, manage cross-functional initiatives, and help scale the business efficiently. This role is ideal for someone with a finance, consulting, or venture capital background who wants to transition into a strategic operator role inside a fast-growing company.

Key Responsibilities
1. Strategic Planning & Execution - Partner with the Founder/CEO to define company goals, OKRs, and strategic initiatives. Drive cross-functional alignment and ensure timely execution on key priorities. Conduct market, financial, and competitive analyses to support strategic decisions.
2. Finance & Investor Relations - Build financial models, dashboards, and reports for leadership and investors. Work on fundraising materials, investor updates, and due diligence processes. Coordinate with finance and accounting teams to track KPIs and cash flow metrics.
3. Operational Excellence - Identify bottlenecks and design processes to make teams more efficient. Manage key special projects from new product launches to partnership evaluations. Support recruitment for key leadership roles and own internal communication rhythms.
4. Cross-Functional Leadership - Serve as a bridge between the Founder and department heads. Translate strategy into action by ensuring initiatives are tracked, reported, and delivered. Step in as proxy for the Founder in key meetings when needed.

Ideal Candidate Profile
- Experience: 35 years [likely "3-5 years"] across venture capital, consulting, investment banking, or operating roles in a startup or high-growth environment.
- Skills: Strong analytical and financial modeling ability, exceptional communication, structured problem-solving, and stakeholder management.
- Mindset: Bias for action, comfort with ambiguity, high ownership, and eagerness to learn.
- Tools: Proficiency with Excel/Google Sheets, Notion, Airtable, and presentation tools; bonus if familiar with startup tools like Asana or ClickUp.

Why This Role?
- Work directly with the Founder and leadership team on high-leverage problems.
- Shape company strategy and see your work translate into real outcomes.
- Get exposure across product, fundraising, operations, and people - ideal for future founders, operators, or investors.
- High growth trajectory with visibility and autonomy.
