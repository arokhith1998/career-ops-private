## Role overview
Campus Switching team, building the technical foundation for the Catalyst 9000 portfolio, Campus Fabrics, and security solutions. PM serves as the main subject matter expert, turning technologies like AI, security, and fabric networking into business solutions: creating deployment guides and demos for sales teams, working with Engineering on roadmaps, validating features in labs, acting as the customer's technical voice.

## Minimum qualifications
- A bachelor's degree with 5+ years of relevant experience, or a master's with 4+ years, in product management or technical marketing.
- Experience with enterprise networking, including campus switching, routing, and network security.
- Experience defining product requirements and working with Engineering to deliver networking products.
- Experience engaging customers and field teams to guide product priorities.

## Preferred qualifications
Owning product roadmaps and driving adoption of enterprise networking solutions; knowledge of network fabrics and VXLAN-based virtualization (SD-Access, BGP EVPN/VXLAN); experience with campus deployments, segmentation, identity-based access, network management/automation; familiarity with AI use cases in networking and network security technologies; ability to drive cross-functional decisions and explain technical/business tradeoffs.
