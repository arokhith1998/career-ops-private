## Full JD text

ZoomInfo is seeking a Senior Product Manager to lead the transformation of ZoomInfo's apps for the era of AI agents and generative AI. The role involves reimagining how go-to-market teams discover, engage, and convert target audiences using AI technologies.

### Key Responsibilities

- Own the AI product lifecycle from ideation through launch and iteration
- Set vision and roadmap for AI agents reshaping GTM workflows
- Engage customers to identify where AI-driven automation delivers value
- Define and track AI-centric metrics using tools like Amplitude and Tableau
- Lead cross-functional teams including engineers, AI/ML specialists, designers, and GTM teams
- Communicate AI product strategy to executives and partners

### Required Qualifications

- Strong AI builder background with hands-on experience
- Experience building products using LLMs, autonomous agents, and generative AI
- Deep familiarity with GTM (sales, marketing, customer success) workflows
- UX obsession with focus on simplicity and design excellence

### Why Join

- Proprietary data foundation (600M+ contacts, 100M+ companies, 1.5B signals)
- Central role in company's AI strategy with dedicated engineering teams
- Greenfield opportunity to define AI-native GTM experiences
- Culture emphasizing speed, entrepreneurship, and innovation
