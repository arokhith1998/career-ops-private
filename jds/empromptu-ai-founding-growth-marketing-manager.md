Company Description Empromptu AI helps regulated institutions build custom AI models for organizations that operate production AI at scale and want to own their core AI asset instead of renting generic foundation-model capabilities. The company provides an integrated orchestration substrate for inference routing, retrieval, evaluation, and observability, so teams can focus on high-impact applications rather than infrastructure. Domain experts then label critical edge-case data, turning the unique signals from each production environment into proprietary training assets.
Empromptu.ai closes the loop by training and deploying branded, customer-owned models that capture each organization’s asymmetric advantage. This approach is designed for teams with scale, in-house subject-matter expertise, clear rights over training data, and a desire to move beyond perpetual foundation-model “rent.”
Our customers include Communicare Health across 140 facilities, along with enterprises in manufacturing, government, finance, and education.
Role Impact
This is not a traditional “marketing manager” role. You will own the entire go-to-market motion end to end. You'd be working closely with the CEO to scale the work she has been executing.
About the Role
You'll own how Empromptu AI shows up in the world, from the story we tell to how developers and enterprises discover, adopt, and champion what we build. There's no team to inherit and no playbook to run. You'll work at all altitudes, setting the high-level strategy and then doing the tactical work yourself, from positioning down to the copy.
What you'll own
- Narrative and content: shape the company blog, thought pieces, and our unique perspective on AI, and position our founders and researchers as leading voices
- Product marketing: define our category and translate deeply technical capabilities into messaging that developers resonate with
- Brand and channels: own our company brand, website, and social cadence across X and LinkedIn
- Launches and announcements: coordinate product releases and announcements from press to podcasts to events
- Demand generation: build developer and enterprise interest to build our pipeline
Who you are
- Ideally have experience targeting CIOs, and/or developers
- Ideally have run their own marketing agency for clients or have built a marketing function from zero at a start-up and can both set strategy and execute it
- Must be able to own a technical narrative, launches, demand, and growth experiments across channels from social to SEO to email
- Must have an analytical mind. You can create systems to clearly show ROI while balancing creative.
- Must be able to translate technical concepts into simple viral messages that anyone can understand.
- Have 5+ years of professional expertise and operating in ambiguous environments
- Write exceptionally well, clear, technical, and fast
- Have taste: know what's credible, what's hype, and what's premature
- Are fluent in the AI industry, leverage tools yourself, and can credibly talk models and APIs with our engineers and researchers
- Show complete ownership, think at the level of goals, not tasks or tickets
Success Metrics
After 3 months
- Drive and capture demand
- Build and extract value from partnerships and creator relationships
- Set up and run marketing automations and GTM tooling
After 6 months
- Execute and own a full product launch
- Drive consistent inbound demand
- Establish Empromptu as a credible voice in AI
After 12 months
- Help drive the company to its ARR goal
- Own a repeatable GTM motion that ties content, launches, partnerships, and automation to revenue
Must and Nice to haves
Must-Have
- Product marketing and go-to-market experience
- Experience bringing a new product to market
- Proven ability to generate and grow revenue
- Ability to execute independently with limited direction
- Comfort owning multiple functions in a zero-to-one environment
Nice-to-Have
- Experience with tools like Attio, Intercom, Zapier, or similar automation platforms
- Experience working with early-stage B2B SaaS companies
- Background in technical or developer-focused products
Team and Culture
- This is the only marketing role at Empromptu today
- You will work closely with the CEO, product, and engineering
- The culture favors ownership, clarity of thinking, and bias toward action
- You should be comfortable making decisions, testing ideas quickly, and iterating in real time
People who thrive here are builders — they like blank pages, unclear playbooks, and real responsibility.
Growth Potential
This role is designed to grow.
As Empromptu scales, this position can evolve into:
- Head of Marketing
- VP of Marketing
You’ll have direct influence over strategy, positioning, and future team structure, with opportunities to build and lead a marketing organization over time.
