## Role summary
Defines, builds, and launches technology solutions that let store teams sell carrier-activated devices at the best prices while positioning Apple's full offering. Partners with Engineering, Design, Commercial Programs, and other cross-functional teams.

## Responsibilities
- Drive Retail's product vision/roadmap for core in-store carrier sales experiences (authentication, promotions, financing, activation flows).
- Lead end-to-end delivery for in-store carrier-connected sales experiences, from problem definition through launch and post-launch iteration.
- Partner with Commercial and Operations stakeholders on business problems, market opportunities, and process gaps.
- Act as a trusted advisor across Engineering, Design, Legal, Finance, and other stakeholders.
- Lead design reviews and prioritization discussions.

## Minimum qualifications
- 7+ years of dedicated Product Management experience with a proven record of delivering product roadmaps end to end.
- Demonstrated experience partnering with Engineering and Design on complex, cross-functional technology initiatives.
- Proven ability to build relationships and influence senior stakeholders without formal authority.
- Experience as a PM for native (iOS/Android) and/or web applications at a retail, consumer products, consulting, and/or SaaS company.
- Experience with consumer financing features and payments processing in retail POS and/or e-commerce.

## Preferred qualifications
Experience designing/shipping/integrating AI-powered or agentic features and applying AI tools to one's own PM workflow; advanced data analytics/A/B testing experience; experience owning e-commerce carrier sales or add-on subscription products end to end.
