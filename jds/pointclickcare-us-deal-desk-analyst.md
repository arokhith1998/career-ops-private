## Job Description (summary - WebFetch extraction tool limited verbatim quoting; this is a close paraphrase)
Deal Desk is a centralized Revenue Operations function reviewing non-standard, high-value commercial deals. The Analyst (IC, reports to Manager, Deal Desk) handles intake/review of concession and pricing exception requests:
- Intake and review: point of contact for requests, validating details, classifying/routing to approvers, documenting decisions
- Pricing and deal analysis: pricing models, billing ramps, revenue forecasts; discount/term-change impact on MRR and deal economics
- Data and reporting: approval tracking, data quality checks, monthly reporting on request volume, concession types, outcomes
- Sales support: answering rep questions, confirming approved terms in contracts/quotes
- Process improvement: documenting processes, suggesting policy improvements including templates and AI-assisted workflows

Minimum qualifications: 1-3 years in Revenue Operations, Sales Operations, pricing, finance, or related analytical role; strong Excel; clear communication. Preferred: Salesforce, SaaS pricing knowledge, AI/automation experience, SaaS or healthcare industry experience.
