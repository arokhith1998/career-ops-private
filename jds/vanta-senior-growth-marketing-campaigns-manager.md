## Job Description

At Vanta, our mission is to help businesses earn and prove trust. We believe that
security should be monitored and verified continuously, and we empower companies to
practice better security and prove it with ease.

The Senior Growth Marketing Campaigns Manager sets the strategic direction for
Vanta's integrated campaign programs for one of our core segments, connecting the
right story, the right audience, and the right growth bets to build pipeline for the
best Agentic Trust Platform on the market.

You'll own demand generation end-to-end: from the annual campaign strategy for your
segment to the post-event debrief to the budget that makes it all move.

### What you'll do

- Architect and execute integrated campaign strategy (brand, launch, event, demand)
  across key channels
- Own the numbers and define what good looks like: MQL, pipeline contribution,
  conversion rates. Set the standard, report against it, and optimize
- Build the campaign infrastructure for your segment, from frameworks, playbooks, to
  templates that scale output with AI tools
- Partner with Product Marketing as a strategic peer to design launch campaigns,
  inform demand campaigns, and plan sales plays
- Lock arms with Sales and SDR on lead routing, follow-up SLAs, and feedback loops
- Serve as the campaign roadmap owner for your segment, run the campaign calendar and
  budget
- Lead the ABM strategy for priority verticals in your segment
- Implement AI solutions across the function
- Mentor junior marketers and raise the overall craft of the campaigns function

### How to be successful in this role

- 8+ years in B2B SaaS demand generation or growth marketing
- A proven track record owning integrated campaigns end-to-end: from strategy and
  brief to results and budget accountability
- Analytical fluency
- Cross-functional range with Sales, SDR, creative, and Product Marketing
- Hands-on with marketing automation (HubSpot) and Salesforce
- Executive-level communicator
- A builder's instinct

### What you can expect as a Vanta'n

Industry-competitive salary and equity; comprehensive medical, dental, vision
coverage; 16 weeks fully-paid Parental Leave; health & wellness stipend; remote
workspace/internet/cellphone stipend; commuter benefits (SF/NYC office reporters);
family planning benefits; matching 401(k) with immediate vesting; flexible PTO plus
80 hours Sick Time; 11 company-paid holidays. Offices in SF, NYC, London, Dublin,
Tel Aviv, and Sydney.
