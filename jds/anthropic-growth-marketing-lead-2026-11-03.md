## Role summary (as extracted)

The Growth Marketing Lead owns and scales Anthropic's performance marketing for Claude across self-serve and sales-assisted journeys, covering consumer, developer, and business audiences, and the Growth team's performance marketing, lifecycle, and SEO work.

**Key Responsibilities (paraphrased):**
- Own the global performance marketing channel strategy, including forecasting, planning, audience targeting, and budget pacing
- Run acquisition, activation, and retention campaigns end to end
- Lead self-serve and product-led B2B demand generation, and partner with Enterprise Marketing and GTM on sales-assisted demand
- Work with Data Science on incrementality, holdouts, attribution, lifetime value, and payback
- Drive experimentation and turn results into playbooks; work with Brand, Content, and Product Marketing on creative
- Present complex analysis to senior leadership, shape the long-term roadmap, and lead and grow the growth marketing team

**Qualifications (paraphrased):** 10+ years in growth or performance marketing at high-growth tech companies with a self-serve or product-led motion; experience leading a growth marketing function (team, budget, KPIs) while staying hands-on; technical background or ability to work effectively with engineers. Preferred: experience marketing to developers or on an AI product, SEO and lifecycle experience, dual B2C/B2B funnels, consumer subscriptions, or a founder background.
