## Full JD text (verbatim)

Imagine what you could do here. At Apple, great ideas have a way of becoming outstanding products, services and customer experiences very quickly. We believe that if you bring passion and dedication to your job, there's no telling what you could accomplish!

As a member of our Worldwide Product Marketing group, you'll represent customers throughout product and feature journeys, from concept through development and launch. You'll develop and apply insights to help infuse innovative features into our product development process. Together with our team, you'll work to craft groundbreaking ideas into tangible products and features that excite the world, and help lead the way on how we bring them to our users and developers.

At Apple, we combine product management and product marketing responsibilities into a single, cross functional Product Management role that collaborates with design, engineering, finance, legal, marketing communications, public relations, market research, sales, and support for maximum impact across the organization.

**Description**

We are looking for a highly motivated Product Manager to help define and shape how Siri AI can deliver genuinely useful, delightful experiences for users across Apple's ecosystem.

This role requires a deep understanding of AI, including consumer experiences, industry trends, and how AI can be genuinely useful in everyday workflows. You are comfortable with complex and ambiguous problems, adept at identifying risks, and committed to excellence in your work. You think broadly and strategically about the role of AI in people's day-to-day lives. You bring both creative and critical thinking to every challenge.

**Responsibilities**
- Help drive the definition and launch of meaningful AI experiences and features across Apple's platforms and products
- Think strategically about how AI can blend into and enhance everyday workflows
- Play an instrumental role on the Siri AI product marketing team by collaborating with engineering and design to investigate new concepts and being the voice of the market to Apple during the development phase
- Craft product messaging briefs, guide marketing communications on launch materials and act as a spokesperson in press briefings and public events
- Together with partner teams, deliver products that leverage the latest innovations in AI to enrich people's lives

**Minimum Qualifications**
- 6+ years experience in product marketing and/or product management roles, including at least one entire product development cycle from concept to launch with consumer products or software platforms
- Hands-on experience formulating product messaging and creating outbound marketing for both technical and consumer audiences, and/or partnering with engineering and design during early research and development
- Recent experience within the last 3 years in one or more of the following software segments: operating systems, AI, consumer apps, consumer services, or adjacent
- Deep collaboration skills, and the ability to build strong relationships with cross-functional teams
- Ability to drive communication with key stakeholders, the media, and public

**Preferred Qualifications**
- A dedicated self-starter with creative and critical thinking abilities
- Deep understanding and passion for the AI landscape, products, and experiences
- Proven record working collaboratively with engineering, design, and marketing peers
- Strong ability to lead through influence, debate, and focusing on what's best for users
- High aptitude for uncovering insights through data exploration and analysis
- Best-in-class story-telling, presentation, and written communication skills
- Press and media experience with success as a product spokesperson on a national or global level
- Strong attention to detail
- Willingness to travel
- Bachelor's degree; MS/MBA preferred
