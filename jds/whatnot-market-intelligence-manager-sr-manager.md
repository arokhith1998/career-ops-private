## Job Description (full, verbatim)
Join the Future of Commerce with Whatnot!
Whatnot is the largest live shopping platform in North America and Europe to buy, sell, and discover the things you love.

Role
At Whatnot, we are deeply focused on building the best possible experience for both buyers and sellers. To support this work, we must understand how the buyer and seller experience on Whatnot compares to the experience at other livestream shopping marketplaces and adjacent marketplaces. That's what this role is for.

The Market Intelligence Manager / Senior Manager owns how Whatnot understands the livestream shopping market and adjacent markets, so we always have an accurate understanding of where we can improve our buyer and seller experience to stand out.

This role is equal parts builder and advisor: turning fragmented and noisy data into a trusted picture of the market, and spotting what's coming next before it becomes urgent.

See the market clearly
- Monitor livestream shopping market performance and maintain a point of view on how Whatnot compares
- Own data source and vendor relationships - from evaluation and acquisition through ongoing quality management
- Build and maintain the dashboards and reporting that make this data usable and trustworthy for stakeholders across the business, including senior leaders

Get ahead of changes in the market
- Spot patterns and changes in market pricing, promotions, product, and policy, and build a point of view on whether and how Whatnot should respond
- Build leading indicators so teams have runway to act pre-emptively rather than reactively
- Stand up the forums and escalation paths that turn a signal into a coordinated, timely response from the right stakeholders

Turn insight into action
- Translate imperfect, incomplete external data into clear, decision-ready recommendations
- Own recurring market intelligence reporting (dashboards, exec-facing decks, ad hoc analyses) and make sure it drives real decisions, not just awareness

Build tools and infrastructure that scale
- Bring structure, prioritization, and rigor to a naturally ambiguous, continually-changing space
- Build tooling and documentation so we can scale the amount of market intelligence we ingest
- Continuously evaluate and evolve the data sources and vendors we rely on as the market and our needs change

We offer flexibility to work from home or from one of our global office hubs. Team members in this role must live within commuting distance (50 miles) of our Los Angeles, Seattle, San Fran or New York hubs.

You
- Bachelor's degree in Business, Economics, Data Analytics, or a related field
- 5-6+ years of experience in consulting, investment banking/private equity, a fast-growing startup, or a similarly rigorous analytical environment
- Strong analytical rigor - you're strong with SQL and have comfort owning data infrastructure end-to-end (not just querying others' tables); tools like Sigma, Hex, or Tableau are a strong plus
- Demonstrated ability to work and influence cross-functionally
- Fluency in using AI tools to enhance problem-solving, analysis, and execution across a wide range of business challenges
- Experience building and maintaining dashboards and reports for varied audiences
- Experience managing external vendor relationships, including ongoing data quality management
- Ability to think strategically and execute methodically in a fast-moving, ambiguous environment

Nice to Have
- Experience with live commerce, e-commerce, resale, or other two-sided marketplace platforms
- Experience with Python or similar for data collection, cleaning, or classification workflows
- Prior experience working in a market intelligence function
