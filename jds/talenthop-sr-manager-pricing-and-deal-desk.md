About Our Client:
This organization operates globally in the eDiscovery, information governance, and data recovery industry. It serves law firms, corporations, and government agencies by addressing complex data challenges throughout the entire data lifecycle, including forensic collection, legal review, production, and managed hosting. The company offers a range of services, managed services/SaaS solutions, and support, positioning itself as a comprehensive partner in managing data-related needs.
About the Opportunity:
The SENIOR MANAGER, PRICING AND DEAL DESK is responsible for overseeing pricing, deal structuring, and governance of complex transactions across services, SaaS, and hybrid offerings. The role acts as a critical gatekeeper, ensuring deal integrity from quote to close by coordinating with financial planning, revenue operations, sales, and executive leadership. This position focuses on implementing and enforcing pricing strategy through AI-assisted tools and governance processes, enabling scalable analytical capacity and maintaining margin protection on complex deals.
Responsibilities:
Serve as the gatekeeper for non-standard pricing, discounting, and complex deal approvals before legal and finance review.
Design and enforce approval workflows and escalation tiers for deal governance.
Ensure all concessions, including waived or free services, are properly recorded to prevent margin leakage.
Manage the operating cadence and execution of the cross-functional Pricing Council.
Translate council directions into documented pricing policies, discounting guardrails, and approval matrices.
Maintain consistent pricing views across all offerings to avoid siloed approaches.
Develop and run AI-assisted pricing tools, including scenario modeling and simulations.
Automate recurring analyses such as margin tracking and discount pattern detection.
Collaborate with sales and delivery teams to identify and mitigate cost exposures in complex deals before contract signing.
Ensure pricing and quote data integrity within CRM and CPQ systems.
Tag deals accurately for revenue reporting and maintain KPI reporting on pricing metrics.
Requirements:
8 to 10+ years of experience in pricing, deal desk, revenue operations, or commercial finance with direct P&L or margin responsibility.
Proven ability to design and run governance processes and lead cross-functional teams.
Proficiency with AI-assisted analytics tools and strong Excel skills for validation.
Experience with complex B2B deals, preferably in services, SaaS, or legal technology sectors.
Executive presence and ability to engage with senior leadership.
Strong influence skills to drive discipline across Sales and Delivery without direct authority.
Preferred Qualifications:
Experience in eDiscovery, legal tech, or managed/usage-based services pricing.
Familiarity with AI-assisted modeling platforms such as Claude or Copilot.
Knowledge of Salesforce, CPQ, or similar deal-desk workflow tools.
Understanding of QoE/credit-agreement addback processes and covenant reporting.
Pay Range and Compensation Package:
The expected annual base salary range for this role is $160,000 to $170,000.
