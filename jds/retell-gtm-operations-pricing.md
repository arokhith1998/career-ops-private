About The Role

Retell AI transforms customer experience with voice AI for enterprises, including customers like CVS/Aetna, San Antonio Spurs, Lenovo, and Grab. We have more customer stories than we can tell!

As Retell's first dedicated pricing hire, you will build pricing from zero across both of our motions: self-serve, product-led usage-based pricing, and enterprise pricing for large contracts. You will sit in Operations, report directly to a founder, and work every day with Sales, Finance, Product, and Growth. This is a hands-on role for someone who combines strategy with execution: you will design the pricing, run the analysis, and get into live enterprise negotiations, at a company running at $100M+ in annualized revenue.

Key Responsibility

This hire owns how Retell prices its products across self-serve and enterprise, from usage-based pricing and packaging strategy through to closing large deals with Sales.

- Own usage-based pricing and packaging for the self-serve (PLG) motion: pay-as-you-go rates, plans, free credits, and add-ons, so pricing drives conversion and expansion while protecting gross margin.
- Build Retell's first enterprise pricing framework (commits, volume tiers, usage, outcome-based, or hybrid models) and structure large deals with Sales, including joining key negotiations.
- Set up a models for deal desk: discount guidelines, approval thresholds, order form templates, and quoting that scales with deal volume.
- Build the pricing data foundation (price realization, discount leakage, usage versus revenue, conversion and expansion by plan, margin by customer) so every pricing decision is backed by numbers.
- Partner with Finance, Legal, Product, and Growth on contract terms, new product pricing, and price changes, with no surprises for customers.

Must Have

What We're Looking For

- 4+ years in pricing, monetization, growth at a high-growth B2B SaaS, infrastructure, or AI company (or in consulting or banking followed by a startup operating role)
- Hands-on experience designing usage-based or consumption pricing, ideally for a product-led (self-serve) motion
- Experience structuring and pricing complex enterprise deals from proposal through close
- Strong financial modeling. You can model unit economics and gross margin yourself
- Have built a framework or process from scratch (a price book, plan structure, discount policy, or deal desk) in a fast-moving environment

Nice To Have

- Experience pricing AI-native products, developer tools or APIs, voice AI, or contact center software
- Experience with outcome-based pricing and building ROI or business cases for customers
- Worked with quoting and billing systems (Salesforce CPQ, Stripe Billing, Metronome, or similar)
- Python or AI tooling for pricing analysis and automation

How We Evaluate

We care less about checkboxes and more about:

- the quality of your pricing judgment when data is incomplete and the answer is not obvious
- rigor on unit economics: you know what each minute of a voice call costs us and what it should earn
- judgment in customer-facing negotiations, including when to hold firm and when to flex
- speed and ownership: how quickly you turn a pricing idea into a shipped, measured change

You Might Thrive If

- You would rather ship a v1 pricing change in a week and iterate than plan for a quarter
- You are equally comfortable in a spreadsheet, a self-serve funnel, and the negotiating table
- You want to build the first version of pricing yourself, hands-on, rather than manage a team
- You are curious about how AI costs work under the hood and how they should show up in price
