## About

## Role Summary
SEO Manager develops and executes strategies driving traffic and leads through **organic search AND AI discovery channels** (Google, ChatGPT, Perplexity). Collaborates with content, product, engineering, and agency partners.

## Key Responsibilities
- Develop and execute SEO/GEO strategies increasing organic traffic, AI citations, and qualified leads across traditional search and AI platforms
- Keyword research and competitive analysis for existing + emerging product categories
- Partner with content teams to create authoritative, well-structured content that ranks + earns AI citations
- Optimize on-page (titles, headers, schema markup) for search visibility + AI summaries
- Analyze site performance using SEO tools; track KPIs (traffic, rankings, share of voice, citation rate, leads)
- Regular site audits: technical SEO, on-page, E-E-A-T
- Collaborate with engineering on technical SEO improvements + site issues affecting search + AI visibility
- Stay current on algorithm updates, GEO developments, emerging AI platforms
- Learn renewable energy industry for content + SEO opportunities
