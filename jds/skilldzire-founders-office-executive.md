**Role Overview**
SkillDzire Technologies (Hyderabad) is looking for a proactive, highly organized, and articulate Founder's Office Executive to work directly with the Managing Director (MD) in a work-from-office capacity at Hyderabad. In this role, you will serve as a vital communication bridge between the MD's office, internal operational departments, sales teams, and external academic stakeholders (colleges and universities across India). This high-visibility opportunity offers deep exposure to strategic operations, executive decision-making, and organizational coordination. The candidate may be required to travel with the MD as and when required.

Company: SkillDzire Technologies (Hyderabad)
Position: Founder's Office Executive
Department: Founder's Office / Executive Administration
Location: Hyderabad
Employment Type: Fulltime
Salary: 20 - 50k per month
Experience: 0-3 years

**Key Responsibilities**
- Operations & Team Follow-ups: Track, follow up on, and consolidate updates on pending, ongoing, and future projects across internal teams, presenting regular status reports to the MD.
- Academic & Institutional Coordination: Maintain active communication regarding university tie-ups, college partnership updates, MOOCs, and student program implementations.
- Sales & Activity Tracking: Follow up on institutional sales activities, monitor pipeline progress as directed by the MD, and ensure sales team alignment with executive targets.
- Executive Communication & Correspondence: Draft, review, and dispatch formal emails, notices, and updates on behalf of the MD office to internal teams and external partners.
- Meeting & Schedule Management: Organize meetings (Zoom/Google Meet and in-person, as required), prepare agenda notes, record actionable minutes of meetings (MOM), and assist with MD calendar priorities.

**Qualifications & Requirements**
- Education: Bachelor's or Master's degree in Business Administration, Management, Communication, or related fields (Freshers/Final year students welcome).
- Communication Skills: Exceptional written and verbal communication skills with a professional demeanor.
- Work Location & Travel: This is a work-from-office role based in Hyderabad. Must be willing to travel with the MD as and when required.
- Organization & Follow-Up Skills: Ability to manage multiple tasks, follow up proactively on deadlines, and keep projects moving forward.
- Technical Proficiency: Hands-on experience with MS Office (Word, Excel, PowerPoint), Google Workspace, and video conferencing tools (Zoom, Google Meet, Teams).

**What You Will Gain**
- Direct mentorship and operational exposure while working closely with senior leadership (MD Office).
- 360-degree operational view of a fast-growing Hyderabad-based EdTech enterprise.
- Opportunity to build high-level executive experience in a professional, work-from-office environment.
