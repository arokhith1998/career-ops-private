**URL:** https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/view/4483557248

About the Role
We’re looking for a hands-on, founding Product Marketing Manager to build our product marketing function from the ground up. You’ll own positioning, product launches, competitive intelligence, sales enablement, and go-to-market strategy for an innovative enterprise integration and AI platform.

Key Responsibilities
Own product positioning, messaging, and category narrative.Lead product launches and end-to-end GTM execution.Develop sales decks, competitive battle cards, and enablement materials.Drive website content, blogs, customer stories, and thought leadership.Manage analyst relations with Gartner, Forrester, and industry experts.Build persona-based messaging for product, platform, and delivery leaders.

Requirements
5–8 years of Product Marketing experience in B2B SaaS.Experience as a founding or first PMM at a startup, ideally Series A/B.Strong experience in product launches, positioning, messaging, and sales enablement.Familiarity with AI, enterprise integrations, APIs, infrastructure, or developer tools.Excellent communication skills and ability to thrive in a fast-paced startup.

Preferred: Experience with Gartner/Forrester, AI products, HubSpot, Clay, Apollo, Python, or SQL.
