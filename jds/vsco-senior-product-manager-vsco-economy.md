## Full job description

VSCO exists for photographers. The company is building the full photographer's workflow: from creating and editing work, to delivering it to clients, to running a business. VSCO is looking for an ambitious Senior Product Manager to bring to life new product experiences helping photographers build sustainable businesses, defining what this future looks like by identifying opportunities, validating promising ones, and leading product development that helps photographers unlock more income through VSCO.

**Key responsibilities:** Set strategic direction and roadmap for VSCO Economy; deeply understand workflows, businesses, and economic needs of professional and aspiring photographers; oversee delivery of innovative products ensuring successful outcomes for users and VSCO; identify high-potential opportunities and lead discovery efforts to validate problems and business viability; regularly speak with existing and potential customers; use AI tools to accelerate research, spec writing, data analysis, and decision-making; build lightweight prototypes using AI-assisted no-code tools; create clarity in ambiguous problem spaces and influence executives; be ruthless about scope and priority; help shape future business lines for VSCO; collaborate cross-functionally.

**Requirements:** 5-8 years as full-time Product Manager with creator economy experience; strong systems thinking and 0-to-1 product experience driving business growth; strategic judgment on monetization design and take-rate tradeoffs; experience building payout infrastructure (Stripe Connect, PayPal Payouts, similar); delivered infrastructure-heavy work with deep engineering partnerships; understanding of KYC and compliance aspects of payouts; strong product strategy skills; excellent spoken and written communication; fast-moving with high ownership; strong cross-functional partnership skills; excellent prioritization; thrives in fast-paced startup environments with ambiguity tolerance.

**Nice-to-have:** Deep understanding of photography and creative tooling industries; AI-native product practices; founder-like accountability levels.

**Benefits:** Competitive salary and equity, healthcare (medical, dental, vision), flexible time off, paid parental/medical/caregiver leave, 401K, 11 paid holidays.
