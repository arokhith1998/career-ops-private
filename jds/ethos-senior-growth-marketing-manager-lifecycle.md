## Full Job Description

### About Ethos
Ethos is a leading life insurance technology company on a mission to protect families by democratizing access to life insurance and empowering agents at scale.

### About The Role
We are seeking a data-obsessed, US-based Senior Growth Marketing Manager to own the lifecycle across three distinct customer journeys: converting prospects into customers, expanding existing customers through cross-sell and upsell, and scaling a recently launched product line. The through-line is conversion, retention, and maximizing long-term customer value (LTV).

Prospect-to-customer conversion blends self-serve digital and human follow-up (live agents and voice AI). Cross-sell, upsell, and the new product line are entirely self-serve. You operate on shared OKRs with the product managers who own each area, and in close partnership with Sales leadership, agents, and the voice AI team for the conversion journey.

### Duties and Responsibilities
- Own the Lifecycle Strategy and Messaging across all three journeys.
- Convert Prospects into Customers: own the post-lead journey where self-serve and human touch meet.
- Drive Cross-Sell and Upsell: fully self-serve motion; own triggered messaging, on-page experiences, offer structures.
- Scale a Recently Launched Product Line end to end (0->1 becoming 1->10).
- Own Retention and LTV as a number you move, not a metric you report.
- Build Trust Signals Across the Funnel (credibility, social proof, carrier proof points, transparency).
- Own the Data Behind Conversion and Drop-Off; be the expert on cohorts and funnel curves.
- Run Experiments End to End; report statistical significance; roll out winners.
- Cross-Functional Ownership with product managers and Sales leadership.

### Qualifications and Skills
- 6+ years of experience. Senior-level track record in Lifecycle, Growth Marketing, or CRM at a B2C Tech, FinTech, Insurtech, or Marketplace company. Higher-consideration/higher-AOV product experience strongly preferred.
- Copywriting and messaging strategy for high-consideration financial decisions.
- Self-serve conversion instincts (no human in the loop).
- Comfortable partnering with a Sales org.
- Multi-channel mindset (email, SMS, voice/conversational AI, in-product, on-page, human-delivered follow-up).
- Analytical mindset: cohort retention, statistical significance, LTV.
- Technical CRM proficiency: Braze, Iterable, Customer.io, HubSpot, or similar.
- PM collaboration experience with shared OKRs.
