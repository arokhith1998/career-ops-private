## Job Description (extracted, verbatim from posting)

Talkiatry is seeking a results-driven Senior Growth Marketing Manager, Paid Social to own the strategy, execution, and optimization of Meta, TikTok, and other paid social platforms as key drivers of new patient acquisition. Combines senior ownership with day-to-day campaign management, focusing on optimizing and scaling direct response paid social campaigns while improving CAC efficiency and, over time, managing toward LTV. Reports to the Director of Paid Growth.

**You Will:**
- Plan, execute, optimize performance marketing campaigns across paid social (Meta, TikTok primarily)
- Own overall paid social strategy, media mix recommendations
- Manage budgets to maximize ROI
- Direct data-driven creative strategy with Creative team
- Build forecasts and scenario models
- Conduct A/B testing, audience segmentation, experimentation
- Own paid social landing page testing roadmap
- Use AI tools to streamline analysis and reporting

**You Have:**
- "Bachelor's degree and relevant experience."
- "5+ years of experience in performance marketing, with deep, hands-on expertise in managing and scaling paid direct response campaigns on both Meta and TikTok."
- Personally managed large-scale monthly budgets ($1M+) across paid social channels
- SQL and BI tools (Omni, Looker, Tableau)
- Experience marketing B2C services/subscriptions or non-ecommerce, higher-consideration direct response business
- Nice to have: healthcare/healthtech or regulated industry marketing experience

Talkiatry is the largest dedicated psychiatry practice in the US (750+ psychiatrists, 250+ therapists, 2M+ patient visits).
