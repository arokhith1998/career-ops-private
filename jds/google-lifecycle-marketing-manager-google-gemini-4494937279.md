Minimum qualifications: Bachelor's degree or equivalent practical experience. 6 years of experience in growth, CRM, or lifecycle marketing. Experience working cross-functionally with various stakeholders and teams.

Preferred qualifications: Experience working with global teams across multiple time zones. Experience utilizing AI productivity tools to solve complex, multi-layered problems and streamline business or development workflows. Measurement-driven with the ability to use benchmarks and performance metrics to track progress. Strong communications skills. Strong organizational and time management skills.

About the job: Whether you're on a consumer product (like Gmail, Search, Maps, Chrome, Android) or a business product (Google Ads, AdSense, Google Marketing Platform, Analytics), you take part in a complete marketing experience as you lead every facet of the product's journey. As Lifecycle Marketing Manager for Gemini, you will manage and execute CRM strategies that drive user growth across Gemini's Apps. You will work closely with stakeholders across marketing, product, policy, support, and engineering, as well as agencies/vendors, to design and deliver communications and experiences that educate and delight Gemini users at key moments in their lifecycle.

Individual pay is determined by factors including job-related skills, experience, and relevant education or training. US: $141000 - $205000 (USD) + 15% bonus target + equity + benefits.

Responsibilities: Manage and execute the global Gemini CRM strategy and support the related Martech roadmap; maintain and manage data structures across systems; utilize CRM analytics to drive customer-centric action plans; partner with Product, Design, Brand and Creative teams; prioritize, test, and launch experiments and campaigns across a user's lifecycle, from acquisition, onboarding and activation, to feature adoption, churn prevention, and resurrection.
