## Full JD text (verbatim)

This position is listed on behalf of a partner company, who manages all applications and next steps. Our partner is looking for a Campaign Marketing Manager based in the United States.

The Campaign Marketing Manager will lead multi-channel campaigns that strengthen brand affinity, deepen product engagement, and drive revenue growth across a large B2B user base. This role owns the lifecycle and campaign marketing strategy, from planning and execution to performance analysis and continuous optimization. Working across email, paid media, social, affiliates, partnerships, and content, you will help users discover more value in the product and move from free to paid plans. You will also reactivate inactive users, support sales pipeline generation, and promote research, webinars, newsletters, and other educational resources. Collaborating closely with marketing, product, customer success, and leadership teams, you will manage multiple projects and stakeholders in a remote, asynchronous environment. You will join an established marketing operation with existing onboarding journeys, nurture programs, and campaigns, with an expectation to launch your first initiatives within 30 days.

**Accountabilities**
- Own multi-channel campaigns: Plan, coordinate, and execute brand and promotional campaigns for the user base, including initiatives such as Messaging Madness, State of Email Outreach, and Email vs. Email Outreach.
- Drive free-to-paid conversion: Develop campaigns that encourage free users to adopt paid plans, with conversion performance as a core measure of success.
- Improve retention and reactivation: Re-engage lapsed and inactive users through relevant communications that explain product capabilities, encourage adoption across different product surfaces, and contribute to revenue growth.
- Manage lifecycle and email marketing: Lead retention, win-back, newsletter, promotional, and cold email campaigns, building on existing onboarding and nurture journeys.
- Promote content and research: Create, test, and distribute valuable resources, including blogs, research reports, guides, playbooks, webinars, newsletters, and podcast content, to help users improve their outreach.
- Support sales enablement: Identify users and accounts that may benefit from onboarding assistance, plan optimization, or multi-user adoption, and engage them proactively to generate sales opportunities.
- Develop channel and partner marketing: Manage affiliate communications, strengthen partner relationships, and coordinate co-marketing initiatives that expand audience reach.
- Coordinate social and content activities: Oversee social media campaigns and written and video content, ensuring consistent brand messaging across channels.
- Measure and optimize performance: Track campaign results, analyze quantitative and qualitative insights, and experiment with conversion-rate optimization and growth opportunities through small, measurable tests.
- Improve AI-supported marketing workflows: Develop and continuously refine practical uses of Claude, ChatGPT, and Gemini to improve campaign planning, content production, communication, and execution.
- Collaborate across teams: Work with marketing, product, customer success, and other internal and external stakeholders to align campaign priorities, manage projects, and deliver measurable outcomes.
- Report on impact: Share campaign performance, insights, and recommendations, focusing on free-to-paid conversion, customer lifetime value, retention, and revenue contribution.

**Requirements**
- At least 5 years of experience in campaign marketing, digital marketing, lifecycle marketing, demand generation, or a related field.
- Demonstrated success influencing revenue and retention in a product-led growth (PLG) business model.
- Strong experience owning cold email, email outreach, or outbound go-to-market campaigns, with a deep understanding of the email outreach landscape.
- Ability to understand B2B users and buying cycles, translating customer needs and business milestones into timely, relevant, and motivating communications.
- Proven ability to own campaign strategy and execution across email, cold email, paid advertising, social media, affiliate marketing, and partner channels.
- Strong project and stakeholder management skills, including the ability to coordinate multiple initiatives, processes, and internal and external contributors.
- Curiosity and creativity in developing messaging that educates users, encourages product adoption, and builds long-term brand affinity.
- Data-driven mindset, with the ability to interpret quantitative and qualitative results, plan experiments, optimize conversion rates, and identify scalable growth opportunities.
- Strong written and verbal communication skills, including the ability to incorporate communication skills effectively into AI-supported workflows.
- Technical confidence and willingness to learn new systems. Familiarity with tools such as Customer.io, WordPress or another CMS, Canva, Claude, Planable, PostHog, Linear, and Notion is valuable.
- Self-starter attitude, sound judgment, and the ability to work independently while managing competing priorities.
- Experience working remotely and communicating effectively in an asynchronous, distributed team.
- Availability to work within the specified North American or European time zones.
- Flexibility to attend team retreats in Europe twice a year.

**Benefits**

- Automatic annual salary increase: 5% per year.
- Paid time off: Five weeks of paid vacation annually.
