Contribute to developing processes within the Product Management team and partner organizations that are customer-centric and focused on best-in-class execution. Handle product from cradle to grave to ensure product financial plans are met (full P&L ownership). Ensure product requirements and specification meet target OEMs and segments. Drive product development teams to meet product objectives and close / raise open issues affecting OEM opportunities. Drive product development teams to be accountable in 3rd party component selection (controllers, capacitors, substrates, etc.). Generate product collateral (roadmaps, sales tools, etc.). Perform ad-hoc analysis for senior management on relevant metrics of the product line.
