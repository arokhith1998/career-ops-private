## Full JD text

Who We Are

Applied Materials is the global leader in materials science and engineering solutions at the foundation of virtually every new semiconductor chip and advanced display in the world.

What We Offer

Salary: $86,500.00 - $119,000.00
Location: Austin, TX, Santa Clara, CA

TEAM OVERVIEW: Build a product management career with Applied Materials' Spares Product Line Management team. As a Spares PLM, you will work across engineering, supply chain, operations, field service, and business teams to manage spare parts throughout their lifecycle, drive commercialization/service readiness, and support long-term customer needs.

KEY RESPONSIBILITIES:
- Support spare parts through the product lifecycle (changes, upgrades, obsolescence, replacements, alternate sourcing, cost reduction).
- Develop semiconductor process/equipment knowledge across process areas and spare part applications.
- Support Product Change Management (PCM) activities (ECOs, PCNs, product configuration changes).
- Help maintain accurate Bills of Materials (BOMs) and product configurations.
- Support component roadmaps/strategies for obsolescence, replacements, alternate suppliers, cost reduction.
- Analyze product/cost/supply/service data to identify trends and improvement opportunities.
- Collaborate with Engineering, Supply Chain, Operations, Field Service, Product teams.
- Support resolution of supplier/supply-continuity/quality/product-lifecycle issues.
- Use data, analytics, AI-enabled tools to generate insights and improve decisions/processes.
- Prepare presentations/analyses/project updates for cross-functional and leadership reviews.

QUALIFICATIONS:
- Masters degree in Engineering, Physics, or Chemistry
- GPA 3.0 or above preferred
- 0-2 years of experience (internship, co-op, or project management experience encouraged)
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills
- Excellent communication and teamwork abilities
- Proficiency with Microsoft Excel and PowerPoint
- Interest in semiconductor technology, product management, general curiosity to learn

PREFERRED: internship experience in manufacturing, engineering, product management, or semiconductor technology; data analytics and AI tools experience.

WHAT YOU'LL GAIN: experience supporting top semiconductor customers; experience leveraging data/analytics/AI tools; exposure to product development/service operations/lifecycle management; direct work with global engineering and customer-facing teams; development path toward leadership roles.

Applications reviewed on a rolling basis; apply by December 30, 2026 (may close early based on volume/selection).

Additional Information: Time Type: Full time. Employee Type: New College Grad. Travel: Yes, 10% of the Time. Relocation Eligible: Yes.
