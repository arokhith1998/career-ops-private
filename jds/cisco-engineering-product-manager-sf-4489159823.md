## Role Summary
Product Manager for Network Platform on the Cisco Cloud Control team, unifying the management experience across Cisco's networking portfolio (cloud-managed and hybrid platforms). Works with networking leadership, executives, and cross-functional sales, marketing, and partner teams.

## Responsibilities
- Define customer use cases and requirements to unify Cisco networking into one platform across on-prem, cloud, and hybrid deployments.
- Work with engineering and product design to simplify the customer experience across Cisco.
- Build an integrated purchase, installation, expansion, and renewal lifecycle aligned with the wider Cisco portfolio.
- Coordinate roadmaps across Networking, Security, and Collaboration business units.
- Work with executive leadership on multi-quarter product strategy; support sales, marketing, and partner efforts.

## Minimum Qualifications
- A bachelor's degree with 10+ years of related experience, a master's with 8+ years, or a PhD with 5+ years, or equivalent work experience.
- 10+ years in product management or technical product leadership for enterprise SaaS or cloud-managed platform software.
- Technical background working directly with engineering and architecture teams on complex platform projects.
- **Degree field requirement (verbatim):** "Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, Engineering, or a related technical discipline."

## Preferred Qualifications
Experience partnering with UX/product design on enterprise customer journeys; strong customer empathy; technical curiosity across enterprise networking, data center, cloud, emerging technologies; record of breaking down silos across matrixed business units.
