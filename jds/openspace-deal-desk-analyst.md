## Role summary
Supports OpenSpace's Sales team by preparing accurate quotes and order forms, coordinating approvals, and resolving routine commercial issues, using Salesforce and CPQ.

## Responsibilities
- Create, review, and process quotes/order forms in Salesforce/CPQ, checking products, pricing, approvals, documentation.
- Clarify sales requests, identify the underlying problem, communicate requirements to Finance/Legal/Accounting.
- Apply documented deal processes, approval rules, standard terms, approved contract fallbacks; escalate outside guidance.
- Support basic contracting tasks (approved legal language, summarizing non-standard issues).
- Own assigned tickets through resolution; follow up proactively; keep stakeholders informed.
- Coordinate quoting, order processing, provisioning, and billing issues across teams.

## Qualifications
- 2+ years in Deal Desk, Sales Operations, order management, commercial operations, or related role.
- Hands-on quoting/order-form experience in Salesforce and a CPQ system (Salesforce CPQ preferred).
- Experience working within documented approval processes across Sales and other teams.
- Strong verbal communication, active listening, comfort with live phone/video conversations.

## Preferred (not required)
B2B SaaS or recurring-revenue experience; familiarity with SaaS order-form language/contract redlines; ticketing-system and spreadsheet experience.
