As the Youth GTM Marketing Manager, you will lead global go-to-market strategy and execution at the intersection of product and reputation marketing for YouTube's youth and family experiences, owning the global marketing playbook for reaching parents through paid media, influencer campaigns, and the global parent website, while leading programs such as the Teen Digital Wellbeing initiative.
