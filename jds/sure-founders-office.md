## Full JD text (verbatim, via LinkedIn jobs-guest API)

About The Role
For problem-solvers who want to learn fast, own real outcomes and build alongside the founders.

Sure is an early-stage fintech, and this role sits right next to the founders. There's no fixed lane. Each week you'll be pointed at whatever matters most: launching something new, fixing something broken, cracking a partnership, getting a deal over the line, or sorting out a messy problem nobody owns yet. You take it from "we should figure this out" to done.

If you like variety, hate waiting for permission and get a kick out of making things happen, this is built for you.

If you want to learn faster than your job title suggests and you're the person who gets things done, we should talk.

What you'll do
- Founder priorities: research, plan and execute the initiatives the founders are driving, from idea to launch
- Growth and partnerships: help find, pitch and close partners, run experiments and figure out what moves the numbers
- Business operations: build and fix the processes behind the company, including vendors, finance and compliance, hiring and onboarding
- Data and decisions: pull the numbers together, find the insight and turn it into a clear recommendation
- Fundraising and investors: decks, data rooms, investor updates and diligence
- Special projects: anything that's important, urgent and doesn't have an owner yet

What we're looking for
- A problem-solver. You take vague, messy problems, break them down and find a way through
- A go-getter. You don't wait to be told. If something is stuck, you chase it, unblock it and close the loop
- A fast learner. You're happy to work on things you've never done before and get good at them quickly
- Comfortable with ambiguity. A partner pitch in the morning and a broken process in the afternoon doesn't faze you
- Sharp and structured. You think clearly, write clearly and are comfortable with numbers, spreadsheets and AI tools
- Any background works: engineering, commerce, CA, MBA, consulting, or a startup you ran yourself. We care about how you think and what you've done
- Experience: we don't count years. Show us something you made happen

What we offer
- Work directly with the founders: sit in the room where decisions get made on product, growth, fundraising and hiring
- Learn on the job, fast: you'll get exposure in months that would take years in a large company
- Real ownership: your work ships and you'll see its impact straight away
- Grow with Sure: as we scale, this role can lead into running a function, whether that's strategy, growth, operations or something we haven't named yet
