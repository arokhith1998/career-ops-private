Entrepreneur in Residence (Marketing), Create Workflow
Remote

About us
Create Workflow is a small Airtable/Softr and AI automation agency. We are an early stage business with a small team.

We are looking for someone who thinks like a founder: picks the marketing bets, builds the engine that turns attention into booked calls and paying clients, and owns the result. Our Target audience is early stage businesses in US, UK.

The role in one line
You own Create Workflow's marketing outcome, with the founder as your partner, not your manager. Target: 30-80 qualified leads and 2-4 paid clients a month.

Business Areas you will own:
- Marketing strategy: decide which 1-2 channels we bet on each quarter, and which we stop, based on data.
- Positioning and offer: sharpen who we serve, what we sell and why we win, into one clear line, an entry offer and pricing that converts.
- Demand generation: build repeatable inbound (Google Ads, SEO/AEO, founder-led content) and outbound (LinkedIn, cold email, communities) lead flows.
- Funnel and conversion: own the path from first touch to booked call to paid client, including website, landing pages, forms and follow-up.
- Team and systems: guide marketing interns, write the SOPs, and use AI tools and agents to do more with a small team.
- Experiments: run small, cheap tests with a clear hypothesis, budget and kill rule for each.
- Numbers: own the weekly marketing scorecard (spend, cost per lead, conversion rates, booked calls, revenue) and turn it into 1-3 decisions.
- New bets: spot and test new service lines, partnerships and channels (for example Airtable partner directory, AI services, Meta/WhatsApp ads).

First 90 Days:
- Month 1: Audit the full funnel from ad click to paid client, fix tracking, and present a one-page marketing plan with 2 channel bets.
- Month 2: Launch both bets, rewrite the main landing page and offer, and set up the weekly scorecard and intern SOPs.
- Month 3: Double down on the channel that works, kill the one that doesn't, and hit a steady flow of qualified leads every week.

You'll be a fit if you
- Have started something of your own (a business, side project, community or brand), or want to, and think like an owner.
- Have hands-on marketing experience: ads, content, outbound or growth, with numbers to show for it.
- Make decisions from data and first principles, not gut feeling alone.
- Can work with ambiguity: you turn unclear problems into clear plans and ship without waiting for instructions.
- Are careful with money: you treat our budget like your own.
- Can lead and coach interns, and communicate clearly with the founder and clients.
- Understand B2B services, no-code or AI automation (nice to have).

What you get
- Real ownership of a business function, with a seat at the founder's table on strategy.
- Direct work with the founder on positioning, offers and growth decisions.
- A track record of building a marketing engine from zero, with measurable results.
- 10% commission of the revenue you help us generate.
- A path to Growth Lead or CMO role, with compensation and upside tied to results.
