## Full JD Text

Legora wants a Performance Marketing Manager to own and scale digital campaigns that drive pipeline and revenue for targeted accounts. The role is hands-on and execution-focused, with a strong emphasis on Account-Based Marketing (ABM). Reports to the Senior Director, Growth Marketing; works with product marketing, content, and design.

Responsibilities:
- Build and manage targeted campaigns to acquire new prospects.
- Use AI tools for campaign work, creative variants, ad copy, research, and reporting.
- Run cross-channel campaigns, including podcasts, legal newsletters, and emerging platforms.
- Develop ABM strategies aligned with sales priorities.
- Create tailored messaging and run A/B tests.
- Monitor CAC, ROAS, and pipeline contribution.
- Own budget planning and spend pacing.
- Report on results and optimize based on attribution and engagement.

Requirements:
- 5+ years of digital performance marketing experience, with B2B and ABM focus.
- Experience running multi-channel campaigns on LinkedIn, Google, Meta, and emerging media.
- Hands-on experience with paid campaign management and performance tracking.
- Experience working with sales and BDR teams.
- Familiarity with HubSpot, Dreamdata, and LinkedIn Campaign Manager.
- Strong communication, analytical skills, and an execution-oriented mindset.
- Comfort using AI tools for targeting, creative testing, and reporting.

Benefits (as listed): medical, dental, vision plans; HSA or healthcare FSA; generous parental leave; Maven Clinic access; dependent care FSA; 401(k) with company match; unlimited PTO; life insurance and STD/LTD; pre-tax commuter benefits; daily company-provided lunch for the in-person office.
