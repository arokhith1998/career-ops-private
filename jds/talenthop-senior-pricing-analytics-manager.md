About the Opportunity:

The SENIOR PRICING & ANALYTICS MANAGER role focuses on developing and optimizing pricing and packaging strategies to drive revenue growth and improve customer satisfaction. This position plays a critical role in informing strategic pricing decisions by leveraging data analysis, market research, and customer insights. The manager collaborates with cross-functional teams to translate complex analytics into clear commercial recommendations, influencing the organization’s pricing approach and market positioning.

Responsibilities:

Develop and refine product packaging, feature bundling, and pricing tiers based on consumption-based pricing trends.

Analyze market landscapes, competitor strategies, and customer usage to recommend value capture and revenue growth strategies.

Lead pricing operations including profitability, price elasticity, and financial modeling to optimize margins and support renewal and migration.

Design pricing tools and resources to enable sales and field teams in enterprise negotiations.

Establish pricing governance frameworks and discount policies ensuring pricing consistency and integrity across regions.

Requirements:

Bachelor’s degree in Business, Economics, Data Analytics, or related field; Master’s preferred.

Experience in pricing strategy, data analytics, or similar roles within SaaS, technology, or consulting.

Proficiency in data analysis tools such as Excel, SQL, Python, and BI platforms like Tableau, Looker, or ThoughtSpot.

Knowledge of SaaS pricing models including subscription and consumption-based approaches.

Strong business acumen with experience in market and competitive analysis.

Excellent problem-solving, project management, and cross-functional collaboration skills.

Effective communication skills to convey complex analytical concepts to diverse audiences.

Bonus: Familiarity with behavioral economics or pricing psychology principles.
