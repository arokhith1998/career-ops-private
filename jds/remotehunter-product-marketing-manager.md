# Product Marketing Manager

**Company:** RemoteHunter
**Location:** United States
**URL:** https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/view/4483610742

About Our Client:
This organization operates in the cybersecurity industry, focusing on behavioral AI-driven solutions to combat cybercrime. It addresses challenges in email security and is expanding into identity, AI security, and insider threat protection. The company serves over 4,500 customers, including more than 25% of the Fortune 500, and is experiencing significant growth exceeding 40% annually.

About the Opportunity:
The PRODUCT MARKETING MANAGER is responsible for developing clear and differentiated product messaging that highlights the organization's unique approach to cybersecurity. The role involves leading product launches, collaborating with marketing teams to drive demand throughout the buyer's journey, and serving as a subject matter expert on email security for both customers and analysts. This position contributes by shaping the product narrative and supporting sales enablement to enhance market position.

Responsibilities:
Develop distinctive product messaging and positioning.Lead internal and external product launches across various channels.Collaborate with Marketing to align messaging with the buyer's journey and drive pipeline growth.Manage launch strategies for both major updates and incremental feature releases.Create and update product content including collateral, presentations, and digital media.Conduct customer research to refine messaging.Enable sales teams and channel partners on product value.Represent the company at external forums.

Requirements:
5+ years of experience producing buyer-focused written content, preferably in cybersecurity, with available samples.Experience managing product launches in fast-paced settings.Strong technical curiosity and ability to explain complex cybersecurity concepts clearly.Excellent verbal and written communication skills.Strategic mindset with capability for independent and efficient execution.Ability to influence stakeholders across organizational levels.Strong listening skills, empathy, and collaboration.Commitment to customer and partner success.

Pay Range and Compensation Package:
Base salary range: $122,400—$176,000 USD.Compensation includes potential bonus or incentive pay, equity, and a comprehensive benefits package.

Benefits & Perks:
Medical insurance.Dental insurance.401(k) retirement plan.
