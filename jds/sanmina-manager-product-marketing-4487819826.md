## Full JD text (verbatim)

Sanmina Corporation (Nasdaq: SANM) is a global powerhouse in integrated manufacturing solutions serving the fastest growing segments of the global Electronics Manufacturing Services (EMS) market.

**Core Responsibilities**
- Translate dense technical documentation into high-conversion marketing copy, narrative frameworks, and presentation decks.
- Launch one-to-one ABM and multi-channel campaigns across capabilities, services, and product offerings, prioritizing cloud and AI infrastructure, communications, and power markets.
- Deploy and optimize paid digital media, owned content programs, and targeted marketing initiatives.
- Collaborate with creative teams to translate technical narratives into value-driven visual assets, website content, and presentation themes.
- Manage the production and delivery of marketing deliverables that communicate advanced design and manufacturing capabilities.
- Conduct competitive analysis of marketing collateral and industry positioning.
- Partner with the VP of Marketing to establish corporate positioning and refine strategic narratives.
- Provide objective feedback on core value propositions.

This is a hybrid role requiring 3-5 days a week in the office, varying on location.

**Must-Have Requirements**
- Minimum 8+ years of product marketing experience within the technology sector or an adjacent hardware industry.
- Excellent writing and communication skills.
- Direct experience overseeing multi-channel marketing campaigns, ABM programs, corporate events, content, and digital media execution.
- Proven history of managing complete marketing campaigns from initial ideation through final execution.
- Strong operational discipline.
- Experience working within complex, multi-divisional, and multi-geography organizations.

**Bonus Qualifications**
- Prior product marketing and content creation experience within the Electronic Manufacturing Services (EMS) industry.
- Functional knowledge of the cloud and AI data center infrastructure market.

Sanmina employees also receive benefits including: Comprehensive medical and dental coverage, retirement benefits, and for formal education related to advancing your career at Sanmina, reimbursement for certain educational expenses -- including tuition."
