## Job Description (extracted, verbatim from posting)

MIPS is building a modern ecommerce platform to make it easier for customers, partners, and developers to discover, evaluate, purchase, and manage MIPS products and services (digital goods - software, licenses, documentation, training, support; and physical goods requiring fulfillment, inventory, logistics, tax handling, import/export compliance). As Product Manager / Product Owner, you will own the ecommerce roadmap, requirements, and backlog.

**Essential Responsibilities:**
- Own the ecommerce product backlog (catalog, pricing, cart, checkout, payments, tax, fulfillment, entitlement, support, account management)
- Shape a rolling 12-18 month ecommerce roadmap
- Represent the buyer and internal operator
- Deliver commerce capabilities via agile planning (quote-to-order, license delivery, payment processing, order tracking, returns)
- Enable digital and physical goods (licensing, entitlement, inventory, shipping, logistics)
- Drive tax, trade, and compliance requirements with finance/tax/legal/security teams (sales tax, VAT, invoicing, export controls, import restrictions, restricted-party screening, product classification, data privacy, auditability)
- Support customer/partner programs (beta, pilot launches, integrations)
- Measure and optimize (conversion, activation, order accuracy, fulfillment cycle time, compliance exceptions)

**Required Qualifications:**
- "8+ years in product management, product ownership, ecommerce, digital platforms, enterprise software, marketplace platforms, or commerce operations"
- Experience with ecommerce platform capabilities (catalogs, pricing, cart/checkout, payments, subscriptions, tax calculation, invoicing, order management)
- Working knowledge of supply chain/fulfillment for physical goods
- "Familiarity with sales tax, VAT, import/export restrictions, export controls, restricted-party screening, product classification, invoicing, and audit requirements for global commerce."

MIPS develops high-performance, real-time RISC-V processors. To apply: resume + cover letter.
