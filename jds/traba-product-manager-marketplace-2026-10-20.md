## Full JD Text

About Traba

Traba is building the AI operating layer for the light industrial supply chain. We started in the workforce space because contingent labor is the biggest operational pain point our manufacturing and logistics customers face. We used it to embed ourselves inside their daily operations and create a far better customer experience through technology. Now those same customers are pulling us beyond staffing into the broader operational workflows that run their facilities. That foundation gave us proprietary data from millions of shifts and deep enterprise relationships. But our edge is more than data. By connecting to the systems running across every facility and activating the workers already on our platform to execute against them, we are building applied AI that drives real productivity gains and transforms how the global supply chain operates at scale. We are backed by Founders Fund, Khosla Ventures, and General Catalyst.

The Role

The product is the labor itself: the workers we match and deploy into warehouses and manufacturing facilities across the country (and eventually, the world). Every product decision impacts supply, demand, fulfillment, customer outcomes, and marketplace health. We are looking for an entrepreneurial and experienced Product Manager to own and evolve our staffing product that sits at the center of Traba's marketplace. This role reports directly to our VP of Product.

What you'll do:
- Architect and steward product strategy across critical staffing and marketplace workflows
- Solve complex marketplace problems balancing customer needs, operational realities, business outcomes
- Write detailed product requirements, partner closely with engineering teams
- Build systems improving marketplace liquidity, fulfillment, matching quality, operational efficiency
- Use data to uncover hidden patterns and answer marketplace/customer questions
- Translate operational complexity into scalable product systems; design internal tooling
- Launch and iterate products across customers, regions, operational environments
- Socialize work across levels/departments; synthesize stakeholder feedback

What we're looking for: customer-obsessed; AI-native operator (prototype before spec, check in code, build internal agents); marketplace operator (two-sided marketplace experience); enterprise B2B muscle; first-principles thinker; operations systems builder; builder mentality; deeply collaborative.

What you'll bring:
- 3-5 years of experience as a Product Manager
- Evidence of shipping products that materially moved business outcomes
- Strong experience in two-sided marketplaces with real supply and demand dynamics
- Experience building enterprise products with multi-stakeholder customers
- Experience building internal operational tooling/systems
- Strong technical fluency, startup experience, wide-surface-area appetite

Benefits: start-up equity, competitive salary, 100% paid health/dental/vision, dinner via Grubhub (NY), commuter benefit, team building, Gympass, flexible PTO, One Medical, HSA via Optum, Talkspace, Health Advocate, Teledoc Health.
