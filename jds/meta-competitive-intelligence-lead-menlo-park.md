## Full Job Description

As an Analyst in Meta's Competitive Intelligence organization, you will operate at the intersection of analytics, data science, and market strategy. You will work on major projects and product areas (often in environments of significant ambiguity or technical complexity) to drive both technical and business outcomes. This is a hands-on, high-impact role for builders who thrive on solving real problems. This role demands a unique blend of analytical and statistical knowledge, strategic thinking, and the ability to translate complex insights into impactful product and business decisions.

### Competitive Intelligence Lead Responsibilities
- Market Strategy: Influence organization-level product direction through data-driven narratives and an in-depth understanding of the competitive landscape.
- Demonstrated experience operating at scale, across ambiguous environments, with working knowledge of econometrics.
- As a quantitative market-strategist, blend practical and applied understanding with technical expertise (pressure-testing data quality, understanding data biases, being solution-driven).
- Analytics Leadership: Conduct analyses with 3P datasets, develop statistical models and forecasts, deliver actionable insights.
  - Data onboarding: identify, onboard, and rigorously evaluate 3P datasets.
  - Data triangulation: triangulate data from many sources of imperfect information; synthesize low-fidelity 3rd-party signals into high-fidelity trend reports (Bayesian aggregation or other methods).
  - Data transformation: apply quasi-experimental designs (synthetic control, diff-in-diff) to isolate impact of exogenous market shocks and competitor actions.
- Technical & Methodological Knowledge: causal inference, Bayesian aggregation, driving org-wide best practices.

### Minimum Qualifications
- Bachelor's degree and a minimum of 4 years of work experience (minimum of 2 years with a Ph.D.) in business intelligence, product analytics, or economic/strategy consulting in a technology environment with increasing scope and impact.
- Demonstrated skill to ethically source, validate, and synthesize high-signal insights from people (stakeholder interviews, field research) while maintaining privacy/consent/integrity standards.
- Proficiency in AI-powered tools: working knowledge of Generative AI (LLM and AI agents), prompt engineering, orchestrating AI systems.
- Practical working understanding of data-analytics tools; direct experience with 1P and external 3P datasets.
- Experience with SQL, Python, and/or R.
- Experience with statistical analysis including causal inference (RCTs, synthetic control, diff-in-diff, meta-analyses) and/or Bayesian aggregation.
- Demonstrated communication skills for technical and non-technical stakeholders.
- Demonstrated experience thriving in ambiguous environments.

### Preferred Qualifications
Master's or Ph.D. in a quantitative field (Quantitative Economics, Political Science, Operations Research, Data Science, Computer Science, Physics, Business, Mathematics); demonstrated ability to integrate AI tools to redesign workflows; responsible/ethical AI practice experience; ongoing AI skill development.

### About Meta
Meta builds technologies that help people connect, find communities, and grow businesses.
