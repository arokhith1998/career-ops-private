## Role framing

Senior IC digital marketing role owning authenticated-channel strategy on Truist.com. Drives revenue, engagement, cross-sell for existing customers through personalization, segmentation, and test-and-learn programs. Heavy Adobe stack dependency.

## Responsibilities

- Research, develop, execute strategy for Truist authenticated digital channels: acquisition, retention, profitability, cross-sell across product lines
- Partner with business marketing partners and strategists on product-specific roadmaps
- Apply consumer behavior models and user journeys to banking products and services
- Leverage web analytics and performance data to refine strategies, improve conversion, enhance client experience
- Maintain reporting capabilities, track KPIs, communicate value to senior management
- Develop personalization and targeting based on segmentation studies and client interactions
- Research industry trends, competitive environment, emerging technologies

## Required qualifications

- Bachelor's in Marketing, Communications, Business Administration, or related field
- 7+ years related experience (or equivalent education and training)
- Deep SME or broad knowledge in marketing discipline
- Sound business + organizational strategy understanding
- Ability to interpret business challenges and implement best practices
- Lead complex projects with risk exposure
- Influence others, industry landscape knowledge, regulatory awareness
