## Job description (WebFetch extraction)

**Role summary:** An individual contributor on the Revenue team, responsible for accurate revenue reporting, the monthly close, contract documentation, revenue accounting treatment, revenue controls, and support for quarterly forecasting.

**Responsibilities (paraphrased):**
- Perform month-end close work, including revenue analyses, journal entries, account reconciliations, and revenue recognition
- Analyze ERP sales transactions to verify data accuracy and revenue schedules, including allocations
- Validate revenue treatment across all revenue streams
- Review contract write-ups and advise on ASC 606 treatment
- Establish, maintain, and review standalone selling price ranges and methodologies
- Manage quarterly reviews and year-end audits by external auditors
- Oversee and mentor Revenue Analysts
- Support executive analytics and revenue disclosures
- Work cross-functionally with OTC and FP&A
- Handle special projects and ad hoc requests

**Qualifications (paraphrased):**
- Bachelor's degree in Accounting; "CPA preferred." Requires at least 8 years in a similar role at a public, high-growth SaaS or technology company
- Experience with revenue operations, revenue recognition, and reconciliation, including sales data, customer contracts, and new product offerings
- Working knowledge of ASC 606, including SaaS and usage-based revenue models
- Strong Excel skills and experience with large data volumes
- Knowledge of ERP and CRM systems (NetSuite, Salesforce)
- Strong analytical, research, problem-solving, organizational, and time-management skills
- Ability to take ownership of complex areas, influence cross-functional partners, and drive projects independently
- Strong communication and business-partnering skills

**Success measures:** Within 30 days, become familiar with the business and revenue streams. Within 3-6 months, perform assigned close tasks independently. Within 6-9 months, work directly with Billing/OTC and FP&A.
