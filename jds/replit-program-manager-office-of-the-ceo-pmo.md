## Job Description (full, verbatim, via Ashby posting-api JSON descriptionHtml)
Replit is the agentic software creation platform that enables anyone to build applications using natural language. With millions of users worldwide, Replit is democratizing software development by removing traditional barriers to application creation.

About the Role
We are looking for a hands-on Program Manager to operationalize the CEO's external presence and growth work: founder-led marketing, founder-led sales, investor relations, and strategic go-to-market (GTM) efforts around the world. Reporting to the Chief of Staff and working on strategic special projects, you will turn the CEO's priorities into repeatable systems, clear workplans, and completed actions.
This is an execution-heavy role. You should be equally comfortable building a founder-led sales playbook, preparing an investor update, and following up on an overdue deliverable. Success requires strong judgment, attention to detail, discretion, and a willingness to take on both high-impact initiatives and the routine work that keeps them moving.

Key Responsibilities
Founder-led marketing: Build and run the operating system for the CEO's marketing presence (content calendar, speaking/event schedule, media/podcast opportunities, social channels); turn the CEO's ideas into repeatable content with Brand, Comms, Content; set up intake/approval/review workflows; track reach, engagement, and pipeline influence.
Founder-led sales: Operationalize the CEO's role in the sales motion (target account lists, executive outreach, customer/partner meetings, follow-up); partner with Sales, Partnerships, RevOps on briefs and next steps; maintain a tracker of CEO-sourced/assisted opportunities; document the playbook.
Investor relations support: Support investor updates, board materials, fundraising/diligence materials; maintain the investor/stakeholder tracker; coordinate data requests across Finance/Legal/functional leaders; prepare briefing documents.
Strategic GTM, globally: Support GTM strategy/execution across regions (market entry, partnerships, government/ecosystem engagement, regional events); coordinate with regional teams/partners; translate initiatives into workplans; surface cross-regional insights/risks.
Strategic special projects and operating cadence: Own time-sensitive special projects assigned by the Chief of Staff; run project check-ins; build agendas and document decisions for recurring leadership meetings; maintain trackers/dashboards/scorecards; document SOPs and replace manual work with AI-enabled automation.

Qualifications
- 3+ years of experience in program management, business operations, chief of staff or executive operations, GTM operations, or a comparable execution-focused role.
- Demonstrated experience managing cross-functional projects and recurring operational processes.
- Strong organizational skills, with the ability to maintain detailed trackers and manage competing deadlines.
- Excellent written and verbal communication skills, including turning complex inputs into concise executive materials.
- Proficiency with AI. Ability to work independently, adapt quickly, and make practical decisions with incomplete information.
- Discretion with confidential investor, customer, and company information.
- Willingness to perform hands-on administrative and logistical work alongside program management responsibilities.

Preferred experience
- Experience supporting a founder, CEO, or Chief of Staff.
- Experience in marketing, sales, or investor relations at a fast-paced or high-growth company.
- Experience working across regions or with international teams and partners.
- Experience preparing board and investor materials.
- Experience improving workflows through automation and AI-enabled tools.
This is a full-time role based in the Foster City, CA office, with an in-office requirement of Monday, Wednesday, and Friday.
