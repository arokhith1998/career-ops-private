## Job description (full text via LinkedIn guest API)

Growth Marketing Manager
Remote, United States
Full Time | $105,000 to $115,000 base, plus annual bonus | Remote, US

Description

We are seeking a Growth Marketing Manager to join GlobalTimesOnline, a digital news and publishing organization that delivers trusted journalism to readers in over 120 countries. This role grows our audience and turns readers into paying subscribers, for someone who loves running experiments, digging into data and scaling what works. The ideal candidate is part strategist, part analyst and always testing.

Responsibilities
- Own growth goals for new readers, newsletter sign ups and paid subscriptions
- Plan and run performance marketing across paid search, paid social and display
- Run A/B tests on landing pages, offers, emails and paywall messaging
- Build lifecycle campaigns that turn new readers into loyal subscribers
- Track spend, cost per subscriber and return on every channel
- Partner with product, editorial and data on new growth ideas
- Share clear weekly results and recommendations

Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in marketing, business, economics or a related field
- 4+ years of growth or performance marketing experience
- Hands on with Google Ads, Meta Ads and analytics tools
- Track record of growing subscriptions, memberships or a consumer audience
- Comfortable with data, A/B testing and spreadsheets
- Strong written and verbal communication
- Experience in media or subscription businesses is a plus
- Must be authorized to work in the United States

Benefits
- Medical, dental and vision, with generous employer contribution
- 401(k) with a 4% company match
- Annual performance bonus
- 15+ days of PTO, paid holidays and a December company wide week off
- Flexible hours and a $1,000 home office setup budget
