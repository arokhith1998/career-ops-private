About The Job

We currently have an exciting opportunity for a Sr. Product Manager to join our Digital team. We're looking for a Senior Product Manager who combines strategic thinking, customer empathy, analytical rigor, and strong execution to deliver measurable customer and business outcomes across our e-commerce domains. You'll own a product domain from opportunity identification through measurable impact, partnering closely with Engineering and Design to solve meaningful customer problems while balancing business objectives, technical considerations, and customer value

What You’ll Do | Key Accountabilities

-  Own the strategy, roadmap, execution, and measurable outcomes for a product domain.

-  Use customer research, analytics, experimentation, and business insight to identify and prioritize the highest-impact opportunities.

-  Lead cross-functional discovery with Engineering and Design to define customer problems before developing solutions.

-  Make thoughtful product decisions by balancing customer value, business impact, technical complexity, and delivery risk.

-  Partner with Engineering throughout delivery, supporting release planning, experimentation, and post-launch measurement.

-  Build alignment across stakeholders through clear communication, evidence based recommendations, and influence without direct authority.

-  Continuously improve products through measurement, iteration, and learning.

-  Leverage AI throughout the product lifecycle to improve research, planning, analysis, and execution while maintaining full accountability for every product decision.

What You’ll Bring | Skills & Experience

-  8+ years of product management experience delivering customer-facing digital products.

-  Bachelor’s degree in business, marketing or another related field preferred.

-  Demonstrated success leading products from discovery through measurable customer and business outcomes.

-  Strong experience defining product strategy, prioritization, experimentation, and roadmaps.

-  Excellent analytical, communication, and stakeholder management skills.

-  Ability to navigate ambiguity, make sound product decisions, and influence cross-functional teams.

-  Experience partnering closely with Engineering, Design, and business stakeholders throughout the product lifecycle.

-  High degree of ownership, accountability, and bias toward action.

Preferred Qualifications

-  Experience managing customer-facing e-commerce or omnichannel digital products.

-  Experience with experimentation platforms and modern product analytics tools such as Heap, Google Analytics, Adobe Analytics, Amplitude, Mixpanel, or similar.

-  Demonstrated curiosity for emerging AI technologies with hands-on experience using AI coding tools (e.g., Cursor, Claude Code, GitHub Copilot, or similar) to prototype ideas, automate workflows, and build lightweight solutions.

-  Experience integrating modern SaaS commerce technologies, including search, personalization, content management, payments, customer engagement, or related platforms.
