## About the team
The Integrity team builds systems that help OpenAI understand, prevent, and respond to misuse. Works with Product, Policy, Safety Systems, User Operations, Security, Legal, Privacy, OpenAI for Government, and research teams.

## About the role
Owns Integrity product strategy for sensitive deployments: government, regulated or high-trust enterprise, privacy-constrained (including zero data retention), and multi-step agentic workflows. Also covers high-risk domains like healthcare and reusable agentic detection/enforcement capabilities.

## Responsibilities
- Own the roadmap for sensitive-deployment Integrity controls and readiness criteria.
- Define how to measure residual risk, decision and review quality, and mitigation effectiveness.
- Build agentic investigation and context-assembly workflows for complex misuse.
- Partner across Policy, Legal, Privacy, Safety Systems, User Ops, Government, and product teams.
- Create launch and deployment playbooks for sensitive contexts.

## Preferred background
Experience shipping complex product systems in AI, integrity, trust and safety, security, privacy, risk, government, regulated enterprise, or AI safety. Ability to turn ambiguous policy and threat models into requirements, metrics, and workflows. Interest in agentic AI and responsible deployment.
