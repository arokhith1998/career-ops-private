## About the role

Role: Chief of Staff
Location: Delhi NCR / Field-Heavy
Reporting To: CEO / Founder
Type: Full-time

Overview:
We're looking for a hands-on, detail-obsessed Chief of Staff to ensure daily alignment, follow-through, and performance across all core functions -- Sales (Channel, D2C, Inside), Finance/Underwriting, Customer Support, and Data. You'll work closely with the founder to translate high-level goals into daily execution, ensure teams are coordinated, and build a high-trust, high-accountability work culture.

This is not a desk job -- it's for someone who thrives on making sure things get done, loves solving people/process problems, and can work closely with diverse teams across functions. Your job: zero excuses, zero rework.

Why This Role is Critical:
This role will shape how execution happens at our company as we scale. It's ideal for someone who wants to be part of a founder's core team, cares about outcomes, and wants to help build a lean, execution-obsessed, people-first company from the inside.

Key Responsibilities:
- Drive Execution: Follow up on decisions from founder/leadership meetings and ensure timely execution across functions.
- Track Team Progress: Monitor and report daily/weekly progress of sales, underwriting, support, and data functions.
- Unblock Teams: Coordinate across teams to resolve operational bottlenecks, communication gaps, or ownership issues.
- Lead Reviews: Run daily or weekly huddles with teams to drive accountability and alignment.
- Ensure Visibility: Maintain dashboards or trackers (sheets or tools) for lead conversion, loan processing, customer issues, and other core metrics.
- Culture Champion: Engage with individual team members regularly to ensure a motivated, open, and collaborative work environment.
- Be the Founder's Right Hand: Act as an extension of the CEO to drive clarity, speed, and execution focus across teams.

Ideal Profile:
- 4-8 years of experience in operations, sales execution, or business management roles
- Experience working in startups, EV, fintech, mobility, lending, or logistics preferred
- Can work across teams -- from salespeople and underwriters to support staff and data folks
- Strong interpersonal skills -- knows when to push, when to support, and how to lead without ego
- Excellent with Google Sheets / Excel; comfort with CRM or project tools a plus
- Fluent in Hindi and English
- Bias for action and clarity -- gets things done without getting lost in process
- Graduate from a reputed college is preferred

KPIs:
- % of meeting action items closed on time
- Daily/weekly task closure across teams
- Internal team satisfaction and unblock rate
- Sales-to-finance-to-install TAT
- Operational hygiene (trackers, reviews, reporting)
