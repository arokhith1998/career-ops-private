ABOUT THRIVE MARKET: Thrive Market was founded in 2014 with a mission to make healthy and sustainable living easy and affordable for everyone. As an online, membership-based market, we deliver the highest quality healthy and sustainable products at member-only prices, while matching every paid membership with a free one for someone in need. Over 1,700,000+ members. Certified B Corporation, Public Benefit Corporation, Climate Neutral Certified company.

THE ROLE / WHO THIS PERSON IS: You're a performance marketer with a storyteller's heart. You turn what members tell us into messages, briefs, and ads that bring new members through the door. You pair persuasive storytelling with analytical rigor. You'll own creative strategy for your assigned personas and campaigns, from research and concept development to production feedback, testing, and the next iteration. You'll report to the Head of Performance Creative and partner with Growth Marketing, our in-house Performance Creative team, and outside creative partners.

RESPONSIBILITIES: Own creative strategy for assigned personas and campaigns; develop distinctive hooks, messaging, and narratives; write clear, actionable creative briefs and copy for video ads, static ads, and landing pages; conduct consumer, product, and competitor research; analyze creative performance using Meta Ads Manager, TikTok Ads Manager, and internal reporting; build creative testing plans; lead concept development and brainstorms; partner with designers, editors, creators, and agencies; contribute to creator and shoot briefs; turn test results into actionable learning; maintain a high creative bar.

REQUIREMENTS: At least 4+ years of relevant experience in creative strategy, performance marketing, or direct response creative, ideally within a DTC CPG brand, ecommerce, or subscription business. A portfolio demonstrating successful performance creative. Strong understanding of paid social creative, particularly Meta and TikTok; YouTube a plus. Experience using Meta Ads Manager and other performance reporting tools. Strong creative judgment. Excellent written and verbal communication skills. Strong project management skills. Proficiency with Google Workspace. Bachelor's degree or equivalent relevant experience.

BELONG TO A BETTER COMPANY: Comprehensive health benefits; competitive salary (DOE) + equity; 401k plan; 9 observed holidays; flexible PTO; subsidized ClassPass; office in Playa Vista; free Thrive Market membership; life coaching & therapy coverage.

Employment with Thrive Market requires that employees be based in the United States. This is a condition of employment and must be maintained throughout the duration of employment.

JOB INFORMATION: Total Compensation includes Base Salary, An Annual Bonus, Stock Options, Health & Wellness Benefits, Flexible PTO, and more! This position requires traveling to the HQ office in Los Angeles, California, twice a year for all-company summits (summer and winter).
