Must have demonstrated experience in:

- Sales Operations support for a technology company
- Writing and using SQL queries to investigate business issues
- Root cause analysis of sales process, pricing, promotions, account management, or data quality issues

- Process improvement and workflow automation within Sales Operations

- Investigated and resolved data integrity issues impacting sales forecasting and pipeline reporting.
