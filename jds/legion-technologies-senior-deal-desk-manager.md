Role overview: This is a senior individual contributor role reporting to the Head of Revenue Operations. The person would own how Legion prices, structures, approves, and contracts global deals, and would scale the existing Deal Desk through systems and automation rather than added headcount.

Responsibilities:
- Structure complex new business, expansion, and renewal deals with sales leaders.
- Own the approval matrix, discount guardrails, non-standard terms policy, and approval SLAs.
- Support global deals across currencies, contracting entities, regional terms, tax, and time zones.
- Work with Legal and Finance on contract standards, regional requirements, revenue recognition, and order-to-cash.
- Design AI-centered workflows, such as deal pre-screening, AI-generated approver summaries, and AI-assisted redline review.
- Build internal tools like pricing calculators, quoting tools, and approval automations.
- Set guardrails for AI use, including human approval steps, data handling, and audit trails.
- Own the quoting and contracting stack (CPQ, CLM, DocuSign) with Salesforce as the system of record.
- Build reporting on discount trends, packaging adherence, approval turnaround, and automation impact.
- Present deal analysis in executive pricing reviews and forecast discussions.
- Operationalize pricing and packaging changes with RevOps, Product Marketing, and Finance.

Required qualifications:
- 8+ years in Deal Desk, RevOps, or Sales Ops at B2B SaaS companies, including 3+ years owning Deal Desk operations.
- Experience building Deal Desk processes from scratch at early- or growth-stage companies.
- Hands-on use of AI tools to automate real Deal Desk or RevOps work, with an example to share.
- Experience structuring international SaaS deals across regions, currencies, and legal entities.
- Deep SaaS pricing expertise across subscription, usage-based, consumption-based, and hybrid models.
- Experience implementing CPQ and CLM systems, plus strong Excel or Google Sheets modeling.
- Ability to influence Sales, Finance, Legal, and executives without formal authority.

Preferred qualifications:
- Experience launching AI, agentic, or data product SKUs, or partner/marketplace deal structures.
- Advanced Salesforce CRM proficiency.
- Experience in WFM, HCM, or enterprise SaaS selling to retail, hospitality, or healthcare.
- A builder's mindset, strong ownership, and comfort with ambiguity.
- Flexibility to cover urgent approvals across time zones, especially at quarter-end.

Compensation and benefits:
- Base salary $100,000-$140,000, plus bonus and stock equity. Range may be adjusted by location, experience, and internal equity.
- Benefits: health benefits, paid time off and holidays, parental leave, equity, monthly wellness reimbursement, and monthly lunch.
