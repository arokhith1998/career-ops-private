If you're ready to contribute to something bigger than yourself and help transform the future of healthcare, you'll find your purpose here.

Job Description

Essential Job Duties

Product Strategy and Roadmap Development
- Develop next generation software product roadmap across multiple product franchises in the Ion business units
- Develop a deep knowledge of relevant clinical spaces, including monitoring and consuming clinical literature, attending medical society meetings and engaging with physician key opinion leaders

Market Landscape and Voice of Customer
- Understand competitive, emerging, and complementary technology, and its impact on Intuitive's endoluminal product lines

Marketing Claims
- Develop customer segmentation and marketing claims strategy for next generation product franchises
- Drive cross-functional alignment on pre and post-launch product roadmap and data collection in pre-clinical and clinical settings

Product Feature Development and Prioritization
- Partner with product franchise leads to develop and prioritize software product investigations, features, and product requirements for next generation software
- Partner with UX and Clinical to prototype and assess prototype product solutions. Evaluate critical program requirements and potential product impact

End-to-End Ownership of Software Features
- Own and scope the end-to-end software experience for users across next generation product franchises and global geographies to ensure a unified, consistent, and streamlined user experience
- Drive desirability, feasibility, and viability assessments of next generation software product features to prioritize next generation product portfolio

Business Analysis
- Collect, organize, and leverage datasets to build business cases and drive business decisions for next generation software product lines
- Identify high-leverage software innovations by customer desirability, business viability, and longer-term NPV methodologies

New Technologies and Global Launches
- Partner with engineering and business development teams to assess new technologies, potential partnerships, and prioritize the new business development software pipeline
- Collaborate with regional teams to ensure that product features and NextGen portfolio align with user needs across geographies
