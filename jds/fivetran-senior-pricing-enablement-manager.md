## Full JD Text (verbatim via browser-extract.mjs, NY posting; TX/CO identical body except location/pay band)
Fivetran and dbt Labs are bringing together two companies with a shared mission: helping organizations unlock the full value of their data. Pricing at the company is evolving quickly, and sellers need to keep pace. Seeking a Senior Pricing Enablement Manager to translate packaging, curve, consumption, and ELA updates into clear enablement: what changed, why, what sellers can offer, and when approval is required. Owns the enablement timeline across Pricing, Product, Deal Desk, and Product Marketing; uses field feedback to help shape future pricing decisions. Full-time remote, preference for candidates near New York, Austin, or Denver.

Technologies used: Thought Industries, Salesforce, Gong, Notion, Claude.

What you'll do:
- Partner with Pricing and Product Operations on enablement for net-new packaging, curve, discounts, consumption, bundles, and ELA changes; build multi-modal enablement delivered via live sessions, leader briefings, and LMS content.
- Translate approved pricing policies into clear seller guidance; work with Strat Ops, Pricing, and Deal Desk to confirm quoting/approval/CPQ workflows match policy; train the field on system changes.
- Support post-launch reinforcement using deal reviews, Deal Desk escalations, call recordings, and sales input; iterate on reinforcement until seller behavior changes.
- Maintain a single enablement timeline across Deal Desk, Pricing, Product, and Product Marketing; run a regular cadence to surface upcoming changes early.
- Gather structured input from Sales, Customer Success, and field leaders on pricing effectiveness and operational challenges; synthesize into recommendations.
- Bring field insights to Pricing Strategy, Product, and Sales leadership.

Skills required: 5+ years in sales enablement, product marketing, pricing, deal desk, or sales strategy at a B2B software company; experience enabling sellers on complex pricing/packaging/consumption-based models; understanding of how pricing/packaging flows into CPQ and Salesforce; ability to build cross-team trust; skill designing/delivering learning across formats; analytical approach to field feedback; strong written/verbal communication; ownership and judgment managing multiple priorities.

Benefits: 100% employer-paid medical insurance, generous PTO/flexible vacation, paid sick time, inclusive parental leave, paid holidays plus year-end Global Week of Rest, volunteer days, RSU stock grants, professional development funds, monthly cell phone stipend, mental health support platform.
