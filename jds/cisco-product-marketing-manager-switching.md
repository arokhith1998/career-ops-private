Meet the Team
Join Cisco's Networking Product Marketing team and help shape how customers understand and adopt the technologies that connect their workplaces. We work across product management, engineering, sales, and marketing to turn networking innovation into clear customer value, relevant stories, and effective go-to-market programs.

As a Product Marketing Manager supporting Cisco's switching and campus connectivity portfolio, you'll contribute to messaging, product launches, content, competitive positioning, and field enablement. You'll work with an experienced, collaborative team while building your expertise in Cisco's networking portfolio and the needs of our customers.

Your Impact
In this role, you will lead the go-to-market strategy for our switching and campus connectivity portfolio. By partnering across product, engineering, and sales, you will influence portfolio positioning, equip partners with high-impact enablement, and drive industry conversations that showcase Cisco's resilient, AI-ready infrastructure.

Key Responsibilities
- Strategy & Messaging: Develop differentiated positioning for switching and campus solutions by translating complex technical capabilities into measurable customer outcomes.
- Launch & GTM Execution: Orchestrate integrated go-to-market programs, product launches, and announcements to maintain portfolio momentum.
- Content Creation & Enablement: Deliver high-impact sales assets, including presentations, talk tracks, and solution briefs, to empower field and partner teams.
- Cross-Functional Leadership: Partner with internal stakeholders, including product management, engineering, and digital marketing, to synthesize market insights and drive portfolio growth.
- Performance Tracking: Measure campaign effectiveness and industry developments to inform future messaging, competitive strategies, and overall business priorities.

Minimum Qualifications
- Bachelor's degree or an equivalent combination of education, skills, and experience
- 6+ years of experience in product marketing, technical marketing, product management, solutions marketing, or related discipline
- 3+ years of experience working with networking, enterprise infrastructure, cybersecurity, cloud, or related technology portfolios
- Experience translating technical information into messaging for customers, sellers, or partners
- Experience collaborating across product management, engineering, sales, and marketing organizations
- Experience developing content and enablement materials that support product launches, customer engagement, or revenue growth

Preferred Qualifications
- Familiarity with enterprise switching, campus networking, security, and cloud-managed ecosystems
- Knowledge of emerging trends including AI workloads, post-quantum cryptography, digital twins, and edge computing
- Proven success in project management, B2B storytelling, and navigating complex, multi-stakeholder organizations
- A resourceful, self-driven approach to solving obstacles and managing shifting market priorities
- Willingness to travel up to 25%

We've been innovating fearlessly for 40 years to create solutions that power how humans and technology work together across the physical and digital worlds.
