## Job Description

RxSense is a healthcare technology company offering SaaS platforms for pharmacy benefit management. The Manager of Pricing leads pricing administration for Enterprise clients and pharmacy partners, with MAC (maximum allowable cost) management as the core of the role. Responsibilities: create/maintain MAC lists (including client-specific), manage MAC factor adjustments with quality checks before go-live; monitor pricing performance against client/pharmacy guarantees and develop corrective strategies; set up pricing for new/renewing clients; handle MAC escalations and complex pricing tickets; review contract terms for pricing implications; support quarterly/annual reconciliations; partner with Network Operations, Clinical, Underwriting, AI/data teams including adopting automated pricing tools; lead/coach the pricing team. Qualifications: hands-on MAC management experience in a PBM or pharmacy setting; bachelor's degree in finance/business/economics or related field; 5+ years of PBM or retail pharmacy pricing experience including 2+ years in a supervisory/lead role; advanced Excel; SQL/Tableau/BI tools and AI-enabled pricing tools preferred.
