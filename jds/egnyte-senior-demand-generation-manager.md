ABOUT EGNYTE
Egnyte is the secure multi-cloud platform for content security and
governance that enables organizations to better protect and collaborate on
their most valuable content. Established in 2008, Egnyte has democratized
cloud content security for more than 22,000 organizations, helping customers
improve data security, maintain compliance, prevent and detect ransomware
threats, and boost employee productivity on any app, any cloud, anywhere.

What You'll Do

- Strategy & Execution: Architect comprehensive, end-to-end demand
  generation strategies that align multi-channel campaigns - including
  webinars, email nurtures, and paid media - with pipeline requirements
  across horizontal and vertical-specific markets (e.g., AEC, FSI, Life
  Science, Media & Ent). Lead the end-to-end lifecycle - from initial brief
  and creative direction to execution, launch, and measurement - ensuring
  every touchpoint is optimized for engagement and conversion.
- Cross-Functional Orchestration: Serve as the central strategic hub,
  orchestrating initiatives across field marketing, product marketing,
  content strategy, and web development teams. Drive organizational
  alignment by establishing clear campaign objectives, defining creative
  direction, and setting measurable performance targets (e.g., lead volume,
  MQLs, SQLs) for execution teams to guarantee cohesive messaging and a
  unified market approach.
- Performance & Optimization: Own comprehensive, end-to-end campaign
  reporting and attribution. Actively monitor funnel health to identify
  friction points and bottlenecks; execute real-time optimizations based on
  data-driven insights. Rigorously analyze lead volume, quality, and
  velocity to consistently exceed quarterly pipeline targets while
  optimizing for ROI.
- Sales & BDR Alignment: Partner with BDRs and SDRs to ensure they are
  enabled for effective lead qualification and outreach sequences.
  Maintain a continuous, bidirectional feedback loop to improve lead
  quality, resolve conversion bottlenecks, and ensure messaging alignment
  across the funnel.
- Budget & Strategic Innovation: Manage demand gen budgets with
  accountability for forecasting and resource allocation. Spearhead the
  integration of AI-driven solutions to enhance advanced audience
  targeting, enable content personalization at scale, and drive more
  predictive performance analysis and forecasting.
