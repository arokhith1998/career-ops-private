About The Role: Marketing team shapes how the world sees/experiences Attio -- acquisition to brand to product marketing. - Bring customer stories to life: case studies, video, web, social.

- Partner with EPD: translate what's built into customer outcomes; bring market signal back into building.
- Champion the customer: in-house expert on who buys, why, and what's next.
- Sharpen competitive edge: know the market cold, where Attio wins/loses against legacy CRMs and new challengers.

What you'll bring:
- 6+ years of relevant experience in product marketing.
- Great writer (positioning, web copy, scripts, case studies).
- Great taste, even if not a designer by trade.
- Bias for speed; owns projects start to finish.
- Builder mindset -- defines playbooks, doesn't inherit them.
- Fluent in AI.

Stand out if: produced beautiful work across formats (video/web); prior experience at a design-led company.

What we offer: competitive range $145,000-$240,000; equity in an early-stage tech company; 25 days holiday plus local public holidays; Apple hardware; medical/dental/vision through Sequoia One; 401K; enhanced family leave; team off-sites (Barcelona, Lisbon, Malta, Split).
