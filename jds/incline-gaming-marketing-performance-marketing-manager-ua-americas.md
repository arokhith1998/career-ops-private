## Full JD text (verbatim)

As Performance Marketing Manager (User Acquisition), you will own paid acquisition end to end across Incline's client portfolio. That means defining UA strategy, planning channel mix and budgets, running campaigns hands-on inside Meta, Google, Apple Search Ads and programmatic networks, testing creative and bid strategies, and reporting daily, weekly and monthly against CPI, CPA, LTV and payback targets. You will work across multiple operator brands rather than a single product. You will also help shape the UA playbook as the agency scales its client base in a booming regulated market.

**What You Will Do**
- Define and execute user acquisition strategy aligned with each client's goals
- Plan channel mix, budgets and performance targets across the portfolio
- Execute and optimize paid campaigns across Meta, Google, Apple Search Ads and other networks
- Own creative testing, bid strategy, targeting and optimization
- Troubleshoot under-performance and scale winning campaigns
- Monitor performance against CPI, CPA, LTV and payback targets
- Produce accurate reporting and translate data into actionable insight
- Collaborate cross-functionally with CRM, creative and partner-success teams

**What You Need**
- Hands-on paid user acquisition experience in performance-driven, regulated categories. Sportsbook or online casino experience is a plus, not a requirement.
- Proven track record scaling paid UA budgets across Meta, Google, Tik Tok and Apple Search Ads.
- Strong command of down-funnel metrics: CTR, CVR, CPI, ARPU, retention, LTV.
- Solid knowledge of tagging, tracking and attribution across app and web funnels.
- Experience owning UA strategy, not just executing someone else's plan.
- Agency or multi-market experience is a plus.
- Comfort with AI tools for creative ideation, analysis, reporting automation or audience research.
- Ability to thrive in a fast-paced, global environment.

"If you want high autonomy over channel strategy and budgets, a portfolio of operator clients instead of a single brand, and the chance to help shape how a growing specialist agency runs UA, this is worth a conversation."
