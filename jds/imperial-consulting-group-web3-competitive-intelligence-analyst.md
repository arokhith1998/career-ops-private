Company Description:

 Imperial Consulting Group is a student-led, pro-bono consulting organization associated with Imperial College London, focused on strategy consulting, market research, business strategy, and business development. The group works on projects involving market entry, competitive analysis, partnerships, and growth strategy.

Role Description:

 We are hiring a Web3 Competitive Intelligence Analyst (Remote, US) to research the rapidly evolving blockchain and digital asset landscape and provide strategic insights to support consulting engagements.

This role focuses on monitoring Web3 markets, analyzing competitors, evaluating emerging technologies and business models, and translating market intelligence into actionable strategic insights for clients.

Responsibilities:

- Monitor Web3, blockchain, cryptocurrency, DeFi, and digital asset markets.
- Conduct competitive intelligence research on Web3 companies, protocols, platforms, and emerging technologies.
- Analyze competitors’ products, business models, market positioning, partnerships, funding activity, and growth strategies.
- Track industry trends, regulatory developments, technological developments, and changes in market structure.
- Develop competitor profiles, market maps, industry reports, and strategic research briefs.
- Evaluate new Web3 business models and identify emerging opportunities and competitive threats.
- Conduct market sizing, segmentation, benchmarking, and comparative analysis.
- Research blockchain ecosystems, Layer 1 and Layer 2 networks, DeFi protocols, stablecoins, wallets, exchanges, and Web3 infrastructure.
- Monitor venture funding, M&A activity, strategic partnerships, product launches, and ecosystem developments.
- Translate complex market data into clear insights and recommendations for consulting projects.
- Support market-entry, growth strategy, and business-development engagements with research and competitive intelligence.
- Prepare presentations, dashboards, and written reports for clients and internal stakeholders.

Qualifications:

- Strong understanding of Web3, blockchain technology, cryptocurrency markets, and digital asset ecosystems.
- Experience in competitive intelligence, market research, strategy consulting, business analysis, or investment research is preferred.
- Strong analytical and research skills with the ability to evaluate large amounts of information.
- Knowledge of DeFi, stablecoins, tokenomics, Layer 1 and Layer 2 ecosystems, and Web3 infrastructure.
- Ability to conduct structured competitor analysis and identify meaningful market trends.
- Strong financial, quantitative, and commercial analysis skills.
- Excellent written and verbal communication skills.
- Ability to present complex technical and market information clearly.
- Strong attention to detail and ability to validate information across multiple sources.
- Proficiency with Excel, Google Sheets, PowerPoint, or comparable analytical and presentation tools.
- Experience with blockchain analytics platforms, market intelligence tools, or on-chain data is a plus.
- Ability to work independently and manage multiple research projects in a remote environment.

Benefits:

- Fully remote work environment within the United States.
- Opportunity to work at the intersection of strategy consulting, Web3, blockchain, and digital assets.
- Exposure to real-world competitive intelligence, market research, and strategic consulting projects.
- Opportunity to develop expertise across emerging blockchain markets and technologies.
- Professional development opportunities in strategy, consulting, market intelligence, and Web3.
- Collaborative environment with exposure to diverse business models, industries, and emerging technologies.
- Opportunity to build a specialized career in Web3 strategy and competitive intelligence.

﻿Compensation:

 This position offers a salary range of $90,000–$120,000 USD per year depending on experience, analytical expertise, and Web3 industry knowledge.
