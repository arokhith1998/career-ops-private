## Full Job Description

### About Column
For companies building financial technology and transforming the financial services space, the biggest bottleneck to their growth and innovation is often the underlying banks and infrastructure stack they rely on. We have spent our careers founding and scaling companies like Plaid, Square, Meta, Blend, and Affirm, and have seen this problem firsthand -- builders and developers needing to partner with traditional banks, and creating API and abstraction layers over the patchwork that is the bank, its core, and many other vendors. All of this results in a complex (and often expensive) banking supply chain involving a user, fintech, BaaS middleware provider, bank, core and the Federal Reserve.

At Column, we set out to simplify and fix this. We are a bank and a software company built from the ground up, offering builders and developers technology-forward banking solutions that cut out the hundreds of vendors, middleware providers, and abstraction layers.

### The opportunity
Column moves over $4 trillion a year for some of the most sophisticated fintechs and financial institutions in the world. In this role, you'll be the person who makes sure that volume stays safe -- and you'll be the first Product Manager Column has ever hired to do it.

There's no existing roadmap to inherit, no legacy backlog, no "how we've always done it." You'll be defining what product management looks like at Column, starting with one of the most consequential challenges the company is facing: turning risk from a manual, tribal-knowledge function into a real internal product -- the infrastructure that lets Column say yes to more volume, more partners, and more markets without opening the door to fraudsters, criminal networks, or state adversaries.

This role will live within our Product & Engineering org and is based in-person at our Presidio office in San Francisco (3-4 days a week).

### What you'll do
- Own the roadmap for a brand-new Risk platform at Column, turning a vision into a shipped product.
- Figure out the best way to weave AI into the platform -- decide which risk-related judgment calls a machine should make, and which should always stay human.
- Work directly with the Founder/CEO, General Counsel, and Chief Compliance Officer to understand user needs and define product requirements.
- Write the specs and PRDs that you and Column's Engineering team will use to bring the product to life -- jump in yourself to push PRs and features where you can.
- Define the metrics that prove the platform is working, and use them to inform what gets built next.
- Grow this from an internal platform into an external product that customers can plug into.

### What you'll need to be successful
- 4+ years in a high agency, fast moving role (product management or adjacent role at a high-growth startup/tech company, top-tier management consulting firm, or investment firm).
- A track record of taking a messy, undefined problem and turning it into something that actually shipped.
- Ability to get technical very quickly -- comfortable in the terminal, using Claude Code or similar tools, and understanding how to architect solutions.
- Strong analytical instincts.
- Scrappy, roll-up-your-sleeves attitude.
- Familiarity with banking and fintech preferred, but not required.

### What you'll get from us
Comprehensive health/dental/vision, family planning and fertility benefits (Carrot), rent subsidy near office, FSA/HSA, 401k, flexible time off, paid parental leave, backup childcare reimbursement, catered meals for SF employees, commuter benefits, team events.

### Pay transparency
The annual base salary range for this position is $140,000 - $220,000 + equity.
