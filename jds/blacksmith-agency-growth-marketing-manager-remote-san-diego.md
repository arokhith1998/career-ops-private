## Description Summary
Blacksmith Agency is growing its marketing division to serve PPC and SEO clients in multifamily, B2B, and technical sectors. The Growth Marketing Manager owns strategy, performance, and client leadership across paid search, paid social, SEO, and conversion optimization; mixes strategy and hands-on oversight (joining sales calls, supervising internal/external specialists, building growth roadmaps, reporting results to clients).

Key responsibilities: lead strategy for PPC and SEO clients across industries; join sales calls to validate scope and support closing deals; create 90-day, 6-month, and annual growth roadmaps per client; manage performance across Google Ads, Meta, Microsoft, and organic search; audit campaigns, keywords, bidding, landing pages, and CRO opportunities; oversee technical SEO, on-page SEO, link building, programmatic pages, and AI-driven content; own KPI tracking, attribution, and analytics accuracy; provide weekly direction and quality control, coordinating with a marketing project manager; present monthly/quarterly performance reports to clients.

Qualifications: 4 to 7 years in PPC, SEO, or multi-channel strategy; experience running paid and organic channels together; proficiency with Google Ads, GA4, Google Search Console, keyword research tools, and paid media optimization; ability to communicate strategy to clients and account executives; experience overseeing execution teams and deliverable quality; strong analytical skills.

Nice to have: agency experience managing multiple clients, sales-call involvement, familiarity with multifamily or real estate lead generation, experience with AI SEO, programmatic SEO, technical audits, or CRO.
