Opportunity Overview
As a Senior Product Manager - Optical Instrumentation & Automation, you will lead the strategy, definition, commercialization, and lifecycle management of high-volume optical testing solutions supporting the optical communications and E-O transceiver markets. You will work cross-functionally with Engineering, Applications, Sales, and Operations teams to deliver innovative instrumentation and automation solutions that address evolving customer requirements and drive business growth.

Key Responsibilities
Product Strategy & Definition
- Develop and execute the product strategy for high-volume E-O transceiver testing solutions, including both instrumentation and automation needs.
- Define product specifications, features, and interfaces based on customer requirements and market trends.
- Lead the product lifecycle, from concept and development to validation, go-to-market launch, and post-sales support.
- Manage the product portfolio, including sampling scopes and general-purpose optical instruments, ensuring alignment with customer needs and business objectives.

Customer Engagement & Market Research
- Collaborate with the Sales team to gather customer feedback on required features and functionality to develop winning products.
- Act as the voice of the customer, ensuring their needs are reflected in product development and prioritization.
- Conduct market research and competitive analysis to identify trends, opportunities, and areas for differentiation.

Product Development & Commercialization
- Develop product specifications, collateral, pricing strategies, commercial targets, and price list to support go-to-market efforts.
- Work closely with the Engineering and Applications teams to prioritize software release planning, new feature development, and general product support.
- Partner with Operations to ensure smooth product delivery and address specification or manufacturability challenges.

Sales Enablement & Support
- Create and deliver sales training materials, including product presentations, datasheets, and competitive positioning guides.
- Support the Sales team in customer engagements, acting as a liaison between Sales, Engineering, and Operations.
- Provide technical and commercial expertise to drive customer adoption and satisfaction.

Industry Representation & Thought Leadership
- Represent the company at industry events, conferences, and trade shows to promote products, gather market insights, and build relationships with key stakeholders.
- Stay informed about emerging technologies and market trends to ensure the product roadmap remains competitive and innovative.
- Represent Teradyne at industry conferences, trade shows, and customer events to strengthen market presence and gather industry insights.
- Monitor emerging technologies and market developments to maintain an innovative and competitive product roadmap.

Technical Expertise

Skills & Competencies
