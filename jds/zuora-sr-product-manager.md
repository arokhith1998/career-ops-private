About Zuora

At Zuora, we help businesses grow smarter and adapt faster. Our platform powers modern business models -- from subscriptions and usage-based pricing to AI-driven and outcome-based offerings -- helping companies launch new products, automate complex billing, and unlock predictable, recurring revenue.

We've led the Subscription Economy for more than a decade. Now we're evolving again by building the definitive platform for quote to cash and helping companies monetize their products and services with an adaptable, AI-ready foundation.

The Opportunity

If you are looking to help the world's best companies win through total monetization and drive impactful innovations in the payments space, we have a very important role to offer. We are looking for a seasoned and visionary Product Manager to join our Product & Technology team and play a key role in shaping the product offerings in payments. This role offers fantastic opportunities to work with wider ecosystems like payment providers, banks & various partners, translating market needs into feature sets, and working closely with engineering, product marketing, sales, and customer success teams to deliver world-class solutions.

Your key responsibilities:
- Pioneer AI & Agentic Product Strategy: define and execute the product roadmap for AI-driven automation, agentic workflows, and reasoning-driven features. Track frontier model breakthroughs and rapidly prototype high-leverage enterprise solutions. Prioritize roadmap to support key business objectives and corporate strategy.
- Simplify Complex Workflows: Deconstruct ambiguous, multi-step enterprise processes into modular systems solved by autonomous agents, deterministic APIs, and modern UIs.
- Strategic Leadership: Spearhead the product strategy, product management and execution of cutting-edge payment solutions. Stay attuned to the dynamic needs of the business and address the pain points of our customers with innovative approaches.
- Customer-Centric Approach: Engage directly with customers to understand their needs, and translate pain points into AI solutions. Break down and simplify complex payments concepts for customers, partners, and internal stakeholders.
- Stakeholder Management: Collaborate with internal and external stakeholders such as engineering, design, product marketing, solution management, legal, sales, customer success, as well as customers and partners, and industry analysts. Deliver high impact presentations, pitches and workshops to diverse audiences, including customers, partners, and employees.
- Market Insights: Stay abreast of market trends and emerging technologies
- Product Launch and Lifecycle Management: Plan and execute product launches, including coordination with marketing and product marketing. Monitor product performance post-launch and iterate based on feedback and metrics. Manage the entire product lifecycle, from conception to maturity.
- Result oriented: Ability to connect the dots and align the product outcomes with product strategy & corporate strategy. Define product objectives & deliver key results.

About You

What experience makes you excel in this role:
- Demonstrated AI fluency: You actively use, prototype with, and test modern LLMs, autonomous agents, and developer tooling.
- Systems & API Thinking: Solid understanding of modern software architecture, API design, data pipelines, and scalable cloud platforms. Experience with agentic frameworks, multi-agent orchestration, or autonomous tool-calling systems.
- 5-8+ years of experience in product management with a track record of taking initiatives from idea to market.
- Excellent analytical and problem-solving skills.
- Outstanding communication, storytelling, & interpersonal skills. Emphasis on distilling complex concepts and problems into simple narratives.
- Proven track record of successfully launching and scaling software products.

Nice to haves:
- Bachelor's / Master's degree in Computer Science or related field.

Zuora Payments offers cutting-edge technology in orchestrating and optimizing payments for businesses across the globe. By streamlining the payment operations and abstracting the complexities of the payments ecosystem, we help companies capture every dollar flowing through their business. Zuora Payments adds value to businesses in acquiring, engaging and retaining customers by providing the choice of payments and reducing involuntary churn.
