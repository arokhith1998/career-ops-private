## Description (full text as rendered by LinkedIn)

We're looking for an experienced Consumer Product Marketing Manager to join our product marketing team, and to support our global audience. As a Consumer Product Marketer at Meta, you will help grow products used by billions of people all over the world across our apps, including Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, Threads, and Reality Labs, and drive complex cross-company initiatives.We're looking for a seasoned, strategic data-driven, consumer-focused Product Marketing Manager to join our global team. This role is responsible for leading inbound and outbound product marketing initiatives. You will be responsible for influencing product strategies and positioning for target audiences, leading go-to-market efforts that drive awareness and adoption, and working cross-functionally with partners at various altitudes throughout the company. If you are a strategic marketer who is analytical and creative, have experience leading complex projects in environments with evolving priorities and timelines, and are passionate about connecting consumers with great products, then we'd like to connect with you.

Consumer Product Marketing Manager, Cross-Meta Ecosystem Responsibilities:

Influence and shape product strategies, positioning and narratives through the use of data and insights for target audiences in close collaboration with research, product, comms and policyDevelop and execute on go-to-market strategies including goals, metrics for success, asset development and integrated launch planning with cross functional partnersPartner with brand and integrated marketing on outbound marketing and advertising strategies and programs to drive awareness, adoption and long-term engagementTackle complex problems with a strategic mindset, and create thoughtful recommendations and action plans for solving those problems

Minimum Qualifications:

5+ years of experience in consumer or product marketingDirect experience in Product Marketing, partnering closely with Product teamsExperience with consumer GTM strategies based on consumer driven insightsExperience working with product, marketing, policy and communications teams at all levels of management, both internally and externallyExperience driving presentations with executives and cross functional teamsExperience with consumer facing product launches and consumer campaigns, from strategy through execution to deliver business impactExperience developing and driving analytically rigorous online and offline marketing campaigns with a variety of internal teams and external stakeholdersProject management experienceBachelor's Degree in Business, Marketing or Creative discipline, or equivalent practical experience

Preferred Qualifications:

Demonstrated ability to integrate AI tools to optimize/redesign workflows and drive measurable impact (e.g., efficiency gains, quality improvements)Experience adhering to and implementing responsible, ethical AI practices (e.g., risk assessment, bias mitigation, quality and accuracy reviews)Internet or technology industry experienceMBADemonstrated ongoing AI skill development (e.g., prompt/context engineering, agent orchestration) and staying current with emerging AI technologies
