DescriptionThis highly visible role will work across AWS Software & Technology partners globally to develop and implement strategic plans that include building awareness, generating demand and driving pipeline and revenue. The ideal candidate will have a deep understanding of B2B marketing strategies, coupled with hands-on experience in integrated marketing, digital demand generation, and partner or alliance marketing. You will be the subject matter expert for marketing partner solutions with and through partners across sales segments and geographies.The role will partner with product, field, brand, digital, partner marketing teams and agencies to define and execute a global demand, content and campaign strategy that drives acquisition and engagement. You will develop targeted marketing programs aligned with business priorities that engage and nurture prospects throughout the buyer's journey, measuring and optimizing performance against goals and ultimately driving conversion and revenue growth. You will work closely with partners and our Geo based partner teams to help execute these programs regionally and be an expert resource on marketing processes and guidelines for partners.This role requires a proven leader with the demonstrated ability to think strategically to build and convey compelling value propositions and content with technology partners. They will work closely with partners, partner management and marketing teams to identify specific use-cases, customer personas and industry verticals to build and deliver joint value propositions for using AWS.The ideal candidate will have creative ideas, extensive analytic and data-driven customer demand generation experience and a proven track record of driving successful demand generation programs. The ability to effectively communicate across both technical and non-technical audiences is critical. You should be detail-oriented and organized, capable of handling multiple projects at once, and comfortable dealing with ambiguity and rapidly changing priorities. A solid understanding of the cloud landscape and software providers are critical to this role.At AWS we prize innovation, agility and a builder mentality. The organization is purposefully flat to enable fast decision-making and we are empowered to resolve issues, deliver new ideas and optimize existing ones that serve our customers. Scaling through partners is a key part of how we help customers move to cloud services that power strategic workloads.
