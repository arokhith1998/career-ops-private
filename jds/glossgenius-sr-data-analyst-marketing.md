## Job Description
Enhance the Go-to-Market team's analytical capabilities. Partner with VP of Growth Marketing to strengthen marketing attribution systems, deep dive into customer acquisition funnels, and improve overall efficiency of the growth machine.

## Responsibilities
- Execute high-impact campaigns, experiments, and projects across GTM teams
- Identify optimization opportunities through funnel analysis and customer performance data
- Develop and refine multi-touch attribution models and channel lift-testing
- Define and analyze marketing metrics: CAC, LTV, payback period
- Implement analytical frameworks and testing structures with data platform teams
- Leverage AI tools and automation to scale analysis
- Advise leadership on strategic customer base growth

## Requirements
- 5+ years in a highly analytical role, 2+ years in marketing analytics or science (ideally SaaS)
- Demonstrated AI tool usage (Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor)
- Strong grasp of marketing experimentation lifecycle and A/B testing
- Statistical expertise: regression, conversion rate modeling, media mix modeling
- SQL and Python proficiency
- SQL skill level self-assessment required

## Preferred
- Experience with Hex, Looker, or Tableau
- Familiarity with Iterable, HubSpot, Eppo, and similar martech platforms
