About LocutaLocuta is a live AI-powered communication coaching platform that helps individuals improve their speaking skills through structured practice and personalized AI feedback. We are building our long-term core team to scale the product and business.
About the RoleWe are looking for a high-ownership Strategy & Operations Lead to work directly with the founder.
This is not an executive assistant role. You will take ownership of business priorities, strategy execution, operations, and cross-functional initiatives. We are looking for someone who can think strategically, execute independently, and challenge ideas constructively.
This is a long-term core team opportunity. Candidates must be willing and able to transition into a full-time role following funding.
Key Responsibilities- Work with the founder on business strategy, planning, and execution.
- Own business operations, internal processes, and cross-functional coordination.
- Conduct market research, competitor analysis, and business analysis.
- Drive strategic initiatives, partnerships, and business priorities.
- Coordinate across product, technology, marketing, and GTM teams.
- Support recruitment, team building, and investor-related preparation.
- Identify bottlenecks, solve problems, and ensure execution progress.
What We're Looking For- 1–2 years of experience in strategy, business operations, startup operations, or a related role.
- Strong analytical, problem-solving, and communication skills.
- Ability to manage multiple priorities and work independently.
- Strong ownership, accountability, and follow-through.
- Comfortable working in an early-stage startup environment.
Preferred: Experience in a startup, Founder's Office, product management, growth, or business development. Familiarity with AI or SaaS is a plus.
Long-Term Opportunity & CompensationLocuta is pre-funding, and we are building a core team committed to growing with the company.
The full-time compensation package following funding will include:
- Competitive salary
- Joining and/or performance-based bonus
- ESOPs
- Employee benefits
- Long-term leadership growth opportunities
Engagement and compensation terms will be discussed with shortlisted candidates.
How to ApplySend your resume, LinkedIn profile, and a brief introduction explaining your relevant experience and interest in Locuta's Founder's Office to info@locuta.in.
We are looking for someone who wants to build alongside the founder, take ownership, and help shape Locuta's future.
