About The Team

The Field Pricing Strategy team is focused on driving adoption of new and existing pricing and contracting approaches through Workday. We support our team of global account executives with a "tier 2" level of deal specific support, guide our Revenue Operations deal teams on new pricing model and practices, and help mold the shape of new offerings as they come to market.

About The Role

The Field Pricing Manager will play a key role on Workday's Pricing team, focusing on the Sales and Revenue Operations facing activities of SaaS and AI pricing; including deal specific support, global commercial strategy, enablement activities, instructional content creation, new product support and performance analytics. You'll be challenged to execute on both tactical and strategic initiatives; such as assisting in tier-2 deal support one minute and then introducing negotiation strategy to our global sales team the next. You'll be tasked with solving mysteries on performance trends, and then presenting back to our leadership team proposals for next steps. Cross functional partnership is key as you'll partner with stakeholders from across the organization to accomplish critical objectives.

Key Responsibilities:
- Support sales teams (including sales leadership) and revenue operations on deal pricing strategy during negotiation cycles
- Conduct extensive analysis on commercial selling performance; including trend analysis, data model construction and recommendations for impactful change
- Drive sales effectiveness and alignment to global pricing policies
- Partner with product pricing team on bringing new products to market; including influencing all aspects of the quote to cash process
- Create impactful educational content/presentations a part of new product and strategic initiative rollouts; and then deliver such content to wide audiences
- Participate in system testing of our quoting platform for both pricing and Order Form accuracy
- Assist in coordinating detailed technical and business requirements related to Commercial Order Form improvement with internal and external partners
