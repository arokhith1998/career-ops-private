## Full Job Description

### About Poshmark
Poshmark is the leading fashion marketplace where style comes alive through discovery, self-expression, and human connection. Powered by a vibrant community of 165 million members.

### About the Role
Poshmark is seeking a strategic and execution-oriented Sr. Manager, Product Marketing to lead go-to-market initiatives and lifecycle marketing programs that drive customer growth, engagement, and retention across the marketplace.

This role sits at the intersection of Product, Growth, Creative, and Community. The Sr. Product Marketing Manager will own launch strategy and communications for new features/platform updates, and develop lifecycle strategies supporting customers from onboarding through long-term engagement.

### Responsibilities

**Go-to-Market Strategy & Execution**
- Lead end-to-end GTM for features and platform initiatives -- positioning, messaging, channel strategy, launch execution, measurement.
- Translate product capabilities into crisp customer value propositions.
- Partner with Product, Creative, CRM, and Growth to ship launches that hit business targets.

**Customer Lifecycle Marketing**
- Design, optimize, and scale customer lifecycle programs across onboarding, activation, engagement, retention, reactivation.
- Identify key behavioral moments for intervention.
- Partner with CRM and Growth on lifecycle journeys across email, push, in-app messaging.

**Customer Positioning & Messaging**
- Develop and maintain customer-facing messaging frameworks and positioning strategies.
- Create channel-specific messaging and copy.
- Ensure consistency of voice and positioning across touchpoints.

**Customer & Competitive Insights**
- Be the internal authority on customer motivations, segments, and behaviors.
- Monitor competitive landscape; translate findings into strategic recommendations.

**Cross-Functional Leadership**
- Act as a strategic bridge between Product, Growth, Creative, CRM, and Community teams.
- Model excellent PMM work for a developing team.

### Requirements
- 5-8 years of experience in Product Marketing, Product Management, Lifecycle Marketing, or related strategic marketing functions with a track record of positioning, launch strategy, lifecycle programs, customer insights.
- Strong experience leading both GTM initiatives and lifecycle/retention programs within a consumer app, marketplace, platform, or e-commerce environment.
- Experience marketing to customer, creator, supply-side, or marketplace communities.
- Proven GTM instincts; deep familiarity with lifecycle channels (email, push, in-app); sharp strategic thinker; exceptional writer across formats.

### Nice to Have
Background in resale/fashion/peer-to-peer marketplace; hands-on Klaviyo (or comparable) experience; customer education/community-led growth experience; prior experience managing/mentoring PMMs.
