## Full JD Text (verbatim)

...a central point of coordination, helping drive execution, track progress, and ensure key decisions and action items are followed through -- even when the Founder is unavailable.

**Key Responsibilities**

- Business Operations: Oversee and review progress across departments, identify gaps, and ensure teams are aligned with business priorities.
- Execution & Follow-ups: Track meetings, decisions, action items, and deadlines to ensure timely completion and accountability.
- Founder's Office: Act as a trusted point of contact and support business continuity in the Founder's absence.
- Program Management: Coordinate cross-functional projects, monitor progress, resolve bottlenecks, and ensure smooth execution.
- Leadership Meetings: Organize key meetings, prepare agendas, document discussions, and maintain clear action trackers.
- Board Meetings: Coordinate board meeting preparation, maintain meeting notes, and track decisions and follow-up actions.
- Fundraising Support: Assist with investment-raising activities, investor coordination, and preparation of relevant business information.
- Finance & Compliance Coordination: Work closely with the CA and CS teams on financial, compliance, and corporate matters.
- Process Improvement: Review existing workflows and help establish efficient systems, reporting structures, and operating processes.

**Requirements**

- 6+ years of experience in business operations, program management, strategy, Founder's Office, or a similar role.
- High integrity, discretion, and trustworthiness when handling confidential business information.
- Strong ownership, organizational, and execution skills.
- Excellent communication, coordination, and stakeholder-management abilities.
- Ability to manage multiple priorities and work independently in a fast-paced environment.
- Experience coordinating with leadership teams, finance, and compliance stakeholders.
- Strong problem-solving skills and the ability to turn discussions into actionable plans.

**Ideal Candidate**

Someone who can take ownership, connect the dots across departments, bring structure to business operations, and ensure that important decisions translate into action -- with minimal supervision.
