- Lead territory planning and capacity analysis
- Support compensation planning and administration
- Translate business requirements into scalable CRM and BI solutions
- Eliminate operational bottlenecks across the sales cycle
- Establish data governance and consistent metric definitions

- Use AI tools and automation to reduce manual work

Requirements:
- "5+ years of experience in Sales Operations, Revenue Operations, GTM Strategy...preferably in a high-growth technology or SaaS company."
- Strong understanding of sales processes, forecasting, pipeline management, quota and territory planning, and compensation
- Proficiency in Excel/Sheets, BI tools (Tableau, Looker, Power BI), and Salesforce CRM familiarity
- Commercial judgment, cross-functional program management, and "AI fluency and curiosity"
