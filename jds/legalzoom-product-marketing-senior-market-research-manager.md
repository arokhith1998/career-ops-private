## Job Description (extracted, verbatim from posting)

LegalZoom operates with a human-in-the-loop model, where AI enhances rather than replaces human judgment. "To align with our AI-integrated operating model, our interview process will assess AI fluency, curiosity, and measurable/impactful workflow integration."

As a Senior Market Research Manager, you will be part of the Product Marketing team, conducting market research projects and serving as a research partner to teams across the organization.

**You will:**
- Define market research priorities and proactively address key business decisions
- Independently manage ongoing and ad hoc research studies end to end (briefs, surveys/discussion guides, field management, data analysis, presentation)
- Administer online enterprise data platforms
- Drive customer journey and persona research to inform marketing and product strategy
- Conduct competitive intelligence studies
- Own ongoing brand health, awareness, consideration, and claims research
- Manage relationships with external research vendors and consultants
- Oversee budgets and timelines
- Lead development and adoption of agentic research workflows to scale insight generation

**You have:**
- "8-10+ years of experience in client-side market research, with at least 3-5 years in the software/technology sector"
- Ability to apply the right research approach (primary/secondary, in-house and external)
- Deep knowledge of qualitative and quantitative research methodologies
- Experience with Qualtrics, SPSS, ThoughtSpot, Amplitude, Tableau or similar
- "Experience optimizing workflows and tasks by leveraging AI, including Claude, as a power user"
- "Bonus if you have an advanced degree"
- Bonus for data visualization talent / slide design passion

Benefits: medical/dental/vision, 401k with matching, PTO, HSA/FSA, short/long-term disability, fertility, mental health, One Medical.
