About Nometria

Nometria is an early-stage AI infrastructure company based in Bangalore. We're a small, founder-led team working with engineering teams that are building and deploying AI in production.

About the Role

We're looking for a GTM Associate to join the Founder's Office and work directly with our CEO on go-to-market. You'll help build our pipeline, run growth experiments across channels, and bring back what you learn from the market to shape how we position and sell. It's a high-ownership role with broad exposure, suited to someone who wants to learn how an early-stage company finds its first customers.

What You'll Do

Growth experiments: plan, run and measure experiments across LinkedIn, Reddit, YouTube and Instagram, and double down on what works

Outbound and pipeline: research target accounts, run personalised outreach on LinkedIn and email, and book meetings for the founders

Community and events: build relationships with founders and engineers, and represent Nometria at meetups and in person

Founder's Office projects: take on cross-functional projects as priorities change

What We're Looking For

0 to 2 years of experience in growth, sales, marketing, consulting or a startup role

Excellent written and verbal communication, and comfort with cold outreach

A high sense of ownership, with a bias for action and the ability to work through ambiguity

Strong organisation: you can turn a broad goal into a clear plan and follow through

Curiosity about AI and technology, and the ability to pick up new concepts quickly

Nice to Have

Experience growing a social media account, newsletter or community

Prior exposure to B2B SaaS or developer tools

Experience at an early-stage startup or in a Founder's Office role
