## Company / context

Thumbtack is a two-sided marketplace for local services (customers + pros). Virtual-First company. Growth Marketing team owns paid acquisition on both sides. Publicly traded adjacency: competes with Angi, Yelp, TaskRabbit, HomeAdvisor for SEM talent.

## Role framing

Senior IC role owning Thumbtack's SEM program across Google + Microsoft. Focus on customer-side demand capture. Partners with Data Science on LTV targets, Analytics on iROAS/incrementality, Engineering on bid automation tooling.

## Responsibilities

- Execute and improve Thumbtack's paid search strategy across Google and Microsoft
- Manage, optimize, and grow SEM programs while adhering to KPIs
- Develop experimentation roadmap for ad copy, keywords, and bidding
- Partner with data science, analytics, and engineering teams on automation and scalable tools
- Own tracking, attribution, and measurement of performance metrics (iROAS, incrementality, lift)

## Required qualifications

- 8+ years paid digital advertising experience with proven track record scaling programs
- Demonstrated SEM campaign improvement through testing, optimization, automation, creative
- Vendor and partner relationship management capability
- Ability to define and execute 6-12 month roadmaps with aggressive milestones
- TROAS + LTV bidding experience
- Strong data analytics skills (pivot tables, vlookups, basic modeling)
- "Strong fluency with generative AI and automation tools" integrated into SEM execution
