## Job Description (extracted, verbatim from LinkedIn posting)

Snap Inc is a technology company. We believe the camera presents the greatest opportunity to improve the way people live and communicate. Snap contributes to human progress by empowering people to express themselves, live in the moment, learn about the world, and have fun together.

The Company operates Snapchat, a visual messaging app that enhances your relationships with friends, family, and the world, and Specs Inc., a wholly-owned subsidiary dedicated to making computing more human, in addition to Bitmoji, Saturn, and other digital services.

The Product Marketing team uses creativity, market research, and insights to optimize and inspire marketing efforts and roadmaps for products across Snap Inc. This team of Product Marketers works in a highly collaborative environment to deliver go-to-market efforts and strategies to demonstrate the role that Snapchat plays in the lives of our community and helps businesses grow.

We’re looking for a Lead Product Marketing Manager - Measurement at Snap, Inc!

What You'll Do

- Own global product marketing strategy for Snap’s measurement solutions, with a primary focus on first-party measurement (e.g., Conversion Lift) and a secondary focus on signal and 3P measurement solutions.
- Shape measurement product strategy and roadmaps by translating advertiser feedback, industry trends, and competitive insights into product priorities and opportunities for differentiation.
- Define positioning, messaging, and thought leadership that strengthen Snap’s measurement value proposition, advertiser confidence, and market competitiveness.
- Lead global go-to-market strategy and execution, partnering with Product, Engineering, Sales, Marketing Science, Solutions Engineering, and Customer Support to drive launches, seller readiness, and adoption.

Knowledge, Skills & Abilities

- Strong understanding of digital advertising and advertiser measurement needs.
- Demonstrated ability to influence product strategy and roadmaps through advertiser insights, competitive analysis, and sound commercial judgment.
- Ability to translate complex technical products into clear, differentiated advertiser value.
- Proven cross-functional leadership across Product, Engineering, and Sales.
- Excellent analytical and communication skills; self-directed with strong ownership and judgment.

Minimum Qualifications

- BS/BA degree or equivalent years of experience
- 8+ years of experience in product marketing, product strategy, or other relevant digital advertising roles

Preferred Qualifications

- Product marketing or product strategy experience with advertising products at scale in a technology company
- Hands-on experience with digital ads measurement, including lift, attribution, incrementality, or signal solutions
- Third-party measurement expertise or thought leadership in the space
