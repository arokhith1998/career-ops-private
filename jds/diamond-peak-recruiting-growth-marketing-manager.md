## Job Description (full, verbatim)
About Diamond Peak Recruiting
Diamond Peak Recruiting is a nationally recognized recruiting firm specializing in the Architecture, Engineering, and Construction (AEC) industry. Founded in 2018, we've built a strong national presence, established relationships with leading contractors, and earned multiple Inc. 5000 recognitions.

As we continue to grow, we're looking for a Growth Marketing Manager to build and lead our marketing efforts.

The Opportunity
We're looking for a hands-on marketing professional who enjoys building, executing, and taking ownership.

You'll work directly with company leadership to develop and execute marketing initiatives that strengthen our brand, engage our candidate and client networks, and support business growth.

With an established database, a significant professional audience, and existing marketing technology, you'll have the resources and autonomy to make a meaningful impact.

What You'll Do
- Marketing Automation: Develop targeted email campaigns, automated workflows, and engagement strategies using our CRM and marketing platforms.
- Database Marketing: Build campaigns to engage and reactivate candidates and clients, generate referrals, and support business development.
- Social Media & Brand: Manage our company social media presence, content strategy, and brand positioning.
- Employee Marketing: Create content, templates, and resources that help our recruiting and account management teams expand their professional networks.
- Website & SEO: Drive qualified website traffic, manage blog content, and improve organic search visibility.
- Analytics & Growth: Track campaign performance, identify opportunities, and develop repeatable marketing strategies that support measurable business outcomes.

What We're Looking For
- 5+ years of experience in growth, digital, lifecycle, or B2B marketing.
- Hands-on experience with CRM marketing, email automation, and campaign management.
- Strong understanding of social media, content marketing, SEO, and website analytics.
