What You'll Do
- Partner closely with the Team Lead to build and maintain a strong network of industry analysts and research firms, systematically gathering, validating, their views on memory demand/supply dynamics across AI, Server Infrastructure segments and PC/Consumer industry.
- Partner with Sales and Marketing teams to translate customer conversations and market "sensing" into structured intelligence
- Develop data-driven narratives and forward-looking scenarios (base/bull/bear cases) to support executive decision-making
- Leverage AI tools to accelerate data synthesis, pattern detection, and report generation across large, multi-source datasets
- Present findings to senior leadership in a clear, story-driven format that connects technical detail to business implications
- Build and maintain database of analyst and partners for Memory Marketing team to track market share, pricing, and inventory trends.

Location:
Daily onsite presence at our San Jose, CA in alignment with our Flexible Work policy
Reports to: Senior Director, Market Intelligence - Memory

What You Bring
- Bachelor's degree required (EE, CS preferred, MBA preferred)
- 10–15 years of experience in market intelligence, strategic marketing, or related analytical roles within the semiconductor or technology industry
- Strong written and verbal communication skills; ability to synthesize complex data into compelling narratives, with exceptional note taking ability
- Demonstrated experience in market "sensing" — gathering and validating signals from sales, customers, Industry ECO-partners and industry analysts.
- Proficiency with AI-based tools for research, analysis, and content generation
- Ability to build scenario models and forward-looking supply/demand narratives

Preferred Skills
- Experience at a Memory Marketing research/intelligence firm (e.g., TrendForce, TechInsights, Omdia, Yole)
- Direct marketing or market analysis experience at a Memory Company.
- Familiarity with AI/Server OEM/ODM customer dynamics and buildout cycles
- Sufficient knowledge preferred for Memory Supply chain such as WFE, CPU, GPU industry.
- Engineering background (Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, or related), providing technical fluency in memory architecture and system-level design
