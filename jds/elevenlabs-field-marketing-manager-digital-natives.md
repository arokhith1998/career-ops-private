## Job description (full text, via Ashby posting-api)

About ElevenLabs: (same company boilerplate as the Product Marketing - Agents posting; AI research/product company, voice AI, $781M raised, $22B valuation, ElevenAgents/ElevenCreative/ElevenAPI platforms.)

About the role: ElevenLabs is a voice AI company and wants to meet the startup founders and developers building on its platform in person. Looking for a Field Marketing Manager to own events and field programs for startups and founders in the US: run the founder dinner series, a quarterly demo day in SF, and happy hours/workshops/meetups that bring developers into the product. Build Founder's Day Summit, a flagship event for about 100 founders, from first idea to execution. Decide where ElevenLabs shows up at other events (AI conference sponsorships, side events, co-hosted programs with VCs/accelerators/partner brands). Hands-on role: build guest lists, host the room, work with researchers, startup team members, the Grants program, and VC partners.

What you will do: own a founder exec dinner series (10-20 guests); run a quarterly SF demo day; lead Founder's Day Summit (~100 founders); plan happy hours/mixers/workshops/meetups (4-5 in H1); manage sponsorships at AI conferences and founder events (HumanX, Slush, YC Startup School); plan side events around big conferences; co-host events with VCs/accelerators/partner brands; build a pipeline of high-quality founders with the Grants program and startup GTM team; brief researchers/speakers/hosts; manage event budgets/venues/vendors/logistics (SF/NYC); track attendance, sign-ups, activated accounts, and pipeline; test new event formats.

Requirements: experience owning founder/developer/startup events end to end at a technology company; track record of measurable event results; network in the SF or NYC startup community; experience running small curated events; experience managing event budgets/vendors/sponsorships; cross-functional and external-partner experience; familiarity with the AI/developer tools landscape. Bonus: experience at a startup-facing platform (Brex, Ramp, Clay, Vercel, or similar); experience running researcher/developer events; experience with Luma/Eventbrite/Cvent; building an events program from scratch at an early-stage company; demo day/founder dinner/YC-style program experience.

Growth potential: lead a team of field marketers as programs scale; move into broader field marketing (enterprise/regulated industries); expand into partner/ecosystem marketing.

Location: based in San Francisco, about two-thirds of events in SF and one-third in NYC; occasional travel expected (#LI-Remote).
