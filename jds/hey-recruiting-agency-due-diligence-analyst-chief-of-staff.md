## Full JD Text

Due Diligence Analyst (Chief of Staff)

MUST HAVE: McKinsey, BCG or Bain background (Associate / Consultant level or above).

Reports to: Co-CEOs
Location: Remote, working UK hours

ABOUT THE COMPANY

We are a fast-growing group of aesthetics and skin clinics in the UK, building a category-leading group. We're growing both organically and through acquisitions, and this role is central to both. As Chief of Staff, you'll sit at the centre of that growth: finding the clinics we want to join us, leading financial due diligence and closing the deals, and making each one stronger once it's part of the group.

THE ROLE

This is a rare chance to move from advising businesses to building one. You'll work directly with the Co-CEOs as a thought partner and operator, with real ownership from day one and no layers between you and the decisions.

Your time will be split roughly 60% M&A (including financial due diligence) and 40% strategy and operations.

WHAT YOU'LL DO

M&A (c. 60%)

• Own the pipeline: Find, screen and prioritise acquisition targets, and run first conversations with clinic owners
• Run deals end to end: Lead valuation, LOIs and negotiation support, and coordinate lawyers, accountants and other advisers
• Lead financial due diligence: Drive financial, commercial and operational due diligence, and write the investment papers
• Build and own the models: Target valuations, the group model, synergies and scenarios
• Make acquisitions work: Support post-merger integration and track each deal's performance against its business case

Strategy & Operations (c. 40%)

• Run consulting-style projects across the group: Clinic performance, pricing, treatment mix, staffing and new site rollout
• Turn analysis into action: Develop clear recommendations, then drive them through to delivery with clinic and central teams
• Build the operating rhythm: Set up and run group KPIs, reporting and the management meeting cadence
• Partner with the Co-CEOs: Prepare board and investor materials and lead special projects

WHAT WE'RE LOOKING FOR

Essential

• Minimum 2-5 years at McKinsey, BCG or Bain, at Associate / Consultant level or above
• Strong financial modelling and valuation skills, with hands-on experience of transactions or financial due diligence
• Excellent structured problem-solving and communication, from Excel through to board-ready slides
• Comfortable working hands-on in a fast-moving, entrepreneurial business with no large team behind you
• The ability to build credibility with both clinic owners and clinicians

Strong plus

• Private equity, corporate development or M&A experience, during or after consulting
• Exposure to multi-site, consumer, healthcare or buy-and-build businesses

YOU MIGHT BE A GREAT FIT IF YOU...

• Are entrepreneurial and commercially sharp, with a bias for action
• Are happy to roll up your sleeves and get into the detail
• Want a broad operator role with a clear path to growth
• Are excited by wellness and aesthetics, and by building a category-leading group

WHY JOIN US

• Direct access to the Co-CEOs and real influence on strategy
• End-to-end ownership of deals, not just a slice of the process
• A fast-growing platform with a clear buy-and-build roadmap
• Remote working with UK hours
