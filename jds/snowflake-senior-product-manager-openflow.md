We are hiring a Product Manager for our Openflow team. Openflow is Snowflake's native data integration platform, powered by Apache NiFi and deployed as a Snowflake-native service, enabling customers to replicate data from databases and SaaS applications into Snowflake at scale. In this role you will own the full lifecycle of database and SaaS connectors, working at the intersection of technical depth and customer impact to drive both platform strategy and field enablement.

AS A SENIOR PM AT SNOWFLAKE, YOU WILL:- Own the end-to-end product roadmap for database and SaaS connectors, from customer discovery through GA launch and full lifecycle management, including deprecation

- Partner with engineering, design, field sales, and support to define requirements, manage the backlog, and drive on-time connector releases across globally distributed teams

- Lead connector strategy decisions, including architecture transitions, naming standards, runtime consolidation, and TCO optimization initiatives such as bin-packing

- Drive customer and field enablement through observability improvements, including connector dashboards, health metrics, pricing tools, sizing calculators, and field guides

- Use data-driven insights, including AI, SQL, usage telemetry, and customer signals, to inform prioritization and product decisions

- Manage tradeoffs across reliability, performance, and customer experience while independently owning a technical product area from discovery through GA

OUR IDEAL CANDIDATE HAS:- 5+ years of product management experience in data infrastructure, ETL/ELT, or a data integration product

- Hands-on familiarity with Change Data Capture, including database replication, log-based CDC, and the operational realities of keeping pipelines reliable at scale

- A track record of independently owning a technical product area from discovery through GA, managing tradeoffs across reliability, performance, and customer experience

- Strong cross-functional collaboration skills, with experience driving alignment across engineering, field, and support in globally distributed teams

BONUS POINTS:- Familiarity with the connectors and ETL tool ecosystem, such as Fivetran, Airbyte, HVR, Stitch, or Informatica
