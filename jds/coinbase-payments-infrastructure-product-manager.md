Payments Infrastructure is the layer that makes that volume possible: a unified, intent-based money movement layer across any asset, any rail, any geo. As the Payments Infrastructure PM, you will own the roadmap behind CDP's payments volume growth: instant funding rails, global fiat expansion, and a second revenue surface in payments financing.</p>
<p>What you'll do:</p>
<ul>
<li>Own the Payments Infrastructure roadmap end to end: routing across fiat and crypto rails, payment methods, risk and compliance checks, ledgering and settlement, and the observability and ops tooling that keeps it running 24/7.</li>
<li>Ship the instant funding stack across rails, using risk-underwritten credit to deliver instant experiences where rails alone cannot.</li>
<li>Drive global expansion: payments in new countries and asset expansion beyond stablecoins.</li>
<li>Partner directly with enterprise customers and internal product teams to convert roadmap into live customers.</li>
</ul>
<p>Required Skills and Experience</p>
<ul>
<li>5+ years of product management experience on payments, money movement, or fintech infrastructure products at production scale.</li>
<li>Demonstrated proficiency in fiat payment rails, including a strong grasp of ledgering, and settlement mechanisms.</li>
<li>Experience shipping API-first products where developers are the customer, with the technical depth to earn engineering's trust on tradeoffs.</li>
<li>Demonstrated ability to operate across compliance, risk, and legal constraints and turn them into product surfaces rather than blockers.</li>
</ul>
