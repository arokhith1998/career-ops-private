About The Role

Happen Bank is looking for a Sr Product Manager to help shape and deliver the technology and tools that support our Financial Crimes Compliance (FCC) organization. In this role, you'll partner across FCC, Fraud Operations, Engineering, Data, and Risk to modernize investigator workflows, strengthen compliance capabilities, and improve how financial crime investigations are managed.

You'll help define the long-term product vision, lead product discovery, and deliver scalable solutions that improve investigator efficiency, consistency, and quality while meeting the regulatory and operational requirements of a financial institution.

What You'll Do

Product Vision & Strategy

- Help define the product vision and strategy for FCC technology, including platforms, workflows, and investigator tools
- Translate operational challenges, investigator needs, and business objectives into an outcome-driven product roadmap, prioritizing investments based on measurable impact, success metrics, and strategic alignment
- Partner with FCC leadership and cross-functional stakeholders to align product priorities, technology capabilities, and operational requirements

Product Discovery & Outcome Ownership

- Lead product discovery to understand investigator behavior, workflow inefficiencies, system gaps, and operational challenges, validating solutions through research, prototyping, data, and experimentation

Execution & Delivery Excellence
