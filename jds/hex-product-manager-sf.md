## Job Description (full text via Ashby posting API)

### About Hex
Hex is a data/analytics platform (natural-language + code exploration). Customers include Ramp, Figma, StubHub, Anthropic, Gamma. Raised over $100M from Sequoia, a16z, Snowflake, Amplify. Offices in San Francisco and New York plus remote US/UK.

### About the Role
"Hex is looking for a Product Manager to join the team and help drive the future of how teams work with data... We're not big on titles, but this role might index to a Senior Product Manager at another company." Owns analytical features: notebooks, dashboarding, conversational AI interfaces (Threads, Notebook Agent).

### What You Will Do
- Own an area of the product and drive a coherent vision that resonates with customers.
- Early discovery; help define which problems are worth solving.
- Get deep with customers to understand roles, goals, and how to make them more impactful.
- Competitive research; understand the data/analytics market.
- Collaborate closely with engineering, design, and GTM teams.
- Learn from the sales process to see what's resonating.

### About You (verbatim)
- "5 years+ of hands-on experience as either a Product Manager, product-engaged Solutions / Sales Engineer, or customer-engaged Engineer"
- Experience with technical products; ability to dive deep into complex systems.
- "We're a growth-stage startup, so we're looking for PMs that can ship quickly and iterate."
- Preference for data/analytics experience or strong interest.

"This role will be hybrid (1-2 days per week in office) out of our San Francisco headquarters."
