About Conversion

Conversion is the agentic marketing automation platform for modern enterprises such as Plaid, ClickHouse, Webflow, and Productboard. Our platform lets growth teams run their entire go-to-market motion in one place, from acquisition through retention, with AI agents doing the work that legacy tools like Marketo, HubSpot, and Pardot can't.

We've raised $28M+ from Abstract Ventures, True Ventures, and HOF Capital. The team is based in San Francisco and includes engineers, designers, and operators from Airbnb, Palantir, Pinterest, IMC, Shopify, LinkedIn, and Microsoft.

About The Role

Hey, Kyle here. I lead marketing at Conversion, and I'm excited to meet the right person for our first product marketer!!

Something a little unique about working at Conversion: we ship as fast and as often as we can. We ship every week. Most weeks that's a new agent, a new integration, or something a customer asked for on Tuesday.

That pace makes messaging hard. Someone has to own what we say about each launch, how the launches tie together, and how all of it builds into one story about the business. Nobody owns that full time today.

I think of a PMM as connective tissue across marketing, product, and engineering. We don't put PMMs on product pods, so you're thinking about the whole business: the narrative, our ICP, why enterprise MOps teams are leaving legacy platforms, and why Conversion is the right fit for them.

You'll also sit very close to customers. You'll be on sales calls, in onboarding, and in deal reviews.

This role is in-person 5 days/week in San Francisco.

Responsibilities

The company narrative: positioning and messaging for Conversion as a whole. Weekly launches plus the bigger tentpoles, across all three of our launch tiers. Breaking big releases into a run of smaller moments so they keep showing up in market. Brand voice and copy standards across the site, launches, and sales materials. Launch channel strategy: what goes where, and why. Competitive positioning against Marketo, HubSpot, and Pardot. Sales enablement: the pitch, one-pagers, battlecards. Customer insight: calls, win/loss, customer stories.

You might be a good fit if you: Have done PMM at a B2B company selling into enterprise. Have shipped on a weekly or biweekly launch cadence. Can show messaging you wrote and what changed after it shipped. Like being on customer calls and do it without being asked. Move fast and are fine iterating in public. Don't mind the high-volume parts of the job. Write clearly and quickly.

You might be a great fit if you: Have marketed to marketers or MOps teams. Have built a narrative from zero at an early-stage company. Have run a tentpole launch end to end. Follow people like Kyle Poyar, Emily Kramer, and Dave Gerhardt. Want to be on a high-intensity, in-person team.

Bonus Points If: You've worked at a high-growth startup and have seen what it looks like to scale a growth motion quickly.
