## Role Summary
Drives product direction for data platform services, tools, and experiences - trusted data
products, scalable analytics, AI-powered workflows, data mesh architecture.

## Key responsibilities
- Support vision, roadmap, execution for data platform services enabling trusted data
  consumption, analytics, AI/ML workflows, data product adoption.
- Turn business/technical needs into product requirements, prioritized roadmaps,
  user-focused capabilities for producers/consumers/analysts.
- Partner with engineering, architecture, governance, analytics, business teams on
  accessibility, reliability, security, performance, compliance.
- Apply semantic layer/ontology concepts for shared terminology, metric consistency,
  cross-domain interoperability.
- Support natural language analytics by grounding queries/AI agents in governed
  metrics/metadata.
- Drive adoption via stakeholder engagement, training, documentation, feedback loops.
- Define/track success metrics (adoption, self-service usage, data trust, reliability, AI
  answer quality).
- Track trends in data platforms, cloud ecosystems, query engines, AI-enabled analytics.

## Required qualifications
- 5+ years of product management experience with data, analytics, AI, developer tooling,
  or internal platform products.
- Understanding of modern data architectures, data modeling, BI concepts, cloud data
  ecosystems, platform integration patterns (APIs, SDKs).
- Experience turning complex requirements into roadmaps, user stories, measurable outcomes.
- Experience working with engineering, data, governance, security, business stakeholders.
- Working knowledge of semantic layers, governed metrics, metadata, business glossaries.
- Working knowledge of AI/ML and generative AI (LLM capabilities, limitations, responsible
  use in governed environments).
- Experience using telemetry, product analytics, customer feedback for decisions.
- Strong communication, stakeholder management, prioritization skills.

## Preferred qualifications
Analytics tools (Jupyter, Superset, Tableau, Power BI); semantic models, knowledge graphs,
governance tools; lakehouse/cloud warehouse architectures; software/data engineering
background; vendor platform management; regulated industry experience (insurance,
financial services).
