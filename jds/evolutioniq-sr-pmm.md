### Go-to-Market
- Direct GTM efforts for evolving solutions

- Build feedback loops connecting Product, Sales, and Client Services

### Content & Execution
- Create sales decks, demo stories, case studies, and proof-point content
- Define lightweight content systems and roadmaps
- Balance speed with organizational scalability

- Demonstrated success enabling sales teams on complex/technical products
