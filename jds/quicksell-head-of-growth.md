Job Title: Head Of Growth
Company: DoubleTick
Location: Mumbai
Experience Required: 7-12+Years

About DoubleTick: DoubleTick is a Multi-Number Conversational Intelligence and WA CX Platform designed specifically to enable Frontline Executives, Sales Teams, Relationship Managers, Dealerships, Retail Stores WhatsApp Handles and centralise, govern, automate and scale Customer Experience using Business Messaging on WhatsApp by helping brands unlock the true voice of the customer and control CX across 1000s of these individual WhatsApp handles. It boosts operational efficiency by enabling AI Bots, automations, WhatsApp Voice, centralized WhatsApp broadcast and analytics across all WhatsApp handles of the organization.

DoubleTick is recognised as Emerging Technology Partner of the Year by Meta at WhatsApp Business Summit 2025. Backed by InfoEdge Ventures and BeeNext Singapore, DoubleTick serves over 10,000+ businesses in India and UAE across 100+ verticals. Brands using DoubleTick include IDFC First Bank, MakeMyTrip, Bajaj Finserv, AngelOne, Piramal Finance, Kotak Securities, upGrad, Royal Enfield, CoinDCX, D-Link, ForeverMark, GRT Jewellers, Jaro, SabyaSachi, DarGlobal and more.

Roles and Responsibilities
As Head of Growth, you will be responsible for driving predictable, scalable growth across the entire customer lifecycle. You'll work closely with Product, Sales, Marketing, and Leadership to design, execute, and optimize growth strategies grounded in data and experimentation. This is a hands-on leadership role - you'll set strategy, build the growth team, and still get into the weeds when needed.

- Own company-wide growth metrics: ARR, MRR, CAC, LTV, activation, retention, expansion
- Define and execute Doubletick's growth roadmap across PLG and Sales-led motions
- Identify and prioritize high-impact growth opportunities across funnels
- Scale inbound and outbound growth channels (SEO, paid, partnerships, content, lifecycle, referrals)
- Optimize conversion across landing pages, signup flows, and demos
- Work closely with Sales to improve pipeline quality and velocity
- Drive activation, onboarding, and time-to-value improvements
- Partner with Product to design experiments that improve usage, engagement, and retention
- Use customer insights to influence product roadmap
- Build a strong experimentation culture (A/B testing, cohort analysis, funnel optimization)
- Define growth dashboards and KPIs
- Turn data into actionable insights and fast iterations
- Build and mentor a high-performing growth team (marketing, lifecycle, analytics, growth PMs)
- Align cross-functional teams around growth priorities
- Be a thought partner to founders and leadership on growth bets

You should have:
- 7-12+ years of experience in growth, marketing, or revenue roles in SaaS
- Proven track record of scaling B2B SaaS growth (PLG or sales-assisted)
- Strong understanding of funnels, metrics, and experimentation frameworks
- Experience working cross-functionally with Product, Sales, and Engineering
- Data-driven mindset with the ability to move fast with imperfect information
- Hands-on operator who can zoom in and out seamlessly

Preferred
- Experience scaling from early-stage to Series B/C+
- Background in developer tools, productivity, or messaging platforms
- Experience building growth teams from scratch
- Familiarity with tools like HubSpot, Segment, Amplitude, Mixpanel, GA, etc.

Why Work With Us?
Accelerated Career Growth, Challenging Work Environment, Career Flexibility, Supportive Culture, Founder-Led Exposure, Sharpen Customer-Centric Skills.
