About the Role

As NYEI continues to grow, we are expanding our Founder's Office and looking for a Founder's Office Associate who is versatile, adaptable, and comfortable taking on diverse responsibilities. The ideal candidate should be a quick learner who can navigate unfamiliar areas, take ownership of new challenges, and adapt to changing priorities with ease. The Associate will work closely with our Founder and CEO, along with other senior leaders, including the Director and Project Managers. The role involves coordinating and executing strategic initiatives delegated by the leadership team, supporting key business priorities, and assisting the Founder with research, resources, and other requirements to drive execution.

Key Responsibilities

1. Design and Video Editing: Create marketing creatives, ABM materials, event collaterals, presentations, and reports for internal and client-facing purposes. Edit videos and develop visual content as required, with proficiency in tools such as CapCut, Canva, Adobe Premiere Pro, and PowerPoint.

2. Financial Modelling, Research and Business Analysis: Analyse data from internal platforms and external sources to build financial models, dashboards, forecasts, and reports. Conduct scenario analysis, market and competitor research, identify industry trends, analyse client behaviour, and evaluate potential vendors to generate actionable insights and support business decisions. Proficiency in Excel and Google Sheets, along with working knowledge of Power BI, is preferred.

3. Project Management and Execution: Coordinate with internal teams, senior leadership, and external stakeholders to drive projects and strategic initiatives, track progress, and ensure timely execution. Strong communication, organizational, and stakeholder management skills, along with the ability to manage multiple priorities calmly, are essential.

4. Business Development and Tendering: Identify and reach out to prospective clients, support lead generation, and assist in preparing proposals, tenders, and other client-facing documents. Strong communication, research, and presentation skills, along with an understanding of business development processes, are desirable.

5. AI and Automation: Use AI tools effectively across research, financial modelling, creative development, and other business functions. The ability to build AI-powered workflows, integrate different tools, and automate processes is highly desirable, with prior experience building AI-based solutions being an added advantage.

6. HR, People and Culture, and Corporate Governance: Support hiring, employer branding, and people initiatives while developing frameworks to track employee happiness, engagement, and well-being. Contribute to upskilling strategies, employee development roadmaps, and tracking mechanisms to strengthen workplace culture and organizational effectiveness.

What is in it for you?

1. Learn Directly from the Founder: Work closely with and learn from Anuj Srivastava, Founder and CEO of NYEI, gaining first-hand insights into building and scaling a multi-million-dollar business from the ground up.

2. Holistic Professional Growth: Gain hands-on experience across diverse business functions, from strategy and finance to marketing, research, and operations, in a fast-paced environment.

3. Ownership and Leadership Opportunities: Take ownership of the Founder's Office, with the opportunity to shape its functioning, drive new initiatives, and eventually lead the function as the company grows.

4. Competitive Compensation: Earn above-industry-average compensation that reflects your skills, contributions, and growth.

This is a dynamic role, beyond the listed responsibilities, you will be expected to step in and own any high-priority tasks as needed.
