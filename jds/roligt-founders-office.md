## Full description (verbatim via headless browser render of the LinkedIn guest posting page)
About Roligt Foods
Roligt Foods is building a next gen clean label beverage brand out of Hyderabad — short, honest ingredient lists. Pre-launch, running live activations across the city and rolling out smart vending kiosks.

The role
This Founder's Office role works directly with the founder and sees the whole business: kiosks, events, brand, sales, production, finance. Runs the operating rhythm of the company; takes on problems without an owner yet.

What you will do
- Track weekly numbers of every vertical (kiosk sales/uptime, events/leads, brand, production, cash).
- Run the weekly leadership review; prepare dashboard; capture decisions; chase actions to closure.
- Own special projects for the founder: new channels, partnerships, pricing, launch plans.
- First-pass analysis on business questions with a recommendation, not just data.
- Unblock work between kiosk/events/brand/finance leads.
- Prepare investor, board and partner material.
- Lead the Generalist Interns in the Founder's Office and review their work.

You will do well here if you have
- "An MBA (final year or recent graduate) or an undergraduate degree from a strong programme. Any stream. Up to 3 years of experience in consulting, startups, FMCG or operations is a plus."
- Strong Excel/Sheets and structured thinking; clear short writing; comfort moving between strategy and execution; high ownership.

What we offer: fair competitive pay; fast-track growth working directly with the founder; broad exposure; real ownership in a small team.

Please do not apply if: not based in/willing to relocate to Hyderabad; want a fixed job description; not comfortable being challenged directly by the founder.

How to apply: "Apply with your resume. At the top of your resume, add two lines on one problem you owned end to end and what the result was. We are selective about who we hire. If you do not hear back within three weeks of applying, please assume your application has not been taken forward this time."
