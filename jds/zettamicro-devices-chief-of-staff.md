 Hiring for Chief of Staff at ZettaMicro Devices
Location: Bengaluru, India
Experience: Fresh graduates or up to two years

Zetta Micro Devices is building one of India’s most exciting hardware startups. We’re looking for an exceptional early-career generalist to work directly with the CEO and help turn the company’s vision into action.

This is not a coordination role. You will be the CEO’s execution partner: taking important, often ambiguous problems and driving them to completion. You’ll work across product, technology, sales, marketing, strategy, and operations. The work will change as the company’s priorities change.

You may be a good fit if you:
Think clearly and learn quickly.
Take ownership without waiting for detailed instructions.
Bring structure to ambiguous problems.
Communicate well with technical and business teams. 
Are fresh out of college or have up to two years of experience.
Want to build a career in product, strategy, operations, or entrepreneurship. 

We care about your ability, curiosity, and pace: not your college, degree, or current title. You’ll get an unusually close view of how a hardware company is built: from making important decisions to doing the difficult work of making them happen.

To apply, send your CV to george@zmd.tech with the subject: Chief of Staff Application - [Your Name]
