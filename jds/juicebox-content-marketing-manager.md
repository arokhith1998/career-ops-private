## Position Summary
Content Marketing Manager owning Juicebox's content strategy and execution. Become the primary storyteller for Juicebox. Manage content assets that reach prospects and customers outside the product interface (blog posts, case studies, SEO content, videos, guides, thought leadership).

## Responsibilities
- Own Juicebox's content strategy and execution.
- Author and distribute blog posts, case studies, and search-optimized content.
- Conduct customer interviews to develop compelling narratives.
- Create diverse content formats including videos, guides, and thought leadership pieces.
- Oversee external writer contributions and maintain consistent brand voice.
- Identify and pursue new content opportunities and distribution channels.
- Ensure published work aligns with brand voice and product expertise.
- Position SEO as a major customer acquisition driver.
