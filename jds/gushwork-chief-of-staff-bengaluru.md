**About the company:** Gushwork is building an AI "Agent Operating System" for small and mid-sized businesses. It started with an SEO agent and is expanding into Paid Ads and Developer agents. Roughly 90 employees, based in Bengaluru, backed by Lightspeed and SIG.

**About the role:** Partner with the cofounders on company priorities, operating cadence, and execution. Own 2-3 high-impact cross-functional projects, and act as a force multiplier for leadership.

**Responsibilities:**
- Run the weekly, monthly, and quarterly operating cadence.
- Own cross-functional projects to completion.
- Represent the cofounder with other leaders.
- Manage a small operations team.
- Build company systems for goals, hiring, performance, and manager health.
- Do unglamorous operational work.
- Challenge the CEO with facts and alternatives, then execute his decision.

**Must-haves:**
- 7+ years of work experience.
- Startup experience.
- At least one year of direct people management, including hiring and firing.
- Comfort working across all functions.
- A track record of building something others still run.
- Owning an outcome end to end.
- Long stretches of operational work.
- Past disagreement with a cofounder or manager.
- Clear, brief writing.

**Preferred:**
- Tier 1 institute background.
- Strategy, business operations, or founder's office experience.
- US SaaS/AI experience or sales to US customers.
- Founding or co-founding experience.
- Close work with a CEO or founder.
