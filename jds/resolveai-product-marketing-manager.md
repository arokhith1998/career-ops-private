What You’ll Do
As our Enterprise Product Marketing Manager, you’ll be a key member of Resolve AI’s go-to-market team and the owner of how we tell our story to our enterprise segment around the world. You’ll sit at the intersection of sales, product, engineering, and customers, translating a deeply technical, category-defining product into clear, compelling narratives that drive awareness, adoption, and revenue.
This is a highly visible, hands-on role where you’ll help define positioning, lead launches, and build the foundational Enterprise GTM playbook as we scale. You’ll have real ownership, real impact, and a front-row seat to shaping a new category in agentic AI.
Your responsibilities will include:
- Define and evolve Resolve AI’s positioning and messaging, clearly articulating our value to enterprise buyers: CIO, CTO, and VP of Eng.
- Translate complex technical capabilities into simple, differentiated narratives that resonate with both hands-on practitioners and decision-makers
- Support product launches end-to-end, from messaging and content to cross-functional coordination and execution
- Create high-impact content, including technical blogs, product demos, videos, and thought leadership pieces, to build awareness, credibility, and trust
- Partner closely with sales to build enablement materials, pitch decks, product narratives, competitive battlecards, and training that improve win rates and shorten sales cycles
- Act as the voice of the customer by gathering insights from users, prospects, and the field, and synthesizing competitive and market intelligence
- Collaborate with product and engineering to inform roadmap decisions and ensure market insights are reflected in product strategy
- Partner with Growth Marketing to plan and execute campaigns that drive awareness, adoption, pipeline, and revenue growth
- Establish Resolve AI as a trusted, respected brand within the engineering and developer community
- Continuously measure performance, iterate on messaging, and refine GTM strategies based on data and feedback
What We’re Looking For
- 5+ years of experience in product marketing, product management, or a related role within B2B SaaS, enterprise software, or AI-driven products
- Exceptional storytelling ability, with a talent for turning technical depth into clear, compelling value propositions
- Ability to think strategically while also rolling up your sleeves to execute in a fast-moving environment
- Proven experience marketing to technical audiences such as engineers, SREs, developers, and engineering leadership
- A strong track record of ownership across launching products and executing successful go-to-market initiatives
- Deep customer empathy and a strong instinct for understanding real-world pain points
- Experience partnering cross-functionally with product, engineering, sales, and demand generation teams
- Analytical mindset, comfortable using data, metrics, and research to inform decisions and measure impact
- Comfort operating in ambiguity and excitement about building foundational processes from scratch
- Genuine enthusiasm for Resolve AI’s mission and the opportunity to shape a category-defining company from the ground up
