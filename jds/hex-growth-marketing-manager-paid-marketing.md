ABOUT HEX

Hex is growing our team of builders on a mission to make everyone a data person. Our platform solves key pain points with today's data and analytics tooling, and empowers anyone to explore data using natural language, with or without code, on trusted context. THE ROLE

AI is changing what's possible in growth marketing faster than most teams can keep up. We're looking for a performance marketer who's building for the future, not sticking to playbooks from the past. You'll be Hex's first dedicated performance hire, taking full ownership of paid-attributed S1s and pipeline ARR, with real budget from day one.

WHAT YOU'LL DO

Own paid media end-to-end: drive paid strategy and execution across Google Ads, LinkedIn Ads, Meta Ads, and emerging channels; manage and optimize 7-8 figure annual paid budgets; own paid-attributed S1s and pipeline ARR contribution.

Build the machine: build automated performance marketing systems (audience pipelines, creative testing frameworks, reporting infrastructure, bid management) integrated into Hex's internal tools; use Hex's own agentic features to build and document paid marketing use cases.

Connect paid to the full funnel: drive top-of-funnel paid acquisition for the PLG motion; partner with Brand Design on creative strategy; stay ahead of emerging channels and AI-native campaign tools.

WHAT WE'RE LOOKING FOR

Experience: "5-8 years in performance marketing, with direct ownership (not just oversight) of 6- and 7-figure paid budgets." Hands-on experience running Google Ads, LinkedIn Ads, and Meta Ads. Background at a B2B SaaS, developer tools, or data infrastructure company preferred.

Skills: experience building marketing automation and systems using tools like Clay, Zapier, HubSpot, and APIs; familiarity with both PLG and SLG funnels; "Demonstrated use of AI tools - Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, or similar - to meaningfully automate or accelerate your work. We want specific examples." Familiarity with Salesforce nice to have.

WHAT SUCCESS LOOKS LIKE (within 12 months)

Paid channels have clear ownership of sourced S1s, pipeline ARR, and self-serve revenue contribution; an automated performance marketing system is in production; Hex has documented, repeatable paid marketing use cases built on its own agentic platform.
