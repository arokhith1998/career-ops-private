WHO YOU ARE
Snowflake is seeking a strategic, customer-obsessed Senior Manager, Customer Marketing to own and scale Snowflake's premier co-marketing program that puts our most strategic, iconic customers at the center of our biggest storytelling moments.

You are the architect of Snowflake's most visible customer moments: flagship films, marquee keynote appearances, integrated paid campaigns, and web experiences that put our best customers on the world's biggest stages. At the same time, you are grounded in the fundamentals of customer marketing, sourcing the right customer voices, building durable advocacy, and turning technical outcomes into reference-ready narratives that create mutual value across the customer lifecycle. You see customer storytelling as a growth lever, not just a content exercise,  a way to deepen trust with strategic accounts and act as a true partner to Sales.

You thrive at the intersection of executive relationship management, campaign strategy, and creative ambition. You know how to find the right story, earn the trust of a Fortune 500 CDO, navigate complex brand and legal approvals, and orchestrate a cross-functional team from concept through launch. You are equally comfortable in a room with a customer's Head of Communications and in a working session with a creative agency, and you operate with a bias toward bold storytelling, a high tolerance for ambiguity, and a deep commitment to making the customer the hero.

This role reports to the Director of Customer Marketing and partners closely with Brand, Creative, Social, PR, ABM, Executive Communications, and Web teams, as well as Snowflake's external agency partners.

- Serve as the central orchestrator across Brand, Creative, Social, Web, PR, ABM, and Exec Comms throughout the campaign lifecycle, owning timelines, dependencies, and launch readiness

- Connect campaigns to Snowflake's highest-visibility moments, Summit keynotes, product launches, and marquee events,  sourcing and prepping customer speakers from selection through execution
