Strategy | Operations | Growth | AI | Special Projects
Company: Communezen Ventures Private Limited (UniLiv & Huddle Stays)
Location: Noida | Work from Office
Experience: 0–3 Years | Exceptional Freshers Welcome
Reporting To: Utkarsh Singh – Head of Customer Experience & Founders' Office

About the Role
This is not a conventional 9-to-5 job.
We are looking for hustlers, problem-solvers, and ambitious generalists who think like founders, take complete ownership, and thrive in fast-paced, high-pressure environments.
As a Founder's Office Associate, you will work directly with leadership across business strategy, operations, growth, expansion, customer experience, fundraising, and special projects.
There are no rigid departmental boundaries here. You will be expected to solve problems, drive execution, and take initiatives from idea to implementation.

Key Responsibilities
Drive high-priority strategic and operational initiatives across multiple business functions.
Work closely with leadership on business expansion, growth opportunities, partnerships, and special projects.
Identify operational inefficiencies, solve complex business problems, and implement scalable solutions.
Conduct market research, competitor analysis, financial assessments, and business feasibility studies.
Build management presentations, dashboards, MIS reports, business plans, and execution frameworks.
Coordinate with internal teams and external stakeholders to ensure timely execution.
Leverage AI and automation to optimize workflows, enhance productivity, and build smarter business processes.

Who We're Looking For
Hustler Mindset: Extremely resourceful, proactive, ambitious, and execution-driven.
Founder-Like Ownership: Someone who takes responsibility beyond assigned tasks and does not believe in "That's not my job."
AI Proficiency – Mandatory: Strong hands-on knowledge of ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, and AI-powered productivity/automation tools.
Analytical Thinking: Strong command of Excel/Google Sheets, presentations, research, and problem-solving.
Adaptability: Comfortable working across departments, handling ambiguity, and navigating changing priorities.
Commitment: Willingness to go beyond conventional job boundaries and support critical business requirements outside regular hours when necessary, in accordance with applicable policies and law.
Execution Excellence: Ability to independently deliver results with minimal supervision.
Our Expectations
We are not hiring someone to simply complete tasks. We are hiring someone who gets things done.
This role demands exceptional ownership, flexibility, intellectual curiosity, urgency, and resilience. Responsibilities will evolve with business priorities, and performance will be measured by impact, not hours spent at a desk.
If you are looking for a predictable role with narrowly defined responsibilities, this may not be the right opportunity.
If you want to build businesses, solve real problems, work directly with leadership, and accelerate your career through hands-on execution, we want to hear from you.
