## Full job description (verbatim)

Why join us


Brex is the intelligent finance platform that enables companies to spend smarter and move faster in more than 200 markets. By combining global corporate cards and banking with intuitive spend management, bill pay, and travel software, Brex enables founders and finance teams to accelerate operations, gain real-time visibility, and control spend effortlessly. Brex’s AI-native automation and world-class service eliminate manual expense and accounting tasks for customers so they can focus on what matters most. Tens of thousands of the world's best companies run on Brex, including DoorDash, Coinbase, Robinhood, Zoom, Plaid, Reddit, and SeatGeek.


Working at Brex allows you to push your limits, challenge the status quo, and collaborate with some of the brightest minds in the industry. We’re committed to building a diverse team and inclusive culture and believe your potential should only be limited by how big you can dream. We make this a reality by empowering you with the tools, resources, and support you need to grow your career.

Marketing at Brex


Marketing tells the story of Brex to the world. From acquisition to activation, we translate product value into business results. Our team spans Revenue, Product, and Brand Marketing, and works closely with nearly every function at Brex. We move fast, experiment often, and think deeply about customer behavior. If you want your creativity to drive growth and shape perception, this is the place.


What you’ll do


As a Senior Lifecycle Marketing Manager, you will own how Brex grows revenue within its existing customer base. You’ll lead the strategy and execution for specific areas of the customer journey like onboarding, expansion, rewards, and offers programs, etc: getting customers to set up their accounts, adopt additional products, activate and engage with rewards, and take advantage of the right offers at the right time. You’ll design programs across email, in-product, and other owned channels, and you’ll be accountable for incremental impact on adoption, retention, and revenue, not just engagement metrics. You’ll bring a strong point of view on what to test, what to scale, and what to stop.


Where you’ll work


This role will be based in our San Francisco office. We are a hybrid environment that combines the energy and connections of being in the office with the benefits and flexibility of working from home. We currently require a minimum of three coordinated days in the office per week, Monday, Wednesday and Thursday. As a perk, we also have up to four weeks per year of fully remote work!


Responsibilities



- Own the strategy and roadmap for existing-customer lifecycle, including account set up, cross-sell and upsell, feature and product adoption, rewards activation and engagement, and partner and promotional offers.

- Build segmentation and trigger logic grounded in customer behavior, spend patterns, product usage, and account attributes so each customer receives the most relevant message, offer, or next best action.

- Design and orchestrate multi-channel programs across email, in-product, push, and other owned channels, with clear rules for frequency, suppression, and channel priority.

- Design measurement for incrementality, including holdouts and controlled experiments, and report on lift in adoption, retention, and revenue rather than opens and clicks.

- Run a disciplined experimentation program across audiences, offers, timing, and creative to identify the levers that drive incremental growth, and scale what works.

- Partner with Product, Product Marketing, Rewards/Partnerships, Finance, and Data to define offer strategy, eligibility, economics, and launch timing for new features, rewards, and promotions.

- Partner with Sales, Customer Success, and Account Management so lifecycle programs complement human-led expansion motions, with clear handoffs, shared signals, and no conflicting outreach.

- Work with Product Marketing and Design to craft creative briefs and messaging that translate product value into clear customer benefits.

- Manage prioritization of incoming requests across stakeholders, and clearly communicate tradeoffs and timelines.

- Monitor program performance and share regular readouts with the team and leadership, translating insights into recommendations and next steps.

- Maintain strong governance over compliance, deliverability, and data hygiene in partnership with Legal, Compliance, and Marketing Ops, especially for offers and rewards communications.

- Mentor and provide guidance to more junior team members, and contribute to team best practices.


Requirements



- 6+ years of marketing experience, with significant experience in lifecycle, CRM, or retention marketing.

- Proven track record of building and scaling lifecycle programs across multiple channels that drove measurable incremental impact on expansion, adoption, retention, or revenue.

- Hands-on experience with customer marketing for existing customers, such as cross-sell/upsell, loyalty, rewards, or promotional offers programs.

- Strong grounding in segmentation, trigger design, and orchestration, beyond batch-and-blast email.

- Experience designing and interpreting experiments, including holdouts and incrementality testing; comfortable pushing back on vanity metrics.

- Strong analytical skills; comfortable querying and interpreting data with tools such as SQL, Looker, or similar BI tools.

- Ability to ruthlessly prioritize among multiple projects and timelines.

- Ability to build trusted cross-functional relationships and manage stakeholders at all levels, including Product, Sales, and Finance.

- Strong organizational, writing, and communication skills.


Bonus points



- Experience in fintech, payments, B2B SaaS, or other financial services with regulated marketing communications.

- Experience with rewards, loyalty, or partner-funded offer programs, including offer economics and ROI.

- Experience with our tools: Looker, Marketo, Salesforce, Hex, Knak.

- Experience with in-product messaging and customer data platforms.


Compensation


The expected salary range for this role is $131,000 - $188,000. However, the starting base pay will depend on a number of factors including the candidate’s location, skills, experience, market demands, and internal pay parity. Depending on the position offered, equity and other forms of compensation may be provided as part of a total compensation package.
