## Full Job Description

As the Manager of Sales Strategy and Operations, you will be the strategic backbone of go-to-market execution -- bridging sales strategy, financial predictability, and rep motivation. Highly cross-functional, sitting at the intersection of Sales Leadership, Finance, and HR. Mission: design and execute commission plans that drive the right sales behaviors, build the analytics framework to measure performance, and run a highly predictable weekly and quarterly sales forecasting cadence.

### Sales Compensation Design & Execution
Partner with Sales Leadership and Finance to design, model, and implement annual and mid-year Sales Incentive Compensation Plans (OTE structures, accelerators, SPIFFs, and gates) aligned with company ARR goals. Ensure data accuracy, timely payouts, and dispute resolution using incentive compensation management software. Conduct ongoing analysis on plan performance.

### Sales Forecasting & Revenue Predictability
Own and optimize the weekly, monthly, and quarterly sales forecasting process. Manage and configure forecasting platforms to track pipeline health, historical conversion rates, and deal slippage.

### Sales Performance Analytics & Reporting
Build, maintain, and optimize automated dashboards tracking core SaaS metrics. Deliver proactive insights identifying underperformance and top-performing behaviors. Provide data foundations for Quarterly Business Reviews and executive board meetings.

### Qualifications & Experience
4-6 years of experience in Sales Operations, Revenue Operations, or Sales Finance in fast-growing B2B SaaS. Advanced CRM knowledge (Salesforce/HubSpot) required. High proficiency in Excel/Google Sheets. Experience with forecasting tools and Incentive Compensation Management software preferred. Data visualization experience (Tableau, Looker, PowerBI) highly preferred. Strong data-modeling skills and high emotional intelligence required.
