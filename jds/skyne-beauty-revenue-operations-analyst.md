Looking for a highly analytical, technically skilled Revenue Operations & Data Analyst to strengthen revenue infrastructure, optimize sales operations, support data-driven business growth. **What You'll Own:**

- Integrate data from CRM platforms and business tools

**What We're Looking For:**

- SQL and data visualization tools (Looker Studio, Tableau, Power BI)
- CRM experience (HubSpot, Salesforce)
- Python/APIs/data integrations familiarity is a strong plus
- Experience with consumer brands, beauty, skincare, wellness, or CPG preferred
