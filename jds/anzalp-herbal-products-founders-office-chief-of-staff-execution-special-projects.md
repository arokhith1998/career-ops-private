## Job Description (as extracted)

Role centers on bridging execution gaps. Primary duties include:

- Capturing all founder directives via meetings, voice notes, or messages into a structured system with assigned owners and deadlines
- Following up on delegated work across business development, operations, and vendor management
- Identifying and resolving blockers independently when possible
- Escalating decisions in a "decision-ready format" only when genuinely needed
- Delivering daily written progress briefs covering closed items, at-risk tasks, and bottlenecks
- Verifying completion against concrete evidence rather than verbal confirmation
- Flagging stale tasks and repeated delays proactively
- Leading special cross-business initiatives until transition or completion

### Authority and Boundaries

The role grants authority to request updates, coordinate meetings, challenge incomplete responses, and escalate delays, treated with founder-level seriousness. However, the person cannot independently approve payments, sign contracts, make hiring decisions, or alter business strategy.

### Required Qualifications

Background in a founder's office, CEO office, chief of staff, program management, or operations roles. Pharma, FMCG, or manufacturing experience is "useful but not mandatory; execution ability matters more than industry pedigree." Proficiency in Google Workspace and task-tracking tools like Notion required.

### Critical Traits Emphasized

"Persistent without creating friction" is identified as the single most important quality, along with high agency, strong organization, written communication excellence, sound judgment, and discretion regarding sensitive business information.
