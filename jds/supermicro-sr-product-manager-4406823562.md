****Job Req ID: 28873  
  
**About Supermicro  
  
**Supermicro® is a Top Tier provider of advanced server, storage, and networking solutions for Data Center, Cloud Computing, Enterprise IT, Hadoop/ Big Data, Hyperscale, HPC and IoT/Embedded customers worldwide. We are the \#5 fastest growing company among the Silicon Valley Top 50 technology firms. Our unprecedented global expansion has provided us with the opportunity to offer a large number of new positions to the technology community. We seek talented, passionate, and committed engineers, technologists, and business leaders to join us.  
  
**Job Summary  
  
**Supermicro is seeking a Sr. Product Manager who can lead the development and integration of server/workstation system products for cloud and data center infrastructure. This is a key product management role focused on server/storage product solutions where communication among remote teams and on-site product leadership for our development teams is critical to develop and enable operations to fulfill roadmap expectation. In addition to developing new top-of-the-line systems, product manager is expected to collaborate with marketing and sales departments to promote products and interact with customers directly to sell these products. As a product manager, you will use strong technical and communication skills towards product definition strategy for Supermicro's core product family including research into vertical-based customer solutions and creation of aggressive go-to market plan. Ultimately, Product Manager will become a core member of the server product management team and engage in technical, product development, marketing, and pre/post-sales support activities.  
  
**Essential Duties And Responsibilities  
  
** **Essential Duties and Responsibilities:  
  
**Includes the following essential duties and responsibilities (other duties may also be assigned):  
  

-   Product Strategy & Lifecycle Ownership

-   Own end-to-end product lifecycle management for Client Systems / AI PC / Workstation portfolio, including product launch, refresh planning, and EOL strategy

-   Define product positioning and segmentation across Enterprise, Commercial, Consumer, and AI PC segments

-   Develop and maintain product roadmap aligned with AI PC trends, cost dynamics, and market demand shifts

-   Market-Driven Product Definition

-   Translate market trends (AI PC adoption, cost pressure, regional demand) into clear product requirements and portfolio strategy

-   Drive competitive analysis, pricing strategy, and feature differentiation

-   Ensure product-market fit by aligning customer needs, business objectives, and engineering feasibility

-   Cross-Functional Leadership & Execution

-   Partner closely with Engineering, Architecture, Operations, and Sales to influence product direction and ensure execution alignment

-   Drive decision-making across global teams (US HQ, Taiwan, EMEA) to enable timely product delivery and launch readiness

-   Act as a bridge between business and engineering, ensuring strategy is translated into executable plans

-   Product Marketing & Promotion

-   Lead product marketing strategy, messaging, and value proposition development

-   Develop and deliver customer-facing materials, including product presentations, positioning decks, and training content

-   Support regional go-to-market execution, including customer engagement, demos, and promotional activities

-   Customer & Stakeholder Engagement

-   Engage directly with customers, partners, and internal stakeholders to:

-   -   Communicate product vision and roadmap
    -   Gather feedback and market intelligence
    -   Drive design wins and business opportunities

-   Influence stakeholders at multiple levels, including executives, engineering teams, and sales organizations

-   Business Execution & Performance Management

-   Support business growth through portfolio planning, cost optimization, and lifecycle management

-   Identify risks and proactively escalate issues to ensure on-time execution and business continuity

-   Enable scaling of Client System business through structured processes, tools, and execution discipline  

**Qualifications  
  
** **Education & Experience  
  
**

-   Bachelor’s degree in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, Business, or related field; MBA or Master’s degree preferred
-   10+ years of experience in PC / computer hardware product management, product marketing, or business strategy roles
-   Proven experience managing end-to-end product lifecycle in PC, Client System, or related industries  
      

**Technical & Business Expertise**  
  

-   Strong understanding of PC architecture, platform roadmap (Intel/AMD), and system-level design

-   Solid business acumen with experience in:

-   -   Product positioning and segmentation
    -   Cost structure and pricing strategy
    -   Market and competitive analysis

-   Experience in AI PC or emerging technology segments is a strong plus  

**Leadership & Execution Capability  
  
**

-   Demonstrated ability to lead cross-functional teams without direct authority
-   Proven track record of driving product strategy into execution across global teams
-   Strong problem-solving skills with the ability to operate effectively under ambiguity and fast-changing market conditions  
      

**Communication & Influence**  
  

-   Excellent communication and presentation skills, with ability to influence:

-   -   Senior leadership
    -   Engineering teams
    -   Customers and partners

-   Ability to clearly articulate complex technical concepts into business value propositions  

**Personal Attributes  
  
**

-   Highly self-driven with strong ownership mindset
-   Strategic thinker with hands-on execution capability
-   Comfortable working in a fast-paced, high-growth, and cost-sensitive environment
