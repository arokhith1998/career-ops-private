About Us
At LangChain, our mission is to enable every company to own its intelligence. We believe agents will transform how companies operate, and we're building the foundation to make that future possible. Our open-source harnesses and LangSmith platform give developers and enterprises the infrastructure to run, improve, and govern agents at scale.
We've raised $460M to date, with our most recent funding round valuing the company at $3B, backed by leading investors including Sequoia, Benchmark, IVP, and CapitalG. As we continue to expand our platform and tackle some of the hardest challenges in agent engineering, every team member has the opportunity to make a meaningful impact and help shape both our technology and the future of the industry.
More than 7,000 organizations rely on LangSmith, from fast-growing startups to Fortune 500 enterprises, including NVIDIA, Bridgewater, LinkedIn, Workday, and Rippling. We're just getting started on making high-performing agents easier to build and manage at scale.

About the Team
The Monetization team sits at the center of LangChain's growth engine. We own the systems, surfaces, and strategies that connect the value of LangSmith to revenue - from pricing and packaging to billing infrastructure, upgrade flows, and the product mechanics that move users from free to paid to enterprise. We work closely with Product, Engineering, Sales, and Finance to make sure the way we charge for our platform reflects the value it delivers and scales as our customers scale.

About the Role
We're looking for a Product Manager to own monetization across LangSmith. This is a high-leverage, high-visibility role: you'll define how we price and package a rapidly evolving platform, build the in-product experiences that drive conversion and expansion, and make sure our billing infrastructure keeps pace with the business.

What You'll do
- Own the end-to-end monetization product surface: pricing pages, upgrade flows, usage dashboards, billing management, and the in-app signals that drive conversion and expansion.
- Define and iterate on LangSmith's pricing and packaging - usage-based, seat-based, or hybrid - in close partnership with Finance, Sales, and leadership.
- Instrument and analyze the funnel: identify where users drop off, where expansion signals appear, and what product changes move the needle on activation, retention, and revenue.
- Build the billing and entitlement infrastructure alongside engineering - usage metering, plan enforcement, trial mechanics, and enterprise contract support.
- Partner with the Sales and Solutions Engineering teams to understand enterprise buying motions and design packaging that maps cleanly to how large organizations buy software.
- Translate customer conversations and data signals into crisp requirements; ship iteratively and measure impact.
- Help shape LangChain's broader go-to-market strategy alongside our founders and leadership team.

What you'll bring
- 4+ years of product management experience, with meaningful time spent on monetization, growth, or platform pricing at a B2B SaaS or developer-tools company.
- Strong analytical instincts - you're comfortable in the data, know which metrics matter, and can tell the difference between a signal and noise.
- Experience working on billing, entitlements, or usage-based pricing systems, and an appreciation for the technical complexity underneath them.
- A sharp intuition for the developer and enterprise buyer experience - you understand that a pricing page and a checkout flow are product problems, not just marketing ones.
- Ability to navigate ambiguity and move quickly: you can go from a rough hypothesis to a shipped experiment without needing everything to be perfectly defined first.
- Genuine curiosity about the AI/LLM space and how the infrastructure layer is evolving - you don't need to have worked in AI, but you need to want to learn it fast.
