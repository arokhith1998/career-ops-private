About the Opportunity:

The SENIOR DEAL DESK ANALYST will manage the end-to-end deal desk process from quoting to booking, serving as a key resource for sales teams on deal structuring. This role collaborates across functions including Revenue Operations, Legal, and Accounting to develop scalable processes and best practices that maintain sales velocity without sacrificing accuracy. The position offers an opportunity to impact commercial operations and support growth by ensuring consistent, effective deal management.

Responsibilities:

- Own deal desk and order management processes.
- Act as primary contact for Sales and Account Management on deal structure, pricing, quoting, and approvals.
- Structure and review deals, focusing on non-standard requests while balancing commercial policies.
- Maintain authoritative sources for deal requirements, sales templates, and approval matrices.
- Review closed deals, amendments, and cancellations in Salesforce for accuracy.
- Collaborate with RevOps, Finance, and Legal to ensure aligned decision-making.
- Build trusted relationships across functions to facilitate smooth deal progression.
- Analyze deal data to identify trends and improve pricing and policies.
- Identify opportunities to automate or simplify processes.
- Develop training materials and support Sales enablement on deal desk practices.

Requirements:

- Minimum 4 years experience in Deal Desk, Sales Operations, Revenue Operations, Finance, Sales Support, or related analytical roles.
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with business impact awareness.
- Knowledge of SaaS metrics and commercial concepts such as ARR, bookings, recurring revenue, and discounting.
- High attention to detail and accuracy.
- Effective communication skills across multiple stakeholder groups.
- Strong organizational abilities managing multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment.
- Experience with Salesforce required; CPQ experience strongly preferred.
- Finance or FP&A background is advantageous.
