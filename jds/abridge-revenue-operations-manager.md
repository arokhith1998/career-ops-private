About Abridge
Founded in 2018, Abridge is the first AI-native clinician intelligence platform organized around the patient, built for clinicians, and designed to help health systems coordinate the clinical, financial, and evidence-based decisions for every moment of care. Powered by purpose-built AI for healthcare, Abridge connects patients, providers, payers, and life sciences companies. This year, the platform will support more than 100 million patient-clinician conversations across more than 300 of the largest and most complex health systems in the U.S. With deep workflow integration, the Abridge clinician intelligence platform has been validated to support encounters in 28+ languages across specialties and care settings.

Abridge is setting the industry standard for the responsible deployment of AI across health systems with continual and transparent AI evaluation. Clinical decision support references and cites gold-standard sources of evidence, and features like Linked Evidence map AI-generated documentation to source data, helping clinicians quickly trust and verify the output.

Abridge was awarded Best in KLAS for Ambient AI in 2025 and 2026, in addition to other accolades, including TIME Best Inventions of 2024 and 2025, CNBC Disruptor 2025, and Fast Company's Most Innovative Companies 2025.

We are a growing team of practicing clinicians, AI scientists, PhDs, creatives, technologists, and engineers working together to transform healthcare. We have offices located in San Francisco, New York, and Pittsburgh.

The Role:
We are looking for a strategic and results-oriented Revenue Operations Manager to join our Revenue Operations team as the operational backbone of our Partner Experience (PX) organization, Partner Success, Clinical Success, Implementation, Support, and PX Ops. In this key role, you will own the systems, workflows, and analytics that power PX, including administration of Planhat and Zendesk, while keeping the post-sale motion tightly aligned with Sales, Marketing, and Finance.

As a trusted partner to leadership, you will provide actionable insights that align operations with company goals, helping to accelerate growth and optimize the partner journey, from implementation through expansion and renewal.

What You'll Do:
- Own the PX Tech Stack: Serve as administrator and product owner for Planhat and Zendesk, health scoring, playbooks, workflows, triggers, SLAs, routing, Salesforce integrations, and reporting. Assist with administration of our LMS/CMS platforms supporting partner education and enablement. Continuously improve these systems so PX teams maintain optimal performance.
- Optimize Operational Workflows: Design, refine, QA, and implement workflows and processes that enable PX teams to operate at peak efficiency. Streamline operations to ensure seamless handoffs across the full partner lifecycle, from deal close through implementation, clinical adoption, support, and renewal.
- Empower Data-Driven Insights and Strategic Decisions: Develop and maintain reports and dashboards that provide leadership with real-time visibility into key metrics, including partner health, adoption and utilization, support volume and SLA attainment, and retention trends. Use data to drive actionable strategies that improve decision making and operational performance.
- Enhance Revenue & Forecast Clarity: Partner with Finance to support renewal and retention forecasting, analyze commission reports, and generate revenue-related insights that align with business goals and drive clarity across the organization.
- Business Partner to PX Leadership: Act as the dedicated operations partner to leaders across Partner Success, Clinical Success, Implementation, Support, and PX Ops, translating their priorities into systems, process, and reporting, and representing PX needs in cross-functional RevOps planning with Sales, Marketing, and Finance.

What You'll Bring:
- 5+ years of experience in Revenue Operations, Customer Success Operations, Sales Operations, or a similar role in a SaaS company (healthcare experience is a plus).
- Extensive experience with SaaS tools such as Salesforce, Planhat (or Gainsight/similar CS platform), Zendesk, and exposure to LMS/CMS administration. Administration experience with a CS platform or support platform strongly preferred.
- Strong understanding of go-to-market strategies and key metrics that influence success in Sales, Marketing, and Partner Success.
- Direct experience business-partnering with post-sale leadership, Customer/Partner Success, Clinical or Implementation teams, or Support, you understand how these functions operate and what they need from operations.
- Fluency in post-sale metrics: net revenue retention, gross retention, adoption and utilization, NPS, CSAT, and support SLA performance.
- Exceptional AI and Business Intelligence experience (Claude, OpenAI, Excel/Google Sheets, SQL, Business Intelligence tools, etc.), with the ability to interpret complex data, identify trends, and translate insights into actionable operational plans.
- Proven track record in building and maintaining commercial dashboards and reports that provide clarity and support data-driven decision-making.
- Demonstrated ability to align cross-functional teams around shared goals, priorities, and revenue-driving strategies.
- Excellent project management skills, with a history of successfully leading and driving complex initiatives to completion.
- Strong communication and interpersonal skills, with the ability to influence stakeholders and collaborate effectively across teams.
- A proactive, problem-solving mindset, with a focus on continuous improvement and operational scalability.
