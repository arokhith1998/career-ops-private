## About the Role

A hands-on Product Marketing Manager to lead US product marketing. Must be a strong writer with solid technical depth, capable of owning positioning and messaging, building the sales toolkit, and turning engineering data into proof for technical buyers.

## Responsibilities

- Positioning & Messaging: own ICP, value proposition, website and pricing page; build the sales toolkit (master deck, competitive battlecards, one-pagers).
- Proof & Content: turn engineering data into proof (benchmark reports, migration guides, customer case studies); build proposal templates and reusable sales content.
- Launches & Market Feedback: lead launches for new models and product capabilities; bring customer/sales feedback back into product and pricing.

- A strong writer who produces finished work without a team behind you.
