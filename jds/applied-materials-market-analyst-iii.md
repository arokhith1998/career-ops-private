## Role
Market Analyst III at Applied Materials. Conducts complex benchmarking, market sizing, competitive analysis, and pricing/margin analysis to support business unit strategy and product roadmaps.

## Responsibilities
- Research marketing trends and conduct complex benchmarking analysis of the company's markets, competition and product mix.
- Manage market sizing and segmentation data for business unit strategy.
- Contribute to product roadmaps with pricing and margin analysis.
- Provide complex product forecasts. Develop product financial plans including cost reductions, margin maintenance and return on investment goals.
- Support field teams with direct product marketing to key accounts.
- Collect and analyze data for Red Team analysis and business reviews.
- Focus on KPU growth through data tracking and analysis.

## Required Qualifications
- MS in Data Science, Business Analytics, Statistics, or related quantitative field (BS considered with relevant experience).
- 3-5 years professional experience with data in analytical or business-facing roles.
- Proficiency in Python, SQL, and Excel for data analysis, transformation, and modeling.
- Experience with data visualization tools like Tableau.
- Ability to translate analytical results into clear insights.
- Strong analytical thinking and problem-solving skills.
