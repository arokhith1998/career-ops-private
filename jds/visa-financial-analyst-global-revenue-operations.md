**Summary:** The Global Revenue Operations team wants an analyst with finance, operations, analytics, and revenue systems experience to support revenue operations, pricing review approvals and setup, process improvements, and revenue/pricing system initiatives.

**Core duties:**

- Work with regional revenue operations teams and global stakeholders on contract reviews, pricing implementation, and fee guide updates
