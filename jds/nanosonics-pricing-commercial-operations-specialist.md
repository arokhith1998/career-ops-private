Nanosonics is a company that specializes in the development of innovative solutions for the automated reprocessing of reusable medical devices. Today, Nanosonics has a global presence with offices across Australia, Canada, the United States of America, Europe, the United Kingdom and Japan. Global HQ is located in Macquarie Park, Sydney, where all Research & Development, Manufacturing and Corporate functions reside. The US Corporate HQ is located in Indianapolis, IN. Position Purpose The Pricing & Commercial Operations Specialist provides experienced pricing analysis, operational discipline, and commercial support within the Commercial Operations team. Working under the direction of the Manager, Commercial Operations - Pricing & Commercial Programs, this role maintains pricing controls, evaluates pricing performance, develops fact-based recommendations, and supports the implementation and measurement of pricing and commercial programs. The position also owns the day-to-day administration of GPO rosters and customer activations, helping ensure accurate eligibility, timely processing, and effective coordination across internal teams and external partners. Working across Sales, Finance, National Accounts, Marketing, and Sales Operations & Analytics, this position provides the insight and operating discipline needed to connect pricing decisions with revenue, margin, attach rates, product mix, and broader commercial objectives. Core Responsibilities Maintain and administer approved price books, floor prices, pricing rules, and related controls across commercial systems, ensuring changes are accurate, timely, documented, and aligned with approved strategy. Analyze pricing performance and develop fact-based recommendations for pricing structures, discount guidance, approval thresholds, and related controls for review by the Manager, Commercial Operations - Pricing & Commercial Programs. Review and evaluate pricing exception requests before escalation to the designated approver, assessing business rationale, precedent, customer value, strategic fit, and potential revenue and margin impact. Monitor completed transactions for compliance with approved pricing, discount, promotion, and exception terms; identify patterns or control gaps and coordinate corrective action with appropriate stakeholders. Prepare pricing analyses, insights, and recommended actions for recurring reviews led by the Manager, Commercial Operations - Pricing & Commercial Programs, and participate in presenting findings as appropriate. Partner with Commercial Operations & Analytics to develop reliable pricing metrics, dashboards, and analyses that show how pricing decisions affect revenue, profitability, product mix, attach rates, promotional performance, and other commercial outcomes. Work closely with the National Accounts Manager and other commercial leaders to ensure enterprise pricing recommendations support customer value, long-term relationships, and Nanosonics' strategic and financial objectives. Provide pricing guidance and decision support to Sales leaders and field teams, clearly communicating approved guardrails, available programs, exception requirements, and the financial implications of proposed offers. Evaluate pricing performance by product, customer type, geography, channel, and commercial program; identify opportunities to improve price realization, consistency, adoption, and return on investment. Support the design, launch, measurement, and post-program review of promotions and commercial offerings, including defined objectives, pricing controls, eligibility, approval requirements, and success measures. Collaborate with Finance to validate pricing economics, revenue and margin assumptions, rebates, credits, fees, and other elements that influence net realized value. Partner with Marketing, National Accounts, Government Accounts, Customer Service, Contract Management, and other stakeholders to operationalize approved pricing and commercial programs. Apply AI-enabled analytics and automation, within approved company governance and with appropriate human review, to identify pricing patterns, surface anomalies, improve forecasting, and accelerate recurring analysis and reporting. Own the day-to-day management of GPO membership rosters and customer activations, including validation, processing, tracking, and resolution of discrepancies. Coordinate with National Accounts, Customer Service, Finance, Contract Management, and Sales Operations to ensure eligible customers are accurately aligned with approved GPO agreements and pricing. Monitor activation status, aging, data quality, and processing exceptions; provide regular visibility into outstanding items and required actions. Maintain supporting documentation, process controls, and audit-ready records for GPO affiliations, roster changes, and activations. Identify opportunities to improve or automate roster-management and activation processes while maintaining appropriate controls. Maintain clear documentation, standard operating procedures, decision records, and audit-ready support for pricing changes, exceptions, and commercial programs. Contribute to continuous improvement of pricing processes, data quality, system capabilities, and cross-functional operating mechanisms Experience & Qualifications Required Qualifications Bachelor's degree in business, finance, economics, analytics, marketing, or related discipline, or an equivalent combination of education and relevant experience. Five or more years of experience in pricing, commercial operations, revenue management, finance, sales analytics, or a related discipline; healthcare or medical device experience preferred. Demonstrated experience developing or administering price structures, discount controls, approval thresholds, price books, and exception-review processes. Strong analytical ability with experience translating transactional and financial data into clear commercial insights and recommendations for senior and field sales leaders. Understanding of price realization, discounting, revenue, margin, product mix, attach rates, promotions, rebates, and other factors that influence net commercial performance. Advanced Microsoft Excel skills and experience with Salesforce, Microsoft Dynamics 365 or a comparable ERP, Power BI or a comparable visualization platform, and related commercial systems. Ability to facilitate recurring business reviews, constructively challenge assumptions, and communicate complex pricing concepts in practical business terms. Strong data quality, documentation, and control orientation, with the judgment to recognize when an issue requires escalation.
