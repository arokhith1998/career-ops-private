Meet the Team

Our Sales Operations team serves as the backbone of our regional success, bridging the gap between high-level strategy and daily execution. We operate at the intersection of sales, finance, and business operations, ensuring our sellers have the data and tools they need to drive revenue and exceed targets. Our team is a diverse, collaborative group of analysts and operational experts who thrive on precision, automation, and continuous improvement. We are particularly excited about our current shift toward leveraging predictive modeling and advanced CRM automation to help our sales pods scale in an increasingly complex market.

Your Impact

- Analyze complex sales data to uncover performance trends, identify potential risks, and highlight critical gaps in the pipeline.
- Build and maintain sophisticated forecasting models and quota frameworks to provide real-time visibility into revenue health.
- Design scalable sales processes by leveraging CRM platforms and automation tools to streamline the quote-to-cash workflow.
- Integrate delivery timelines, customer schedules, and operational inputs to ensure our revenue projections remain accurate and actionable.
- Collaborate across sales, finance, and business functions to align operational execution with our broader go-to-market strategy.

Minimum Qualifications

- 5+ years of experience in sales operations, revenue analytics, or business process management.
- 3+ years of experience building and maintaining complex forecasting models and pipeline visibility tools.
- 3+ years of experience managing CRM platforms (e.g., Salesforce) and designing automated sales workflows.
- 3+ years of experience in conducting quantitative analysis of sales trends, margin performance, or pricing structures.
- 2+ years of experience partnering with finance and sales leadership to support go-to-market strategy execution.

Preferred Qualifications

- Expertise in SQL, Tableau, or PowerBI to drive data-backed insights and reporting automation.
- Experience in a cross-functional role supporting multi-cloud or AI-focused product lines.
- Strong verbal and written communication skills with the ability to present complex findings to senior sales leadership.
- Demonstrated ability to influence stakeholders and drive adoption of new operational tools or processes.
- Adaptability in fast-paced, high-growth environments with a focus on project management and prioritization.

Employees On Sales Plans Earn Performance-based Incentive Pay On Top Of Their Base Salary, Which Is Split Between Quota And Non-quota Components, Subject To The Applicable Cisco Plan. For Quota-based Incentive Pay, Cisco Typically Pays As Follows

- .75% of incentive target for each 1% of revenue attainment up to 50% of quota;
- 1.5% of incentive target for each 1% of attainment between 50% and 75%;
- 1% of incentive target for each 1% of attainment between 75% and 100%; and
- Once performance exceeds 100% attainment, incentive rates are at or above 1% for each 1% of attainment with no cap on incentive compensation.

For non-quota-based sales performance elements such as strategic sales objectives, Cisco may pay 0% up to 125% of target. Cisco sales plans do not have a minimum threshold of performance for sales incentive compensation to be paid.
