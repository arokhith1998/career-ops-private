- Build and scale internal pricing / commercial processes to match Decagon's growth
- Help shape Decagon's commercial strategy alongside the rest of the Founder's Office team
- Be a thought leader in how to price agentic AI solutions

- 2+ years of experience in investment banking / private equity / venture capital or high-velocity startups
- Deep curiosity and desire to solve hard problems
- Comfort working in fast-moving, ambiguous environments
- Strong cross-functional collaborator
- Top tier communication skills -- both verbal and written
