## Full description (verbatim via Greenhouse Job Board API, HTML stripped)
Innovate with purpose. At BILL, we believe in empowering the businesses that drive our economy, replacing outdated financial processes with innovative tools. Remote-first, with office spaces in San Jose, CA and Draper, UT.

Make your impact within a rapidly growing Fintech Company. The Product Marketing team at BILL drives bringing products to market and growing their impact. As a Staff Product Marketing Manager, you will architect the go-to-market (GTM) strategy for the Accounting Channel and build the systems that help teams execute across the full GTM lifecycle, partnering across Marketing, Product, Sales, and Customer Success. You'll lead GTM strategy for Accounting Channel (AC) acquisition, retention, and enablement across new-business and managed sales motions, and lead competitive analysis, market research, customer advocacy.

Key Responsibilities
- Architect GTM strategy for the Accountant Channel; set KPIs with Sales/Product; track progress.
- Influence roadmap/investment decisions for the segment; define research learning agenda.
- Champion for accountants: turn insights (needs, journeys, segments) into messaging/programs; understand accounting workflows, client management needs, AP advisory practice.
- Lead audience segmentation, positioning, messaging; create compelling product narratives.
- Lead complex, multi-team launches/programs end to end; iterate the GTM playbook.
- Partner with Sales on enablement content/tools/processes.
- Run win/loss, competitive, funnel analysis; develop pricing POVs for the Pricing team.

We'd love to chat if you have
- "12+ years of professional experience in Product Marketing (or closely related role), with direct experience at the Senior Product Marketing Manager level."
- Proven B2B SaaS and/or fintech experience, preference for companies selling to accounting/finance professionals or financial operations software.
- Track record of successful product/feature launches (positioning, messaging, GTM strategy).
- Experience supporting a channel sales or indirect distribution motion.
- Market research, competitive analysis, customer segmentation experience.
- Demonstrated ability to influence senior stakeholders without direct authority.
- Strong written/verbal communication, VP/executive presentation experience.

Preferred Qualifications: product/segment marketing to accountants/financial advisors/B2B enterprise channels (payments/fintech experience heavily preferred); training/battle cards/objection handling/payments playbooks experience; ability to set direction in ambiguous environments; skilled storyteller across products/segments; pricing/packaging exposure.
