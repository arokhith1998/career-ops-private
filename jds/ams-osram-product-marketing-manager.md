**Job description:**

The Product Marketing Manager will help drive the growth of our Optical Interconnect business by serving as a key interface between customers, partners, engineering, and business leadership. This individual will support customer engagement, business development, product positioning, and the creation of compelling marketing and technical content.

**Key Responsibilities**

- Support customer meetings, technical reviews, and commercial discussions.
- Develop and maintain customer-facing presentations, messaging, and value propositions.
- Generate content for blogs, webinars, conference presentations, and executive communications.
- Conduct market, customer, and competitive analysis to identify business opportunities.
- Support business development activities, ecosystem partnerships, and lead generation efforts.
- Work closely with product management, engineering, and sales teams to translate technology into clear customer benefits.
- Help define go-to-market strategies for next-generation optical interconnect products.

**Profile description:**

- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Engineering, Physics, Marketing, Business, or related field.
- 5+ years of experience in product marketing, product management, business development, or technical sales.
- Experience in semiconductors, photonics, optical communications, datacenter infrastructure, or related technologies preferred.
- Strong communication and presentation skills, with the ability to explain complex technical concepts to business and technical audiences.
- Comfortable engaging directly with customers, partners, and senior leadership.
