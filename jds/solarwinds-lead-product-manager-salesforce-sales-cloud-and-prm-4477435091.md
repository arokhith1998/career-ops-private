We are seeking a Lead Product Manager to own the Salesforce Lead-to-Opportunity and Partner Relationship Management (PRM) domains within the IT Business Applications team. This is a senior individual contributor role for a true top-of-funnel expert, someone who has lived the mechanics of lead capture, scoring, routing, and conversion, who can speak fluently about MQL, SAL, and SQL definitions and the handoff points between them, and who understands what breaks when marketing-to-sales handoff is poorly designed.You will own the full path from first touch to qualified opportunity across both direct and partner-led motions: lead management and routing, marketing-to-sales handoff, account and territory management, deal registration and partner co-sell, and the data foundation that pipeline and forecasting depend on. You will set the roadmap for these domains, lead solution design, and direct an offshore Salesforce delivery team through execution.
This role sits at the center of the go-to-market engine. You will partner daily with leaders across Marketing Operations, Sales Development, Sales Operations, and Channel, and your decisions will show up directly in conversion rates, speed-to-lead, partner-sourced pipeline, and forecast accuracy.Key ResponsibilitiesLead Management & Top-of-Funnel ProcessOwn the end-to-end lead lifecycle in Salesforce: capture, enrichment, deduplication, scoring, qualification, routing, conversion, and recycling/nurture return paths
Define and govern lead stage and status models, including MQL, SAL, and SQL definitions, entry and exit criteria, and the disposition reasons that make funnel reporting trustworthy
Design and continuously tune lead routing and assignment logic, round robin, territory-based, account-based, and capacity-aware models, along with SLA enforcement and escalation for unworked leads
Own the marketing-to-sales handoff: alignment with Marketing Operations on campaign attribution, Marketing Automation Platform, MAP-to-CRM sync, form and inbound channel intake, and clean transfer of context to Sales Development
Improve speed-to-lead and lead-to-opportunity conversion through automation, data quality controls, and AI-assisted qualification where it delivers measurable value
Define and drive lead-to-account matching and account-based motions, ensuring inbound demand lands with the right owner on the right account

Partner Relationship Management & ChannelOwn the PRM platform and the partner-facing experience: partner onboarding, tiering, user provisioning, and portal capability
Design and own the deal registration process end to end, submission, validation, approval, expiry, renewal, and channel conflict resolution rules
Enable partner-sourced and partner-influenced pipeline tracking with clean attribution across direct, reseller, distributor, and co-sell motions
Partner with Channel Sales and Partner Marketing on MDF, incentive, co-marketing, and partner performance reporting capabilities
Align partner and direct territory, account ownership, and crediting rules so both motions can operate without contention

Account, Territory & ForecastingOwn account and contact management standards: hierarchy design, account segmentation, ownership rules, data stewardship, and enrichment strategy
Own territory management, territory model design, assignment rules, quota alignment, and the annual and mid-year planning and realignment cycles
Own opportunity management standards including stage definitions, exit criteria, required fields, and pipeline hygiene controls
Deliver forecasting capability in Salesforce: forecast categories, roll-up hierarchies, submission and commit workflows, adjustments, and forecast accuracy reporting
Partner with Sales Operations and Finance to ensure pipeline, bookings, and forecast data reconcile across CRM and reporting systems

Product Strategy & RoadmapOwn a minimum four-quarter rolling roadmap for the Lead-to-Opportunity and PRM domains, sequenced with functional leadership
Build business cases for significant investments, quantifying impact on conversion, cycle time, pipeline coverage, and partner revenue
Define and report on domain KPIs, speed-to-lead, MQL-to-SAL-to-SQL conversion, routing SLA compliance, deal registration cycle time, partner-sourced pipeline, forecast accuracy, and CRM data quality
Provide strategic guidance on process design and technology footprint, including evaluation of Salesforce native capability versus AppExchange or adjacent GTM tooling
Contribute to CRM platform architecture and governance decisions alongside the Director and peer product managers

Delivery & ExecutionLead discovery and design for new capabilities, producing functional and high-level technical design documents for the offshore delivery team
Work closely with offshore Salesforce development teams across sprint cycles, owning backlog priority, story quality, and acceptance
Coordinate cross-system impacts across marketing automation, data enrichment, CPQ, and downstream reporting and data platforms
Own release readiness, test strategy, UAT coordination with business users, deployment planning, and post-release validation
Manage concurrent workstreams and set clear expectations with stakeholders on scope, sequencing, and timelines

Stakeholder Leadership & EnablementAct as the trusted advisor to Marketing Operations, Sales Development, Sales Operations, and Channel leadership on funnel and partner process design
