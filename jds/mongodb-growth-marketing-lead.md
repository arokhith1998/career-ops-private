## Role Summary
Develops and runs lifecycle marketing programs driving developer adoption, retention, and
expansion on MongoDB Atlas. Centers on product-led growth, personalized messaging (email,
in-product, web), experimentation. Described as highly visible, requiring cross-functional
leadership with Product Management, Analytics, Data Science, Customer Success, Product
Marketing, Developer Relations.

## Responsibilities
- Partner with Product on digital onboarding, adoption, retention, expansion experiences.
- Define/execute marketing plans; turn ideas into measurable campaigns.
- Optimize programs/channels through experiments and A/B tests.
- Improve user journey at onboarding, activation, re-engagement.
- Brainstorm new content and repurpose existing content.
- Perform product, market, customer analysis to find growth opportunities.
- Identify emerging channels to accelerate growth.
- Monitor, analyze, report KPIs against revenue objectives.
- Align messaging with Product Marketing and product team around launches.

## Requirements
- 5+ years of B2B or B2D lifecycle and/or growth marketing experience. Product-led growth
  experience desired; agent-led growth experience a bonus.
- Deep understanding of marketing to developers or a highly technical audience.
- Experience owning end-to-end lifecycle campaigns, optimizing across the customer journey.
- Data-driven decision-making, commitment to measuring results.
- Collaborative, owner mindset, comfort with ambiguity.
- Proven ability to turn ideas into measurable programs.
- Knowledge of product-led growth strategies and KPIs.
- Strong writing skills; ability to storyboard campaign themes/messages.
