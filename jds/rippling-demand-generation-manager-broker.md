## Job Description (extracted, verbatim from posting)

Scaling to $1B ARR and beyond means Rippling needs a go-to-market engine driving growth across dozens of product SKUs, sales segments, buyer personas, and countries - the Broker channel is one of the most important levers for growth. The Demand Generation Manager aligned to the Broker channel will help deepen engagement, drive pipeline, and make Rippling the go-to solution for benefits brokers and their clients, combining demand generation, partner marketing, product marketing, content strategy, and channel-facing communications. Highly cross-functional with Broker Sales and the broader Marketing org.

**What You Will Do:**
- Build and execute quarterly campaign themes across email, webinar, social, events, plus always-on broker nurture programs
- Own broker messaging and positioning
- Create sales-support content (co-branded assets, case studies, first call decks)
- Partner with Channel Sales to identify gaps and deliver tailored campaigns
- Analyze campaign ROI and iterate quickly
- Collaborate across Sales Leadership, Revenue Operations, Product Marketing, Partner Success, and Marketing Operations

**What You Will Need:**
- "4-6 years of B2B marketing experience with a strong foundation in demand gen, product marketing, or partner/channel marketing"
- Proven track record running outbound campaigns and digital programs
- Full-funnel thinker; data-driven and results-oriented
- Strong cross-functional communicator

Preferred: experience in broker marketing, benefits, insurance, HRIS, payroll ecosystem; 0-to-1 program building; Salesforce campaign/report fluency; strong copywriting.
