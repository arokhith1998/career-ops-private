## Full JD Text

Role Overview

We’re looking for a finance professional with 1–2 years of experience who is eager to take on a broad, high-ownership role within the Founders’ Office. At Arbor Mobility, you’ll work closely with the founders and business teams across finance, operations, strategy, and business planning in the fast-growing electric mobility and energy space. You’ll get hands-on exposure to key business decisions, financial analysis, growth initiatives, and cross-functional projects while helping build and scale an infrastructure-heavy venture from the ground up.

Key Responsibilities

- Work closely with the founders on financial modelling, forecasting, budgeting, and business planning.

- Support on investor decks, financial analysis, fundraising and data rooms.

- Provide financial inputs and analysis for business expansion, fleet scaling, new regions, and infrastructure decisions.

- Drive and streamline finance processes to improve efficiency and business visibility.

- Coordinate on purchase orders (POs), month-end payroll, and other finance-related operational activities.

- Track and analyse cost structures across vehicles, energy, and operations to identify opportunities for optimization.

- Support pricing, cost optimization, and margin improvement initiatives.

- Work closely with cross-functional teams to bring financial discipline and data-driven thinking into day-to-day business decisions.

- Oversee day-to-day accounting, financial reporting, and compliance including GST, TDS, Income Tax, and ROC.

- Prepare and analyse financial statements, MIS, and cash flow reports to provide real-time visibility into the business.

- Work directly with auditors, consultants, regulatory bodies, and external stakeholders as required.

- Take ownership of ad-hoc financial and business analysis to support founders in key strategic decisions.

Qualifications Required:

- B.Com/M.Com/MBA Graduate or recently qualified CFA/CMA/CA (01 years) with strong fundamentals in financial modelling, forecasting, budgeting, accounting and Excel proficiency.

- High ownership mindset and attention to detail, paired with sharp analytical thinking and problem-solving skills

- Comfortable thriving in a fast-paced, evolving startup environment

Company Details

Arbor Mobility is a 2W EV start-up based out of Bengaluru, India, focused on developing a performance two-wheeler EV motorcycle. The company was founded in November 2022 and is rapidly expanding. Arbor Mobility aims to increase the adoption of EVs in India by addressing issues such as charging, range anxiety, and safety for consumers with an innovative customer-centric approach to product development. Joining Arbor Mobility offers an exciting opportunity to contribute to the future of the automotive industry in India and globally.
