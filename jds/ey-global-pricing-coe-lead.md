The opportunity

The Global Pricing CoE Lead is responsible for advancing EY’s pricing agenda by driving excellence in pricing execution, commercialization, and innovation. The role focuses on enabling value-led pricing for the firm’s most strategic and transformative opportunities, supporting the adoption of global pricing standards, and contributing to the evolution of EY’s technology- and AI-enabled pricing ecosystem. Working closely with regional pricing teams, the Global Pricing CoE Leader supports the effort to drive consistent, scalable, and value value-led approach to pricing across the firm.

The role is responsible to lead the commercialization of the firm’s most strategic, complex, and innovative opportunities through dedicated pursuit support and drive our firm’s profitable growth ambitions. The role engages in cross-Service Line, cross-Region pursuits spanning top-end audit, transformation, and managed services, executing innovative positioning, pricing, monetization and commercialization strategies aligned with the differentiated value of our services. The leader helps accelerate profitable growth through improved win rates, revenue expansion, and margin enhancement.

The Global Pricing CoE Leader will support in the development, governance, and continuous evolution of EY’s pricing frameworks, methodologies, standards, and leading practices. Working closely with Regional Pricing Leaders and the Regional Pricing Coordinator, the leader maintains a pulse on market dynamics, emerging pricing trends, and successful regional innovations. The role identifies high-value practices, tools, and methodologies developed across regions, helps codify them into scalable and repeatable assets, and champions their adoption as global standards and leading practices across the pricing community. Through thought leadership, stakeholder engagement, and influence, the leader promotes the adoption of pricing methods and processes while balancing global consistency with the flexibility required to address local market needs. Together, these efforts help strengthen EY’s pricing capabilities, improve commercial outcomes, and enhance the firm’s competitive position globally.

In addition, the Global Pricing CoE Leader supports the evolution of EY’s pricing technology and innovation agenda, contributing to the development of the firm's pricing roadmap, including pricing tools, standards, and the broader AI-enabled pricing ecosystem. The role provides strategic input and governance support for PINPoint and related pricing technologies, partnering closely with the PINPoint Lead to help align platform priorities, capability enhancements, adoption objectives, delivery milestones, and business outcomes with EY’s growth and commercial transformation agenda.
