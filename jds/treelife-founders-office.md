Founder’s Office (4 – 6 Years Experience, BKC Mumbai)

About the Role
We’re looking for a smart, driven, high-ownership generalist to work directly with the Founders. Someone who is young, hustling, structured, and can convert ideas into execution with precision. If you're a task master with strong business instincts and exceptional PPT skills, this is for you.

Key Responsibilities

- Act as the Founder’s right hand across strategy, operations, and execution.

- Build high-quality presentations, reports, business cases, and internal documents.

- Drive projects end-to-end: planning → execution → follow-up → closure.

- Coordinate across teams and ensure timely delivery of tasks and priorities.

- Manage daily workflows, communication, and stakeholder updates.

- Step in wherever needed — no task is too big or small.

Requirements

- 4 – 6 years experience in operations, founder’s office or biz ops.

- Excellent PowerPoint & presentation-building skills; strong storytelling ability.

- High business acumen — ability to understand numbers, markets & decision drivers.

- Task-master mentality — follow-ups, structure, speed, and accountability.

- Strong communication & stakeholder management skills.

- Comfortable with AI tools, Excel/Sheets, and general tech tools.

- Thrives in fast-moving, ambiguous environments.

Culture & What to Expect

- Work directly with the Founder — high visibility, high trust, high learning.

- Fast execution environment; no ego, no bureaucracy.

- Opportunity to see 10→100 building across functions.

- Ideal for someone ambitious who wants accelerated growth.
