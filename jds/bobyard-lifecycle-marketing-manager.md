About Bobyard Construction is a $10 trillion industry still running on manual work. Contractors measure and count materials off drawings by hand — it takes forever, and it caps how many jobs they can bid. Bobyard uses AI to automate takeoffs and estimates, cutting takeoff time by 50–90% and letting contractors send more bids. Driving end-to-end strategy, execution, and conversion targets, you will map user journeys and deploy targeted programs across email, in-app touchpoints, and educational collateral.
