## Job Description (extracted, verbatim from posting)

We are looking for a strategic and analytical Revenue Operations Manager to join the Brands team at Roblox. In this role, you will build and own the RevOps function for our Brands advertising business, establishing the rhythm of business, forecasting infrastructure, and GTM operational backbone that enables our Sales and Ad Ops teams to scale.

**You Will:**
- Revenue Planning & Forecasting: own pipeline and revenue forecasting for the Brands business, build operational dashboards, define metric standards
- Rhythm of Business (RoB): design and run the Brands weekly business review (WBR)
- GTM & Cross-Functional Execution: translate GTM priorities into actionable plans, resolve cross-functional misalignment, support quarterly planning
- Reporting Infrastructure & Automation: automate manual reporting workflows, maintain CRM data quality standards
- Operational Rigor & Process Design: document/systematize repeatable operational processes

**You Have:**
- "8+ years of experience in revenue operations, programmatic, sales operations, or a related function within digital advertising or media."
- Strong analytical skills, SQL or equivalent a plus
- Experience designing and running a rhythm of business (WBR, QBR, or equivalent)

Roles based in San Mateo, CA HQ are onsite Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday.
