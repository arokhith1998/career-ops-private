## Position Summary
Establish and scale OpenAI's advertiser-facing reporting, measurement, and attribution credibility. Combine deep measurement expertise with judgment and cross-functional leadership to define how advertisers understand performance on OpenAI. Initial IC role building the methodological foundations of OpenAI's advertising measurement system; over time, define strategy, operating model, and team needed to scale this function globally.

## Responsibilities
- Define OpenAI's advertiser measurement strategy: reporting aligned with multi-touch attribution (MTA), incrementality and lift testing, marketing mix modeling (MMM), geo experimentation, and partner measurement frameworks.
- Build the foundation of OpenAI's Marketing Science function. Lead initially as IC; design long-term team structure, operating model, and measurement programs.
- Lead advertiser-facing measurement discussions; represent OpenAI in executive briefings, measurement escalations, and industry conversations; build trust in methodologies and reporting.
- Develop clear advertiser narratives that translate causal inference, attribution models, and statistical methodologies into decision frameworks for technical and non-technical audiences.
- Build scalable measurement frameworks that reconcile results across MMM, MTA, and lift testing; help advertisers triangulate OpenAI performance within broader marketing measurement systems.

## Required Qualifications
- Deep expertise in advertising measurement: experimentation, incrementality testing, attribution modeling, econometric approaches such as MMM.
- Ability to translate complex statistical concepts into practical decision frameworks for technical and non-technical audiences.
- Build functions from the ground up; execute hands-on; partner with Product and Engineering to translate measurement science into scalable product capabilities.
- Thrive in fast-paced, high-ambiguity environments.
- Care for trusted, privacy-forward measurement systems for long-term advertiser confidence.
