## Job Description (full, verbatim)
In this role, you'll help build how AI agents and AI factories are secured at runtime. As agents gain autonomy, take actions, and call tools across the AI factory, security can no longer live only inside the software it protects. You'll guide the product definition of NVIDIA Sentry and DOCA security features, including confidential computing. You'll engage with multiple teams across NVIDIA, create collaborative value offerings with prominent security partners, and encourage technology and DOCA adoption among customers. This is a rare opportunity to influence a new security category as it reaches a pivotal moment.

Ways To Stand Out From The Crowd
- Product leadership in AI security, cloud workload protection, or network security at a leading cybersecurity company.
- Experience developing security solutions on DPUs or SmartNICs, such as BlueField, or using DOCA.
- Hands-on experience with confidential computing technologies, such as Intel TDX, AMD SEV-SNP, Arm CCA, or confidential GPUs and trusted I/O.
- Experience in agentic AI security, AI guardrails, agent identity, AI gateways, or agent runtimes and reference architectures like NVIDIA OpenShell.
- Proven history of developing combined solutions alongside security associates and encouraging platform use by customers.
