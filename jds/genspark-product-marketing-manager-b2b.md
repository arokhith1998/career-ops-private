We're looking for a Senior Product Marketing Manager, B2B to shape how Genspark positions and brings its AI platform to businesses around the world.

As a foundational member of our B2B marketing team, you'll turn Genspark's rapidly evolving capabilities into compelling business value, build differentiated product narratives, and help drive adoption and revenue growth. Reporting to the Head of B2B Marketing, you'll partner closely with Product, Sales, Demand Generation, and our Consumer Product Marketing team to build a strong, cohesive global go-to-market presence.

This role is ideal for a strategic storyteller and hands-on builder who thrives in a fast-moving environment and is eager to shape Genspark's B2B product marketing function.

What You'll Do

Own B2B positioning and messaging
- Develop differentiated positioning, messaging, and value propositions for Genspark's business offerings across SMB, mid-market, and enterprise segments.
- Translate complex AI capabilities into compelling business benefits, use cases, and customer outcomes that resonate with buyers and end users.
- Build deep expertise in customer needs, buyer personas, and the competitive landscape to inform positioning and product strategy.
- Partner with Consumer Product Marketing to develop consistent, scalable product narratives tailored to business audiences and global markets.

Drive product go-to-market strategy
- Develop GTM strategies for new products, features, and business offerings in partnership with Product and Marketing leadership.
- Lead B2B product launches from positioning and messaging through enablement and execution, coordinating with Consumer Product Marketing on shared launches.
- Partner with Demand Generation and regional marketing teams to bring product stories and use cases to market through integrated campaigns.
- Leverage customer insights, market research, and competitive intelligence to influence product strategy, pricing, packaging, and GTM priorities.

Enable sales and bring customer value to life
- Equip Sales teams with compelling presentations, competitive battlecards, objection handling, and product narratives that help win business.
- Develop customer-facing assets, including solution briefs, use-case stories, demos, and website content that communicate Genspark's business value.
- Partner with Sales and Customer Success to capture customer success stories, quantify business impact, and build credible proof points.
- Continuously refine messaging and sales enablement based on customer feedback, competitive insights, and sales performance.

Who You Are
- Experienced B2B product marketer: 6+ years in product marketing, ideally in high-growth SaaS, AI, or enterprise technology, with a track record of building differentiated positioning and successful go-to-market programs.
- Exceptional storyteller and strategic thinker: Skilled at translating complex technical capabilities into clear, compelling narratives that connect with business buyers and differentiate products in competitive markets.
- Customer-obsessed and commercially minded: Deep understanding of B2B buyer journeys, customer research, segmentation, competitive positioning, and the factors that drive purchase decisions.
- Hands-on GTM leader: Proven ability to own and execute product launches, develop sales enablement, and create high-impact marketing assets. You use AI tools, agents, and workflows to accelerate research, content creation, and product marketing execution.
- Collaborative builder with a global mindset: Experience developing scalable product marketing programs for global markets, with the resourcefulness, curiosity, and adaptability to thrive in a fast-moving environment.

Location: Remote-friendly across the US. Preference for NYC and Palo Alto.

About Genspark

Genspark.ai is a Palo Alto, CA-based technology company building agentic AI for more than one billion global knowledge workers. Founded by veterans from Microsoft, Google, Meta, YouTube, and Pinterest, Genspark has raised $645 million from investors including Emergence Capital Partners, LG, SBI, UpHonest, and Temasek's Pavilion Capital. Genspark orchestrates 70+ state-of-the-art AI models to turn business objectives into finished deliverables, including boardroom presentations, financial models, client-ready documents, full-stack web applications, mobile apps, and more. Empowered by partnerships with OpenAI, Anthropic, AWS, Microsoft, and many other industry leaders, Genspark serves both individual users and enterprise clients worldwide. Built for every knowledge worker, whether leading a team or just starting their career. Learn more at genspark.ai.
