Founder's Office -- Growth & Partnerships @ 1acre.in

At 1acre.in, we are building the digital infrastructure for India's land marketplace. Land is the country's most opaque asset class, and we are solving it. Take a look at the platform at https://1acre.in to understand what we do.
This role is the human face of 1acre for the people who use it most -- Subscribers, Developers, and Corporate partners. You'll guide them through the product, run demos, and drive outreach.

What you'll do
Product Guide -- Help Subscribers and Developers get the most out of the platform. Answer their questions, walk them through features, and spot the serious ones who fit our transactions.
Lead Handling -- Own incoming leads for our high-value products and services -- Syndicates, Corporate land acquisitions, and more. Qualify them, respond fast, and take them through to the deal team.
Feedback Loop -- Every conversation is a feedback channel. Capture what's working, what's broken, what's missing -- and push it back to product and engineering so the platform gets sharper with every interaction.
Demos -- Run product demos online and offline, for Subscribers, Developers, and Corporates.
Outreach -- Reach out directly to Corporates and Developers when we have a transaction they should be part of, or a potential partnership worth exploring.

Must-haves: Clear communication, a real interest in the land market, and fluency with AI tools -- you'll use them every day.
Who we're looking for: Fresh grads or 0-3 years of work experience.
Strong bias for execution
Comfortable on calls and in meetings
Clear writer -- emails that get replies, not ignored

What you get: Complete ownership of the segment, and more as your scope scales.
Pay: Rs 50,000 per month + steep performance-linked incentives
