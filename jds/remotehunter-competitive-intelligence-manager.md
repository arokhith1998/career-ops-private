## Full Job Description

### 1. About Our Client
The organization operates in the competitive intelligence space within the broader technology sector, specifically addressing the challenge of interpreting market signals to provide actionable insights. It supports strategic decision-making by monitoring competitors, market dynamics, emerging technologies, and customer trends. The organization collaborates across various functions such as product marketing, sales, customer success, and executive leadership to influence strategy and market positioning.

### 2. About the Opportunity
The Manager, Competitive Intelligence leads and develops a team of analysts responsible for gathering and analyzing competitive and market information. This role focuses on delivering strategic insights that shape business decisions, influence go-to-market execution, and strengthen the organization's market differentiation. It is a leadership position that combines team management with analytical and business expertise.

### 3. Responsibilities
- Lead, coach, and develop a team of Competitive Intelligence Analysts.
- Set team priorities and manage resources aligned with business objectives.
- Develop scalable processes for monitoring competitors and market trends.
- Deliver actionable insights to inform product strategy and executive decisions.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to identify opportunities and mitigate risks.
- Create executive briefings, market assessments, and enablement resources.
- Gather and synthesize intelligence from various external and internal sources.
- Establish quality standards and best practices for competitive intelligence.
- Utilize AI-powered tools to enhance research efficiency and impact.
- Measure and communicate the impact of competitive intelligence programs.

### 4. Requirements
- Minimum 5 years of experience in competitive intelligence, market intelligence, product marketing, strategy, consulting, or related fields.
- Proven experience leading and developing knowledge-based professionals.
- Skilled in conducting research that informs strategic business decisions.
- Strong analytical capabilities, synthesizing qualitative and quantitative data.
- Excellent communication and presentation skills for senior and executive stakeholders.
- Ability to influence cross-functional teams without direct authority.
- Effective prioritization and project management skills.
- Familiarity with AI-enabled research and intelligence workflows.

### 5. Pay Range and Compensation Package
- Annual cash compensation range: $133,700 to $213,900 USD.
- Includes base salary, potential commission or bonus targets, and eligibility for equity plans where applicable.
