- Stakeholder management: Serve as a central coordination point for internal and external stakeholders, prioritize requests, manage confidential matters, and organize leadership meetings and events.
- Office operations: Manage the CEO's calendar and engagements, coordinate multi-location meetings with advance materials, and streamline administrative, logistical, and travel arrangements.

- Postgraduate degree from a recognized business school or equivalent institution.
- 8 to 10 years of relevant experience in project or program management, strategy, business operations, the CEO's Office, or similar roles.
- Experience managing complex, cross-functional initiatives with multiple stakeholders.
- Proficiency with project planning tools, dashboards, Gantt charts, management reporting, and executive presentations.
- Strong written and verbal communication skills.
- Prior education-sector experience is an advantage, not a requirement.
- Key competencies: strategic execution, analytical and reporting skills, executive communication, prioritization, risk management, stakeholder management, judgment and discretion, problem-solving, operational agility.
