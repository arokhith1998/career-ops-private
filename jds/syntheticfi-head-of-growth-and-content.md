## Company

SyntheticFi disrupts the ~$1T US securities-backed loans industry with box spreads - an innovative financing tool now available to retail investors via independent Registered Investment Advisors (RIAs). The industry is dominated by legacy banks; SyntheticFi delivers low-cost securities-backed lending to RIAs and their clients as a differentiator that retains AUM and wins new clients. Growth has been 20%+ MoM over the past year. HQ in San Francisco with a remote team globally.

## Role

Head of Growth and Content - joining the leadership team at the intersection of growth marketing and content generation. Works directly with cofounder Joseph Wang to design and implement the growth playbook, work with top partners, and lead a team driving SyntheticFi's nationwide expansion. Despite the "Head of" title, JD scope reads as Manager-level: design + execute + iterate, not pure leadership / org-build.

## Responsibilities

- Design, execute, and monitor SyntheticFi's top-of-funnel growth strategy. Build and scale multi-channel engines across email, direct mail, phone campaigns; paid advertising; social media campaigns; conference and podcast appearances; SEO and GEO; and others (press, influencer sponsorship).
- Own end-to-end campaign development, execution, and attribution. Launch integrated campaigns across digital, field, and partner channels and iterate based on performance for qualified pipeline.
- Produce high-impact content across the full funnel - articles, visuals, videos, websites for existing and prospective RIA partners and channel partners.
- Partner closely with top partners and key stakeholders. Serve as a liaison between advisors, stakeholders, and internal teams.
- Build systems, processes, and feedback loops. Partner with Sales and CX to track performance, generate insights, and improve growth efficiency and lead quality.
