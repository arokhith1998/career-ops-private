## Description
Cribl frames itself as "the AI Platform for Telemetry," remote-first. The Revenue Operations Senior Analyst supports Cribl's Sales organization with data-driven GTM insights, optimizes systems/processes, and enables Sales leadership decisions. Reports to the Sr. Director, Revenue Operations. Responsibilities: segmentation models, territory design and compensation plans for annual planning; strategic advisor to Sales VPs via business reviews and pipeline diagnostics; maintains Salesforce/Gong/Omni dashboards; drives CRM workflow/data-quality improvements; maintains account/opportunity/territory data integrity; cross-functional work with Sales, Finance, Marketing, People; occasional work outside standard hours due to remote/multi-timezone team.

- Exceptionally detail-oriented
