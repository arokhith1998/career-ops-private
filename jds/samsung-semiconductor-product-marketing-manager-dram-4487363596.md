What You'll Do
Memory sits at the heart of AI, cloud and datacenter growth, and Samsung is the industry's volume and technology leader in DRAM. As a Product Marketing Manager on our DRAM team, you will be the market expert who shapes how Samsung leads with some of the world's leading technology customers.

You'll work at the center of the business: analyzing demand and pricing, informing supply and production plans, and equipping Sales with the insight to negotiate. It's a strong role for someone with a few years of product or strategic marketing experience who wants broader scope, exposure and real influence on business decisions.

Location: Daily onsite presence at our San Jose, CA office in alignment with our Flexible Work policy
Reports to: Sr Dr Product Marketing

- Read the market. Track customer and market trends, identify gaps and opportunities, and analyze segment demand and TAM.
- Shape pricing. Develop pricing strategies by customer and segment, using feedback from the field to finalize decisions.
- Influence supply. Understand customer demand, forecasts and supply in detail to inform allocation and production build plans.
- Set strategy. Lead strategic analysis, marketing strategy and competitive analysis.
- Model the landscape. Distill analyst reports into key messages and model competitive TAM, SAM and market share.
- Arm Sales to win. Partner with technical teams to build positioning and data-backed analysis that sharpens our competitive edge.
- Be the voice of Samsung DRAM. Create and deliver customer and sales presentations, represent Samsung in customer meetings as the expert on market conditions, and support company and customer tech events.
- Build industry presence. Collaborate across the industry and evangelize Samsung DRAM products.

What You Bring
- Bachelor's degree (EE or CS preferred; MBA a plus).
- 3+ years of strategic or product marketing experience in DRAM or the broader semiconductor industry.
- Strong analytical skills, with the ability to turn complex financial, technical and market information into clear strategy.
- Excellent written and verbal communication, including confident presentation and public speaking.
- Strong Excel and PowerPoint skills.
- Ability to manage multiple projects in a fast-paced environment and work with cross-functional headquarters teams in Korea.
- The drive and energy to champion new opportunities and influence stakeholders at every level.

Nice to have
- Experience with enterprise OEM and datacenter customers.
- Familiarity with Korean language and culture.
- Strong interpersonal skills and a collaborative approach in a dynamic, multicultural environment.

Don't meet every requirement? If the role excites you, we encourage you to apply. Strong candidates often come from adjacent backgrounds such as storage, server or cloud hardware, or technology pricing and strategy.

Why Samsung Semiconductor
- Work at the center of the memory market with the industry's volume and technology leader in DRAM and NAND Flash.
- Partner with leading technology companies across multiple industries.
- Collaborate with Korean headquarters.
