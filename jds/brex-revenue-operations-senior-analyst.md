## Full JD text (verbatim, via headless browser, LinkedIn mirror)

Why join us: Brex is the intelligent finance platform that enables companies to spend smarter and move faster in more than 200 markets. Tens of thousands of the world's best companies run on Brex, including DoorDash, Coinbase, Robinhood, Zoom, Plaid, Reddit, and SeatGeek.

Sales at Brex: The Sales team is the driving factor behind revenue for Brex.

What You'll Do: Brex is looking for an individual to join the Revenue Operations Team, which is responsible for driving revenue growth in close partnership with post-sales revenue leaders.

Responsibilities:
- Create and update dashboards to give revenue leaders and operations partners the data they need to run their businesses.
- Proactively identify opportunities to provide additional reporting or analytical clarity.
- Run deep dive analyses to identify challenges and opportunities within the business.
- Partner with Revenue Operations business partners and revenue leaders to design operational solutions, implement them, and track success.
- Evaluate various tool usage and effectiveness.
- Build complex capacity and funnel models for annual planning and scenario evaluation.
- Support data requests for deal deep dives or compensation tracking.
- Own Revenue Operations processes for Post Sales, including intake, forecasting maintenance, onboarding, routing, and coverage models.

Requirements:
- 2+ years of experience in revenue operations, sales operations, business operations, sales finance, or similar; preferably at a Fintech or SaaS company.
- Strong problem solving skills and collaborative approach to solutioning.
- Excellent written and verbal communication.
- Analytical mindset and desire for high data precision and accuracy.
- Self-motivated and able to run with projects autonomously.
- Advanced spreadsheet and modeling skills (Google Sheets or Excel).
- High familiarity with CRM data models (Salesforce preferred).

Bonus Points: Experience with sales compensation design and tracking; knowledge of revenue recognition principles and finance systems; moderate SQL skills with data visualization tools (Looker or Hex a plus).

Compensation: The expected salary range for this role is $114,808 - $143,510. Starting base pay depends on candidate's location, skills, experience, market demands, and internal pay parity. Depending on the position offered, equity and other forms of compensation may be provided.
