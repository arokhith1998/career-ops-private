## Full JD text

We're building the AI-driven future of customer success, from retention to growth! Gainsight is the AI-powered retention engine behind the world's most customer-centric companies. The Gainsight CustomerOS platform orchestrates the customer journey from onboarding to outcomes to advocacy. More than 2,000 companies trust Gainsight's applications and AI agents to drive learning, adoption, community connection, and success for their customers.

About This Role:

We're looking for a full-time Chief of Staff to join our Executives team reporting to the Chief Executive Officer. This role is a remote role based in the USA.

In this role, you'll play a key role in amplifying the CEO's impact and keeping the executive team aligned by owning the executive operating rhythm, driving cross-functional initiatives, and authoring the CEO's internal and external communications. This is a great opportunity for someone who thrives in a high-visibility, fast-moving, AI-forward environment and enjoys working cross-functionally with teams like the Executive Leadership Team, the Board, Finance, and Marketing. The ideal candidate brings strong skills in executive writing, strategic planning, and AI fluency.

What You'll Do:

CEO Communications (Internal & External): Develop and deliver high-impact CEO communications including presentations, scripts, talking points, and messaging for internal meetings, industry conferences, customer engagements, and stakeholder interactions. Prepare the CEO and other executives for public speaking engagements and media appearances, owning pre-prep, vetting, and follow-through on inbound and outbound CEO speaking requests in partnership with Marketing. Draft company-wide announcements (executive hires, departures, org changes, M&A) and CEO thought leadership in his voice, newsletters, blogs, LinkedIn, partnering with Marketing and PR on press releases, books, and other external media. Coordinate CEO-driven cultural initiatives, including Childlike Joy videos and other programs.

Executive Team Operations: Set the agenda, drive discussion, and own follow-through for executive team operating meetings, offsites, and deep dives, ensuring clear ownership of decisions and actions. Organize and facilitate the fiscal year's key meeting calendar, Board meetings, executive offsites, QBRs, All Hands, Weekly Huddles, ELT, and weekly operations meetings, partnering with the events team and other Chiefs of Staff on logistics, agenda, and follow-up. Attend strategic meetings, conferences, and offsites with the CEO, driving prep, capturing decisions and timelines, and owning follow-through.

Board & Investor Engagement: Own the narrative, content, and coordination for board decks, board letters, and investor materials, ensuring the CEO is fully prepared for high-stakes Board moments. Support logistics and prep for the CEO's weekly Board Working Team (BWT) call; manage Board document distribution in Box and serve as the day-to-day point of contact for the Vista team.

Company-Level Strategy & Annual Planning: Run the full life cycle of the annual strategic planning process (AOP) in partnership with Finance, the CFO, and ELT, translating strategic priorities into resource and investment decisions for CEO and CFO review. Own and run the V2MOM process, defining at the ELT level and supporting cascading throughout the rest of the organization, driving mid-cycle tracking and end-of-cycle reporting. Provide analytical and project management support on M&A and strategic partnership evaluations as needed.

Project Ownership: Drive high-priority, cross-functional CEO initiatives end-to-end, defining the problem, assembling the working team, managing stakeholder alignment, and monitoring progress through to delivery. Build and maintain the data layer for executive decision-making, performance dashboards, KPI tracking, and business reviews that synthesize cross-functional and BU-level data for the ELT, embedding AI into these workflows. Serve as a shared strategic resource to BU GMs and functional leaders, providing executive visibility on issues that span organizational boundaries.

Hyper-Responsiveness and Flexibility: Be hyper-responsive across email, Slack, text, and phone, including outside of normal business hours and Sunday evenings to prepare for the business week. Travel locally, domestically, and internationally to be onsite with the CEO as needed.

This is not a complete list of responsibilities, and the scope of the role may evolve with the needs of the team and business.

What We're Looking For:

5-10 years of related experience and a Bachelor's degree (or equivalent experience), with a proven track record in a Chief of Staff, strategy, business operations, management consulting, investment banking, venture capital, or private equity role.

Exceptional written communication, with demonstrated experience writing in a senior executive's voice across formats: board materials, all-hands narratives, internal announcements, LinkedIn and thought leadership, and customer-facing keynotes.

Strong structured thinking, with the ability to synthesize ambiguous information into clear recommendations and crisp, executive-ready materials.

Demonstrated ability to manage multiple complex, cross-functional workstreams concurrently, driving accountability and follow-through without formal authority.

Analytical fluency, including comfort building financial models, synthesizing data from multiple sources, and translating analysis into executive-level insights.

Proficiency in Excel, PowerPoint/Google Slides, and modern data and collaboration tools, with the ability to build and present executive-grade materials independently.

Strong executive presence and interpersonal skills, with the ability to engage credibly with C-suite leaders, Board members, and advisors.

Strong AI fluency and a clear point of view on how to apply modern AI tools to executive-level work; daily, hands-on user of leading LLMs and AI assistants for drafting, research, synthesis, and analysis.

Understanding of how to embed AI into operating rhythms to make planning, reporting, and communications faster and higher-quality.

Experience owning or significantly contributing to operating cadence design, annual planning cycles, and company-level goal-setting processes.

Familiarity with OKR/V2MOM/goal-setting processes and annual planning cycles at the company level.

Trusted partner, force multiplier, exceptional communicator, applies AI as a core competency, comfortable with ambiguity, naturally curious and intellectually humble (described in detail in original posting).

Nice-to-have skills or experience: MBA from a top-tier program; familiarity with customer success, post-sale SaaS, or the broader CS technology landscape; hands-on experience building AI workflows, custom GPTs, agents, or lightweight automations to support executive work or team operations (strong plus); prior exposure to M&A due diligence or post-merger integration work, and experience working with Private Equity sponsors.
