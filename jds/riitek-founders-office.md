Company Description

Riitek is an Indian technology brand with a global vision, focused on building performance-driven products for the modern digital workspace. The company designs tools that help professionals multitask, maintain focus, and work without traditional limits. Riitek's solutions are tailored to enhance productivity and efficiency in fast-paced professional environments. Team members join a product-driven organization that values innovation, practical design, and user-centric technology.

Role Description

The Founders Office role is a full-time, on-site position based in the Pune District. This role supports the founders across strategic planning, project management, and day-to-day business operations. Typical responsibilities include preparing reports and presentations, conducting market and competitor research, coordinating cross-functional initiatives, and tracking key business metrics. The role also involves organizing meetings, managing follow-ups and action items, and ensuring smooth communication between the founders and internal teams. The person in this role is expected to work closely with leadership, take ownership of high-priority tasks, and contribute to decision-making with data-backed insights.

Qualifications
Strong analytical, research, and problem-solving skills, with the ability to translate data into clear insights and recommendations.
Experience in project management, operations, or business strategy, preferably in a tech or product-focused environment.
Excellent written and verbal communication skills, including the ability to prepare structured reports, presentations, and executive summaries.
High level of ownership, attention to detail, and organizational skills, with the ability to manage multiple priorities and deadlines.
Comfort working closely with founders or senior leadership, maintaining discretion and professionalism with sensitive information.
Proficiency with productivity and collaboration tools (e.g., spreadsheets, presentation software, task management platforms).
Bachelor's degree in business, engineering, economics, or a related field; a higher degree or startup experience is an advantage.
Interest in technology, digital workspaces, and performance-driven products, with a proactive and learning-oriented mindset.
