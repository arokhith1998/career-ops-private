The Opportunity

As a Product Manager, you will work with teams across Adobe to understand customer needs and help develop products and experiences. You will use research, data, and customer feedback to identify opportunities and recommend solutions. You will transform ideas into clear plans, bring teams together around priorities, and help develop products that deliver value for customers and the business.

What You’ll Do

- Partner with Engineering, Design, Marketing, and other teams to develop product strategies and experiences.

- Use customer research, data, and market insights to identify needs and opportunities.

- Transform insights into clear product requirements, use cases, and recommendations.

- Help teams evaluate opportunities, set priorities, and make informed product decisions.

- Present ideas and recommendations that build alignment and help shape product direction.

What You Need to Succeed

- Currently enrolled in a full-time MBA program, graduating between December 2026 and June 2027.

- 2+ years of relevant professional experience.

- Ability to define use cases, requirements, and user stories that develop product roadmaps.

- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills and can turn insights into action.

- Clear written and verbal communication skills and the ability to explain complex ideas simply.

- Ability to work effectively with people across different teams and backgrounds.
