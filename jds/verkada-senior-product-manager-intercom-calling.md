About The Role

Intercom is one of the fastest-growing and most-loved product lines at Verkada, in a $1B+ market we've only begun to tap. At its core, the intercom connects people with the businesses and organizations they're visiting through a call. Whether you need to get into a building, drop off a package, ask about store hours or get help in an emergency, the intercom is how you reach a person who can help.

Calling is at the heart of the Intercom experience, and we need a product manager to own it end to end: every call, from the moment someone presses the button to the moment someone answers and helps them.

You'll own the underlying calling pipeline, enterprise-grade call routing, the context and controls that help people answering make better decisions, and the AI that automates call responses. Your job is to make every call reliable, productive and delightful.

We are committed to a thriving in-office culture. This role requires you to work onsite at our HQ in San Mateo, CA.

What You’ll Own

Calling stack: make every call great

Define the roadmap for our signaling and media paths, across first- and third-party and online and offline pipelinesDeliver a consistent answering experience on every endpoint: iOS, Android, web browsers, PSTN and VoIPTrack the metrics that matter: ring rate, connection success rate, dropped calls and audio/video quality (load time, latency, packet loss, jitter, MOS)Address failure modes on real-world networks, including weak cellular links, restrictive enterprise firewalls and NAT traversalManage relationships with subprocessors and technology partners, including corporate phone and communications platforms

Call routing: get every call to the best person available

Add intelligence to routing based on audio and video triggers, building state (such as after hours or lockdown), schedules and staff availabilityDefine what the caller experiences while their call is being routedDesign strategies for concurrent calls: ring strategies (simultaneous, sequential, round robin), triage, queuing, forwarding and failoverBuild tools that let admins configure, test and manage routing at scale across many sites, users, and devices (including receiver groups, SCIM syncing)

Call context and controls: give the person answering superpowers

Enrich calls with context for better decisions, such as live captions, nearby security camera feeds, visitor history and access logsBuild advanced controls that put fast, powerful options at the answerer's fingertips, such as real-time voice translation, security escalation and multi-door unlockingDrive sophisticated video features such as two-way video, auto-framing and AI filters that improve the calling experience

AI receivers: use voice AI to streamline responses

Today, conversational AI handles low-risk tasks like sharing basic information about a business and its policies (Info Desk) and finding the right tenant to call (Voice Directory). We see many more interactions it can automate, including wayfinding, appointment management and granting building entry. 

Define human escalation rules, clean handoffs and firm guardrails for AI conversations, especially anything safety-relatedBuild frameworks to measure quality, including escalation rate, accuracy, latency, system observability and model evalsIdentify, prototype and launch new voice AI use cases with the goal of finding product-market fit
How You'll Work

Own customer discovery: talk to customers, go on site walks and see pain points firsthand in the fieldSet product strategy and prioritize the roadmap, balancing tradeoffs between reliability, new capabilities and customer needsPartner closely with engineering and design, bringing both technical depth and design senseDrive execution from spec to launch with obsessive attention to detailCraft the product pitch with marketing and salesStay on top of the competitive landscape and market trends
