Title: Sr. Manager, Product Marketing - New Products

Location: Austin, TX | Dallas, TX | Morristown, NJ | San Jose, CA (Hybrid)

Reporting to: Director of Performance Marketing

About Hippo: Hippo was built on a promise: make homeownership effortless. We use technology and data to help our customers stay ahead of problems and protect what matters most. Hippo operates as a diversified carrier platform, partnering with MGAs to deliver tailored program solutions.

About This Role: This is a rare opportunity to build a function, not just fill a role. Hippo has led innovation in consumer home insurance since inception, and we are now standing up a product marketing discipline that carries that innovation to market.

Your first mandate is Hippo+: a net-new owned product, a bundled home protection offering, one of three core engines driving Hippo's 2027 Homeowners Growth strategy. You will be the voice, the strategist, and the architect of how we bring Hippo+ to market.

Reporting to the Director of Performance Marketing and working cross-functionally across product, insurance, legal, and sales, you will drive everything from persona definition and competitive positioning to sales enablement and launch readiness. You will also direct a significant portion of the agile workforce building this product.

What You'll Do: Own positioning and message hierarchy for the Hippo product, including the insurance-plus-warranty category. Define, refine, and maintain target personas. Lead competitive intelligence across the insurance-and-warranty category. Build and maintain sales enablement content including battlecards, objection handling guides, one-pagers, and state-specific plan descriptions in partnership with Cinch. Develop and manage the pre-approved language library. Own launch readiness by state. Drive product team alignment across marketing. Lead marketing experimentation and optimization in support of the Hippo+ learning agenda. Direct a significant portion of Hippo's agile workforce. Build the durable product marketing foundation. Deliver against hard phase gates: documented target persona and pricing risk identification by December 31, 2026; personas, state-prioritization model, and rebuilt creative locked by end of Q1 2027.

Must Haves: 8+ years of D2C product marketing experience, ideally within fintech, insurance, or a similarly regulated consumer category, with a proven track record of thriving in complex environments. A builder's mindset. Experience getting excellent work out of people who don't report to you. Appetite for building the system, not just the deliverable. Sharp judgement on messaging in regulated environments. A track record of building sales enablement content that actually moves conversion metrics. A natural collaborator. Fluency with AI tools.

Benefits And Perks: Healthy Hippos Benefits (medical plans, 100% employer covered dental & vision, 401(k), short & long-term disability, employer-paid life insurance, FSA, EAP). Equity. Training and Career Growth. Flexible Time Off. Little Hippos Program (12 weeks parental leave). Hippo Habitat.

The Morristown, NJ and San Jose, CA base pay range for this role is $168,000 - $225,000.

Hippo is an equal opportunity employer.

Seniority level: Not Applicable. Employment type: Full-time. Industries: Insurance.
