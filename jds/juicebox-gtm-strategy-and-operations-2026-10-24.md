About The Role
We're hiring a GTM Strategy & Operations generalist to partner closely with our VP of Sales and GTM leadership team on the highest-impact growth initiatives across the business.
This is a highly strategic role focused on identifying, evaluating, and executing opportunities that accelerate revenue growth and improve go-to-market effectiveness. You'll work across Sales, Marketing, Customer Success, Partnerships, and Product to drive critical initiatives that shape how we acquire, monetize, and retain customers.
As one of the first GTM Strategy & Ops hires, you'll have outsized ownership and visibility, leading ambiguous, high-priority projects that influence company growth, from pricing and packaging strategy to AI-native workflows and new revenue opportunities.

You Will
Lead high-impact strategic initiatives across the GTM organization, from problem definition through execution
Partner with GTM leadership to identify growth opportunities, evaluate tradeoffs, and drive key business decisions
Analyze and recommend improvements to pricing, packaging, monetization, and go-to-market motions
Identify, size, and validate new markets, customer segments, and revenue opportunities
Drive special projects across Sales, Marketing, Customer Success, and Partnerships that unlock growth and improve operational efficiency
Design and implement AI-native workflows that help GTM teams operate more effectively and scale faster
Build business cases, financial models, and analyses to support investment, resourcing, and prioritization decisions
Develop dashboards, KPIs, and reporting that provide visibility into pipeline, conversion, retention, and overall GTM performance
Improve GTM processes, systems, and operating rhythms as the company scales

You Have
3+ years of experience in Business Operations, Consulting, Investment Banking, or a similar generalist role (4+ years preferred)
Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to structure ambiguous business problems and drive them to resolution
Experience working cross-functionally with Sales, Marketing, Customer Success, Product, or Finance teams
A strong interest in go-to-market strategy, revenue growth, and how businesses scale
Proficiency with spreadsheets, analytics tools, and data-driven decision making
Exceptional communication skills and the ability to influence stakeholders across functions
A bias toward action and comfort operating in a fast-moving, high-ownership environment

Nice to Have
Experience in B2B SaaS, recruiting tech, or AI
Experience with AI tooling / have built your own tools
Experience as an early BizOps, Strategy, or first operations hire at a startup
