## Job Description (full, verbatim)
The Senior Product Marketing Manager is responsible for the business success of one or more product lines within the FaST portfolio. The role drives product strategy, market positioning, lifecycle management, and customer adoption by working closely with Engineering, Applications, Manufacturing, Sales, and Field Marketing teams. The individual serves as the primary business owner for their assigned products and is accountable for identifying market opportunities, translating customer requirements into product strategy, and achieving revenue, market share, and profitability objectives.

Key Responsibilities (per posting)
- Develop revenue, adoption, and market share growth plans for assigned products.
- Support forecasting, pricing strategy, and product mix optimization.
- Partner with Field Marketing to define customer penetration and adoption strategies.
- Support major customer evaluations, beta programs, and early adoption initiatives.
- Resolve issues that may impact product adoption, customer satisfaction, or business objectives.
- Ensure readiness of customer support and field teams for successful product adoption.

Qualifications
- 8+ years of semiconductor industry experience.
- Experience managing product introductions and customer adoption programs.
