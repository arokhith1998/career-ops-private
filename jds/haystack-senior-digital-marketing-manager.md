This employer is hiring for a Senior Digital Marketing Manager to lead end-to-end digital channels and drive inbound pipeline growth in a hybrid role.

  

The Role

  

• Own and execute the digital marketing strategy across content, email, paid ads, social media, and SEO to establish thought leadership and drive demo requests.

• Manage organic search strategy, website optimization, and AI search visibility (AEO/GEO) to increase qualified traffic and brand presence.

• Plan, launch, and optimize enterprise B2B paid campaigns across search, social, and display channels.

• Oversee end-to-end email marketing programs, focusing on segmentation, deliverability, nurturing, and conversion testing.

• Manage company social media presence with a primary focus on LinkedIn engagement and reach.

  

What You'll Need

  

• 7+ years of experience in B2B marketing, ideally within an enterprise SaaS environment.

• Hands-on expertise managing organic search, AI search optimization, paid advertising, and email marketing programs.

• Strong analytics and tracking skills utilizing tools such as GA4, Google Tag Manager, and CRM attribution platforms.

  

What's On Offer

  

• Annual salary of $135,000 to $170,000.

• 401(k) retirement savings plan.
