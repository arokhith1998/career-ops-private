Job Summary
Supermicro® is a Top Tier provider of advanced server, storage, and networking solutions for Data Center, Cloud Computing, Enterprise IT, Hadoop/ Big Data, Hyperscale, HPC and IoT/Embedded customers worldwide. We are the #5 fastest growing company among the Silicon Valley Top 50 technology firms.

As we scale our AI infrastructure business, we need to move faster with NVIDIA and put more, better promotion into our pipeline. This role is the critical link between Supermicro's technical product teams and NVIDIA's marketing organization. You will translate complex AI infrastructure capabilities into clear value propositions for our mutual enterprise customers. You will also drive the volume and quality of co-marketing activity, from joint campaigns to sales enablement and product launches. This role is focused on positioning, content, and go-to-market rather than hands-on lab or benchmark work.

Essential Duties And Responsibilities

Technical Marketing and Messaging
- Translate the technical features of NVIDIA-based platforms (Blackwell and Blackwell Ultra GPU systems, liquid cooling, rack-scale and DCBBS offerings) into customer-relevant value propositions and differentiated messaging
- Develop technical positioning and messaging frameworks for Enterprise AI solutions, including use-case and vertical narratives
- Create technically credible content: solution briefs, white papers, reference architectures, datasheets, technical blogs, web content, and presentations
- Work closely with engineering and product management to stay current on the roadmap and turn it into launch-ready content
- Act as a subject matter expert on AI infrastructure for internal teams, partners, and customers

NVIDIA Alliance and Co-Marketing
- Serve as a day-to-day marketing counterpart to NVIDIA's partner and product marketing teams, building strong working relationships
- Plan and execute joint marketing programs, campaigns, events, and announcements aligned to NVIDIA's go-to-market calendar
- Manage co-marketing processes: joint messaging and brand approvals, program compliance, and funding (e.g., MDF) where applicable
- Identify and capture NVIDIA promotional opportunities, including launches, reference programs, and amplification channels, to raise Supermicro's visibility in NVIDIA's ecosystem
- Track and report on joint activity, pipeline contribution, and ROI

Launch and Campaign Velocity
- Own go-to-market plans for new product and solution launches, coordinating across product, sales, PR, web, and field marketing
- Build repeatable templates and workflows that shorten the cycle from technical release to market-ready assets
- Develop campaigns that generate and accelerate enterprise AI pipeline in partnership with demand gen and regional teams

Sales Enablement
- Build sales tools: battlecards, pitch decks, competitive positioning, FAQs, demo and talk tracks, and technical one-pagers
- Train sales, SEs, and channel partners on AI solution positioning and the NVIDIA joint value story
- Gather field feedback to refine messaging and close content gaps

Qualifications

Required
- 8+ years in technical product marketing, solutions marketing, or partner/alliance marketing in enterprise infrastructure, semiconductors, or cloud/AI
- Working knowledge of AI infrastructure and software: GPU servers, accelerated computing, networking, storage, and data center fundamentals
- Proven ability to explain complex technical topics to both technical and business audiences
- Strong writing and messaging skills, with a portfolio of technical content
- Experience managing multiple launches or campaigns simultaneously in a fast-moving environment
- Experience working with cross-functional teams (engineering, product management, sales, PR, brand)
- Bachelor's degree in marketing, engineering, computer science, business, or equivalent experience

Preferred
- Direct experience with NVIDIA or another strategic technology partner's co-marketing programs, including MDF and joint approval processes
- Familiarity with NVIDIA's AI platform and enterprise AI deployment patterns (training, inference, AI factories)
- Background in server, storage, or data center hardware, including liquid cooling or rack-scale integration
- Experience building sales enablement programs
- Technical degree or hands-on technical background (e.g., solutions engineering)
- Comfort with AI-assisted content workflows to increase output speed
- Willingness to travel for events and partner meetings
