## Full JD Text

About the Company
SUNA Optoelectronics. Suzhou Suna Optoelectronics (SUNA) is a leading IDM supplier of silicon lenses and silicon capacitors for high-speed optical communication and emerging CPO architectures, with end-to-end wafer-level manufacturing and global customer coverage.

About the Role
Reporting to the leadership team, this role owns strategic business growth with top global AI customers. You will elevate SUNA Optoelectronics from a secondary optical-module vendor to a trusted core-component strategic partner, and secure long-term design-ins for our silicon-based products within next-gen CPO ecosystems.

Responsibilities
- Lead SUNA Optoelectronics' strategic upgrade: transition the company from a secondary-tier optical module vendor into a preferred strategic core-component supplier for leading AI hyperscalers.
- Deliver a meaningful business shift from indirect component supply to deep, locked-in partnerships with these key customers.
- Leverage the critical market window for Co-Packaged Optics (CPO).
- Drive our silicon lens and silicon capacitor products into leading customers' next-generation CPO reference designs, locking in 3-5 year future supply opportunities.
- Manage full end-to-end commercial cycles for target key accounts and their extended supply-chain partners: new customer acquisition, need discovery, commercial negotiations, technical alignment, project execution through to collections.
- Collaborate directly with customer engineering and R&D teams to deliver technical briefings, push forward NPI and supplier qualification processes.
- Align internal cross-functional teams including R&D, manufacturing, quality and supply-chain to deliver and execute customer projects successfully.
- Build out organizational capabilities to support enterprise-grade engagements with world-leading tech companies.
- Synthesize actionable customer insights to inform and guide the company's global go-to-market strategy.
