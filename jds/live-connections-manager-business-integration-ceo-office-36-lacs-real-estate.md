Job Title: Manager – Business Integration | CEO Office
Yelahanka, Bangalore
Budget: 36 Lacs (All Inclusive)

Job Overview
We are looking for a highly organised and strategic professional to work closely with the CEO, supporting executive management, business integration, strategic initiatives and cross-functional coordination.

The role requires strong capabilities in management reviews, business planning, performance monitoring, stakeholder management and executive communication, ensuring effective implementation of key business priorities.

Key Responsibilities

- Facilitate management control, reviews and implementation of agreed action plans.

- Coordinate strategic planning, annual operating plan (AOP) reviews and half-yearly reviews.

- Monitor operational matters and key projects being implemented across the organisation.

- Ensure timely and accurate data availability for CEO reviews and management discussions.

- Track action points and follow up with departments to ensure timely closure.

- Monitor implementation of strategic and business initiatives from the CEO's office.

- Provide regular updates on sector developments, industry issues, opportunities and challenges.

- Support special projects and cross-functional business initiatives.

- Identify and escalate critical, urgent, sensitive and confidential matters.

- Support CEO approvals relating to purchase orders, NFA and other business requirements.

- Draft and review executive communications, emails, memos and announcements.

- Prepare briefing documents, management updates and strategic discussion materials.

- Coordinate with department heads and external stakeholders to ensure effective communication and execution.

Candidate Profile

- MBA or equivalent qualification.

- Minimum 8 years of experience, including at least 4 years assisting CEOs of large organisations.

- Strong exposure to CEO Office operations, business integration and executive coordination.

- Experience in management reviews, strategic planning, AOP and performance monitoring.

- Strong PMO, MIS, data integration and change management capabilities.

- Excellent stakeholder management and communication skills.

- High emotional maturity, integrity and confidentiality.

- Strong analytical, presentation and problem-solving skills.

- Ability to work with senior leadership and manage multiple strategic priorities.
