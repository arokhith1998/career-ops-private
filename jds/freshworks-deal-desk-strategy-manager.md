## Responsibilities

Run point on Deal Desk activities for the largest and most strategic transactions in the region, embedded directly with assigned AEs and RVPs. Proactively initiate deal strategy conversations with sellers before paperwork or approvals are required. Document the sequence of events required to close each assigned deal and drive the cadence needed to hit it. Own deal strategy and structuring end-to-end, including all order form updates and documentation. Exercise judgment to triage deal complexity - resolving strategic, high-stakes deals personally while routing standardized work. Identify and secure all necessary internal approvals; own correspondence with Legal on contract terms and redlines. Provide ongoing, proactive deal enablement for regional account teams. Support quarter-end and month-end close crunch periods, including flexibility for after-hours support.

## Requirements

7-10+ years of experience in Deal Desk, Sales Operations, Revenue Operations, or similar deal-support function. Strong working knowledge of contract structuring, order forms, pricing and discounting frameworks. Comfort partnering directly with Legal, Finance, and Sales Ops to resolve complex questions. Excellent written and verbal communication skills. Ability to manage a high volume of concurrent deals under tight deadlines.
