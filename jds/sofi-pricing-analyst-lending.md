The Role
We are seeking a Pricing Implementation Analyst to join our Pricing team in the Lending Organization. This is an exciting role for someone to make a direct impact on the revenue of SoFi. As Pricing Implementation Analyst, you will be working closely with a world-class team of business leaders and data scientists to help SoFi implement, monitor, and advance pricing controls and the execution of pricing strategies across our consumer lending products.
What you’ll do:
Pricing Execution (60%)

- Materialize strategy implementation by translating high-level strategies into tactical steps, assessing system implication, and ensuring specifications are complete and aligned.

- Maintain robust audit trails and enforce governance requirements, including Jira and Gitlab process controls.

- Collaborate closely with Engineering teams to troubleshoot and resolve system issues, ensure successful pricing deployments, and continuously monitor data pipelines to verify pricing strategy is running as expected.

- Develop and maintain systematic validation functions to ensure implementation accuracy across all products

Controls & Process Improvement (25%)

- Develop and maintain controls, including building automated validations alerts and anomaly/exception detection Tableau dashboards

- Identify opportunities to enhance execution efficiency and advance control maturity

- Support risk management and new product initiatives through script development, documentation, reporting, and ad hoc analyses as needed

Cross-Functional Representation (15%)

- Work with external partners to ensure flawless price execution across affiliate partner environments

- Serve as a contact for pricing implementation with internal partners, covering technology initiatives, operational execution, and risk management.

What you’ll need:

- B.S. or M.S. in a quantitative field such as Computer Science, Statistics, Economics, Engineering, or Mathematics

- 4+ years of experience in financial services, preferably with pricing, data analysis, risk governance

- Excellent programming skills in Python and SQL, with hands-on proficiency in building production-level dashboards in Tableau

- Strong attention to detail, effective communication skills, and ability to explain complex models in simple terms

- A collaborative approach and comfort working in a fast-paced, cross-functional environment

Nice to have:

- Experience with Snowflake and GitLab

- Experience collaborating with product and/or engineering teams

- Experience in pricing strategies /  risk management / data governance
