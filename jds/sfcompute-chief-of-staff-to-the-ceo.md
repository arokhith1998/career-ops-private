### About San Francisco Compute
San Francisco Compute exists to give ambitious teams large scale supercomputers without taking on a catastrophic balance sheet risk. Most GPU clouds force you to sign long term contracts with no way out. If you underbuy, you miss the window. If you overbuy, you'll blow up with no way out. It's a desperate gamble that San Francisco Compute does not force you to play.

Originally, SFC was an AI lab that bought too big of a GPU cluster and was forced to sublease it or go under. Today, we build data centers, supercomputers, and then lease those machines on contracts that let our customers sublease. To make it possible to sublease, we invented the first compute market and became the first one to offer physical settlement.

Our team includes senior & technical leadership from places like Lambda, Crusoe, Digital Ocean, AWS, and Hut8. Our CTO is the cofounder and former CEO of Voltage Park. In prior roles, our team has deployed 8GW of datacenter capacity & hundreds of thousands of GPUs.

### About The Role
SF Compute is seeking a Chief of Staff to the CEO to serve as a partner and force multiplier to the CEO. This role combines operations, and execution, working closely with the CEO on the highest-impact priorities and decisions shaping the company's future. The Chief of Staff will drive cross-functional initiatives, help translate company priorities into action across the leadership team, and ensure key decisions move from alignment to measurable outcomes across the organization. This role is a hybrid role and requires up to five days in-person presence in our San Francisco office.

### What You Will Do
- Serve as a trusted partner to the CEO to execute the goals & vision of the company.
- Help identify organizational bottlenecks and ambiguous problems, structure them into actionable plans, and drive resolution with the right owners.
- Translate and coordinate key decisions, analysis, and recommendations into clear actions the leadership team can execute quickly by aligning stakeholders and following through on commitments.
- Build enough understanding of our business, market, financing, and deal dynamics to engage in strategic discussions and tradeoffs.
- Represent the CEO in select internal and external conversations with customers, partners, and stakeholders.

### Who You Are
- Strong technical fluency and ability to engage with Engineering, GTM, and Product leaders on complex topics (AI/ML, infrastructure, architecture, etc.)
- Experience operating in high-growth, 0-to-1 environments where priorities evolve quickly.
- High-agency operator who takes ownership, works through ambiguity, and closes loops without needing direction.
- Trusted, discreet, and low ego, with strong judgment around sensitive information and relationships.
- Strong communicator who can create alignment internally and represent the company externally when needed.
