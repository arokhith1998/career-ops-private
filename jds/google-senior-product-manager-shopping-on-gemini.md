About The Job

At Google, we put our users first. The world is always changing, so we need Product Managers who are continuously adapting and excited to work on products that affect millions of people every day.

In this role, you will work cross-functionally to guide products from conception to launch by connecting the technical and business worlds. You can break down complex problems into steps that drive product development.

One of the many reasons Google consistently brings innovative, world-changing products to market is because of the collaborative work we do in Product Management. Our team works closely with creative engineers, designers, marketers, etc. to help design and develop technologies that improve access to the world's information. We're responsible for guiding products throughout the execution cycle, focusing specifically on analyzing, positioning, packaging, promoting, and tailoring our solutions to our users.

The Consumer Shopping team's mission is to make Google the most trusted place for people to discover and decide what to buy. Commerce is undergoing an unprecedented, AI-driven transformation, creating a pivotal opportunity to redefine shopping. Our vision is to build a personal shopper for everyone that has deep category expertise and understands individual user needs. To deliver on this, we are partnering with Google's AI teams to combine advanced LLM reasoning, multimodal understanding (voice, image, text), and the world's most comprehensive Shopping Graph. This will enable us to deliver deeply personalized, grounded, and actionable shopping experiences on flagship surfaces like the Gemini App, Chrome, and Android. We are seeking a senior product leader to drive this multi-year vision and build the core capabilities to bring it to life.

"The Google Commerce team is driving the shift toward agentic commerce—moving beyond static interactions to build intelligent, adaptive shopping experiences. By bringing Gemini into AI Mode in Search and the Gemini app, we're making shopping even more intuitive. Powered by the Google Shopping Graph's 60B+ listings, our team is helping to establish open industry standards like the Universal Commerce Protocol. We're building shopping experiences across Google, including Search, the Gemini app, YouTube, and Ads, to help people discover and buy products from the brands they love and trust on Google.
