Build leading indicators and early-warning triggers for potential issues in coverage or velocity.

Collaborate with GTM Engineering to automate reporting and leverage AI for analysis efficiency.

8+ years in revenue operations, sales operations, or GTM strategy in high-growth B2B SaaS, PaaS, or AI infrastructure companies.
Deep Salesforce reporting fluency and strong command of BI tools like Looker or Tableau.
Fluency in enterprise sales processes and pipeline coverage math, with experience in stage-gate frameworks like MEDDIC.
Proven track record of creating impactful dashboards that drive decision-making.
Ability to structure ambiguous questions and develop systematic solutions.
Commercially and technically curious, with knowledge of AI products and enterprise buyer concerns.
