## Description

Keysight seeks a marketing leader to shape how the company engages with the rapidly evolving AI infrastructure sector. The role emphasizes translating industry shifts in compute architectures, interconnects, power, and data center systems into strategic messaging and campaigns.

Key Responsibilities:
- Monitor emerging trends in AI infrastructure and translate findings into actionable recommendations
- Develop Keysight's narrative around AI infrastructure work for both technical and executive audiences
- Create educational content including white papers, webinars, and customer stories
- Represent Keysight at industry events and cultivate relationships with analysts and influencers
- Collaborate cross-functionally with product marketing, sales, and creative teams
- Measure campaign effectiveness and optimize accordingly

Required:
- Bachelor's degree in electrical engineering, computer engineering, computer science, or related field (or equivalent experience)
- 5+ years in industry/product/technical B2B marketing
- Knowledge of AI infrastructure, data centers, accelerated computing, interconnects, or power systems
- Strong ability to explain technical concepts clearly in writing and presentation
- Experience building comprehensive marketing programs

Preferred:

- Existing relationships in AI infrastructure ecosystem
