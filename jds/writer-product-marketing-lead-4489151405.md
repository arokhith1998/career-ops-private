## Role summary
WRITER is an enterprise AI platform for agentic work. The product marketing lead owns go-to-market strategy for new products and features, helps prospects understand the platform's value to marketing and sales teams, and drives adoption among current customers.

## Responsibilities
- Own end-to-end product marketing for core products: positioning, messaging, launch strategy, competitive differentiation.
- Partner with product, demand generation, content, comms, and customer marketing on launches.
- Turn product investments into sales-ready narratives and assets (pitch materials, one-pagers, objection-handling guides) with sales enablement.
- Drive feature adoption through user communications and programs.
- Run ongoing competitive intelligence and market research.
- Keep product messaging consistent across the buyer journey.
