About The Role

We are looking for a high-calibre Entrepreneur in Residence (EIR) - who brings a strong combination of business acumen, strategic thinking, revenue ownership, and execution capabilities.

This is not a conventional strategy or operations role. We are looking for someone who has built, scaled, and driven business outcomes, someone who understands how businesses operate, how revenue is generated, how marketing drives growth, and how to manage profitability.

The ideal candidate should have hands-on experience across P&L management, business strategy, growth, digital marketing, organic marketing, and sales. They should have owned business metrics, driven revenue targets, managed growth initiatives, and demonstrated the ability to take businesses or business verticals from 0 to 1 or scale existing operations.

Working directly with the founders and leadership team, the EIR will identify high-impact opportunities, solve complex business challenges, and take complete ownership of strategic initiatives that drive revenue, growth, and business profitability.

What You'll Do

Business Strategy & P&L Ownership
- Own and drive business P&L, including revenue, costs, margins, and profitability.
- Develop and execute business strategies aligned with the organization's growth objectives.
- Identify revenue opportunities, optimize costs, and improve overall business performance.
- Monitor key business metrics and take data-backed decisions to improve profitability.
- Translate business goals into actionable growth plans and measurable outcomes.

Growth & Revenue Ownership
- Drive end-to-end business growth initiatives with direct accountability for revenue outcomes.
- Identify new revenue streams, business opportunities, and scalable growth models.
- Develop strategies to improve customer acquisition, conversion, retention, and revenue.
- Work closely with sales and marketing teams to build predictable and sustainable revenue channels.
- Own growth targets and ensure execution against business objectives.

Marketing & Customer Acquisition
- Develop and execute digital marketing strategies across paid and organic channels.
- Drive organic growth through SEO, content marketing, social media, brand positioning, and other relevant channels.
- Understand performance marketing, customer acquisition costs (CAC), ROI, lead generation, and conversion metrics.
- Identify opportunities to optimize marketing spends and improve marketing efficiency.
- Build scalable customer acquisition strategies that contribute directly to business growth.

Sales & Business Development
- Develop and execute sales strategies to achieve business and revenue targets.
- Understand the complete sales funnel, from lead generation and acquisition to conversion and closure.
- Identify gaps in the sales process and implement strategies to improve sales productivity and conversion rates.
- Work closely with sales teams to drive revenue performance and improve overall business efficiency.

Strategic Initiatives & Founder's Office
- Work directly with founders and leadership on high-priority business initiatives.
- Identify business challenges, evaluate opportunities, and independently drive solutions.
- Lead cross-functional projects across marketing, sales, product, operations, and business teams.
- Drive 0-to-1 initiatives, from identifying opportunities to execution and scaling.
- Build processes, systems, and business frameworks that improve organizational efficiency.
- Bring structure to ambiguous problems and ensure execution with measurable business impact.

What We Are Looking For
- 3-5 years of experience in startups, business strategy, growth, business operations, entrepreneurship, or a Founder's Office role.
- Proven experience in owning and managing P&L, with a strong understanding of revenue, costs, margins, and profitability.
- Hands-on experience in driving business growth and taking ownership of revenue targets.
- Strong understanding and execution experience in digital marketing, performance marketing, and organic marketing.
- Experience in developing and executing business and growth strategies.
- Exposure to sales, business development, lead generation, and revenue conversion.
- Strong business acumen with the ability to understand the bigger business picture and identify growth opportunities.
- Excellent analytical and problem-solving skills with the ability to translate insights into business decisions.
- Ability to independently take ownership of initiatives and drive them from strategy to execution.
- Strong stakeholder management and communication skills.

Preferred Experience
- Experience working directly with founders, CEOs, or leadership teams.
- Prior experience managing a business vertical, category, business unit, or independent P&L.
- Experience scaling a startup, launching new business initiatives, or building a business from scratch.
- Experience in EdTech, Consumer Tech, D2C, SaaS, or other high-growth startup environments.
- Demonstrated success in improving revenue, profitability, customer acquisition, and business growth.
