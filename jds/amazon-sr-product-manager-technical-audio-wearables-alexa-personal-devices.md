Description

n this role you will own product definition, development, and launch - accountable end to end for how the product comes together for the customer, from hardware and audio to the app, UX, and the AI-forward experiences that set these products apart. As the single-threaded owner, you will translate customer needs into crisp requirements, write the narratives that align the company, drive tradeoffs across cross-functional teams, and carry products from concept to in-market. This is a high-ownership role with senior-leadership visibility, and you will be the voice of the customer in every room.

Key job responsibilities

Own product definition, development, and launch end to end - from concept to in-market.

Be the voice of the customer, using research, data, and competitive analysis to prioritize and decide.

Own the customer experience across every aspect of the product: hardware, industrial design, audio, device and app software, UX, packaging, and OOBE.

Work with cross functional teams and across disciplines to deliver great experiences on our products.

Define the AI-forward experiences that differentiate the product.

Define and lead a new product concept from zero to one - vision, PRFAQ/narrative, business case, and go-to-market.

Drive execution across cross-functional teams: hardware, audio software, firmware, mobile app, UX, human factors, applied science, program management, and go-to-market. Manage the business, owning costs, pricing and margins.

Own launch readiness, and represent your products in senior-leadership reviews.

About The Team

We are a customer-obsessed team building the next generation of Alexa Mobile Devices. We move fast, invent on behalf of customers, and hold a high bar for the experiences we ship. We value builders who bring strategic clarity, attention to detail and product obsession.
