## Job Description (extracted, detailed paraphrase)

LiveKit develops infrastructure for voice-driven AI computing. Founded 2021; enables developers to build, deploy, and scale voice agents in production. Clients include OpenAI, xAI, Salesforce, Coursera, and Spotify, processing billions of calls annually.

This is the inaugural Product Manager position for LiveKit Telephony, which lets AI agents interact with traditional phone networks (landlines, mobile, enterprise PBX). Serves call centers, customer support, healthcare triage, sales outreach, and appointment scheduling use cases.

**Core Responsibilities:**
- Roadmap Development: establish and communicate telephony product direction with engineering leadership
- Enterprise Compatibility: navigate SIP implementations and vendor ecosystems for integration with carrier systems, PBX, contact center solutions
- Global Expansion: scale LiveKit Phone Numbers internationally beyond current U.S. operations, securing regulatory compliance
- Customer Engagement: customer research and data analysis of production deployment patterns
- Business Economics: pricing strategy, packaging decisions, margin optimization

**Preferred:** telephony/CPaaS/SIP/voice product experience; knowledge of SIP, RTP/SRTP, DTMF, voice codecs, call transfer; carrier industry/regulatory experience; developer tools/infrastructure background; usage-based pricing experience.
