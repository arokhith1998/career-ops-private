## Role summary
Join BILL's Payments and Financial Services team and own the Ledger Subscriber Ecosystem: the reports, dashboards, data products, portals, APIs, and workflows that turn the TrueNorth ledger into the operating backbone for Treasury, Accounting, FP&A, Tax, Audit, Payment Ops, and every other finance function at BILL. Internal-facing, not consumer-facing.

## Responsibilities
- Define and own the 3-year vision, strategy, and roadmap for the Ledger Subscriber Ecosystem
- Build and ship subscriber platform capabilities: L1/L2 reconciliation reports, exception and aging queues, cash positioning views, revenue and expense journal feeds into ERP/revenue systems, ledger-backed data products
- Drive adoption of ledger-based workflows across Treasury, Accounting, and Payment Operations
- Act as single-threaded owner across Product, Engineering, Data Platform, Treasury, Accounting, FP&A, Tax, Audit, Legal, Payment Ops, Compliance, and CXO
- Represent subscriber needs in ledger architecture decisions
- Set and own measurable outcome metrics (percentage of TPV on-ledger, reduction in reconciliation days, time to produce regulatory reports, audit finding reduction)
- Communicate trade-offs/progress/risks to executives; mentor other PMs

## Requirements
- "8 or more years in Product Management, with at least 4 years in fintech, payments, financial systems, or enterprise SaaS focused on financial data, reconciliation, ledgering, or treasury."
- Direct experience building products for internal finance/operations users (Treasury, Accounting, FP&A, or Payment Ops)
- Deep understanding of financial data flows: ledgering/sub-ledgers, bank and GL reconciliation, month-end close, revenue recognition, treasury and cash management
- Track record of platform delivery owning vision, writing PRDs engineering shipped from, driving end-to-end execution, tracking self-set metrics
- Proven ability to lead cross-functional programs including Finance, Risk, Compliance, or Legal stakeholders
- Clear, structured written and verbal communication skills

### Desired (not required)
- Experience with Fragment, Modern Treasury, or event-driven architectures (Kafka, Flink) in financial systems
- Background in treasury/accounting/FP&A; familiarity with SOX controls, audit, or regulatory reporting
- Prior B2B payments, BaaS, card platforms, or working capital products; processor settlement reports (Stripe, Chase, Adyen, Marqeta)
