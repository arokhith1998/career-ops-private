## Full JD text (verbatim, source: LinkedIn jobs-guest posting API)

Founder's Office - Generalist (Shadow CEO) | GenZDealZ.ai | Andheri East, Mumbai

We're hiring one person to sit next to the founder and run the things that keep a young consumer-tech company moving.

About us

GenZDealZ.ai is building for India's Gen Z - deals, rewards and AI tools in one app (Android, iOS and web). We work with partners like HDFC Bank and Visa on campus programmes, and we move fast.

The role

This is not a support role. You'll act as the founder's shadow - in every meeting, every partner conversation, every campaign - and gradually take ownership of the pieces you prove yourself on.

You will:

- Plan and run all our campaigns end to end - campus drives, partner offers, app launches
- Own our social media pages: content calendar, posting, community, performance
- Own in-app push notifications and user communication
- Be the day-to-day point of contact for partners such as HDFC Bank and Visa - follow-ups, MOMs, decks, coordination
- Sit in on all founder meetings, track decisions and action items, and chase them to closure
- Jump between tech, sales, marketing and ops as the week demands
- Visit campuses to meet GenZ and the college campus management pan India
- Participate in trade fairs and events and also take care of the marketing materials printing, carrying etc.
- Its an Ops heavy profile.

Who we're looking for

- Excellent written and spoken communication; a warm, confident, people-first personality
- Comfortable with tech - you understand apps, analytics, basic tools and can talk to developers
- Strong instincts for sales, marketing and coordination
- Organised, proactive, and happy to own things nobody has handed you yet
- Freshers welcome. We care about how smart and hungry you are, not years on a CV
- Full-time, from our office in Andheri East, Mumbai

What you get

- A front-row seat to how a startup is actually built - partnerships, product, growth, fundraising
- Direct mentorship from the founder and real ownership from month one
- The kind of exposure that usually takes 5 years to earn
- Smart dynamic freshers are welcome
- Join us more for the experience and less for the remuneration
