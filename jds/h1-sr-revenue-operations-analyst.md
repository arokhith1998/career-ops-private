## Full JD text

At H1, mission is to provide a platform informing every doctor interaction globally -- health equity and trust in healthcare systems, using data and AI to unlock medical insights and accelerate drug development.

The Revenue Operations team plays a pivotal role driving H1's revenue engine.

What You'll Do At H1: looking for an operational, analytical, detail-oriented Sr. Revenue Operations Analyst supporting the global Commercial organization. Contributes to core planning, performance, and operational processes scaling the Commercial team. Partners with Commercial leadership and cross-functional teams (Marketing, Sales, Customer Success, Enablement, Finance). Reports to Director of RevOps.

You will:
- Support revenue planning, reporting, analysis on territories/quota/KPIs across functions, regions, segments, verticals.
- Build/maintain dashboards and reports monitoring productivity/performance.
- Analyze Commercial performance (win/loss, funnel, pipeline health); translate business questions into data pulls/findings/recommendations.
- Develop AI agents, skills, and workflows improving RevOps efficiency (data analysis, process development, reporting).
- Support operational initiatives improving Commercial effectiveness (lead routing, pipeline generation, sales process).
- Execute Deal Desk workflows: structuring, reviewing, approving non-standard deals with Sales/Finance; improve deal desk workflows.
- Oversee CRM data quality, field governance, audit processes.
- Contribute to change management/enablement (training materials, documentation).

Requirements:
- 3+ years of experience in Sales Strategy, Revenue Operations, or Go-to-Market Operations, ideally in a B2B SaaS company.
- Eastern or Central time zone preferred (global team support).
- Demonstrated ability to design/improve/operationalize business processes at scale.
- Familiarity with Salesforce, CPQ tools, and other GTM tools (Salesloft, ZoomInfo, Ringlead, Chamelio).
- Experience using AI assistants (e.g., Claude) to accelerate RevOps workflows.
- Strong analytical skills, advanced Excel/Google Sheets proficiency.
- Strong communicator presenting to cross-functional stakeholders and Commercial leadership.
- Comfortable with ambiguity and fast-changing priorities.
