## Job Description

### About the role

Human Archive (YC W26) is hiring someone to lead our end-to-end data annotation
operations. You will work directly with the founder to build and manage a
high-performing annotator workforce for robotics data. Your job is to ensure we
consistently have the right people, quality, and throughput across multiple projects
running simultaneously. This is a highly hands-on role for someone who is excellent
with people, metrics, and fast-moving operations.

### What you will do

- Recruit, evaluate, and manage high-quality data annotators
- Maintain a high bar for annotation quality and workforce performance
- Allocate annotators across multiple simultaneous projects
- Track daily throughput, quality, attendance, utilisation, and project targets
- Build dashboards and operating systems with our engineering team
- Identify bottlenecks and improve annotation workflows
- Design incentives that help teams consistently meet daily targets
- Forecast staffing requirements for upcoming projects
- Manage team leads and resolve day-to-day operational issues
- Build processes that can scale from hundreds to thousands of annotators

### Who we are looking for

- Extremely good with people and frontline teams
- Strong analytical ability and comfort with metrics and dashboards
- Excellent at prioritisation and resource allocation
- Highly organised and able to manage multiple projects at once
- Creative about incentives, hiring, performance, and operations
- Clear, direct, and fast communicator
- High ownership, energy, urgency, and agency
- Comfortable working in a demanding, fast-changing startup environment
- Experience at companies such as Surge AI, Scale AI, Mercor, Turing, Outlier,
  Remotasks, or similar workforce and data-operations businesses is a strong
  advantage.

Ideally 2+ YOE.

Location: On-site in Bangalore.
