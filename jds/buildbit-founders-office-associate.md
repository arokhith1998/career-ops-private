## Full JD Text

We're hiring a Founder's Office Associate at BuildBit. Ahmedabad, on-site.

Most people spend three years learning how a company works. You'd learn it in six months, sitting next to the people making the calls.

BuildBit builds production software for clients across the US, UK, Middle East, Africa and Australia: AI automation, web and mobile apps, Web3, industrial automation. Wellcome Care, Frakt360, NeamBladi and may more have trusted our work.

The role
There's no fixed job description, because the job changes as fast as the company does. One week it's product, the next it's ops, hiring, or a client pitch. You'll:
- Work directly with the CEO on the week's real priorities
- Take features from idea to shipped: specs, coordination with design and engineering, testing, docs
- Keep operations tight across teams: trackers, timelines, reporting, vendors
- Build decks, proposals and research for client and business conversations
- Own small projects end to end and report outcomes, not status updates
- Find what's broken internally and fix it before anyone asks

🙅🏻‍♂️If you want a narrow, predictable role, this isn't it.

You'll fit if you have
- Sharp written and spoken English (you'll write a lot)
- Structured thinking: you can turn a vague ask into steps
- High ownership: you close loops without reminders
- Comfort with Sheets, docs and decks, and the appetite to learn AI tools fast
- Real curiosity about technology and how software businesses run

Any degree. Freshers and experienced candidates both welcome.

What you get
A front-row seat to how a software company is actually run. Real ownership from week one, not shadow work. A clear path into product, operations or business, depending on where you're strongest. Scope and compensation that grow as fast as you deliver. Full-time, permanent. 6 days a week, on-site at Ahmedabad Office.

How to apply
Email your CV to shandhen@buildbit.ai with the subject "Founder's Office, Ahmedabad". In the body, tell us why this role, and one thing you've built, run or fixed on your own.
