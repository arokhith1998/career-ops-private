Company Description: ResultFlow is focused on transforming the direct-to-consumer (DTC) experience through true 1:1 personalization at scale. The company is building solutions that help brands engage each individual customer with tailored interactions and offers. ResultFlow emphasizes innovation, data-driven decisions, and customer-centric product development. Team members join an environment where experimentation, rapid learning, and collaboration are encouraged. As ResultFlow grows, there are opportunities to influence strategy, operations, and the future of personalized customer experiences.

Role Description: The Chief of Staff is a full-time, on-site role based in Bengaluru, supporting the executive leadership team in driving strategic initiatives and operational excellence. This role coordinates cross-functional projects, ensures alignment between departments, and acts as a key liaison between leadership and internal stakeholders. Day-to-day responsibilities include structuring and tracking strategic priorities, preparing executive briefs and presentations, managing leadership meeting agendas, and following up on action items to ensure timely completion. The Chief of Staff will analyze business performance, identify operational bottlenecks, and recommend improvements to processes and communication flows. This position also involves supporting decision-making with data, overseeing special projects, and helping shape organizational planning and culture as the company scales.

Qualifications:
- Strong strategic planning and business analysis skills, with the ability to translate high-level goals into clear roadmaps and measurable outcomes.
- Excellent project and program management capabilities, including organizing cross-functional initiatives, managing timelines, and driving execution.
- Outstanding written and verbal communication skills, with experience preparing executive-level reports, presentations, and strategic briefs.
- Demonstrated stakeholder management and collaboration skills, including the ability to work closely with senior leaders and diverse teams.
- Proficiency with productivity and collaboration tools (e.g., spreadsheets, presentation software, project management platforms).
- Experience in fast-paced, high-growth environments, preferably in technology, data, or DTC-focused businesses.
- Ability to work on-site in Bengaluru and manage multiple priorities with strong judgment and discretion.
- Bachelor's degree in business, management, engineering, or a related field; an advanced degree (MBA or equivalent) is a plus.
