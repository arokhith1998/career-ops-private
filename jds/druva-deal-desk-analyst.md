What we would be looking for in the future when a Deal Desk Analyst position opens up:

Role Purpose & Impact:

We are looking for an energized Deal Desk Analyst to help support our global field sales teams in building pricing proposals and deal structures that provide the optimum balance between the right commercial terms for our customers and the best results for Druva. As a key member of the Deal Desk team, this position will interact with colleagues across Sales, Sales Ops, Finance, Pricing, Order Management, Revenue Ops, and Legal. This individual will help drive standardization and business process improvements that result in increased efficiency and sales velocity as Druva continues to scale and grow.

What You'll Do:

- Partner with sales teams to structure complex deals, providing guidance that balances profitability with strategic goals and deal velocity.

- Drive continuous improvement by streamlining approval workflows, fixing process gaps, and leveraging AI tools to automate routine tasks and unlock data-driven insights.

- Translate deal data into actionable intelligence, preparing key reports on renewal metrics, partner incentives, and performance trends to influence leadership strategy.

- Collaborate cross-functionally with Revenue Operations, Legal, Finance, and Product teams to maintain robust compliance and scalable approval processes.

- Develop and maintain documentation to support internal and external processes, ensuring team efficiency and knowledge sharing.

- Tackle ad-hoc analysis and strategic initiatives to support the scaling needs of the business.

What Makes You a Great Fit:

- 2–3 years of experience in Deal Desk, Sales Ops, Finance, or Revenue Operations, preferably within the SaaS or enterprise software sector.

- Bachelor’s degree in Business, Finance, Economics, or a quantitative field.

- Proficiency in Salesforce (SFDC) and advanced Excel modeling (pivot tables, complex formulas, and scenario analysis).

- Deep understanding of SaaS business metrics (ACV, ARR, Churn, Net Retention, ARPU) and their impact on deal profitability.

- Proven ability to influence cross-functional stakeholders (Legal, Sales, Finance, Product) and build productive working relationships.

- Strong project management skills, with the ability to prioritize and execute multiple initiatives under tight deadlines.

- A strategic mindset for process improvement and a track record of using AI tools to automate workflows and drive efficiency.

What Success Looks Like

Success in this role means acting as a high-velocity, strategic engine for sales while safeguarding profitability as Druva scales. You win by partnering with sales teams to architect creative, cross-functionally aligned deal structures that accelerate sales cycles without sacrificing margin. Beyond managing daily deal flow, you drive process transformation by leveraging Salesforce, advanced Excel modeling, and AI automation to eliminate workflow friction and deliver data-driven insights. Ultimately, you serve as a trusted bridge across Sales, Legal, Finance, and RevOps, maintaining flawless compliance while empowering the team to close larger SaaS deals faster.
