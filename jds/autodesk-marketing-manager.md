Position Overview

Autodesk is hiring a senior manager of brand management to help bring one of the world’s most recognized design and technology brands to life. You’ll sit at the heart of our brand work, running the day-to-day of our biggest integrated campaigns and serving as the main point of contact for our agency partners.

This role is for a brand marketer with strong creative instincts and a knack for getting things done, someone who’s equally at home in a concepting session, an agency check-in, and a stakeholder review. You’ll help make sure Autodesk looks, sounds, and feels like itself everywhere, while keeping the work bold and fresh.

Location: United States (hybrid/remote)

Reports to: Director, Brand Management

Responsibilities

- Manage integrated advertising campaigns end to end, from brief through launch, and help the creative ideas get sharper (and stay intact) along the way
- Keep every brand expression true to Autodesk’s brand platform, narrative, and creative standards across channels and regions
- Be a creative partner teams want in the room: someone who spots a strong idea and helps make it better without slowing it down
- Manage day-to-day relationships with our brand agency partners, keeping scopes clear, collaboration strong, and output high quality
- Assess and optimize our Autodesk agency bullpen — pushing to create a best-in-class Integrated Agency Team
- Run briefing, feedback, and performance practices, and help us keep improving how we work together
- Share brand guidance on strategy, positioning, and execution with teams across Autodesk, supporting Brand Management as a center of excellence
- Work closely with marketing, product, communications, events, and regional teams so the brand shows up cohesively and with impact
- Navigate a busy stakeholder landscape with clarity and good humor, bringing people along without lowering the bar
- Turn business goals into clear brand and creative direction that teams and agencies can get behind
- Help shape the processes and rhythms that let brand management scale with a fast-growing, global SaaS business
- Weigh long-term brand building against near-term needs, keeping work moving quickly without cutting corners on quality
- Add to the culture of the Brand & Creative team

Minimum Qualifications

- 7+ years in brand management, brand marketing, or campaign management at a high-growth product or SaaS technology company, or at an agency serving one
- A track record of managing large, multichannel brand campaigns
- Hands-on experience working with external agencies and cross-functional partners

Preferred Qualifications

- A strong creative eye and sound judgment about what makes work great
- Agency management skills: you can push, partner, and elevate at the same time
- Strategic thinking balanced with strong follow-through
- A clear, confident communicator who brings order to ambiguity
- A calm, collaborative style and the ability to influence senior stakeholders
- Experience helping a brand grow or scale in a fast-moving organization
- Comfort working in a global, matrixed environment
