Pricing Analyst III

This position presents the opportunity to work in a strategically important and fast paced analytics team. The Pricing Analyst III will work cross-functionally and will be responsible for delivering data-driven, actionable insights that increase Sales and Profits.

Essential Duties And Responsibilities

- Analyze large amounts of data, synthesize, and communicate actionable insights / recommendations to business leaders that drive positive impact to Sales and Profits. Some examples of Strategic Pricing decisions include Promotional Optimization, Price Investments, Category Re-balancing, Competitive Pricing analysis, Price Zoning & Store Clustering, and program measurement/reporting.

- Develop KPI reports with clear and concise communication to executive leadership.

- Oversee large data-related pricing projects, collaborating with cross-functional departments to develop and implement improved reporting and data processes.

- Work closely with cross-functional teams to develop and produce reporting that measures and drives our merchandising objectives.

- Continually evaluate competitors and marketplace dynamics to assess the impact on our business and develop related pricing strategy recommendations.

- Develop deep understanding of category financial levers including sales, markdowns, funding, and profitability as inputs to our pricing strategy.

- Develop knowledge of customer shopping behaviors, customer segmentation, category strategies, and key business drivers as inputs to our pricing strategy.

- Partner with leadership to understand business initiatives and related pricing implications. Anticipate marketplace events and develop “what-if” scenarios to address changing business dynamics.

- Evaluate trends, customer purchase patterns and results to determine business impacts of marketing and promotional activities and identify improvement opportunities.

- Lead and execute complex business analyses and projects from concept to completion to drive business decision and efficiencies, including making the results accessible and relevant to business users.

- Build a strong partnership and collaboration with stakeholders across the organization, consulting on how to attain their business goals by leveraging data and insights.

- Design reports and executive summaries that clearly and concisely communicate analytical findings and recommendations to key stakeholders.

- Develop and implement recommendations for continuous process and system improvements, including identifying ways to automate analyses and processes.
