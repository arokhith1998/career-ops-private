## Full job description (verbatim)

Role: Growth Marketing Manager

Location: San Francisco Bar Area

Salary range per year: 145,000–175,000 USD

Alcatraz is looking for a Growth Marketing Manager to drive pipeline across the full customer journey, from first touch to expansion. This is a hands-on role: you will plan and run campaigns, steer our digital channels and webinar program, build lifecycle programs for prospects and customers, and report on what each one delivers.

Reporting to the Head of Growth Marketing, you will work closely with Sales, SDRs, Product Marketing, and Events to turn plans into pipeline. You'll get regular coaching and a front-row seat to how a growth-stage company builds its demand engine, making this a strong step toward a growth marketing leadership role.The right candidate is a detail-oriented operator with a record of shipped campaigns, energized by learning fast and finding smarter ways to get things done.

 

WHAT YOU'LL OWN:

Integrated Campaigns

• Build and maintain quarterly campaign plans that span a mix of channels and tactics

• Run integrated campaigns end to end, from brief to results, coordinating inputs, timelines, and the campaign calendar across Product Marketing, Content, Events, Ops, and Sales

• Partner with Sales and SDRs on ABM, campaign priorities, and follow-up, and apply their feedback on lead quality to the next campaign

• Turn campaigns that perform into repeatable programs and playbooks

Digital Programs

• Own the strategy for paid digital channels, including channel mix, audiences, and testing priorities, alongside the specialist who manages day-to-day platform work

• Keep paid programs supplied with fresh creative and assets by sourcing them from Product Marketing, Content

• Run the webinar program as an ongoing series tied to campaigns, from topics and speakers to the calendar

• Experiment with messaging, targeting, offers, and landing pages, and scale what works

Lifecycle Marketing

• Plan and maintain email nurture programs for prospects and existing customers that move them toward sales conversations, and work with marketing operations to launch them

• Audit the full customer journey across touchpoints, from first visit to expansion, and identify where to remove friction or improve conversion

• Run expansion campaigns for existing customers, partnering with Sales to spot opportunities and time outreach to where each customer is in their journey

Performance & Reporting

• Track campaign and channel performance against pipeline goals, from MQL to opportunity

• Analyze results after each campaign and adjust targeting, offers, and timing based on the data

• Maintain the weekly pipeline dashboard and prepare weekly marketing updates for leadership, marketing, sales, and the broader company

• Track campaign spend against budget

 

WHAT YOU BRING:

• 5–7 years in go-to-market roles, such as demand gen or growth marketing, sales, or SDR/BDR

• Track record of running integrated, multi-channel campaigns from brief to results that drove pipeline

• Fluency with B2B funnel metrics: you know how leads become opportunities and which levers move them

• Working knowledge of marketing automation and CRM reporting

• Exceptional attention to detail: you catch the broken link, wrong date, or missing tracking before launch

• Project management rigor and comfort running multiple workstreams and stakeholders at once

• Strong analytical instincts: you can pull a report, spot what changed, and explain why

• Clear, concise communication, including presenting results to leadership

• Strong writing skills, especially for campaign emails and promotional copy

• Hands-on experience using AI tools to work faster or automate workflows

• Bachelor's degree in any field, including technical disciplines

 

NICE TO HAVE:

• Experience in physical security, access control, or adjacent enterprise hardware/software markets

• Experience marketing to enterprise buyers through long, multi-stakeholder sales cycles

• Experience building repeatable programs from scratch at a Series B–D stage company

• Experience with HubSpot, GA4, Webflow, Claude, and project management tools like Jira
