Company Description Chai Nagri is a fast-growing F&B and retail brand on a mission to make Indian chai a globally loved beverage. Founded in 2017 by two IT engineers turned entrepreneurs, the brand blends traditional Indian chai with a contemporary café experience that appeals to modern audiences. With 30+ outlets across multiple Indian states, Chai Nagri offers freshly brewed chai in diverse variants along with a range of comfort food. The company is expanding into retail with ready-to-brew chai boxes, premium tea gifting under Chai Nagri Creations, and corporate gifting solutions. Chai Nagri also provides franchise opportunities, inviting partners to join its nationwide chai community.

Role Description The Founder's Office Executive role is a full-time, on-site position based in Sahibzada Ajit Singh Nagar. This role supports the founders in strategic planning, project execution, and day-to-day operational coordination across café operations, retail, and franchise initiatives. Responsibilities include preparing reports and presentations, tracking key business metrics, conducting market and competitor research, and coordinating with internal teams for timely execution of initiatives. The executive will help streamline processes, manage follow-ups on critical tasks, and assist in organizing meetings, reviews, and communication with stakeholders. The role calls for a proactive individual who can prioritize multiple workstreams, maintain high-quality documentation, and act as a trusted partner to the founding team.
