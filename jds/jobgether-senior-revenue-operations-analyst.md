## Full JD text

This position is listed on behalf of a partner company, who manages all applications and next steps. Our partner is looking for a Senior Revenue Operations Analyst based in the United States.

We are seeking a highly analytical and detail-oriented Senior Revenue Operations Analyst to support and optimize sales operations across the Americas. Reporting to the Sales Operations Manager, you will play a key role in delivering reliable revenue insights, maintaining CRM data integrity, and improving commercial processes. You will lead recurring sales reporting, manage inbound lead workflows, support quoting and order-book accuracy, and contribute to monthly commission cycles. A major focus of this position will be supporting the transition to Salesforce, helping design and build the CRM environment, including data structures, pipelines, dashboards, and integrations with SAP and other business tools. Working closely with Sales, Finance, Marketing, and global technology teams, you will help establish consistent reporting standards and scalable processes. This is a fully remote opportunity for an experienced professional who enjoys building solutions, improving systems, and turning complex commercial data into actionable insights.

Accountabilities

As a Senior Revenue Operations Analyst, you will oversee critical reporting, CRM administration, data management, and sales planning activities. You will help strengthen the operational foundations of the Americas sales organization by ensuring accurate information, efficient workflows, and consistent performance measurement.

Sales Reporting and Analytics:

Prepare and deliver weekly and monthly sales operating review reports covering order intake, pipeline performance, forecasts, and renewals.

Maintain the dashboards and reporting frameworks used by sales leadership.

Respond to ad hoc analytical requests from the Head of Sales Americas and regional sales directors, ensuring all reported figures are accurate and traceable.

Monitor lead conversion by source and routing channel.

Track software and subscription bookings, including iCMORE, APIDS, and IOA, alongside equipment orders.

Maintain consistent KPI definitions across reports and business reviews.

CRM Administration and Data Quality:

Manage daily CRM administration, including user access, fields, pipelines, reports, and dashboards.

Support the transition from HubSpot to Salesforce, contributing to the design and implementation of the new CRM environment.

Help define CRM objects, data structures, workflows, reporting requirements, and integrations with SAP and quoting tools.

Conduct regular data quality audits covering deal ownership, sales stages, close dates, amounts, products, and loss reasons.

Correct records, coordinate bulk updates with sales representatives, and document data governance rules.

Train new sales team members, including software, inside sales, and channel hires, on CRM processes and best practices.

Lead Flow and Inside Sales Support:

Manage the inbound lead process, ensuring leads are captured accurately in the CRM and routed to field sales, partners, or inside sales teams.

Monitor lead follow-up activities and ensure opportunities are tracked through to closure.

Maintain consistent lead management standards and improve routing processes where needed.

Account Planning and Territory Management:

Maintain account planning templates and supporting documentation.

Ensure territory assignments, account ownership, and channel allocations remain accurate.

Support the annual sales planning cycle through territory and account data analysis.

Quoting and Order Book Management:

Support sales representatives with the quoting tool, including pricing, lead times, Incoterms, and product configurations.

Log, investigate, and coordinate the resolution of quoting tool issues.

Maintain accurate pricing tiers and discount rules within the quoting system.

Reconcile monthly order-book information against SAP and Finance records in collaboration with the Order to Invoice team.

Ensure bookings, backlog figures, and revenue data are consistent and accurate.

Commissions and Planning Support:

Prepare monthly commission inputs, including closed orders, compensation plan assignments, and commission splits.

Maintain supporting commission workbooks and documentation for approval.

Contribute to annual planning activities through territory, quota, and customer segmentation analysis.

Support the preparation of sales compensation plan letters.

Cross-Functional Collaboration:

Serve as the primary operational contact between Sales and Finance, Order to Invoice, Marketing, and the global Salesforce program.

Coordinate data definitions, process handoffs, system improvements, and issue resolution across teams.

Contribute to the development of scalable reporting, automation, and CRM processes.

Use AI-assisted tools to improve analytical workflows, documentation, and scripting activities.

Requirements

The ideal candidate combines extensive revenue operations or sales analytics experience with strong technical expertise in CRM administration, reporting, and business intelligence. You should be comfortable working with complex commercial datasets, building systems from the ground up, and supporting senior sales stakeholders with accurate, actionable insights. Strong communication skills, attention to detail, and the ability to manage competing priorities are essential.

Bachelor's degree in Business, Finance, Economics, Data Analytics, or a related field. An MBA is considered an advantage.

At least 5 years of experience in Sales Operations, Revenue Operations, Financial Planning and Analysis (FP&A), Commercial Analytics, or a similar data-intensive role.

Experience in manufacturing, technology, or government contracting environments is preferred.

Salesforce Certified Administrator certification is required.

Hands-on Salesforce administration experience, including building reports and dashboards, managing users and fields, maintaining pipelines, and ensuring data quality.

Advanced Excel skills, including lookups, Power Query, and pivot models.

Experience with SAP or another enterprise resource planning (ERP) system is preferred.

Familiarity with Power BI or another business intelligence and reporting platform.

Previous HubSpot experience is an advantage, particularly HubSpot Sales Hub or Revenue Operations Academy certifications.

Comfortable using AI assistants such as Microsoft Copilot for data analysis, documentation, and scripting.

Ability to build scripts and workflow automations with AI-assisted tools.

Knowledge of SQL or Python is a plus.

Strong analytical and problem-solving abilities, with the capacity to translate complex data into meaningful business insights.

Excellent attention to detail and accuracy, particularly when working with financial and commercial reporting.

Strong written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to present findings to sales representatives and senior leadership.

Ability to document analytical findings and provide clear, traceable sources for reported figures.

Strong organizational skills and the ability to deliver accurate work under tight deadlines.

Must be a U.S. Person (U.S. citizen or lawful permanent resident) under applicable U.S. export control regulations.

Must be able to pass a federal background check.

Visa sponsorship is not available for this position.

Ability to travel up to 10% of the time, including approximately quarterly visits to Edgewood, Maryland, for onboarding, training, and the annual sales kickoff. Potential for future international travel may arise.

Ability to work fully remotely within the United States, with a preference for candidates located in the Eastern or Central time zones to facilitate collaboration with operational and corporate teams.

Ability to maintain working-hour overlap with teams based in Edgewood, Finance, Order to Invoice, and headquarters.
