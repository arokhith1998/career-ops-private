### About Thrive Market
Thrive Market was founded in 2014 with a mission to make healthy and sustainable living easy and affordable for everyone. As an online, membership-based market, we deliver the highest quality healthy, and sustainable products at member-only prices, while matching every paid membership with a free one for someone in need. Every day, we leverage innovative technology and member-first thinking to help our over 1,700,000+ members find better products, support better brands, and build a better world in the process. We are also a Certified B Corporation, a Public Benefit Corporation, and a Climate Neutral Certified company.

### The Role
We are seeking an experienced and forward-thinking Senior Product Manager to lead the development and enhancement of our enterprise accounting/finance systems. In this role, you will play a critical role in driving the strategic direction of our financial systems, with a focus on supporting the unique needs of our e-commerce online grocery business. You will also be responsible for ensuring alignment with our supply chain and e-commerce systems and processes to optimize efficiency and scalability.

### Responsibilities
- Lead the product vision, strategy, and roadmap for enterprise accounting/finance systems, with a focus on process/system Automation and data quality
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams, including supply chain, finance, accounting, operations, IT, and E-Commerce to define product requirements and prioritize features that align with business objectives
- Drive the agile development process, including sprint planning, backlog grooming, and release management, to deliver high-quality solutions on time and within budget
- Work closely with key stakeholders to gather feedback, validate product concepts, and iterate on features to improve usability, performance, and customer satisfaction
- Develop a deep understanding of supply chain and e-commerce systems and processes and identify opportunities for integration and optimization with enterprise accounting/finance systems
- Partner with supply chain, operations, and e-commerce teams to optimize accounting/finance processes, streamline workflows, and drive operational efficiency
- Act as a subject matter expert for enterprise accounting/finance systems and provide guidance and support to internal teams and external stakeholders

### Qualifications
- 7+ years of experience in product management, with a proven track record of successfully delivering enterprise software solutions, preferably in the e-commerce or retail industry
- Strong understanding of accounting principles, financial processes, and enterprise software systems, with experience in the e-commerce sector being highly desirable
- General understanding of supply chain and e-commerce systems and processes
- Excellent analytical and problem-solving skills
- Strong communication and interpersonal skills
- Experience working in an agile development environment, with proficiency in agile methodologies and tools (e.g., Jira, Agile)
- Proven ability to lead and motivate teams, drive results, and thrive in a fast-paced, dynamic environment
- Experience with cloud-based technologies, SaaS platforms, and enterprise integrations is a plus
