Company: Uni Seoul, an impulse lifestyle brand founded in 2023 and based in Pune. Runs 18 stores, sells 1,000+ in-house designed products priced from Rs. 99 to Rs. 2,999, and sells through its website (uniseoul.in) and quick-commerce platforms. Closed a Series A round in 2026 and plans to expand to 500 stores across India, the UAE, and Southeast Asia.

Role: owns growth for the D2C website and quick-commerce channels, including:
- P&L, contribution margin, and channel growth plans
- Paid acquisition on Meta, Google, and quick-commerce ad platforms, plus agency partners
- Building viral drops, collaborations, and influencer/affiliate programs measured on sales
- Website conversion, A/B testing, and average order value
- Leading the cataloguing and visual commerce team
- CRM and retention journeys, plus a unified growth dashboard
- Hiring and leading growth team heads, and presenting growth performance to the board

Must-haves: 10+ years in D2C, e-commerce, or consumer internet, including 4+ years leading growth for a consumer brand; experience growing a brand on Blinkit, Zepto, or Swiggy Instamart; a campaign or product that went viral with clear, stated results; strong command of unit economics, cohorts, and attribution; experience managing agencies and multi-function teams.

Good-to-haves: experience in lifestyle, beauty, fashion, gifting, toys, or home categories; marketplace experience (Amazon, Myntra, Nykaa); Shopify skills; omnichannel or fast-growth brand experience.

How to apply: send a CV to talent@uniseoul.in with subject "Application for Head of Growth - D2C & Quick Commerce." Posting asks applicants to include, in three lines or fewer, something they made go viral and what it actually sold.
