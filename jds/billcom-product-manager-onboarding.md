## About
BILL replaces outdated financial processes with tools to help businesses (startups to established
brands) make smarter decisions and gain control of operations. Remote-first, with offices in San Jose, CA
and Draper, UT.

## Role
Join the Onboarding team: own features within the onboarding experience for how small/midsize businesses
sign up for, get started with, and gain value from the BILL platform. Works with a squad of engineers and
designers, with guidance from senior product leaders. Uses product analytics and customer feedback to
improve activation, conversion, and early retention in the first 90 days.

## Responsibilities
Owns features within AP onboarding, working independently with the squad to develop/launch/maintain
them; drafts goals and success metrics, shares regular status updates; writes product requirements and
drives scrum activities with Engineering (scoping, dependency tracking, delivery of medium-sized
features); partners with UX/UI designers/researchers; works with Analytics and product leaders on metric
goals and data-driven hypotheses; collaborates with Marketing, Sales, Customer Success on launches/GTM
readiness; contributes to the team roadmap aligned with broader AP/AR/accountant-experience goals;
advocates for customer needs throughout product development.

## Requirements
Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Business, or a related field, or equivalent practical
experience; 1 to 2 years of product management experience; experience owning a feature and shipping it
with an engineering/design squad; experience using data and customer feedback for product decisions;
clear communication/collaboration across design, engineering, GTM teams. Desired: B2B products experience;
FinTech industry experience; exposure to onboarding, activation, or growth funnels.
