## Role Summary
Company is building a large food manufacturing unit to US FDA and BIS specifications. Role
supports the founder across two phases plus ongoing founder support.

## Responsibilities
- **Phase 1 (unit detailing/execution):** Translate project vision into technical/
  operational specs; maintain master project plan; run review meetings with
  consultants/vendors; coordinate MEP consultants, food technologists, machinery makers;
  maintain FDA/BIS compliance matrix; support audit readiness; review quotations/BOQs;
  flag risks early; conduct site visits.
- **Phase 2 (post-commissioning):** Lead high-priority strategic projects, build business
  cases, coordinate cross-functional initiatives.
- **Founder support (ongoing):** Prepare decision notes/status reports; track
  budgets/capex with finance; represent founder in meetings.

## Requirements
- Engineering degree (see above); IIT/tier-1 background preferred.
- 4-8 years of experience, ideally combining engineering execution with management
  consulting; MBA a plus.
- Experience on a manufacturing, food processing, or industrial greenfield project.
- Working knowledge of MEP systems, plant utilities, process equipment.
- Familiarity with regulated manufacturing and project tools (MS Project, Primavera,
  AutoCAD review, advanced Excel/PowerPoint).
- Strong analytical, communication, follow-through skills; comfortable with ambiguity;
  discreet. English and Hindi fluency preferred.

## Good to Have
Food, pet food, dairy, FMCG, or cold chain experience; exposure to FDA rules, BIS
certification, GMP, HACCP, or ISO 22000; vendor negotiation/contract management.

## Offers
Direct access to founder; ownership of a large FDA/BIS-compliant facility from detailing
through commissioning; long-term path into broader leadership.
