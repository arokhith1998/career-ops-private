The Role
We're hiring a Demand Generation Manager to build and scale the demand engine at Suite.

This role is responsible for generating qualified pipeline by creating awareness, capturing buying intent, and converting interest into sales opportunities. You'll own the strategy, execution, and optimization of our demand generation programs across digital channels while continuously improving how prospects discover, engage with, and buy from Suite.

This is more than campaign execution. We're looking for someone who understands how to build demand from first principles - someone who can identify where growth opportunities exist, develop hypotheses, launch programs quickly, measure results, and continuously improve performance.

You'll work closely with Sales, Product Marketing, Revenue Operations and Leadership to ensure marketing is driving measurable business impact.

Most importantly, you're an independent operator who combines creativity with analytical rigor and uses AI to accelerate execution, not replace strategic thinking.
