Quadric is redefining edge AI with the industry's first General Purpose Neural Processing Unit (GPNPU), enabling developers to run both neural network inference and conventional C++ code on a single programmable architecture. Our technology powers intelligent edge devices across automotive, industrial, robotics, and embedded systems.Founded in 2016 and based in downtown Burlingame, California, Quadric is building the world's first supercomputer designed for the real-time needs of edge devices. Quadric aims to empower developers in every industry with superpowers to create tomorrow's technology, today. The company was co-founded by technologists from MIT and Carnegie Mellon, who were previously the technical co-founders of the Bitcoin computing company 21.The OpportunityQuadric has created an innovative General-Purpose Neural Processing Unit (GPNPU) architecture—the Chimera GPNPU—which executes both neural network inference workloads and conventional C++ DSP code on edge and endpoint devices. As the Senior Product Manager, Hardware, you will own the Chimera GPNPU IP roadmap across five strategic tracks: Compute, Efficiency, Scalability, Safety, and Integration. Setting feature sets for upcoming IP generations, driving architecture freeze decisions, and partnering directly with anchor SoC customer teams worldwide, you'll ensure our silicon IP powers production devices shipping in 2027 and beyond.What You'll DoHardware Roadmap & Architecture FreezeTranslate customer requirements, competitive pressures, and architectural constraints into track-by-track feature lists with explicit gating. Build the business and technical case for contested architecture decisions and lead trade-off reviews with architects to bring clear recommendations to the Product Steering Committee (PSC). Drive IP usability by identifying silent customer workarounds (API wrappers, custom RTL hooks, glue logic) and productizing proper architectural solutions. kernels when required. Customer Engagement & Technical OwnershipServe as the named hardware product owner for key anchor accounts, presenting new features and integrating formal architecture feedback into the product roadmap. Conduct architecture reviews with technical buyers, including Tier-1 automotive and OEM SoC design teams. Partner with international customers, particularly supporting key anchor accounts in the Japanese and global semiconductor markets. Competitive Intelligence & SoC IntegrationAnalyze competitive architectures (e.g., Synopsys NPX6, Arm Ethos-U85, Ceva NeuPro-M, NVIDIA Jetson) to anticipate market needs and inform product requirements before gaps appear in customer evaluations. Define and position SoC integration features, specifying which knobs (AXI4/ACE-Lite, CoreSight, power domains, performance counters) are exposed and productized. Sequence functional safety positioning, lockstep configurations, FMEDA work, and safety-island architectures for automotive and critical application standards. Productize key integration and usability knobs to streamline customer IP integration cycles.
