Founded in 1936, GEICO is a member of the Berkshire Hathaway family of companies and one of the largest auto insurers in the United States.

- 5+ years of experience in product management, with a track record of delivering successful voice and conversational AI products in a fast-paced environment.

Preferred Qualifications
- Advanced degree in Computer Science, Human-Computer Interaction, Business, or related field.
- Direct experience with Amazon Connect ecosystem (Amazon Lex, Contact Lens, Voice ID).
