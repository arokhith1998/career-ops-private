## Full JD Text (verbatim)

Title: Sales Operations Analyst - OEM
Location: Santa Clara, CA 95054
Duration: 12 months Contract

Position Summary:
We are seeking a detail-oriented, self-motivated, hands-on Operations Analyst to support the expansion of our global partnership with Nokia. The strategic partnership spans complex OEM fulfillment, joint telecom solutions, and multi-region supply and support models.

In this role, you will serve as the operational backbone for the Nokia business, supporting order-to-cash workflows, managing price books and quotes, coordinating lab evaluation logistics, tracking incentive programs, and aligning internal cross-functional teams (Legal, Finance, Deal Desk, RevAcc, and Support).

Key Responsibilities:
- Support with quoting, POs intake, and fulfilling orders across standard OEM and "OEM Lite" sales motions.
- Serve as the central operational point of contact for field requests (nokia.quotes and nokia.presales distribution lists)
- Intake of Nokia Deal Registration and Quote Request
- Quote process OEM and OEM Light: creation, approval, submission to Nokia
- Quote process alignment in OEM Light: produce quotes for support only, and connect those with the HW/SW quote to end-customer (coordinate with Everpure end-customer sales team)
- Updating of Quote Tracker (internal and Nokia)
- Keep track of Quote SLAs and feed that back into MBRs
- Interface daily with Deal Desk, Field Sales, and Regional Teams to support order intake and ensure correct booking, fulfillment, and accurate CRM maintenance.
- Review Nokia orders and support with order booking according to agreed SLAs
- Update Nokia on the delivery status of the orders
- Expedite orders when necessary
- Keep track of orders delivery accuracy and feed that back into MBRs
- Coordinate custom supply models and delivery processes.
- Coordinate PERs with Nokia
- Trigger install services team once PER is received
- Update final install addresses in SFDC
- Monitor and audit OEM incentive funding commitments and track payments to Nokia.
- Assist in generating sales reports and in MBRs with Nokia.
- Manage logistics, inventory, and Evaluation Agreements for lab equipment allocated across global integration labs (e.g., Sunnyvale, Tampere, Naperville).
- Track action items from weekly cadence meetings and MBRs
- Keep our SOP always updated
- Liaise with OEM PM to shape internal processes and tools to better support the OEM model
- Maintenance of the Everpure-Nokia Microsite.

Qualifications & Requirements:
Experience: min 3 years of sales operations, deal desk, or partner operations experience in enterprise IT, storage, or telecommunications environments.
Familiarity with OEM sales models or other complex order-to-cash processes, multi-tiered channel structures, and hardware/software support models.
Proficiency in CRM software (SFDC), ERP system workflows, deal desk procedures, and financial reporting.
Ability to drive alignment across cross-functional teams, specifically Deal Desk, Logistics, and Global Sales.
Capability in modeling and process documentation (e.g. SOPs, RACI diagrams).

Languages: English. Other languages a plus (Spanish, Portuguese, French)

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):
- On-time turnaround and accuracy of OEM price quotes and booking execution.
- Smooth implementation and compliance of OEM / OEM Lite SOPs.
- Accurate financial tracking and reporting.
- Efficient lab equipment deployment and contract turnaround.
