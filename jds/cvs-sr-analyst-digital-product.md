## Job Description
Bolster Signify Health product team. Bringing healthcare services into homes through in-home and virtual care solutions. Acts as "a force multiplier for the Product team" supporting multiple product lines.

## Responsibilities
- Partner with Senior Director on team objectives, capacity planning, and cross-functional alignment
- Support market research and opportunity assessment across the product portfolio
- Prepare executive materials and stakeholder communications
- Collaborate with Product Managers on requirements documentation, user research, and MVP delivery
- Analyze operational and clinical data to identify improvement areas
- Support full product lifecycle from discovery through retrospective analysis
- Conduct qualitative and quantitative research on product opportunities and care models
- Develop value propositions through stakeholder needs assessment
- Build performance dashboards and competitive benchmarking tools
- Synthesize member and provider feedback for design iteration
- Coordinate quarterly and annual planning activities
- Maintain centralized documentation and roadmaps
- Synthesize clinical, operations, engineering, and finance inputs

## Requirements
- 2+ years in product management, operations, analytics, consulting, or strategy (healthcare preferred)
- Data synthesis and analytical capability
- Product lifecycle exposure across discovery, design, development, measurement
- Value proposition development experience
- Cross-functional team collaboration skills
- Proficiency with Excel, PowerPoint, JIRA, Confluence

## Preferred
- Healthcare or health tech background
- Product management experience
- Passion for improving healthcare access
