## Job description (full text via LinkedIn guest API)

About Canals

Canals builds software for wholesale distributors, helping them operate more efficiently through automation and AI.

Our customers are the companies responsible for moving the materials that power the real economy: electrical supplies, plumbing products, roofing materials, HVAC equipment, and more. Every day, thousands of people rely on Canals to help process orders, manage purchasing, handle accounts payable, and streamline critical business workflows.

We're a rapidly growing company with a team of roughly 140 people distributed across North and South America.

The Opportunity

As the Product Marketer for all Canals solutions, you'll be building the function from the ground up and playing a key role in establishing our messaging. We currently have two products with strong market fit, a third product in beta testing, and a roadmap of major new features, along with frequent feature updates based on customer feedback.

In this role, you'll lead all product and feature launches, from market research to go-to-market strategy and execution. You'll work closely with stakeholders across the company, including Engineering, Sales, Customer Success and Product, as well as Marketing teammates, to drive adoption and cross-sell. As our needs grow, there will also be opportunity for you to grow and lead a team of Product Marketing Managers.

What You'll Do
- Lead the product and feature launch process
- Plan and manage launch audiences, tactics and timelines
- Develop messaging and positioning based on primary and secondary research
- Train and enable go-to-market teams on the new product/feature
- Develop supporting marketing materials, e.g., one pagers, website pages
- Manage communications to customers through marketing channels, e.g., email
- Coordinate cross-functional teams to execute launch tactics
- Create and maintain personas, product messaging, and positioning, based on the latest market insights and learnings
- Translate messaging and positioning into marketing and sales assets, e.g., slide decks, campaign ads, thought leadership
- Capture and organize competitive intelligence to support Sales and Marketing
- Track and measure adoption and effectiveness
- Influence the product roadmap based on market feedback
- Serve as a go-to resource for questions about product features and functionality

What We're Looking For
- 5+ years experience in Product Marketing
- Excellent written and verbal communication skills; ability to take complex concepts and distill them into digestible, relatable, and easy-to understand writing
- Strong cross-functional collaborator, works well with and can "speak the language" of Product, Sales, Customer Success, Support and more
- Self-motivated and proactive - someone who gets things done without hand-holding
- Comfortable juggling multiple projects and shifting timelines
- Experience in a startup or fast-paced environment is a plus

Why Join Canals
- We're building software that solves real problems for an industry that keeps the world running.
- We've found strong product-market fit and continue to grow quickly.
- We're remote-first, flexible, and distributed across North and South America.
- We value ownership, transparency, and continuous improvement.
