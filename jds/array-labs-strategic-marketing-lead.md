## Full JD Text

About Us

Array Labs builds advanced radar systems to help humanity understand and respond to changes across the physical world.

We're launching a coordinated fleet of radar satellites to create a high-resolution 3D map of the Earth, updated in real time, enabling faster, smarter decision-making for government and commercial organizations supporting disaster response, infrastructure resilience, and mission-critical geopolitical intelligence.

We design and build our satellites end-to-end, producing the world's most advanced earth observation satellites. Our fleet will deliver unprecedented levels of accuracy, coverage, and responsiveness to power critical insights precisely where they're needed most.

About The Job

Array Labs is looking for a Strategic Marketing Lead to shape how we tell our story to customers, investors, and partners. This is a hands-on role at a fast-moving company: you'll help craft the strategy, the narrative, and the materials we use to represent Array Labs externally. Working alongside our comms, design, and engineering teams, your work will sit at the center of how Array Labs presents itself to the world.

Responsibilities

- Build out Array's marketing strategy as we grow the brand, break into new markets, launch products, and pursue high-stakes business development opportunities
- Work closely with comms, engineering, business development, and brand to translate highly technical content into accessible, compelling narratives
- Build and maintain pitch decks, sales presentations, and briefing materials for government and commercial audiences
- Create core materials to carry the strategy to market, communicating Array's value proposition and products across different contexts and buyers
- Define Array's positioning in the market, mapping the competitive landscape and shaping our message for different external audiences

Basic Qualifications

- 2–5 years in a marketing-focused or content-forward role, ideally at a defense tech, aerospace, satellite, or similarly technical company
- Bachelors degree in relevant subjects, or in Government, History, English, or other Liberal Arts degrees
- Demonstrated experience in brand marketing, product marketing, or communications
- Proven ability to work with highly technical subject matter and extract clear, compelling narratives from engineers and experts for non-technical audiences
- Strong writing and slide design skills with hands-on ownership of written content, data visualization, and visual layout (PowerPoint, Google Slides)
- Experience setting direction and establishing ownership in a fast-paced, often ambiguous environment, with an eye for detail and consistency

Preferred Skills & Experience

- Background in a STEM field or strong marketing and communications training, ideally both
- Experience developing and owning marketing or messaging strategy, crafting narrative frameworks for a product line or brand
- Experience positioning against competitors and tailoring a message to distinct buyer audiences
- Familiarity with Government contracting
- Strong editorial judgment and a low tolerance for jargon and filler
- Prior experience at a high-growth startup or early-stage company where marketing and/or communications function was being built, not inherited
