## Description

About Arango:
Arango delivers a unified, natively multimodel contextual data platform that powers AI agents, assistants, and applications with the unified, current, and trusted business context needed to reason, decide, and act at scale.

The Arango Contextual Data Platform connects fragmented enterprise data with LLMs, copilots, and AI agents through a simplified architecture delivered out of the box. By combining graph, vector, document, key-value, and search capabilities in a single platform, Arango eliminates the complex stacks many organizations build to operationalize enterprise AI.

Trusted by organizations including NVIDIA, HPE, the London Stock Exchange, PSI CRO, the U.S. Air Force, NIH, Siemens, Transient.AI, Matpriskollen, and Articul8, Arango helps enterprises move from AI pilots to reliable production systems faster while lowering infrastructure complexity and total cost of ownership. Arango is a proud member of the NVIDIA Inception Program and the AWS ISV Accelerate Program.

**About The Role**
We are seeking a highly analytical, creative, and hands-on Senior Manager, Digital & Performance Marketing to lead the execution and optimization of Arango's digital acquisition and web experience, from how target audiences discover us through paid, organic, AI search, and video to how they engage with our website and move into meaningful product experiences.

This is a player-coach role for someone who combines deep performance marketing expertise with strong capabilities across web, search, creative, and video.

**What You'll Do**
- Paid Acquisition & Performance: own hands-on paid media execution and optimization across paid search, paid social, display, YouTube, retargeting, and other relevant digital channels; manage and optimize allocated paid media spend.
- Website & Product Experience: own website performance and conversion optimization; play a key role in launching and optimizing Arango's new product experience.
- SEO, AEO & GEO: own SEO strategy and optimization while expanding Arango's visibility across Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) and Generative Engine Optimization (GEO).
- Video & Digital Creative: develop and optimize Arango's YouTube and digital video strategy across paid, organic, website, and social channels.
- Demand Generation & Campaigns: define quarterly and annual demand goals with Sales leadership; drive outbound strategy; manage events and field programs; own inbound lead management, forms, and campaign tracking, co-managing HubSpot with the Director of Growth Marketing.
- Optimize & Scale: build a disciplined experimentation approach; evaluate new channels, formats, technologies, and AI-powered tools.

**What We're Looking For**
- 7+ years of progressive experience in digital, growth, or performance marketing, ideally within high-growth B2B SaaS or enterprise technology.
- Deep hands-on experience managing paid acquisition across Google and LinkedIn, with experience in YouTube and other relevant digital channels.
- Strong experience across website optimization, CRO, landing pages, digital analytics, A/B testing, and digital funnel analysis.
- Strong working knowledge of SEO and emerging AEO/GEO strategies.
- Experience optimizing web-to-product journeys, free trials, interactive product experiences, self-service experiences, or other product-led digital motions.
- Experience effectively managing agencies, freelancers, or other external creative and technical resources.
- AI-forward, with demonstrated use of AI tools to accelerate creative development, content production, analysis, experimentation, and marketing workflows.

**Preferred Qualifications**
- Industry experience: Background in databases, data management, data platforms, AI, developer tools, or data infrastructure.
- Entrepreneurial, builder mindset.
- Experience marketing to technical and enterprise audiences, including developers, data engineers, architects, AI leaders, and technology executives.
