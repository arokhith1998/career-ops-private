Role: Deal Desk Strategy Manager
Company: Freshworks
Employment type / seniority: Full-time, Mid-Senior level
Industry: IT Services and IT Consulting
Pay: Base salary of $156,825-$193,725/yr, plus bonus eligibility. Compensation depends on factors including location, experience, and level.

Experience required
- 7-10+ years in Deal Desk, Sales Operations, Revenue Operations, or a similar deal-support function, including enterprise or strategic-tier deals

Role overview
- Senior individual contributor reporting to the Senior Director of Deal Desk
- Works with Account Executives and regional sales leaders on large, complex deals, from early strategy through close
- Coordinates with Legal, Finance, Sales Ops, and Operations

Key responsibilities
- Lead deal desk work on the largest deals in the region
- Start deal strategy conversations early, before approvals or paperwork are needed
- Own deal structuring, order form updates, and documentation
- Manage internal approvals and contract redlines with Legal
- Join customer calls to discuss deal terms
- Escalate and unblock stalled deals
- Mentor newer Deal Desk team members
- Recommend process and workflow improvements
- Support quarter-end and month-end close periods, including some after-hours work

Key requirements
- Experience acting as a trusted advisor to sales leaders
- Working knowledge of contract structuring, order forms, pricing, and discounting
- Ability to work with Legal, Finance, and Sales Ops on contract and revenue recognition questions
- Strong judgment in triaging deal complexity
- Excellent written and verbal communication, including with customers
- Interest in growing into people leadership
- Analytical, process-oriented mindset
- Flexibility for after-hours support during peak close windows
