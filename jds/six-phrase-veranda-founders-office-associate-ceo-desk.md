Job Position: Founder's Office Associate

Responsibilities:

1) Working with the CEO of the organization

2) Shadowing CEO's activities of the organization

3) Manage CEO's calendars and set up the meetings

4) Scheduling meetings with Internal Teams and External Stake Holders.

5) Take minutes during meetings and circulating the MOM with the Teams.

6) Oversee the entire operations activities of the organisation and doing regular follow-ups with the team.

7) Collecting Data metrics from the Internal Stake Holders.

8) Aligning with the overall objective of the organization

Strategic Partnership: Partner closely with the CEO to develop and implement the company's strategic vision and long-term goals.

Project Management: Oversee and lead key strategic initiatives, managing timelines, budgets, and resources.

Communication & Collaboration: Facilitate effective communication and collaboration between the CEO, executive team, and other key stakeholders.

Information Management: Gather and synthesize critical information from across the organization to inform the CEO's decision-making.

Problem-Solving: Identify and address operational and strategic challenges proactively, presenting solutions and recommendations.

Confidentiality & Discretion: Maintain the highest level of confidentiality and discretion in all matters related to the CEO and the company.
