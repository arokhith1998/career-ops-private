**What You Can Expect**

In this role, you'll shape the direction of cloud telephony features as part of the Zoom Phone product team, owning objectives and KPIs that directly influence how millions of people communicate at work. You'll work at the intersection of customer insight, data, and cross-functional execution, translating complex telephony requirements into reliable, user-centered product experiences. This is a role for someone curious about how enterprise communication evolves and energized by delivering scalable solutions in a fast-moving environment. This is a hybrid position that requires on-site presence 2 days per week at our San Jose, CA office. Remote arrangements are also available for this role.

**About the Team**

The Zoom Phone team leads the development and evolution of Zoom's cloud-based phone services, defining product strategies that deliver scalable, reliable, and user-friendly communication solutions for enterprise customers worldwide. We partner closely with engineering, design, sales, and support to ensure Zoom Phone integrates seamlessly within Zoom Workplace, continuously raising the bar for the telephony experience.

**Responsibilities**
- Define and prioritize product features and enhancements for Zoom Phone, grounded in customer insight and strategic business value.
- Translate customer needs into well-defined user stories, business requirements, and functional specifications for core telephony systems and services.
- Own and maintain the product backlog, partnering with engineering and design to drive iterative, agile delivery.
- Partner with product managers, product marketing, and cross-functional stakeholders to define and evolve the core telephony platform roadmap.
- Analyze product KPIs and usage data to surface trends, validate requirements, and drive improvements that increase customer satisfaction.
- Evaluate competitive and adjacent products, track industry trends, and engage directly with customers and stakeholders to sharpen product direction.

**What We're Looking For**
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, or a related technical field, or equivalent experience, with 5+ years of product management experience focused on telephony solutions.
- Excellent communication skills, with a demonstrated ability to influence across teams, simplify complex ideas, and drive clear decisions in ambiguous environments.
- Proven success in telephony-centric businesses, including enterprise SaaS, communication, and collaboration products, with the ability to deliver innovative solutions in dynamic environments.
- Strong understanding of cloud and on-premise enterprise unified communications platforms, with a track record of translating technical complexity into compelling product experiences.
- Familiarity with telephony protocols including SIP signaling, RTP, WebRTC, and H.323, as well as hardware such as PBX systems and IP phones.
- Working knowledge of network quality factors, including QoS, latency, jitter, and packet loss, and their impact on call quality.
- Experience integrating telephony services via RESTful APIs and managing product delivery using Agile and scrum methodologies.
