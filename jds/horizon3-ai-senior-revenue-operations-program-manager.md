## Full JD text

Horizon3 is a fast-growing, remote cybersecurity company dedicated to the mission of enabling organizations to proactively find and fix and verify exploitable attack vectors before criminals exploit them. Our flagship product, the NodeZero platform, delivers production-safe autonomous pentests and other key assessment operations that scale across the largest internal, external, cloud, and hybrid cloud environments.

**About the role:** Horizon3.ai is building a Strategy & Programs function within Revenue Operations to help the organization successfully execute its highest-priority cross-functional initiatives as we continue to scale. We are looking for a RevOps Program Manager to lead complex initiatives that span Revenue Operations and the broader business. This role will partner closely with teams including Sales Operations, Systems Operations, Deal Desk, Customer Operations, Partner Operations, Finance, Product, Engineering, Marketing, and other cross-functional stakeholders. This is not a traditional IT Project Manager role, nor is it a project coordinator position. The ideal candidate combines strong program management discipline with business acumen, sound judgment, and the ability to create structure in ambiguous environments.

**What you'll do:**
- Lead Strategic Initiatives: lead moderately complex, cross-functional initiatives from initial scoping through implementation and closeout with little to no day-to-day direction; translate strategic priorities into clear project plans, milestones, workstreams, dependencies, and success measures; establish project governance, operating cadences, decision-making structures; manage multiple concurrent initiatives.
- Drive Cross-Functional Execution: coordinate work across RevOps functions and the broader organization; facilitate working sessions and decision-making forums; identify dependencies, risks, and blockers; build alignment across teams.
- Provide Project Governance & Visibility: maintain project plans, milestones, risks, dependencies; develop executive-ready project updates; support portfolio-level visibility; help establish repeatable PM standards.
- Support Business & Operational Transformation projects such as sales process/operating model changes, territory/segmentation initiatives, pricing and packaging rollouts, partner/channel program launches, customer lifecycle improvements, Quote-to-Cash transformation, revenue technology implementations, CRM/GTM systems initiatives, and process redesign.

**What we're looking for:**
- 5+ years of project, program, business operations, Revenue Operations, or related experience leading cross-functional initiatives.
- Demonstrated ability to independently manage moderately complex projects from scoping through implementation.
- Experience working in a high-growth SaaS, technology, or similarly fast-paced environment.
- Strong project planning, organization, prioritization, and risk-management skills.
- Strong business acumen and the ability to quickly understand unfamiliar business processes and operating models.
- Demonstrated ability to lead through influence rather than formal authority.
- Experience coordinating stakeholders across multiple functions and levels of an organization.
- Strong written and verbal communication skills.
- Comfortable operating in ambiguity.

**Experience that could make you successful:** Revenue Operations, Sales Operations, Customer Operations, Partner Operations, Deal Desk, or Business Operations; cross-functional GTM initiatives or commercial transformation; business systems or revenue technology implementations; CRM, CPQ, CLM, ERP, or other enterprise technology programs; product or engineering partnership; process improvement and operating model design; change management; data/analytics/data governance initiatives.
