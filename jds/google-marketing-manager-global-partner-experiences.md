About The Job

As a Marketing Manager, Global Partner Experiences on the Google Cloud Global Partner Marketing team, you will play a central role in showcasing the strength, innovation, and joint value of the Google Cloud partner ecosystem to partners, customers, and industry influencers worldwide. In this highly visible role, you will lead the end-to-end content strategy and executive keynote narratives for marquee global events—including Google Cloud Next—while driving the overarching partner communications calendar, social media campaigns, and strategic newsletters that scale global partner engagement.

Know the user. Know the magic. Connect the two. At its core, marketing at Google starts with technology and ends with the user, bringing both together in unconventional ways. Our job is to demonstrate how Google's products solve the world's problems--from the everyday to the epic, from the mundane to the monumental. And we approach marketing in a way that only Google can--changing the game, redefining the medium, making the user the priority, and ultimately, letting the technology speak for itself.
