## Full Job Description

### About Poshmark
Poshmark is the leading fashion marketplace where style comes alive through discovery, self-expression, and human connection. Powered by a vibrant community of 165 million members.

### Role Overview
This role sits at the intersection of product, data, and customer communication. You will own Poshmark's marketing technology stack, including the customer engagement platform (CEP), customer data platform (CDP), email, push notifications, promotional systems, and CRM strategy for merchandising and seller teams.

The work is both strategic and deeply technical: evaluating CDP architecture decisions, running send time optimization experiments, defining segmentation logic for seller re-engagement campaigns, and partnering with engineering to improve the transactional email template system. You own the infrastructure and the outcomes, not just the roadmap.

The growth pod is lean; you'll work directly with the Head of Growth Product and partner daily with DS/ML, engineering, marketing ops, and merchandising. Poshmark PMs on this team use AI tools actively to prototype, build, and ship.

### What You Will Own

**Customer Engagement Platform (CEP) and Channel Orchestration**
- Define orchestration logic across channels: which message, to which user, through which channel, at what moment
- Build and refine audience segmentation strategy across buyer lifecycle stages and seller engagement tiers
- Lead personalization initiatives including an ML-powered delivery timing program
- Establish standards for how the team builds, tests, and iterates on campaigns

**Customer Data Platform (CDP) and Data Infrastructure**
- Own implementation strategy and roadmap connecting Redshift data warehouse to downstream engagement tools
- Define the data model for customer attributes, behavioral signals, lifecycle events
- Partner with data engineering on audience definitions
- Own the roadmap for expanding CDP usage over time

**Targeted Lifecycle Messaging**
- Set standards for email/push quality and deliverability health
- Run structured experiments to improve open rates, click rates, conversion
- Own the product layer for promotional campaigns: discount mechanics, offer construction, audience targeting, measurement
- Coordinate promotional events with broader lifecycle programs

**CRM for Merchandising**
- Build product workflows that let the merchandising team trigger targeted outreach at scale without engineering
- Partner with merchandising to translate shopper/seller communication needs into durable platform capabilities

### What We Are Looking For
- 4+ years in a product management or growth engineering role with direct ownership of a martech stack
- Hands-on experience with a customer engagement platform (MoEngage, Braze, Iterable, Salesforce Marketing Cloud, or similar) at a product/implementation level
- Working knowledge of how CDPs function: data ingestion, identity resolution, audience construction, downstream activation
- SQL fluency, understanding of event schemas, ability to work directly with data engineers
- Comfortable reading API documentation, reviewing data schemas
- Track record of running structured experiments with proper methodology
- Willing/able to use AI-assisted development tools (Codex, Claude Code, or equivalents) to build prototypes, draft specs, accelerate output

### Nice to Have
Experience at a consumer marketplace (Etsy, eBay, Whatnot, Depop); email deliverability background; AI-driven personalization; composable CDP integration (Hightouch, Segment).
