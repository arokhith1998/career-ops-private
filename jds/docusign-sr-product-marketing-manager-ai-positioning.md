Company Overview

Docusign brings agreements to life. Over 1.5 million customers and more than a billion people in over 180 countries use Docusign solutions to accelerate the process of doing business and simplify people’s lives. With intelligent agreement management, Docusign unleashes business-critical data that is trapped inside of documents. Until now, these were disconnected from business systems of record, costing businesses time, money, and opportunity. Using Docusign’s Intelligent Agreement Management platform, companies can create, commit, and manage agreements with solutions created by the #1 company in e-signature and contract lifecycle management (CLM).

What you'll do

As the Sr. Product Marketing Manager for AI Positioning, you will own the strategic positioning, differentiation story, and go-to-market narrative for Docusign's AI engine— Iris. This is a highly visible, cross-functional role at the intersection of AI strategy, product storytelling, and enterprise go-to-market execution. You will serve as the company's primary AI narrative authority—maintaining the Iris core messaging across all customer-facing and field-facing content, ensuring every launch, campaign, and enablement asset tells a consistent, compelling, and differentiated story. You will partner closely with Product, Sales, Legal, and Field Marketing to build the messaging infrastructure that helps Docusign win in an increasingly competitive AI market.

This position is an individual contributor role reporting to the Director, Product Marketing.

Responsibility

- Own, regularly update, and maintain the holistic positioning, differentiation story, and core narrative bill of materials for Docusign Iris — serving as the single source of truth across all teams and programs
- Review all AI-related product launches for narrative consistency — flagging conflicts, resolving messaging gaps, and determining updates needed to the core Iris story to ensure a coherent platform message at every release
- Own AI trust positioning and customer communication in close collaboration with Legal — clearly articulating Docusign's data boundaries, consent controls, and governance framework in a way that builds enterprise confidence
- Clearly articulate why Docusign's AI wins — not just what it does, but why it outperforms alternatives and how it complements other AI tools in the enterprise ecosystem
- Enable field and cross-functional teams on the AI and agentic market landscape, Iris capabilities, and IAM platform differentiation — combining individual features into a larger, compelling platform story that drives enterprise deals and expands adoption
- Serve as a core contributor to high-impact programs — including analyst relations, Momentum, and Momentum OnTour — packaging the Iris and IAM narrative for industry analysts, executive briefings, and customer conferences to ensure a consistent platform message across every high-visibility moment
