# Senior Product Manager: Extensible Compute

**Company:** Snowflake Inc.
**URL:** https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/snowflake/ba140960-361c-4357-8a8e-e4825f0d200b
**Location(s):** US-CA-Menlo Park; US-WA-Bellevue (hybrid)

**Compensation (stated):** $200K-$287.5K

## Role Summary

Senior Product Manager for the Extensible Compute team, which builds Snowpark Container Services (SPCS) -- the compute platform running customer container workloads and Snowflake's own first-party products (Openflow, Notebooks, Streamlit, AI agents).

## You Will

- Own the SPCS product roadmap: compute instance portfolio across AWS/Azure/GCP (CPU, ARM, GPU), storage/filesystem capabilities.
- Turn first-party product-partner requests into platform capabilities; decide what becomes a standard feature vs. a one-off.
- Take features from early preview to general availability; hold the launch bar on security/reliability/unit economics.
- Own platform health customers feel (startup time, isolation, storage reliability, unit economics); partner with Finance on pricing.
- Be the product point of contact for infrastructure partners (AWS, Azure, GCP, Nvidia) and account teams qualifying strategic customers onto SPCS.

## You Have

- 5+ years of product management experience, including several years on a platform, infrastructure, or developer-facing product.
- Hands-on depth in cloud infrastructure, container/serverless platforms, developer runtimes, or distributed systems.
- Track record taking a platform capability from 0-to-1 or early adoption to scale.
- Clear writing/communication with engineering leads, partner PMs, finance, account teams.
- Bachelor's degree in a technical field, or equivalent experience.
- Bonus: container platform/Kubernetes/serverless compute experience; GPU/accelerated compute qualifying/launching; data/AI/developer workload experience on a shared platform; unit-economics familiarity.
