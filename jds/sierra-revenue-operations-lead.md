## Full job description (verbatim)

About usSierra is the leading platform for customer-facing AI agents, working with many of the world's biggest brands — including The GAP, Rocket Mortgage, SoFi, Sutter Health, and SoftBank — to transform how they serve customers and grow their businesses. We are primarily an in-person company based in San Francisco, with growing offices across North America, Europe, and Asia.

We are guided by a set of values that are at the core of our actions and define our culture: Trust, Customer Obsession, Craftsmanship, Intensity, and a commitment to balancing Family along the way. These values are the foundation of our work, and we are committed to upholding them in everything we do.

Sierra was co-founded by Bret Taylor and Clay Bavor who first met while working together at Google over 20 years ago. Bret currently serves as Chairman of the Board of Open AI. Previously, he was co-CEO of Salesforce and CTO of Facebook. Clay spent 18 years at Google, where he most recently led Google Labs.

You'll own how Sierra's revenue organization forecasts, assigns and credits deals, and makes decisions, and you'll set the global rules our sales teams follow as we grow. Our deals are long, involve many stakeholders, include an Agent Development implementation, and use outcomes-based pricing, so standard SaaS playbooks don't fit. You'll write the ones that do. This is a senior individual contributor role with a lot of room to build. Success looks like a forecast leaders trust, disputes resolved in days rather than weeks, and policies teams actually follow.

What you'll do:- Own the global forecast process. Set the cadence, the forecast categories and method, and how numbers roll up from rep to executive. Define pipeline hygiene standards and hold teams to them. Prepare leaders for each forecast call and follow through on what it decides.

- Define and maintain global rules of engagement: account ownership, crediting, territory conflicts, and handoffs between Sales and other GTM teams. Settle disputes fairly and quickly.

- Write and govern sales policies, including opportunity rules, stage definitions, booking criteria, and exception processes. Keep them clear, documented, and followed.

- Lead cross-functional GTM initiatives from plan to rollout, including the rules, policies, and system changes that new sales motions and launches need. Keep plans, owners, deadlines, and decisions aligned across Sales, Pricing, Deal Ops, Finance, Legal, Systems, Planning & Analytics, and Comp.

- Find where the revenue process breaks or slows down, size the problem, and fix it with the right mix of process, systems, and enablement.

- Build how the RevOps team itself works: intake, prioritization, documentation, and change management, so the team grows without becoming a bottleneck.

- Use AI tools to automate forecast prep, answer policy questions, and handle routine requests, so your time goes to the decisions that need judgment.

What you'll bring:- 10+ years in Revenue Operations, Sales Operations, Sales Strategy, or GTM program management at high-growth B2B software companies.

- Hands-on ownership of a forecast process, rules of engagement, or sales policy for an enterprise sales organization.

- Experience with complex enterprise sales: long cycles, many stakeholders, and a services or implementation component.

- A record of leading complex cross-functional programs from start to finish, with clear plans, owners, and results.

- Strong operating judgment: you know when a problem needs a policy, a process, a system, or just a decision.

- You create structure where there is none and stay effective when priorities shift quickly.

- The ability to influence senior leaders and earn their trust in your calls.

- Clear, plain writing. People can follow your policies and plans without asking you what they mean.

- Fluency in Salesforce and comfort using data to diagnose problems and measure impact.

- Hands-on, daily use of AI tools in your own work.

Even better...- Experience with outcomes-based, usage-based, or consumption pricing

- Experience building a RevOps function at a company scaling to $1B+ ARR

- Formal program or change-management experience

Our values- Trust: We do the right thing, even when no one is looking, and even when it’s hard. When we screw up, we take accountability, fully and completely. We act in such a way that if all of our actions were completely visible to our customers, we would be proud.

- Customer Obsession: Our entire business is designed to ensure every agent built on Sierra succeeds, and that we are accountable to our customers and their success. We do whatever it takes to make our customers successful, and we go the extra mile in how we show up as a partner with unreasonable hospitality.

- Craftsmanship: A great company is the aggregation of thousands of things done well. As they say, “how you do anything is how you do everything”. Most important is craftsmanship in building agents that are so great that people thank them at the end of a call. That’s the standard.

- Intensity: We operate with urgency, and we want to win. We are never complacent. We fight to win every deal, big or small, using the full force of the company. And when we fail, we talk about it openly and without blame so we succeed the next time.

- Family: We know that balance and intensity are compatible, and we model it in our actions and processes. We work hard, and turn on the afterburners when needed. But we pick up our parents at the airport when they’re visiting from out of town. We go to our kids’ events. And we show up for one another, in moments of celebration and hardship.
