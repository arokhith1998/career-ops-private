## Full JD Text

We're looking for a Product Marketing Manager to drive successful market entry and accelerate growth across global markets for our wearables portfolio. You will own the end-to-end go-to-market strategy for new market launches and in-market growth, from identifying the right markets to enter, to developing launch playbooks, to scaling what works. You'll serve as the central strategic lead for global expansion, partnering across product, regional marketing, sales, channel, ops, legal, and policy to turn ambitious goals into measurable results. The ideal candidate brings a combination of strategic vision, data-driven decision-making, proven cross-functional leadership, and hands-on product launch experience. You thrive in fast-moving, ambiguous environments — comfortable making strategic calls without perfect data, iterating quickly, and influencing without authority.
