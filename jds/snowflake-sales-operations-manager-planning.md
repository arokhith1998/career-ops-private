## Role summary (verbatim excerpt)
"Snowflake's Revenue Operations team is hiring a Sales Operations Manager to
own planning operations for our Partner and Product Specialist
organizations. In this role, you'll run complex planning workstreams end to
end, including territory design, quota setting, capacity planning, and
in-year quota management."

## Responsibilities (verbatim/paraphrased)
- Own end-to-end delivery of territories, quotas, and compensation plans for
  Partner and Specialist organizations across all geographies; drive
  alignment with Finance, Commissions, HR, and Field Ops
- Define and govern global policies for account moves, quota changes, and
  in-year adjustments (methodology, guardrails, documentation, change
  management)
- Lead quota and attainment reviews across global Partner and Specialist
  teams; find root causes, surface tradeoffs, drive resolution with senior
  stakeholders
- Anticipate downstream effects on field, Commissions, reporting, systems;
  design scalable solutions
- Translate business requirements into process, data, and tooling changes in
  Pigment, Salesforce, and Snowflake
- Design workflows that produce decision-ready outputs with validation built
  in
- Encode institutional knowledge (quota methodology, territory logic,
  planning rules) in versioned skills/documentation
- Build and maintain production AI skills encoding planning methodology and
  recurring analysis
- Lead small cross-functional project teams, present recommendations to
  senior leadership

## Requirements ("ON DAY ONE WE WILL EXPECT YOU TO HAVE," verbatim/paraphrased)
- 6 to 8+ years of experience in GTM strategy, planning, or sales operations,
  ideally at a high-growth enterprise technology company
- Track record of owning complex planning workstreams end to end (territory
  design, quota setting, capacity modeling, or compensation operations)
- Experience solving ambiguous, cross-functional problems, especially during
  periods of rapid growth
- Strong influence skills with senior stakeholders
- Hands-on experience building automated or AI-assisted workflows with
  measurable results (hours saved, errors caught, cycles shortened)
- Deep experience with Salesforce and sales planning platforms such as
  Pigment or Anaplan
- A bachelor's degree in business, statistics, economics, or another
  quantitative field; MBA a plus
