**About The Role**

ID.me is hiring a Manager of Sales Operations & Deal Desk to be the operational engine behind our sales process - from pipeline hygiene and CRM administration to deal structuring and approvals. This role sits within Revenue Operations and is the day-to-day partner Sales reps and managers turn to for anything that touches process, systems, or deal terms. Given ID.me's consumption-based pricing model, deals often involve non-standard structures - usage tiers, ramp schedules, minimum commitments - that require careful review and consistent governance. This role is a strong fit for someone who's detail-oriented, systems-fluent, and energized by being the person who keeps sales moving efficiently without sacrificing deal quality or compliance.

Level (Senior Analyst vs. Manager) will be determined based on the candidate's experience.

**What You'll Do**

Deal Desk:
- Review and approve deal structures, pricing exceptions, discounting, and non-standard contract terms in line with company policy
- Partner with Sales, Legal, and Finance to structure deals for ID.me's consumption-based pricing model - usage tiers, minimum commitments, ramp schedules, and renewal terms
- Maintain deal desk approval workflows and playbooks, ensuring consistent turnaround times and clear escalation paths
- Track and report on deal desk cycle time, exception volume, and win/loss patterns tied to deal structure

Sales Operations:
- Own CRM data integrity and administration (Salesforce): pipeline hygiene, opportunity stages, fields, and reporting structure
- Support territory design, account assignment, and quota allocation in partnership with Sales leadership
- Build and maintain sales dashboards and reporting for pipeline coverage, forecast accuracy, and rep productivity
- Identify and implement process improvements across the quote-to-cash lifecycle, reducing friction for reps and improving data quality for Finance

Cross-Functional Partnership:
- Act as the go-to resource for sales reps and managers on process, systems, and deal questions
- Partner with Finance/FP&A to ensure deal terms and CRM data flow cleanly into revenue reporting and forecasting
- Support onboarding and training for new sales hires on CRM usage, deal desk process, and sales tools
- Assist with ad hoc analysis and projects as Revenue Operations scales

**What You'll Bring**
- 3+ years (Senior Analyst) or 5+ years (Manager) of experience in sales operations, deal desk, or a related analytical function, ideally in B2B SaaS
- Strong hands-on experience with Salesforce (or comparable CRM) - administration, reporting, and workflow configuration
- Strong Excel/spreadsheet skills; comfort building clean, reliable analysis under time pressure
- High attention to detail and a process-oriented mindset - comfortable being the person who enforces consistency
- Clear communicator who can explain policy and process to sales reps without being a bottleneck
- Comfort operating in a fast-paced, high-growth environment where process is still being built

**Nice to Have**
- Experience with deal structuring for consumption/usage-based pricing models - Experience with CPQ tools or quote-to-cash process design
- Exposure to sales compensation, territory planning, or forecasting
