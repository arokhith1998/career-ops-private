Chief of Staff – Technical Generalist (Early-Stage SaaS)
The Company
A high-growth technology startup based in San Francisco is seeking a highly organized and resourceful Chief of Staff to join the Operations department. We are a technical, product-driven company scaling our engineering and go-to-market teams. This is a high-ownership, high-pace operator role designed for an individual who thrives on variety and is eager to understand the inner workings of a scaling company.
The Role
The Chief of Staff will act as a force multiplier for leadership, executing high-priority initiatives across recruiting, Go-To-Market (GTM) strategy, product, and general operations. This role is ideal for a "technical generalist" who enjoys automating manual processes and taking ambiguous problems from concept to completion. You'll be expected to operate at the intersection of business and engineering, leveraging automation and AI to drive efficiency.
Key Responsibilities
- Process Automation: Utilize tools such as n8n, GPT, and various AI integrations to automate internal workflows and GTM infrastructure.
- Operational Management: Oversee daily office logistics, vendor management, and the onboarding of new team members. Provide "all-hands-on-deck" assistance across the business.
- Recruiting & Talent: Lead sourcing efforts and manage 3rd-party recruiter relationships to scale the engineering and go-to-market teams.
- Financial Modeling: Build and maintain lightweight financial models, including headcount modeling, revenue projections, and budget forecasting.
- Cross-Functional Project Management: Partner with leadership to drive experiments and ensure complex projects reach the finish line.
- Internal Communications: Structure product feedback from GTM initiatives for the R&D team and facilitate seamless communication across Slack and async documentation. Qualifications
- 1+ years of professional experience in a fast-paced startup environment.
- Technical proficiency: Comfortable with light Python or scripting (leveraging AI tools where necessary) and advanced spreadsheet modeling.
- Exceptional written and verbal communication skills across various platforms and technical fluencies.
- Highly resourceful with the ability to figure out complex tasks independently while knowing when to seek strategic guidance.
- A technical or quantitative background (STEM, Economics, or similar) is highly preferred.
- Ability to work on-site in the San Francisco office 5 days per week. Compensation & Benefits
- Base salary range: $120,000 – $200,000
- Significant equity package commensurate with a high-impact, early-stage role
- Direct exposure to startup scaling and real-time feedback
