## Full description (verbatim via Ashby API, HTML stripped)
SpaceX was founded under the belief that a future where humanity is out exploring the stars is fundamentally more exciting than one where we are not. Today, SpaceX is actively developing the technologies to make this possible, with the ultimate goal of making life multiplanetary and expanding consciousness to the stars.

SpaceXSI is building Super Intelligence to help humanity understand the universe. Our teams are building the full Super Intelligence stack: from our Colossus supercomputers, to Grok models, to our suite of applications, including Grok Bot, Grok Build, Grok Imagine, Grok Voice, and X. We're creating a powerful flywheel where more compute trains better models, better models power more useful products, and what we learn from people using those products shapes what we build next.

### PRODUCT MARKETING MANAGER
We're looking for a Product Marketing Manager who can translate deeply technical products into clear, compelling stories that resonate with developers. This is a highly cross-functional, high-impact role. You'll work closely with product, engineering, and GTM to help define what we build and how we bring it to market. You won't just launch features — you'll influence product direction, define positioning, and help developers understand why SpaceXSI matters.

### RESPONSIBILITIES
- Product Launches: Own end-to-end go-to-market strategy for new features and major releases; write launch materials (blog posts, landing pages, demos, videos); partner with product and engineering to ensure strong product-market fit before launch
- Positioning & Messaging: Develop clear, differentiated positioning for SpaceXSI and its features; turn complex AI + developer workflows into simple, compelling narratives
- Developer-Focused Content: Partner with Developer Relations to create high-signal content for technical audiences
- Go-To-Market Enablement: Translate product capabilities into messaging sales and GTM teams can use effectively
- Growth & Distribution: Identify and test new channels to reach developers (Twitter/X, GitHub, YouTube, etc.); work with growth to improve activation, onboarding, and retention messaging
- Customer & Market Insight: Talk to users regularly; analyze competitors and the evolving AI/dev tooling landscape

### BASIC QUALIFICATIONS
- 3+ years in product marketing, product management, growth, consulting, banking or a similar role

### PREFERRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE
Ideally in developer tools or SaaS; strong technical intuition without needing to be an engineer; strong product instincts; exceptional communication skills; high ownership; comfort in a fast-moving, ambiguous environment.

### ADDITIONAL REQUIREMENTS
Onsite role: must be able to work in person at the San Francisco or New York office.
