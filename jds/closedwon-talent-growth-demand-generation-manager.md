About Us

We're ClosedWon Talent, a sales and go-to-market recruiting agency that specializes in working with fast-growing startups. If you're exploring your next move, we might have just the opportunity for you, including this one.

About the company

Captura.io is a photography software platform serving 500+ studios across schools, sports, and labs. Their suite covers studio workflow, AI photo enhancement, print lab production, and yearbook creation, helping photography businesses run end-to-end from shoot to final sale.

Studio Ninja is Captura's CRM and studio-management platform for wedding and portrait photographers. It handles client management, workflows, contracts, invoicing, and scheduling for independent photographers and small studios across the US, UK, and Australia. The business has 11,000+ paid subscribers, will finish this year at roughly $4M in revenue, is growing about 20% a year, and is highly profitable.

About the role

Captura is hiring a fast-executing manager of growth & demand generation to drive qualified traffic, lead capture, and subscription growth for Studio Ninja. Reporting directly to the GM, you will combine strategic scope with hands-on execution to build and scale multi-channel growth initiatives across paid acquisition, organic/AI discovery, and promotional campaigns. This is a dynamic growth leadership role designed for a hands-on builder who thrives on rapid testing, launching campaigns quickly, maintaining high output, and efficiently leveraging external agency resources to scale results.

Key responsibilities

- Paid Performance Marketing & Agency Management: Build, monitor, and scale direct response campaigns on Meta and Google Ads. Manage external agency partners, freelancers, or contractors where needed to extend creative and media capabilities while ensuring strict CPA and payback targets.
- Organic & AIO Execution: Plan and structure website content to address both traditional search engine optimization (SEO) and artificial intelligence optimization (AIO) touchpoints across Google AI Overviews, Claude, ChatGPT, and emerging answer engines.
- Promotional Strategy & Sales Campaigns: Drive seasonal promotions, special offer campaigns, and trial-incentive launches. Lead full-funnel tracking, performance evaluation, and conversion rate optimization (CRO) for all promotional initiatives.
- Lifecycle & Automation Touchpoints: Oversee automated chat, lead routing, and customer onboarding sequences in Intercom to maximize lead-to-trial and trial-to-paid conversion rates.
- Tracking & Analytics Infrastructure: Maintain accurate pixel tracking across all touchpoints. Build and deliver weekly executive reporting dashboards in GA4 and custom reports tracking traffic, MQLs, conversion velocity, CPA, and payback metrics.

Qualifications & Requirements

- Proven Growth Leadership: 4+ years of hands-on growth marketing experience with a track record of driving revenue growth in B2B SaaS or digital subscription products.
- Hands-on Paid Media Expertise: Mastery of Meta Ads Manager and Google Ads with experience managing budgets, running creative testing frameworks, and managing third-party media or creative agencies.
- Modern SEO & AIO Mastery: Practical experience executing entity mapping, structured data formatting, and content strategies designed specifically for Generative AI engines alongside traditional search engines.
- Tool Stack Proficiency: Deep familiarity with GA4, Google Search Console, and Intercom (chatbots/automations/in-app messaging).
- Speed, Agility & Ownership: Able to own results and to direct agency resources, run rapid weekly sprints, and adjust tactics based on real-time data feedback loops.

Nice-to-have

- Located on the East Coast, preferably near Boston, New York, or Raleigh
- Experience working at a startup, preferably serving an SMB audience
- Photography knowledge
