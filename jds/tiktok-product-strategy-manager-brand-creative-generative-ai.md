About the Team
TikTok's Global Monetization Product and Technology (GMPT) team builds the advertising products that help businesses of every size grow on TikTok. Within GMPT, the Product Strategy team is a thought partner to product and business leaders. We bring market intelligence, sharp problem framing and clear trade-offs to the decisions that shape our products.

About the Role
You will support the product leaders behind TikTok's brand, traffic and creative advertising products. The initial focus is creative and generative AI: how AI is changing the way ad creative is made, scaled and measured, and what that means for GMPT's creative solution. As priorities evolve, the scope may extend to other strategic topics across the portfolio. The role sits where one of the fastest-moving areas of AI meets one of the world's largest advertising platforms. It suits someone who enjoys defining the problem as much as solving it.

Responsibilities
- Build a sharp, fact-based view of the generative AI creative landscape (market size and structure, model and tool providers, startups and their business models, competitors' AI creative offerings) and translate it into clear implications for product, customer and go-to-market decisions.
- Frame and own a research agenda around the questions that matter most for the business, not only the ones already asked. Set milestones, share work in progress early, and iterate with product and business leaders.
- Partner with product teams on AI creative products: value proposition, business model, adoption barriers (e.g., brand safety, controllability, workflow fit) and go-to-market, with particular attention to North American advertisers and agencies.
- Look across the creative ecosystem, including advertisers, agencies, creators and AI tools, to identify structural opportunities, and turn cross-functional findings into proposals that product, sales and operations teams can act on.
- Combine primary research (advertiser and agency conversations, expert interviews, hands-on testing of AI tools) with data analysis to support key decisions.
- Flexibly take on other strategic priorities across brand, traffic and creative products as the business evolves.

Qualifications
Minimum Qualifications:
- Bachelor's degree or above, and several years of experience in strategy consulting, corporate or product strategy, business analytics, or a related field.
- Strong structured problem-solving: you can take an ambiguous question, define it, break it down and drive it to a decision-ready answer.
- Quantitative rigor, including market sizing, data analysis and precise metric definitions.
- Clear, conclusion-first communication with leaders, in writing and in person.

Preferred Qualifications:
- Experience in digital advertising, ad tech, marketing tech, creative production, or at an agency.
- Hands-on familiarity with generative AI (e.g., image and video generation, AI agents). You try new tools yourself and form your own view.
- Experience engaging advertisers or agencies directly, or running customer and expert research.
- A mix of consulting and in-house strategy experience.
- A track record of owning an open-ended, long-horizon topic and building a point of view that influenced real decisions.
