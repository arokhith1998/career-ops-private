About the Role

We're looking for a Lifecycle Marketing Manager to drive prospect engagement and conversion across Fivetran's global Commercial business. As part of the Commercial demand generation team, this role combines a strong email marketing background with a lifecycle mindset to nurture new business prospects through the buying journey.

You will own email strategy and execution, building personalized, automated journeys that deliver the right message at the right time. Through compelling copy, thoughtful segmentation, and ongoing experimentation, you will turn engagement into measurable progress toward MQLs and pipeline. You'll collaborate with demand generation, content, marketing operations, and sales to connect email nurture with broader campaigns and prospect outreach.

The ideal candidate brings experience in B2B demand generation, hands-on email campaign execution, and a data-driven approach to improving conversion and driving business impact.

This is a full-time hybrid position based in our Oakland, California office.

Technologies You'll Use

Marketo, Google Analytics, SFDC, Chat GPT, Claude, and Looker.

What You'll Do

- Own email marketing strategy and execution for Commercial new business prospects, driving engagement, lead-to-MQL conversion, and pipeline growth.
- Build and optimize automated nurture journeys that guide prospects through the buying process, using behavior, interests, and funnel stage to deliver relevant messaging.
- Develop audience segmentation and personalization strategies in partnership with demand generation, sales, and marketing operations.
- Craft compelling email copy and collaborate with product marketing, content, and brand teams to drive engagement, demo requests, and conversions.
- Connect email nurture with digital campaigns, paid media, web experiences, and BDR outreach to create coordinated prospect journeys.
- Manage the email calendar, contact frequency, and campaign production process, including quality assurance, deployment, and ongoing optimization in partnership with marketing operations.
- Plan and execute experiments across messaging, content, design, timing, and frequency to improve email and nurture performance.
- Define KPIs and report on engagement, lead-to-MQL conversion, and pipeline contribution, translating results into actionable recommendations.
- Partner with Marketing Operations to build campaigns in Marketo, maintain database health and deliverability, and ensure compliance with applicable privacy requirements.

Skills We're Looking For

- 4+ years of relevant B2B marketing experience, with direct ownership of email marketing and lead nurture programs.
- Hands-on experience with Marketo, including building automated journeys, audience segmentation, personalization, and campaign reporting.
- A proven ability to develop and optimize nurture campaigns that drive prospect engagement
- Strong email copywriting skills and an understanding of email design, deliverability, and quality assurance best practices.
- A lifecycle mindset, with the ability to build relevant prospect experiences based on buyer needs, behavior, and funnel stage.
- A data-driven approach to defining KPIs, designing and analyzing experiments, and translating results into actionable insights.
- Strong collaboration and communication skills
- A strong sense of ownership, critical thinking, and problem-solving skills, with the ability to work independently and manage multiple priorities.
