About Our Client:
This organization operates in the AI-empowered product sector, focusing on unifying security and operational data to address the complexities of modern cybersecurity and cloud operations. The company's services support customers worldwide in enhancing security, reliability, and gaining insights into their digital environments.

About the Opportunity:
The DEAL DESK ANALYST role supports the EMEA and East Coast sales teams by providing expert guidance on deal structuring, pricing, quote creation, negotiation, and contract review. This position serves as a strategic advisor to sales, collaborating across multiple departments to facilitate efficient deal closure and optimize deal outcomes. The analyst works closely with senior leadership to ensure alignment and successful execution of commercial agreements.

Responsibilities:
- Serve as the primary contact and advisor to sales on pricing, deal economics, and structure
- Collaborate with finance, legal, sales, and operations on complex transactions, offering insights and recommendations
- Manage daily deal processes, including quoting and commercial terms approval
- Deliver regular training to sales on deal structure and pricing topics
- Participate in projects to enhance systems, tools, and processes related to deal management
- Define and monitor key metrics to evaluate deal performance and identify improvement opportunities

Requirements:
- Bachelor’s degree in finance, economics, accounting, or related field preferred
- 3-5 years of experience in pricing, finance, deal desk, or analytics
- Proficiency with Excel, Google Sheets, Salesforce, and Salesforce CPQ
- Experience working cross-functionally with sales, finance, and legal teams
- Knowledge of SaaS subscription models and revenue recognition
- Ability to work effectively in a fast-paced environment with occasional extended hours
- Strong interpersonal, verbal, and written communication skills
