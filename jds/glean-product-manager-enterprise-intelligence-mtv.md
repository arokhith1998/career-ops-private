## Description
Glean seeks a strategic, execution-oriented PM to define and build the Enterprise Intelligence product area. The role bridges AI, enterprise workflows, analytics, and product strategy. The PM will transform Glean's enterprise context layer into proactive, high-value intelligence products for leaders and teams, working closely with cross-functional partners to identify product opportunities and translate concepts into concrete experiences.

- Define product strategy and roadmap for Enterprise Intelligence, translating company vision into specific bets and execution plans
- Spend significant time with customers and prospects understanding high-value use cases
- Build products turning enterprise context into actionable workflows, dashboards, and proactive experiences
- Partner with engineering, design, and data teams to scope products with clear user flows
- Drive customer-focused prioritization balancing long-term opportunities with urgent needs
- Collaborate with go-to-market and customer-facing teams
- Establish product foundations for a new category
- Maintain high standards for product craft with detailed workflows and UI specifications
