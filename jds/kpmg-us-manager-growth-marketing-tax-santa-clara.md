KPMG is currently seeking a Manager, Growth Marketing Tax to join our Clients and Markets organization.

Responsibilities:
- Help turn Tax growth priorities into a clear, buyer-focused marketing plan and design repeatable programs that can scale across Tax services
- Partner with Tax growth strategists to focus on the issues Tax buyers care about most and coordinate with fellow growth marketers so Tax programs fit the firm-wide marketing plan
- Write clear campaign briefs with goals, target audiences, and buyer journeys and lead campaigns from kickoff to completion with content, digital, and events teams; manage the Tax marketing calendar to avoid overlapping outreach to the same buyers; keep campaigns on time, on budget, and on quality standards
- Manage and track the campaign budget with strong fiscal discipline; monitor results against sales-aligned KPIs, such as conversion rates, influenced pipeline, and closed opportunities, and optimize as needed
- Report on campaign ROI and business impact to Tax leaders and marketing peers
- Share best practices and campaign templates with team members; support a collaborative culture focused on continuous learning and consistent, high-quality execution
- Act with integrity, professionalism, and personal responsibility to uphold KPMG's respectful and courteous work environment

Qualifications:
- Minimum five years of recent B2B marketing, demand generation, or campaign experience, ideally in professional services
- Bachelor's degree from an accredited college or university in marketing, business, finance, or a related field
- Experience writing campaign briefs and leading multi-channel campaigns with execution teams
- Ability to turn business priorities into a clear plan and identify programs that can be repeated at scale; experience managing project budgets and tracking performance
- Strong communication and collaboration skills, with the ability to build alignment across teams
- Knowledge of tax services and their buyers is a plus
- Travel may be required
