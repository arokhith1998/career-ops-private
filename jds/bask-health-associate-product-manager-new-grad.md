About Bask Health

Bask Health provides a full-service SaaS platform that enables doctors, entrepreneurs, and healthcare businesses to build, manage, and grow direct-to-consumer telehealth businesses.

We're an entrepreneurial, product-focused team that values ownership, speed, and exceptional execution. We work in small teams, tackle complex problems, and expect everyone to take an active role in building and improving our products.

About the Role

We're looking for an Associate Product Manager, New Grad to help build and improve the software that powers Bask Health's telehealth platform. This is an early-career opportunity for someone who has recently completed their education and wants to develop a career in technical product management. You'll work alongside experienced product managers, engineers, and designers to understand customer problems, evaluate solutions, and help bring new features to life.

At Bask, product managers are closely involved in how software is built. You'll need to understand technical concepts, communicate effectively with engineers, and take an active role in moving projects forward. You'll gain experience across product discovery, technical planning, development, testing, and launches while gradually taking ownership of smaller features and initiatives.

What You'll Do

Work with product managers, engineers, and designers to develop and improve features across Bask's telehealth platform. Help identify customer problems and translate them into clear product requirements. Gather feedback from merchants, patients, clinicians, and internal teams. Assist with defining product requirements, user stories, acceptance criteria, and technical documentation. Use coding knowledge to investigate technical issues and communicate solutions. Help develop prototypes and early feature demonstrations. Investigate bugs, reproduce issues, and document findings for engineering teams. Gradually take ownership of smaller features and product improvements as you develop your skills.

Requirements

Recent graduate or early-career candidate with a strong interest in technical product management. Foundational understanding of software development, web applications, and how technology products are built. Hands-on coding experience with JavaScript, TypeScript, Python, or another programming language through coursework, internships, personal projects, or professional work. Experience building or meaningfully contributing to at least one software application, website, prototype, or technical project. Ability to read and understand basic code, investigate technical issues, and communicate effectively with engineers. Strong analytical thinking and problem-solving skills. Strong organizational skills and attention to detail. Excellent written and verbal communication skills.

Nice to Have: Bachelor's degree or equivalent practical experience in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Business, Information Systems, or a related field. Familiarity with JavaScript, TypeScript, React, Next.js. Experience with APIs, databases, Git, or GitHub. Familiarity with product development methodologies, user stories, acceptance criteria. Interest in SaaS, e-commerce, consumer-facing applications, or telehealth/digital health.
