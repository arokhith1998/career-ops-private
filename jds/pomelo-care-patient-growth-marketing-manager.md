Pomelo Care is the leading virtual medical practice for women and children, providing care across pregnancy, postpartum, pediatrics, menopause, and perimenopause. About The Role Your North Star: Optimize our patient enrollment funnel to increase our enrollment rate. In this role, you will execute, and optimize end-to-end, omnichannel marketing strategies to drive patient enrollment and retention. You will be responsible for defining segmentation strategies, building and optimizing patient marketing journeys and campaigns using multivariate A/B tests, and measuring performance. This role is ideal for someone with B2B2C marketing experience who has previously served in roles supporting lifecycle marketing for high growth healthcare start-ups. This role reports to the Director of Lifecycle Marketing and is open to any candidates located in the continental US. What You’ll Do Develop and launch end-to-end, owned channel marketing plans and campaigns (e.g., e-mail, direct mail, SMS, paid media) to drive new patient enrollment, developing efficiencies and automations that allow for personalization and improve performance. Create co-marketing plans and playbooks to enable employer and commercial health plan partner marketing that reach our goals. Define, scope, and prioritize A/B tests to optimize enrollment funnel performance and improve retention. Develop reports and dashboards in collaboration with the Data team to track and measure patient funnel and campaign performance to identify opportunities to increase enrollment rate, decrease time to conversion, and improve retention. Manage new partner marketing implementation projects and timelines, ensuring on-time launch though managing the approvals process with partners. Collaborate closely with the Marketing, Customer Success, Outreach, and Clinical teams to ensure we are delivering on contract requirements, the partner’s needs, Pomelo’s ability to deliver care, and adjust our marketing campaigns and strategy accordingly. Manage relationships with external agencies and vendors as needed, ensuring timely and high-quality deliverables. Ensure compliance and best practices with CAN-SPAM, TCPA, HIPAA, and other applicable laws. Who You Are 4+ years of experience in B2B2C lifecycle, growth, or email marketing at a high growth startup Strong analytical skills, a sharp attention to detail, and experience running A/B tests to drive performance. Comfortable with ambiguity and thrive in fast paced environments. Creative, scrappy, and willing to wear many hats.
