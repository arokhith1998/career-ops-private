## Job description (full text via LinkedIn guest API)

About The Role

The role owns demand generation for a well-funded technology company, driving qualified pipeline through integrated campaigns across paid, organic, email, and social channels. This is a hands-on position that sits at the intersection of brand and revenue: every campaign is measured by its contribution to pipeline and customer acquisition, not just impressions.

You will work closely with sales leadership, content, product marketing, and design to translate positioning into programs that convert. The team moves fast, ships weekly, and treats the funnel as a system to be tested and optimized continuously.

Key Responsibilities
- Plan, launch, and optimize integrated demand-generation campaigns across paid search, paid social, email, and webinars
- Own the SEO/SEM strategy: manage keyword portfolios, landing page optimization, and agency relationships for Google Ads and Bing
- Build and maintain multi-touch nurture programs in HubSpot or Marketo, with lifecycle stages mapped to sales handoff criteria
- Run structured A/B tests on ad creative, messaging, and landing pages; document results and fold learnings into playbook
- Own campaign reporting in Looker or Tableau: CAC, MQL-to-SQL conversion, pipeline contribution, and channel ROI reviewed with leadership monthly
- Manage a six-figure media budget across channels, reallocating spend based on performance data
- Partner with BDR and AE teams on lead quality feedback loops and sales-enablement collateral

What We Are Looking For
- 4-7 years of demand-generation or digital marketing experience, ideally in B2B SaaS or high-growth tech
- Hands-on expertise with HubSpot or Marketo, Google Ads, LinkedIn Ads, and GA4
- Demonstrated track record of owning a pipeline target and hitting quarterly demand goals
- Strong analytical skills: comfortable building funnel models and presenting ROI data to executives
- Excellent copywriting instincts and the ability to brief designers and writers crisply
- Experience managing agency and vendor relationships against budget
- Bonus: experience with product-led-growth motions, intent data platforms (6sense, Bombora), or marketing operations
