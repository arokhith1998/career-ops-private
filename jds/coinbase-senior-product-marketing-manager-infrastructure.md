## Description
The Infrastructure Marketing team builds awareness and demand for Coinbase's stablecoin payments, trading, agentic finance, and custody products. As Senior Product Marketing Manager, lead integrated marketing initiatives spanning content, demand generation, events, and sales enablement; own go-to-market strategy for key product areas; drive programs generating qualified pipeline.

- Lead cross-functional marketing initiatives (content, demand gen, events, communications, sales enablement) for infrastructure products.
- Develop/execute go-to-market strategies for PSPs, money movers, cross-border companies, banks.
- Drive marketing for agentic finance (Coinbase for Agents, agentic payments): thought leadership, social campaigns, product announcements.
