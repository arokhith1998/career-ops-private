## Job Description (summary)
Drives account expansion and deal acceleration through integrated regional marketing programs for assigned AMER markets, with Sales as primary stakeholder.

- Contributes to/executes the regional field marketing plan (digital programs, webinars, trade shows, regional/pipeline events, annual roadshow).
- Builds programs engaging executives, business, and technical leaders at strategic accounts.
- Works with account teams on priorities, buying committees, expansion barriers; turns insights into programs.
- Supports expansion across enterprise accounts/personas (developers to C-level).
- Supports developer community initiatives; reports on developers enabled.
- Creates programs contributing to pipeline, expansion, and net ARR; tracks results.
- Manages budgets for assigned programs/markets. 30-50% travel to assigned markets.
