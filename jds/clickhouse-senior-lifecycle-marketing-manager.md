## Full JD text (verbatim, via LinkedIn jobs-guest API)

ClickHouse is hiring its first Senior Lifecycle Marketing Manager to own how
we engage, activate, and grow our user base across the entire customer
journey. Your mission is to turn signups, trial users, and prospects into
pipeline and self-serve MRR through behavior-driven lifecycle programs
across email and in-product channels. You will design the nurture engine
that powers both our PLG and sales-led motions. You will build from the
ground up, try new marketing strategies, and work with a passionate
community of ClickHouse users.

What You Will Be Doing

- Own lifecycle marketing strategy end to end, from signup onboarding
  through activation, conversion, expansion, and re-engagement, with
  accountability for trial-to-paid conversion, MQL volume, and influenced
  pipeline.
- Build behavior-triggered nurture programs that respond to product usage,
  content engagement, and intent signals, so the right message reaches the
  right user at the right stage.
- Design and optimize onboarding journeys for ClickHouse Cloud signups,
  partnering with Growth PMs and the product team to align email
  touchpoints with in-product experiences and drive activation.
- Run always-on nurture for the sales-led funnel, with segmented streams by
  persona, use case, and funnel stage that warm prospects for sales and
  accelerate open opportunities.
- Build and run a testing roadmap across subject lines, send cadence,
  content formats, and channel mix, using results to improve engagement and
  conversion.
- Report on lifecycle funnel metrics for PLG and SLG, analyze conversion at
  each stage, identify drop-off points, and implement improvements.
- Track industry trends, competitor activity, and emerging technologies to
  identify new lifecycle opportunities.

What You Bring

- Minimum of 6 years of experience in lifecycle, email, or growth marketing
  within the database or developer tooling space.
- Proven track record of building lifecycle programs that measurably
  improved activation, conversion, and pipeline at a PLG company.
- Analytical mindset, with the ability to interpret data, identify trends,
  and make data-driven decisions.
- Excellent written and verbal communication skills, with demonstrated
  ability to create engaging content for developers.
- Strong project management and organizational skills, with the ability to
  manage multiple programs simultaneously and meet deadlines.
- Preferred: experience at a company with an open-source product.

Perks

- Flexible work environment - ClickHouse is a globally distributed company
  and remote-friendly. We currently operate in over 25 countries.
- Healthcare - Employer contributions towards your healthcare.
- Equity in the company - Every new team member who joins our company
  receives stock options.
- Time off - Flexible time off in the US, generous entitlement in other
  countries.
- A USD$500 Home office setup if you're a remote employee.
- Global Gatherings - We believe in the power of in-person connection and
  offer opportunities to engage with colleagues at company-wide offsites.

Culture - We All Shape It
As part of a rapidly scaling start-up, you will be instrumental in shaping
our culture.
