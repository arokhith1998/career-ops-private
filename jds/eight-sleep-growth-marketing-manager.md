## Role summary
Reports to the Growth Marketing Lead within the Global Growth Marketing team. Focuses on optimizing and scaling digital advertising across all regions and managing multiple ad accounts.

## Responsibilities
- Lead performance channels (Search, Shopping, YouTube, TikTok); collaborate cross-functionally on Meta, affiliate, lifecycle, and influencer work.
- Build AI-driven growth and automation systems to reduce manual marketing work.
- Analyze campaign KPIs with the performance/data team to improve efficiency.
- Produce reports with synthesized insights and next steps.
- Recommend copy, creative, bidding strategies, and testing structures at a fast pace.
- Track industry trends, new ad products, and best practices.

## Qualifications
- 3+ years of experience with at least two of Google, YouTube, Meta, and TikTok Ads, including DTC campaigns with budgets above $1M/year.
- Blend of analytical and creative skills.
- Experience building marketing/analytics tools with AI (Claude, ChatGPT, n8n, Cursor).
- Proficiency with Excel/Sheets, Looker, SQL, Amplitude, or Search Console.
- Ability to work independently and build plans from scratch.
- Familiarity with attribution platforms (Northbeam, Triple Whale, Rockerbox).
- Experience designing or interpreting geo-lift, conversion-lift, or brand-lift studies.
