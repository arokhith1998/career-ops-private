About Bask Health

Bask Health provides a full-service SaaS platform that enables doctors, entrepreneurs, and healthcare businesses to build, manage, and grow direct-to-consumer telehealth businesses.

We're an entrepreneurial, product-focused team that values ownership, speed, and exceptional execution. We work in small teams, tackle complex problems, and expect everyone to take an active role in building and improving our products.

About the Role

We're looking for a Junior Product Manager, Intakes & Conversion to help improve how patients move through intake questionnaires, eligibility screening, and checkout across Bask Health's telehealth platform. You'll work closely with product managers, engineers, designers, and clinical operations teams to identify friction in the patient journey. This role combines technical product management with user experience and conversion optimization. You'll help investigate where patients abandon questionnaires, understand why certain steps create confusion, and support improvements that make the journey from initial intake to completed checkout more effective.

What You'll Do

Support product development across Bask's patient intake questionnaires, eligibility workflows, and checkout experiences. Help identify friction and abandonment points throughout the patient intake and purchase journey. Help improve questionnaire navigation, conditional questions, form validation, and the clarity of patient instructions. Support improvements to eligibility screening and treatment selection while maintaining required clinical safeguards. Assist with optimizing checkout steps, payment-related messaging, and the transition from intake to purchase. Support product experiments and A/B testing to evaluate changes to forms, messaging, and user flows. Help analyze funnel metrics, step completion rates, abandonment rates, and checkout conversion. Work with clinical and compliance stakeholders to ensure proposed improvements preserve necessary patient information and eligibility requirements.

Requirements

Hands-on experience contributing to software products through coursework, personal projects, internships, or professional work. Foundational understanding of software development, web applications, and how digital products are built. Practical coding experience with JavaScript, TypeScript, Python, or another programming language. Ability to read code, understand basic technical concepts, and communicate effectively with engineers. Strong analytical thinking and problem-solving skills. Ability to identify user friction, investigate problems, and recommend practical improvements. Strong attention to detail, particularly when testing multi-step forms and conditional workflows. Familiarity with basic data analysis and interpreting product performance metrics.

Nice to Have: previous internship or early-career experience in product management, software engineering, or a related technical role. Experience with onboarding flows, questionnaires, forms, checkout experiences. Familiarity with conversion funnels, A/B testing, or experimentation. Experience with e-commerce platforms, subscription products, or consumer-facing applications. Understanding of healthcare privacy, patient eligibility, or regulated software workflows.
