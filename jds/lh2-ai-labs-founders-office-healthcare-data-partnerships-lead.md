## Description

The company partners institutions with private, high-quality datasets and domain experts across multiple verticals including healthcare. The role focuses on building healthcare's data supply pipeline. The successful candidate will identify data owners (hospitals, diagnostic chains, EHR vendors, payers), understand frontier model requirements, negotiate licensing rights, and close agreements.
