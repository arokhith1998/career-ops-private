## Role Summary

The Chief of Staff is the connective layer across Product's 6 pillars (Core Broking, Design, Growth, Wealth/MF/Fixed Income, Post Trade, Self-Service), translating Vision 2031 strategy into a coordinated operating cadence, a single metrics view, and a consistent narrative for leadership. A working partner role: owning the planning calendar, chasing decisions to closure, giving the Head of Product early visibility into risk.

## Key Responsibilities

- Strategy & narrative: consolidate roadmaps across all 6 pillars; translate Vision 2031 themes into quarterly OKRs; own the Head of Product's executive narrative.
- Operating rhythm: run the planning calendar (weekly/monthly/quarterly); track decisions and owners to closure, including the KCP to Neo Assist transition and a named handover.
- Cross-functional coordination: primary coordination point with the Corporate President's office, Technology, Compliance, and Marketing (especially post the Oct 2026 Kotak Neo rebrand); manages named instrumentation/release-queue seams.
- Metrics & insight: stand up a single north-star dashboard across pillars (OPC, Activation, AUM, MTF health, CSAT); spot patterns before they become surprises.
- Special projects: lead time-bound cross-pillar initiatives directly for the Head of Product; support org design, succession, and talent reviews.

## Candidate Profile

- 8-12 years in strategy consulting, product/business operations, or a prior Chief of Staff / BizOps role, ideally in fintech, broking, or wealth management.
- Strong structured-thinking and written communication; comfortable driving decisions without formal authority over peers.
- Working fluency with product/business metrics (funnels, cohorts, unit economics); high discretion and judgment.
- Comfortable with a multi-city rhythm; Mumbai-based with periodic travel to Bengaluru.
