Description
This role is central to developing and optimizing customer offers that drive subscriber growth, strengthen customer value, and maximize long-term profitability across acquisition, existing customer, retention, and SVOD portfolios.You'll combine analytical expertise, financial acumen, and marketing strategy to shape their portfolio of offers. Beyond building business cases, you'll partner with strategy owners to challenge assumptions, refine offer concepts, and identify better solutions before they reach execution. Success in this role requires thinking holistically across products, channels, customer segments, and lifecycle touchpoints-recognizing dependencies across pricing, packaging, promotions, and customer experiences to develop strategies that are financially sound, operationally executable, and compelling for customers.
