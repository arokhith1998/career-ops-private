About Viz.ai

Viz.ai is the leader in building and deploying AI-powered Care Pathways and helping doctors do their work. The Viz Platform is deployed in 2,000 hospitals across the United States and trusted by many leading life sciences companies. Viz.ai was the first company to be awarded CMS reimbursement for AI and is ranked #1 Healthcare AI Platform by hospitals/health systems in the Black Book Research survey.

The Role

We are looking for a dynamic, highly motivated Product Marketing Manager with B2B healthcare marketing experience to own commercial go-to-market strategy for AI-powered SaMD solutions, with accountability for Viz Pulmonary Suite and other solutions within the Viz portfolio. Highly cross-functional role, partnering with Product, Sales, Clinical, Regulatory, Customer Success, and Life Sciences teams.

You Will:
- Own marketing strategy and GTM execution for a core product suite -- positioning through launch and sales enablement -- accountable for commercial performance.
- Partner with outbound Product Management to translate roadmap/clinical strategy into market-ready positioning and GTM plans.
- Set positioning and messaging strategy for distinct functional audiences.
- Build and maintain the core marketing asset library (webinars, blogs, podcasts, brochures, sales enablement).
- Plan and lead campaigns with demand generation.
- Represent Viz at conferences and customer-facing events.
- Turn market insights and competitive intelligence into actionable plans.
- Own quarterly business performance analysis for product marketing scope.

Requirements:
- Bachelor's Degree (required)
- 3-5+ years of B2B marketing experience in healthcare, medical device, diagnostics, or pharma
- AI-native marketer, experience leveraging tools such as Claude, Claude Design, ChatGPT, etc.
- Demonstrated experience developing messaging, positioning, and go-to-market content
- Comfortable with ambiguity and shifting priorities
- Thrives in a highly-matrixed environment

Preferred: experience in 2+ clinical audiences supported by Viz.ai; clinical/healthcare SaaS marketing; startup familiarity; fluency in FDA promotional regulations for med devices; experience with external partnerships.

Compensation: "Viz.ai is committed to providing highly competitive cash compensation, equity, and benefits... Compensation Range: $109K - $150K." Medical, dental, vision, 401(k), generous vacation, and additional benefits for full-time employees.
