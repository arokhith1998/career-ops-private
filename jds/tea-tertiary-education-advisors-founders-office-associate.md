## Full JD Text

Company Description
Tertiary Education Advisors (TEA) is a higher education and personality development consultancy dedicated to helping students gain admission to leading universities worldwide. Through personalised mentorship, strategic guidance, and holistic development, we empower students to grow academically, professionally, and personally, preparing them to thrive in a global landscape.

Role Details
Full-Time | Offline | 6 days a week
Open to third and fourth year students and recent graduates

What You'll Be Exposed To
You'll work across all verticals of the company, including:
- Strategy and growth planning
- Sales, partnerships and incentives
- Client-facing operations
- Research and insight development
- Marketing and social media strategy
- Internal systems, processes and execution
- Team and stakeholder coordination

From research and reports to decision-making support, this role gives you a front-row seat to founder-level thinking.

Why Join TEA
- Direct mentorship from the Founder & the Executive Team
- Access to all functions: strategy, sales, marketing, operations, client success
- Above-market compensation with performance-based incentives
- Sales incentives tied to contribution and impact
- Steep personal and professional growth curve
- Real-world skills colleges don't teach: ownership, communication, prioritization, decision-making
- Work with a mission-driven team focused on youth empowerment and long-term impact

Who We're Looking For
- Third or fourth year student, or a recent graduate (any discipline)
- Strong research, analytical and problem-solving skills
- Excellent written, verbal communication and speaking skills
- Highly organised, proactive and self-driven
- Comfortable with Google Workspace and MS Office
- Genuine interest in education, leadership, startups or youth development
- Prior exposure to education, consulting, sales or leadership programs is a plus
