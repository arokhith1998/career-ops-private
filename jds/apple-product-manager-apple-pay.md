You will partner directly with Engineering, Human Interface, Trust and Safety, Business Development, and other teams across Apple. You will also work closely with external partners such as payment networks, issuers, merchants, and other ecosystem players to deliver results. We are looking for a motivated candidate who is passionate about great customer experiences and wants to invent the future of authentication and fraud prevention.Responsibilities
Guide teams across Human Interface, Engineering, Legal, Business Development, Marketing, Analytics, and Operations, leading intricate cross-functional projects.
Drive innovation in provisioning and fraud prevention that protects users while reducing friction.
Help envision and build new strategies, roadmaps, and product features for the provisioning and risk platform.
Drive progress by evangelizing product strategy to build alignment across executives, cross-functional partners, and external stakeholders.
Drive go-to-market planning and execution, ensuring readiness across all partners and optimizing for measurable impact.
Drive adoption and collaborate with strategic partners — networks, issuers, merchants, and other ecosystem players — on product development.
Minimum Qualifications
8+ years of product management experience building and launching consumer products with designers, engineers, and data scientists.
Demonstrated ability to lead cross-functional initiatives spanning engineering, design, marketing, and legal.
Strong technical foundation, with the ability to engage with engineering teams and understand system-level trade-offs.
Fluency with payment ecosystem roles, mechanics, and strategies (networks, issuers, PSPs, e-commerce platforms, and merchants).
Fluency in leveraging AI tools for product management workflows.
Proven ability to synthesize complex topics, drive alignment, and communicate effectively with senior leadership.
Preferred Qualifications
Proven track record of moving key provisioning metrics, including conversion, consumer and merchant adoption, fraud rates, and payment volume.
Working knowledge of how AI impacts fraud management, identity, and commerce.
