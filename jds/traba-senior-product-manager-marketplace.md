## Full JD Text

About Traba

Traba is building the AI operating layer for the light industrial supply chain. We started in the workforce space because contingent labor is the biggest operational pain point our manufacturing and logistics customers face. We used it to embed ourselves inside their daily operations and create a far better customer experience through technology. Now those same customers are pulling us beyond staffing into the broader operational workflows that run their facilities. That foundation gave us proprietary data from millions of shifts and deep enterprise relationships. But our edge is more than data. By connecting to the systems running across every facility and activating the workers already on our platform to execute against them, we are building applied AI that drives real productivity gains and transforms how the global supply chain operates at scale. We are backed by Founders Fund, Khosla Ventures, and General Catalyst.

The Role

The product is the labor itself: the workers we match and deploy into warehouses and manufacturing facilities across the country (and eventually, the world). Every product decision impacts supply, demand, fulfillment, customer outcomes, and marketplace health. We are looking for an entrepreneurial and experienced Product Manager to own and evolve our staffing product that sits at the center of Traba's marketplace. You'll own the staffing product at the center of Traba's marketplace, working across both sides: the enterprise customers who run distribution centers and manufacturing lines, and the workers whose trust and reliability are what those customers are actually buying. In addition to collaborating with software engineers, product designers, data analysts, and other product managers on the team, you'll also work closely with our operations and field teams to design, build, and ship practical solutions into industrial facilities. This role reports directly to our VP of Product.

What you'll do
- Architect and steward our product strategy across critical staffing and marketplace workflows
- Solve complex marketplace problems by balancing customer needs, operational realities, and business outcomes
- Write detailed product requirements and partner closely with engineering teams to execute solutions
- Build systems that improve marketplace liquidity, fulfillment, matching quality, and operational efficiency
- Use data to uncover hidden patterns and answer critical marketplace and customer questions
- Translate operational complexity into scalable product systems
- Scale operations by designing smart internal tooling and technical solutions
- Launch and iterate products across customers, regions, and operational environments
- Visualize and communicate complex ideas from systems and workflows to operational models and product strategy
- Socialize your work across all levels and departments while synthesizing diverse stakeholder feedback

What we're looking for
- Customer-obsessed. Product judgment is downstream of customer truth, and you act like it.
- AI-native operator. You've changed how you work because of AI, not just added it to your stack.
- Marketplace operator. Deep experience building products in two-sided marketplaces.
- Enterprise B2B muscle. Built products for complex customers with multiple stakeholders, long sales cycles, and operational rollout challenges.
- First-principles thinker.
- Operations systems builder.
- Builder mentality.
- Deeply collaborative.

What you'll bring
- 5 - 7 years of experience as a Product Manager
- Evidence of shipping products that materially moved business outcomes
- Strong experience in two-sided marketplaces with real supply and demand dynamics
- Experience building enterprise products with multi-stakeholder customers and complex operational environments
- Experience building internal operational tooling or systems that supported real business execution
- Strong technical fluency and ability to work deeply with engineering teams
- Experience operating in startup environments where speed and ownership matter
- Desire to work at a startup and cover an exceptionally wide surface area
- A strong work ethic and desire to build a category-defining company
