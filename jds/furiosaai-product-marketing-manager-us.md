## Job Description (full, verbatim)
About FuriosaAI
FuriosaAI builds high-performance, high-efficiency AI compute for the Inference Era. Founded in 2017 by veteran semiconductor and AI algorithm engineers, Furiosa operates globally with offices in Korea and Silicon Valley, along with a compiler-focused R&D lab in Lisbon.

Our vision is to make AI computing sustainable, enabling access to powerful AI for everyone on Earth. We solve the AI hardware energy and operational cost crisis at the architectural level, rather than through brute force, building the world's first truly AI-native compute platform to unlock the full potential of artificial intelligence for every enterprise.

About The Role
We're looking for a Product Marketing Manager to help tell our story. You'll take complex hardware and software innovations and turn them into clear, engaging, and shareable content for developers, researchers, enterprises, and the broader AI community.

This is a hands-on role where your work shapes Furiosa's technical voice globally. You'll collaborate closely with engineers, researchers, product managers, and marketing to make sure our blog content reflects both the depth of our technology and the excitement of our mission.

Key Responsibilities
- Write technical blog posts, case studies, tutorials, and thought leadership content that clearly explains complex AI hardware and software concepts
- Collaborate with engineering and research teams to translate technical breakthroughs into stories that resonate with technical audiences
- Edit and refine content for clarity, accuracy, and engagement while maintaining a consistent Furiosa voice
- Work with team leads to plan content calendars and drive blog strategy to maximize reach and impact
- Translate technical insights into visual or interactive content, such as diagrams, infographics, or example workflows
- Keep up-to-date with AI, hardware, and deep learning trends
- Occasionally contribute to social channels, newsletters, or technical presentations to amplify blog content

Minimum Qualifications
- 10+ years of experience writing for technical audiences in AI hardware or software
- Strong ability to understand, synthesize and communicate complex technical concepts clearly and accurately
- Portfolio of published technical writing, ideally including blogs, tutorials, or research summaries
- Collaborative mindset - comfortable working with engineers, product, and marketing teams
- Fluency in English; Korean a plus for collaboration and localized content
- Passionate about AI, hardware, and developer communities

Why Join FuriosaAI
With our flagship chip, RNGD, in mass production today and our next-generation platform in development with Broadcom, we are proving that full-stack, tensor-native compute is the future of AI infrastructure.
