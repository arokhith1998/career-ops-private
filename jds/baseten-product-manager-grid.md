## Full JD text (verbatim)

ABOUT BASETEN
Baseten powers mission-critical inference for the world's most dynamic AI companies, like Cursor, Notion, OpenEvidence, Abridge, Clay, Gamma, and Writer. By uniting applied AI research, flexible infrastructure, and seamless developer tooling, we enable companies operating at the frontier of AI to bring cutting-edge models into production. We're growing quickly and recently raised our $1.5B Series F, led by Altimeter Capital, Conviction Partners, and Spark Capital.

PRODUCT AT BASETEN
Baseten is building a new cloud for AI. Our ambition is to make inference, training, and agent execution part of one coherent platform, and to shape how the next generation of AI products gets built. We're hiring builders and tastemakers with the ambition to help create that future.

THE ROLE
Build the future of AI model routing, where every task gets the right model, automatically. Today, developers often have to choose between models that are faster, cheaper, or more capable. But the right model isn't always the same for every request. Grid is Baseten's intelligent model routing platform, designed to help developers get the best possible results across open and frontier models without manually managing every decision. We're starting with coding agents, where model choice can have an enormous impact on quality, speed, and cost. As Product Manager for Grid, you'll work with engineers, researchers, and customers to build a new category of AI infrastructure.

RESPONSIBILITIES
- Own the product roadmap for Grid, from early developer adoption to production deployments and enterprise expansion.
- Work with researchers and engineers to improve intelligent model selection, routing policies, fallbacks, and controls.
- Build evaluation frameworks that measure real-world outcomes, including task quality, completion time, latency, and total cost.
- Shape the developer experience for onboarding, configuration, observability, and routing decisions.
- Help customers understand why a model was selected and give them confidence and control over how routing behaves.
- Work through technical challenges involving conversation history, tool compatibility, prompt caching, retries, and routing overhead.
- Partner with GTM and customers to turn early pilots into sustained adoption and revenue growth.

REQUIREMENTS
- Experience building and commercializing AI/ML products or developer platforms, with demonstrated adoption or business impact.
- A technical background in software engineering, ML engineering, research, or equivalent hands-on experience.
- Experience working with researchers or ML engineers on model behavior, evaluation, or learned systems in production.
- Strong understanding of AI model quality, latency, cost, and the tradeoffs involved in optimizing across them.
- Excellent developer product instincts, with an appreciation for both simple APIs and enterprise-level controls.
- Hands-on experience using coding agents and a strong point of view on where automation helps developers and where they need control.
- Comfort building emerging products where the technical approach and customer expectations are still evolving.

NICE TO HAVE
- Experience with LLM gateways, model routing, AI agents, coding harnesses, or multi-model platforms.

WHAT SUCCESS LOOKS LIKE
Developers can build better AI applications without having to manually choose and optimize every model. Grid consistently delivers measurable improvements in quality, speed, or cost, and becomes a trusted part of customers' production AI stacks.
