## Description
Senior Product Manager to join the Rubrik Security Cloud Control Plane team, owning the product roadmap for Rubrik Security Cloud-Government (RSC-G) and government-focused product initiatives across the portfolio. Works with field team and customers on prioritization; works with Compliance to translate frameworks (FedRAMP, StateRAMP/GovRAMP, CMMC, FIPS) and digital-sovereignty regulations into product requirements.

## What You Will Do
- Own the product roadmap for RSC-G and related government needs; articulate roadmap reasoning to engineering/stakeholders.
- Turn regulatory frameworks/mandates into concrete platform requirements with Compliance, engineering, and other PMs.
- Run own discovery: talk directly with field, government customers, internal stakeholders.
- Build business cases for investing in or deferring capabilities.
- Close parity gaps where government-required capability exists but isn't enabled/documented.
- Use AI agents to automate compliance checks and provide field information.

## Preferred Qualifications
- Solid product management experience owning strategy/roadmap for a defined product area, or equivalent as solutions engineer/architect/technical customer-facing role, ideally with regulated/public-sector exposure.
- Familiarity with FedRAMP, CMMC, FIPS, BSI C3A, Cyber Essentials.
- Comfortable working closely with engineering.
- Experience with enterprise infrastructure/cloud across multiple deployment models (SaaS, customer-managed, private, air-gapped) is a plus.
- AI fluency in daily workflow.
