About The Job

Know the user. Know the magic. Connect the two. At its core, marketing at Google starts with technology and ends with the user, bringing both together in unconventional ways. In this role, you’ll manage the digital front door for Google’s energy and infrastructure story. You’ll oversee web properties, local campus landing pages, and policy topic hubs, while managing Google’s centralized editorial content calendar on energy and infrastructure.

You’ll partner closely with communications, creative, and product teams to build digital storytelling assets that reach local communities and key opinion formers with an eye to aligning messaging across owned digital surfaces. As an agile content strategist with an eye for editorial quality, you will turn policy insights and economic data into clear, engaging digital content.
