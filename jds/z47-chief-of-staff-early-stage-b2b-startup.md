## Summary
Founder's office role focused on fixing operational gaps, aligning priorities across functions, and ensuring work gets completed. Positioned as an extension of the CEO's office and a force multiplier for the leadership team.

## Experience
2-4 years of work experience, preferably at a top consulting firm.

## Requirements and Preferences
- 2-4 years, consulting background or experience at a fast-growing B2B SaaS startup preferred
- Prior entrepreneurial or founding experience is a plus, not mandatory
- Strong bias for action, structured problem-solving, comfort with ambiguity

## Responsibilities
1. **Strategic execution:** Work with the CEO on key priorities and drive them to completion. Turn business goals into cross-functional initiatives with clear accountability. Evaluate and incubate new business opportunities and partnerships.
2. **Business performance management:** Define and track company-wide OKRs, metrics, and dashboards. Monitor business unit performance, identify gaps, run operating reviews with function heads to ensure action items are followed through.
3. **Leadership enablement:** Help the CEO prepare for board meetings, investor reviews, and client interactions. Create briefing notes, strategy documents, and decision frameworks. Serve as a sounding board and thought partner to the CEO.
4. **Cross-functional leadership:** Lead internal projects across Sales, Product, Marketing, and Customer Success. Resolve bottlenecks across teams and keep communication flowing between the CEO, leadership, and the broader organization.
5. **Operational excellence:** Set up scalable systems that speed up decision-making. Establish best practices for governance, meeting cadence, and reporting. Find inefficiencies and drive process improvements.
6. **Special projects:** Own high-priority work that doesn't fit neatly into one function. Lead external research, benchmarking, and market intelligence. Temporarily lead or support functions during leadership transitions or scaling phases.
