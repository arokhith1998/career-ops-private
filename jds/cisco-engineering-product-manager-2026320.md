## Full JD Text

This role is Hybrid requiring 2 days in the San Jose or New York office

Meet the Team

AI infrastructure increasingly spans infrastructure across on-premises and cloud environments. Cloud operators and enterprise platform teams must turn that fragmented infrastructure into secure, governed, self-service token factories while managing capacity, policy, reliability, and lifecycle complexity.

We are a small, senior team building a new product inside Cisco in the full-stack AI Infrastructure space. We work closely with design customers and ecosystem partners, move quickly, and make evidence-based decisions in areas where the market and technology are still evolving.

Your Impact

As a Product Manager, you will own a major product area, from customer discovery and strategy through delivery, launch, adoption, and ongoing improvement. You will turn customer needs into priorities and measurable outcomes for infrastructure operators and workload teams.

This is a senior individual contributor role. You will establish direction, make difficult product and investment tradeoffs, and lead across organizational boundaries without relying on formal authority.

What You Will Do
- Define the product vision, strategy, and multi-quarter roadmap for a major product domain.
- Develop a deep understanding of cloud operators, enterprise platform teams, infrastructure operators, and AI developers through direct customer discovery and design-partner engagement.
- Translate customer problems into clear product requirements, acceptance criteria, measurable outcomes, and phased release plans.
- Turn ambiguity into action by identifying the smallest valuable product increment, establishing evidence-based exit criteria, and adjusting direction as new information emerges.
- Lead build, partner, buy, and open-source decisions in collaboration with engineering, architecture, security, and business development.
- Align engineering, architecture, design, technical marketing, operations, security, sales, marketing, Customer Experience, finance, and partner teams around priorities and launch plans.
- Define and track product and operational metrics such as onboarding time, deployment success, service reliability, adoption, utilization, customer value, and supportability.
- Own market and competitive analysis, business cases, pricing and packaging input, partner strategy, and go-to-market readiness.
- Represent the product with customers, partners, field teams, analysts, and Cisco executives, translating complex infrastructure capabilities into clear customer and business outcomes.

Minimum Qualifications
- Bachelor's degree plus 12 years, master's degree plus 8 years, or equivalent practical experience in product management, engineering, or a related technical field.
- Experience owning an enterprise infrastructure, cloud, or platform product through discovery, definition, delivery, launch, and operation.
- Deep expertise in at least one of Kubernetes, networking, storage, or security, with working fluency across at least two of the remaining domains.
- Experience defining technically complex requirements, including APIs, platform services, lifecycle management, or observability.
- Demonstrated experience creating a product from the ground up, including turning open-ended problems into phased releases, acceptance criteria, and measurable outcomes.
- Proven ability to influence engineering and business leaders, resolve competing priorities, and communicate effectively with practitioners, executives, customers, and partners.
- Excellent written, analytical, and product judgment skills.

Preferred Qualifications
- Direct experience operating production cloud or Kubernetes environments, including service-level objectives, incidents, upgrades, capacity management, observability, security, and cost management.
- Fluency in AI infrastructure topics such as GPU clusters, accelerator scheduling, workload placement, high-performance network and storage paths, model serving, inference, and infrastructure telemetry.
- Hands-on breadth across Kubernetes, networking, storage, and security, with technical depth in more than one domain.
- Experience serving hyperscalers, neocloud or sovereign cloud operators, cloud service providers, or enterprise AI platform teams.
- Experience working with open-source communities, infrastructure partners, or multi-vendor technology ecosystems.
- Experience developing platform business models, packaging, pricing, or consumption-based services.
- A record of thriving in startup-like environments where priorities evolve quickly and individuals are expected to create structure, take ownership, and deliver results.

Why Cisco?

At Cisco, we're revolutionizing how data and infrastructure connect and protect organizations in the AI era -- and beyond. We've been innovating fearlessly for 40 years to create solutions that power how humans and technology work together across the physical and digital worlds.
