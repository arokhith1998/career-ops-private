The Basics

Tanium is seeking a Senior Product Manager to drive delivery across our AI Core Services portfolio. You will be building the core experience and infrastructure that serve our agentic platform.

This is an execution-focused product role. The portfolio is pivoting toward AI and agentic workflows, and we need a PM who ships. You will own the day-to-day product work that turns strategy into delivered software. You will partner directly with engineering and UX teams, own requirements and prioritization for your product areas, and carry capabilities from idea through launch and enablement.

A successful candidate will be an experienced product manager with a strong record of delivery and the cross-functional skill to execute across all levels of the organization. Passion for the product management craft, passion for delivery, and the agility to adjust as the business evolves are the foundation of success in this role.

What You'll Do

- Own the delivery of product capabilities from ideation to launch in partnership with engineering and UX teams, including grooming, prioritization, acceptance criteria, and release readiness
- Shape the core platform workflows and experiences that teams across the organization rely on at enterprise scale
- Translate customer needs, field signals, and support escalations into clear, prioritized requirements and specs. We run a spec-driven, AI-assisted development process where PMs author the specs that drive engineering execution
- Work alongside internal domain experts to ground product decisions in real-world use cases and practice
- Conduct discovery with existing customers and present product vision, updates, and progress to internal and external stakeholders
- Partner with product marketing, enablement, and support to ensure new capabilities land successfully with customers
- Track and deliver objectives and key results (OKRs) to measure product success and guide iterative improvement
- Adapt plans quickly as priorities shift with the business and the market

What You'll Bring

- 3 to 6 years of product management experience, with shipped products you can walk us through
- Strong cross-functional instincts. You influence without authority across engineering, design, marketing, sales, and support, and you communicate clearly with each audience
- Bias to action and outcome orientation. You drive results without complete information and stay anchored on what matters most
- High learning velocity. You ramp quickly in new domains and connect what you learn to what matters for the business
- Hands-on technical experience with a variety of AI tools and a passion for transforming how product management works through AI
- Confidence engaging customers, including senior technical leadership
