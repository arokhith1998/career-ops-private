## Full JD text

About Clerkie: fintech startup democratizing financial wellness using AI. Launched 2018; aims to help ~123M underserved Americans needing financial guidance. Pairs consumer guidance with B2B software (the Clerkie network) for creditors managing loan losses while helping borrowers get out of debt. Remote-first, 40+ employees across 4 US time zones. Raised $41M total, including a $33M Series A in early 2023 (Citi Ventures, Left Lane Capital, Wellington Ventures, Flourish Ventures, Vestigo Ventures).

About the role: Looking for a driven, curious, collaborative Product Manager to join the small-but-mighty Product team. Ideal for a product builder who has shipped software before, ready for deeper ownership of complex systems in a fast-moving startup. Operates at the intersection of technology, business strategy, and real-world financial infrastructure; collaborates with engineers/designers/leadership and directly with external partners. Works on initiatives involving enterprise partners and users in the debt ecosystem. Beyond shipping features -- helps shape how the product org operates (rituals, documentation, culture).

Here's what you'll do:
- Own and lead product initiatives across Clerkie's platform, discovery through release
- Collaborate deeply with engineering/design to shape scalable solutions
- Translate complex partner/ecosystem needs into clear product requirements
- Drive cross-functional alignment across technical/non-technical stakeholders
- Work directly with enterprise partners to understand workflows/opportunities
- Define priorities, manage trade-offs, shape roadmap decisions
- Lead product discovery, experimentation, iteration across initiatives
- Support evolution of product systems, rituals, documentation
- Contribute to building a strong product culture

Recent Product team wins (context): shipped enterprise-partner improvements; SLT scoping/prioritization; enterprise sandbox demos unlocking major deals; shaped product strategy/roadmap across consumer experiences and the Fiber platform; improved internal product knowledge base; onboarded new Product team member; hackathon participation.

Must-haves:
- Experience building/shipping software products with engineering teams
- Strong systems thinking -- breaking complex workflows into clear solutions
- Technical fluency -- comfortable discussing APIs, data flows, system architecture
- Ability to translate real-world problems into clear, prioritized requirements
- Excellent written/verbal communication across technical/non-technical audiences
- Strong ownership mindset
- Ability to operate in a fast-moving startup where priorities evolve quickly
- Comfort working independently in a remote-first team

Nice-to-haves (not required): SaaS/infrastructure/developer-tools experience; fintech/financial-services/regulated-industry familiarity; experience integrating with external systems/enterprise partners; engineering/CS/analytics background; early-stage startup experience; curiosity about financial wellness and tech that improves lives.

Our salary & benefits: Salary range $120-150k; equity; remote-first role (US only); medical/dental/vision; 401(k) + retirement; PTO + annual company retreat; company laptop + home office stipend; collaborative, values-driven culture.

Our Hiring Process: Application (async) -> Intro Call w/ People Team (15-20 min) -> Take-Home Challenge (async, 1 week) -> Take-Home live Presentation (45 min) -> Technical Deep Dive w/ Product Leader (45 min). Standard background check plus reference check required before any offer.
