## Full description (verbatim from LinkedIn jobs-guest API)

Finance and Business Planning is a highly mission-driven organization. Our mission is to enable Meta to plan, prioritize, and execute as effectively as possible. We take a uniquely long-term view towards business problems. It is critical that we operate with an independent and objective perspective and can represent our point of view to our partners and executive stakeholders. We serve as financial advisers to our business partners and own shared business goals and results where appropriate. We strive to balance our responsibility to support business partners with our responsibility to serve the long-term mission and sustainability of Meta and act as stewards of the company's resources. This role requires strategic thinking, building connections and influencing stakeholders through effective communication, an analytical mindset, and a bias towards action. You will be challenged to understand and contribute to rapidly changing business models, drive collaboration across teams, develop recommendations on strategic investment decisions, craft annual and long-term plans, and partner with leaders to solve problems and achieve goals. Our promise: you will gain invaluable experience in a position that offers significant responsibility, interaction with product and business leaders, and an opportunity to have a big impact.

**Business Operations Manager, New Revenue Initiatives Responsibilities:**
- Provide financial leadership across company-wide planning cycles: long-range plan (LRP), annual budget, and half-year roadmapping
- Develop nuanced ROI and impact frameworks across headcount, expenses, and other investments that will influence key decision-makers to allocate resources more efficiently
- Create/socialize data-driven narratives and influence outcomes and decisions at the executive level
- Establish strategic partnerships with leaders of all levels and functions across business functions
- Help define and implement scalable financial processes to make us and our business partners more efficient
- Develop a solid quantitative and qualitative understanding of Meta's GTM, product, and business model with partners

**Minimum Qualifications:**
- 9+ years of experience in business strategy, finance, analytics, or consulting and planning
- BA/BS in a quantitative field (data science, computer science, economics, finance, etc.) or an equivalent degree/experience
- Experience in data analytics, data visualization, and scenario-based analysis
- Experience leading cross-functional projects with executive stakeholders

**Preferred Qualifications:**
- Experience supporting Enterprise or SaaS based Go-To-Market motions and or new revenue lines with an emphasis on AI monetization (e.g. Subscriptions, Enterprise Contracts)
- Experience working in a complex, fast-paced environment or in a high-growth company
- Experience working directly with executives
- High level of ownership and experience in driving cross-functional initiatives with minimal guidance
- Demonstrated ability to integrate AI tools to optimize/redesign workflows and drive measurable impact (e.g., efficiency gains, quality improvements)
- Demonstrated ongoing AI skill development (e.g., prompt/context engineering, agent orchestration) and staying current with emerging AI technologies
- Experience adhering to and implementing responsible, ethical AI practices (e.g., risk assessment, bias mitigation, quality and accuracy reviews)
