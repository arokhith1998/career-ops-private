PAYU PAYMENTS PRIVATE LIMITED
Chief of Staff - PayU Finance
Location: Mumbai, India
Reports to: CEO, PayU Finance

**About The Role**
PayU Finance is looking for a highly motivated MBA graduate to join the CEO's office as Chief of Staff. This role offers a unique opportunity to work closely with the CEO and senior leadership, drive strategic initiatives, support executive decision-making, and contribute to the growth and performance of the PayU Finance business.

The role will provide broad exposure to multiple functions, including sales, product, operations, HR, finance, and business strategy.

**What You'll Work On**
- Strategic Support: Support the CEO in strategic planning, business development research, and analytical evaluation of key business decisions.
- Project Management: Partner with cross-functional teams to drive high-priority projects, ensuring timely execution, alignment, and delivery of key outcomes.
- Business Analysis and Insights: Conduct market research, analyse industry trends, identify business opportunities, and prepare executive summaries and presentations for leadership reviews.
- Executive Communication: Support communication with group executive teams and shareholders, assist in preparing investor relations materials, and facilitate alignment across senior stakeholders.
- Process Optimisation: Identify operational inefficiencies, support business process improvement initiatives, and help implement scalable solutions across functions.
- Special Projects: Lead and manage high-impact initiatives aligned with business objectives and growth strategies. Projects may span sales, product, operations, HR, and other business functions.
- Executive Office Support: Help the CEO track strategic priorities, monitor key deliverables, and ensure effective follow-through on critical business actions.

**What We're Looking For**
- An MBA graduate with 4-5 years of professional experience in a fast-paced and analytical environment.
- Experience in the lending or financial services industry or a consulting environment will be preferred.
- Prior experience working directly with C-level executives, particularly within financial services organisations, will be an advantage.
- Proven experience in programme or project management, preferably involving technology-driven initiatives.
- Strong analytical, problem-solving, and business communication skills.
- Ability to work effectively across multiple functions and manage complex, high-priority initiatives.
- Strong stakeholder management and presentation skills.
- High levels of ownership, maturity, discretion, and attention to detail.
- Ability to handle confidential financial information and strategic business plans with integrity and professionalism.

**What We Offer**
- Competitive compensation and benefits.
- Direct exposure to executive leadership and strategic decision-making.
- Cross-functional exposure across different areas of the organisation.
- Opportunities for accelerated learning, career growth, and professional development.
- A dynamic and collaborative work environment.
- The opportunity to make a meaningful impact on the direction and growth of PayU Finance.
