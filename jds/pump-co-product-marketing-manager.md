As Product Marketing Manager at Pump, you'll be the bridge between product, marketing, and sales. You'll own how Pump is positioned and how every launch lands with customers.

What You'll Do:
- Own Pump's positioning and messaging across AWS, GCP, Azure, and AI/LLM cost savings.
- Plan and run product launches: messaging, sales enablement, launch-day campaigns.
- Turn technical features into clear customer benefits for engineering and finance buyers.
- Build sales collateral: pitch decks, one-pagers, battlecards, ROI calculators, case studies.
- Run customer and competitive research; share findings company-wide.
- Partner with content, SEO/AEO, and paid to keep messaging consistent across channels.

What We're Looking For:
- 3-5 years of product marketing experience, ideally in B2B SaaS or developer tools.
- Strong writer who can make technical products simple without dumbing them down.
- Experience running launches and enabling a sales team.
- Strong project management and data skills; familiar with tools like HubSpot and Google Analytics.
- Ability to thrive in a fast-paced, constantly evolving environment.
- Available to work 5 days a week onsite in the San Francisco office.

Bonus Points: experience in cloud infrastructure, observability, FinOps, or AI infrastructure; experience marketing usage-based or consumption-priced products.

Benefits: competitive compensation + equity; comprehensive healthcare/dental; 401(k); 13 accrued PTO days plus company shutdown Dec 24-Jan 1; free lunch & dinner at the office; annual company-paid retreats; developmental bonuses for professional development tied to cloud and related fields.
