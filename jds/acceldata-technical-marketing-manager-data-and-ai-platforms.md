## Full JD text (verbatim, as extracted)

**Reports To:** CMO

**Why this role exists**

You provide technical validation for xLake Data & AI Platform. You construct demonstrations and reference implementations that substantiate the platform's claims to enterprise data architects, enterprise architects, and AI practitioners. Your work influences architectural decisions before formal sales engagement occurs.

**What You'll Do**

**Build the proof (sample applications and demos)**

- Develop and maintain functional sample applications and reference implementations on xLake demonstrating platform value: agent runtime scheduling across distributed environments, MCP-driven agent scenarios with data governance, and multi-engine observability tracing failures to upstream data lineage and pipeline health.
- Package these into deployable demo assets field teams and marketing personnel can execute independently.
- Maintain demo currency as platform capabilities advance.

**Create technical material for a technical buyer**

- Produce reference architectures, technical analyses, solution briefs, benchmarks, and whitepapers targeting three audiences: data architects (lineage, governance, pipeline health), enterprise architects (cross-environment control plane, no consolidation), and AI practitioners (governed data for agents, model-agnostic runtimes).
- Own technical positioning framework: control without consolidation, runtime versus design-time governance, multi-engine observability, source- and model-agnostic interoperability. Deliver proof through demos, papers and talks.

**Represent Acceldata in the field**

- Execute live platform demonstrations at industry events; support SDRs on early-stage qualification calls distinguishing viable prospects from marginal leads.
- Author and deliver conference sessions and technical presentations; participate in speaking engagements.

**Power product launches and announcements**

- Own technical proof for major launches and general availability releases: operational demonstrations, reference architectures, and launch presentations.
- Rapidly translate new capabilities into credible technical narratives for data architects, enterprise architects, and AI practitioners.
- Coordinate with product marketing and product management ensuring technical positioning resonates immediately.

**Standard technical marketing**

- Collaborate with product marketing, product management, sales engineering, and developer relations on launch content, competitive positioning, and sales enablement.
- Integrate field and practitioner insights into product and positioning strategy.

**What Winning Looks Like (first 6 to 12 Months)**

- A library of live, runnable demonstrations across three solution areas (Data & Analytics, Data Observability & ADM, AI & Applications).
- 10 in-person/online conference presentations delivered to architect and practitioner audiences.
- Field team active utilization of sample applications and technical material in sales activities.

**Bonus**

- Domain experience in data observability, data management, or governance.
- Demonstrated conference speaking history.
- Prior experience building demonstrations or sample applications for platform products.

**Who You Are**

- Hands-on technical contributor: you build implementations, not merely descriptions. Proficient across data engines (Spark, streaming, warehouses, lakes), Kubernetes, APIs, and contemporary AI/agent technologies (LLM APIs, MCP, agent frameworks).
- Can effectively communicate with data architects and AI practitioners with equivalent credibility.
- Strong written and oral communicator with authentic presentation presence.
- 12+ years experience in technical marketing engineering, developer relations, solutions/sales engineering, or hands-on architecture roles.

**Additional Requirements**

This role requires on-site presence at headquarters three days weekly.
