### Job Summary
As a highly strategic Senior Manager within our Revenue Operations team, you will be a lead architect for our global revenue lifecycle. You will not just operate - you will design and build the revenue processes and systems for the AI era, bridging the gap between technical accounting standards and world-class automation. This role focuses on ensuring that every new product launch, complex monetization architecture, and acquisition is operationally enabled and compliant from Day 1.

### Key Responsibilities
- Lead NPI Operational Readiness: Partner with Product, GTM, Legal, and Finance during the inception phase to ensure new offerings - including SaaS, hardware, and consumption-based business models - are operationally enabled and scalable.
- Architect Revenue Models: Design and operationalize complex revenue flows for strategic initiatives like platformization, flexible software licensing, and usage-based offerings.
- Drive Cross-Functional Execution: Serve as the primary Revenue SME across all "speedboats," leading NPI discovery, solution design sign-offs, and ensuring revenue considerations are embedded throughout the development lifecycle.
- UAT Governance: Own the User Acceptance Testing (UAT) lifecycle for revenue, from defining test scenario scope to executing system validations in SAP S/4HANA and SAP RAR to ensure data integrity and compliance.
- Policy Implementation: Partner with Technical Revenue to operationalize ASC 606 revenue accounting policies and formally document revenue checklists and business requirements for technical translation.
- Process Transformation: Identify operational bottlenecks and lead automation initiatives to eliminate manual workarounds, specifically focusing on "shift-left" strategies that move validation upstream in the NPI cycle.
- Team Leadership & People Management: Build, mentor, and lead a high-performing team of revenue operations professionals. Establish clear performance goals, provide ongoing coaching, and foster a culture of accountability, continuous learning, and operational excellence.

### Qualifications
- Education & Experience: Bachelor's degree in Accounting or Finance; 10+ years of progressive experience in Revenue Operations, Revenue Accounting, or Big 4 public accounting, specifically with technology companies.
- Technical Expertise: Deep functional knowledge of Quote-to-Cash (QTC) ecosystems, including SAP S/4HANA, SAP RAR, Salesforce/CPQ, and NetSuite.
- Subject Matter Mastery: Strong understanding of ASC 606 principles and their impact on revenue systems and internal controls (SOX compliance).
- NPI Proficiency: Proven track record managing operational readiness for complex product launches, M&A integrations, and evolving consumption-based models.
- Leadership: Demonstrated ability to lead, mentor, and develop high-performing teams while managing relationships with executive stakeholders across Finance, IT, and Product Management.
- Certification: CPA or equivalent is highly preferred.
