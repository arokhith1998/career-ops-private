About the Opportunity:The DIGITAL MEDIA MARKETING MANAGER role is responsible for leading digital media strategy, execution, and optimization to drive customer acquisition and maximize return on investment. This position acts as the key liaison between media execution and data/martech teams, ensuring data-driven decision making and measurable growth. The role contributes significantly to the organization by optimizing media spend, improving performance through advanced testing, and supporting strategic budget planning.
Responsibilities:Lead design, execution, and optimization of large-scale digital customer acquisition campaigns across multiple channels.
Manage external media agencies and strategic partners to ensure performance targets are met and data-driven optimization is applied.
Define requirements and KPIs for dashboards and metrics in collaboration with MarTech and Data Science teams.
Design and oversee incrementality tests, geo-experiments, and A/B testing to measure media impact.
Partner with MarTech to implement and quality assure tracking mechanisms ensuring accurate data flow.
Advise senior marketing leadership on media mix optimization, forecasting, and budget allocation.

Requirements:Bachelor’s degree or equivalent practical experience.
Eight years of progressive experience in digital media strategy, buying, and optimization with a focus on data-driven performance.
Proficiency with Google Analytics (GA4), tracking technologies such as GTM and pixel implementation, and troubleshooting data discrepancies.
Experience managing large media budgets and presenting performance data to executive leadership.
Proven ability managing media agencies and collaborating with cross-functional teams including MarTech, Creative, and Data Science.
Skilled in data analysis related to paid media, marketing performance, and budget management.

Preferred Qualifications:Experience designing and executing incrementality tests, geo-experiments, or involvement in media mix modeling and multi-touch attribution.
Familiarity with SQL and data visualization tools such as Looker or Tableau.
Understanding of privacy-centric tracking technologies like Conversions API and Ads Data Hub.
Experience in telecom or subscription service models focusing on customer acquisition cost, retention, and lifetime value.
