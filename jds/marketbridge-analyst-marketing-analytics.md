## Full JD text (verbatim)

Who We Are

Marketbridge partners with leading B2B brands to create unified Go-to-Market® systems that connect strategy, AI, creativity, activation and measurement. With a team of 350+ across North America and Europe, Marketbridge supports clients worldwide, ranging from Fortune 1000 enterprises to venture-backed, high-growth companies across technology, financial services, healthcare, industrial and consumer sectors.

Who We're Looking For

Marketbridge is seeking an Analyst, Marketing Analytics to support the reporting, measurement, and analytics work that helps teams understand the performance of modern B2B marketing programs.
This is an early-career role for someone who enjoys working with data and wants to build practical experience in B2B marketing measurement. The Analyst will gather and prepare data from media, web analytics, CRM, and marketing automation systems; help maintain accurate dashboards and reports; and support the quality assurance of web tracking and conversion events.

Working closely with more senior members of the Marketing Analytics team, the Analyst will help turn raw data into consistent, usable reporting. The role focuses on careful execution, data quality, documentation, and clear communication. Senior team members will provide direction on measurement approaches, interpret findings, and lead performance narratives and recommendations.

The ideal candidate is accountable, reliable, detail-oriented, and curious. They bring strong data management and manipulation skills in tools like Microsoft Excel or Google Sheets, communicate clearly, and are interested in growing their knowledge of B2B marketing measurement, analytics platforms, and reporting tools.

**Key Responsibilities**
- Collect and extract reporting data from platforms such as web analytics, paid media, marketing automation, CRM, and other campaign systems; organize and store securely for dashboard integration, analysis, and reporting presentations.
- Prepare, cleanse, normalize, and validate datasets in Excel and other approved tools. Check for missing values, duplicates, inconsistent naming, formatting issues, and other data quality concerns, and document or escalate issues.
- Use Excel and approved AI-enabled tools, including ChatGPT or Claude where appropriate, to support data preparation, QA, and repeatable workflows while following company and client data handling requirements.
- Maintain existing dashboards and reporting assets by refreshing data, checking filters and calculations, confirming date ranges and labels, and flagging unexpected changes or errors.
- Support the formatting and quality assurance of dashboards, charts, tables, and reporting presentations.
- Assist with web analytics tracking QA and troubleshooting, including checking tags, conversion events, URLs, and reporting behavior against documented requirements.
- With support from senior Marketing Analytics teammates, coordinate with Strategy, Media, Marketing Technology & Operations, and other internal teams.
- Support recurring and ad hoc reporting requests, internal presentations, proposal materials, and other analytics work under the guidance of more senior teammates.
- Build technical skills through hands-on project work, feedback, and relevant training or certifications in analytics, tagging, visualization, and marketing platforms.

**Qualifications**
- A recent graduate or early-career candidate with a degree, diploma, or equivalent learning in analytics, marketing, business, economics, mathematics, statistics, information systems, or related field. Relevant project, internship, co-op, or work experience is welcome; extensive professional experience is not required.
- Advanced working knowledge of Microsoft Excel, including formulas, lookups such as XLOOKUP or VLOOKUP, PivotTables, sorting and filtering, charts, and formatting.
- A data-driven mindset, with strong attention to detail.
- Comfort working with structured data and performing basic checks.
- Clear written and verbal communication skills.
- Strong accountability and follow-through.
- Curiosity about B2B marketing measurement, digital analytics, campaign performance.
- Interest in using AI-enabled tools to assist with routine analytics work.
- Experience or familiarity with Google Analytics (GA4), Google Tag Manager, Looker or Looker Studio, Adobe Analytics, Adobe Tags/Launch, Google Ads, LinkedIn Campaign Manager, Microsoft/Bing Ads, or other marketing platforms is an asset, but not required.
- Exposure to SQL, basic statistics, data visualization, or presentation tools such as PowerPoint is an asset.

**Benefits & Perks:** Flexible PTO, summer Fridays, paid parental leave (up to 16 weeks for birthing parents), closed Christmas to New Year's; comprehensive medical benefits + free premium Calm app access; 401(k) with 3% company contribution (US only), life insurance, long-term disability, AD&D; $500 professional development fund; company laptop, monthly WiFi & cell stipend, Perkopolis discounts.

This is a remote role, required to work in EST hours.
