## Full Job Description

Product Manager, GDM Robotics, DeepMind

corporate_fare DeepMind
place Mountain View, CA, USA; London, UK

Note: By applying to this position you will have an opportunity to share your preferred working location from the following: Mountain View, CA, USA; London, UK.

Minimum qualifications:
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Robotics, Mechanical/Electrical Engineering, Physics, a related technical field, or equivalent practical experience.
- 7 years of experience in product management, including taking technical products from concept to launch.
- Experience in robotics, through a robotics degree or prior work in the robotics industry.

Preferred qualifications:
- Experience with robotics safety, functional safety, or safety-critical systems.
- Experience with security for connected devices, robots, or AI systems.
- Experience with simulation, synthetic data, or developer tools, including building or commercializing APIs and SDKs.
- Experience designing evaluations, benchmarks, or reliability metrics for AI/ML or hardware-in-the-loop systems.
- Experience with AI/ML systems such as foundation models, multimodal models, or Vision-Language-Action models.
- Ability to drive ambiguous, zero-to-one initiatives in a fast-paced, research-driven environment, and to align research, engineering, and partner teams.

About the job
As a Product Manager in GDM Robotics, you will work at the intersection of frontier research, physical systems, and developer platforms. You will turn hard research and hardware constraints into clear product strategy and roadmaps, partner closely with research scientists and engineers, and get our models into real-world use with internal teams and external partners.

Google's mission is to organize the world's information and make it universally accessible and useful. Our Product Management team combines technical knowledge with excellent business judgment to drive strategy and communicate the voice of the user to shape and build some of the most innovative products in the world. As a Product Manager at DeepMind, you will help define product strategy and bring Google's AI and robotics research into real-world products.
