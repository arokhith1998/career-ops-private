## Role overview
Sits in the Future Forward group, Intuitive's advanced concepts team. Takes product concepts from early exploration through initial market introduction, in areas where problem and path to market are still undefined.

## Responsibilities
- Build clinical expertise in new areas through literature, medical society meetings, KOL relationships
- Define market and product requirements, including user needs and personas
- Run qualitative and quantitative research and direct user observation
- Align engineering, design, manufacturing, and other stakeholders
- Build the product roadmap and business case: market sizing, pricing, margins, investment
- Work with Clinical Affairs, Market Access, and Regulatory on evidence, reimbursement, path to market
- Plan go-to-market strategy and hand off to commercial teams
