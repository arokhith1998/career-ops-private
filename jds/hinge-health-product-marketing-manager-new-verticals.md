## Role Summary
Reports to Director of Product Marketing, Digital Care. Leads product marketing for new
vertical expansions: go-to-market strategy and execution, positioning, launch planning,
messaging, live enablement. Works with Health Services on health-plan approvals and
Client Success on client adoption. Opening line: "Help expand Hinge Health access by
turning complex products into clear stories."

## Responsibilities
- Build product expertise quickly (strengths, differentiators, messaging).
- Create/improve slides, messaging, FAQs, objection-handling resources for health-plan
  approvals and client conversations.
- Lead enablement sessions; maintain useful resources.
- Own product marketing for new vertical expansion, prioritizing approval needs.
- Drive client readiness/adoption through launch comms and live enablement.
- Define adoption measures with Client Success; use feedback/performance data to refine
  messaging.
- Lead GTM planning/execution aligned with product roadmap.
- Give leadership concise updates on progress, risks, tradeoffs, decisions needed.
- Year-one goals: expand availability across book of business, track adoption separately
  from approval coverage.

## Basic Qualifications
- Experience independently owning product positioning, GTM launches, or adoption
  initiatives with measurable outcomes.
- Ability to create clear messaging/presentation materials and deliver live enablement.
- Experience leading cross-functional work, influencing partners without direct authority.

## Preferred Qualifications
- Product marketing in healthcare, digital health, or another complex, evidence-based
  category.
- Work with benefit managers, health plans, or healthcare services teams.
- Enabling sales/client success/partnership teams to drive adoption.
- Translating clinical/technical info into business-relevant positioning.
- Measuring adoption; using customer feedback to improve GTM execution.
