## Description (full)

About Anthropic: mission is to create reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems.

About the Role: The Strategic Planning team within Anthropic's go-to-market organization decides where to invest, turns those decisions into a plan, and keeps the plan working through the year. This role owns the account side of GTM planning: territories, the data enrichment and system requirements behind them, rules of engagement (RoEs), and the account moves that follow during the year.

Key Responsibilities:
- Build and maintain territory models and methods
- Partner on segmentation definitions and turn them into account rules and criteria
- Carry territories from design through business validation, QA, and go-live
- Own data enrichment and system requirements for territories and accounts, with Systems and Data teams
- Run account moves and RoEs through the year, and turn one-off requests into a repeatable process
- Identify patterns in post-launch breakage and feed them into the next plan's design
- Build tools and automations with Claude to reduce manual work for the Strategy & Operations community

Qualifications:
- Alignment with Anthropic's mission
- Direct experience with territory design
- Hands-on experience in sales planning, GTM strategy, revenue operations, or a similar analytical role
- Advanced spreadsheet modeling skills
- Experience with CRM data such as Salesforce (accounts, ownership, hierarchies)
- Ability to identify key model assumptions and explain their downstream effects
- Experience running multi-stakeholder processes to fixed deadlines
- Ability to influence field leaders and partner teams without direct authority
- Clear written and verbal communication for field and leadership audiences
- Hands-on use of AI tools such as Claude

Strong candidates may also have: experience planning at a company scaling its field team quickly; experience enriching CRM data or writing systems requirements; experience with AI/ML companies or consumption business models.

Other: location-based hybrid policy requiring staff in office at least 25% of the time. Benefits include equity donation matching, generous vacation and parental leave, flexible hours.
