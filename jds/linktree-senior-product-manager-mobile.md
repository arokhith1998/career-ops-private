## Full JD Text

The Role

As Senior Mobile Product Manager, you’ll own the strategy and roadmap for Linktree’s native iOS and Android apps — the experience tens of millions of creators and businesses use to manage their digital presence on the go. You’ll move mobile beyond feature parity with web and grow its contribution to the business.

As a member of the Product team, you’ll work hand-in-hand with the mobile designer and engineering team to develop a mobile strategy outside of performance and feature-parity work — accelerating mobile’s contribution to the 2026 financial model through thoughtful, mobile-native product bets.

Most of all, you’ll help Linktree achieve our mission: to empower anyone to curate, grow, and monetize their digital universe — making sure the mobile experience is one creators love to come back to.

What You’ll Own

Own the strategy and multi-quarter roadmap for Linktree’s native mobile apps (iOS and Android).
Define the mobile metrics that matter and hold the team accountable to moving them.
Partner closely with the mobile designer and engineering team to ship high-quality, mobile-native experiences.
Run an experimentation cadence — forming hypotheses, designing tests, and turning results into shipped improvements.
Translate user research and behavioural data into a prioritised, defensible mobile roadmap.
Develop a mobile strategy beyond feature parity that grows mobile’s contribution to the business.
Influence senior stakeholders and adjacent teams to align on mobile priorities.

Who We’re Looking For

4–5+ years of product management experience, including hands-on ownership of native mobile (iOS/Android) products.
A track record of shipping mobile features that moved meaningful business or engagement metrics.
Strong data fluency — comfortable with funnels, cohort analysis and A/B testing, and able to separate signal from noise.
Deep user empathy and the ability to translate research and behaviour into a prioritised mobile roadmap.
You partner effectively with engineering, design, data and marketing, and influence without formal authority.
You thrive in a fast-paced, agile environment and use build-measure-learn cycles to ship and iterate.
You can work from our Los Angeles or San Francisco office on a hybrid basis.

Linktree is committed to providing a fair and competitive compensation package including cash, equity, and benefits. The base salary offered for this role is targeted at $180,000 - $210,000 for roles based in San Francisco and Los Angeles. Our Story

We’re on a mission to empower anyone to curate, grow, and monetize their digital universe. We created the “link in bio” category — and we’re just getting started. From creators, artists, and entrepreneurs to global brands, we’re redefining how people connect with their communities, showcase their content and promote products — all in one link.

Today, a community of 70+ million use Linktree, including icons like Adidas, TikTok, the UN Environmental Program, The NFL, Manchester United, World Central Kitchen, Sabrina Carpenter, Olivia Rodrigo, and Selena Gomez. With 48,000 new accounts created every day, Linktree is the fastest-growing leader in our space.

We’ve teamed up with some of the world’s biggest platforms including TikTok, Snap, YouTube, GoFundMe, Spotify, Google, Stripe, Reddit, Laylo, and Kajabi, to unify Linkers’ digital spaces, and we partner with retail brands Amazon, Lululemon, Nike, Target, Sephora, Lululemon, and more to share products they love.

We’re a tight-knit, passionate team building best-in-class tools that make online presence simple, powerful, and yours. If you're excited by the idea of shaping a new category, setting the standard, and making space for everyone to be seen and succeed, we’d love to meet you.

Where and How We Work

We're a global, diverse team spread across continents with offices in London, Los Angeles, Melbourne, and San Francisco. We work together flexibly and you can choose the setup that works best for you: fully remote or a hybrid mix of office and home.

We offer autonomy in how you structure your days and weeks. While we’re mindful of time zones and occasionally collaborate outside the typical 9–5, we lean into async work to keep things flowing. Our culture is family-friendly and Zoom-casual where kids, pets, and delivery drivers are welcome.
