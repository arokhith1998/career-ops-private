Role overview: This is a pre-sales, Deal Desk role supporting sales teams with accurate, timely price quotes for XTIUM's core services. The role also covers opportunity coordination, process documentation, and RFx history management.

Responsibilities:
- Build detailed, accurate price quotes for core services by reviewing Salesforce opportunities and pricing requests.
- Apply established service and pricing guidelines to craft deal strategies that fit customer needs.
- Lead cross-functional work with sales, operations, and technical teams to design and validate solutions.
- Maintain Deal Desk documents in SharePoint and Salesforce, including cost models, proposals, SOWs, and templates, keeping them organized and accessible.
- Work directly with customers and sales teams to qualify, scope, design, and present tailored opportunities.
- Coordinate solution reviews and share win strategies with Solution Sales Executives.
- Help prepare and analyze sales data for executive meetings, supporting the Deal Desk Lead.
- Monitor and support KPIs for response timeliness and team efficiency.
- Keep SOWs, proposals, and RFP responses current and aligned with XTIUM's offerings.
- Join internal customer onboarding meetings to share solution insights and support project handoffs.

Qualifications:
- "Bachelor's degree in Information Technology, Computer Science, Engineering, Business, or a related field (or equivalent work experience)."
- 1-3 years in sales engineering, technical sales, pre-sales, or a similar technical support role in IT managed services.
- Technical knowledge of core service areas: cloud (DaaS, IaaS, Azure), unified communications (VoIP), contact center as a service (CCaaS), managed network services, and managed security services (MDR, XDR, EDR).
- Proficiency with Salesforce or similar CRM systems.
- Understanding of pricing models, solution design, and the sales process in a technical setting.
- Strong communication skills for explaining technical solutions in business terms.
- Detail-oriented, organized, and able to manage multiple projects and deadlines in a fast-paced environment.
- Experience working with cross-functional teams to develop and present solutions.
- Proficiency with Microsoft Office (Word, Excel, PowerPoint) and Teams and SharePoint.
- Industry certifications such as Microsoft Azure, Cisco CCNA, Fortinet NSE, or Palo Alto Networks PCNSA are a plus.

Compensation: $75,000-$95,000 per year. Pay may fall outside this range based on qualifications, experience, and location. Benefits include medical, dental, FSA, 401(k), and flexible paid time off.
