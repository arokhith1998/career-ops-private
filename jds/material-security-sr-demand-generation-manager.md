Most security marketing is noise: fear statistics, buzzword bingo, and a webinar nobody asked for. Security buyers learned to tune it out years ago. Material Security is looking for a Sr Demand Generation Manager who has marketed to them anyway and has the pipeline to show for it.

Material Security protects the email, files, and accounts inside Google Workspace and Microsoft 365. Email security used to mean a gateway at the front door. The work that matters now happens inside the workspace, where the sensitive data and the access already live. That's why we're building the category of workspace security.

As our Senior Demand Generation Manager, you'll own the programs that create and capture demand: paid, web conversion, sponsorships, nurture, and whatever you test next. You'll report to the VP of Marketing and work daily with product marketing, marketing operations, sales, and our channel sales leaders.

This is a builder's role. You'll plan the program, write the copy, launch it, and read the numbers yourself. You'll get a real budget, direct access to sales and leadership, and room to change how we go to market.

Responsibilities
- Own the demand generation pipeline target and the program budget behind it, and decide each month where the next dollar goes.
- Run paid programs end to end across LinkedIn, Google, and Reddit: targeting, copy, creative briefs, and analysis.
- Turn website traffic into demo requests through structured testing on our homepage and landing pages.
- Manage sponsorships with security publications, newsletters, and communities.
- Build lead capture and nurture programs in HubSpot that teach before they pitch.
- Work with sales on lead routing, follow-up, and feedback so good leads get worked fast and bad ones get fixed at the source.
- Run partner marketing with our channel sales leaders and the partner marketing counterparts at our VARs: joint campaigns, co-sponsored events, and through-partner programs that source pipeline.
- Test new channels and tactics with a crawl, walk, run plan and a kill threshold set before launch.
- Report every week on what's working, what isn't, and what you're changing.

What We're Looking For
- Demand generation experience at a cybersecurity company or a close neighbor such as GRC, identity, or IT security. This one is required. You know how security and IT buyers evaluate vendors, where they spend their attention, and what they ignore.
- 6+ years in demand generation or growth marketing at B2B SaaS companies, including time at a startup of fewer than 300 people where you did the work hands-on.
- A track record of owning a pipeline or qualified-opportunity target and hitting it.
- Deep, recent, hands-on experience building and managing paid campaigns, not only directing an agency.
- Fluency in HubSpot, Google Analytics, and Clay.
- Comfort in the data. You can pull a funnel apart in a spreadsheet, find the broken stage, and act on it the same week.
- A history of close work with sales, including the uncomfortable conversations about lead quality.
- Nice to have: experience marketing to SMB and mid-market companies, especially on Google Workspace.
- Nice to have: experience building signal-based or AI-assisted workflows for targeting and follow-up.
- Nice to have: experience running partner or channel marketing programs with VARs or resellers.
