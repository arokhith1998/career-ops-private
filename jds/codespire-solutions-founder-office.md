We’re Hiring: Founder’s Office – Executive Assistant & Group Project Manager

Company: Dynatech Controls Pvt. Ltd.

Location: Greater Noida / Noida (on-site)

Experience: 8–12 years

Reporting to: CEO / Founder

Employment: Full-time

Dynatech Controls is looking for a high-trust, execution-focused professional to work directly with our CEO across Dynatech and selected group ventures. This role is the CEO’s control tower: making sure every priority is captured, assigned, tracked and closed.

What you’ll do

- Run the CEO office: calendar, meetings, briefs, minutes and a daily action tracker, with follow-up to closure.
- Maintain group-wide dashboards for projects, RFQs, customer commitments, KPIs and risks, and run structured weekly and monthly business reviews.
- Coordinate manufacturing projects across Production, Quality, NPD, Engineering, Purchase, Planning and Dispatch, including shop-floor visits when needed.
- Own RFQ, costing and quotation follow-up: drawings, specifications, feasibility, costing inputs and turnaround SLAs.
- Track NPD, samples and PPAP milestones through to customer approval.
- Coordinate with customers and suppliers on RFQs, pricing calls, 8D/CAPA actions and delivery commitments.
- Prepare executive review packs and KPI dashboards for group businesses.
- Build SOPs and trackers, support critical hiring, and use AI tools (ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot) to improve productivity.
-

What we’re looking for

- B.E./B.Tech (mandatory); MBA or PMP preferred.
- 8–12 years in manufacturing projects, PMO, CEO/MD office, NPD, customer programs or business operations.
- Hands-on understanding of manufacturing, engineering drawings, RFQs, costing, production, quality and NPD.
- Strong Excel, PowerPoint and MIS skills.
- Excellent English communication and confidence dealing with domestic and international customers.
- Assertive internally, diplomatic externally, and persistent until closure.
- Comfortable with a fast-paced founder’s office environment and travel within India.
-

Why join

- Work directly with the CEO on decisions that shape the business.
- Exposure across manufacturing, technology and new ventures within the group.
- A clear leadership path: Head of Founder’s Office, Chief of Staff, Business Operations leadership, or a business leadership role within the group.
-

How to apply: Send your resume to hr@codespiresolutions.com with the subject line “Founder’s Office – EA & Group PM”.
