## Role Summary
Senior Growth Marketer to engage, grow, and nurture Handshake AI's expert network. Multi-channel growth: paid + SEO + lifecycle + community. Bridge academia and industry. Partner with product, ops, and engineering.

## Responsibilities
- Develop and implement growth strategies to expand the AI research community of graduates.
- Create and execute multi-channel marketing campaigns, including content, social, email, paid.
- Partner with internal teams on targeted outreach, university partnerships, and academic collaborations.
- Optimize digital marketing strategies using A/B testing, analytics, and performance tracking.
- Manage and grow presence on LinkedIn, Twitter, research-focused forums.
- Manage and optimize lifecycle marketing flows to nurture and retain community members.

## Requirements

**Must-have:**
- 6+ years growth/performance marketing, preferably to technical or academic audiences.
- Strong digital marketing channel understanding (SEO + paid acquisition - LinkedIn, Meta, Google).
- Efficiently managed at least a 7-figure budget across a minimum of 2 paid marketing channels (e.g., Meta and Google ads).
- Proficiency in analytics tools such as Google Analytics and in SQL.

- Ability to work independently and thrive in a fast-paced, remote environment.
- Passion for AI research and fostering academic communities is a plus.

**Bonus:**
