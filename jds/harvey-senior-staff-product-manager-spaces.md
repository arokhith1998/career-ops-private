## Full description (verbatim via Ashby posting-api, HTML stripped)
WHY HARVEY
At Harvey, we're transforming how legal and professional services operate, combining frontier agentic AI, an enterprise-grade platform, and deep domain expertise.

ROLE OVERVIEW
Spaces bring people, context, and agents together in one place, helping legal teams organize their work, collaborate securely, and use Harvey across the lifecycle of a matter or project. We're looking for a Senior Staff Product Manager to own the core Spaces experience — how users create/navigate Spaces, bring in context, run work, and return to Spaces as an ongoing part of their workflow. Senior individual-contributor role.

WHAT YOU'LL DO
- Own the core Spaces experience: vision, strategy, roadmap.
- Simplify complex workflows: intuitive experiences/mental models across navigation, information architecture, resources, activity, agents.
- Drive product quality with Design/Engineering.
- Grow adoption: onboarding, activation, entry points, defaults, repeat usage; establish success metrics.
- Lead product development end to end: discovery through launch, measurement, iteration.
- Stay close to customers: law firms, in-house legal teams, end users.
- Work across Harvey: partner with adjacent product teams.

WHAT YOU HAVE
- "5+ years of product management experience, including meaningful ownership of a major user-facing product or core product experience."
- Track record building excellent B2B SaaS, productivity, workflow, collaboration, or knowledge-management products.
- Strong product/design judgment simplifying complex workflows/information architecture.
- Experience taking products through early definition and subsequent scaling/adoption/refinement.
- Strong growth instincts (activation, onboarding, engagement, retention).
- High agency in fast-moving, ambiguous environments.
- Deep customer orientation; strong cross-functional leadership (Engineering, Design, GTM).

PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS
Experience building multi-user workspaces/sharing-and-collaboration products; enterprise complexity (user types, permissions, governance, sensitive data); AI-powered products/agent workflows; high-growth technology company experience.
