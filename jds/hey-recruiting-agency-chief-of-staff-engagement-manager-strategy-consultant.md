Chief Of Staff- Engagement Manager / Senior Strategy Consultant | Ex-MBB | US- Remote

Company Overview

A fast-growing US-based business seeking exceptional strategy talent to drive critical business priorities, strategic initiatives and commercial outcomes while working closely with senior leadership.

About the Role

We are looking for exceptional former consultants from McKinsey, BCG or Bain for a high-impact strategic role.

This opportunity is specifically targeted at consultants who have demonstrated strong performance and received at least one promotion during their MBB tenure, ideally progressing to Engagement Manager, Project Leader or equivalent.

Key Responsibilities

- Lead complex strategic workstreams and solve high-impact business challenges.
- Drive quantitative analysis, business performance assessments and commercial strategy.
- Develop actionable recommendations and translate them into measurable outcomes.
- Engage with senior leadership and influence critical business decisions.
- Identify growth opportunities and drive strategic initiatives.

What We're Looking For

- Former/Current MBB consultant.
- At least one promotion during MBB tenure (mandatory).
- Ideally Engagement Manager / Project Leader or equivalent.
- Typically 4–8 years of overall experience.
- Exceptional analytical, problem-solving and commercial judgement.
- Proven workstream leadership and senior stakeholder management.
- Strong executive communication and ownership mindset.

Industry experience is flexible. Consulting pedigree, career progression and demonstrated performance take priority.

Hiring Focus: A highly selective search for exceptional former MBB consultants. We are prioritising quality and proven performance over application volume.
