At ACT, Your Work Makes a Difference

ACT's mission is to guide every learner, along every pathway, every time. Now part of ETS, ACT is entering its next chapter of growth, and we are hiring a Growth Marketing Manager to lead it on the consumer side of our business, where millions of students come to us directly as they navigate college and career pathways.

Partnering with the VP of Growth Marketing, you will own the full-funnel growth strategy and build the engine that delivers it. This is a lean team, which makes the role as much builder as leader: you will set direction for a small group of specialists and develop them, and you will do a real share of the work yourself. We are looking for a genuine expert with a track record of building from the ground up.

We want our team members to have the well-being and confidence they need to do their best work, in an environment where both they and ACT thrive. To support this, our total rewards include company paid life insurance, medical, dental, vision, flexible health and dependent spending accounts, 401(k) retirement savings with company match, paid holidays, paid time off, and so much more. You can find a comprehensive list of our benefits here.

The finalist will be hired at the salary and level commensurate with their qualifications. For this position, we anticipate offering an annual salary between $130,000 to $150,000. ACT manages salaries within this range based on relevant factors, including skills, experience, and internal equity. This position is not incentive-eligible.

Location: This is a remote position, based in the United States.
Visa: Applicants must be authorized to work in the U.S. without the need for visa sponsorship.

What You Will Be Working On
- Full-funnel strategy: Own growth end to end, from acquisition through onboarding, and expand lifetime value through cross-sell and upsell of ancillary products. Bring new channels and growth motions into the mix as the business scales
- Brand and creative: Partner with brand and creative to sharpen how ACT shows up, from positioning and messaging to look and feel, so the work earns trust with students and parents rather than just attention
- Paid acquisition: Define and execute the paid strategy, setting budget allocation and channel mix across paid search, paid social, display, video and whatever comes next, actively managing performance day to day and bringing new ideas to the growth engine, carrying the volume and efficiency targets and answering for what it costs us to acquire a customer
- Lifecycle and CRM: Own the lifecycle strategy end to end, working with our CRM team to design and run the programs that turn interest into a first purchase and a first purchase into the next one
- Experimentation: Build the testing program across paid, web, lifecycle and SEO/AEO. Decide what to test and in what order, ship it, and make sure every result becomes a learning the next test builds on
- Measurement: Work with our media agency to sharpen the media mix model and run incrementality tests, then reconcile that against in-platform results. Know where the marginal CPA curve bends in each channel, and move budget accordingly
- AI-native capability: Make AI part of how the team operates rather than a side project, using it to move faster and to raise the quality of the work
- One team: Partner with the B2B school-day team to share knowledge, assets and learnings, so consumer and school-day growth reinforce each other

This could be the job for you if you have (minimum requirements):
- At least 7 years' experience in growth, performance or consumer digital marketing, including time leading a team
- Deep, hands-on expertise across paid search, paid social, display and video. You have run these channels yourself, not only through an agency
- Proven experience managing a large paid media budget: you know how to allocate spend across channels, pace it, and hold efficiency targets as spend scales
- Fluent in measurement: you partner with agencies to design incrementality tests and sharpen media mix models, and you can explain why platform-reported and incremental numbers disagree
- Fluent with AI tools, and clear-eyed about where they help and where they do not
- Entrepreneurial and a mentor to the team: you create a high-performing team that is excited about the work, and you develop the people on it
- A strong communicator who can make a case, land it with different audiences, and get people moving
- Bachelor's degree in Marketing, Business Administration, Communications, or a related field, or an equivalent combination of education and experience from which comparable knowledge and abilities can be acquired.

It's a Plus If You Have
- Consumer or B2C marketing experience
- You have built a growth function and designed flywheels that compound
- Familiarity with HubSpot or a comparable marketing automation platform
- East Coast-based candidates preferred to support regular in-person meetings with key stakeholders

About ACT

When ACT was founded in 1959, it disrupted the assessment industry with a new approach to helping students better understand their readiness so they could take steps to improve it. By leveraging our expertise and authority in assessment and research, we will again disrupt the industry - helping more people learn, better measure their progress, and improve their navigation through life's transitions.

More than ever before, ACT is fulfilling its mission of helping people achieve education and workplace success. We're doing it by pushing the boundaries of learning innovation through the work of our people, who we call team members because we're all in this together.

We know transformation does not come without challenge. That's why ACT invests in a variety of experiences for team members to strengthen their connections, explore ideas, learn from customers, and celebrate success.
