**About the Team**

Customer Education helps customers build the practical skills and confidence to use AI and OpenAI products safely and effectively. We bring together customer insight, learning and enablement expertise, product signals, and measurement to help organizations move from access to durable adoption.

We are exploring how learning, practice, assessment, and evidence of capability can become part of the product experience for people using ChatGPT, Codex, and other OpenAI products at work. Our work focuses on helping people build useful AI skills in the flow of work while giving organizations a clearer view of progress, capability, and readiness.

**About the Role**

As Product Lead, Native Learning & Credentials, you will define and drive a new product area at the intersection of AI, enterprise learning, assessment, and safe and beneficial use.

This is a senior individual-contributor role for someone who is at their best close to the work. You will take a broad customer and business opportunity and make it concrete: identify the first problem worth solving, define the learner and enterprise journeys, sequence an initial product, and lead the work through engineering alignment, launch, measurement, and iteration. You will work with a small, cross-functional group.

We're looking for people who combine product depth with GM-level ownership. You should be comfortable moving between customer needs, product strategy, technical architecture, operating design, evidence standards, and business outcomes. You will partner closely with engineering, design, program, learning, and assessment experts, bringing the judgment to make sound tradeoffs while respecting their domain expertise.

The area is early, and the path is not predetermined. Success will come from simplifying ambiguity, shipping focused product proofs, and using evidence to guide investment. If the area earns sustained investment, this role can grow into broader leadership of the product and program over time.

This role is based in San Francisco, CA. We use a hybrid work model of 3 days in the office per week and offer relocation assistance to new employees.

**In this role, you will:**
- Define the customer, learner, and business problem for Native Learning and Credentials, starting with enterprise adoption and workforce capability.
- Own the product strategy and roadmap across learning, applied practice, assessment, credential decisions and issuance, learner state, and enterprise reporting.
- Personally own the product brief, user journeys, requirements, PRDs, roadmap, milestones, non-goals, and decision records that turn an ambiguous opportunity into an executable plan.
- Partner deeply with engineering and design to define the experience and technical foundations, make architecture and sequencing tradeoffs, and deliver high-quality products.
- Decide which capabilities OpenAI should build, buy, or access through partners, balancing speed, differentiation, trust, quality, and operational simplicity.
- Work with learning, assessment, legal, safety, privacy, security, and accessibility partners to establish credible evidence standards and a trustworthy learner experience. Ensure that credential claims are supported by reliable evidence of capability.
- Establish instrumentation and evaluation across product quality, learner progress, application to real work, adoption, and enterprise value. Use these measures to guide iteration and investment.
- Create a coherent experience across OpenAI product surfaces and relevant learning, credential, and partner systems, with clear system boundaries and ownership.
- Remain accountable beyond launch, working with data, support, and operations partners to improve the product, refine the business model, and develop a resource plan grounded in evidence.
