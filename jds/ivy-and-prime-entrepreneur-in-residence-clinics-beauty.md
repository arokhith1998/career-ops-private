About Ivy & Prime
Ivy & Prime is a cross-border market-access and venture-building platform operating across health, beauty, wellness, retail and professional networks.
We help global and Asian brands reach Indian consumers through an integrated ecosystem of:
Airports | Clinics | Retail | E-commerce | Distribution | Professional Networks
We combine brand curation, consumer discovery, clinical and professional access, strategic partnerships and market-entry execution to help promising brands build visibility, credibility and sales in India.
Ivy & Prime is now building its Clinic Division - a network of modern Asian-inspired beauty, aesthetics and wellness destinations that bring together clinical expertise, beauty innovation, curated retail and experiential formats.
The initial concepts include hybrid models such as:
Clinic + Cafe and Lifestyle Experience

Role Overview
We are looking for a highly entrepreneurial Entrepreneur-in-Residence to work directly with the founding team and help build Ivy & Prime's Clinic Division from the ground up.
You will help convert the clinic concept into operating locations by identifying the right doctors, clinical partners, salon and spa operators, space owners, investors, brands and service providers.
This is not a conventional business-development or marketing position. It is a hands-on venture creation, partnerships and execution role.
You will be expected to move comfortably between strategy and field execution--from preparing a business model and evaluating a location to meeting doctors, developing partnership structures, coordinating a pilot launch and tracking commercial performance.

What You Will Take Ownership Of

1. Clinic Venture Development
Help develop and outline Ivy & Prime's clinic formats
Convert the overall vision into practical business plans, launch roadmaps and operating models
Research successful clinic, wellness and experiential-retail concepts
Identify the right combination of clinical services, beauty treatments, wellness experiences, retail products and customer journeys
Help determine which concepts should be tested first and how they can be scaled across multiple locations

2. Clinical and Strategic Partnerships
Identify and build relationships with dermatologists, aesthetic doctors, cosmetologists, nutritionists, wellness professionals and clinic operators
Develop partnerships with salon owners, spa operators, cafe entrepreneurs, commercial space owners and investors
Convert initial conversations into structured meetings, proposals, pilot agreements and long-term partnerships
Help define clear roles, responsibilities, investment commitments and commercial structures for each partner

3. Location and Pilot Development
Identify potential clinic and suitable-format locations across Delhi NCR
Evaluate locations based on catchment, customer profile, rent, accessibility, competition and revenue potential
Support the launch of the first one to two pilot locations
Identify problems early and take ownership of resolving them

4. Business Models and Unit Economics
Help prepare location-level business plans and financial projections
Estimate setup costs, operating expenses, staffing requirements, service capacity and revenue potential
Compare whether each pilot is meeting its commercial and operational targets

5. Service and Experience Development
Work with clinical partners to develop service menus, treatment pathways, memberships and customer packages

6. Operations, SOPs and Scale
Help establish operating procedures for appointments, consultations, treatments, product sales, customer follow-ups and partner coordination
Coordinate clinical staff, front-desk teams, therapists, consultants and external partners
Support recruitment, onboarding, training and performance tracking

7. Brand and Market-Access Integration
Connect the Clinic Division with Ivy & Prime's wider brand and market-access platform
Develop opportunities for brand activations, treatment integrations, launches and professional partnerships

8. Founder's Office Execution
Work directly with the founder on research, meetings, presentations, proposals and commercial negotiations
Maintain a structured pipeline of doctors, locations, investors, operators, brands and strategic partners
Track meetings, follow-ups, proposals, agreements, pilot milestones and revenue opportunities
Prepare concise decision notes and recommendations
Manage multiple priorities independently and ensure that important follow-ups do not get delayed
Take responsibility for execution--not merely coordination

What Success Looks Like
Success in this role will be measured by:
A strong pipeline of qualified doctors, clinic partners, location partners and operators
High-quality partnership meetings with relevant decision-makers
Partnership proposals converted into signed pilot arrangements
Suitable locations identified and evaluated
The first clinic or hybrid-format pilot launched successfully
Clear business models, budgets, SOPs and launch plans created
Clinical services and brand partnerships activated
Revenue generated through treatments, products, memberships and partnerships
A repeatable model developed for expansion into multiple locations
Founder's Office priorities completed accurately and on time
