The Basics: The Senior Manager of Sales Operations is the operational right hand to Tanium's velocity leader and the sales operations partner for the U.S. mid-market and commercial sales teams. This leader builds and runs the operating engine for down market and product-led motions: the funnel from first visit to trial to paid to revenue, lead routing, forecasting, segmentation and coverage, compensation mechanics, and the data and systems that tie them together.

This position follows the Company's hybrid schedule which currently requires employees to work in the office at one of the following locations a minimum of three days per week: Addison, TX; Bellevue, WA; Durham, NC; Emeryville, CA; or Reston, VA.

What You'll Do: Down Market Operations & Funnel Design; Forecast & Pipeline Management; Planning, Segmentation & Coverage; Compensation, Deal Support & Governance; Systems, Data & Automation; Cross-Functional GTM Partnership. (Full detail: translating the down market plan into sales operations, owning a single funnel view from website visit to trial to paid to revenue, driving the forecast process for mid-market and commercial, applying global account segmentation and territory design, partnering on incentive mechanics, owning sales operations requirements across Salesforce/billing/BI tools.)

Work Requirements: Travel up to 15%, primarily within the United States. Standard business hours with flexibility for global and cross-time-zone meetings.

Required: 7+ years in sales operations, sales strategy, revenue operations, or GTM-focused consulting, primarily in B2B software. Direct experience supporting mid-market, commercial, SMB, or high-volume sales organizations. Hands-on fluency with Salesforce and forecasting platforms such as Gong or Clari. Advanced Excel and modeling skills, experience with a BI tool such as PowerBI. Experience in cybersecurity or IT infrastructure software. Bachelor's degree in Business, Economics, Finance, or a related field; MBA a plus.

Preferred: 9+ years of experience. Experience standing up or scaling a new go-to-market motion. Experience with direct online purchase flows and billing systems. Experience deploying AI tooling and automation.

What You'll Get: The annual base salary range for this full-time position is $165,000 to $253,000, plus equity awards and benefits.
