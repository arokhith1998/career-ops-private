## Description
This position is listed on behalf of a partner company, which manages all applications and next steps. Our partner is looking for a Head of Growth Marketing based in India. The Head of Growth Marketing will lead customer acquisition, retention, and reactivation strategies for a digital storefront offering in-game currencies and digital goods across Africa and the Middle East. This hands-on leadership role combines strategic ownership of paid acquisition budgets with the development of high-impact CRM and lifecycle marketing programs. You will oversee performance across multiple channels, optimize customer acquisition costs and returns, and drive sustainable revenue growth through data-informed decisions. Working closely with Product, Data, creative specialists, and market teams, you will turn commercial priorities into effective campaigns and measurable results. You will also build and develop a high-performing team while establishing a culture of continuous experimentation and creative optimization. This is an exciting opportunity for a commercially minded growth leader who thrives in a fast-paced, remote-first environment and enjoys balancing strategic direction with hands-on execution.

**Accountabilities:**
- Own paid user acquisition across Meta, Google, TikTok, Snapchat, and relevant advertising networks, managing budgets and performance against customer acquisition cost (CAC), return on ad spend (ROAS), and payback targets
- Develop and execute comprehensive CRM and lifecycle marketing strategies covering customer segmentation, onboarding, repeat purchases, cross-selling, win-back campaigns, and re-engagement
- Manage lifecycle communications across push notifications, email, SMS, and WhatsApp to improve customer engagement, retention, and lifetime value
- Lead app store optimization initiatives and improve conversion rates from storefront visits to first purchases
- Allocate and continuously optimize marketing budgets across channels, markets, and game titles based on unit economics and evolving campaign performance
- Collaborate with the Data Team to establish a reliable view of acquisition and lifecycle performance, including attribution, incrementality testing, and campaign effectiveness
- Establish a high-tempo experimentation program across advertising channels, target audiences, promotional offers, messaging, and creative formats
- Lead and develop paid acquisition and creative specialists, setting clear objectives, creative briefing standards, and structured testing processes
- Partner with market and game-title owners, Product, and Data teams to translate commercial objectives into campaigns that can be executed efficiently and measured accurately
- Monitor performance metrics, identify growth opportunities, and rapidly adjust strategies to maximize profitability and sustainable customer growth
- Balance long-term growth planning with hands-on execution, including campaign launches, audience development, creative briefs, and performance analysis

**Requirements:**
- At least 6 years of experience in growth marketing or performance marketing within gaming, e-commerce, consumer applications, or a similar digital business
- Demonstrated success managing paid user acquisition at scale and building CRM or lifecycle marketing programs, with measurable results across CAC, ROAS, retention, and reactivation
- Ability to translate quarterly growth strategies into practical execution, moving comfortably between high-level planning and hands-on campaign management
- Strong analytical skills and experience using mobile measurement partners (MMPs), such as AppsFlyer or Adjust, alongside analytics and performance reporting tools
- Proven ability to design, evaluate, and interpret experiments, understand attribution and incrementality, and make decisions based on unit economics
- Strong creative judgment and an understanding of performance marketing creative, including the ability to develop briefs and establish effective creative testing processes
- A high degree of ownership, commercial awareness, and accountability for marketing performance and business outcomes
- Ability to thrive in a fast-moving, growth-stage environment and collaborate effectively with distributed, cross-functional teams
- At least 2 years of team management experience is preferred
- Experience developing direct-to-consumer strategies for a gaming publisher, particularly in webshop or digital storefront environments, is an advantage
- Familiarity with African markets, the Middle East, or other emerging markets is considered a plus

**Benefits:** Company equity; remote work allowance of $600/year; equipment provided; learning and development budget; paid birthday leave; international team; significant ownership of growth strategy.
