From Fivetran's founding until now, our mission has remained the same: to make access to data as simple and reliable as electricity. With Fivetran, customer data arrives in their warehouses, canonical and ready to query, with no engineering or maintenance required.

About Us: Fivetran and dbt Labs are bringing together two industry-leading companies with a shared mission: helping organizations unlock the full value of their data.

Skills We're Looking For: Experience using Salesforce, Salesforce CPQ required; experience with a high volume of transactions; proven success in SaaS/B2B Industry, Deal Desk, Sales Operations and/or Order Management; prior Deal Desk, Sales Operations or Order Management experience required; ability to work extended hours at end of month/quarter; experience with Ironclad or comparable CLM software preferable.

Pay Range: CRC 20,825,000 - 25,000,000.

Perks and Benefits: 100% employer-paid medical insurance; generous paid time away from work; RSU stock grants; professional development; monthly cell phone stipend; mental health support platform.
