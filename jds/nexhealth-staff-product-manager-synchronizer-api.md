About NexHealth

Our healthcare system remains frustratingly analog. When you live in a world of one-tap car rides, instant meal delivery, and unlimited streaming, why do you still have to call to schedule a doctor’s appointment and fill out a clipboard in the waiting room?

NexHealth’s mission is to accelerate innovation in healthcare by connecting patients, providers, and developers. We’re building the infrastructure layer for modern healthcare, connecting thousands of fragmented, on-premise, and closed EHR systems into a single, modern platform that powers software, APIs, payments, and patient experiences across the ecosystem.

- Founded: 2017

- Headquarters: San Francisco, CA

About The Role

Synchronizer is our unified API platform. One integration gives developers real-time read and write access to patient records, scheduling, insurance eligibility, ledger payments, forms, and clinical data across 15+ PMS platforms. It powers hundreds of healthtech companies across 10,000+ connected practice locations.

Synchronizer is mission-critical infrastructure for our customers. That makes reliability, data fidelity, and developer experience the product. We're looking for this role to own the Synchronizer: its strategy, roadmap, and outcomes, from the sync engine that talks to each PMS to the endpoints, webhooks, docs, and sandbox developers build on. You'll influence which systems and data models we support next, how we meet enterprise-grade reliability expectations, and how we make integration fast for the next thousand developers.

Your north star is product excellence: a platform developers trust enough to build their business on. You'll work closely with a dedicated engineering team, our integrations and data teams, and the Sales, Solutions, Partnerships, and Support teams who serve API customers every day.

What You’ll Do

- Own the Synchronizer strategy and roadmap. Set the multi-year vision for our unified API and decide what to build across new PMS integrations, data domains, endpoints, and platform capabilities.

- Design and build exceptional end-to-end user experiences—from backend architecture to delivery mechanisms and UI layers.

- Partner with Design and Engineering through tight, fast build cycles.

- Make reliability and data fidelity first-class product features. Define and own platform metrics such as sync latency, write-back success rate, uptime, and error rates. Work with Engineering on SLAs, observability, and incident learnings that enterprise customers can rely on.

- Embed with Sales, Customer Success, and GTM teams to support launch, pricing and packaging, adoption, and feedback loops.

- Partner with integrations and partnerships teams to prioritize new systems and deepen coverage on existing ones, balancing technical constraints, vendor relationships, and customer demand.

- Make HIPAA, data privacy, and security requirements part of how the product is designed, not an afterthought.

What Success Looks Like

- Systems are simple, elegant, and scale as the business grows.
