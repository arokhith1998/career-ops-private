## Description
Responsible for analysis of market trends, supplier performance, internal developments, and objectives to support Arrow's adder and quoting strategy. Facilitates interaction/feedback between segment managers, product specialists, and sales management. Monitors and reports supplier market price performance; prepares negotiation material and supports the negotiation process with technology business managers and product specialists. Handles strategic customer quotes and associated supplier strategies, and communicates with suppliers on customer/quote priority. Reports performance metrics specific to market price, segmented by supplier and selling company.
