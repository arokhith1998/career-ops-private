About the Role
We’re looking for someone to work closely with the Founder and help manage the day-to-day execution of the business.
This is not a typical admin role and not a pure marketing role.
You’ll work across operations, marketing, campaigns, internal coordination and founder-level priorities. A big part of your job will be making sure things actually get done.
You should be comfortable taking ownership, following up with different teams, working with numbers and handling confidential business information.
What You’ll DoFounder’s Office & Operations
Work directly with the Founder on day-to-day priorities and execution.
Keep track of important tasks, deadlines and follow-ups.
Coordinate with internal teams and make sure work moves forward on time.
Prepare meeting notes, action points, reports and presentations when required.
Follow up with vendors, agencies, partners and other external stakeholders.
Help with administrative and operational requirements of the Founder’s Office.
Maintain important documents, trackers and business information.
Handle confidential company and founder-level information responsibly.
Identify things that are getting delayed and proactively push them towards closure.
Marketing Operations
Coordinate ongoing marketing campaigns and launches.
Work with performance marketing, social media, content, design and video teams.
Understand Meta Ads and Google Ads performance.
Track important metrics such as ROAS, CAC, CTR, CPC, conversions, marketing spend and revenue.
Coordinate campaign creatives, offers, landing pages and timelines.
Support influencer, UGC and partnership campaigns.
Maintain marketing calendars and campaign trackers.
Review campaign performance and highlight what needs attention.
Coordinate with external marketing agencies where required.
Business & Reporting
Maintain daily and weekly business trackers.
Help prepare sales, marketing and operational reports.
Work comfortably with Google Sheets/Excel and basic business data.
Track pending work across different functions.
Help the Founder get a clear picture of what is happening across the business.
Support new projects and initiatives from planning through execution.
Who We’re Looking For
Someone who is:
Highly organized and dependable.
Comfortable working directly with a Founder.
Strong at follow-ups and getting work completed.
Able to handle multiple priorities without losing track.
Comfortable with numbers and basic business analysis.
Familiar with digital marketing and performance marketing.
Good with Excel/Google Sheets.
Clear and professional in written and verbal communication.
Comfortable taking responsibility instead of waiting for instructions at every step.
Discreet and responsible with confidential information.
Comfortable working in a fast-moving startup environment.
Experience in a D2C, e-commerce, consumer brand or startup will be an advantage.
What This Role Is Not
This is not a role where you will work on one fixed function every day.
Some days you may be reviewing a marketing campaign. Another day you may be coordinating with a business partner, preparing a report, following up with the team or helping the Founder execute a completely new initiative.
We need someone who enjoys that kind of ownership and variety.
Why Join Matri?
Matri is building a fast-growing women’s wellness brand.
You’ll work directly with the Founder and get exposure across marketing, operations, growth, partnerships and business strategy.
You won’t be watching the company being built from the sidelines. You’ll be part of the team building it.
