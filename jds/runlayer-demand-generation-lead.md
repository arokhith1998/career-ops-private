About Runlayer

AI is transforming how every company operates, but most enterprises are
stuck. They want to move fast with AI Agents, tools, and workflows, but they

Our team built AI Actions for OpenAI, shipped Zapier Agents to millions of

About The Role

As our Demand Gen Lead, you'll build Runlayer's demand generation engine
from zero - paid acquisition, inbound, email and lifecycle, ABM, and the
attribution infrastructure underneath it all. We run no paid today (all
inbound is organic), but we have the budget, the deal sizes, and the
pipeline pressure to make paid a real channel. This is a true zero-to-one
build: you'll be hands-on-keyboard as a player-coach first, then hire and
lead a team underneath you as the function scales.

Reports to the Head of Marketing. Remote-friendly (we have a NYC office on
Bond Street if you're local). 20% travel.

Why You'll Thrive Here

- Impact: Build the demand gen motion from scratch for the enterprise
  platform to run AI faster & safely
- Excellence: Work alongside founders and a senior GTM team closing Fortune
  500 deals through pure inbound
- Ownership: Own demand gen end-to-end - channel strategy, campaigns,
  landing pages, systems, and attribution - and grow a team beneath you

What You'll Do

- Build and run paid acquisition across Google, LinkedIn, and other B2B
  channels - starting from zero, experimenting fast, and scaling what works
- Own ABM strategy and execution against our target enterprise accounts
- Own landing page strategy and production for campaigns and paid traffic
- Own email and lifecycle marketing: nurture programs, newsletters, and
  re-engagement of our existing database
- Drive SEO and AEO as AI search becomes a core lead source
- Build the marketing systems foundation hands-on - campaign tracking, lead
  capture, UTM/attribution instrumentation, and reporting in HubSpot (we're
  building this from scratch; you won't inherit a stack)
- Define lead quality, routing, and handoff with SDRs and sales - and own
  pipeline reporting for marketing-sourced leads
- Run growth experiments across channels to find what scales, and kill what
  doesn't
- Partner with PMM on campaign messaging and with field marketing on
  digital amplification (conferences, partner campaigns, events)
- Hire and manage the demand gen team as the function grows

What We're Looking For

- 5-7 years in demand gen or growth marketing at B2B SaaS, infra, or
  developer tools companies - senior manager level, or director at a smaller
  company
- Experience in a sales-led, high-ACV enterprise motion (six-figure deals,
  long sales cycles) - this is not a PLG/self-serve role
- People management experience: you've managed at least 1-2 people and can
  build and lead a team, but you're comfortable operating solo as a
  player-coach first
- Proven track record building demand gen or paid acquisition programs from
  scratch - you've done the zero-to-one, not just optimized an inherited
  machine
- Hands-on operator, not a project manager: you set up your own campaigns,
  systems, and reporting rather than handing work off to marketing ops
- Strong paid ads chops across Google, LinkedIn, and emerging channels
- Data-driven: comfortable with attribution models, funnel analysis, and
  pipeline reporting, and able to build that instrumentation yourself
  (HubSpot experience a plus)
- Understanding of SEO and AEO; experience with tools like Profound is a plus
- Comfortable running experiments fast and killing what doesn't work
- Gritty and startup-tested: you've built in scrappy environments and know
  what it means when nothing exists yet

Bonus Qualifications

- Deep technical product background - you can learn the product yourself
  rather than relying on PMM for every brief
- AI-native, uses Claude
