Overview

Due to growth, we are adding an Analyst, Customer & Market Intelligence to our team.

The Analyst, Customer & Market Intelligence researches and analyzes customer, market, industry, product, and competitive developments to identify emerging trends, customer needs, and strategic opportunities. This position synthesizes internal customer insights and/or product sales/utilization data, with external research to develop evidence-based findings and actionable recommendations that inform Customer Experience (CX), Product, Business Development, Sales, and broader business strategies. The Analyst partners with cross-functional stakeholders to support strategic planning, customer research, competitive benchmarking, product go-to-market or brand positioning, and experience improvement initiatives.

Responsibilities

How do I make an impact on my team?
- Conduct market, industry, and competitive intelligence research to evaluate competitor capabilities, service offerings, customer experiences, business strategies, and market positioning.
- Monitor emerging industry trends, market developments, and competitive activity to identify potential implications, risks, and opportunities for Lumicera and its customers.
- Develop competitive and industry benchmarks to evaluate Lumicera's relative position, identify performance gaps, and inform strategic improvement opportunities.
- Research customer needs, expectations, behaviors, and emerging preferences, including how competitors and other organizations are responding to evolving customer demands.
- Synthesize findings from industry publications, market research, analyst reports, public company information, competitor communications, and other credible sources into clear, actionable research findings and recommendations.
- Integrate external market research with internal customer feedback and operational insights to identify patterns, evaluate emerging trends, and identify customer experience improvement opportunities.
- Conduct qualitative and quantitative customer and market research, including interviews, surveys, and secondary research, to address identified knowledge gaps and support business decisions.
- Translate research findings into strategic implications and evidence-based recommendations that inform CX strategy, customer journey improvements, product development, and business planning.
- Develop executive-ready intelligence briefs, research summaries, competitive assessments, and recurring market updates that communicate key findings, emerging developments, and recommended actions.
- Maintain competitive intelligence resources, market benchmarks, and research repositories to support ongoing monitoring, knowledge sharing, and organizational decision-making.
- Provide actionable customer and market intelligence to inform Product, Business Development, and Sales strategies, including product positioning, go-to-market opportunities, competitive differentiation, and potential market expansion.
- Evaluate customer and market evidence to support experience design, concept testing, pilot initiatives, and strategic recommendations developed by CX Strategy and cross-functional business partners.
- Other duties as assigned.

- Demonstrated experience conducting research using multiple information sources to identify customer, market, industry, or competitive trends required.
- Experience analyzing and synthesizing qualitative and quantitative information to identify meaningful findings and business implications required.
- Experience translating research findings into actionable recommendations that support business decisions required.
- Experience developing research reports, summaries, or presentations to communicate findings to business stakeholders preferred.
