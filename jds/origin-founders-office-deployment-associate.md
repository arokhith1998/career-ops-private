## Full description (verbatim via headless browser render of the LinkedIn guest posting page)
About Origin
Origin (previously 10xConstruction) is building general-purpose autonomous robots for US construction to tackle rising costs, safety risks, and labour shortages. Modular, multi-trade platform combining purpose-built hardware with real-time site intelligence. Trained in high-fidelity simulation, already deployed on live sites. "Join India's talent-dense robotics team, with colleagues from IITs, Stanford, UCLA, and other leading institutions."

About The Role
As Founder's Office, Deployment Associate, you will work closely with the founders and deployment team on high-priority initiatives spanning product, operations, customers, field execution — taking new robot capabilities from internal prototype to successful customer pilot, resolving operational ambiguity, building repeatable commercial-deployment playbooks.

Key responsibilities
- Drive assigned pilot workstreams from planning through execution and post-pilot review (scope, site readiness, deployment schedules, acceptance criteria, action tracking).
- Support conversion of paid pilots into commercial deployments with Sales and deployment teams.
- Translate customer/field needs into product, hardware, software, operational requirements.
- Coordinate engineering, deployment, safety, commercial, customer stakeholders around milestones/decisions.
- Build/maintain playbooks for mobilization, operator training, site support, issue escalation, handover.
- Support customer adoption; track actions to expand deployment at a site.
- Track pilot economics, utilization, reliability, customer outcomes, blockers; follow through on corrective actions.
- Investigate issues during live deployments; capture lessons for product/operational improvement.
- Research new use cases, partners, geographies, operating models; prepare recommendations for founders.

Required Qualifications and Skills
- Hands-on experience in deployment, program management, operations, product operations, consulting, founder's office, or a comparable execution-focused role.
- Evidence of delivering projects involving physical products, industrial systems, automation, robotics, or construction technology in customer environments.
- Strong structured problem solving, project planning, stakeholder coordination, written communication.
- Comfort working at sites, understanding technical issues, driving closure across functions.
- Ability to turn ambiguous goals into practical plans with owners/milestones/measurable outcomes.
- Willingness to travel frequently and work around customer/site schedules.

Preferred Experience: construction operations, robotics deployment, industrial automation, heavy equipment, hardware startups; customer-pilot/operating-playbook support; safety/commissioning/field-service/unit-economics/commercial-deployment familiarity.
