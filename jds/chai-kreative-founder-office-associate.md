Company Description

Chai Kreative is a growing creative and strategy-focused organization working with diverse stakeholders across industries to build impactful brands and experiences. Based in India, the company blends data-driven insights with storytelling, design, and community engagement to help clients navigate complex markets. Chai Kreative values collaboration, experimentation, and a hands-on approach to solving business and social challenges. Team members have the opportunity to work closely with founders and senior leaders, contributing directly to strategic decisions and long-term initiatives.

We are setting up a dedicated Founder Office in Lucknow and are looking for a sharp, ambitious professional to work closely with the Founder on government strategy, research, mapping, and stakeholder engagement.

This is a high-visibility role for someone who wants to operate at the intersection of policy, strategy, and execution. You will be involved in research and mapping of government work, preparation of strategic notes and pitches, coordination across stakeholders, and initial networking and engagement with bureaucrats and ministers. The role requires both independent thinking and the ability to work as a core team member of the Founder Office.

What You Will Do

- Research and map relevant government schemes, policies, departments, and opportunities across priority sectors
- Prepare crisp, well-structured briefs, pitch documents, and background notes for meetings and proposals
- Coordinate internal and external stakeholders for smooth execution of government-facing work
- Support initial pitching, relationship building, and networking with bureaucrats, ministers, and key officials
- Work hands-on with AI tools for research, analysis, documentation, and content preparation
- Act as a lead or core team member of the Founder Office, owning specific workstreams with minimal supervision

Who We Are Looking For

- Ambitious professional with a sharp mind and strong sense of ownership
- Excellent communication skills, both written and verbal, with the ability to hold conversations with senior stakeholders
- Confident, composed, and able to represent the organisation with elegance and class
- Comfortable working with AI tools for research, drafting, and analysis
- Strong coordination and organisational abilities
- Preference for candidates with prior exposure to government, policy, consulting, or strategic roles (not mandatory if the other qualities are strong)

Why This Role

You will work directly with the Founder Office on meaningful government and strategic work, with high visibility and the opportunity to shape how initiatives are positioned and executed. The environment rewards sharp thinking, clean communication, and the ability to move things forward with elegance and speed.
