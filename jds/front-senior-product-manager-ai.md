## Job Description (extracted, verbatim from posting)

As a Senior Product Manager on the Copilot team, you will own the strategy, roadmap, and execution for the AI assistant product embedded in Front's inbox - the central place where teams triage, collaborate, and resolve high-stakes customer communication. This role is central to Front's strategy: embedding Copilot into the moments that matter most in the Inbox (summarize, prioritize, draft, classify, route, and learn) while maintaining the speed, quality, and trust required for customer-facing work. High-visibility role that interfaces regularly with the executive team.

**What will you be doing?**
- Own the product vision, strategy, and roadmap for Front's Copilot product
- Make high-judgment decisions on priorities, tradeoffs, and sequencing
- Use quantitative and qualitative data, market/user research, and product intuition to prioritize opportunities
- Lead end-to-end product development from problem definition through launch and iteration
- Partner with engineering and design; collaborate with marketing, data, sales and GTM
- Drive clarity and alignment among key stakeholders, including executives

**What skills and experience do you need?**
- "7+ years of experience in product management, or related roles, in a technology-driven environment."
- Strong design thinking and systems thinking skills
- Excellent product intuition and taste
- Ownership mindset; customer empathy
- Excellent communication and collaboration skills with senior/executive stakeholders
- Execution-oriented, comfortable managing many work streams
- Demonstrated fluency with AI (used or built AI tooling)

What We Offer: competitive salary, equity (post-Series D, backed by Sequoia Capital / Salesforce Ventures among others, $204M raised total), private health insurance, paid parental leave, flexible time off, WFH flexibility (in-office Mon/Fri unless fully remote), mental health support, family planning support, $100/mo lifestyle stipend, wellness days, winter break closure.
