## Full JD text

ManageCasa is looking for a hands-on Marketing Manager who loves turning ideas into campaigns and campaigns into meaningful customer conversations. You'll help tell our product story, run email campaigns, and work closely with sales, social media, creative, and SEO to grow awareness and generate qualified leads.

We're looking for someone smart, flexible, organized, and detail-oriented who takes ownership from the first idea through execution and follow-up. You should enjoy both the creative work and the daily coordination that makes marketing effective.

What You'll Do
- Run email campaigns: Plan, write, launch, and improve campaigns that engage prospects, nurture leads, and support customer growth
- Write product marketing copy: Turn product features into clear customer benefits across emails, website pages, landing pages, sales materials, and product announcements
- Collaborate on social media and creative: Develop fresh campaign ideas and work with our social media and design contributors to bring them to life
- Follow up on campaign leads: Coordinate outbound follow-up with sales, keep lead information organized, and help move interested prospects toward a demo
- Work closely with SEO: Align content, keywords, and campaign priorities with our SEO team and broader marketing strategy
- Keep execution on track: Manage calendars, deadlines, campaign details, and handoffs so projects move forward
- Measure and improve results: Track engagement, qualified leads, and demo conversions, then use those insights to improve future campaigns

What We're Looking For
- Experience in marketing, with strong email campaign and copywriting skills
- The ability to explain software products in simple, compelling language
- A practical understanding of lead generation, nurturing, SEO, and social media
- Strong organization, attention to detail, and consistent follow-through
- A proactive mindset: you identify what needs to happen, ask thoughtful questions, and take action
- Flexibility to shift between strategy, writing, coordination, and hands-on execution
- Accountability for your work, deadlines, and results
- Curiosity and a real commitment to becoming an excellent marketer
- Experience in B2B SaaS, property management, or HOA/community management is a plus

Why ManageCasa?

ManageCasa builds software for HOA and community association management and rental property management. You'll work closely with our team, contribute ideas, and see the direct impact of your work on a growing business.

If you love marketing, take pride in getting the details right, and want to keep learning while owning meaningful work, we'd love to hear from you.

To apply, please share your resume and examples of email campaigns or marketing copy you've created. Tell us what you owned and what results you achieved.
