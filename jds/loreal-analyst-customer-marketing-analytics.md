## Role
Support retailer activations and provide analytical insight to drive brand growth for L'Oreal Consumer Products Division. Cross-functional liaison between retailers and internal teams. Conduct customer marketing analysis and maintain performance dashboards.

## Responsibilities
- Support end-to-end execution of in-store activations and media plans; obtain customer approval and route internal authorizations.
- Serve as primary contact between retailers and internal teams (Brand Activation, Creative, Supply Chain).
- Conduct customer marketing analysis for business reviews and evaluate activation ROI using consumer analytics.
- Maintain performance dashboards and develop post-activity recaps highlighting KPI performance.

## Required Qualifications
- 1-2 years in business analysis, sales, or trade marketing.
- Advanced proficiency in Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook.
- Knowledge of retailer and syndicated data (Nielsen, IRI, retailer portals).
- Understanding of beauty/personal care industry landscape.
- Strong multitasking and deadline management.
- Excellent verbal and written communication.
