## Role Summary

Rivian is looking for a Senior or Staff Product Manager to lead the evolution of web experiences across the upper funnel: discovery, consideration, configuration, lead capture, and purchase intent. Operates at the intersection of product, marketing science, data, design, technology, and commercial strategy.

## Responsibilities (selected)

- Own product strategy and roadmap for upper-funnel web experiences.
- Partner with Marketing, Brand, Design, Analytics, Data Science, and Engineering on personalization and growth across the web funnel.
- Translate marketing science (segmentation, positioning, messaging, attribution, lifecycle, behavioral psychology, conversion) into DTC product strategies.
- Establish a disciplined experimentation practice: hypothesis development, test design, prioritization, measurement.
- Identify opportunities to use AI and agentic capabilities for discovery, education, personalization, and decision support.
- Partner across the customer data/marketing technology ecosystem (CDP, Salesforce CRM, marketing automation, experimentation, analytics).
- Coach and elevate other product managers.

## Qualifications

- Significant product management experience building consumer-facing digital products, web experiences, personalization, growth products, or marketing technology.
- Deep understanding of the web funnel; strong foundation in marketing science.
- Demonstrated cross-functional experience with marketing, design, analytics, data science, engineering.
- Experience with personalization, recommendation, search, ranking, lifecycle, or ML-powered consumer product areas.
- Technical fluency in personalization tradeoffs (signal quality, cold start, feedback loops, relevance, explainability, experimentation, decisioning).
- Preferred: automotive/mobility/retail/travel/financial services background; Staff-level experience setting direction across multiple teams.
