Role summary: The role supports U.S. marketing for Women's Health, covering Gynecology and Breast procedures. The position works under U.S. Clinical Marketing leadership and coordinates across marketing, sales, training, clinical affairs, legal, regulatory, and Market Access/Health Economics. It also involves external Key Opinion Leaders (KOLs) and surgical society leaders.

Responsibilities:
- Lead integrated marketing strategies to speed da Vinci adoption in Gynecology and Breast, with emphasis on da Vinci 5 and SP.
- Build and run marketing plans and tactical programs that meet budgeted growth targets in key Women's Health areas.
- Analyze quantitative and qualitative data on existing and new markets, surgeons, practices, patient journeys, and clinical procedure steps.
- Create strategies and value messaging (clinical, economic, operational, strategic), collateral, and programs that demonstrate ROI.
- Track market trends and competitor activity, and build competitive differentiation.
- Help manage budget, resource allocation, and financial planning, with measurable ROI across channels such as webinars and sales materials.
- Work with KOL surgeons to understand how the system and instruments are used, and turn that into messaging for other physicians.
- Develop KOL relationships by market segment so Intuitive becomes the preferred industry collaborator.
- Build relationships with society leaders so Intuitive is involved in congress planning and guideline development.
- Coordinate trade shows, digital and website work, and campaign tracking, and recommend future promotions based on results.
- Coach other marketing staff on onboarding, marketing standards, and business acumen in clinical, hospital, or academic settings.
- Lead cross-functional teams on high-value marketing projects.
- Present to senior and executive leadership.
- Serve as the internal expert on Gynecology and Breast surgery, using approved claims, surgeon feedback, and sales input to grow Intuitive's footprint.
- Model and promote the Intuitive culture and its expectations for success.
