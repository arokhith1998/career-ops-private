About Upstart
At Upstart, we’re united by a mission that matters: to radically reduce the cost and complexity of borrowing for all Americans. Every day, we bring creativity, experimentation, and advanced AI to reshape access to credit, helping millions move forward financially with clarity and confidence.
As the leading AI lending marketplace, we partner with banks and credit unions to expand access to affordable credit through technology that’s both radically intelligent and deeply human. Our platform runs over one million predictions per borrower using more than 3,000 signals, powering smarter, fairer decisions for millions of customers. But the numbers only hint at the impact. Every idea, every voice, and every contribution moves us closer to a world where credit never stands between people and their financial progress.
We’re proudly digital-first, giving most Upstarters the flexibility to do their best work from wherever they thrive, alongside teammates across 80+ cities in the US and Canada. Digital-first doesn’t mean distant. We’re intentional about in-person connection through team onsites, planning sessions, and moments that spark creativity and trust. And whether you choose to work primarily from home or collaborate in-person from one of our offices in Columbus, Austin, the Bay Area, or New York City, you’ll have the support to work in the way that works best for you.
If you’re energized by tackling meaningful problems, excited to innovate with purpose, and motivated by work that truly matters, we’d love to hear from you.
The Team: 
Upstart’s Growth team is focused on expanding access to affordable credit for millions of Americans. The team combines marketing, data, and technology to scale customer acquisition in efficient and measurable ways. Direct Mail is a core channel within Growth and plays a critical role in reaching new borrowers and driving business performance.
As the Senior Performance Marketing Manager, Direct Mail and Offline Growth, you will own one of Upstart’s most impactful acquisition channels end-to-end, with a primary focus on our Home Equity Line of Credit (HELOC) product. This is one of the company’s fastest-growing and most strategically important offerings. You will define channel strategy, optimize targeting and creative, and lead execution to drive efficient, incremental growth. This role offers significant ownership, including P&L responsibility, and the opportunity to solve complex, high-impact problems in partnership with cross-functional teams.
How you’ll make an impact

Own end-to-end Direct Mail strategy and execution for HELOC, including audience targeting, creative development, testing, and campaign operations
Build our broader offline growth channels (OOH, linear TV, and brand testing)
Manage and optimize millions in annual spend, with accountability for ROI and incremental business impact
Partner with Machine Learning and Data Science teams to develop and refine targeting models that improve customer acquisition efficiency
Design and execute rigorous testing frameworks (e.g., incrementality, geo-lift, A/B testing) to evaluate performance and inform strategy
Analyze campaign and customer data to generate insights and translate findings into actionable growth opportunities
Develop new playbooks to reach and convert underserved or underperforming customer segments, including emerging HELOC use cases
Collaborate cross-functionally with Product, Engineering, Analytics, and Creative to scale channel performance and influence roadmap decisions
Communicate strategy, performance, and insights to leadership, connecting channel outcomes to broader business goals

Minimum Qualifications 

Bachelor’s degree or equivalent practical experience
5+ years of experience in performance marketing, growth marketing, or analytics-driven marketing roles
Experience owning and executing end-to-end marketing campaigns, including strategy, testing, and performance analysis
Experience managing marketing budgets and optimizing for measurable ROI or P&L outcomes
Experience designing and analyzing controlled experiments (e.g., A/B testing, incrementality testing, or similar methodologies)

Preferred Qualifications

Experience with Direct Mail marketing strategy, execution, or channel ownership
Experience working across multiple marketing channels (e.g., digital, offline, lifecycle)
Knowledge of measurement frameworks such as incrementality testing, geo-lift, or attribution modeling
Experience working in regulated industries such as financial services, insurance, or healthcare
Skills in SQL, Looker, or similar tools to analyze and interpret large datasets

 
Position location This role is available in the following locations: Remote 
Time zone requirements The team operates on the East/West coast time zones.
Travel requirements As a digital first company, the majority of your work can be accomplished remotely. The majority of our employees can live and work anywhere in the U.S but are encouraged to to still spend high quality time in-person collaborating via regular onsites. The in-person sessions’ cadence varies depending on the team and role; most teams meet once or twice per quarter for 2-4 consecutive days at a time.
