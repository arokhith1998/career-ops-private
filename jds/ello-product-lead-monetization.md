## Full JD text

Our mission at Ello is to maximize the potential of every child, everywhere. Building the world's first AI teacher. First product, Read with Ello, already helps tens of thousands of kids/week learn to read. Public benefit corporation; featured on TIME's Best Inventions and Fortune's Change the World. ~50 people, HQ San Francisco + office in Nairobi, Kenya. Part of YC W20; funded by Goodwater Capital, Homebrew, Reed Hastings, Common Sense Growth, Ravensburger, Project A Ventures, Reach Capital, Khosla Ventures, WndrCo, Visible Ventures, K9 Ventures.

About the Role

You'll own everything to do with how Ello makes money: what's free and what's paid, pricing and packaging, the paywall, trial conversion, renewals, dunning, cancellation, winback. Build the monetization plan with the Head of Growth and CEO. Ello is a public benefit corporation with a public commitment to a free, excellent teaching experience for every family -- teaching itself never sits behind a paywall. Selling to two people at once: the child doing the lesson and the parent deciding whether to pay. The more families pay, the more children can be taught for free (including every child using Ello in Kenya today).

What You'll Do:
- Own pricing and packaging: what's free, what a subscription includes, and what it costs.
- Own the paywall and checkout (placement, timing, design, copy) and run the experiment program.
- Own everything after the first payment: trial conversion, renewals, failed payments/dunning, cancellation saves, winback.
- Build the monetization model feeding the revenue forecast.
- Build monetization reporting with the data team: cohorted LTV, ARPU, payback, retention curves.
- Work with lifecycle marketing on conversion/retention emails/messages; with engineering/design on what ships.
- Use AI to do the work of a much bigger team; grow into hiring/leading the monetization team.

Key Requirements:
- Owned consumer subscription revenue on mobile, iOS and Android -- for most people, six or more years as a PM (though "we care about what you've done more than the years").
- Run paywall/pricing experiments yourself, with before/after numbers and what you got wrong.
- Can build the subscription model yourself: LTV, ARPU, trial-to-paid, involuntary churn, payback period.
- Pull your own data (SQL or equivalent) to define metrics, build funnels, check results.
- Know app store billing well enough to write the spec: StoreKit, Play Billing, receipt validation, subscription state.
- Use AI seriously and hands-on in your own work.
- Built agreement across departments (pricing touches product, marketing, finance, support).

Nice to Have: kids'/family product experience; monetizing trust-sensitive categories (privacy-regulated children's product, health, finance); products where most users never pay by design; in-product growth mechanics (nudges, rewards, cross-sell, referral, lifecycle automation); worked at a company roughly $1M-$10M revenue stage and seen what breaks at 10x that; run a small team or ready to.

About Ello: public benefit corporation; team expertise from Stanford, Berkeley, Google, Apple, and more. If you have questions about immigration, reach out to recruiting@ello.com.
