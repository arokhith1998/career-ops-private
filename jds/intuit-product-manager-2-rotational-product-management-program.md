## Description

Intuit's Rotational Product Management (RPM) program is a selective two-year growth accelerator that offers several years' worth of product experience through high-impact assignments, professional development, coaching from product leaders, and networking opportunities across Intuit. Many graduates from the program have become executives at Intuit and leaders across the tech industry. The anticipated start date is expected to be Tuesday, September 7th, 2027.

This role is ideal for those at the start of their professional journey, with less than two years of industry experience who are eager to learn, grow, and make an immediate impact.

Responsibilities:
- Lead cross-functional teams of engineers, designers, UX researchers, data analysts, data scientists, marketers, and/or customer success
- Become an expert on the customer through qualitative and quantitative data gathering
- Gather requirements, define experiences, develop data-backed learning plans, and create product roadmaps
- Ship product improvements using rapid tests and experimentation
- Partner with AI across the product lifecycle
- Apply innovative technologies to improve customer experiences

Qualifications:
- Bachelor's degree in Business, Marketing, Computer Science, Engineering, Management Information Systems, or related field
- Familiarity with AI tools and curiosity about real-world applications
- Ability to work in hybrid environment at an Intuit office location
- Strong communication, leadership, and collaboration skills
- Demonstrated adaptability to change
