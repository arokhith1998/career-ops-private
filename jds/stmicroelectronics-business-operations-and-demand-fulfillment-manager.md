## Full JD text

At STMicroelectronics, we believe in the power of technology to drive innovation and make a positive impact on people, businesses, and society. Global semiconductor company; more than 115 nationalities, present in 40 countries, 50,000+ employees.

Job Title: Business Operations and Demand fulfillment manager

Act as the primary interface between customers and ST for operational communication, demand fulfillment, forecast alignment, supply execution, and service performance through best-in-class customer service. Proactively act as the official and reliable source of information on customer demand and execution constraints.

Responsibilities:
- Manage the order life cycle, including firm orders, NRE POs, and samples, plus contract and pricing setup.
- Maintain and optimize the backlog; update requests, monitor confirmations/deliveries, capture customer signals to improve forecast accuracy.
- Achieve revenue plans by driving optimal backlog coverage with customer, sales, and champions.
- Translate key supply drivers (internal/external inventory, goods in transit, constrained demand, product lead times/delays, demand layering) into a clear, reliable supply commitment via capacity visibility and alignment with sourcing agreements.
- Proactively manage shipment risks: resolve delays, expedite urgent shipments, address customs issues, manage trade compliance.
- Lead customer communication on operational topics (lead time, delivery status, improvements).
- Partner with the sales team and manage escalations.
- Collaborate with regional business intelligence and Sales on centralized regional reporting.
- Perform data-driven analytics to identify trends and areas for improvement.
- Monitor forecast process performance with continuous feedback loop.
- Demand forecasting: detect future supply issues and resolve through daily collaboration with Business Operations, division planning directors, and Central Supply Chain.

Your Skills & Experiences:
- 10-15 years' experience in Supply Chain or Demand Planning/Divisional Planning.
- Experienced operations leader with deep expertise in sales processes, demand and supply management, and customer service.
- Strong understanding of supply chain principles/processes/best practices.
- Collaborative partner with business operations, sales, finance, divisions, and supply chain teams.
- Strong communication and problem-solving skills.

Compensation: base salary is one part of total comp, determined within a range depending on skills/qualifications/experience/location. Bay Area: $150,800-$188,500 USD. Non Bay: $129,800-$162,300 USD.
