About Faire

Faire is a technology wholesale platform built on the belief that the future is local. Independent retailers around the globe collectively represent a multi-hundred-billion-dollar wholesale market that has historically been fragmented and offline. At Faire, we're using the power of tech, data, and machine learning to connect this thriving community of entrepreneurs across the globe. Picture your favorite boutique in town — we help them discover the best products from around the world to sell in their stores. With the right tools and insights, we believe that we can level the playing field so businesses can grow and local communities can thrive.

We’re looking for smart, resourceful and passionate people to join us as we power the shop local movement. If you believe in community, come join ours.

About this role

Brand Growth is one of Faire's top company objectives - it's one of the biggest levers we have to unlock retailer share of wallet and enable retailers to do more and more of their spend on Faire. As a Strategy & Operations Senior Lead, you'll take end-to-end ownership of our Enterprise opportunity globally, across NAM, Europe, and future markets - defining how we identify, win, and grow our largest and most strategic brand partners. You'll operate as a senior individual contributor with significant scope: setting the strategic agenda, driving the analyses and business cases that shape it, and leading the cross-functional execution that turns strategy into results.

You'll partner closely with Product, Sales, Marketing, Data Science, and Program Management to build the go-to-market motion, operating cadence, and investment priorities for Enterprise, and you'll regularly present performance, insights, and recommendations to cross-functional leadership. Beyond scaling today's Enterprise motion, you'll explore entirely new opportunities to unlock supply growth - from 1P fulfillment models to deeper business development partnerships. You won't have direct reports, but you will be a de facto leader on the team - setting direction for how Enterprise is prioritized, and acting as a thought partner and mentor to other Strategy & Operations teammates.

This is an ideal role for someone who wants outsized ownership of a high-visibility, high-growth, global area of the business - combining deep analytical rigor with the strategic judgment and cross-functional influence to drive a new opportunity from thesis to scaled execution.

What you’ll do

- Own the global Enterprise opportunity end-to-end across NAM, Europe, and future markets - set the strategy, size the opportunity, and build the roadmap to capture it

- Shape pricing strategy and other core commercial and go-to-market levers for our largest brand partners

- Lead cross-functional strategic initiatives with Product, Sales, Marketing, Data Science, and Program Management, turning strategy into measurable business impact

- Explore and stand up entirely new opportunities to unlock supply growth, from 1P fulfillment to deeper business development partnerships

- Own the most important Enterprise metrics - tracking performance, surfacing the most salient insights, and building scalable operational processes to close gaps

- Regularly present Enterprise performance and strategic recommendations to Faire's broader leadership team

- Act as a thought partner and informal mentor to other Strategy & Operations teammates

Qualifications

- 8+ years of relevant work experience in an analytical and strategic role across Strategy & Operations, Business Operations, Consulting, or Finance

- Experience owning a strategic initiative or business area end-to-end, ideally including exposure to Enterprise, key account, or B2B go-to-market motions

- Experience with pricing strategy, global/international expansion, or new business model development is a plus

- You are a strategic problem solver. You can break down ambiguous problems and apply a first-principles approach to solving them

- You are highly analytical and metrics-focused. You can execute complex analyses, define KPIs, size opportunity areas, and measure and report on performance across the business

- Advanced technical skill set with strong proficiency in Excel + SQL

- You're fluent with AI tools and use them to move faster - accelerating analysis, research, and execution

- You are a strong communicator who can crisply translate data-driven insights into recommendations for audiences up to and including senior leadership

- You have strong product and business intuition, and can contextualize insights within broader business goals

- You are highly collaborative and can influence and drive results through others, without relying on formal authority

- You are scrappy and resourceful - when something needs to get done, you find a way to make it happen

- You are an extraordinarily fast learner with the curiosity and intellectual horsepower to master new topics quickly
