## Job Description (extracted, verbatim from posting)

Yelp's Growth Marketing team drives user acquisition, activation, and long-term engagement. The Growth Marketing Manager will own strategies and execution for consumer onboarding, retention/re-engagement, and acquisition across Services and Restaurant, Food & Nightlife (RFN) verticals. Partners with Marketing Operations, Growth Science, Creative Design, Marketing Technology, and Product teams.

**What You'll Do:**
- Design and optimize onboarding journeys
- Build segmentation frameworks for at-risk/lapsed user cohorts, design reacquisition journeys
- Lead content strategy for consumer campaigns, optimize for AEO/GEO discoverability
- Lead mid-funnel marketing to acquire new Services users through off-platform channels
- Lead the development, execution, and optimization of growth experiments (A/B testing, segmentation, analytics)
- Leverage AI tools and emerging technologies to enhance consumer insights

**What It Takes To Succeed:**
- "5-8 years of experience in growth marketing, lifecycle marketing, or related roles."
- Proven experience building/optimizing onboarding and lifecycle programs
- Direct experience developing winback/re-engagement strategies
- "Bachelor's degree in Marketing, Business, Communications, or related field (or equivalent practical experience)."
- Experience with, or strong enthusiasm for, applying AI solutions in growth marketing

"We'd love to have you apply, even if you don't feel you meet every single requirement in this posting."

What You'll Get: full medical/vision/dental from day one, 15 days PTO (increasing with 2+ years tenure), 12 paid holidays + 1 floating, up to 14 weeks parental leave, monthly wellness subsidy, WFH reimbursement, FSA, 401(k), ESPP.
