Why AssemblyAI
AssemblyAI builds the best-in-class Voice AI models powering the next generation of voice applications. We have a lab, and we also power our own models in our own proprietary inference environment. Our models serve 500M+ inference calls monthly, and process nearly 2 million hours of audio daily. The Voice AI space is at an inflection point; we're looking for the best talent we can find to join a rapidly scaling team that's defining the future of the industry.

We are one of the most capital-efficient AI companies on the planet - and are rapidly approaching $1M ARR per employee. We sit among the top 5 most revenue-dense teams within the fastest-growing AI companies today. We have thousands of customers building with us including the top startups and world-renowned enterprises, and are serving at tremendous scale: we process nearly 2 million hours of audio daily and handle more than 15 million API calls every day.

About the role:
We're hiring a Growth Marketing Manager to run paid programs aimed at our target accounts. That means account-based marketing through paid channels, for new logos and for existing customers we want to expand. Target-account programs are the core of this job, but you will own the entire paid budget line-item and decide how to divide it.

AssemblyAI sells Voice AI APIs to engineering teams: Pre-recorded and Realtime Speech-to-Text, Text-to-Speech with instant voice cloning, LLM Gateway, Speech Understanding, and the Dictation API, with more launches on the way. Our buyers pick an API on features and benchmarks, so we primarily market specific products with concrete proof. Your job is to get that proof in front of the right engineers at the accounts our sales team most wants to win, and to show what it produced in those accounts.

Day to day, you'll build cross-platform retargeting for target accounts. You'll use paid to boost our account-based programs: direct mail, Vibe, webinars and launches. You'll own geo-targeted paid for our events and field activations, and test new channels and formats quickly. You'll work with Sales, Revenue Operations and Events on ABM. You'll also run the paid side of our expansion work with the marketer who owns cross-sell and lifecycle programs.

You'll also own the full paid budget and our conversion channels: Google Ads, paid social, YouTube and others. Your closest partners are Product Marketing on launches, Brand Studio on creative, and our analytics team on measurement.

Why This Role Exists: Paid is one of our largest marketing investments, and we want to ensure that investment is working on the accounts we most want to win.

What You'll Do: Cross-Platform Retargeting to Target Accounts
- Build and run retargeting across LinkedIn, Meta, Vibe, YouTube, display and connected TV for engineers at target accounts who have visited the site, read the docs, watched a launch video or attended an event
- Build person- and company-level audiences from Salesforce and enrichment tools like Clay. Tailor the message by buying committee (engineering leaders, ML and voice engineers, product owners) and by account tier
- Keep target accounts out of mass campaigns where Sales owns the first touch

Paid Amplification for ABM Programs
- Own paid ABM for target accounts, both new logos and existing customers we want to expand. This is the core of the role
- Put paid behind our account-based programs: direct mail (run with Sales and BDRs), Vibe audiences, webinar promotion and launch promotion
- Track account-level signals such as ad engagement, site visits, docs activity, sign-ups and API usage, and get warm accounts to the right AE or BDR quickly

Events & Geo-Targeting
- Own geo-targeted paid for our in-person events and field activations, aimed at people at target accounts who haven't engaged with us yet
- Turn our early wins into a repeatable playbook covering audiences, radius and timing, creative, budget, registration tracking and follow-up with Sales
- Report each event's results by account: who registered, who attended, and which accounts were new to us

Channel Experimentation
- Run experiments every week across LinkedIn, YouTube, Reddit, connected TV, Meta, X and emerging channels such as AI assistant and agent ad placements
- Write and test many ad variations quickly, and tell Brand Studio and content creators what to make next based on what works
- Decide what to scale, what to cut, and when a channel has had a fair test, and say why
- Set and pace the monthly budget across channels and report spend against plan

Google Ads & Paid Search
- Own our Google Ads account: brand and non-brand search, YouTube, and Performance Max where it fits
- Structure campaigns by product line, with feature-led copy that names the product and the proof (benchmarks, latency, language coverage, price), and optimize toward API activation, not clicks or sign-ups alone
- Keep conversion tracking accurate with our data team, including offline conversions sent from our warehouse

Measurement & AI-Native Workflow
- Own one view of paid and ABM performance, led by account-level results: new engagement in target accounts, event and webinar registrations from those accounts, and pipeline in the target list
- Work with analytics to build that view in Looker and our warehouse so anyone on the team can see it
- Write a short weekly update: results against goals, what's working, what isn't, and what changes next
- Report on the pipeline and revenue your programs source and influence
- Use AI tools and agents for the work that slows paid teams down, such as ad copy variations, audience builds, search-term reviews, budget pacing, anomaly alerts, and reporting

What You'll Need:
- 4+ years in B2B paid acquisition or demand generation, personally managing six-figure monthly budgets
- A track record in both ABM and conversion marketing, including LinkedIn and connected TV awareness programs that measurably moved engagement or pipeline in a named target account list
- Experience running account-based programs on paid channels: cross-platform retargeting, ABM platforms, and paid support for direct mail
- Experience using paid to drive attendance at events, webinars and launches, including geo-targeted campaigns aimed at specific accounts
- Deep, hands-on Google Ads skills: account structure, bidding strategies, conversion setup and search-term management
- Experience at a startup or on a fast-paced, scrappy team where you built campaigns yourself rather than managing an agency
- Experience testing new paid channels and formats, and making clear calls to scale or cut backed by data
- Experience marketing technical, developer-facing products
- Comfort with conversion tracking, attribution and audience syncing (Google Ads, LinkedIn Campaign Manager, a CDP such as RudderStack or Segment, and enrichment tools such as Clay)
- Data fluency: you can pull and analyze your own numbers with SQL, Looker or similar tools
- Strong copywriting and a sharp eye for creative that performs
- Comfortable with AI-assisted bidding (Smart Bidding, Performance Max, predictive audiences) and AI-enabled workflows

Nice to Have:

- Experience with intent data and ABM platforms (Vibe, 6sense, Demandbase, or similar)
