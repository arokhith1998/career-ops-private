## Full description (verbatim via Ashby posting-api, HTML stripped)
Why Socure?
Socure is building the identity trust infrastructure for the digital economy — verifying 100% of good identities in real time and stopping fraud before it starts.

Job Overview
Socure is hiring a Lifecycle Marketing Manager to own how prospects and customers move from first signal to pipeline. Run lifecycle strategy across email and webinars; build signal-driven, agent-powered programs that decide who hears from us, when, and with what message. Individual-contributor builder role: define signals predicting buying intent, then build AI agents/automated workflows that act on them (research account, write personalized message, send via right channel, hand warm accounts to Sales with context). Work closely with AI-native Sales Development, Digital Marketing, Field Marketing, Product Marketing, Marketing Operations.

Job Responsibilities (selected)
- Own lifecycle strategy end to end: set roadmap from first engagement through opportunity/expansion/closed-lost re-engagement tied to funnel definitions/revenue targets.
- Build signal-based programs: define/rank first- and third-party signals (web/content engagement, webinar behavior, intent data, hiring/funding changes, Salesforce opportunity-stage movement); turn into triggers with entry/exit rules.
- Design and deploy AI agents that run lifecycle programs in market (research account/contact, draft personalized email/LinkedIn copy, choose timing/channel, route high-intent accounts to SDRs); set guardrails (brand voice, compliance review, human approval, monitoring); prototype with Claude, Clay, Zapier/n8n.
- Own email as a channel (signal-triggered sends, nurture, re-engagement, customer/expansion programs, launch announcements).
- Turn webinars into pipeline (Zoom Webinars/ON24; topic selection through promotion/production/follow-up; repurpose via AI into recap emails/clips/social/sales assets).
- Partner with Sales and Marketing Operations; measure and experiment (signal-to-meeting, signal-to-SQL, pipeline velocity); protect trust/compliance (CAN-SPAM, GDPR, CASL).

What You Bring
- "3-5+ years in B2B lifecycle, demand generation, or growth marketing at a high-growth technology company, with a track record of growing pipeline from mid-funnel programs."
- Hands-on experience building signal/trigger-based programs connecting intent/engagement data to outreach and sales handoff.
- Proof of having built/shipped AI workflows or agents that run real marketing work.
- Working knowledge of a marketing automation platform (Marketo, HubSpot, Customer.io or similar) and Salesforce.
- Experience running a webinar program that produced measurable pipeline.
- Strong writing/editing; comfort with data (SQL or Python a plus).
- Nice to have: marketing to fraud/risk/identity/compliance buyers in financial services or regulated industries.

Socure is an equal opportunity employer.
