**About MISUMI Americas  
  
**MISUMI Americas, a division of MISUMI Group, is a leading provider of standard, configurable, and custom manufacturing solutions. By integrating a vast catalog of components with a world-class digital manufacturing platform, MISUMI Americas empowers engineers and procurement teams to accelerate innovation across the entire product lifecycle. With operations in the San Francisco Bay Area and Chicago, the company serves as a vital partner for the most innovative companies in the Americas.  
  
**Impact In This Role:  
  
**As the Product Manager for Manufacturing Business Systems, you will shape and drive the internal systems that power Fictiv and MISUMI Americas’ manufacturing operations — including PLM, ERP, TMS, MRP, and other business systems we adopt or build over time to support how the business runs. You will contribute to the strategy and roadmap for this systems landscape and own the execution of solutions that deliver against it, balancing customer satisfaction, growth, reliable delivery, and operational efficiency.  
  
This role is focused on solution implementation and optimization rather than IT support. You'll identify and validate business opportunities that can be unlocked through technology, partner with engineering to define the right solution, build the business case for the investment, and drive execution from planning through implementation, qualification, and launch. The solutions you contribute to are foundational to the MISUMI Americas manufacturing business - enabling it to scale, deliver differentiated value, and drive growth by serving product innovators.  
  
**Areas of Responsibility  
  
Business acumen and domain awareness  
  
**

-   Research and evaluate the needs of internal users (operations, finance, supply chain, engineering, IT) to understand priorities and pain points within manufacturing workflows, and assess their feedback on current systems.
-   Understand the needs of Fictiv and MISUMI Americas customers, and how the workflows and tools used by internal teams shape the experience those customers receive. Use that lens to identify and evaluate opportunities.
-   Develop deep knowledge of Fictiv and MISUMI Americas PLM, ERP, and other manufacturing business systems — including data models, integrations, and dependencies — and translate that knowledge into product opportunities.
-   Assess the needs of our growing network of manufacturing partners where they touch internal systems, and identify opportunities to improve data flow, traceability, and partner experience.
-   Contribute to the strategy and roadmap for evolving the manufacturing business systems landscape to support Fictiv and MISUMI Americas growth trajectory.
-   Roadmap execution and project delivery
-   Analyze, comprehend, and take ownership of the assigned roadmap items related to manufacturing business systems and translate high-level goals into detailed objectives and projects.
-   Contribute to building the business case for system investments (new systems, modules, configurations, integrations) and secure alignment on those investments with leadership and stakeholders.Develop a framework for tracking input and output metrics that provides a quantitative view of the success and impact of each project.
-   Scope and define requirements for each roadmap item to ensure clarity and alignment with product and operational objectives.
-   Surface trade-offs between speed of delivery, configuration vs. customization, and long-term scalability of the architecture.
-   Proactively identify risks (data, integration, change management) and work with teams to develop mitigation strategies.  
      

**Cross-functional collaboration  
  
**

-   Based on research, analysis, and deep understanding of the problem space, develop conviction in your product initiatives and vision, and communicate them effectively across the business to drive awareness and alignment.
-   Build and maintain strong working relationships with stakeholders across operations, finance, supply chain, engineering, IT, and external system implementers and vendors.
-   Work with cross-functional partners — software engineering, IT, UX, operations, finance, sales, and marketing — to develop hypotheses and high-impact solutions.
-   Lead scoping and prioritization discussions across teams, bringing the right people in at the right time and incorporating their feedback to sharpen the work.
-   Execution and risk management
-   Use Agile methodologies to organize and manage sprints across development and configuration work.
-   Analyze metrics to measure success and optimize systems for business outcomes and strategic priorities.
-   Keep stakeholders informed of progress, milestones, risks, and timeline changes as work progresses.Partner with development and configuration teams in India and the US, communicating business needs, requirements, and priorities while removing friction where needed.
-   Drive launches and go-lives by ensuring the organization is prepared, including training and change management.
-   Travel 10% annually for team meetings, research, and training.
-   Maintain a daily time overlap with stakeholders in India and China to facilitate communication with collaborating development teams, stakeholders, and users.
