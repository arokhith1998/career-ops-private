## Full JD text

Meet the Team

At Cisco, we are redefining customer experience through the power of Cisco IQ. Our global team operates at the frontier of innovation, building intelligent platforms that anticipate needs rather than just reacting to them. We leverage cutting edge AI to solve complex enterprise challenges, fostering a culture driven by curiosity, collaboration, and rapid iteration.

Your Impact

As our AI Delivery Researcher, you will step into a high impact role where you directly shape the future of our services by uncovering groundbreaking market insights and driving competitive intelligence.

- Research market trends and competitor strategies to identify AI driven automation opportunities to keep our portfolio ahead of the curve.
- Analyze delivery workflows to provide actionable outside-in perspectives that empower internal service teams to elevate customer value.
- Collaborate closely with technical product managers and engineering peers to translate findings into feature and roadmap recommendations to accelerate product innovation.
- Deliver comprehensive competitive briefs and capability matrices to guide internal product development decisions with clear market intelligence.
- Evaluate emerging artificial intelligence solutions to ensure delivery tooling remains competitive in the enterprise space.

Minimum Qualifications
- Bachelor's degree in Information Technology, Computer Science, Business Administration, or a related field.
- Minimum of 5 years of professional experience in product management, technical marketing, market research, or enterprise services delivery.
- Demonstrated experience conducting market research or competitive analysis for customer experience or services businesses.
- Strong technical familiarity with enterprise software systems, architectures, and applied AI/ML frameworks.
- Experience collaborating with technical stakeholders such as engineers, product managers, and cross functional teams.
- Proficiency in using AI tools to capture data, conduct analysis, and generate actionable insights.

Preferred Qualifications
- Exceptional storytelling and communication skills, translating complex research insights into compelling narratives.
- Experience within a consulting, professional services, or customer experience environment.
- Direct familiarity with post sales workflows and technical support operations.
- Demonstrated experience presenting research findings and strategic recommendations to senior leadership.
