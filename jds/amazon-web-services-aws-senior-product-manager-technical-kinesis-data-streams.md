**Description:** Amazon Kinesis Data Streams (KDS) is a fully managed, serverless streaming data service. Customers use it to capture, process and store data streams at any scale. Tens of thousands of AWS customers run real-time workloads on KDS, including clickstream analytics, IoT telemetry, log and event pipelines, change data capture, fraud detection, and feeding data to generative AI and ML applications. We're looking for a Senior Product Manager - Technical to help set where real-time data streaming on AWS goes next.

You will own a major area of the KDS product, such as capacity modes and scaling, consumer experience (including enhanced fan-out), data retention, pricing and packaging, or integrations with the wider AWS analytics and AI stack. You will set the product vision, write the strategy and requirements, and work with engineering, UX, solutions architects, and go-to-market teams to deliver features that customers love and that grow the business.

**Key job responsibilities**
- Own the multi-year vision and roadmap for your KDS area. Write PR/FAQs, 6-pagers, and PRDs
- Go deep on customer problems through customer meetings, support trends, field feedback, and usage data
- Work with engineering on technical tradeoffs in distributed systems, including throughput, latency, durability, partitioning and sharding, and cost-to-serve
- Define and track metrics for your area; run business reviews (WBR/MBR)
- Drive pricing and packaging analysis, and model revenue and margin for new features
- Lead launches from start to finish with Product Marketing, Docs, Solutions Architects, and Sales (including re:Invent)
- Analyze competing streaming products (Apache Kafka/managed Kafka services, Confluent, cloud-native alternatives)
- Work across AWS teams (Amazon MSK, Amazon Data Firehose, AWS Lambda, Amazon Managed Service for Apache Flink, Amazon S3, Amazon SageMaker, Amazon Bedrock)

**Basic Qualifications**
- 5+ years of technical product management with internet business experience
- 5+ years of working as a Technical Product Manager experience
- 3+ years of technical (software development, network development, IT, other related) experience
- 7+ years of full product life cycle experience
- 5+ years of P&L management and pricing experience
- 5+ years of creating written docs for development of new products experience
- 5+ years of enterprise security product experience
- 5+ years of product management in the cloud computing technology space experience
- Bachelor's degree in computer science, engineering, math, finance, or economics
- Experience in taking a product from conception & definition phase through engineering design and taking it to market
- Experience delivering large-scale SaaS, PaaS or LaaS products where you are responsible for the full product lifecycle, from concept through GTM

**Preferred Qualifications**
- Knowledge of engineering practices and patterns for the full software/hardware/networks development life cycle
- Experience working within teams delivering software products and features using agile methodologies
