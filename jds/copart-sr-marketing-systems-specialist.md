## Job Description
Design and optimize lifecycle marketing initiatives across multiple channels including email, SMS, and push notifications for member base. Bridge marketing strategy with technical implementation, requiring data-driven analysis and customer journey optimization across a global platform.

## Responsibilities
- Develop lifecycle programs aligned with customer journey stages, addressing engagement gaps
- Design omni-channel communication flows with testing and deployment oversight
- Build responsive email templates using HTML/CSS with cross-platform compatibility
- Execute A/B and multivariate testing to enhance messaging and segmentation
- Write advanced SQL queries for audience and campaign data analysis
- Collaborate across teams to translate business needs into automated solutions
- Continuously refine marketing automation workflows and data quality

## Requirements
- Bachelor's in Information Systems, Business Intelligence, Computer Science, or Marketing Technology
- 3+ years in lifecycle marketing, marketing automation, or related systems roles
- Omni-channel program experience across email, SMS, push channels
- Advanced HTML/CSS proficiency for responsive email design
- Deep knowledge of email deliverability and personalization
- Advanced SQL querying capabilities
- A/B and multivariate testing expertise
- Hands-on marketing automation platform experience (Salesforce, Braze, Marketo, HubSpot)
- Cloud data warehouse familiarity (BigQuery or similar)
- Reporting tools proficiency (Tableau, Looker, Excel)

## Preferred
- Master's degree
