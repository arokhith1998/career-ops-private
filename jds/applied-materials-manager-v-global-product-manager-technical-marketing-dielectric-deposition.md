What You'll Do:

This role is responsible for all business aspects of Applied Materials DDP Service Business Unit – Dielectric Deposition Service Product Segment supplying the semiconductor industry. This person will engage cross-functionally to drive all aspects of the business and product lifecycle to deliver to customer and business expectations. This is a high growth business opportunity within Applied Materials Global Service Division.

Role Responsibilities:

- Leads business and product strategy, planning and execution for the Dielectric Deposition service product segment/portfolio including business ownership
- Builds understanding of customer and market requirements to support above planning/execution
- Responsible for service product management activities, developing product value proposition, business sizing, product scorecards, Go-To-Market strategy including 7Ps of service marketing
- Leads the development of pricing models/strategy, financial plans, business case assessment and analytics for the service product portfolio and business
- Engages directly with customers and value chain partners to deliver to business and stakeholder expectations (including supporting commercial/contractual negotiations)
- Sponsors/drives inorganic growth activities and partnership development to support business execution and delivery.
- Drive program and business outcomes utilizing applied and industry best practices/processes.
- Deeply engages with technical application and product development execution to ensure program success (Performance/Capability, Cost, Timing)
- Full product lifecycle ownership facilitating PDP and end of life support programs
- Drives activities for the Service Business Unit through strong cross-functional engagement across multiple geographies & external partners in Sales, Marketing, Engineering, Operations, Finance, Legal etc… to deliver to release schedules and business financials
- Makes customer and management presentations on Service Business Unit products to support product development, strategic and business planning, and service opportunity penetration.
- Communicates business progress and execution to senior executive and stakeholders to successfully secure necessary support and resources to deliver to growth expectations.
- Uses business analytics tools to support internal business analysis and reporting

- Higher Degree or equivalent experience with minimum 15+ years of industry experience. Demonstrates depth and/or breadth of expertise in Wafer Fabrication Equipment and Dielectic Deposition Products operation and business or related fields
- Demonstrates understanding of service business principles
- Customer focused - Strong business and commercial acumen with previous product leadership, management or marketing/sales experience
- Strong program management and technical acumen with past product development experience
- High financial acumen and analytical/data oriented decision making style
- Positive can do entrepreneurial attitude that builds support
- Strong communication skills and interpersonal presence that extends confidence and humility
- Experience building and delivering executive level presentations.
- Ability to innovate and solve unique and complex problems with broad impact on the business.
- Critical thinking and ability to influence and communicate effectively with multiple internal stakeholders (technologists, engineering, Ops, finance, marketing, strategy).
- Ability to anticipate customer needs, business and regulatory issues; recommend product, process or service improvements.
- Excellent time management and project management skills.
