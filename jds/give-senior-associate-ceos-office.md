Role overview: Give works to increase giving in India, through give.do (individual giving: crowdfunding, monthly giving, workplace giving) and Give Grants (company services: impact advisory, an outsourced CSR team, tech platforms, and research). The company has about 100 people, all remote.

The role is a two-year starting position in the CEO's Office, working directly with the CEO. It is intended as a stepping stone to larger responsibilities at Give. The posting describes three main areas:
1. Cross-team initiatives: Own organization-wide initiatives from start to finish.
2. Performance tracking: Monitor each division's performance and turn the data into insights about what is working and why.
3. New opportunities: Evaluate new ideas through research, conversations, quick prototypes, and tests, then decide whether to pursue or drop them.

Qualifications and fit:
- Early career, with 0-2 years of work experience and a strong academic record.
- A master's degree or professional qualification (such as MBA, MSW, or CA) is preferred. Bachelor's degree holders may apply if the rest of the profile fits.
- Interest in building a career in social impact, CSR, or philanthropy.
- Works effectively with AI tools and thinks about getting work done with AI.
- Comfortable with technology and systems. Tech experience or education is a plus.
- Strong finance skills: can read a balance sheet, build a simple financial model, and notice inconsistencies in numbers.

What the role is not:
- Not an executive assistant role.
- No prior social impact sector experience required.
- Not an advisory role. It is an execution-focused role involving independent work, detailed hands-on tasks, and decision-making.

Hiring process: An initial conversation, an assignment, and two interview rounds.

How to apply: Apply via LinkedIn, or email a CV and a cover letter of up to 300 words on "why we must talk" to talentacquisition@give.do.
