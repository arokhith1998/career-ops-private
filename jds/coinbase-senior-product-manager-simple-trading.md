## Full JD text (verbatim)

Ready to do the most impactful work of your career? At Coinbase, we are uncompromising on our mission to increase economic freedom. The bar is high, the environment is intense, and we like it that way. Coinbase is a remote-first, but not remote-only company. Expect to get together quarterly for intense in-person working sessions called "surges."

As a Senior Product Manager on the Simple Trading team within the Consumer & Business group, you'll own core parts of the retail trading experience used by millions of customers every day. This team builds delightful user experiences and UIs that optimize the primary buy, sell, and convert flows that drive Coinbase's consumer trading business. You'll define the product vision for your area, ship high-impact improvements across web and mobile, and partner closely with Engineering, Design, and Data Science.

**What you'll do:**
- Own the end-to-end product roadmap for your area of the Simple Trading experience, from discovery and definition through execution, launch, and iteration across web and mobile UIs and platforms.
- Drive measurable improvements in trading conversion, user engagement, and revenue by identifying friction points, running experiments, and shipping data-informed solutions at scale.
- Partner with Engineering, Design, and Data Science to deliver high-quality consumer trading experiences, balancing speed, reliability, and usability.
- Shape the long-term product strategy for retail trading by synthesizing user research, market trends, regulatory requirements, and competitive dynamics.
- Lead cross-functional alignment across Consumer Trade and partner teams, ensuring stakeholders across Tax, Legal, Compliance, and Finance are informed and engaged.

**Required Skills and Experience:**
- 5+ years of Product Management experience shipping UI-heavy consumer products at scale, including at least 2 years working on trading, fintech, or marketplace products where conversion and revenue metrics were primary success measures.
- Deep familiarity with retail trading products, order types, and user workflows.
- Proven ability to define and execute a product roadmap in high-ambiguity environments.
- Deep expertise in experimentation methodology, including designing A/B tests, defining success metrics, and interpreting results with minimal analyst support.
- Fluency with data tools (SQL, Amplitude, or similar) sufficient to self-serve analyses and monitor product health independently.
- Track record of driving alignment across senior stakeholders and cross-functional teams.
- Utilizes generative AI responsibly, maintaining human oversight to deliver business-ready outputs.
