At Clover Health, we are committed to providing high-quality, affordable, and easy-to-understand healthcare plans for America’s seniors. We prioritize preventive care while leveraging data and technology through the Clover Assistant, a powerful tool that helps physicians make informed health recommendations. By giving doctors a holistic view of each member’s complete health history, we ensure better care at a lower cost—delivering the highest value to those who need it most.

The Finance organization owns product strategy and bid development, long term profitable growth strategy and new market evaluation and execution, and strategic projects. In this role, you will report to the Chief of Staff to the CFO.

Clover Health is seeking a dynamic, strategic, and highly analytical leader to join our team as the Senior Manager, Medicare Product & Market Intelligence. This pivotal role is responsible for long-term profitable growth strategy, bid strategy and pricing, AEP planning, and execution of strategic projects.

Reporting to the Chief of Staff to the CFO, this role will lead the development and execution of Medicare growth, product, and bid strategies, bringing together financial, market, operational, and competitive insights to influence enterprise decisions. The Senior Manager will frame strategic options, recommend tradeoffs, and align cross-functional leaders around plans that support profitable growth.

This is an ideal opportunity for a strategic thinker who excels at complex problem-solving and can translate data into actionable, market-specific plans, and execution.
