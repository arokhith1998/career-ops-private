## Description (verbatim, Greenhouse content field)
Our mission at Oura is to empower every person to own their inner potential... We are looking for a Packaging Product Manager to drive the vision, strategy, and execution of Oura's packaging experiences. Packaging is often the first physical touchpoint with our customers, and this role is critical in ensuring that our packaging reflects our brand values, protects our products, and delivers a world-class unboxing experience.

**What You Will Do:**
Packaging Ownership: Own Oura's packaging roadmap, aligning with product launches, global and retail expansion, and partnerships. Lead packaging projects through research, design, prototyping, testing, validation/approvals, production, and scaling. Oversee in-box content (quick-start guides, compliance sheets).

Requirements Management: Write and manage packaging requirements (PRDs)/briefs; coordinate with product managers and product marketing managers; identify and resolve packaging-related tradeoffs.

Customer & Brand Experience: Ensure packaging delivers a premium, on-brand unboxing experience; champion environmentally responsible materials and processes.

Market & User Insights: Conduct competitive benchmarking and customer feedback analysis.

Communication & Collaboration: Partner with design, operations, manufacturing, and marketing; communicate strategy and progress to stakeholders.

"This is an in-office role in our San Francisco office."

**Requirements:**
- "3+ years of experience in product management"
- Experience with packaging development from concept to production
- Knowledge of packaging materials, manufacturing processes, and supply chain considerations
- Knowledge of packaging standards, testing certifications and compliance requirements
- Excellent communication and interpersonal skills

**Nice to have:** experience in consumer electronics, wearables, or premium consumer goods; experience working with global teams and suppliers.
