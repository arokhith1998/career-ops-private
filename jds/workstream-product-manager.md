## Full JD text (source: Greenhouse public job-board API, content=true)
Workstream is a mission-driven company building the all-in-one HR, payroll, and hiring platform for managing the hourly workforce. There are 2.7 billion hourly workers, making up 80% of the global workforce, but this market has been heavily underserved by technology and deserves better. Workstream has been purpose-built for the hourly workforce from day one so that these businesses and their employees can thrive.

Our customers include leading brands from multiple sectors, including Burger King, Carl's Jr./Hardee's, IHOP, KFC, and Culvers. We are a high growth series B company and quickly expanding our product portfolio to deliver on our vision. We are backed by legendary VCs and industry experts like Founders Fund, BOND, and Coatue.

### Grow With Us

We are looking for a highly customer-obsessed, strategic, and execution-driven Product Manager to own key product areas across Workstream’s platform.

You'll own your product area end-to-end — defining its strategy, roadmap, and long-term vision. From customer discovery and hypothesis development through execution, iteration, and measurable business outcomes, you'll shape the product narrative, set north-star metrics, and drive the investments that improve how hourly businesses hire, manage, and retain their workforce.

This role is built for someone who thrives in fast-paced startup environments, genuinely enjoys spending time with customers, and pairs sharp strategic thinking with relentless execution. You'll partner closely with Engineering, Design, Sales, Customer Success, Business Development, and Leadership to surface opportunities, prioritize ruthlessly, and ship products that move the needle.

This is a full-time, hybrid position with 1 to 3 days a week in-office to support close cross-functional collaboration.

### Day In The Life

- Own product strategy, roadmap, and execution for a core Workstream product area

- Define product vision, north star metrics, and success criteria aligned with company priorities

- Spend significant time with customers, users, and customer-facing teams to identify patterns, pain points, and emerging market opportunities

- Partner closely with Sales, Customer Success, Business Development, and Operations to gather business requirements and translate them into scalable product solutions

- Build and manage relationships with strategic external partners (e.g. job boards, integrations, ecosystem partners) and continuously improve product capabilities through these partnerships

- Conduct market, competitive, and customer research to develop and validate product hypotheses

- Take a hypothesis-driven, experimentation-first approach to product discovery and iteration

- Write detailed product specifications, user stories, and UX requirements that enable high-quality execution

- Collaborate closely with Engineering and Design teams to ship products quickly while balancing speed, quality, and long-term scalability

- Be highly resourceful and pragmatic in solving customer problems—whether through automation, integrations, operational workflows, or lightweight solutions

- Drive alignment across Product, Engineering, Design, GTM, and executive stakeholders

- Present product strategy, roadmap progress, and business impact across all levels of the organization
