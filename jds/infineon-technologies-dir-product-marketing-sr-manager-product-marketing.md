Your Role

Key responsibilities in your new role

- Develop and drive differentiated product definition and business plans with target markets, customers, applications, partners, and relevant go-to-market strategies for Wi-Fi combo connectivity solutions
- Define and drive product development and planning of hardware/software/system solutions to enable ease of use, and to lead key customer engagements
- Translate customer needs into use cases and technical requirements for R&D
- Create marketing collaterals including value proposition, competitive positioning, product features and milestones, whitepapers, etc
- Work closely with global sales and regional marketing teams to engage lead customers in key market segments
- Collaborate with engineering, applications team, project management, and operations to guide product execution, priorities and delivery.
- Periodic domestic and international travel required to our global sites and key customers in North America, Europe and Asia

Your Profile

Qualifications And Skills To Help You Succeed

- 8+ years of experience in product marketing, product management, applications, or engineering role
- Strong knowledge of wireless connectivity technology (Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, 15.4, Thread, Matter, and others), and embedded systems and software
- Proven track record of defining and delivering successful connectivity products to market
- Strong understanding of automotive and consumer ecosystem and customer use cases for high performance Wi-Fi
- Exceptional communication and presentation skills
- Self-driven, organized with attention to detail. Ability to motivate and influence customers and internal stakeholders
- Master's/Bachelor’s in Electrical or Computer Engineering, MBA preferred
