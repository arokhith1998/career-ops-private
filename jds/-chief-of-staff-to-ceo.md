We are looking for a high-calibre Chief of Staff to work closely with the CEO of a fast-growing organisation and drive strategic priorities, growth initiatives and execution across the business.

📍 Ahmedabad | 💼 10–12+ Years | 💰 50–60 LPA
👩 Preferably Female | CA / MBA preferred
🏢 Industry: Management Consulting / Business Transformation

What we’re looking for:
• Strong business & commercial acumen with an entrepreneurial mindset
• Proven experience in strategy, business transformation, growth initiatives or operations
• Ability to work closely with the CEO as a strategic thought partner
• Highly agile and comfortable operating in a fast-paced, constantly evolving environment
• Strong-headed, resilient and thick-skinned, with the confidence to challenge and influence
• Excellent stakeholder management, analytical and communication skills
• Someone who can bring structure to ambiguity and drive execution with speed and ownership

This is a high-impact CEO Office role for someone who enjoys solving complex problems, driving change and making things happen.
