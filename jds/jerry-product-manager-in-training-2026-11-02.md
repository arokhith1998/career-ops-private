Jerry.ai is a Series C, YC-backed tech startup with 5M+ customers and $240M in funding. Founded in 2017, product launched in 2019, and achieved profitability in 2024.

Our next chapter is about accelerating growth - from 5M to 50M customers by 2030.

This year alone, we launched 5 new products - home insurance, property tax, motorcycle insurance, car repair shop bids, and roadside assistance. Each of these new businesses comes with its own operational complexity, distinct customer pain points, and its own path to scale.
