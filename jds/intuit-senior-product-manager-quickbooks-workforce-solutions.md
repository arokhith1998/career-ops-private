## Full job description

Intuit's Global Business Solutions Group (GBSG) is seeking a Senior Product Manager to join the Workforce Solutions Product Management organization within the Employment Policies capability, transforming Workforce Solutions from a reactive system into a proactive, intelligent compliance platform for small and mid-market businesses. The role owns the Time Off (PTO) policy experience and contributes to a stronger onboarding journey.

**Responsibilities:** Own the roadmap and day-to-day execution for Time Off (PTO) policy features within QuickBooks Payroll, working toward a consolidated experience across QuickBooks Payroll and QuickBooks Time. Define product requirements and success metrics for Time Off features, and partner with engineering and compliance teams to ensure accuracy across federal, state, and local jurisdictions. Support Workforce Setup / Onboarding experiences and the Overview page. Collaborate with cross-functional teams (engineering, design, GTM) to deliver end-to-end improvements. Use data, user feedback, and AI tools to identify friction points and translate them into clear product requirements. Contribute customer and data insights to the broader Employment Policies roadmap.

**Qualifications:** 2+ years in product management, ideally with exposure to Payroll, Time Management, HR compliance, or related domains. Strong project management and execution skills. Comfortable operating within a defined scope while partnering with senior/Principal PMs on a shared roadmap. Working knowledge of, or strong interest in, multi-jurisdictional compliance fluency (US federal/state/local, Canada and UK bonus). Excellent ability to translate customer and stakeholder input into clear requirements, using AI tools to work efficiently.
