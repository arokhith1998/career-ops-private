About us: We are a well-funded, seed-stage sodium-ion battery company building grid-scale stationary energy storage, founded by former Tesla leadership. We are scaling from R&D to our first pilot line, and we're moving fast.

About This Role We are seeking a highly motivated, action-oriented business generalist to be our first business operations hire as we scale from R&D to pilot scale at breakneck speed. You will work directly with our cofounders to execute cross-functional projects across the organization and do whatever it takes to keep us moving.

What you'll do. The following responsibilities include, but are not limited to:
- Own the financial model and the company's key metrics: forecasts, actuals, and making sense of it all.
- Track pilot line costs against budget and flag variances early.
- Run PO and vendor administration, spend tracking, and contract hygiene in partnership with Supply Chain.
- Support fundraising: pitch materials, data room, investor updates, board materials, and cap table.
- Support non-dilutive funding: DOE and state grant applications and reporting.
- Keep the day-to-day business running: outside accountants and counsel, insurance, vendor contracts, and whatever else it takes.

What you'll bring
- Bachelor's degree in a quantitative field, or equivalent experience
- 1-3 years of experience in management consulting, investment banking or similar analytically rigorous field
- Exacting attention to detail. Small errors get expensive.
- AI-native. Use LLMs and agents daily; build with them.
- High ownership; stays cool with ambiguity. Comfortable context switching and working vague problems.
- Crisp communication.

Bonus If You Have...
- Prior experience in a manufacturing environment or large-scale industrial projects

What We Bring / Work Environment
- Ground-floor opportunity to shape both the business and the culture of a well-funded seed-stage company
- Direct access to founders; no bureaucratic barriers to pursuing bold ideas
- Fast-paced, flexible environment
- Small, growing, international team from 7+ countries
- Onsite in Santa Clara, CA
- Work on a problem that matters. Sodium-ion is one of the most consequential battery chemistries of the next decade.

Benefits: As a full-time regular employee, you are eligible for the following benefits:
- Competitive salary + meaningful early-stage equity
