## Job Description (extracted, verbatim from posting)

Hiring a Senior Product Manager to own the roadmap for Braintree's mobile SDKs on iOS and Android, working with merchants and developers from solo sellers to enterprise Braintree teams. Tracks mobile trends, developer frameworks, how SDKs compare to market. Drives decisions through A/B testing. As developer behavior shifts toward AI coding assistants and LLMs, stays ahead of how these tools interact with the SDKs.

**Responsibilities:**
- Own product strategy and roadmap for Braintree's iOS and Android SDKs
- Partner with engineering and developer relations to prioritize features, drive SDK releases
- Write/maintain integration guides and documentation (including for AI coding agents - Markdown structuring, MCP servers)
- Gather/synthesize feedback from merchants, partners, internal stakeholders
- Define/track success metrics around SDK adoption and integration health
- Own go-to-market for shipped features (launch plan, docs, sample code, enablement)

**Minimum Qualifications:**
- "3+ years relevant experience and a Bachelor's degree OR Any equivalent combination of education and experience."

**Preferred Qualifications:**
- Strong experience with JIRA/Confluence
- "5 years of Mobile (iOS/Swift and Android/Kotlin) product management experience."
- SQL and data visualization tools (Tableau/Excel/Power BI)
- Nice to have: Mobile SDK experience specifically, API experience, familiarity with LLMs/AI coding assistants (MCP, context/plugin patterns)

PayPal's balanced hybrid model: 3 days in office, 2 days office or home.
