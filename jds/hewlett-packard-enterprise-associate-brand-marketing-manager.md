Who We Are
Hewlett Packard Enterprise is the global edge-to-cloud company advancing the way people live and work. We help companies connect, protect, analyze, and act on their data and applications wherever they live, from edge to cloud.

About The Role
As a Brand Marketing Manager, you'll help bring the HPE brand to life through integrated marketing campaigns, brand activations, content, and strategic programs. Working closely with teams across Brand, Communications, Product Marketing, Demand Generation, Events, Social, Digital, and Creative, you'll help execute marketing initiatives that build awareness, strengthen brand perception, and support business priorities.

This role is ideal for a marketer who loves turning ideas into action. You're organized, curious, collaborative, and excited about managing projects from concept through execution. You'll gain exposure to many facets of marketing while helping deliver impactful campaigns that connect with customers, partners, employees, and prospects.

Key Responsibilities

Brand Campaign Execution & Coordination
- Support the planning and execution of HPE's integrated brand awareness campaigns across multiple channels, including digital marketing, paid social, display advertising, web, events, video, and emerging media.
- Coordinate campaign timelines, project plans, and deliverables to ensure successful and timely execution.
- Partner with creative teams and agencies to develop campaign assets and marketing materials that align with business objectives.
- Assist in managing campaign assets and workflows between internal teams, agencies, and external partners.
- Help ensure marketing materials are delivered accurately and on schedule across all channels and markets.

Project Management & Collaboration
- Track campaign milestones, action items, and project progress while maintaining clear communication with stakeholders.
- Support cross-functional collaboration with brand, content, creative, digital, social, web, communications, and demand generation teams.
- Participate in campaign planning meetings and contribute ideas that support brand and business objectives.
- Help drive alignment across stakeholders and facilitate communication between teams.
- Coordinate reviews and approvals across key business partners to keep projects moving efficiently.
- Help manage relationships with agency partners and vendors to ensure projects are delivered efficiently.

Brand & Marketing Support
- Ensure campaign activities align with HPE's brand standards, messaging, and strategic priorities.
- Assist in maintaining consistency across campaign touchpoints and customer experiences.
- Support the development of marketing briefs, presentations, campaign reports, and stakeholder communications.
- Stay informed on industry trends, competitive activity, and marketing best practices to identify new opportunities for brand growth.

Performance & Optimization
- Monitor campaign performance and assist in analyzing marketing metrics and reporting.
- Work with marketing and analytics teams to track awareness, engagement, and conversion performance.
- Help identify insights and recommendations that improve future campaign effectiveness.
- Support reporting efforts, presentations, and executive updates.

Qualifications
- Bachelor's degree in marketing, communications, business, or related field.
- 5+ years of experience in brand marketing, campaign management, digital marketing, advertising, or a related role.
- Understanding of integrated marketing campaigns and digital marketing channels.
- Strong project management and organizational skills with the ability to manage multiple priorities.
- Excellent written, verbal, and presentation communication skills.
- Proficiency in Microsoft Office Suite (PowerPoint, Excel, Word, Teams).
- Familiarity with marketing analytics, campaign reporting, and performance metrics.
- Understanding of brand, paid media strategies, and integrated marketing campaigns.
- Strong attention to detail and commitment to quality execution.
- Ability to work collaboratively in a cross-functional team environment.
- Experience in B2B technology marketing is a plus.
- Basic knowledge of Adobe Creative Suite, project management tools, or content management systems is a plus.
- Interest in AI, cloud, networking, or enterprise technology trends.
