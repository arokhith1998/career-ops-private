## Job description (full text via LinkedIn guest API)

Reporting to: Founder, Kuku's (YNR Innovations Pvt. Ltd.)
Location: Hyderabad
Employment Type: Full-time

About the Role

We are looking for a highly organised, proactive, resourceful and dependable Executive Assistant to work directly with the Founder's Office. This is not a conventional administrative role. The candidate will work closely with the Founder across multiple businesses, initiatives, projects, social-impact activities and personal professional commitments.

Key Responsibilities

1. Founder's Office & Executive Support
- Manage the founder's daily calendar, appointments, meetings and schedules.
- Coordinate meetings with investors, clients, vendors, partners and other stakeholders.
- Prepare meeting agendas, take minutes and maintain follow-up records.
- Manage emails, calls, communications and important correspondence.
- Handle confidential information and sensitive business matters.
- Manage travel arrangements, bookings and itineraries when required.

2. Business Operations & Coordination
- Coordinate day-to-day activities across Kuku's and other business ventures.
- Follow up with employees, vendors, manufacturers, distributors and external partners.
- Track deliverables, deadlines, pending payments and operational requirements.
- Prepare reports, presentations, spreadsheets, proposals and business documents.
- Conduct research, identify opportunities and prepare information for decision-making.

3. Projects, Partnerships & New Initiatives
- Assist the founder in planning and executing new projects and business ideas.
- Coordinate with external agencies, collaborators, consultants and stakeholders.
- Research potential partnerships, sponsors, investors and business opportunities.
- Assist in preparing proposals, pitch decks and partnership presentations.

4. NGO & Social Impact Initiatives
- Coordinate activities related to the founder's NGO and social-impact initiatives.
- Assist with planning community programmes, awareness campaigns and events.
- Assist with fundraising activities, partnerships and CSR proposals.

5. Events & Public Relations
- Assist in organising business events, networking meets, workshops and community events.
- Handle invitations, guest lists, registrations and event logistics.
- Assist with influencer outreach, collaborations and PR coordination.

6. Research, Communication & Documentation
- Conduct online and offline research as required.
- Prepare professional emails, letters, reports, presentations and proposals.
- Maintain organised digital records, folders, databases and contact lists.

7. General Founder's Office Responsibilities
- Take ownership of tasks assigned by the founder, irrespective of the business, initiative or project.
- Be available to coordinate urgent requirements and time-sensitive assignments.
- Maintain confidentiality, professionalism and accountability at all times.

Skills & Qualifications
- Excellent verbal and written communication skills.
- Strong organisational and time-management abilities.
- Good command of English; Telugu and Hindi are an advantage.
- Proficiency in Google Workspace, Microsoft Office, Excel and PowerPoint.
- Comfortable using AI tools, online research platforms and digital productivity tools.
- Ability to multitask and manage multiple projects simultaneously.
- Professional attitude, discretion and attention to detail.

The Kind of Person We Are Looking For: proactive, able to manage responsibilities independently, comfortable in a fast-paced entrepreneurial environment, adaptable, strong sense of ownership and accountability.

Seniority level: Executive | Employment type: Full-time
