Job Purpose/Summary
We are looking for a Chief of Staff to work closely with the CEO, acting as a strategic partner in driving key business priorities, execution excellence, and organizational alignment.

This role is designed for someone who thrives in high-impact, high-visibility environments and can operate at the intersection of strategy, execution, and leadership.

Job Description
The Chief of Staff will act as a strategic partner to the CEO, driving business priorities, execution excellence, and organizational alignment. This role requires a high-caliber professional who can seamlessly operate across strategy, operations, and leadership, ensuring that key initiatives are executed with speed and precision.

Responsibility
- Collaborate directly with the CEO on strategic initiatives, business priorities, and critical decision-making processes.
- Drive cross-functional projects across various business units, ensuring timely execution and measurable outcomes.
- Act as a thought partner in shaping business strategy, identifying growth opportunities, and solving complex problems.
- Track and drive key business metrics reviews and governance mechanisms to ensure alignment with organizational goals.
- Facilitate effective communication and alignment between leadership teams and key stakeholders.
- Lead special projects including new business initiatives, transformation programs, and operational improvements.
- Bring structure to ambiguity and ensure execution rigor across all functions, maintaining a focus on results.
