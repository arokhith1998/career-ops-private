About the Team

The Lifecycle Marketing team owns Plaid's owned-channel strategy and partners with teams across the business. As part of the Growth Marketing organization, we focus on driving sustainable growth and long-term retention.

We build data-driven lifecycle programs that engage people at every stage of their journey with Plaid. As we expand into consumer products, we're shaping the experience that helps people understand, trust, and get lasting value from Plaid across push, in-app, email, and SMS.

About the Role

You'll lead the design and execution of lifecycle strategy for Plaid's consumer audience. Using behavioral data and a modern consumer engagement platform, you'll deliver timely, personalized experiences that help users reach value quickly and keep coming back.

You'll work closely with product, product marketing, growth marketing, design, and analytics, moving fast with a small team to turn product insights into journeys and learnings into the next experiment.

This is a chance to build the foundation for consumer engagement at Plaid. The trust we build with people strengthens the network our business customers rely on, so every touchpoint you design contributes to Plaid's long-term growth.

Responsibilities
- Design and launch lifecycle journeys: Build automated, behavior-triggered journeys for onboarding, activation, and recurring usage, including re-engagement for users who stall or lapse.
- Define and drive activation: Partner with product to identify and validate the activation moment and instrument the key events behind it.
- Segment and personalize: Use behavioral, lifecycle-stage, and product data to deliver relevant messaging across push, in-app, email, and SMS, refining segmentation as data grows.
- Own the consumer engagement platform: Serve as the hands-on owner of our engagement platform (Braze, Iterable, or similar), building audiences, triggers, workflows, and frequency and suppression logic.
- Experiment and iterate: Build and run a testing roadmap across messaging, timing, channel, and flow design, using holdouts and clear success metrics.
- Collaborate cross-functionally: Work with product, PMM, growth marketing, design, analytics, brand, and comms to align on launches and keep the experience consistent.
- Support launches and campaigns: Partner on feature releases and integrated launches, using lifecycle channels to drive awareness and adoption.
- Analyze and report: Track onboarding completion, time-to-activation, D7/D30 retention, and weekly active rate using BI and product analytics tools, and share clear recommendations with stakeholders.

Qualifications
- 5+ years of experience in lifecycle, email, or CRM marketing, preferably with B2C and B2B products
- Proven track record of designing and running lifecycle campaigns that measurably improve conversion
- Hands-on experience with engagement platforms like Braze, Iterable, or Customer.io
- Expertise in segmentation and personalization, with a deep understanding of consumer behavior and engagement best practices
- Data-driven, with experience using BI tools like Tableau (or similar) to report on, analyze, and improve campaign performance
- Strong communicator who collaborates well across marketing and GTM teams and is a trusted lifecycle channel expert
- Thrives in fast-paced, ambiguous environments and adapts well to change
- Proactive self-starter with a builder's mindset who enjoys developing and scaling marketing programs
