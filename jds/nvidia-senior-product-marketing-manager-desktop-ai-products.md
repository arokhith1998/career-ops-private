What You'll Be Doing
- Own product marketing and positioning of NVIDIA desktop products with precision.
- Lead cross-functional teams to execute business-critical go-to-market strategies, from planning through launch and post-launch optimization.
- Represent NVIDIA product and messaging to OEM and channel partners, ensuring consistent, compelling messaging across all partners.
- Collaborate alongside product, sales, and marketing teams to bring complex, first-of-kind technologies to market with clarity and impact.
- Train and coordinate NVIDIA regional sales alongside marketing teams to drive pipeline and sell-through.

What We Need To See
- 12+ overall years of experience in product marketing for highly technical products, with a track record of successful launches.
- Ability to articulate value propositions to both technical and non-technical audiences with clarity.
- Deep familiarity with AI/ML technologies and the evolving landscape of AI-accelerated computing.
- Bachelor's Degree or equivalent experience; MBA preferred.

Ways To Stand Out From The Crowd
- Proven experience engaging with OEM, direct, and retail/etail channels, with a demonstrated history of measurable success.
- Extensive experience in the PC and/or workstation industry with a deep understanding of market dynamics.
- Experience in software developer or technical marketing crafting AI models, tools, technologies and demos.
- Demonstrated success in owning and growing a product line go to market activities, showcasing ambitious growth.
- Experience leading product launches at scale, including analyst and influencer relations, to amplify market impact.
