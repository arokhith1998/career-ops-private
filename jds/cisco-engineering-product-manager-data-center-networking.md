Cisco Data Center Networking is the infrastructure foundation for the AI era. We are a global leader in technology innovation, dedicated to delivering advanced networking solutions that power AI factories and data centers across the globe.

We are committed to fostering a collaborative, diverse and inclusive work environment where creativity and innovation can thrive.

We are seeking a Senior Product Manager to manage our product portfolio and drive the product definition of new products.

Your Impact

- Lead and collaborate with cross-functional teams to help define Cisco's Data Center Networking roadmap for secure, scalable AI/ML fabrics and enterprise data center fabrics

- Define new product requirements and enhancements with clear value proposition for customers
- Influence and work with engineering, supply chain, finance, customer service, sales, and channel partner teams to build differentiated products and drive business.
- Own the complete product lifecycle for the products from product definition to product launch to product end-of-life and everything in between.
- Develop business plans for product offerings including market analysis, competitive analysis, financial models, and pricing proposals
- Translate product capabilities into simple, compelling messages that resonate with customers
- Build impactful product marketing content and collateral such as presentations, blogs, and videos
- Provide solution/product briefings to customers or partners and cultivate relationships
- Represent or present Cisco's Data Center Networking vision and solution/product innovations at internal and external events
- Partner with the broader marketing and sales team to amplify product messaging and drive market success

Minimum Qualifications

- Bachelor's degree in Electrical Engineering, Computer Science, or related technical fields
- 8+ years of working on networking concepts, protocols, and architectures, or related areas
- Experience with data center networking technologies and working with cross-functional teams to bring new products or solutions to market

Preferred Qualifications

- MBA or Master's degree in Electrical Engineering, Computer Science, or related technical fields
- 3+ years of product management experience in related areas
- Solid understanding of data center networking concepts, technologies, and system design
- Experience in financial analysis, forecasting, market analysis, and competitive analysis
- Experience in ramping up and sustaining products by building opportunity pipeline and closing bookings
