Program Manager, MD & CEO's OfficeKeep ONDC's priority initiatives on track and turn them into clear takeaways for leadership.New Delhi | 4-10 years | Full-time | In Office

About ONDCONDC is building an open network for digital commerce in India — enabling buyers, sellers, logistics providers and technology platforms to connect and transact across an interoperable network.

We are working at the intersection of technology, commerce, regulation and public digital infrastructure. The problems are complex, the ecosystem is evolving, and many of the challenges do not come with an established playbook.

ONDC is a Digital Public Infrastructure initiative under DPIIT, Government of India. It enables open, interoperable digital commerce across Retail, Logistics, Mobility, Transit and Tourism, and Financial Services, and builds shared infrastructure such as DigiCatalog that the wider ecosystem can use.

That is where you come in.

The RoleThe MD & CEO's Office tracks and drives ONDC's priority initiatives, prepares leadership for reviews with the board, government and partners, and runs programs that cut across teams. This role sits in that office.

You will keep track of where every priority initiative stands, work with data to turn it into clear takeaways for leadership, and take day-to-day ownership of selected programs and special projects.

It is a hands-on role with wide exposure. You will work with business, product, technology, analytics, legal and finance teams, and with government and industry stakeholders, across all of ONDC's verticals.

What You'll Own- Maintain a single, current tracker for ONDC's priority initiatives, covering status, owners, timelines, dependencies and risks across Business, Product and Tech, and Operations.
- Follow up with initiative owners on deliverables and timelines, and close the loop on action items until they are done.
- Spot delays, blockers and cross-team dependencies early, and escalate them with a clear view of what is needed to resolve them.
- Run review cadences: prepare agendas and pre-reads, record decisions and action items, and circulate follow-ups.
- Take day-to-day ownership of selected programs, such as network participant support programs, audit and compliance workstreams, and internal process or tooling rollouts, from planning through to closure.
- Work with the Analytics team to pull and analyse network and business data, including transactions, network participant onboarding and initiative KPIs, and turn it into clear takeaways and next steps.
- Build and maintain trackers and MIS reports for leadership reviews, along with monthly and quarterly progress updates.
- Track progress against the annual operating plan and quarterly targets, and consolidate inputs from teams during planning cycles.
- Prepare accurate, verified numbers for government reviews, parliamentary questions and external data requests.
- Draft and prepare briefs, notes and presentations for leadership reviews, board materials, ministry and government meetings, and partner discussions.
- Build decks end to end: shape the storyline, write slide titles that state the takeaway, and check every number before it goes to leadership.
- Write notes, emails and talking points for senior leadership, pitched right for the audience, from ministries to industry CEOs.
- Turn large volumes of unstructured input into crisp one-pagers, summaries and decks.
- Support recurring stakeholder communication, such as newsletters and periodic updates to government and industry.
- Use AI tools day to day to speed up research, first drafts, data analysis and tracking, and help the office find better ways of working with them
- Set up simple AI-assisted workflows for recurring work such as status updates, meeting notes and report formatting.
What Success Looks LikeLeadership has a single, current view of every priority initiative, and reviews with the board, government and partners go in with verified numbers and clear takeaways. Blockers are escalated early, action items are closed, and the programs you own run from planning through to closure.

What We're Looking For- 4-10 years of experience in program or project management, consulting, strategy, business operations or a PMO role.
- Strong working skills in Google Sheets or Excel, and comfort working with data to find trends and explain what they mean.
- Excellent writing: clear, crisp notes, briefs, emails and correspondence for senior government, board and industry audiences, with a good ear for tone.
- Strong presentation skills: able to structure a storyline, write slide titles that carry the takeaway, and turn rough inputs into polished, executive-ready decks.
- Smart, everyday use of AI tools (such as Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini) for research, drafting, analysis and building trackers, with the judgement to check outputs before they go out.
- Strong organisational skills and attention to detail, with a habit of following things through to closure.
- Ability to work with people across levels and functions and get things done without formal authority.
- Comfort with ambiguity and shifting priorities, and a fast learner across domains, from tech integrations to on-ground pilots.
- Bachelor's degree in any discipline.
- Preferred: exposure to technology, e-commerce, digital public infrastructure or government.

This Role Is For You If...You like keeping a complex set of initiatives on track and following things through to closure.

You enjoy turning data and unstructured input into crisp takeaways for senior audiences.

You write and present with clarity and a good ear for tone.

You use AI tools to work better, and you check the output before it goes out.

Why ONDC?This is an opportunity to work on open digital infrastructure at national scale — at the intersection of technology, commerce and policy.

You won't just do a job.

You will help shape the ecosystem it is building.
