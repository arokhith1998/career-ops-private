## Role summary
Owns deal pricing, renewal pricing, deal structuring, and deal analytics for Netradyne's global enterprise business. Advises Sales on pricing and structure; runs the deal desk's quote-to-approval workflow and price book governance. Reports to the Sr. Director of Revenue Operations.

## Responsibilities
- Modeling multi-year, bundled hardware and subscription deals
- Setting renewal uplift and escalator policy
- Building deal and pricing analytics and dashboards (Tableau or Power BI, Salesforce, DealHub, NetSuite)
- Advising on revenue recognition (ASC 606) for non-standard terms
- Coaching Sales on alternatives to discounting
- Driving process automation with Business Systems and IT

## Required qualifications

- Strong Excel/Sheets modeling and a BI tool; SQL preferred

## Preferred qualifications
