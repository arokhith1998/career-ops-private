Mynzo Carbon is hiring for our Founder's Office. This role is for someone who wants to work directly with the founder and run everything at Mynzo except the tech: business development, programs, compliance, growth and operations. It is not a support role.

About Mynzo Carbon: Mynzo is a climate-tech company helping governments, project developers and large corporates make Net Zero real with assets, not offsets. We use AI and satellite data to measure carbon, track biodiversity and monitor green cover down to the individual tree.

Responsibilities:
- Business development: research and qualify prospects, draft proposals/pitch decks/commercial offers, join client meetings with the founder, follow up on leads.
- Program and project management: run client projects day to day (timelines, field partners, deliverables, reporting); coordinate between tech team, on-ground partners and clients; set up trackers and review rhythms.
- Compliance and governance: manage statutory, legal and secretarial compliance with CA, CS and legal advisors; support third-party audits, certifications and client vendor onboarding; keep contracts and company records organised.
- Growth and strategy: work on fundraising material, investor updates and data room upkeep; prepare applications for accelerators, grants, awards and industry events; track market, competitors and climate regulations (BRSR, TNFD, SBTi and others).
- Operations: handle hiring coordination, vendor management, finance follow-ups and internal processes; pick up whatever the founder needs closed.

Qualifications - Must have: Graduate from a Tier 1 or Tier 2 college, in commerce or science; 2 to 4 years of work experience; clear, crisp writing and comfort presenting to senior stakeholders; strong with Excel or Sheets, slides and documents.

Good to have: Prior experience in a Founder's Office, investment banking or program management role; exposure to a startup or a fast-moving team; interest in climate, sustainability or ESG.

Location: Gurugram (Hybrid).
