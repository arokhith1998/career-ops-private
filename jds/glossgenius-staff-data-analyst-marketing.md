## Job Description
Set analytical direction for the Go-to-Market team while leading a group of marketing analysts. Focus on evolving marketing measurement strategy and driving growth machine efficiency.

## Responsibilities
- Partner with GTM teams to execute high-impact campaigns, experiments, and projects
- Lead, mentor, and develop marketing analyst teams with emphasis on analytical rigor
- Identify optimization and revenue growth opportunities through funnel and performance analysis
- Own multi-touch attribution models and channel lift-testing improvements
- Define and govern marketing metrics (CAC, LTV, payback period) across teams
- Implement analytical frameworks with Data Platform and peer Analytics teams
- Leverage AI tools and automation to scale analysis capabilities
- Serve as strategic advisor to go-to-market leadership

## Requirements
- 7+ years analytical experience; 3+ years marketing analytics/science (preferably SaaS)
- 1+ year directly managing or leading analyst teams
- Demonstrated AI tool usage (Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor)
- Strong experimentation lifecycle knowledge
- Statistical proficiency (regression, A/B testing, media mix modeling)
- SQL and Python proficiency
- Legal work authorization in the US
- Ability to work in NYC office

## Preferred
- Experience with Hex, Looker, Tableau, or similar visualization tools
- Familiarity with CRM/Martech tools (Iterable, Hubspot, Eppo)
