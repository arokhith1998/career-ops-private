## Description

Ready to do the most impactful work of your career? At Coinbase, we are uncompromising on our mission to increase economic freedom. The bar is high, the environment is intense, and we like it that way. Coinbase is a remote-first, but not remote-only company. Expect to get together quarterly for intense in-person working sessions called "surges."

The brand team at Coinbase leads defining the vision for the Coinbase brand and how this can be best delivered in marketing and across the company. We are looking for a senior strategist to bring brand and product closer together, and ensure everything we do flows from and reinforces the Coinbase brand.

What you'll do:
- Close the gap between what Coinbase promises and what customers experience by helping the brand shape and impact every surface, from GTM to product to CX. This role sits at the center of Integrated Marketing and owns how the brand travels beyond campaigns into the way we build, launch, and show up across the company.
- Author creative GTM strategies for product and partner initiatives, working closely with Product Marketing and Partnership teams, and help shape the output from these briefs.
- Work with product marketers and partnership marketers to drive strategy upstream into the products delivered and the partnerships built, and the role within them.

Required Skills and Experience:
- Experience setting brand and product strategy either internally at companies or at agencies
- Experience in shaping go to market strategy
- Experience in delivering a brand across an organization, not just inside the marketing org
- Strong persuasive writing skills, comfortable partnering with Insights to shape and design research and apply it to the problems being solved
- Utilizes generative AI responsibly, maintaining human oversight to deliver business-ready outputs and drive measurable improvements in workflow efficiency, cost, and quality
- Demonstrates the ability to responsibly use generative AI tools and copilots (e.g., LibreChat, Gemini, Glean) in daily workflows, continuously learn as tools evolve, and apply human-in-the-loop practices to deliver business-ready outputs and drive measurable improvements in efficiency, cost, and quality
