Excels at navigating complex global organizations to find impactful innovation narratives. Conceptualize customer advocacy strategy, advise on storytelling best practices, partner with customers on innovation narrative, and manage major global programs including CX's involvement in the Cisco Customer Achievement Awards. Experienced marketing professional with proven record in Customer Advocacy and Strategic Communications, ideally in global technology or B2B. Strong consulting skills and deep understanding of how to translate technical milestones into business-focused success stories.
