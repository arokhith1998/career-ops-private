## Full JD text

AMAX is a global provider of AI, HPC, GPU, and advanced computing infrastructure solutions, supporting enterprise, research, and cloud customers. Looking for a hands-on pricing professional who understands B2B hardware cost structures, supply dynamics, and deal-based pricing.

This role owns pricing strategy and business optimization for AMAX's AI, HPC, GPU, and server infrastructure solutions -- cost structure, supply constraints, customer-specific deal execution. Ideal candidate has hands-on pricing experience in B2B computing hardware.

Primary Responsibilities:
- Own end-to-end pricing strategy across assigned product lines (servers, GPU systems, integrated infrastructure solutions).
- Translate component-level cost structures (BOMs, GPU pricing, supplier terms) into competitive, profitable system pricing models.
- Analyze market dynamics, competitive positioning, customer demand for pricing/margin optimization opportunities.
- Monitor pricing performance at product/customer/deal levels; adjust based on market shifts, lifecycle stage, supply conditions.
- Support pricing negotiations with suppliers/partners/internal stakeholders (cost transparency, scenario analysis).
- Collaborate with sales, product, procurement, supply chain, engineering on deal-specific pricing proposals.
- Identify cost reduction/margin improvement opportunities across the supply chain/product configuration.
- Prepare executive-ready reports/recommendations.

Requirements:
- Bachelor's degree in Business, Finance, Engineering, or a related field.
- 1-3 years of hands-on pricing or business analysis experience in the computing hardware industry (servers, GPUs, storage, networking, or related).
- Proven ability to work with complex cost structures and variable supplier pricing.
- Strong analytical skills turning data into practical pricing actions.
- High attention to detail; strong ownership/accountability.
- Clear, confident communicator able to work cross-functionally.
- Advanced Excel skills; experience with pricing, BI, or data analysis tools.

Nice to have: direct experience in B2B infrastructure/AI/HPC; familiarity with GPU ecosystems, accelerated computing, server configurations, system-level pricing; experience supporting deal-based pricing with sales; comfortable with supply volatility/rapid cost changes.

Benefits: medical/dental/vision; FSA; HSA; 401k match; commuter benefits; life/accidental insurance; short/long-term disability. Located near I-680/I-880, Warm Springs/South Fremont BART, grocery/coffee/restaurants within 1 mile.

About AMAX: established 1979, global leader in GPU-accelerated IT infrastructure; AI, cloud computing, autonomous vehicles, HPC; pioneered liquid-cooled HPC systems for semiconductor industry; North America/Europe/Asia footprint.
