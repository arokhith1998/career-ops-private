## About OnePay

OnePay is the consumer fintech trusted by millions of Americans to make money better. All-in-one financial services platform: banking, high-yield savings, credit cards, point-of-sale lending, investing, and crypto. Backed by Walmart and Ribbit Capital.

## The Role

Credit cards are one of the most powerful products in consumer finance, and OnePay has built one with Walmart. The program launched in 2025 and is still early. You'll own a large part of the card, from the first screen a customer sees to the economics behind every reward.

- Own the growth and performance of the card across the customer journey: acquisition, activation, and ongoing engagement.
- Work on the rewards program and the economics behind it.
- Build and grow the product relationship with partners, bringing data and a point of view to decisions.
- Work with engineering and analytics to decide what to build, make the case for it, and monitor after launch.
- Work directly with the Product Lead on priorities and run big parts of the roadmap yourself.

## You Bring

- Ownership of a credit card product, or a real piece of one.
- A working grasp of card economics (interchange, rewards cost, interest income, credit losses) and how issuers, networks, processors, and partners fit together.
- Experience shipping with engineers, and comfort pulling and reading your own data.
- Results you can put numbers on, like approval rates, activation, spend, or losses.
- Experience working with partners outside your company, such as banks, card networks, or retailers.
