## Role overview (paraphrased)
Leads strategy, modeling, and financial execution for Devoted's Commercial
lines, including Group and Self-Funded/ASO. Centers on rating model
architecture, underwriting strategy, and state rate filings.

## Responsibilities (paraphrased)
- Build and maintain scalable actuarial rating models and pricing engines for
  Commercial Large Group
- Partner on benefit pricing, cost-sharing design, and profitability
  trade-offs
- Establish and document pricing assumptions (manual rates, age/gender and
  area factors, trend, expense allocations, margin targets)
- Provide actuarial oversight for underwriting, including experience rating
  and credibility logic
- Design ASO, fee structure, and stop-loss pricing methodologies
- Direct annual and quarterly Commercial rate filings across multiple state
  regulators
- Serve as primary actuarial contact for state regulators during rate
  reviews
- Oversee ACA risk adjustment, MLR rebates, state rate caps, No Surprises Act
  pricing mechanics
- Work with Finance, Healthcare Economics, Sales, Network Management,
  Clinical Operations
- Prepare executive and board-level presentations on filing strategy and
  margin projections

## Required (paraphrased, note the seniority band)

- Hands-on experience with underwriting alignment and portfolio performance

- Track record of meeting complex, time-sensitive deadlines
