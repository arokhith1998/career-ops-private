Job Description
Visa is seeking a strategic, analytically minded leader to help shape the future of North America Marketing. As Manager, Marketing Strategy & Portfolio Planning, you will serve as a key thought partner to marketing leadership, helping define how marketing investments support Visa's long-term growth ambitions and 2030 Strategy.
This role sits at the intersection of strategy, planning, portfolio management, and business performance. You will maintain a holistic view of Visa's North America Marketing portfolio across Consumer, B2B, and Marketing Services, ensuring investments, initiatives, and resources are focused on the opportunities that drive the greatest business impact.
Success in this role requires the ability to connect strategy to execution, translate complex data into actionable recommendations, and influence decision-making across senior stakeholders. You will work closely with Marketing, Product, Strategy, and Analytics teams to identify growth opportunities, assess portfolio performance, prioritize investments, and drive greater organizational impact.
This position reports to the Senior Director of Strategy, Planning & Operations and is based in Visa's San Francisco office.
This opportunity is especially attractive for professionals with backgrounds in strategy consulting, corporate strategy, business operations, analytics, portfolio management, or technology who enjoy solving complex business challenges, influencing executive decisions, and driving enterprise-wide outcomes.
Key Responsibilities
Portfolio Strategy & Business Planning:
Own and maintain a comprehensive view of Visa North America's Marketing portfolio, including strategic priorities, investment allocation, business objectives, and expected outcomes.
Partner with leadership to evaluate portfolio performance and identify opportunities to optimize investments, accelerate growth, and improve business impact.
Support annual and multi-year strategic planning efforts by providing data-driven perspectives on business priorities, market opportunities, and emerging trends.
Develop frameworks and approaches that help leadership prioritize resources and make informed investment decisions.
Strategic Analysis & Executive Decision Support
Transform complex data from multiple sources into actionable insights and strategic recommendations for executive audiences.
Identify connections across Consumer, B2B, and Marketing Services initiatives, proactively highlighting risks, dependencies, and opportunities.
Structure and solve ambiguous business problems by evaluating alternatives, quantifying trade-offs, and recommending clear paths forward.
Create executive-ready presentations, business cases, and strategic narratives that enable informed decision-making.
Cross-Functional Strategy Leadership
Partner closely with North America Product and Strategy teams to ensure marketing investments align with product roadmaps, business priorities, and market opportunities.
Serve as a strategic connector across Marketing, Analytics, Product, Finance, and regional teams to drive alignment and maximize portfolio effectiveness.
Lead special projects and strategic initiatives that require cross-functional collaboration and executive visibility.
Performance Measurement & Portfolio Optimization
Monitor portfolio performance against strategic goals and key business outcomes.
Analyze trends, identify performance gaps, and recommend actions to enhance effectiveness and accelerate results.
Drive continuous improvement in measurement frameworks, reporting approaches, and strategic planning processes.
Innovation & Future Readiness
Identify opportunities to leverage AI and emerging technologies to improve planning, decision-making, operational efficiency, and marketing effectiveness.
Pilot new approaches, develop business cases, and help scale innovative solutions across the organization.
Visa requires at least 3 days in office, expectations of these days will be confirmed by your Hiring Manager.
