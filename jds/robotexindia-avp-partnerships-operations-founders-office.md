## Full JD Text

About Robotex India
Robotex India is a non-profit on a mission to make future skills (robotics, coding, AI, IoT and STEAM) accessible to every child from government schools and colleges in India.

Founded in Pune in 2018, Robotex India brought one of the world's largest robotics festival, Robotex International, to Indian classrooms. Our focus has been on children who rarely get this opportunity: students in government, rural and tribal schools/colleges, majorly first-generation learners.

What we've built so far
- Scale: 1.5 million+ students reached across 15 states and 500+ schools
- Teachers: 300,000 teachers training in progress on AI in pedagogy with Microsoft
- Girls in STEM: 520,000+ girls brought into robotics, coding, STEM and AI
- Robotex for Rural: a CSR-funded programme, launched in 2019, that teaches coding, programming, AI and robotics to students from marginalised communities
- Government partnerships: we work directly with state governments and Zila Parishads so that programmes reach the most underserved communities
- India's first digital hackathon: organised during the pandemic with FICCI FLO Pune and the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology, keeping learning alive when schools shut
- National Championship: the Robotex India National Championship is one of the country's largest grassroots technology platforms. So far 250+ government school students won podium places and represented India at Robotex International in Estonia. All from government, rural and tribal schools.
- STEAM and robotics labs: set up in government schools and colleges through CSR sponsors
- Innoveda: our future-skills programme for schools and colleges, now expanding to new cities

The Role
This is the most important hire we'll make this year.

You'll be directly working with the Founder: a senior leader who shares the load across fundraising, grants, operations, growth and outreach, and who owns outcomes end to end. You'll be handed goals and trusted to get there.

This role suits a seasoned AVP or senior manager from the NGO, CSR or development sector. It also suits an exceptional, qualified Executive Manager or Chief of Staff who is ready to step up and grow into a leadership position. What matters most is ownership, judgement and drive.

You'll report directly to the Founder & Director and work closely with the programme, finance, outreach and digital teams.
