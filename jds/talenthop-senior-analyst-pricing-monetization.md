About Our Client:
The organization operates within the commerce and financial services industry, addressing the challenge that sellers face with disparate, outdated tools that do not integrate well. It provides integrated, omnichannel solutions that enable sellers to manage online sales, inventory, financing, appointments, customer loyalty, and staffing. This approach supports merchants of various sizes, from startups to enterprise-scale businesses, helping them grow and compete at larger scales.

About the Opportunity:
The SENIOR ANALYST, PRICING & MONETIZATION is responsible for shaping the pricing strategies of the organization's largest partnerships. This role impacts growth and profitability by developing custom pricing proposals, setting portfolio-level pricing targets, and refining processes that govern deal approvals. The position ensures that pricing decisions balance growth opportunities with margin protection and contributes to strategic financial decision-making.

Responsibilities:
- Advise on complex deals by partnering cross-functionally to build custom pricing and incentive proposals
- Design portfolio-level pricing targets and deal-level guardrails to enable quick decision-making while protecting margins
- Develop and improve processes for pricing and approving large deals across teams
- Analyze pricing trends within the portfolio and the broader payments industry and provide recommendations
- Present complex financial models and recommendations to senior leadership

Requirements:
- Minimum of 5 years of relevant experience with a Bachelor's degree; or 3 years with a Master's degree; or a PhD without experience; or equivalent work experience
- Advanced financial modeling skills, including building P&L and unit economics models from scratch
- Strong commercial judgment balancing growth, margin, and risk in deal-making
- Experience building trusted relationships with senior leaders and influencing decisions through analysis
- Proven ability to own projects from initial framing to decision implementation
- Aptitude for building repeatable systems and comfort using AI tools to scale work
