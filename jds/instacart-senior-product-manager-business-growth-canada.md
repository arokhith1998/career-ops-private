## Full JD text (verbatim)

We're transforming the grocery industry. At Instacart, we invite the world to share love through food. Instacart has become a lifeline for millions of people, and we're building the team to help push our shopping cart forward.

Instacart is a Flex First team. Our employees have the flexibility to choose where they do their best work.

**Overview:** Instacart Business is hiring a Senior Product Manager to drive growth on Instacart Business. You'll own how we acquire, activate, retain, and expand business customers across SMB, mid-market, and enterprise.

**About the Role:** You'll own the growth roadmap end to end -- setting the goals, sizing the opportunities, running the experiments, and deciding what to scale and what to kill. Instacart Business's features (team accounts, spending controls, invoicing, tax-exempt purchasing, integrations) are one of your biggest levers.

**About the Team:** Instacart Business brings Instacart's same-day delivery to companies of every size. Growth sits within the product team, alongside the PMs building payments, invoicing, and integrations.

**What You'll Do:**
- Own the business customer funnel: acquisition, activation, habit formation, retention, and winback across SMB, mid-market, and enterprise.
- Grow existing accounts: turn single-buyer accounts into team accounts and single-location customers into multi-location ones.
- Drive adoption of business features as a dedicated growth program.
- Build growth into the product: onboarding, in-product education, and lifecycle experiences.
- Grow business on retailer sites powered by Instacart.
- Grow through partners: co-marketing and integrations with Partnerships.
- Build and expand features that drive growth.
- Use AI in the product (personalization, targeting, churn prediction) and in your own workflow.

**Minimum Qualifications:**
- 5+ years of product management experience, including ownership of a growth area with measurable, durable results in acquisition, activation, retention, or lifecycle.
- Strong at experimentation and analysis; designs A/B tests, works in the data, makes clear calls on ambiguous results.
- Weighs tradeoffs between growth and profitability and between short-term wins and compounding levers.
- Has shipped product features end to end.
- Writes and communicates clearly; can align senior partners around a decision.

**Preferred Qualifications:**
- Experience with B2B, SMB, marketplace, or SaaS growth.
- Experience growing customers across segments, from SMB through enterprise, or driving account expansion.
- Experience partnering with Growth or Performance Marketing on attribution, channels, and budget.
- Experience with growth partnerships, co-marketing, or product integrations with external partners.
- Experience applying AI/ML to growth problems.
- Familiarity with grocery, retail, or CPG.
