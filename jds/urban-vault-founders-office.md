## Full job description (verbatim)

Role Overview

Urban Vault is looking for a Founder's Office professional to work directly with the Co-Founder & CEO on the company's most important problems: improving unit economics, sharpening decision-making across business units, and driving high-priority initiatives from idea to result.

This is not a coordination or reporting role. The ideal candidate is an owner who reads a P&L, spots what is broken or underused, makes a call, and follows it through until the numbers move.

Key Responsibilities

- Own and track the P&L at the company, city and centre level; know which centres, clients and business lines make money and why.
- Run the monthly business review (occupancy, revenue per seat, cost per sq. ft., EBITDA by centre, collections, cash flow) and turn the numbers into decisions on where to expand, which centres to fix or exit, how to price and where to put capital.
- Build financial models for new centres, lease deals, large enterprise contracts and fit-out projects, and give a clear go / no-go view.
- Find and capture cost savings across rent, CAM, utilities, housekeeping, security, F&B, maintenance and vendor contracts; renegotiate and consolidate vendors.
- Improve capex efficiency on new centre build-outs (cost per seat, time to go live) and set up cost controls so leakages are caught early.
- Drive occupancy and pricing: reduce vacant inventory, improve realisation per seat and keep discounting disciplined.
- Grow revenue through new streams and upsells (meeting rooms, parking, value-added services, fit-out and facility management) and work with sales on large-account strategy, deal structuring and renewals.
- Analyse churn and client feedback to fix the reasons clients leave.
- Lead high-priority cross-functional initiatives across sales, operations, projects, finance and HR, end to end.
- Takes in charge of new initiatives 
- Support decks and lender discussions; evaluate new markets and partnerships.
- Put in place the processes, metrics and operating rhythm that let the company scale without losing control.

Ideal Candidate Profile

- Experience: 4+ years in a Founder's Office, strategy, business finance, management consulting, investment banking / PE, or a P&L-owning business role.
- Financial Acumen: Can read, build and challenge a P&L, balance sheet and cash-flow model, with a strong grasp of unit economics.
- Track Record: Proven results in reducing costs and/or growing revenue, with measurable outcomes to point to.
- Ownership: Takes problems to closure without waiting to be told, and is comfortable being accountable for outcomes.
- Thinking: Sharp, structured and decisive under ambiguity; makes calls with incomplete data and adjusts fast.
- Communication: Can present to founders, enterprise clients, and push back with data.
- Tools: Advanced Excel / Google Sheets; comfortable with dashboards and AI tools.

Good to Have

- Experience in coworking / flexible workspaces, commercial real estate, hospitality, facility management or another multi-site, asset-heavy business.
- Experience in a high-growth startup or scale-up.
- MBA from a reputed institute.
- Hands-on experience reducing costs and increasing revenue, or building and driving a new business unit.

Key Attributes

P&L Ownership | Business Acumen | Decision-Making | Cost Discipline | Revenue Focus | Execution

What We Offer

- Work directly with the founders and see how a fast-growing company is built from the inside.
- Real ownership: your decisions show up in the P&L, not just in a deck.
- Broad exposure across real estate, sales, operations, finance.
- Competitive compensation, commensurate with experience.
- Group health insurance for self and family.
- A fast track to leadership roles as the company scales.
- A collaborative, low-hierarchy culture where direct communication is the norm.

Our Values

8 principles that define how we work, grow, and show up every day.

- 1. Pride in the Brand — The UV Standard. Our name means something. We show up worthy of it every day.
- 2. Customer Wins Are Our Wins — We don't just lease space. We earn trust. When they win, we win.
- 3. Ownership Over Excuses — Hold the keys, all the way. We don't hand problems back. We close them.
- 4. Build Like It Scales — Build for the tenth floor, not the first. Every process should work at 10× our current size.
- 5. Speed With Direction — Move fast. Know where you're going. Speed without clarity is just noise.
- 6. Integrity in Every Action — Do the right thing, even when no one's watching. Our word is our foundation.
- 7. Communicate Clearly & Directly — Glass walls, open doors. No hidden corridors. Say it straight.
- 8. Grow Together — No silos. One floor plan. We build leaders, invest in people, and win as one team.
