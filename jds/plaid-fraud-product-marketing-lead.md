## Full JD Text (verbatim, via browser-extract.mjs)
Plaid's Product Marketing team owns how the world understands what Plaid's platform does. Fraud Product Marketing owns Plaid's Fraud Intelligence products (Protect, Signal, identity verification) and how they turn the Plaid Network into decisions that stop fraud before it lands, with less friction for legitimate users.

"We work from evidence, not opinion. We treat positioning as a discipline, not a tagline exercise. And we build for the field: if sellers don't use it and buyers don't believe it, it doesn't count."

As Fraud Product Marketing Lead, owns positioning, go-to-market, and enablement for Plaid's Fraud Intelligence portfolio, and leads the team behind it. Takes Protect from beta into scaled adoption, defines how Plaid shows up against fraud incumbents, ensures campaign narrative matches what wins in the room. Reports to the Head of Product Marketing; works directly with Fraud Product, Risk, the Anti-Fraud sales team, Brand, and Comms. Described as a "player-coach role."

Responsibilities:
- Own positioning, messaging, narrative for Protect, Signal, identity verification across new-logo and installed-base motions.
- Lead GTM plan to move Protect from beta to scaled adoption (ICP, launch strategy, target-account plays, proof points, pricing/packaging inputs, success metrics).
- Build and maintain fraud sales enablement (pitch, discovery, objection handling, competitive battlecards, customer evidence) for the Anti-Fraud overlay team and segment AEs; measure adoption.
- Be the voice of the fraud buyer inside Plaid (win/loss, customer/call research, analyst and market intelligence feeding product roadmap and GTM decisions).
- Partner with Brand Marketing and Comms on the fraud narrative and category-level campaigns.
- Represent Fraud in company-level narrative work (platform positioning, product architecture, investor/analyst/major event moments).
- Manage, coach, and grow the Fraud PMM team.

Qualifications:
- "10+ years of B2B product marketing, with 3+ years leading PMM teams, in fintech, infrastructure, or a technical/regulated industry."
- Several years marketing fraud, risk, identity, or trust & safety products.
- Proven positioning rigor (rebuilt positioning for a product that wasn't landing, with results).
- Experience taking a product from early adoption to scale.
- Sales enablement that sellers actually use, with adoption evidence.
- Evidence-driven (win/loss, call analysis, customer research, market data).
- Strategic thinker who is also a doer.
- Strong cross-functional operator across Product, Sales, Brand, Comms.
- Practiced at using AI as a research/drafting/enablement multiplier.

Nice to have: experience marketing a data-network or consortium-based product; experience selling a second/third product into an installed base; identity verification/KYC/AML/account takeover/payments fraud/ACH return risk familiarity; analyst relations experience in fraud/identity.
