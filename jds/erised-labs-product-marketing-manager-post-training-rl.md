About Erised Labs

Erised Labs focuses on LLM post-training, with an emphasis on reinforcement learning environments and post-training services. We help AI teams turn real-world tasks into environments where models can learn, be evaluated, and improve.

We're looking for a technically fluent Product Marketing Manager to bring our work to researchers, developers, and AI teams. You'll work closely with research, engineering, and product to shape our positioning, create compelling technical content, and drive product adoption.

What you'll do

- Own product positioning and messaging. Define the target audiences, use cases, and differentiated value of our RL environments and post-training services.
- Create technical content. Work with researchers and engineers to produce technical blogs, tutorials, demos, launch materials, and case studies covering environment design, rewards, evaluation, rollouts, and training workflows.
- Lead product launches. Coordinate launches across our website, technical content, social channels, and developer communities, helping users move from discovery to hands-on experimentation.
- Turn results into credible stories. Translate experiments and customer outcomes into benchmarks and case studies, clearly explaining methodology, limitations, and improvements in model capability, training efficiency, or cost.
- Build relationships with technical audiences. Engage ML researchers, AI engineers, and open-source communities through technical discussions, workshops, events, and ecosystem partnerships.
- Support customer conversations. Develop product presentations, use-case materials, and competitive insights that help customers understand our services and evaluate their fit.
- Bring market feedback into the product. Identify adoption barriers, emerging use cases, and customer needs, and work with the team to address them.

What we're looking for

- Experience in technical product marketing, developer relations, technical content, or a related role for AI or developer products.
- A working understanding of LLM post-training, including SFT, reinforcement learning, reward design, and evaluation.
- Excellent English writing and communication skills, with examples of content created for technical audiences.
- The ability to read technical documentation, research papers, and basic code; try APIs; run demos; and explain what you learn accurately.
- Strong judgment in connecting technical capabilities to concrete user problems and product value.
- The ability to take projects from initial research and interviews through writing, launch, distribution, and measurement.
- Familiarity with the open-source AI ecosystem, including projects or platforms such as SGLang, vLLM, verl, slime, and Fireworks.

Nice to have

- Hands-on experience with RL environments, agent evaluation, coding agents, or post-training infrastructure.
- Experience marketing to ML researchers or AI infrastructure teams.
- Python proficiency and practical experience running model training or evaluation workflows.
- A track record of technical talks, open-source community contributions, benchmark publications, or building a technical content program.
- Experience turning customer deployments or training results into public case studies.

What success looks like

You'll help establish a clear, credible voice for Erised Labs in post-training. Your work will build technical trust, reach the right audiences, and generate qualified product trials, useful developer feedback, and customer opportunities.

How to apply

Please include 2-3 examples of your work, such as technical articles, product launches, tutorials, demos, talks, or customer case studies. Briefly describe your contribution to each.
