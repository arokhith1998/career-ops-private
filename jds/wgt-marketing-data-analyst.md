## Job Description
Analytical engine behind user acquisition and marketing efforts. Performance measurement, channel analysis, and audience insights informing marketing spend and scaling decisions. Bridges paid media, organic growth, and product analytics.

## Responsibilities
- Manage reporting and analysis across paid UA channels (Meta, Google, Apple Search Ads)
- Build and maintain performance dashboards tracking campaigns and audience trends
- Analyze relationships between paid and organic growth channels
- Support audience segmentation and targeting strategies
- Partner with UA and creative teams on creative performance evaluation
- Conduct incrementality, attribution, and funnel analyses
- Execute ad hoc analyses including channel evaluation and ROI assessments
- Translate findings into recommendations for practitioners and leadership

## Requirements
- Bachelor's in Analytics, Statistics, Economics, Mathematics, or quantitative field
- 2-4 years in data or marketing analytics, preferably mobile gaming or app-based UA
- Strong SQL proficiency
- Mobile Measurement Partners experience (Appsflyer, Adjust)
- Dashboard building (Amplitude, Power BI, Tableau)
- UA metrics: ROAS, CPI, CPM, CTR, LTV, D1/D7/D30 retention
- Player LTV modeling and bidding targets
- Retargeting campaign experience and incrementality measurement

## Preferred
- Mobile gaming UA with F2P monetization models
- Media mix modeling or incrementality testing
- Creator/influencer performance measurement
- Data warehouse experience (Snowflake, BigQuery)
- SKAdNetwork and ATT framework understanding
