## Full description (verbatim via headless browser render of the LinkedIn guest posting page)
About Us
Fireworks is the platform for specialized intelligence, enabling companies to build, train, and serve AI models tailored to their own data, workflows, and products. Founded by the team behind PyTorch and backed by AMD, Atreides, Benchmark Capital, Index Ventures, Lightspeed, NVIDIA, Sequoia Capital, and TCV. Fireworks is a Series D company valued at $17.5 billion.

The Role
Reporting to the GTM Planning Lead, the Central Planning Manager is responsible for translating GTM strategy into headcount capacity plans, quotas, and pipeline-generation targets that drive the right outcomes across direct and partner motions. You will own the planning models and be a trusted partner to GTM and Finance leadership. Consumption and hybrid commit + usage economics are core to how Fireworks sells.

Responsibilities
- GTM Planning, Capacity & Quotas: drive the annual GTM planning process for the COO organization; own capacity planning and target-setting across Sales, Field Engineering, CS; build scenario models on headcount/pipeline/compute supply/productivity/revenue tradeoffs; own pipeline-generation (pipegen) targets; partner with GPU management team on demand forecast.
- Systems, Forecasting & Automation: build and own native end-to-end business plan modeling automation; partner with Data Engineering and GTM Engineering on source-of-truth data (CRM, billing/usage, roster/quotas).

Qualifications
- 6+ years in GTM planning, quota setting, and/or productivity-related revenue operations workstreams.
- Strong understanding of go-to-market design and sales planning.
- Significant experience presenting analysis to senior leaders (Director/VP level).
- Advanced analytical and financial modeling skills.
- Hands-on experience building headcount/ramp/quota capacity models for a consumption-based or hybrid commit-plus-usage business.
- SQL and BI fluency; experience with a planning/forecasting platform (Anaplan, Clari or Xactly).
- BA/BS degree in engineering, accounting, finance, economics or other quantitative fields preferred.

Preferred: AI/ML infrastructure or developer-tools background; familiarity with overlay roles, marketplace/channel nuances; hypergrowth planning experience; investment banking/finance/consulting background.

Fireworks AI is an equal-opportunity employer.
