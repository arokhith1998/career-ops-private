At Meta Reality Labs, we build tools that help people feel connected anytime, anywhere. **Monetization & Business Economics Manager Responsibilities:**
- Own financial and unit economics analysis across products, channels, and markets to inform commercial strategies that lead to sustainable growth

- Define commercial KPIs that enable data-driven commercial decisions
- Partner with Finance, Sales, and Operations to shape commercial planning
- Synthesize insights into clear strategic recommendations for leadership

**Minimum Qualifications:**
- 7+ years of experience in product management, go-to-market, or management consulting
- Experience building financial models, including P&L analysis and unit economics, to inform business decisions
- Experience in analyzing complex, long-term, critical problems and driving solutions for them
- Track record of building cross-functional partnerships to shape go-to-market strategies
- High tolerance for ambiguity and the ability to create execution plans where none existed
- Bachelor's degree

**Preferred Qualifications:**
- Demonstrated ability to integrate AI tools to optimize/redesign workflows and drive measurable impact (e.g., efficiency gains, quality improvements)
- Experience adhering to and implementing responsible, ethical AI practices (e.g., risk assessment, bias mitigation, quality and accuracy reviews)
- Demonstrated ongoing AI skill development (e.g., prompt/context engineering, agent orchestration) and staying current with emerging AI technologies
- Experiences owning and overseeing multiple portfolio areas and marketing strategies
- M.S./M.B.A. degree in a relevant field
- Experience bringing new consumer technology products to market
- Experience and/or domain knowledge in AI, augmented reality, virtual reality, mixed reality, wearable devices, mobile devices, and/or hardware/software platforms
