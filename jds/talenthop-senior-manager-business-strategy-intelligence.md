About Our Client:

The organization operates in the consumer intelligence and real-time activation industry, providing data-driven solutions for brands to build audiences, activate media, and measure performance. It addresses challenges related to understanding consumer behavior and marketing effectiveness through proprietary analytics platforms, supporting thousands of brands.

About the Opportunity:

The SENIOR MANAGER, BUSINESS STRATEGY & INTELLIGENCE role focuses on delivering insights that help leadership understand business performance and make strategic decisions. This position analyzes complex data across products, customers, and markets to identify growth drivers, risks, and opportunities, directly influencing decisions in product, pricing, go-to-market strategies, and investments.

Responsibilities:

- Develop a deep understanding of products, customers, revenue models, and market dynamics.
- Analyze performance metrics such as product adoption, revenue, customer behavior, pricing, retention, and expansion.
- Address ambiguous business problems by structuring questions, investigating data, and generating actionable recommendations.
- Inform strategic decisions on product, pricing, packaging, customer engagement, go-to-market, and investments.
- Monitor customer behavior shifts, competitive positioning, and market trends to assess business implications.
- Create and prioritize test agendas with defined success measures.
- Automate recurring analyses and workflows for organizational efficiency.
- Collaborate with data teams to identify and close data gaps for better decision-making.

Requirements:

- 7–10 years of experience in strategy, business operations, management consulting, product strategy, or strategic analytics.
- Strong grasp of the connections between products, customers, revenue, and go-to-market processes, preferably in data-driven B2B environments.
- Proven ability to structure and solve ambiguous business problems using data analysis.
- Proficiency in SQL and analytical tools; experience with BI tools, BigQuery, and Salesforce is advantageous.
- Strong product curiosity and sound commercial judgment.
- Excellent communication skills for presenting analysis and recommendations to senior leadership.
- Ability to work independently in a lean environment and lead from question to recommendation.
- Experience integrating AI into analytical workflows.
- Experience in adtech, martech, measurement, SaaS, or related data-driven B2B sectors is a plus.
