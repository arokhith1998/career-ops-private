## Full Job Description

### About the Role
The American Med Spa Association (AmSpa) is seeking a Brand & Growth Marketing Manager to shape how AmSpa shows up in the market and turn audience engagement into measurable business growth.

This role sits at the intersection of brand, digital marketing and performance. You will own AmSpa's brand presence across key channels while developing integrated marketing strategies that grow audience, strengthen engagement, drive membership and support AmSpa's broader portfolio of programs and events.

### What You'll Own

**Brand Strategy & Management**
- Own and evolve AmSpa's overarching brand strategy, positioning, voice and visual consistency.
- Protect the AmSpa brand across channels, programs, campaigns and audience touchpoints.
- Ensure marketing communications reinforce AmSpa's position as a trusted authority in medical aesthetics.

**Social Media & Audience Engagement**
- Own organic social strategy: content planning, creation, publishing, optimization.
- Grow and engage AmSpa's social communities.
- Partner with Content team on KOL/ambassador activation.

**Growth & Digital Marketing**
- Develop and execute integrated digital acquisition and demand-generation campaigns.
- Own email marketing strategy (segmentation, lifecycle journeys, testing); manage external email agency.
- Own paid media strategy and execution.
- Own search and SEO strategy and execution.
- Monitor performance and continually test/optimize.

**Website & Conversion**
- Own marketing strategy for AmSpa's website and digital user journeys.
- Develop and optimize website messaging, landing pages, conversion paths.

**Integrated Campaign Strategy**
- Connect content, social, email, paid media, search and web into cohesive campaigns.
- Partner with Content, Membership, Events and Sales.

**Analytics & Performance**
- Own marketing performance measurement across channels.
- Develop and maintain dashboards, reports, KPI tracking.
- Partner with internal stakeholders on campaigns, sponsorships, partnerships, events.

### What We're Looking For
- 5-7+ years of experience in digital, growth, brand or integrated marketing.
- Demonstrated experience developing and executing multi-channel marketing campaigns end to end.
- Strong working knowledge of paid media, organic social, SEO, web marketing, email/CRM marketing, conversion optimization.
- Experience managing external agencies/partners.
- HubSpot or similar CRM/marketing automation experience strongly preferred.

### The Person Who Will Thrive Here
Sees marketing as an interconnected system, curious, resourceful, comfortable taking ownership without a large team or perfect playbook.

**Salary:** $65,000-$70,000 commensurate with experience. **Fully Remote.**
