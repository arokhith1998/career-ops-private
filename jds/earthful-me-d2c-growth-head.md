## About

Earthful is a plant-based nutrition brand for women's health, backed by Fireside Ventures and V3 Ventures. This is a leadership role: own the D2C (earthful.me) growth number end to end, and build the team behind it as the channel scales.

## Role Overview

Leads Earthful's D2C growth engine: full ownership of the website's revenue number, driven through performance marketing, conversion optimization, and a high-velocity creative pipeline. Directly accountable for hitting D2C growth targets set in the Annual Operating Plan (AOP), decides how budget is allocated across products, and builds the team needed to scale that ownership over time. Starts as a hands-on individual role, expected to grow into leading a small team.

## What You'll Own

- D2C Revenue Growth & Ownership: own the D2C (earthful.me) revenue number end-to-end, driven primarily through performance marketing across Meta, Google, and other paid channels; directly accountable for hitting D2C growth targets set in the AOP; coordinate with the organic/content team.
- Creative Pipeline for Performance: direct the creative team's output, run structured creative testing, brief the next round of creative based on data.
- Budget, ROAS & Site Conversion: decide budget allocation across products and ROAS targets; own website conversion end-to-end (landing page CVR, checkout drop-off, AOV); own the full D2C P&L view.
- Team Building & Leadership: build and lead a small performance marketing/growth team as the channel scales; set the growth roadmap and testing cadence.
- Cross-functional: track CAC/LTV alongside channel ROAS; coordinate with Supply Chain on demand forecasting; maintain a structured testing roadmap.
- Optional scope: retention/CRM levers, on-site pricing and promotions strategy.

## What We're Looking For

- Ideally has been part of a D2C/e-commerce brand's growth or marketing team during a scale-up phase, joining when revenue was roughly INR 0-30 Cr and helping scale it to INR 300 Cr or more.
- MBA from a top-tier B-school strongly preferred, with 4-6+ years of post-MBA experience in performance marketing or growth.
- At least 2+ years of experience owning a full P&L or AOP-style revenue number.
- Direct, hands-on experience running and optimizing paid campaigns on Meta and Google Ads.
- Prior experience building or leading a team, or clear readiness to do so.
- Comfortable making budget allocation calls across multiple products.
- Understanding of website conversion levers and experience working with a website/CRO team.
- Strong analytical and Excel skills.
