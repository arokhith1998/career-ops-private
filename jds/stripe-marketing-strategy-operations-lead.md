Who we are

About Stripe

Stripe is a financial infrastructure platform for businesses. Millions of companies from the world’s largest enterprises to the most ambitious startups use Stripe to accept payments, grow their revenue, and accelerate new business opportunities. Our mission is to increase the GDP of the internet, and we have a staggering amount of work ahead. That means you have an unprecedented opportunity to put the global economy within everyone’s reach while doing the most important work of your career.

About The Team

The Marketing Strategy & Operations team is responsible for driving organizational effectiveness and leading transformational programs across Marketing, Self-serve, Startup and Sales Development. We create the processes and conditions that enable high performance across teams and help Stripe scale with focus and discipline.

We partner closely with the CMO, Marketing and Sales Development leadership team to define strategy, lead operational planning, and steward execution across global programs. This role partners directly with the head of Global Demand, Campaigns, and Field Marketing.

Our work spans cross-functional efforts that are critical to Stripe’s growth. From delivering strategic insights and frameworks to leading complex initiatives, we focus on what moves the needle most for the business. We care about results, not activity, and we have fun doing it. If you like hard problems, are analytical, and are an amazing teammate, we want to hear from you.

What you’ll do

Responsibilities

- Drive our annual strategic plan and operating plan, working in partnership with the Global Demand, Campaigns, and Field Marketing leadership team to define our must-wins, build plans to win, and allocate our resources to deliver on the plan.
- Define and manage our most critical OKRs that instill transparent, data-driven accountability for delivering our strategy to Stripe leadership.
- Run the core rhythms of the business and reporting to focus attention, inspection, and strategic steers in the right places at the right time.
- Drive critical leadership and team-wide communication forums (e.g. leadership onsites, all hands) to effectively cascade strategy and comms
- Leverage an AI-first approach to aggressively automate marketing reporting.
- Bring clarity to ambiguity; navigate complexity, distill insight from noise, resolve issues quickly, and empower teams to make smart, confident decisions.
- Work directly with senior leaders across the company and with cross-functional partners to accelerate progress, solve hard scaling initiatives, and incubate new programs.
- Own and improve the campaign operations process end-to-end across digital campaigns and events - from planning and briefing through asset production, regional activation, and performance measurement - with a focus on efficiency and collaboration.
- Build tooling and automation to integrate marketing workflows and automate global calendars of marketing activity and impact
- Define how we should effectively measure the success of our marketing efforts and align with Data, MarTech, and Eng teams to build the infrastructure to support our reporting goals
- Use data to tell a compelling story and drive alignment on where we’re winning and where we need to focus.

Who you are

We’re looking for someone who meets the minimum requirements to be considered for the role. If you meet these requirements, you are encouraged to apply. The preferred qualifications are a bonus, not a requirement.

Minimum Requirements

- 10 years of experience in consulting, strategy and operations, business operations, product strategy, product management, or similar roles with exposure to both business and technical skills
- Proven people management experience leading a team in strategy and operations, marketing operations, or a similar function, with a track record of hiring, developing, and retaining high-performing talent
- Background in B2B marketing, and core capabilities of Campaign and Field Marketing (e.g. digital campaigns, events)
- Experience working in highly cross-functional settings, and in a role that spans strategy, analysis, and operations in a fast-paced environment
- Critical and first-principles thinking, with the ability to zoom out to the most important questions
- Passion for digging deep into data to diagnose business issues and solve problems
- Excellent written and verbal communication, with the ability to speak to leaders across teams including product, engineering, analytics, sales, and partnerships
- An ability to execute with minimal guidance and with a high degree of ambiguity

Preferred Qualifications

- Prior experience in payments, finance, fintech, or high-growth technology companies
- Prior experience working directly with C-level executives and their direct reports
- Experience navigating large data sets and/or SQL

Hybrid work at Stripe

This role is available either in an office or a remote location (35+ miles or 56+ km from a Stripe office).

In-office expectations

Office-assigned Stripes spend at least 50% of the time in a given month in their local office or with users. This hits a balance between bringing people together for in-person collaboration and learning from each other, while supporting flexibility about how to do this in a way that makes sense for individuals and their teams.

Working remotely at Stripe

A remote location is defined as being 35 miles (56 kilometers) or more from one of our offices. While you would be welcome to come into the office for team/business meetings, on-sites, meet-ups, and events, our expectation is you would regularly work from home rather than a Stripe office. Stripe does not cover the cost of relocating to a remote location. We encourage you to apply for roles that match the location where you currently live or plan to live.

Pay and benefits

The annual US base salary range for this role is $171,400 - $257,000. For sales roles, the range provided is the role’s On Target Earnings ("OTE") range, meaning that the range includes both the sales commissions/sales bonuses target and annual base salary for the role. This salary range may be inclusive of several career levels at Stripe and will be narrowed during the interview process based on a number of factors, including the candidate’s experience, qualifications, and location. Applicants interested in this role and who are not located in the US may request the annual salary range for their location during the interview process.

Additional benefits for this role may include: equity, company bonus or sales commissions/bonuses; 401(k) plan; medical, dental, and vision benefits; and wellness stipends.
