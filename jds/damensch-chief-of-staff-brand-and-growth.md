Chief of Staff — Brand and Growth

On-site · Koramangala, Bengaluru | Full-time | Reports to the Founder

Think of a mountaineer’s lead climber — the one who reads the whole face, sets the route, and fixes the ropes so the rest of the team can move fast and safe. That’s this role. You’ll start at the Founder’s side, learn Damensch brand-first, and quickly take the rope on all things brand marketing — creative, influencer, and the operations that hold it together. Then you’ll climb into product: because how we build a product and how we tell its story are the same pitch, you’ll grow into leading product development alongside the Founder.

What you’ll own

-Lead the brand teams — after a ramp. Over your first 3–6 months, training and working shoulder-to-shoulder with the Founder, you’ll step into leading all the brand teams — creative, influencer marketing, and the other teams that sit under brand.

-Brand marketing, end to end. Own the strategy and the day-to-day — campaigns, brand voice, and how Damensch shows up everywhere a customer meets us.

-The creative engine. Get the best out of the creative team even though you’re not the one holding the pen — direction, taste,and ruthless prioritisation.

-Influencer & partnerships. Drive the influencer marketing team and the operations behind every collaboration, from brief to go-live.

-Brand operations. Build the rhythm, the trackers, and the process that keep a fast brand org shipping on time.

-Product, step by step. Learn the product cycle hands-on, then grow into leading product development — building new products with the Founder, story and substance together.

What we’re looking for

-A right-and-left-brain operator — sharply analytical, with real

appreciation for craft and creative.

-A strong project manager who turns ambiguity into plans, owners, and dates.

-The instinct to lead creative and brand people — earning their

trust without needing to out-create them.

-Hungry to go wide: comfortable owning brand today and

learning product tomorrow.

Background

-3–5 years of experience — sharp, fast, and already proven.

-A degree in engineering or an analytical discipline (e.g. commerce) from a Tier-1 institution.

- Exposure to the consumer space is ideal.

- An MBA is a plus, not a must.
