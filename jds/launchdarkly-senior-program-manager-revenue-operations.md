## Description
LaunchDarkly is looking for a Senior Program Manager to help the Revenue Operations team scale efficiently: driving cross-functional programs, streamlining processes, delivering data-driven solutions that accelerate predictable SaaS revenue growth. Translates strategic priorities into structured plans, keeps cross-functional teams aligned, surfaces/resolves risks and blockers.

## Responsibilities
- Drive Strategic RevOps Initiatives: translate priorities into program plans, define milestones/owners/deliverables, track progress, manage dependencies, escalate blockers.
- Establish Program Governance & Cadences: operating rhythms, status/steering meetings, decision and action logs, standardize reporting.
- Manage Cross-Functional Alignment: coordinate RevOps and Field (Sales, CS, Marketing), clarify roles, align priorities/timelines, facilitate decisions.
- Manage Risks, Issues & Dependencies: identify risks early, maintain risk logs, develop mitigation plans, resolve dependencies, escalate decisions.

## Qualifications
- 10+ years of experience in program management, project management, or operations, ideally supporting Revenue Operations, Sales Operations, or GTM.
- Strong track record running cross-functional programs with multiple stakeholders.
- Experience establishing program governance from scratch.
- Experience with Project Management tools (Jira, Asana, Monday).
- PMP, Agile/Scrum certification, or equivalent experience a plus.
- Experience in B2B SaaS preferred.
