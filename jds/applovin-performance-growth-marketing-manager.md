About Gist Gist is a social platform for people who want substance: practical, thoughtful content spanning philosophy debates, local recommendations, seasonal gift guides, wellness tips, and more. Across categories like travel, food, career, and family, we think of Gist as “a handbook for the curious.” Gist is built by AppLovin Corporation, which generated $5.3 billion in revenue last year and is one of the world’s leading ad tech companies, alongside Google and Meta. Fortune has recognized AppLovin as one of the Best Workplaces in the Bay Area, and the company was certified as a Great Place to Work for four consecutive years, from 2021 to 2024. What You’ll Do Run paid acquisition. Manage campaigns across Meta, TikTok, Google, and other ad networks. Launch, optimize, and scale campaigns, and recommend how to allocate budget. Own retention marketing. Build and run lifecycle programs across push notifications, in-app messaging, email, and re-engagement. Study what makes consumer products engaging and apply those insights to bring Gist users back. Explore new growth channels. Test ways to acquire users beyond paid social, including Reddit, Threads, X, Pinterest, Snap, app store optimization, creator seeding, community, and referral programs. Run focused, low-cost experiments and decide quickly what to scale. Lead creative testing. Test hooks, formats, creator and UGC content, static and video ads, and messaging angles. Work with designers and creators, and use AI tools to develop and ship new concepts each week. Build repeatable growth systems. Turn successful manual tactics into scalable processes using AI tools or simple automation. Work across the full funnel. Partner with product and operations to understand what users do after installing the app. Use those insights to improve targeting and creative, and optimize for activation and retention—not just installs. Manage budget and efficiency. Track CAC, CPI, LTV, ROAS, and Day 7 and Day 30 retention. Communicate spend and results clearly, scale what works, and cut what doesn’t. Measure and diagnose performance. Use attribution and analytics tools to spot changes early and turn data into clear next steps. Work closely with product and engineering. Test, learn, and document. Run structured experiments across audiences, creative, bidding, messaging, and lifecycle programs. Treat campaigns as hypotheses and build a playbook from what you learn.
