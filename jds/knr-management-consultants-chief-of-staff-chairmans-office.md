## Job Description (full, verbatim from Weekday posting)
Role: Chief of Staff - Chairman's Office

Location: Delhi HQ, India
Reporting To: Chairman

The Chief of Staff will work closely with the Chairman as a high-trust strategic and financial partner, with primary responsibility for financial reporting, performance review, governance, and execution of key enterprise priorities.

The role requires a qualified CA with strong exposure to financial statements, MIS, board reporting, business performance analysis, and cross-functional coordination across leadership teams.

Key Responsibilities
- Support the Chairman on financial reporting, performance review, and strategic decision-making.
- Review financial statements, management reports, dashboards, and board packs.
- Track revenue, profitability, cash flow, and key business KPIs across business units.
- Drive governance cadence, leadership reviews, and follow-through on action points.
- Coordinate with finance, operations, strategy, and business leaders to ensure execution of Chairman-led priorities.
- Support strategic initiatives, including transformation, cost optimisation, M&A, and growth programs.
- Prepare concise, high-quality presentations and decision materials for the Chairman.

Ideal Profile
- 12-18 years of experience in finance, financial reporting, corporate strategy, or Chairman's/CEO's office roles.
- Qualified Chartered Accountant (CA) mandatory/preferred, with a strong grounding in financial statements, reporting, analysis, and governance.
- Strong business acumen, structured thinking, and execution discipline.
- Proven ability to work closely with CXOs and operate in high-accountability environments.
- Excellent stakeholder management, confidentiality, and executive presence.
