## Role summary
A senior individual-contributor role leading how complex SaaS deals are priced, structured, approved, and contracted across international markets. Builds scalable deal-desk processes, uses AI/automation to shorten deal cycles, and works with Sales, Finance, Legal, and Revenue Operations.

## Responsibilities
- Structure complex new, expansion, and renewal deals while protecting margins and policy compliance.
- Set up pricing governance: approval matrices, discount thresholds, non-standard terms.
- Manage global deal execution across currencies, contracting entities, regional legal/tax requirements, and time zones.
- Standardize contracts with Legal and Finance; optimize order-to-cash.
- Design AI-powered deal-desk workflows (automated screening, AI-assisted contract review) with governance.
- Own the CPQ, CLM, DocuSign, and Salesforce ecosystem.
- Build dashboards tracking discounts, compliance, approval times, and automation performance.
- Present insights in executive pricing and forecasting reviews.

## Qualifications
- 8+ years in Deal Desk, Revenue Operations, or Sales Operations in B2B SaaS, including 3+ years running Deal Desk end to end.
- Experience building Deal Desk processes from the ground up at early- or growth-stage companies.
- Hands-on use of AI tools (Claude, ChatGPT, Copilot, Agentforce) for Deal Desk/RevOps automation.
- Experience structuring international SaaS deals across regions, currencies, and legal entities.
- Knowledge of subscription, usage-based, consumption-based, and hybrid pricing models.
- Experience with CPQ/CLM platforms, plus advanced Excel/Sheets modeling. Strong Salesforce proficiency.
