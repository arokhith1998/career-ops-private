## Company / context

Modern Life is an AI-driven life insurance brokerage for financial advisors. Headquartered in NYC. Founded by Michael Konialian (ex-CoverWallet, now Aon). Backed by Thrive Capital plus 12 unicorn founders (Hippo, Plaid, Reddit, Flatiron Health, Newfront, At Bay, Vouch, Cedar, Lattice). $35M total raised ($15M seed 2023, $20M Series A Nov 2025). Licensed in all 50 states. ~50-100 employees.

Pitch: "AI-driven underwriting to advance the $175B life insurance market." Product sells to life insurance advisors, not end consumers.

## Role framing

Marketing Manager reporting into Marketing leadership (Head of Marketing or CEO-direct at this stage). Broad generalist role owning campaigns, events, sales enablement, and MarTech ops for a Series A AI platform. AI fluency called out as required.

## Responsibilities (from posting)

- Execute marketing campaigns across email, LinkedIn, and paid ads
- Manage conferences and webinars
- Create sales enablement materials
- Optimize the marketing technology stack including HubSpot and Google Analytics
- "Strong AI fluency to scale content production, automate workflows"

## Required qualifications

- 3-5 years in marketing generalist, growth marketing, or demand generation roles (B2B/fintech preferred)
- Proficiency with HubSpot, Webflow, and Google Analytics
- Experience managing paid media campaigns on LinkedIn, Google, or Meta
- Strong content writing and copywriting abilities
- Active use of AI tools in professional workflows
- Data-driven analytical approach
