## Full JD text

This position is listed on behalf of a partner company, who manages all applications and next steps. Our partner is looking for a Revenue Operations Manager based in San Francisco Bay Area.

We are seeking an experienced Revenue Operations Manager to lead and optimize global revenue operations within a fast-growing, internationally distributed technology organization. In this strategic leadership role, you will oversee revenue processes across direct sales, channel partnerships, alliances, and online commercial activities. You will work closely with executive leadership and cross-functional teams to improve sales effectiveness, maximize customer retention, and drive sustainable revenue growth. You will lead a team of revenue operations specialists while developing scalable systems, processes, and performance frameworks. Through advanced analytics, automation, and data-driven decision-making, you will identify opportunities to improve productivity and commercial performance. This is an exciting opportunity for a commercially minded leader who thrives in a dynamic, multicultural environment and enjoys building high-performing teams and scalable operational structures.

Accountabilities

Lead and optimize global revenue operations across direct sales, indirect channels, strategic alliances, partnerships, and online sales activities.

Build, develop, and manage a high-performing team of Revenue Operations Analysts, ensuring effective operational support, process execution, and sales enablement.

Oversee key revenue operations functions, including sales engagement, training and enablement, data enrichment, customer relationship management (CRM), configure-price-quote (CPQ) processes, analytics, and commission management.

Design, implement, and continuously improve scalable processes across the entire revenue operations lifecycle to enhance efficiency, consistency, and commercial performance.

Establish performance metrics and reporting frameworks to monitor lead generation, pipeline development, sales velocity, productivity, customer renewals, and overall revenue effectiveness.

Translate business intelligence and performance data into actionable insights, supporting territory and channel management, resource allocation, and strategic decision-making.

Partner with executive leadership through regular sales and business reviews, identifying performance gaps and implementing initiatives to improve revenue outcomes.

Oversee sales technology platforms and commercial processes, collaborating with systems engineering teams to improve automation, integrations, and operational efficiency.

Lead the implementation of new products, pricing structures, packaging, and bundled offerings, ensuring that commercial systems are intuitive and effective for sales teams and partners.

Maintain high standards of data accuracy and integrity through order processing, data cleansing, process documentation, reporting, and employee onboarding and training.

Lead and enhance commercial review processes for assessing, prioritizing, and managing sales and strategic alliance opportunities.

Take ownership of sales compensation and commission plans, including target setting, performance evaluation, and commission payment processes.

Drive continuous improvement across revenue operations while ensuring that processes remain scalable and aligned with the organization's global growth objectives.

Requirements

Exceptional academic track record, demonstrating strong performance throughout high school and university education.

Undergraduate degree in a relevant discipline, or an alternative professional background supported by a compelling track record of achievement.

Extensive experience in a senior revenue operations leadership position, ideally within a technology-driven, subscription-based business.

Proven experience managing and optimizing direct sales operations, channel partnerships, and strategic alliances within an international commercial environment.

Strong track record of leveraging technology platforms, business intelligence, analytics, compensation systems, and operational processes to deliver measurable improvements in revenue performance.

Deep understanding of revenue operations frameworks, sales processes, CRM systems, pipeline management, customer retention, and commercial performance metrics.

Demonstrated experience leading, developing, and motivating remote teams across multiple geographical regions and time zones.

Strong analytical and problem-solving abilities, with a systematic approach to identifying inefficiencies and implementing repeatable, scalable solutions.

Ability to operate effectively under commercial pressure, establish clear performance expectations, and drive accountability across revenue teams.

Excellent organizational and project management skills, with the ability to manage multiple priorities and coordinate stakeholders at all organizational levels.

Outstanding communication, influencing, and negotiation skills, with the ability to secure stakeholder alignment and drive adoption of new processes and systems.

Fluent business English, both written and spoken.

Willingness and ability to travel internationally up to four times per year for company meetings and team events.

A proactive, results-oriented mindset, combined with strong leadership capabilities, attention to detail, and a commitment to operational excellence.

Benefits

Competitive global compensation: Salary packages tailored to geographical location, professional experience, and individual performance.

Performance-based rewards: Annual bonus or commission opportunities in addition to base compensation.

Regular compensation reviews: Formal compensation assessments twice a year to recognize performance and professional contributions.

Fully distributed working environment: Work remotely from anywhere in the world, with flexibility across different time zones.

International team gatherings: Twice-yearly in-person team sprints, providing opportunities for collaboration and relationship-building.

Annual learning and development budget: USD 2,000 per year to support professional growth, training, and skill development.

Generous annual leave: 40 days of annual leave per year, including public holidays and designated company-wide holiday periods.

Family support: Maternity and paternity leave provisions.

Health and wellbeing: Access to a Team Member Assistance Program and dedicated wellness platform.

Recognition programs: Company rewards and recognition initiatives celebrating outstanding contributions.

International travel opportunities: Opportunities to visit new destinations and meet colleagues, with Priority Pass airport lounge access.

Global career exposure: Opportunity to collaborate with talented professionals across international markets and contribute to the growth of a leading open-source technology ecosystem.
