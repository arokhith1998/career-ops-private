About The Team

Grindr is an AI-native platform powering how millions of gay people connect globally. With 15M+ monthly users, 130B+ annual messages, and a team of fewer than 200, we move fast, stay lean, and tackle technical problems at a scale few companies ever see.

Joining Grindr as a Staff Product Manager for Monetization presents a pivotal opportunity to shape and elevate our leading GBTQ+ social networking platform. Your role will be central to driving revenue growth by crafting insightful product strategies, orchestrating cross-functional collaboration, and enriching user experiences through innovative monetization features and optimizations. With millions of users relying on our platform, your contributions will directly impact and empower our community. This role offers an exhilarating opportunity to drive innovation, while making a positive impact within both the LGBTQ+ community and our business endeavors.

At Grindr, we operate in Grindr Mode. Moderately hardcore day to day, truly hardcore when it counts. It’s about doing great work without burning out. Outcomes over outputs. Smart, driven people who raise your bar, with room to live full lives.

This is a hybrid role based in our LA, SF or New York office and will require you to be in person Tuesdays and Thursdays.

Role Requirements

Define and execute the product roadmap to drive monetization strategies that align with the company's strategic goals and user needs.
Collaborate and lead cross-functional teams to prioritize features, establish project timelines, and ensure successful product delivery.
Design, implement and measure multivariate A/B tests to show incremental impact across new features and optimize current features.
Monitor key product metrics and iterate on features to optimize user experiences, specifically focusing on enhancing the purchase funnel for improved conversion rates and revenue generation.
Communicate and present to effectively collaborate and align with cross-functional teams and stakeholders towards monetization goals.

You May Thrive In This Role If You

7+ years’ experience as a product manager, preferably in a consumer-facing subscription based business.
Solid understanding of the dynamics of subscription revenue generation, acquisition, conversion and retention models.
Proven track record for leading monetization initiatives from ideation to launch.
Ability to identify challenges and creatively find solutions that align with business objectives and user needs.
Experience with A/B testing, user research, and other methodologies to optimize monetization strategies.
Analytical mindset with the ability to gather and interpret data to inform decisions, understand the user behavior and measure revenue opportunities/impact.
Excellent leadership and communication skills to effectively collaborate with cross-functional teams and stakeholders.
Strong understanding of product management methodologies, including market research, ideation, user testing, and agile development.
