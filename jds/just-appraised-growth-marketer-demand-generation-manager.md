About The Role

Just Appraised is looking for a Demand Generation Marketing Manager to build and run the programs that turn our existing customer base and top-of-funnel activity into pipeline: proactive email lead gen, webinars, and case studies, with room to pick up light product marketing work along the way.

This is a full-stack, hands-on role. The right person leans demand gen over pure-play product marketing, is comfortable owning a program end to end rather than a single piece of it, and doesn't need a big team to get things shipped.

What You'll Do

Email Marketing

- Stand up proactive, always-on lead gen email programs. This channel doesn't exist today, so the first job is building it, not just optimizing something already running.
- Own nurture and reactivation sequences, segmentation, cadence, and reporting (opens, clicks, MQLs generated).

Webinars, Full Stack

- Own the entire webinar lifecycle: topic selection, slide creation, promotion, hosting, and post-event follow-up cadences.
- Partner with Sales, CS, and internal subject matter experts to source content and speakers.
- Report on registration, attendance, and pipeline contribution.

Case Studies

- Source, write, and publish customer case studies that give Sales credible proof points to use in active deals.
- Coordinate with Customer Success to identify strong stories and get them through approval.

Light Product Marketing

- Support product marketing on an as-needed basis: sales-facing roadmap slides, launch collateral, competitive input.

Key KPIs For The Role

- Expansion pipeline / ARR → Pipeline and revenue sourced from customer marketing programs, in partnership with CS
- Email-sourced MQLs/SQLs → Since this is a new channel, early KPIs should track whether it's built and producing before shifting to pure optimization
- Webinar performance → Registration, attendance, and pipeline contribution
- Case studies in active use → Case studies published and used by Sales in live deals
- Product marketing turnaround → Sales enablement and roadmap collateral delivered on time for key moments

Qualifications

- B2B marketing experience, demand-gen leaning, ideally in B2B SaaS
- GovTech marketing experience preferred but not required
- Hands-on experience running email marketing programs end to end, not just briefing someone else
- Experience owning webinars start to finish, not just one piece of the process
- Comfortable writing customer case studies and shepherding them through internal and customer approval
- Some exposure to product marketing (positioning, launch content, sales enablement) is a plus, not required
- Design/visual skills are a strong plus
- Full-stack mindset: comfortable being hands-on across multiple channels rather than a narrow specialist
