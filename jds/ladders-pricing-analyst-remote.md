## Full JD Text

For our client, we are seeking a Pricing Analyst (Remote) to join the team of a leader in the Information Technology space.

This role will focus on pricing analysis that supports competitive positioning, margin improvement, and stronger sales execution in a fast-moving IT environment. You will partner with sales and other stakeholders to evaluate complex opportunities, translate data into actionable recommendations, and help drive consistent pricing practices across the organization. The position also plays an important role in leadership reporting and in improving the adoption of pricing tools and best practices.

Location: Remote

Compensation: $110,000 – $120,000 annually

Responsibilities

-  Provide pricing analysis and recommendations to enhance competitiveness.
-  Review business data to identify margin opportunities and performance trends.
-  Support complex sales opportunities by evaluating options and collaborating with stakeholders.
-  Translate analysis into actionable recommendations and leadership reporting.
-  Promote adoption of pricing tools and best practices across the sales team.
-  Document outcomes to improve pricing consistency and effectiveness.

Qualifications

-  3-5 years of experience in pricing, sales operations, or financial analysis.
-  Strong analytical skills to translate data into actionable recommendations.
-  Advanced proficiency in Microsoft Excel for data analysis and reporting.
-  Excellent communication skills, both written and verbal.
-  Bachelor's degree in Business, Finance, Economics, or a related field.

Benefits

-  Comprehensive medical, dental, and vision plans for you and your dependents.
-  401(k) Retirement Plan with Employer Match and Health Savings Account.
-  Training and development programs to enhance skills.
-  Collaborative culture with a focus on work-life balance and generous paid time off.
-  Major offices stocked with snacks and beverages.

Our client is an equal opportunity employer. We encourage you to apply even if you don’t meet every qualification—your background could be exactly what this team needs.
