The Prime Onchain team builds infrastructure letting asset managers, financial institutions, and crypto-native funds earn onchain rewards and access DeFi yield through Coinbase Prime. The PM II defines product strategy for curated DeFi vaults, staking, and institutional reporting, with direct exposure to large clients.

Responsibilities: own end-to-end strategy and roadmap for institutional onchain yield (vaults, staking, reporting); drive delivery of compliant, scalable solutions across multiple chains and DeFi protocols; work directly with institutional clients to gather feedback and maintain security/operational standards; prioritize investments weighing client needs, protocol risk, and market conditions; partner with engineering, design, protocol specialists, sales, and legal on custody, reporting, and regulatory standards.

Required: 5+ years of product management experience including shipping enterprise/B2B crypto products; deep understanding of DeFi yield mechanics (lending, vaults, liquid staking tokens, staking) and their risks; experience turning institutional clients' operational pain points into product requirements; experience with reporting/regulatory considerations for onchain products including qualified custody; familiarity with staking ecosystems (Ethereum, Solana) and hands-on DeFi protocol experience; responsible use of generative AI with human oversight.
