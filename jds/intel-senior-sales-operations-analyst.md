## Role Summary
Foundry Business Group Operations seeks a Senior Sales Operations Analyst to support
forecasting, demand planning, business operations for Intel Foundry customers. Bridges
Business Development, Supply Chain, Manufacturing, Finance; primary operations interface
for Front-End (FE) Wafer demand planning.

## Key Responsibilities
- Manage demand planning/forecasting (production demand in Blue Yonder CDP; engineering
  demand planning in SAP IBP).
- Lead forecast reviews and planning cycles across stakeholders.
- Set up forecasting readiness for new products (demand assumptions, lifecycle planning).
- Support S&OP and LRP with demand scenarios, assumptions, risk analysis.
- Analyze customer forecasts, trends, market conditions to improve accuracy.
- Build executive dashboards (demand, capacity utilization, inventory health, revenue
  opportunities).
- Monitor forecast accuracy, bias, lag; recommend corrective actions.
- Align forecasts with capacity, inventory, supply commitments with Supply Chain and
  Manufacturing.
- Maintain customer rolling forecasts with Customer Business Analysts.
- Identify capacity gaps/fulfillment risks; drive mitigation.
- Support NPI, engineering builds, HVM ramps, customer milestones.
- Drive continuous improvement in forecasting methods, data quality, efficiency.

## Minimum qualifications
- Bachelor's degree (see above).
- 6+ years in sales operations, demand planning, supply chain analytics, business
  planning, or capacity planning in semiconductor or advanced manufacturing.
- 6+ years supporting forecasting, planning, or business operations.
- Experience with S&OP, IBP, or similar planning methods.
- Proficiency with SAP IBP, Blue Yonder, or similar planning tools.
- Advanced Excel skills (complex modeling, forecasting).
- Experience analyzing large datasets, turning findings into recommendations.

## Preferred qualifications
Postgraduate degree (business, supply chain, engineering, finance/MBA, operations, or
similar); foundry/wafer fabrication/advanced tech development experience; semiconductor
supply chain/capacity planning knowledge; Power BI/Tableau experience; executive
presentation/data-storytelling skills; process improvement track record; NPI/engineering/
HVM planning experience.
