## Full JD text (verbatim)

We're looking for a Go-To-Market Compensation Lead to build and scale the incentive compensation programs that support our revenue growth. You'll lead compensation design in partnership with the Head of Revenue Operations and GTM and Finance leadership, translating business priorities into plans that are clear, motivating, and financially sound.

You'll own commission operations end to end, including our incentive compensation management (ICM) system. This is a hands-on leadership role for someone who can move from strategic recommendations to detailed execution, operate independently through ambiguity, and take extreme ownership of both the numbers and the employee experience.

**What you'll do**
- Lead compensation design: Partner with the Head of Revenue Operations and GTM and Finance leadership to develop and evolve incentive plans across GTM roles. Recommend performance measures, payout structures, accelerators, and crediting rules.
- Model business impact: Build scenarios to assess compensation cost, earnings opportunities, and incentive alignment.
- Own commission operations: Run the full commission cycle, from source-data validation and crediting through calculations, reconciliation, approvals, and payroll handoff.
- Own the ICM system: Lead system configuration, plan implementation, testing, administration, and ongoing improvements.
- Build scalable processes and controls: Establish documentation, approval workflows, and audit trails for plans, exceptions, and adjustments.
- Make compensation understandable: Lead plan communications and enablement so employees and managers understand how incentives work.
- Partner with Finance: Provide reliable payout reporting, accrual inputs, forecasts, and variance analysis.

**What you bring**
- Relevant experience: 4+ years of GTM compensation experience in SaaS or another high-growth industry, with meaningful ownership of both plan design and commission operations.
- Autonomy and extreme ownership: a track record of setting priorities, identifying gaps, making sound recommendations, and driving work to completion without close supervision.
- Exceptional attention to detail: a rigorous approach to validating data, testing calculations, documenting decisions.
- Hands-on systems expertise: experience administering an ICM platform and working with CRM, finance, and payroll data.
- Strong analytical judgment: advanced spreadsheet and financial modeling skills.
- Clear communication and partnership: ability to influence senior stakeholders and explain complex compensation mechanics simply.
- AI perspective and drive: a strong perspective on how AI is changing traditional commissions and compensation plan processing.

**Nice to have**
- Evolving business models: experience with consumption-based, usage-based, or hybrid subscription models.
- Systems implementation: experience implementing or migrating an ICM platform and automating compensation workflows.
- Technical fluency: SQL or BI skills to investigate data issues and improve reporting.
