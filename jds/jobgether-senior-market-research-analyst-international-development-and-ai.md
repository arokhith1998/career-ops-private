**Responsibilities (summarized):**
- Lead market research on institutional priorities, AI adoption, and demand for AI training data, multilingual datasets, and human evaluation services in international development.

- Quantitative and qualitative research skills, strong reporting and presentation skills, and excellent English communication.
- Proficiency in research methods, data analysis, and reporting tools.
- Ability to consistently work Central Time (CT) business hours, regardless of location.

**Preferred (not required):**
- Master's degree in Economics, Public Policy, International Development, Development Economics, or a related field.
- Experience with multilateral development banks, development organizations, or government agencies.
- Multi-country research, especially in developing and emerging markets.
- Knowledge of development funding mechanisms and procurement, and of AI or digital development.
- Market-entry, feasibility, or partnership experience.
