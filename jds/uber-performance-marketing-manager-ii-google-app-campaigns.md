## Role summary
Leads strategy, execution, and optimization of Google App Campaigns (UAC) across Uber's global businesses, focused on app acquisition and re-engagement. Works with Google and cross-functional teams: Product, Engineering, Ad Tech, Data Science, Marketing Strategy.

## Responsibilities
- Manage and optimize global UAC campaigns against acquisition, engagement, and efficiency goals.
- Own the UAC roadmap across bidding, audience signals, campaign structure, inventory, measurement, and new Google products.
- Design and lead experiments to measure incrementality and improve optimization signals.
- Partner with other teams to improve measurement, automation, bidding, and campaign management.
- Build the Google relationship, evaluate new products, turn platform opportunities into strategy.
- Use data and experimentation to diagnose performance changes and guide investment decisions.
- Lead complex cross-functional projects on measurement, testing, and channel health.
- Drive process improvements and scalable operating practices.
