Founder’s Office | Strategy & Studio Operations – Junior Executive

We’re looking for a sharp, organised Junior Executive to work closely with the Founder & Creative Director at Studio Take Two.

This is a role for someone who wants to learn how a creative business works, from brand strategy and design to content, operations and business building.

You’ll be right in the middle of things, working closely with the Founder to:

→ Assist with strategy, research and brand projects

→ Manage internal coordination, projects and follow-ups

→ Bring structure and clarity to a fast-moving studio

→ Support founder-led content, LinkedIn and social media

→ Write blogs, posts and other brand communications

→ Help turn ideas into things that actually get done

→ Solve problems, think ahead and keep things moving

We’re looking for someone who

* Is an excellent writer and communicator

* Is highly organised and detail-oriented

* Understands social media and has an interest in branding and business

* Can think independently and figure things out

* Stays calm when there are 20 things happening at once

* Is proactive and doesn’t need to be told what to do at every step

* Enjoys moving between strategy, people, content and execution

0–2 years of experience. (Please note this is an entry level position)

Recent graduates are welcome. Previous agency experience isn’t required.
