About Factory

Our mission is to create a world where software builds and improves itself.

We build the platform for self-improving software development, helping the world's largest enterprise engineering teams turn their organizations into autonomous software factories. Customers including Nvidia, Blackstone, Royal Bank of Canada (RBC), Palo Alto Networks, Adobe, and T-Mobile run Factory to ship code, review PRs, run tests, and deploy continuously - getting smarter with every cycle.

In 2026, we raised $200M at a $5B valuation, bringing our total funding to over $400M. We are backed by world-class investors and operators including Khosla Ventures, Sequoia, Blackstone, Ali Ghodsi (CEO of Databricks), and Frank Slootman (Former CEO of Snowflake).

About The Role

As the PM for Scale at Factory, you'll own how our platform supports end-to-end software development at the largest companies in the world.

Every CIO/CTO is thinking about governing AI proliferation, controlling cost, and measuring token ROI. This role is about making the Factory platform the best way to do this when it comes to how AI is used for software engineering.

This is not a "tick the RBAC and audit logs boxes" role (though getting these features right is critical). It's about empathizing with the challenge of managing AI coding tools within an enterprise, and building the capabilities and visibility that admins, executives, and end-users need to trust Factory and operate it efficiently. And it's about building features that let Factory customers codify their organizational knowledge, rules, and best-practices in a way that lets them raise the bar for the software they build across their company.

You'll own our Enterprise NDR metric, ensuring our customers are retaining and growing with Factory. You'll work across a number of our different engineering pods to help prioritize the right features at the right time. You'll work closely with our Sales, Solutions Engineering, and Deployment teams to make sure we're incorporating feedback from the field, and pushing out new capabilities and techniques effectively. And you'll work directly with customers to make sure we're providing the right primitives to let them build and govern their ultimate software factory.

What You Will Do and Achieve

Own the roadmap for multiple major areas of our product. Partner closely with customers and work closely with data to understand pain points and translate them into product improvements. Work with engineering and research to ship features that keep Factory at the frontier of AI-powered development. Build frameworks and processes that scale as the product team grows.

Qualifications

5+ years of experience spanning product and engineering roles, including a deep developer tools background or at least one year in an engineering role. Deep experience with what large-scale enterprise companies need to deploy developer tools across their organization. Experience working closely with executive buyers at large organizations. Strong technical foundation, with experience partnering closely with engineering teams to design, build, and ship complex technical products. Proven ability to drive product launches, from concept through release, with evidence of meaningful user adoption or commercial impact. Actively engaged with the AI ecosystem, staying current on coding agents, model capabilities, and emerging industry trends through hands-on experimentation. Thrive in fast-moving, high-ambiguity environments and take ownership end-to-end.

The team works in the office 5 days a week in San Francisco (walking distance to Caltrain).
