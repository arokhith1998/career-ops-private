## Job Description (extracted, verbatim from posting)

As a Senior Growth Marketing Manager, you will play a critical role in scaling Roblox's global growth engine, helping millions of users discover, engage with, and return to Roblox. This role sits at the intersection of marketing, product, data science, and engineering. You will lead high-impact growth initiatives spanning user acquisition, lifecycle marketing, personalization, creator growth, and product-led growth. Reports to the Head of Growth Marketing.

**You Will:**
- Own and scale growth programs across user acquisition, reactivation, paid influencer, and product-led growth
- Manage and optimize multimillion-dollar marketing investments (Google, YouTube, Meta, TikTok, Apple Search Ads, DSPs)
- Develop hypotheses, design experiments, establish measurement frameworks
- Partner with Data Science on incrementality, attribution, LTV, retention, monetization
- Shape the user journey with Product, Engineering, Design, Data Science
- Explore emerging channels, partnerships, creators, and platform opportunities
- Influence across Marketing, Product, Engineering, Data Science, Finance, Business Development

**You Have:**
- "12+ years of experience in Growth Marketing, Product Growth, Performance Marketing, or related disciplines at a high growth consumer technology company."
- Advanced SQL proficiency, experimentation methodologies, incrementality measurement, attribution, cohort analysis
- Strong grasp of LTV, retention, monetization, unit economics
- Technical and AI fluency

Preferred: experience scaling a global consumer marketplace/gaming/social platform; managing $10M+ annual paid media budgets.
