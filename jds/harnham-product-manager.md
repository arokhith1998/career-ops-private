## Job Description (extracted, verbatim from LinkedIn posting)

Senior/Principal Product Manager, AI Products

Hybrid (3 days/week) - San Francisco Bay Area or Seattle - Not open for relocation

Base Salary: Up to $200,000 + Potential Additional Compensation

US Citizens or Green Card Holders only (no sponsorship available now or in the future)

I'm partnering with a rapidly growing technology company that is investing heavily in the next generation of AI-powered enterprise software. They're looking for a highly strategic and technically credible Product Manager to lead the development, launch, and growth of a customer-facing AI product suite built on a rich foundation of proprietary data and analytics.

This is a highly visible role where you'll own the product lifecycle end-to-end, from identifying customer problems and defining strategy through commercialization, adoption, and ongoing

optimization.

What You'll Be Doing

- Define and own the roadmap, vision, and execution strategy for AI-driven enterprise products.
- Develop and launch intelligent workflows leveraging modern AI technologies, including LLMs, generative AI, retrieval-augmented systems, and agentic experiences.
- Partner closely with Engineering, Data Science, Design, Sales, and Customer Success teams to bring products from concept to production.
- Lead customer discovery efforts, validate market opportunities, and translate business needs into scalable product solutions.
- Establish product success metrics and use adoption, engagement, and business outcomes to guide roadmap decisions.
- Drive commercialization strategies, pricing approaches, go-to-market execution, and customer adoption initiatives.
- Evaluate AI reliability, governance, guardrails, and production readiness before deployment.

What We're Looking For

Required

- 5+ years of Product Management experience within enterprise B2B software environments.
- 2+ years owning and delivering customer-facing AI, machine learning, analytics, or data products in production.
- Demonstrated success owning product strategy, prioritization, roadmap development, and execution.
- Experience launching AI-powered products that achieved measurable customer adoption and business impact.
- Strong understanding of LLMs, RAG architectures, AI agents, evaluation frameworks, and responsible AI concepts.
- Exceptional stakeholder management and customer discovery skills.
- Ability to operate effectively in a fast-paced, cross-functional environment.

Strongly Preferred

- Zero-to-one product development experience.
- Experience scaling AI products after initial launch.
- Background in workflow automation, analytics platforms, developer tools, or enterprise data products.
- Experience with AI commercialization, pricing strategies, and product monetization.
- Startup or high-growth technology company experience.
- Exposure to industries such as mobility, transportation, connected devices, IoT, fleet management, or automotive technology.

Target Backgrounds

We're particularly interested in Product Managers currently building and scaling AI-powered products at:

- Enterprise AI software companies
- Data & analytics platform providers
- Developer tooling and AI infrastructure organizations
- Modern SaaS companies leveraging generative AI at scale

What Will Make You Stand Out

- You've personally owned an AI product from discovery through production launch.
- You can articulate when to use traditional software, LLMs, RAG, or autonomous agents to solve business problems.
- You have measurable examples of driving product adoption, revenue growth, workflow efficiency, customer retention, or business outcomes through AI-powered solutions.
- You understand both the opportunities and limitations of generative AI in enterprise environments.

If you're excited about building customer-facing AI products that move beyond experimentation and into real-world adoption at scale, I'd welcome a confidential conversation.
