# Role profiles

One profile per role family per region. A profile says how to present the
candidate for that kind of job: which title line, which summary, which skills
lead, which employers and bullets come first, what to leave out.

**Facts come only from `cv.md`.** A profile never adds a fact or a number. When
the JD asks for something `cv.md` does not have, show the closest real analog.

## Pick the profile

1. Decide the track from the posting's location (`config/nightly.yml -> tracks`).
2. Pick the profile whose "Route here when" best matches the JD title and top
   responsibilities. Blended JDs start from the dominant one.

## Rules that apply to every profile

- Header city: `San Francisco Bay Area, CA` (both tracks). Links display
  `LinkedIn` and `Portfolio`; Portfolio carries `/ref/{company-slug}`.
- Titles stay as in `cv.md`. The two Sensata titles show as a promotion under one
  employer: Pricing Lead (Jul 2025 - May 2026), Product & Growth Marketing
  (Sep 2024 - Jun 2025).
- **PriceKeel** leads Experience on pricing roles (`us-pricing`), RevOps roles
  (`us-revops`, where the CRM/CPQ and deal-approval work sits) and both India profiles.
  Elsewhere it is left off. No traction numbers, ever.
- Applied Materials appears on every resume as **Strategic Marketing III** (never shortened or retitled); each profile names which of its `cv.md` bullets lead. No metrics.
- **The Claude AI-agent automation bullet is on EVERY resume**, every profile, both tracks, and each Summary mentions it.
- Education: no graduation years. Qbox line only on India profiles.
- 1 page, packed to the bottom, every bullet tied to the JD. Standard keyboard
  characters only. No visa mention.
- Archived April 2026 variants (pre-PriceKeel, pre-Applied Materials) are in
  `archive-2026-04/` for reference only.
