Hiring Team, Expel

Performance marketing in B2B SaaS breaks at one specific seam: the gap between channel reporting and the pipeline number Sales actually trusts. Most teams have respectable ROAS on LinkedIn and respectable cost-per-MQL on Google and a story for programmatic, and none of those numbers reconcile with the SQL count Sales is tracking. The fix is rarely a new channel. It is closed-loop attribution, suppression rules that actually exclude existing accounts, and an A/B testing cadence that ships faster than the creative team can fatigue.

I have rebuilt that stack at Plug Power. Multi-channel paid across Google Ads, Meta, LinkedIn, and Adobe-driven programmatic display, all stitched into closed-loop attribution on Salesforce, Adobe Analytics, GA4, and GTM. UTM spec from scratch, GTM containers rebuilt, lead routing validated through Salesforce campaign attribution. CPA dropped 25%, conversion went up 20%. Adobe Audience Manager carried the account-level segmentation, retargeting funnels, and suppression-list logic, which is the operating model 6sense and similar ABX platforms productize. The named platform changes; the playbook does not.

At Pixis, I worked inside the same B2B SaaS adtech category Expel sells into. Ran an 8-client B2B SaaS portfolio on a $1.4M media budget across Google, Meta, programmatic, and email. Validated a 3x ROAS lift for a fashion partner via holdout incrementality testing and reconciled it against creative-variant diagnostics that aggregate ROAS would have hidden. Held vendors and clients to delivery and quality standards in writing. Replaced two channels mid-quarter when KPIs slipped. Churn dropped 50% through per-client renewal-aligned playbooks. Pixis is the closest in-category analog you will see in any performance-marketing-manager candidate's CV.

On consumer paid breadth, I delivered 16x ROAS at GenY for a D2C brand by rebuilding Google Merchant Center and Meta catalog feeds. Feed cleanup, schema fixes, structured Shopping and Advantage+ campaigns. The mechanic is different from B2B SaaS, but the discipline of structured testing and creative iteration is the same.

On the experimentation muscle, I shipped SwipeHire end-to-end on Python, OpenAI API, and React with GA4 plus GTM event tracking from day one. Eight A/B tests on activation flows, weekly retention cohort analysis, +25% activation. The instrumentation came before the launch, which is what made the experiments useful instead of decorative.

What I have not run directly is 6sense as a named platform. The ABX operating model (account segmentation, intent signals, sales alignment) is what I built at Pixis on the B2B SaaS account portfolio and at Plug Power on Adobe Audience Manager. 6sense is a week-one ramp, not a new craft.
