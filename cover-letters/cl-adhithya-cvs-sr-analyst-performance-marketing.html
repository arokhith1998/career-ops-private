Performance marketing breaks when the analyst executing the campaign and the analyst measuring the campaign are two different people with two different definitions of success. The best performance marketers are the ones who own both sides: set up the campaign, manage the agency, run the creative review, launch, and then pull the data themselves to tell the team what actually worked and what did not. That closed loop is the only way to get from "we spent $X" to "we drove $Y in measurable customer outcomes."

That closed loop is how I already work.

At Plug Power I owned end-to-end performance marketing across Google Ads, SA360, Meta, and LinkedIn for a B2B audience. I managed the agency briefings, creative reviews, and campaign approvals on the execution side. Then I built the Adobe Analytics + GA + GTM measurement stack from scratch on the analytics side, so I could trace every campaign from ad click to qualified lead. The result was -25% CPA and +20% conversion. The lift came from closing the loop, not from running better ads.
