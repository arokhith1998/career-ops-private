Most market analysis assumes hundreds of buyers and a fragmented competitive set. Semiconductor equipment is the opposite. Five customers (Intel, TSMC, Samsung, plus a small tail) drive most of the demand. Four competitors (LAM, TEL, KLA, ASML for litho) define the competitive frame. That changes the craft. You are not running surveys to estimate demand from populations. You are modeling specific customer capex cycles, building forecasts at the contract and tool level, and stress-testing your own roadmap against four to five named competitors whose moves you can name in advance.
