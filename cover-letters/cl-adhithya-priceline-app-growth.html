Hiring Team, Priceline

Mobile app paid acquisition is one of the cleanest tests in performance marketing. The conversion event is real (install plus first booking), the creative iteration cycle is short, and the post-IDFA attribution work has forced the discipline of the entire industry up to where closed-loop measurement always should have been. Travel apps are an even better test bed because the buyer arrives with intent and the funnel is short enough that you can read week-over-week creative performance instead of guessing across quarters.

Most of what your JD asks for is muscle I have already built. At Plug Power I owned paid acquisition across Google Ads, Meta, LinkedIn, and Adobe-driven programmatic display. I rebuilt closed-loop attribution from ad click to SQL on Salesforce, Adobe Analytics, GA4, and GTM. UTM spec from scratch, GTM containers rebuilt, lead routing validated through Salesforce campaign attribution. CPA dropped 25%, conversion went up 20%. The mental model behind that work is the same as post-IDFA SKAN attribution: deterministic where you can, probabilistic where you cannot, and ruthless about which conversion event you actually trust.

At Pixis I ran an 8-client B2B SaaS portfolio on a $1.4M budget with weekly multi-channel optimization across Google, Meta, programmatic, and email. Validated a 3x ROAS lift for a fashion partner via holdout incrementality testing. The Pixis tenure is also where I trained eight enterprise marketers on AI bidding and creative tools, which is the same iteration-velocity question Priceline app growth has to answer at scale.

On the consumer paid side, I delivered 16x ROAS at GenY for a D2C e-commerce brand by rebuilding Google Merchant Center and Meta catalog feeds. Feed cleanup, schema fixes, metadata, structured Shopping and Advantage+ campaigns. The mechanic of optimizing a Shopping feed for organic and paid visibility is conceptually the same as ASO: structured metadata, conversion-rate-optimized creative, ranking signal management. Different surfaces, same craft.

On app instrumentation, SwipeHire is the case study. I built it solo on Python, React, and OpenAI API, instrumented GA4 plus GTM event tracking from day one, ran eight A/B tests on activation flows, and ran weekly retention cohort analysis. 50+ users, +25% activation. Building the instrumentation before the launch is what turns the test backlog from decoration into a flywheel.

The tools I have not used directly are Appsflyer, Firebase, Adjust, Kochava, and Apple Search Ads specifically. The operating model maps cleanly from Adobe Audience Manager plus Salesforce plus GA4 plus Google Ads, and these are week-one ramps rather than new crafts.
