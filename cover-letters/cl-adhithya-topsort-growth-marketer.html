Growth at adtech-for-marketplaces is a buyer-education problem before it's a channel problem. The marketplace VP of Monetization who'd buy Topsort doesn't Google "retail media platform" at 11pm. She reads a Decibel-style teardown a peer forwarded her, watches one founder talk at IRCE, and then takes a meeting six months later because someone she trusts said it worked. Performance marketing on cold audiences is the wrong instinct here. The interesting growth lever is compressing the education cycle — making the case once, in a format that travels through that buyer's network without you in the room.
