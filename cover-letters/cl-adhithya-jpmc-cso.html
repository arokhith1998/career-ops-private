The reason that compounding ever stops is almost never the creative or the buyer; it is that the measurement framework was not built well enough to support the next experiment in the queue. Owned channel programs that lose their edge are usually programs that lost their measurement plumbing first.

I have rebuilt that plumbing at Plug Power. UTM spec from scratch, GTM containers rebuilt, lead routing validated through Salesforce campaign attribution, the full click-to-SQL chain finally one number rather than three. CPA dropped 25%, conversion went up 20%, and the team could run experiments faster than we could buy traffic. The Adobe Audience Manager segmentation and suppression-list work paired with that. Net-new leads are decided at the bottom of the funnel, not the top, and we rewrote the suppression rules to actually reflect that.
