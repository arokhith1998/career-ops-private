Hiring Team, Strava

B2B retention is the part of growth marketing where every test has a renewal consequence attached. Activation experiments either build the early habit that survives month three, or they decorate the funnel and make the dashboard look better while churn keeps creeping. The ones that stick are usually the ones with a clear thesis about what behavior signals retention, instrumented before the test ships, and documented in a per-account playbook so the team can repeat the wins without rediscovering them.

I have done that work at Pixis. I owned retention across an 8-client B2B SaaS portfolio on a $1.4M budget. The team replaced two underperforming channels mid-quarter when KPIs slipped, rebuilt creative when ROAS plateaued, and rewrote per-client renewal-aligned playbooks in writing so the next quarter started from a stronger baseline. Churn dropped 50%. The discipline that scaled was documentation, not heroics. I led an 8-person team across creative, account management, and platform specialists; ran weekly QBRs that paired ROI with the next two A/B tests in the queue.

On the lifecycle and email side, I rebuilt Plug Power's MarTech stack on Salesforce, Adobe Analytics, GA4, and GTM. Email plus paid integrated, audience segments and retargeting funnels in Adobe Audience Manager, suppression lists for existing accounts. CPA dropped 25%, conversion went up 20%. The lifecycle work was where the compounding lived.

SwipeHire is the activation-and-retention case study. I built it solo on Python, React, and OpenAI API, instrumented GA4 and GTM event tracking from day one, ran eight A/B tests on activation flows and onboarding email sequences, and ran weekly retention cohort analysis. 50+ users, +25% activation. The instrumentation came before the launch, which is what made the experiments useful.

On in-app engagement, I have run feature-adoption work at Sensata across 50K+ connected devices, with weekly KPI cadence to leadership. Adoption up 18%. The mechanic is the same as Strava in-app: design the touchpoint, instrument the response, iterate weekly.
