Hiring Team, Go-to-Market — Skydio

Hardware GTM enablement is structurally different from SaaS enablement, and most orgs miss it. SaaS reps win by being good at the demo. Hardware reps win when the customer's first deploy actually works in the field — which means enablement has to bridge from sales pitch to first successful mission. The gap between "they signed the order" and "the chief actually flew it on a barricade response" is where deals stall, contracts churn, and reference customers fail to materialize. The good enablement orgs build for that bridge from day one. The bad ones build a battlecard and call it done.

That's the part of GTM Enablement I want to work on, and it lines up with what I've actually done.

At Sensata I ran enablement for industrial-sensor sales into OEMs, distributors, and engineering buyers. The product is hardware. The buyers care about spec compatibility, integration, and how fast their team can get something into production. I did 30+ field interviews with reps and partners to figure out where deals were actually getting stuck (it was rarely where Sales said). Then I co-built decks, battlecards, and case studies with the people who'd use them — 50+ reps across NA and EU. Partner win rates went up 30%. The content worked because reps had drafted it. And because we measured adoption (read time, share rate, attach rate to closed-won deals) instead of guessing.
