Beauty marketing analytics is different from every other CPG vertical, and most analysts learn this the hard way. Purchase decisions in beauty are emotionally loaded, category loyalty is fragile, and the gap between "tried once" and "repurchased" is where most activation ROI quietly dies. The analyst who catches this is the one who tracks beyond the first conversion and builds dashboards that show what happens after the promo ends, not just during it.

I learned this at Pixis, where I managed campaigns for one of India's largest beauty brands as part of a $1.4M D2C and e-commerce portfolio. The brand was stuck at flat ROAS. I restructured the full funnel: Shopping feed cleanup, creative refresh aligned to how beauty consumers actually browse (visual-first, ingredient-driven), promotion strategy that drove trial without cannibalizing margin, and lookalike expansion based on repurchase behavior rather than first-click. 3x ROAS in 30 days. The insight was not about spending more. It was about measuring what happened after the first purchase and optimizing for the second one.
