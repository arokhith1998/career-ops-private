Why this deck exists

A CI program's hardest part is not the first battlecard. It is keeping sourcing, fact-checking and distribution to four different teams running every single cycle.

What a CI program runs into

A battlecard lands well once, then goes stale within a quarter because nobody owns the refresh cadence and the vendor/source list behind it.
Win/loss signal, pricing moves and retention risk arrive from Sales, Customer Success, Product and Marketing separately, and rarely get synthesized into one view before a deal needs it.
The fix is not one more report. It is one owner who sources it, fact-checks it, and automates the refresh so the next battlecard is a review, not a rebuild.
