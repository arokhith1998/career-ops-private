First 30 days

What the first month on Alcatraz's product marketing team looks like.

Week 1
Learn the current motion
Sit with the Head of Product Marketing, sales and product to map which battlecards, win/loss notes and demand-gen content exist today for each buyer (security, IT, facilities, executive), and which of it is actually winning deals.
Why first: the strongest positioning pattern is usually visible in what already closed. Same move that drove the 15% conversion lift at Sensata.
Week 2-3
Ship one launch, one buyer, end to end
Take one upcoming feature or launch through positioning, the sales playbook (battlecard, deck, demo video) and buyer-specific translation for one audience, working directly with sales and product.
Why now: building the story on a live launch, not a hypothetical, is what made the Sensata enablement actually get used by 50+ reps.
Week 4
Stand up the win/loss and AI-first loop
Template the win/loss intake and program-performance tracking, then automate the recurring half (competitive scans, report rollups) with AI tools, so the system runs on its own between launches.
Why this fast: a win/loss program only earns its name once it is feeding the next positioning pass, not sitting in a spreadsheet nobody opens.
