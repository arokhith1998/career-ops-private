Why this deck exists

The risk in a first pricing hire is not finding someone who can discuss usage-based pricing. It is finding someone who has already built the governance a pricing model needs once real deals show up.

What a company's first pricing hire usually runs into

Discount exceptions and order forms get handled case by case, so by the time deal volume scales there is no audit trail for why a quote looked the way it did.
Enterprise deals get negotiated one at a time with no shared framework, so every rep reinvents commits, volume tiers and discount thresholds from scratch.
The fix is not a pricing committee. It is one owner who builds the data foundation, the guardrails and the deal desk before the gaps compound.
