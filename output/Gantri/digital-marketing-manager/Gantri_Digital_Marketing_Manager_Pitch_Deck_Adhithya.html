Why this deck exists

The risk in a DTC marketing engine is not the first campaign. It is who keeps the agencies and the reporting accountable every cycle after.

What a DTC marketing engine runs into

Paid media and email/SMS agencies each optimize to their own brief, so CAC, ROAS and MER drift apart unless one person owns both relationships and the targets.
Cross-channel reporting gets built once for a launch readout, then the next cycle is a manual pull because nobody owns the pixels, UTMs and server-side events feeding it.
A testing roadmap stalls between agency check-ins unless someone is running the calendar, not just reviewing it after the fact.

What I have already run, hands on

At Plug Power, I automated the Mar-Tech stack (Salesforce, Adobe Analytics, GA, Google Tag Manager) into one closed-loop, cross-channel tracking and reporting system, not a deck I rebuilt by hand.
At Pixis, an ad-tech platform, I hired and managed an 8-person team running paid media for 8 D2C clients at $15K-$90K/month each, the agency side of the exact relationship this role owns.
At Applied Materials, I automate the recurring part of that reporting work with AI agents built on Claude today.
Own paid media and email/SMS agency relationships, build cross-channel reporting, and run structured testing programs: this is describing the exact loop I already run, from both sides of the agency table.
