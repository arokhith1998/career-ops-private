Three growth-marketing problems unique to the CKE bet
Otter is asking buyers to adopt a category they don't yet have a search term for. That changes how paid social has to work.
Problem 01

Category creation, not category capture.

Buyers won't search "Conversational Knowledge Engine" tomorrow. Paid social has to do double duty: build the category narrative AND capture the demand it creates. Most teams pick one.

My answer: pair a category-narrative content layer (LinkedIn + X) with retargeting that converts the surface area into demos.
Problem 02

Creative velocity is the moat.

Meta, YouTube, TikTok all reward variant volume. Most teams ship 5 creatives a week. AI-native teams ship 50. The work isn't creative production - it is creative briefing, judging, and shipping at speed.

My answer: a weekly creative review cadence with AI-generated variants from a small library of proven hooks.
Problem 03

AI products need AI-native systems.

pLTV-weighted bidding, generative variant testing, agentic experiment briefing. The metrics stack (cohort retention, paid-to-paid LTV) has to keep up with the channel mix.

My answer: an experiment system measured in tests-shipped-per-dollar, not tests-shipped-per-quarter.
Reality check: I have not seen Otter's actual paid stack. These are my reads from the JD, the Forbes 2026 mention, and three weeks watching your category-creation content. Happy to revise once we talk.

My first 90 days at Otter
Concrete, not aspirational. Each milestone is something I have done before, mapped to where Otter likely sits today.
DAYS 1-30

Audit and instrument

  • Channel-level audit on Meta, YouTube, TikTok. Pull last-90 spend, CPA, MQL/SQL ratios by creative and audience.
  • Stand up incrementality holdout structure for Meta + TikTok (have done this twice).
  • Ship first 8 creative variants on the top-funnel mover. Document the hook-claim-CTA framework.
  • Map cohort retention to acquisition source. Surface 1 surprise to Zaw within 14 days.
DAYS 31-60

Test and scale

  • pLTV-weighted bidding pilot on Meta + TikTok. Reweight spend by predicted retention, not just CPA.
  • Launch the category-narrative content layer (LinkedIn + X founder-led) to feed retargeting and brand-lift.
  • First spend-shift recommendation to channel mix. Defensible model on Power BI or Sheets.
  • Ship 20+ creatives across the period. Weekly creative review cadence operational.
DAYS 61-90

Compound and automate

  • Brand-lift study scoped and running on a top-3 channel.
  • Pipeline-attributed reporting live with paid-to-paid LTV by channel and creative theme.
  • Automate briefing, variant generation, and reporting into an AI-driven workflow. Free 8 hours/week.
  • Hand Zaw a 6-month spend plan with explicit experiment bets, not just channel splits.

The bet I would make on day 1: creative velocity. If we ship 50 variants/week instead of 5, the algo learns faster and CPA compounds down. The rest of the plan stays the same; this is the lever I would defend hardest.
