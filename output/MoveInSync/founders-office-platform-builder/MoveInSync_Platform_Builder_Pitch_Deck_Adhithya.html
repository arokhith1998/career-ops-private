First 30 days

What the first month in MoveInSync's founder's office looks like.

Week 1
Learn the real function
Sit with the paired leader and the people doing the work to find the org-chart version and the real one: who owns what, where work gets stuck, which handoffs quietly break.
Why first: the same move behind the Bleeders and Leakers system: you cannot build the right tool until you have seen the actual break, not the reported one.
Week 2-3
Ship a first working version
Agree with the leader on what "good" looks like in a measurable number, then build a scrappy first version with AI tooling doing the heavy lifting, in front of real users fast.
Why now: the same build-fast, iterate-with-real-users habit that shipped SwipeHire and the NSE bot solo.
Week 4
Prove the number moved
Measure the tool against the number agreed in week 2, fix what does not hold, and start connecting this tool to the next function's interface.
Why this fast: a tool nobody uses, or that nobody can prove moved a number, is not a finished project.
