First 30 days

What the first month on Envoy's launch and enablement motion looks like.

Week 1
Run the inbound loop first
Pull recent win/loss reports and run structured interviews with Sales and CS on live deals to find what is actually moving the security-and-compliance buying committee, the same 30+-interview discipline behind the Sensata launch, before touching the narrative.
Why first: research shapes the narrative, not the other way around.
Week 2-3
Ship the launch tiering and workback
Build the narrative, launch tiering framework and cross-functional workback for the next feature or launch on the roadmap, with the sales-enablement assets, decks, one-pagers, BDR sequences, versioned alongside it, not after.
Why now: the same build-it-together pattern that drove $2M+ and a 30-point win-rate lift at Sensata.
Week 4
Close the retrospective loop
Run the post-launch retrospective, feed it and the ongoing competitive tracking into the next cycle's playbook and pricing/packaging input, then automate the recurring reporting step with an AI agent so the loop runs every cycle, not once a quarter.
Why this fast: the same automation habit already running my own reporting at Applied Materials.
