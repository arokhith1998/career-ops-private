The SwipeHire + Sensata playbook for Weave

Weave is selling something engineering teams will only adopt if it respects their domain depth. The Sensata buyer was the same. SwipeHire taught me to ship marketing as fast as the product evolves.

What I have shipped that matters here

  • SwipeHire founder marketing: brand from zero, 5+ ICP landing pages, 12+ LinkedIn + X posts, referral loop driving 60% of signups, weekly product updates with demos and changelog
  • Sensata sales enablement: Library used by 50+ reps. Translated capacitive/pressure/MEMS sensor architecture into VP-ready talk tracks. Lifted win rates 30%.
  • Plug Power AEO/GEO: Shipped programs so the brand surfaced in ChatGPT and Claude buyer queries when engineering buyers asked "how do I measure X". -25% CPA across paid channels.
  • Pixis category-creation: Trained 8 enterprise teams on a new AI marketing platform category. Knew how to communicate AI to a skeptical CFO.
"The Weave buyer wants to see how the measurement model works, not how clean the landing page is. I would write the technical blog, not just the product page."

How I would ramp at Weave

  • Week 1-2: Sit with founders + 3 customers. Read codebase commits and product roadmap. Map 3 buyer personas (Eng Mgr, VP Eng, CTO). Draft brand voice + technical-tone style guide.
  • Week 3-4: Ship 1 founder-led technical post on LinkedIn + Hacker News. Launch comparison content (Weave vs. status quo measurement). Audit website for engineering-buyer trust signals.
  • Month 2: Weekly product launch rhythm with changelog + demo video. 2 customer case studies (Rho, 11x style). Start AEO program so Weave surfaces in ChatGPT engineering queries.
  • Month 3: Flagship long-form (research-grounded report on engineering measurement). Hand off playbooks. Standing weekly rhythm with Product + Eng.
