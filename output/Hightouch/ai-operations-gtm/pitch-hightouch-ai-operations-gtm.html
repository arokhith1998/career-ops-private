Slide 03 | SwipeHire - The Lead Example

An AI product shipped as a GTM motion.

SwipeHire matches job-seekers to roles via an LLM-scored swipe-card UI, auto-tailors a CV to the JD on apply, and is visa-aware out of the box. Built solo on Python + OpenAI API + React. Below: a working screen, the eight layers shipped, and the instrumentation that made the A/B tests honest.

The eight layers, shipped solo

01
ICP defined. Early-career + international students, visa-aware. Sized via 12 user interviews before any code.
02
Acquisition. SEO + referral + paid social, channel-tested at low spend before scaling.
03
Onboarding. Resume parser on the OpenAI API, structured-output JSON, skill normalization on a controlled taxonomy.
04
Match. LLM-scored JD-to-resume fit. Deterministic skill overlap as the floor; LLM reasons on top, not in place of, the score.
05
Apply. One-tap apply with a CV auto-tailored to the JD - 8 keyword alignments, JD-specific summary, generated server-side.
06
Instrument. GA4 + GTM + Amplitude wired day one. Custom events on every meaningful step in the funnel.
07
Test. 8 A/B variants run on registration + activation flows. Sample-size + significance gates set up front.
08
Retain. Weekly cohort retention reviewed. Channel-by-cohort split surfaced where attribution lied.

Slide 06 | Week One

Three things I'd ship in week one.

Concrete, not aspirational. Scoped to where I'd start day one given what's public about Hightouch's stack and what the JD lists as nice-to-have.
Ship 1 - Claude Skill
"AE Account Brief" skill
Takes a Salesforce account ID. Pulls open opps, recent CS activity tagged via Hightouch CDP, last earnings call summary or 10-K diff via Glean. Output: a one-pager that lands in Slack 30 minutes before the call.
Why first. AEs already do this manually. A Claude skill cuts the prep from 20 minutes to 3 with better consistency. Fast first ship; clean enablement story to the GTM org.
Ship 2 - Workflow Audit
Clay enrichment hit-rate vs Hightouch CDP
Compare current Clay ICP enrichment hit-rate against composable CDP segments. Find the 20% of accounts where switching enrichment source compounds the loop. Document where each stack wins.
Why first. Dogfooding. RevOps teams trust a story they can tell with their own product more than they trust slideware. This is also the kind of audit the JD's first responsibility line describes.
Ship 3 - Knowledge Resource
"Why We Won" library in Glean
Tag the last 100 closed-won opportunities by competitor displaced, objection-handling pattern that closed, and the deal that was replaced. Load to Glean as a knowledge resource. Wrap in a Claude skill for SDR objection prep.
Why first. Compounding artifact - every new deal feeds it. It's the kind of thing GTM teams nod about but never actually build, because no one owns it. AI Operations does.
