Why this deck exists

Selling an AI voice platform into six industries is not one launch. It is six different risk conversations run at once, each with its own incumbent to replace.

What that actually requires

A narrative that does not describe "AI voice agents" once, but reframes risk differently for a hospital intake line, a law firm's client call and a utility's dispatch desk, without diluting into generic AI-trend language.
Launch and sales enablement built for reps who are selling an unfamiliar category into compliance-heavy buyers, not a developer audience that already trusts AI tooling.
A brand and social motion that builds category trust in parallel with the launches, so the message compounds instead of restarting every quarter.

What I have already run

At Applied Materials today, I automate my own recurring analysis with AI agents built on Claude, the exact enterprise-system-plus-agent pattern this platform needs a buyer to trust.
At Safran, I built personas and positioning across 4 verticals, healthcare among them, for 3 B2B product lines entering markets the client had never sold into directly.
At Sensata, I built the sales enablement that took 50+ reps to a 30% higher win rate on a product category their customers had never bought from that angle before.
The through-line: every launch I have shipped had to earn trust in an unfamiliar category before it ever reached a buyer. That is this role's job across six verticals at once.

First 30 days

What the first month on this platform's positioning and launch calendar looks like.

Week 1
Pressure-test the current narrative, vertical by vertical
Run the same structured-interview discipline behind the 30+ OEM and distributor conversations at Sensata, this time against healthcare, legal, insurance, utilities and logistics buyers, to find where "AI voice agent" triggers a trust objection before launch, not after.
Why first: the same first move behind the Safran persona work, find the real objection before writing a story around it.
Week 2-3
Rebuild positioning that names what it replaces, per segment
Write the version of the message that names the old world directly, the manual intake line, the paralegal call screen, the dispatcher's phone bank, so each vertical hears what it is losing and gaining, not a single generic AI pitch stretched six ways.
Why now: the same replace-the-incumbent framing that shaped the Safran market-entry work and the Sensata launch positioning.
Week 4
Ship enablement and a brand calendar sellers can run without me
Build the decks, case studies and training that took 50+ reps to a 30% higher win rate at Sensata, scoped to each vertical, then set up a social and brand campaign cadence tied to launches, with the first recurring competitive read automated on a Claude agent.
Why this fast: the same AI-agent automation habit running my own reporting at Applied Materials today, applied to keeping the field current.
