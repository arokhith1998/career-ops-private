Slide 05 | First 60 Days

Three ABM ramp experiments I'd run in the first sixty days.

Concrete and scoped. Each is sized to ship inside two months and produces a measurable engagement-to-pipeline outcome.
Experiment 1 - 1:1 Pilot
Top-10 global retail accounts, 1:1 motion
Pick 10 named global retail brands not yet on YOOBIC's customer list. Build bespoke account plans: buying-committee map (Ops + IT + L&D + frontline ops), persona-based messaging, account-level paid + LinkedIn ABM + outbound sequence + custom landing page. Hand the engagement signal to AEs through Salesforce alerts.
Why first. The 1:1 motion is the fastest test of whether the new account-level stack actually produces pipeline. Pixis Tier-1 playbook ($72-90K clients, 1:1) maps directly.
Experiment 2 - 1:few Tier-2 Audit
Top-50 hospitality accounts, 1:few
Cluster 50 hospitality brands by category (QSR, hotels, casual dining, fast-casual). One messaging spine per cluster, shared creative pods, audience layers by title + region. Biweekly engagement review on engagement-to-pipeline conversion.
Why first. 1:few is where most ABM programs leak. Standardized per-tier playbook + structured review (Pixis Tier-2 analog) cuts the leak. Quick wins in 30-45 days.
Experiment 3 - Builder Credit
An AI-driven intent-signal triage workflow
Cursor + Claude pipeline that ingests intent + engagement signal data, surfaces the top 5 accounts/week with the strongest buying signal, and drafts a 1-paragraph BDR brief per account (recent triggers, buying-committee map, suggested first message). Reviewer-in-the-loop, not auto-send.
Why first. The builder credit. SwipeHire's instrumentation discipline transfers to intent triage. This is where the JD's "test-and-learn builder" mandate becomes a working tool.
