Why this deck exists

The hard part is not more instrumentation. It is turning it into a roadmap call before the opportunity goes cold.

What a product-analytics company runs into on its own roadmap

Everyone can see the usage data, but a dashboard alone does not decide which of a hundred competing feature requests to build next.
Without a backlog anchored to adoption and retention cohorts, roadmap debates default to whoever argues loudest, not the clearest signal in the funnel.
The fix is not another metric. It is a PM who turns cohort behavior and customer conversations into a backlog engineering can actually execute against.

What I have already run, at smaller scale

At Sensata, I owned the roadmap and a 100+ feature backlog for a connected-device line, instrumenting SQL and GA4 cohort tracking across 50K+ devices to find which features actually moved adoption.
I paired that with 30+ customer interviews so the backlog reflected what OEMs and distributors said, not just what the usage logs implied.
I run the same loop solo on SwipeHire: wrote the PRD, scoped the MVP, then instrumented my own GA4 and GTM funnel to find the activation drop-off instead of asking someone else to pull the report.
Roadmap ownership, product discovery and data-driven prioritization are the same loop: watch the cohort, talk to the user, ship the backlog item that moves both.

First 30 days

What the first month on Mixpanel's product team looks like.

Week 1
Read the cohort before the backlog
Pull the adoption and retention cohorts for the product surface I own, and sit with Engineering, Design and Support to hear where the current backlog already disagrees with what the data shows.
Why first: the same move that started the Sensata roadmap rewrite: let the usage data name the priority before anyone else does.
Week 2-3
Run discovery, not a survey
Talk directly to 10-15 customers across the segments the cohort data flagged, then map every insight to a specific backlog item instead of a research summary nobody reopens.
Why now: the same pairing of interviews and cohort data that lifted CSAT 22% and reshaped the Sensata roadmap.
Week 4
Ship the prioritized backlog
Turn the cohort findings and customer conversations into a ranked backlog with Engineering, each item defended by a signal, and agree on the first PRD to write together.
Why this fast: a backlog matters less if it stays a wish list. Shipping the ranking is what made the 100+ item backlog at Sensata move 25% faster.
