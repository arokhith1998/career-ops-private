Why this deck exists

Deal desk for AMER sales verticals is three jobs held by one seat: risk review, concession strategy, and cross-functional governance.

What losing that discipline runs into

A deal desk without deal-by-deal risk review does not fail loudly. It fails as a concession granted without a competitive read behind it, a contractual term nobody flagged for Legal, and a margin number that looked fine in the deal room and did not survive the quarter.
Sales, Finance, Legal and Operations all touch the same complex transaction on its way to close, so a gap in risk mitigation or concession discipline becomes everyone's problem at renewal, not just one team's.
The fix is not another approval gate. It is someone who treats deal review, concession strategy and cross-functional governance as one connected loop instead of three separate jobs.

What I have already run

At PriceKeel, I built the discount guardrail engine and the Finance-auditable decision trail myself, the same final-approval governance this seat owns for Salesforce's own deals.
At Sensata, I reviewed deals for risk one by one inside the Bleeders and Leakers program, then restructured concession terms that moved distributor margins from 53% to 61%.
Both mean I have sat on the deal-review side and the governance-design side of the same gate, not just one or the other.
A Deal Desk Manager who reviews complex deals on behalf of Strategic Business Practices, guides Sales on deal structure, develops concession strategies that optimize revenue and protect margins, and owns the final approval decision is describing this exact loop: review the risk, structure the concession, keep Finance and Legal in the record.
