Why this deck exists

A founding RevOps hire does not lack a job description. It lacks someone who has already run the function solo and can show the receipts.

What a founding RevOps seat runs into

The scope is operating cadence, lead-to-pipeline, deal desk and quote-to-cash, and a GTM data model, all at once, with no team yet to hand pieces off to.
Most candidates have owned one or two of those lanes inside an existing function. Almost none have stood up all of them from a blank page, alone, and still closed the loop on reporting.
The AI-native part raises the bar further: building agentic workflows in a repo instead of configuring another SaaS tool is a different skill than running one.

What I have already run, at comparable scale

At PriceKeel, I set up the CRM, CPQ, forecast and pipeline reviews, and deal approvals myself, with an audit trail Finance can review, built in code and versioned in a repo.
At Sensata, I ran deal desk and quote-to-cash issues end to end: discounting analysis on 1,200 opportunities against a $627M target, recovering $700K+ in two quarters.
At Applied Materials now, I automate repetitive revenue analysis with AI agents built on Claude, the exact habit this role asks for across enrichment, hygiene and reporting.
This req asks for operating cadence, lead-to-pipeline, deal desk and quote-to-cash, a GTM data model, and AI brought into the core of ops. That is the founding loop above, run twice already on two different books of business.

First 30 days

What the first month building Wispr Flow's Revenue Operations function looks like.

Week 1
Map the lead-to-pipeline flow
Sit with the VP of Revenue Operations, Sales and CS to learn how a lead currently moves from qualification and routing to a rep, and where the SLA chain already breaks.
Why first: the same move that started PriceKeel's CRM and CPQ setup, map what exists before building anything new.
Week 2-3
Stand up the deal desk
Build deal structure, pricing and discount approvals against real deal data, pressure-tested with Sales on 1 to 2 live quote-to-cash questions.
Why now: building it against a real deal, not in a vacuum, is what made the 53% to 61% distributor gain at Sensata hold.
Week 4
Ship the first agentic workflow
Automate one manual-load task, enrichment or account research, in code and versioned in a repo, then report conversion and SLA numbers in Hex and Salesforce.
Why this fast: the same habit that keeps Claude agents running recurring revenue analysis at Applied Materials today.
