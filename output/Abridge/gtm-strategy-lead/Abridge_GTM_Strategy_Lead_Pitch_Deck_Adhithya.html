Why this deck exists

The hard part of this req is not pricing a product. It is pricing one with no reference point to borrow from, then making the call stick.

What a commercial strategy function runs into

A new offer has no existing pricing unit, no comparable deal and no benchmark, so the default is to anchor to the nearest adjacent product even when it does not fit the buyer or the value.
The commercial thesis, the economics and the field-ready offer all have to come from the same first draft, written by the person who did the analysis, not handed off halfway.
The fix is not another pricing study. It is someone who builds the model from research, takes a position with the evidence available, and hands the business a rule it can apply without reinterpreting it.

What I have already run, hands on

At PriceKeel, I built a guardrail engine that recommends an explainable discount guardrail learned from each company's own closed-won and closed-lost deals, because no outside benchmark existed to borrow from.
At Sensata, I set the price book and discount floors that Sales and Deal Desk then applied, and changed that structure once the evidence said the old one was wrong, raising distributor margins 53% to 61%.
At Sensata again, I priced a new sensing line with no precedent in that segment from 30+ buyer interviews, then owned the GTM that carried it to $2M+ in first-year revenue.
Define the offer, build the economics from research instead of a comparable, and hand the field a package a buyer can understand: this is the exact loop I already run, on both sides of a deal.
