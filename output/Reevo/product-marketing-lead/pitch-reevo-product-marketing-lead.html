Why this deck exists

Two products, one thesis: the revenue stack should remember what actually closed.

What Reevo is building

An AI-native revenue operating system that replaces the static CRM. It generates its own clean first-party activity data instead of waiting for reps to log it.
It guides reps to the next best action rather than storing a record after the fact.
It unifies marketing, sales, and CS on one surface so the handoffs stop losing context.

What I started building in May

PriceKeel, a decision-integrity layer for B2B SaaS deal pricing. It reads a live discount exception inside the deal platform.
It recommends an explainable guardrail learned from that company's own closed-won and closed-lost history.
It records the human decision with evidence Finance can audit. A margin layer is the next build.
The overlap is not the feature set. It is the conviction that a revenue system earns trust by learning from real outcomes and showing its work at the moment a human has to decide. That is also the hardest thing to write copy about, and it is what this role owns.

First 30 days

What the first month looks like.

Week 1
Sit in the deals
Live calls with AEs. Read every closed-won and closed-lost note from the last 90 days. Audit what exists today against what the JD names: pitch, demo narrative, battlecards, website product story. Write down the exact words customers use for the problem, not the words we use.
Why first: A company that raised $80M and is scaling fast has narrative drift between the website, the deck, and what reps actually say. Find the gap before writing a word.
Week 2-3
Sharpen the wedge
Run 6 structured customer interviews across the core ICP. Turn them into two competing positioning hypotheses for the AI-native-versus-static-CRM wedge, then pressure-test both against Sales and Product. Ship one rewrite of the highest-traffic product page with conversion instrumented.
Why now: This is the same loop that produced +30% win rates at Sensata. Interviews into positioning into a measurable surface, not a deck that sits in Notion.
Week 4
Ship the system
Deliver a battlecard against the top-named competitor, one demo narrative rebuilt around the next-best-action moment, and a launch playbook template that holds up when the team doubles. Report what moved and what did not to leadership.
Why this fast: The JD asks for repeatable systems and AI-assisted throughput. Best way to prove both is to have shipped a reusable one inside a month.
