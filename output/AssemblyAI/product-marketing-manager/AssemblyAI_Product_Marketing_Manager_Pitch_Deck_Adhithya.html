Why this deck exists

The hard part of this seat is not writing one good launch. It is running a launch, enablement update and competitive response every few weeks without the story getting thin.

What a quarterly shipping cadence runs into

New models and capabilities land fast enough that a launch plan written for the last release is already stale by the next one, so the playbook has to be reusable, not bespoke each time.
Buyers split across developers building voice agents, clinical teams running medical scribes, and enterprises buying customer-call intelligence, so one positioning line rarely covers all three without getting vague.
A small, flat marketing team means the PMM writes the copy and ships the page, not just the brief, or the launch slips while someone else gets to it.
