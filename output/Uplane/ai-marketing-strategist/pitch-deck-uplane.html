The Pixis deep dive (closest analog to Uplane)

Pixis is the platform Uplane is replacing - and I have spent 14 months selling its outputs to clients. I know where these engines win, and I know where they quietly break.

What I deployed

  • Pixis Codex: reinforcement-learning cross-channel bid optimization
  • Creative AI: generative variant testing layered on Meta Advantage+ and Google Smart Bidding (tROAS, Max Conversion Value)
  • Audience modeling: pLTV-weighted budget pacing across paid social, search, programmatic
  • iOS mobile UA: Appsflyer + Adjust + SKAN post-IDFA attribution for consumer-app clients

Where AI ad engines quietly break (and how I handled it)

  • Bid model overfit on small-budget accounts: shifted them to manual bidding + creative-only AI
  • Creative gen losing brand voice: locked taxonomy + human review on hero variants
  • Attribution drift between engine and platform pixel: rebuilt UTM spec and reconciled in SQL weekly

What it produced

  • 3x ROAS in one month for a fashion D2C partner (validated via holdout incrementality)
  • -50% client churn across the portfolio
  • $1.4M annualized portfolio managed solo, 8 clients
  • 2 channels replaced mid-quarter when KPIs slipped, zero account loss

Why this transfers to Uplane on day 1

  • The pitch is the same: AI replaces agency labor across channels
  • The buyer is the same: marketing leader who needs the CFO to sign off
  • The failure modes are the same: model trust, brand drift, attribution gaps
  • I have already lived the 5-day-in-office, fast-moving, agency-replacement ride - just with Pixis on the wrapper
