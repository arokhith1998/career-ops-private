Slide 04 | SwipeHire - The WillBot Analog

I built the AI-agent shape solo, end-to-end.

WillBot is an AI agent for performance marketing. SwipeHire is an AI agent for career matching. Different verticals. Same architecture pattern: deterministic input layer + LLM reasoning + measured user outcome on a tight feedback loop. Here is what I shipped.

The architecture, layer by layer

01
Deterministic input layer. Resume parser on the OpenAI API, structured JSON output, skill normalization on a controlled taxonomy. Same shape as WillBot's media-buy input rules.
02
LLM reasoning layer. LLM-scored JD-to-resume fit on top of deterministic overlap; LLM reasons, doesn't replace, the score. Mirrors WillBot reasoning over channel + audience + creative data.
03
Action layer. One-tap apply with a CV auto-tailored to the JD - 12 skill alignments, JD-specific summary, generated server-side. WillBot's action layer would be the bid + creative variant push.
04
Instrumentation. GA4 + GTM + Amplitude wired day one. Custom events on every step in the funnel. 50 users, 8 A/B tests, +25% activation lift V1 to V2, weekly cohort retention.
05
Eval loop. Weekly review of LLM rationale outputs against measured user behavior. Prompt iteration as a daily ritual, run in Cursor + Claude.

Why this matters for Newform

Most growth-marketing applicants have used AI tools. Few have built and shipped an AI product solo end-to-end. The credibility to talk about WillBot with clients comes from having shipped the shape yourself.

Slide 05 | Week One

Three things I'd ship in the first thirty days at Newform.

Concrete, not aspirational. Scoped to where I'd start day one given the JD's emphasis on agency-side scaling + iOS app focus + AI-agent product DNA.
Ship 1 - Client Account Audit
Diagnose one mobile-app client end-to-end
Take the most complex mobile-app account in the book. Audit channel mix, creative cadence, SKAN configuration, post-IDFA conversion-event windows, and attribution model. Output: a 2-page client memo + a next-sprint plan with three named experiments.
Why first. The fastest way to earn trust on a multi-client agency book is to show up to week-one with a real diagnosis. Pixis Client C/F shape - mid-quarter channel swaps when blended numbers hid the truth - is the muscle I'd bring.
Ship 2 - Lift Study Cadence
A quarterly holdout-incrementality template
Build a shared template so every client account runs at least one holdout-incrementality experiment per quarter. Sample-size and significance gates defined up front; outcomes documented in a client-facing one-pager. Standardize the language so all account leads can deliver it.
Why first. The Pixis beauty-client 3x ROAS win wasn't a creative breakthrough - it was reading the numbers honestly. Productizing that diagnostic across the book is the highest-leverage thing an agency-side growth IC can do.
Ship 3 - WillBot Eval Loop
Internal-team WillBot benchmarking pass
Run WillBot on three sample client accounts (sanitized) and compare its recommendations against what a senior media buyer would actually do. Identify where WillBot wins (speed, recall), where it loses (context, judgment), and what prompts/inputs would close the gap.
Why first. The product-DNA fluency comes from putting WillBot through its paces, not from talking about it. SwipeHire's weekly eval-loop discipline transfers directly.
