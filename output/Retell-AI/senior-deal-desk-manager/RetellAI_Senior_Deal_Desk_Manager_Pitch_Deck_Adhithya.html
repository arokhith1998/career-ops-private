Why this deck exists

Deal desk for a voice-AI platform closing tens of millions of calls a month is three jobs held by one seat: speed on non-standard deals, margin discipline before approval, and a quote-to-cash record Legal and billing can both trust.

What slows a growing pipeline down

A non-standard deal that takes days instead of hours does not just delay one contract. It stacks behind the next deal, and the next, until Sales is waiting on Deal Desk instead of closing.
A discount approved without a margin model run first looks fine in the deal room and shows up as a margin problem the following quarter, once CRM, CPQ, Legal and billing have all already recorded it as final.
The fix is not a slower approval gate. It is a matrix that structures the decision before Sales asks, and a weekly report that shows cycle time, discount levels and win rate before they become a surprise.

What I have already run

At PriceKeel, I built the discount approval matrix and the margin model myself, so a non-standard deal gets structured and approved in hours, not days.
At Sensata, I modeled deal margin before every approval inside the Bleeders and Leakers program, then tracked win rate, discount levels and cycle time weekly across 1,200 opportunities.
Both mean I have built the approval matrix and run it against real deal volume, not just read about one.
Structuring and approving non-standard deals in hours, building the discount approval matrix and pricing guardrails, modeling margin before approval, running quote-to-cash across CRM, CPQ, Legal and billing, and reporting weekly on cycle time, discount levels and win rate: this is the exact loop I already run.
