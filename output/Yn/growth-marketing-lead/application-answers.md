## Tell us about the time you most successfully hacked some (non-computer) system to your advantage

At Sensata, the pricing "system" was really a negotiation habit: reps gave discounts by feel, and margin leaked deal by deal without anyone seeing where. Instead of pushing a blanket price increase, which reps would have fought, I mapped 1,200 open opportunities against a $627M target and sorted them into bleeders (margin erosion) and leakers (price leakage). Then I gave each rep a list built from their own deals, with the competitive gap and the customer's willingness to pay next to each one. Reps stopped arguing with the reprice because the evidence was about their accounts, not a policy memo. We recovered $700K+ in margin in two quarters and took distributor margins from 53% to 61%.
