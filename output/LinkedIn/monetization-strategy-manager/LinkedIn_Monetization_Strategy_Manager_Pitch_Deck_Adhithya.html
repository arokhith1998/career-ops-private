First 30 days

What the first month on LinkedIn's Monetization Strategy team looks like.

Week 1
Map the current Premium pricing hypothesis set
Pull existing willingness-to-pay data on Premium tiers and the new AI-based features, find where the member-value story is strongest and where it is still assumed rather than tested.
Why first: the same first move behind the Sensata dashboard, find where the existing evidence is thin before building a new model.
Week 2-3
Build the analytical model for one packaging question
Pick the highest-ambiguity packaging decision, a Creator or Entrepreneur tier, or an AI-feature bundle, and build the quantitative case with Product, Product Marketing and Strategic Finance.
Why now: the same cross-functional build that produced the Bleeders and Leakers case, not a solo analysis.
Week 4
Stand up a lightweight price-governance checkpoint
Apply the audit-trail discipline from PriceKeel to one pricing workflow: a documented decision record Commerce and Engineering can implement against without relitigating the model each time.
Why this fast: the same governance habit I run for PriceKeel's own pricing decisions today.
