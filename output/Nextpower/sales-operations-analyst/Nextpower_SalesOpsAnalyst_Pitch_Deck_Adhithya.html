Why this deck exists

This posting is really three jobs held by one person: quoting-logic owner, CPQ bridge-builder and new-BU integrator, across every product line Nextpower launches.

What losing that discipline runs into

A quoting tool built for one product does not fail loudly. It fails as a workaround nobody documented, a configuration rule that only works until the next BU shows up, and a CPQ migration that has to be reverse-engineered from someone's memory.
Sales, Product, Finance and IT all touch the same quote, so a gap in quoting logic or documentation becomes everyone's delay at the next launch, not just one team's problem.
The fix is not another spreadsheet. It is someone who treats quoting logic, CPQ bridging and new-BU documentation as one connected discipline instead of three separate jobs.

What I have already run

At PriceKeel, I set up and run the CRM, CPQ configuration and the audit trail myself, the same system-ownership and documentation loop this role holds for new BU integration.
At Sensata, I rebuilt configuration and pricing logic inside the Bleeders and Leakers program and built the Power BI dashboard that made 1,200 opportunities auditable across 20 product lines.
Both mean I have sat on the quoting side and the system-configuration side of the same bridge, not just one or the other.
A Sales Operations Analyst who owns quoting tools, bridges to CPQ platforms and integrates new BUs into standard process is describing this exact loop: build the quoting logic, document it, make it portable into a system of record.
