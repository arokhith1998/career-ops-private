First 30 days

Week 1

Pull Dasher-side pain points already surfacing in support tickets, forums and existing research, and size the market for the strongest candidate the same way PriceKeel's first guardrail engine got sized: from real behavior, not a survey.

Write the business case and design the experiment

Why now: the same build-the-case-first discipline that got PriceKeel into live pilots instead of a deck nobody acted on.
Week 4
Launch the experiment and instrument the read
Ship the first experiment, wire the SQL/Excel tracking that reads results without a manual rebuild each cycle, and bring the first automated version of that report using an AI agent, the same habit running at Applied Materials today.
Why this fast: a business case only holds up once the data behind it updates itself.
