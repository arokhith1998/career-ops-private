First 30 days

What the first month sizing and testing a new Dasher earning opportunity looks like.

Week 1
Size the audience and pick the segment
Pull what Dashers are already saying about the newest earning opportunity (support tickets, forums, UXR and Audience Insights data), size the TAM, and rank the segments worth a pilot's budget first.
Why first: the same TAM-to-segment motion behind the Safran study and the Applied Materials market model.
Week 2-3
Run the primary research and set success criteria
Design and run the surveys, interviews and concept tests that confirm or kill the segment hypothesis, working with Product, Design and Strategy & Ops to set the pilot's success criteria before it launches.
Why now: the same 30+-interview discipline that decided Sensata's positioning before it shipped.
Week 4
Launch the pilot and instrument the scale decision
Ship the small-scale pilot across in-app, owned and earned channels, wire the SQL/GA4 read that tells scale from iterate from stop, and bring an AI-agent-automated version of that report, the same habit running at Applied Materials today.
Why this fast: a scale decision only holds up once the segment behind it was sized correctly.
