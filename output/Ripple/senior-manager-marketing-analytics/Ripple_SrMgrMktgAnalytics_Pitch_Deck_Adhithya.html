Why this deck exists

A growth marketing engine scaling with AI needs one analytics seat holding spend, pipeline and CRM data to the same, trusted story.

What losing that discipline runs into

Growth marketing analytics without one owner does not fail loudly. It fails as three dashboards that disagree on last quarter's CAC, a CRM field nobody validated before the forecast ran on it, and an executive review that spends its first ten minutes reconciling numbers instead of deciding on budget.
Growth Marketing, Finance and RevOps all touch the same spend-to-pipeline data on its way to a board slide, so a gap in attribution or CRM data quality becomes everyone's problem at the next planning cycle, not just one team's.
The fix is not another dashboard. It is someone who treats measurement, CRM data integrity and AI-powered automation as one connected system instead of three separate tools.

What I have already run

At Sensata, I built a BU-wide Power BI system that tracked 1,200 pricing opportunities against a $627M target and surfaced $433M in margin gaps, the same spend-to-pipeline visualization this role owns for growth marketing.
At PriceKeel, I run the CRM and CPQ myself and built the audit trail Finance checks against, the CRM data-quality habit this role asks for across marketing and sales systems.
Both mean I have sat on the reporting side and the data-integrity side of the same system, not just one or the other.
A Senior Manager, Marketing Analytics who owns measurement across growth marketing, leads pipeline attribution and ROI analysis, and uses AI-powered tools to automate workflows and sharpen forecasting is describing this exact system: one owner, one trusted number, from spend to revenue.
