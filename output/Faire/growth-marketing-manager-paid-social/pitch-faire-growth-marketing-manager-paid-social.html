Slide 03 | The Diagnostic That Mattered

D2C beauty client at Pixis: 3x ROAS via holdout incrementality.

Blended ROAS looked fine. A holdout slice showed two channels were getting credit they had not earned. I cut the weak spend, doubled down on what was actually driving lift, and the brand cleared 3x ROAS the next month. This is the muscle that protects spend on the retailer side of a two-sided marketplace where the "easy" reengagement channels are usually the most over-credited.
Why this matters for Faire

On a marketplace, retailer-reengagement spend is the channel most likely to get over-credited - the buyer would have come back anyway. Holdout incrementality is the only way to see the true repeat-purchase lift.

The discipline that drove the 3x ROAS - cut weak spend, double down on what works - is the same muscle that protects retailer LTV when blended ROAS looks fine but cohort retention is leaking.

Slide 05 | First Thirty Days at Faire

Three experiments I'd run in the first thirty days on the retailer side.

Concrete and scoped. Each ships inside a month and produces a measurable retention outcome - not a CPL one.
Experiment 1 - Holdout on Reengagement
Retailer-cohort holdout slice on the largest reengagement audience
Pick the biggest retailer-reengagement audience currently running on Meta. Carve a matched holdout cell. Measure incremental repeat-purchase lift over 3 weeks against the exposed cell. Cut over-credited spend, reallocate to true lift.
Why first. On a marketplace, reengagement spend is the channel most likely to get over-credited - the retailer would have come back anyway. This is the Pixis 3x ROAS muscle applied to the retailer side.
Experiment 2 - AI Pilots in the Paid Program
Three AI/automation pilots inside the existing workflow
(a) Creative-variant generation pipeline on the Claude API for retailer-segment-specific copy; (b) Hunch-style dynamic catalog templating for category-level retargeting variants; (c) Automated significance gating on every A/B test so the team stops calling winners early.
Why first. Direct fit to the JD's "leverage AI and automation tools" criterion. I trained 8 enterprise marketers on the Pixis AI bidding + creative platform - same product category as Smartly + Hunch + Omneky. Plus 4 Claude certs + 2 shipped AI products solo.
Experiment 3 - Retention Dashboard
One-page retailer-cohort retention dashboard in Power BI
Build a weekly retailer-cohort retention dashboard: CPM, CTR, CVR, ROAS sliced by retailer cohort + retention curve by months-since-acquisition. Surface the cohorts where reengagement is leaking and the ones where it is compounding. Become the artifact the team opens every Monday.
Why first. Same shape as the $627M Bleeders & Leakers Power BI dashboard at Sensata (1,200 opportunities, 3 regions, 4 product families) - executive-facing artifact built for decision-making, not for show.
