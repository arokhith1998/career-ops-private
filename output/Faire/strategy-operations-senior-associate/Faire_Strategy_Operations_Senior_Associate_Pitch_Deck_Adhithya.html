First 30 days

What the first month on Faire's Strategy & Operations team looks like.

Week 1
Map the advertising GTM metric set
Pull the existing performance data on Faire's advertising line, find where the growth-lever hypothesis is already supported and where it is still assumed.

Week 2-3
Build the model for one growth hypothesis

Present the recommendation to leadership
Apply the executive-presentation discipline from the Sensata and Safran work to Faire's GTM teams, with a documented process so the next cycle builds on it instead of restarting.
