Why this deck exists

This posting is really three jobs held by one person: deal support, DealHub ownership and pricing governance, across three product lines at once.

What losing that discipline runs into

A deal desk without deal-by-deal review does not fail loudly. It fails as a quote that got approved on last quarter's assumptions, a CPQ workaround nobody documented, and a dashboard where nobody can say why a segment's realization slipped.
Sales, Deal Desk and Finance all touch the same quote, so a gap in system ownership or data hygiene becomes everyone's guessing game at the next approval, not just one team's problem.
The fix is not another spreadsheet. It is someone who treats quotation, system configuration and governance as one connected discipline instead of three separate jobs.

What I have already run

At PriceKeel, I set up and run the CRM, CPQ configuration and the audit trail myself, the same system-ownership and governance loop this role holds for DealHub.
At Sensata, I owned deal-by-deal quotation review inside the Bleeders and Leakers program and built the Power BI dashboard that made 1,200 pricing opportunities auditable by segment.

A Senior Pricing Analyst who owns deal support, is the DealHub expert, builds dynamic pricing models and assists pricing governance is describing this exact loop: review the deal, configure the system, keep the governance trail honest.
