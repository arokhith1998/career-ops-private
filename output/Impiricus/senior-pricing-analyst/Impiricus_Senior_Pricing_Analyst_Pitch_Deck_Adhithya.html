Why this deck exists

A pricing analyst role at a multi-product platform does not lack candidates who know pricing theory. It lacks someone who has already owned quote review, CPQ system administration and cross-product dashboards as one job, not three.

A quote only protects margin if it is checked against the prior deal's terms first, not approved in isolation under deadline pressure.
A CPQ platform like DealHub only stays useful if someone owns the setup, trains the sales team on it, and keeps the underlying data clean, not a one-time configuration.
Pricing across several product lines at once only works if the KPI dashboard rolls up consistently, or leadership cannot compare one line against another.
