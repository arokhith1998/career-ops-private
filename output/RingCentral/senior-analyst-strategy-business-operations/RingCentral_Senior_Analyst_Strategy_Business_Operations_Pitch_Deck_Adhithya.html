Why this deck exists

The risk in a financial and GTM analytics suite is not the first build. It is who keeps rebuilding it every cycle without being asked twice.

What a transformation program's reporting layer runs into

Bookings, pipeline, retention and cost views get built once for one executive readout, then the next cycle is a manual refresh because nobody owns the pipeline that feeds them.
Finance, RevOps and Sales each keep their own version of a metric definition, so a baseline set in month one quietly drifts by month six, right when a transformation program needs it most.
The fix is not another one-off dashboard. It is one analyst who owns the build, automates the refresh, and documents the metric so Finance does not have to re-derive it every quarter.

What I have already run, hands on

At Sensata, I built the FY2026 Bleeders and Leakers Power BI dashboard myself: 1,200 opportunities against a $627M target, with its own pipeline funnel, win-rate and stage-distribution views that leadership could trust cycle over cycle.
At Applied Materials, I automate the recurring part of that analysis with AI agents built on Claude, so a refresh is a review, not a rebuild.
I carry P&L and revenue targets at every stop, so a baseline is never set in isolation from the number it is supposed to move.
Build and maintain live financial and GTM analytics, automate recurring reporting cycles, and support transformation programs with baselines and target tracking: this is describing the exact loop I already run.
