01
Why this deck exists

Building Agentic Finance from zero to one is not a roadmap exercise. It is a trust problem wearing a product-strategy costume.

What that actually requires

Inventing a new product and user experience with no existing playbook, then earning product-market fit with real founders who will drop it the moment it wastes their time instead of saving it.
An agent that touches payments, payroll, bookkeeping or tax has to be explainable enough that a founder trusts the output without re-checking it by hand, or the "eliminate the busywork" pitch just becomes more busywork.
Choosing the right AI approach per problem, prompting, fine-tuning, or a fully agentic workflow, instead of defaulting to whichever is trending, while working across engineering, design, commercial and the customer to ship it.

What I have already built at that trust layer

Founded PriceKeel to solve exactly this for pricing decisions: an explainable discount guardrail engine and the audit trail Finance can check, so a human decision stays fast without Finance losing the ability to verify it.
Shipped SwipeHire, an AI job-matching platform, zero-to-one on the OpenAI API, from PRD to 50+ onboarded users, owning the product surface and the AI tooling underneath it myself.
Automate the repetitive parts of pricing and market analysis today with AI agents built on Claude at Applied Materials, so I already default to matching the AI technique to the problem rather than the reverse.
The through-line: I do not just want to use AI. I have already built the part that makes people trust what it decides, which is the actual hard problem inside "Agentic Finance."

First 30 days

What the first month building inside T:0 would look like.

Week 1
Sit with the problem, not the roadmap
Talk to founders and the customers this product is for, plus engineering and design, to find exactly where finance busywork hurts today, the same move that grounded the Sensata backlog before any PRD.
Why first: a zero-to-one roadmap built on assumptions instead of real conversations is the fastest way to miss PMF.
Week 2-3
Ship a narrow agentic slice, then measure trust
Build the smallest version of the workflow and instrument it the way SwipeHire's analytics stack was instrumented, to learn whether founders trust the output enough to stop double-checking it by hand.
Why now: usage without trust is a false PMF signal for a product handling someone's finances.
Week 4
Turn the first proof point into a roadmap
Use what shipped and what founders actually trusted to write the roadmap and delivery plan for what comes next, the same handoff that took Sensata's backlog from one region's pilot to a 3-region rollout.
Why this fast: a zero-to-one product only earns its next investment by showing the path to scale.
