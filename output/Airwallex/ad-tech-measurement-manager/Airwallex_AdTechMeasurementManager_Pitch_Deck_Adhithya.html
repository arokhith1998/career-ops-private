Why this deck exists

Upper-funnel media is easy to run and hard to trust. The trust comes from the plumbing between the ad platform and the CRM, not the platform.

What that actually requires

A measurement stack (tagging, pixels, conversion tracking) that is instrumented correctly and gets troubleshot the moment data quality breaks, not after the budget review flags it.
A real bridge from media signals to CRM lifecycle stage and offline conversions, so Marketing Operations and RevOps are reading the same number as the media team.
Lift tests, geo experiments and holdouts run often enough to produce leading indicators of lead quality, not a single incrementality study filed away once a year.

What I have already built at that layer

Automated the Salesforce, Adobe Analytics, GA and Google Tag Manager stack at Plug Power for closed-loop reporting, troubleshooting tracking issues and tying media signals to CRM lifecycle stage.
Designed 15+ A/B tests with audience and geo holdouts across 50+ inbound campaigns, building the leading indicators of lead quality that told SEM and social apart from MQL noise.
Ran programmatic, paid social and retail media (Amazon Ads, Criteo) for 8 client accounts at Pixis, including iOS paid UA attribution through Appsflyer and Adjust.
The through-line: I treat the measurement stack as the product, not the campaign dashboard sitting on top of it. That is what makes the CRM-lifecycle number trustworthy.
