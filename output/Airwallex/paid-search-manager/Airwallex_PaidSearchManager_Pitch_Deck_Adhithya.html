Why this deck exists

A global SEM strategy is not one keyword list in ten languages. It is a market-by-market rebuild wearing one budget line.

What that actually requires

Rebuilding keyword sets and bid strategy per market instead of translating one list, because search behavior does not carry over word for word.
Running A/B tests on ad copy, landing pages and bidding approaches in each market, then rolling the wins back into the global strategy instead of letting every market drift on its own.
Keeping SEM budgets and ROI reporting legible to leadership across all of it, so a global program does not turn into ten local ones nobody can compare.

What I have already built at that layer

Localized paid search and PPC for 8 client accounts including non-English markets at Pixis, rebuilding keyword sets and bid strategy per market rather than translating one list.
Ran 15+ A/B tests on ad copy, landing pages and bidding across 50+ campaigns at Plug Power, closing the keyword-strategy gaps those tests surfaced.
Built the pacing plans, financial forecasts and leadership reporting behind $100K/month in SEM budgets, keeping spend accountable to CPA and pipeline, not platform dashboards.
The through-line: I do not run one SEM playbook and hope it travels. I rebuild it market by market, test it, and still keep one number leadership can read.
