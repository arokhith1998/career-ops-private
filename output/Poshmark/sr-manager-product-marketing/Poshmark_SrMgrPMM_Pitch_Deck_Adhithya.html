First 30 days

What the first month owning GTM and lifecycle marketing at Poshmark looks like.

Week 1
Map the launch-to-lifecycle handoff
Trace the last 5-10 feature launches to find where the lifecycle program picked up, or didn't, after ship day, and where onboarding, engagement and retention are being run by different teams off different signals.
Why first: the same first move behind the Sensata KPI work, find where the signal breaks before rebuilding the program.
Week 2-3
Rebuild one lifecycle journey end to end
Pick the highest-friction moment, onboarding to first sale, or a churn-risk cohort, and rebuild the messaging, channel mix and positioning together with Product, CRM, Growth and Creative.
Why now: the same cross-functional build that moved Sensata's win rate 30% instead of shipping a deck no one used.
Week 4
Stand up the recurring customer-insight loop
Institute the direct-contact discipline behind 30+ structured interviews at Sensata, applied to Poshmark's seller and buyer community, then automate the reporting step with the first AI agent so the insight ships every cycle, not once.
Why this fast: the same habit that keeps my own reporting moving at Applied Materials today, not a plan for someday.
