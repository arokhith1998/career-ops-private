The trust-and-accuracy problem, in depth

An automated workflow is only as good as the trust it earns. That is a product-design problem, not a model-quality afterthought.

The brief treats trust and accuracy as a constraint on top of automation. I would treat them as the same design decision: what a workflow is allowed to automate should be set by how confidently it can explain itself to the property team or resident on the other end.
