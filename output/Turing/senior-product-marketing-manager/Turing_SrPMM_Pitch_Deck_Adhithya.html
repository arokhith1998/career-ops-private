Why this deck exists

The role is not "write the positioning." It is turning a highly technical product line into a narrative a frontier AI lab's own researchers will trust.

What that actually requires

An ML engineer does not buy a feature list. Positioning for datasets, benchmarks and RL environments has to survive a technical reviewer who already knows the domain cold, not just a procurement conversation.
Customer research and competitive analysis with a technical buyer only work when the marketer can hold a real conversation about the product, not translate secondhand from a spec sheet.
Sales enablement and technical content need to be produced fast, in an environment where the underlying product itself moves every few weeks.

What I have already run

At Sensata, I turned deeply technical specs for new sensing products into a GTM and positioning story that OEM and distributor engineers acted on, driving $2M+ in first-year revenue.
At Sensata again, I built the sales enablement, presentations and case studies that carried 50+ reps through live technical deals, and win rates moved 30%.
At Applied Materials today, I automate my own recurring market-sizing and forecasting work with AI agents built on Claude, the same AI fluency a Frontier AI product line runs on.
The through-line: every narrative I have shipped had to survive contact with an engineer who knew more about the product than I did on day one, and I have already made AI part of how that content gets produced, not a slide about AI.

First 30 days

What the first month owning positioning for Turing's Frontier AI business looks like.

Week 1
Sit inside the technical buyer conversation
Pull the last 10-15 deals where a frontier AI lab evaluated the datasets, benchmarks or RL environments, and map where the current positioning does and does not survive a technical reviewer's questions.
Why first: the same first move behind the Sensata GTM work, listening to where the message breaks before rewriting it.
Week 2-3
Rebuild one product's narrative with the technical team
Pick the dataset, benchmark or RL environment where technical depth and buyer-facing narrative sit furthest apart, and rebuild its positioning and sales enablement together with the researchers and engineers who built it.
Why now: the same cross-functional build that moved Sensata's win rate 30% instead of shipping a deck no one used.
Week 4
Stand up the AI-assisted content loop
Build the first agent or automation that removes a manual step from turning competitive research or customer interviews into technical content, and run it against a live piece of collateral so the win is measured.
Why this fast: the same habit that keeps my own reporting moving at Applied Materials today, not a plan for someday.
