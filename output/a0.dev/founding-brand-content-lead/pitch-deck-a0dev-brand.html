The SwipeHire-to-a0.dev playbook

SwipeHire is the closest analog: AI-product, mobile-friendly, indie-builder audience. I have done it for myself. I would do it for a0.dev.

What I shipped at SwipeHire

  • Voice and tone system: direct, builder-friendly, no AI-hype cliches. Mirrors how I actually talk on LinkedIn.
  • 5+ landing page variants by ICP: early-career, mid-level, transitioning - each had a different hero, CTA, and proof point.
  • Founder-led content (12+ posts): LinkedIn + X. Every post tied to a specific user story or product release. Engagement-per-post was 3x my long-form output.
  • Referral loop: drove 60% of net-new signups. Built it in the same week as the MVP.
  • Weekly product launches: launch post + demo + changelog every Friday.
"a0.dev needs someone who has actually shipped an AI product and marketed it. That is what I would bring to day one."

What I would ship at a0.dev

  • Brand voice and style guide for the AI-builder audience (developer-tone, not corporate-AI)
  • Customer story factory: 1 case study per week, anchored to a single hero metric (time-to-app, MVP-to-launch days, etc.)
  • Founder content rhythm: 5 posts per week across LinkedIn + X + YC channels + Hacker News + Reddit r/programming and r/startups
  • Comparison + AEO content: so a0.dev surfaces when builders ask ChatGPT or Claude "how do I build a mobile app with AI"
  • Demo video factory: shipped weekly with each product update. Cursor / Lovable / v0 model - high velocity, low production overhead
