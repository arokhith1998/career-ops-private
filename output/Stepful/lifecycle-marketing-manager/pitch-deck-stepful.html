The lifecycle deep dive

Lifecycle is "what got the customer stuck and what unsticks them." Stepful's stuck-points are healthcare students dropping off; mine were D2C buyers and B2B leads. Same shape.

What I have shipped on lifecycle

  • GenY D2C consumer brand: Klaviyo + Shopify cart abandonment, post-purchase, re-engagement, win-back. 2x lift email-attributed revenue per send. Coupled with paid acquisition to lift LTV-per-cohort across seasonal SKU drops.
  • Plug Power B2B: Full architecture rebuild on HubSpot + Salesforce + Adobe + GA4 + GTM. Persona-tied nurture sequences. Lifted MQL to SQL conversion across priority product lines.
  • SwipeHire (founded solo): Activation lifecycle on Python + OpenAI + React. 8+ A/B tests on onboarding and matching flows, +25% activation. Designed the "what unsticks them" moments by reading the data.
  • Sensata research: 30+ structured customer interviews; insights drove roadmap and lifecycle program design.
"I think about lifecycle as product UX. The email is the surface. The behavior change is the goal."

How I would ramp at Stepful

  • Week 1-2: Audit current lifecycle flows by stage (enrolled, progressing, stuck, near-complete, post-grad). Pull retention curves and dropout cohorts. Sit with Student Success on the 3 highest-friction moments.
  • Week 3-4: Ship 2 behavior-triggered nudges for the top dropout moments. Stand up A/B test framework. Map persona segments tighter.
  • Month 2: Build cohort dashboards tying lifecycle touches to completion + placement outcomes. Document the "stuck-point library" so program design becomes systematic.
  • Month 3: Standing weekly rhythm with Product + Student Success. Own the completion rate KPI. Hand off playbook for next hire.
