Four ask-notes calibrated by relationship. Each uses standard US-keyboard chars (no em-dash, no smart quotes). Open LinkedIn, find the person, click their profile, click More -> Request a recommendation. Drop the text in the message field. Edit the name placeholders before sending.

## How to send these

1. Open the person's LinkedIn profile.
2. Click "More" -> "Request a recommendation".
3. In step 1, pick the right relationship ("Manager", "Student", "Worked with you at the same company", "Client").
4. In step 2, pick the position you held when you worked together.
5. Paste the text above into the personal message field. Edit the name and any role-specific detail.
6. Send.

LinkedIn will email them. Recommendations land in your "Pending" inbox; you review and approve each one before it shows on your profile.

## Cadence tip

Send all 4 in the same week. Recommendations land asynchronously over the next 1-3 weeks. Even if only 2 of 4 land, that already doubles the trust signal on your profile for any big-co recruiter scanning it.
