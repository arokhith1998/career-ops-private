Why this deck exists

GTM Strategy and Operations at a fast-growing AI company is five jobs held by one seat: forecast owner, territory and quota designer, cadence builder, and Salesforce stack owner.

What a gap here runs into

A segment without a defensible forecast does not fail loudly. It fails as a number sales leadership stops trusting, a territory map nobody revisited after the last hire, and a QBR built on a dashboard that lagged the pipeline by two weeks.
Sales, Finance and Recruiting all plan off the same segment number, so a gap in forecast discipline or quota design becomes everyone's guessing game at the next headcount review, not just one team's problem.
The fix is not another dashboard. It is someone who treats forecast, territory, quota and cadence as one connected system instead of four separate asks.
