Why this deck exists

The problem is not deal volume. It is deals that stall waiting on the next sign-off.

What a fast-scaling Enterprise pipeline runs into

Land, Expansion and Framework deals move through pricing, ROI modeling and EPD staffing inputs before Accounting, Finance, EPD and Legal even see the package, and every non-standard deal adds a new place for it to sit unreviewed.
Without one view of deal status, pricing and margin across the pipeline, Sales and Deal Desk leadership find out a deal stalled after it already cost a quarter.
The fix is not another approval step. It is a dashboard that shows the bottleneck early, and a routing habit that keeps deal packages moving instead of waiting.

What I have already run, at smaller scale

At Sensata, I built the Bleeders and Leakers Power BI dashboard: 1,200 pricing opportunities against a $627M target, with deal status, pricing and margin visible by region and product line.
I did not stop at visibility. I redesigned the deal structures underneath the worst gaps, which is why the 53% to 61% distributor margin lift held past the first quarter.
I am now building PriceKeel, an audit trail and guardrail engine for discount exceptions, because a deal desk needs a reason Finance can point to, not just a report.
Deal Pricing Execution, Pricing and Deal Status Reporting, and Process Optimization are describing this exact loop: see the bottleneck, fix the deal structure behind it, make the fix explainable enough that the next deal does not hit the same wall.

First 30 days

What the first month on Scale's Enterprise deal desk looks like.

Week 1
Map the bottleneck
Pull the last 90 days of Land, Expansion and Framework deals and trace where each one sat longest, whether that is ROI modeling, EPD staffing sign-off, or Legal review. Sit with the Deal Desk Manager and Sales to hear where the current process creates friction.
Why first: the bottleneck is usually visible in the data before anyone names it. Same move that started the Bleeders and Leakers program.
Week 2-3
Ship the dashboard view
Build the pricing dashboard tracking deal status, pricing, margin and deal health across the Enterprise pipeline, and pressure-test it against the top 3 stalled deals with Sales, Finance and EPD before it goes live.
Why now: ranking against a real target, then verifying with the cross-functional group, is what made the 53% to 61% distributor gain stick.
Week 4
Standardize the routing
Turn the deal-review lessons into a repeatable workflow: a routing order for Accounting, Finance, EPD and Legal, and a cycle-time view that flags which stage is slowing a deal down before it stalls.
Why this fast: a dashboard matters less if the routing behind it stays ad hoc. Repeatability is the actual ask behind "process optimization."
