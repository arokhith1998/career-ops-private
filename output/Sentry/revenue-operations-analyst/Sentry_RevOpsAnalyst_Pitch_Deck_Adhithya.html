Why this deck exists

The posting names three pillars, Analytics, GTM Technology and Deal Desk, and says half the job is deal desk ownership.

What losing that discipline runs into

A Deal Desk without a real gatekeeper does not fail loudly. It fails as a quote that ships with the wrong discount, an order form that does not match what Sales promised, and an approval that sits in an inbox while a renewal deadline passes.
Analytics, GTM Technology and Deal Desk all touch the same deal record, so a gap in one pillar becomes noise in the other two: bad approval routing shows up as bad pipeline data, and bad pipeline data shows up as a wrong insight in the next report.
The fix is not another approval step. It is someone who has already run quote creation, discount review and approval routing as one system, not three separate queues.

What I have already run, at smaller scale

At PriceKeel, I built the CRM, CPQ configuration and deal-approval workflow myself, including the self-service guardrails that let a standard deal route without escalation, then the audit trail that gives Finance an explainable record behind every exception.
At Sensata, I led the Bleeders and Leakers program: deal-by-deal review against competitive gaps and customer willingness to pay, recovering $700K+ in margin in 2 quarters.
Both mean I have already run the Deal Desk pillar end to end, not just the CPQ ticket queue behind it.
A Revenue Operations Analyst who splits time between deal desk ownership and the broader Analytics and GTM Technology surface area is describing this exact loop: process the deal, keep the system clean, and surface what the data says next.
