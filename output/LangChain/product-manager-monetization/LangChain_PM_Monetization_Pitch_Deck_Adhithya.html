Why this deck exists

Pricing, packaging and billing for LangSmith are one connected product surface, not three separate jobs.

What losing that discipline runs into

A monetization surface without one clear owner does not fail loudly. It fails as a pricing page that undersells usage, an upgrade flow nobody instrumented, and a billing edge case that only shows up once an enterprise contract hits a plan limit.
Product, Engineering, Sales and Finance all touch the same packaging decision, so a gap in funnel instrumentation or entitlement logic becomes everyone's guessing game at renewal, not just one team's problem.
The fix is not another dashboard. It is someone who treats pricing, packaging and billing as one connected discipline instead of three separate jobs.

What I have already run

At PriceKeel, I set up and run the CRM, CPQ configuration and the audit trail myself, the same system-ownership and plan-enforcement loop a billing and entitlements stack needs underneath usage-based pricing.
At Sensata, I owned the Bleeders and Leakers program end to end and built the Power BI dashboard that made 1,200 pricing opportunities auditable by segment, the same funnel-level instrumentation a conversion and expansion analysis needs.
