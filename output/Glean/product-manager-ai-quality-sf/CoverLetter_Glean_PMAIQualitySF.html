Hiring Team, Glean

When I shipped SwipeHire on the OpenAI API, the hard part was never getting the model to answer. It was deciding when the answer was good enough to show a user, and building the review step that caught it when it wasn't. That is the exact judgment call behind the title "AI Quality."

Most of my product reps are on sensing hardware, not language models, but the underlying job is the same. I owned the roadmap and a 100+ feature backlog at Sensata, and I kept it honest by running 30+ interviews with OEM and distributor engineers to find where the product actually broke, not where I assumed it would, lifting CSAT 22% and feature adoption 18% across 50,000+ connected devices. Spending time with customers to find the real gap, then prioritizing against it, travels cleanly from sensors to an LLM-powered knowledge product.

I also build my own Claude AI agents at Applied Materials to automate the repetitive side of forecasting, and I read what they produce before it reaches a P&L number. Automate the work, then build the check on the automation: that is what I would bring to a team that has to decide how good an AI answer has to be before an enterprise customer sees it.
