Why this deck exists

Gold touches nearly every surface in the app. The hard part is not finding a growth idea, it is picking which funnel stage to bet on first and proving the trade-off was right.

What that actually requires

A way to size acquisition, trial-to-paid conversion and retention against each other, not just the one metric nearest to the team's current roadmap.
A high-velocity experimentation loop with data science: a hypothesis, a sized test, a scale-or-kill decision, on a cadence fast enough to matter.
Comfort making the growth-versus-unit-economics call on offers and pricing, then defending it with data when a stakeholder pushes back.

What I have already run

At Pixis, I cut client churn 50% across an 8-client, $15K-$90K/month portfolio through proactive account strategy and vertical playbooks, the retention half of a membership funnel.
At Plug Power, I ran 15+ A/B tests across Google Ads, Microsoft Ads, Meta and LinkedIn that cut CPA 25% and lifted conversion 20%, the acquisition and trial-to-paid half.
At Sensata, I modeled a BU-wide price-increase system that flagged 50%+ margin gaps, the growth-versus-economics trade-off a Gold offer or price point runs.
The through-line: every funnel stage I have owned had a sized hypothesis and a test behind it, never a feature shipped on a hunch.

First 30 days

What the first month owning a piece of the Gold growth funnel looks like.

Week 1
Map the funnel stage by stage and find where value is leaking
Pull discovery, trial-start, trial-to-paid and churn rates by entry point, and flag the one or two stages where the drop-off is largest relative to the opportunity.
Why first: the same first move behind the Pixis churn program and the Sensata pricing model, find the gap before building a test to close it.
Week 2-3
Design and ship the first experiment with data science
Pick the highest-leverage funnel stage, size the opportunity, and design an A/B test with data science covering entry point, paywall or onboarding copy and UI.
Why now: the same hypothesis-to-test loop behind the 15+ A/B tests at Plug Power, run with a partner team, not solo.
Week 4
Read results, make the growth-vs-economics call, and write the plan
Decide whether the test scales or dies, weigh it against offer and pricing economics, and bring engineering, design and finance along with the plan.
Why this fast: the same trade-off discipline that carried the Sensata price-increase model to a 12% YoY revenue gain.
