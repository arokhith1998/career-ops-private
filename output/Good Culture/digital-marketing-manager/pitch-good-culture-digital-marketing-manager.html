Slide 05 | First Thirty Days at Good Culture

Three concrete experiments to ship inside thirty days.

Each ships in a month and produces a measurable brand or cohort outcome - not a vanity metric one.
Week 1-2 - Culture Map
Cottage-cheese-as-protein cultural-moment audit
Map the conversation currently driving the cottage-cheese wave: which subreddits, which TikTok creators, which Twitter loops, which Substack writers. Score each on velocity, reach, and brand-fit. Output a 2-page culture-map + a list of 25 creators and 10 communities Good Culture should be participating in this quarter.
Why first. The wave is breaking now. The DMM has to know where it lives by the end of week 2 to make any other call. Same audit shape as the SwipeHire community work.
Week 2-4 - Creator Pilot
10-creator UGC pilot anchored to a single SKU
Pick the SKU with the strongest current cultural pull. Brief 10 creators (mix of micro + nano on TikTok + Reels) with a single editorial angle and creative latitude. Day-one instrumentation: UTM + GA4 + post-purchase survey. Sample-size and significance gates set up front. Output a clean read on which creator archetype actually drives the cohort.
Why first. Earns the influencer + UGC budget by proving it inside 30 days, not in a quarterly plan. Same operator discipline as the Pixis weekly creative-test cadence.
Week 3-4 - Editorial Calendar
Quarterly content + influencer plan + brand-voice doc
Build the Q3 quarterly content plan + influencer + UGC calendar + a one-page brand-voice doc that the Marketing Associate, the agency partners, and the creators all work from. Process-led big-thinking. Tied to weekly performance reporting + monthly business review cadence.
Why first. Same shape as the Plug Power UTM spec + GTM container governance and the Sensata cross-region brand-voice rollout. Process + creative big-thinking is not either/or.
