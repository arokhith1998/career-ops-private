That is the same problem your Host Pricing team is solving at a different scale: a host looking at a suggested price needs to understand it before they will act on it, and the moment they cannot, they override it and both sides lose a little.

I have built that kind of trust into a pricing tool before, not just a model that scores well on paper. At Sensata I ran the Bleeders and Leakers program, a pricing recommendation system that scored 1,200 opportunities against a $627M target and told Sales and Finance not just what to change but why, and it recovered $700K+ in margin in two quarters because people actually acted on what it recommended instead of shelving another dashboard. Getting a recommendation adopted is a different problem than getting it accurate, and it is the one I keep ending up in charge of.
