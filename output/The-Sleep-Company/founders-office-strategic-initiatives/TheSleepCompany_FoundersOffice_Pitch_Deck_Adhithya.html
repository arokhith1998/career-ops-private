First 30 days

What the first month on The Sleep Company's strategic-initiatives build looks like.

Week 1
Audit the current initiative pipeline
Pull every live growth, cost and transformation project into one view with owners, timelines and the number each is supposed to move, the same structuring pass that grounded the Safran engagement before a recommendation went to leadership.
Why first: a business case on top of an unclear pipeline just adds a slide, it does not add a decision.
Week 2-3
Build the first business case end to end
Pick the highest-leverage growth or cost-optimization initiative, size it the way the Bleeders and Leakers program sized pricing gaps, and get Finance and the category team to sign off on the number before it reaches the Founder and CFO.
Why now: a case Finance has already stress-tested is the one that survives the room.
Week 4
Stand up the governance cadence
Put a weekly operating rhythm behind the top 2-3 initiatives, the same OKR and leadership-deck cadence run at Sensata and Applied Materials, and automate the recurring data pulls with an AI agent so the review stays on the decision, not the refresh.
Why this fast: a program without a cadence drifts the moment the kickoff energy fades.
