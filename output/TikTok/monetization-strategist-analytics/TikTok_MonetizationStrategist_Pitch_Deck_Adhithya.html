Why this deck exists

A monetization team never runs out of signal. It runs out of time to turn signal into a framework before the trend goes stale.

What a monetization data science team runs into

Every commercial product throws off more events than anyone can read by hand; the job is building the metrics framework that separates a real risk or opportunity from noise, fast.
Attribution is the hard part. A trend that looks causal in one cut of the data often is not, and a wrong call shapes the next quarter's goal-setting.
The output has to move PMs, engineering, sales and strategy at the same time, or the research sits in a deck no one acts on.

What I have already run, at comparable scale

At Sensata, I built a $627M-opportunity Power BI model from 1,200 raw line items, scoring each one and surfacing $433M in margin gaps leadership could act on.
At Pixis, I ran mobile and retail-media attribution (Appsflyer, Adjust, Amazon Ads, Criteo) across an 8-client ad portfolio, then used Amplitude and Tableau to separate signal from noise.
At PriceKeel, I built the risk-versus-opportunity classifier itself: a guardrail engine and audit trail that make the call defensible, not just directional.
This req asks for metrics frameworks, statistical attribution and research that moves business goal-setting. That is the exact loop above, run on monetization data instead of industrial pricing data.

First 30 days

What the first month on TikTok's Monetization Products data science team looks like.

Week 1
Learn how a monetization call gets made today
Sit with the Monetization Products PMs, engineering and strategy teams to map how a recent risk/opportunity call moved from raw data to a decision, and where the metrics framework already exists versus where it is still ad hoc.
Why first: the same move that started the PriceKeel guardrail engine, map what is missing before building anything new.
Week 2-3
Build the first attribution-backed framework
Take one live monetization trend and build the metrics framework scoring it as risk or opportunity, using SQL and the team's data visualization stack, pressure-tested with the PM and sales partners before it reaches a research topic.
Why now: building it against a real trend, not in a vacuum, is what made the $433M in margin gaps hold at Sensata.
Week 4
Ship a research topic that moves goal-setting
Turn that framework into a research topic recommendation with a clear monetization-ROI number attached, so the next business goal-setting cycle starts from a model instead of an anecdote.
Why this fast: a monetization analytics function only scales once its calls are defensible enough for Finance and Sales to act on without a rerun.
