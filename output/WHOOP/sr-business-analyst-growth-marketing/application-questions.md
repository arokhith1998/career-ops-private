# WHOOP Application Questions - Sr Business Analyst (Growth Marketing)

---

## 1. Tell us about an interesting data set you've worked with.

The data set that taught me the most was the attribution data on a beauty brand at Pixis. On paper everything looked healthy. Blended ROAS was acceptable, every channel was credited within range, the dashboards were green. I started pulling channel-by-channel performance against holdout-incrementality slices instead of last-click, and found two channels were taking credit they hadn't earned. One was display programmatic on view-through; the other was affiliate, picking up coupon-driven clicks that would have converted anyway.

We reallocated spend to paid search and prospecting paid social based on true lift, and the brand cleared 3x ROAS the next month. The interesting part wasn't the win. It was that the attribution data had been technically accurate the whole time - it was just answering the wrong question.

That reset how I read dashboards. Aggregate ROAS hides the rot, and the only way to find it is to break the rules and ask "what happens when I turn this off."

---

## 2. What is the first question you would ask on your first day?

Which paid channel are we most under-measuring right now, and where would I be most useful in the first 30 days.

I'd want the honest version, not the polished version - the channel with the biggest gap between confidence in the number and the consequence of being wrong on it. Because the rest of the work flows from that answer. Dashboards get prioritized differently if the gap is on affiliate versus paid social. Incrementality tests get designed differently. The MMM partnership with Data Science gets framed differently.

I'd rather walk in asking that than "how can I help," which I'd guess the team has answered ten times this quarter already.
