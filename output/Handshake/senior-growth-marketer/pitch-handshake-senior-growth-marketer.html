Slide 05 | Week One

Three things I'd ship in week one for the expert network.

Concrete, not aspirational. Scoped to what would compound fastest for an expert-acquisition + retention motion at Handshake AI's current scale.
Ship 1 - Acquisition
Top-50 CS-PhD ABM campaign on LinkedIn
LinkedIn ABM targeting active PhD candidates at top-50 CS departments. Audience layers built on program + research area + advisor. Creative tied to a specific eval-task domain. Lead form connected to a referral incentive paid on first contribution.
Why first. Universities are the deepest source of net-new researcher supply. ABM beats broadcast at PhD-level intent. The referral hook is the kind of structured experiment that pays for itself fast.
Ship 2 - Lifecycle
"First Contribution in 14 Days" email sequence
Five-email lifecycle for newly-onboarded experts. Day 0 welcome with first task suggestion. Day 3, 7, 10, 14 nudges with progressively richer task matches. A/B'd on subject line, on send-time, on task-match algorithm. Drop-off measured at every step.
Why first. Activation is where expert-network economics live or die. A 14-day measured sequence cuts the time-to-first-contribution and gives the team a clean experimentation surface.
Ship 3 - SEO + Content
"Side income for researchers" content cluster
Five-piece content cluster optimized for "PhD AI training income", "academic side work in AI", "research RLHF side gigs", and adjacent intent queries. Schema marked up for AI Overview citation. Founder-led distribution via LinkedIn + Twitter + research forums.
Why first. Organic compounding. The expert network's recruiting story is a content category nobody owns yet. Owning these queries today pays back for years.
