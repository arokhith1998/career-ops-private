First 30 days

What the first month inside Nudge's Founder's Office would look like.

Week 1
Audit the trust gap
Sit with the founder, sales and whatever content exists today to find exactly where a prospect's trust breaks down, the same audit that grounded the Bleeders and Leakers margin program before a dashboard existed.
Why first: a brand-and-trust push built on assumptions instead of the real objection is the fastest way to miss the mark.
Week 2-3
Ship the first proof asset
Turn one real customer result into a case study or partnership asset content and sales can use immediately, the same move that turned scattered Pixis account data into retention playbooks.
Why now: one proof point a rep can actually use beats a week-one content calendar.
Week 4
Turn it into a cadence
Set the recurring rhythm, a proof-library update, a partnership pipeline review, or a win-loss debrief, that keeps producing trust signal without rebuilding it from scratch every cycle.
Why this fast: a cadence only earns the founder's trust once it has already moved one real deal.
