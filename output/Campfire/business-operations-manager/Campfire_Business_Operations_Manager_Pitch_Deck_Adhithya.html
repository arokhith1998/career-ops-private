Why this deck exists

The risk in a broad operations seat is not finding someone who can talk about CRM, vendors and metrics. It is finding someone who has already built and owned all three.

What a fast-growing company's ops seat runs into

CRM and deal-approval workflows get adopted from whatever tool was closest at hand, so nobody owns the data model when Sales, Finance and RevOps each need a different cut of it.
Vendor contracts renew on autopilot because nobody is assigned to actually renegotiate them, so margin erodes quietly until someone finally audits the spend.
The fix is not another dashboard. It is one operator who owns the system, renegotiates the terms, and automates the reporting so it does not re-break every quarter.

What I have already run, hands on

At PriceKeel, I set up and run the company's own CRM and CPQ software, deal approvals, and forecast and pipeline reviews, from zero, with an audit trail Finance can act on.
At Sensata, I renegotiated distributor and channel-partner contracts directly, recovering $700K+ in margin in two quarters and raising those margins from 53% to 61%.
At Applied Materials, I automate the recurring part of that analysis with AI agents built on Claude, writing the code behind each refresh.
Operating rhythm, core metrics, vendor negotiations, sales and GTM operations, and automation: this is describing the exact set of systems I already build and own.
