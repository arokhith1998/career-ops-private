First 30 days

What the first month building Arena's growth-experiment system looks like.

Week 1
Map the funnel stage by stage
Build the discovery-to-repeat-participation funnel view and baseline each stage's conversion, the same mapping step behind the 50K-device segmentation work at Sensata, so the first experiment targets the real leak.
Why first: you cannot run a growth experiment per challenge launch without knowing which funnel stage it is meant to move.
Week 2-3
Launch the first channel-and-segment experiment
Stand up a paid social or technical-community channel test against the highest-leverage funnel stage, segmented the way the 50K-device cohort work at Sensata was, sized the way the $100K/month program at Plug Power was.
Why now: the same build that cut CPA 25% at Plug Power instead of a channel nobody measured against a stage.
Week 4
Stand up the repeat-participation lifecycle loop
Build the email and in-product lifecycle sequence that targets repeat challenge participation, instrumented with the same Amplitude/Tableau discipline that lifted ROAS 40% at Pixis, then automate the reporting step so it ships every cycle.
