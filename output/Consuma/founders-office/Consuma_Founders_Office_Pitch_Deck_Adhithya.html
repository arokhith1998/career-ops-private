First 30 days

What the first month inside Consuma's Founder's Office would look like.

Week 1
Sit inside the real signal
Talk to the founders, client leads, finance and ops to find where client work, hiring or reporting is stalling today, the same audit that grounded the Bleeders and Leakers program before a dashboard existed.
Why first: an undefined mandate built on assumptions instead of real blockers is the fastest way to miss the mark.
Week 2-3
Ship the first tracker or finished output
Take one cross-functional problem, whether a client deliverable, a hiring pipeline or a finance report, and close it end to end, the same move that turned 1,200 scattered opportunities into one shortlist at Sensata.
Why now: one closed-out problem that unblocks the founders beats a week-one list of ideas.
Week 4
Turn the first win into the process
Use what the first deliverable proved to build the simple system around it, be it a vendor follow-up cadence, a client-reporting process or an events checklist, so it keeps running without rebuilding it from scratch.
Why this fast: a process only earns trust once it has already closed one real problem.
