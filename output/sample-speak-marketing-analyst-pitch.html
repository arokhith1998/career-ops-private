05 / 06 | Incrementality Tests

Holdout proved the lift was causal.

"Design and orchestrate incrementality tests to identify stronger causal relationships between campaigns and conversion."
Pixis fashion D2C partner | Geo + creative-variant holdout (30 days)
Test design: Geo + creative-variant holdout. Treatment regions received Shopping feed cleanup, smart bidding, creative refresh, lookalike expansion. Control regions ran baseline. Reconciled the lift against creative-variant diagnostics that aggregate ROAS would have hidden.
