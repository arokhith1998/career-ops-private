Why this deck exists

The mandate is not "run more Meta ads." It is signal architecture, structured testing and a defensible CAC number.

What that actually requires

Meta has to carry both a self-serve motion and a sales-led motion on the same budget, with down-funnel pipeline, not just clicks, as the metric that matters.
Signal quality (CAPI, server-side tracking, offline conversion data) has to improve continuously so the platforms can optimize toward real customers, not just leads.
Testing has to be structured and documented across audiences, bidding, creative and optimization events, so paid social becomes predictable, not a string of one-off wins.
