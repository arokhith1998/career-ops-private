First 30 days

What the first month on Sierra's product marketing team looks like.

Week 1
Learn the current launch motion
Sit with product, sales and agent development to map which industries, personas and launches are live right now, and which messaging has actually closed deals versus just shipped.
Why first: the strongest positioning pattern is usually visible in what already closed. Same move that drove the 15% conversion lift at Sensata.
Week 2-3
Ship one launch end to end
Take one upcoming feature or industry launch through positioning, the sales playbook (deck, landing page, demo) and buyer-specific translation, working directly with sales and agent development.
Why now: building the story on a live launch, not a hypothetical, is what made the Sensata enablement actually get used by 50+ reps.
Week 4
Template the AI-first system
Turn what worked into a reusable AI-first automation, built with Claude or ChatGPT, so the next launch or industry translation starts from a template instead of a blank page.
Why this fast: a template only earns its name once a second launch runs through it without a rebuild.
