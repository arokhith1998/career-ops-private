First 30 days

What the first month inside Green Gold's CEO's office would look like.

Week 1
Audit the existing materials
Go through the current investor decks, data-room files and KPI reports to find where the numbers are stale, the narrative is inconsistent, or a diligence question has no ready answer.
Why first: a new deck built on last quarter's assumptions is the fastest way to lose a room's trust.
Week 2-3
Ship the first forecast or scenario model
Pick the highest-stakes open question (an investor ask, a lender covenant, a partner negotiation) and build the operating model and scenario analysis behind it, the same discipline behind the AP26 models at Sensata.
Why now: one model that survives a tough question beats a week-one slide of ideas.
Week 4
Stand up the KPI-against-plan cadence
Turn the first model into a recurring KPI tracker, flagging risk against plan before it becomes a surprise in the next board or investor meeting.
Why this fast: a tracking cadence only earns trust once it has already caught something real.
