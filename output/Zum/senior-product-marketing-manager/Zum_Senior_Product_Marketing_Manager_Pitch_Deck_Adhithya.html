First 30 days

What the first month building Zum's persona messaging and sales-enablement system looks like.

Week 1
Map the three-persona buying committee
Interview recent closed-won and closed-lost deals to separate what moves a transportation director from what moves an operations leader or a procurement team, the same win/loss discipline behind the 30+-interview program at Sensata.
Why first: message before you build the battlecard, not after.
Week 2-3
Ship the first sales-enablement system
Build the first battlecard, objection-handling guide and ROI one-pager directly with Sales, versioned once as a reusable system instead of rebuilt per rep per stalled deal.
Why now: the same build that moved Sensata's win rate 30% instead of a deck no rep opened twice.

Stand up the competitive-intelligence loop
