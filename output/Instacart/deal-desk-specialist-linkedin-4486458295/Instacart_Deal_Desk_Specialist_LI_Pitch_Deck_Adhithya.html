Why this deck exists

Your posting is explicit: deals are getting more complex faster than the process behind them, and Sales, Finance, Tax and Legal all need to trust the same number.

Where that trust breaks

A slow deal desk does not announce itself. It shows up as a quote stuck in approval while a rep waits, a contract clause nobody flagged until after signature, and a Quote-to-Cash process that worked fine until a new pricing model or acquisition landed on it.
Because Sales, Finance, Tax and Legal all touch the same deal, one missed review step becomes everyone's cleanup problem at close, not just Deal Desk's problem alone.
The fix is not a longer approval queue. It is one person treating deal review, system ownership and the audit trail as a single loop instead of three separate handoffs.

What I already run

At PriceKeel, I configured and personally run the CRM, CPQ, forecast and pipeline reviews, and deal approvals, the same Quote-to-Cash ownership this role holds for Instacart's Sales org.
At Sensata, I read multimillion-dollar customer quotes and AP26 revenue models for risk and approved terms before signature, then scaled that same discipline into a program covering 1,200 pricing opportunities.
Both sides mean I have sat in the deal-review seat and the system-ownership seat on the same approval, not just one of the two.
Deal velocity, risk and compliance, Quote-to-Cash excellence, cross-functional partnership, scalable new programs: that is one connected loop. Review the deal, run the system, keep the trail honest.

Week 1
