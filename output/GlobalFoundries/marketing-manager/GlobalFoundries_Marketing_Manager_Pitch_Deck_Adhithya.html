Why this deck exists

A brand-new RF product line has no playbook yet. Demand, supply and pricing all have to be built at once, not in sequence.

What standing up a new product line runs into

Demand forecasting without a pricing view guesses at revenue. Pricing without a supply view promises what cannot ship. A new team building both separately loses the quarter reconciling them.
Design win and tape-out revenue timing only make sense against a market view that Sales, Engineering and Accounts all recognize as the same number.
The fix is not a bigger spreadsheet. It is someone who has already sized, priced and forecast inside a semiconductor product line, so the first quarter builds the system instead of surviving it.

At Sensata, I led global pricing for a multi-$100M Industrial BU, recovering $700K+ in margin and raising distributor margins 53% to 61%, the same demand-meets-supply discipline a new RF line needs.
I also led the Safran market-sizing engagement, narrowing 170 facilities to a SAM of 28 across 3 product lines, the exact TAM-to-launch arc a brand-new product line runs at day one.
