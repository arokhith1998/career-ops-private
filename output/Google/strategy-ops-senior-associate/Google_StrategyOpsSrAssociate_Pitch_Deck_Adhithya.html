Why this deck exists

This posting is really two disciplines held by one person: the analysis that finds the insight, and the operating cadence that makes it stick.

What losing that discipline runs into

A strategy function that only produces analysis does not fail loudly. It fails as a recommendation that got nodded through in one meeting, then quietly abandoned the next quarter because no cadence existed to check on it.
Sales, Finance, Product and Legal all touch the same cross-functional plan, so a gap in process design or stakeholder alignment becomes everyone's guessing game at the next review, not just one team's problem.
The fix is not another one-off deck. It is someone who treats insight generation, process design and stakeholder alignment as one connected discipline instead of three separate jobs.

What I have already run

At Applied Materials, I align Sales, Engineering and Accounts into one strategic view, then run the OKRs and weekly and quarterly business reviews that keep that view current instead of stale.
At Sensata, I built the Bleeders and Leakers dashboard that made 1,200 pricing opportunities actionable, then carried that same analysis into AP26 revenue models directly with senior executives.
Both mean I have sat on the insight-generation side and the stakeholder-alignment side of the same loop, not just one or the other.
A Strategy and Operations Senior Associate who extracts insights, designs operating cadences, develops cross-functional strategy and presents to leadership is describing this exact loop: find the signal, build the process, keep every function aligned behind it.
