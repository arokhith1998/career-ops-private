Why this deck exists

This req is really two disciplines held by one person: the analysis that finds the signal across a sprawling ecosystem, and the discretion to turn it into one executive call.

What falls apart without that person

A small team covering the entire external tech ecosystem, from early startups to large public companies, cannot afford a slow read: by the time a theme reaches a senior stakeholder, the financials and metrics behind it have to already be reconciled, not still in dispute.
Ad hoc special projects do not come with a template. Without someone who can structure an ambiguous question fast and hold it with discretion, each one either stalls or leaks before leadership has decided what to do with it.
The fix is one person who treats external analysis, executive communication and discretion as one job, not three.

First 30 days

What the first month inside Product Strategy and Operations looks like.

Week 1
Map the ecosystem and the current cadence
Sit with the small team and the senior stakeholders it serves to learn how themes, financials and metrics currently move from raw data to an executive call, and shadow one existing deliverable end to end before touching anything.
Why first: the same instinct that had me learn Applied Materials' Sales, Engineering and Accounts loop before building on top of it.
Week 2-3
Own one ad hoc special project end to end
Take one open cross-ecosystem question, build the model against the underlying financials and metrics, and present a data-driven recommendation to a senior stakeholder with the discretion the project calls for, the same loop that turned Bleeders and Leakers into a leadership decision at Sensata.
Why now: the fastest way to earn trust on an unfamiliar ecosystem is to ship one complete, discreet recommendation, not a slide of open questions.
Week 4
Propose the reporting cadence
Propose the recurring review that keeps that recommendation current: who owns it, how often it gets re-checked against the ecosystem's next move, mirroring the weekly and quarterly business reviews already running at Applied Materials and Sensata.
Why this fast: a strategy function that produces one analysis is not finished; the cadence is what makes leadership act on it every cycle, not just once.
