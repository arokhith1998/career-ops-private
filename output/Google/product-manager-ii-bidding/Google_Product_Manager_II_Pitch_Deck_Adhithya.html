First 30 days

What the first month on Google's Bidding team looks like.

Week 1
Map where the current bidding system leaves value on the table
Pull existing advertiser segments by size, vertical and sophistication, and find where bid strategies already underperform budget pacing or targeting for a specific segment.
Why first: the same first move behind the Sensata price-optimization system, find the gap before building a new model to close it.
Week 2-3
Build the opportunity model for one bidding path
Pick the highest-ambiguity bid strategy question, a budget-pacing edge case or an auction segment underserved today, and build the quantitative case with Engineering and Google Research.
Why now: the same cross-functional build behind the Sensata and Plug Power bid-optimization programs, not a solo analysis.
Week 4
Turn the model into a PRD and OKRs partner teams can build against
Translate the sized opportunity into a PRD, then into OKRs for the engineering and PgM teams who will ship the new bid strategy or backend improvement.
Why this fast: the same PRD-to-OKR habit that carried a 100+ feature backlog through Engineering at Sensata.
