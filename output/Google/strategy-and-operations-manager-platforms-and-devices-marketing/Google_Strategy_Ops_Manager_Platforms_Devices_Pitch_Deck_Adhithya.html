Why this deck exists

A Strategy and Operations seat is really two disciplines held by one person: the tracking framework that makes spend legible, and the cadence that keeps the plan honest all year.

What this seat runs into without that framework

Vendor and agency operations, a bi-annual resource-allocation survey, and an annual AI Transformation plan each produce their own data. Without one tracking and prioritization framework, Central and Product Area teams end up reading three different pictures of the same budget.
A survey on resource allocation versus priorities only changes anything if its findings land inside the next operations review, not six months later as an archived slide nobody reopens.
The fix is one person who treats vendor-ops efficiency, resource-allocation reporting and executive-level planning as a single discipline instead of three separate owners handing off a spreadsheet.
