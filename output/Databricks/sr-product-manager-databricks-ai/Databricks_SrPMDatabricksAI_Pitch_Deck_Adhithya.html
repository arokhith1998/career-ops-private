First 30 days

What the first month owning an agent or model product at Databricks AI looks like.

Week 1
Sit with engineering and research on what ships next
Map the current agent/model capabilities closest to production readiness, and find where a capability is technically done but has no customer-facing use case attached yet.
Why first: the same move behind the SwipeHire PRD: separate what is technically done from what a customer can actually use, before committing a roadmap slot.
Week 2-3
Run customer conversations on the use case most likely to ship first
Go direct to 10-15 target customers or internal champions to pressure-test which agent workflow earns a place on the roadmap first, the same discipline behind Sensata's 30+ OEM interviews.
Why now: a roadmap built from internal opinion ships the wrong thing well; one built from direct customer contact ships the right thing fast.
Week 4
Stand up the SQL-based adoption signal
Build the usage-analytics view that separates a well-demoed capability from one customers actually keep using, then automate its reporting with an AI agent so the signal updates every cycle.
Why this fast: the same SQL-and-GA4 habit from Sensata, and the same Claude-agent automation already running at Applied Materials today, not a plan for someday.
