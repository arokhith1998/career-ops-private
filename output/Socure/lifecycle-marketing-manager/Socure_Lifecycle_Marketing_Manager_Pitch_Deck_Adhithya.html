A ranked set of first- and third-party signals, web/content engagement, webinar behavior, intent data, Salesforce stage movement, turned into triggers with real entry and exit rules, not one static score.
AI agents that research the account, draft the message and choose the channel, with brand-voice and compliance guardrails and a human approval step, not a fire-and-forget bot.
Email owned as a channel end to end, signal-triggered sends, nurture, re-engagement and expansion programs, measured on signal-to-meeting and signal-to-SQL, not open rate.
