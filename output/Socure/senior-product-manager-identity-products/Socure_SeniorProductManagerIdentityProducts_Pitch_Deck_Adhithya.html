The evidence-bar problem, in depth

A notification is only as trustworthy as the source behind it. That is a product-design problem, not a data-engineering afterthought.

The brief treats data-source evaluation as a standalone step before measurement. I would treat them as one loop: the evidence bar a source has to clear should be defined by the same KPIs the product is held to.

First 30 days

What the first month owning the proactive identity-notification product looks like.

Week 1
Pressure-test the event contract
Sit with the engineering and platform teams on how signals get resolved, merged and published today, and draft what the notification's lifecycle semantics need to be for a customer to act on it without a false alarm.
Why first: the same first move behind PriceKeel's guardrail engine, define the evidence bar before building the product around it.
Week 2-3
Stand up the KPI and benchmarking scorecard
Define coverage, precision and recency targets with the Data Partnerships team, and build the internal benchmarking view that turns a candidate source's claims into a buy, build or walk-away decision.
Why now: the same KPI-instrumentation discipline that turned Sensata's dashboard into $433M of prioritized action.
Week 4
Price the first beta cohort
Model the subscription's pricing and packaging with early-adopter customers, using the incremental value per period the KPI work surfaced, and lock the beta-to-GA phasing plan with every function on a committed date.
Why this fast: the same zero-to-one discipline that set PriceKeel's commercial model from day one.
