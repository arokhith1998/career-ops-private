Why this deck exists

An experimentation platform is not a dashboard. It is a trust system that has to survive contact with real product and engineering teams.

What an experimentation-platform PM runs into

Defining a success metric is easy. Getting product, engineering and data science teams to actually adopt data-driven decisions over opinion is the real, repeated work.
An experiment is only as trustworthy as its guardrails: anomaly detection, diagnostics and a standard methodology have to exist before the first real result gets acted on.
Ad tech specifically means the number eventually has to translate into a revenue and margin case, not just a statistically significant lift.

What I have already run, at comparable scale

At Sensata, I ran a 100+ feature backlog in Agile across 3 regions, grounding every call in a KPI framework built in SQL and GA4 across 50K+ devices.
I have run structured A/B testing at three different stops: 15+ tests at Plug Power, 8+ tests on SwipeHire, and Amplitude/Tableau analysis across 30+ campaigns at Pixis.
At Applied Materials now, I build the value-based pricing case that turns an experiment result into a revenue number, automating the recurring analysis with Claude AI agents.
This req asks for statistical frameworks, guardrail monitoring, and cultural adoption of data-driven decisions across product and engineering. That is the same loop above, run repeatedly across three different product contexts.

First 30 days

What the first month on Apple Ads' experimentation platform team looks like.

Week 1
Map the current experiment lifecycle
Sit with Product, Engineering and Data Science to learn how an experiment currently moves from hypothesis to guardrail monitoring to decision, and where today's process already has structure versus where it is still manual.
Why first: the same move that made the Sensata KPI framework hold, map what exists before building anything new.
Week 2-3
Ship one guardrail improvement
Pick the most-cited pain point in experiment diagnostics or anomaly detection and ship a fix, pressure-tested against 1 to 2 live experiments before rolling it out platform-wide.
Why now: shipping against a real experiment, not a hypothetical, is what made the 18% adoption lift at Sensata hold.
Week 4
Drive the first adoption win
Partner with one product team to run their next decision through the platform's standard methodology end to end, then document it as the reference case for cultural adoption.
Why this fast: a platform earns trust once one team has seen the standard methodology win an argument.
