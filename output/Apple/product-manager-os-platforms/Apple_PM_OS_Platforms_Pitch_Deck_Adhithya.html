Why this deck exists

Shipping a software update is easy. Landing release timelines, launch deliverables and one message across dozens of partners, on the same clock, is the real job.

What an OS Platforms PM runs into

Release schedules touch Engineering, PR, marketing communications, legal and sales at once; one missed handoff slips the whole platform's launch window.
A press briefing and a customer-facing changelog have to tell the same story in different voices, on the same day a beta or security release ships.
Competitive signal and usage data have to turn into a recommendation before the next release locks, not after it ships.

What I have already run, at comparable scale

At Sensata, ran a 100+ feature backlog in Agile with Engineering across 3 regions, cutting time-to-market 20% and accelerating releases 25%.
Owned positioning and launch messaging that lifted conversion 15%, and built the sales enablement (decks, case studies) that moved win rates up 30% for 50+ reps.

First 30 days

What the first month on Apple's OS Platforms team looks like.

Week 1
Map the release and launch calendar
Sit with Engineering, PR and marketing comms to learn how a release currently moves from code freeze to launch deliverables, and where today's handoffs are already tight versus still manual.
Why first: the same move that made the Sensata backlog hold, map what exists before changing anything.
Week 2-3
Ship one launch-messaging fix
Pick the most-cited mismatch between the engineering changelog and the customer-facing story, and close it on the next minor release before the next major one locks.
Why now: closing that mismatch on a real release, not a hypothetical, is what made the 15% conversion lift at Sensata hold.
Week 4
Run one release end to end
Partner with one feature team to carry their release from timeline to launch messaging to post-launch read, then document it as the reference playbook.
Why this fast: a platform PM earns trust once one release has gone out clean, on time, with one story.
