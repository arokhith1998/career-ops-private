The Sensata-to-Conveo translation

If I had had Conveo at Sensata, my 30+ interviews would have taken 1 week, not 4. The Conveo buyer is me, 6 months ago, drowning in Calendly invites.

What I learned running 30+ research interviews

  • Time-to-insight bottleneck: 60% of cycle time is scheduling + recap, not interview itself
  • Bias problem: By interview 20 of 30, you start leading. AI-led keeps consistency
  • Synthesis decay: Insights peak 24 hours after interview. Most teams synthesize too late
  • Trust threshold: Research teams adopt fast when transcripts + verbatim quotes are clean
"These are the exact pain points Conveo solves. I can talk to a Head of Insights about them without faking domain knowledge."

How I would market Conveo on day 30

  • Practitioner content: "What I wish I had known before running 30 research interviews" - story-led, friction-first, no platform pitch
  • ICP segmentation: marketing research teams vs. product research vs. UX research. Different proof points each
  • Case study factory: 1 customer case study per week, anchored to a single "decisions made in days" hero metric
  • Comparison content: Conveo vs. traditional research panels, vs. Calendly + Zoom + manual synthesis, vs. generic AI tools
