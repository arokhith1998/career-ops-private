Why this deck exists

This req is really three jobs held by one person: paid-budget ownership, AI-powered campaign automation and lifecycle nurture, all against one pipeline number.

What losing that discipline runs into

A demand-gen seat without someone who already automates does not fail loudly. It fails as a reporting backlog that never gets built into a tool, a CAC number that drifts because paid and lifecycle are not reconciled against the same pipeline target.
Paid acquisition, SEO and lifecycle nurture all feed the same enterprise pipeline number, so a gap in automation or attribution becomes everyone's guessing game at the next pipeline review, not just one channel's problem.
The fix is not another dashboard. It is someone who treats campaign automation, paid ownership and lifecycle handoff as one connected system instead of three separate jobs.
