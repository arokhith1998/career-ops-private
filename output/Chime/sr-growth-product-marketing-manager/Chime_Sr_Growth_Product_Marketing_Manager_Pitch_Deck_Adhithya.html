Why this deck exists

This req is really three jobs held by one person: own a funnel segment, run the test roadmap behind it, and write the creative that goes into those tests. All at once, against one quarterly number.

What losing that overlap runs into

Split a growth seat across a strategist, an analyst and a creative writer and the handoffs are where it breaks: tests ship late because the brief sits in a queue, and insights die in a deck instead of becoming the next hypothesis.
Incentive and promotion tests for qualified acquisition are the clearest casualty: they ship without a clean read because Lifecycle was looped in after the test launched, not before.
The fix is not another reviewer in the chain. It is someone who owns the funnel number, the test calendar and the creative brief as one connected loop.

What I have already run

At Plug Power and Pixis, I owned the acquisition budget, ran the A/B test calendar and wrote and tested the creative myself (feed, bidding, creative refresh) inside the same cycle, not across three handoffs.
At Sensata, I built the GTM plan and the positioning and messaging for the same launch, generating $2M+ in first-year revenue without a separate writer in the loop.
At Applied Materials, I automate the reporting layer with AI agents built on Claude, so strategy time does not get eaten by analysis.
A Sr. Growth Product Marketing Manager who owns a funnel segment end to end, runs the growth experimentation roadmap, and leads creative and messaging for those same tests is describing one loop, not three handoffs.
