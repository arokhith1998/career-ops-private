Why this deck exists

The posting is direct: catch the reconciliation gap before it hits billing, and fix the pattern, not just the deal.

What losing that discipline runs into

A Deal Desk without a real root-cause habit does not fail loudly. It fails as an order form with a non-standard term nobody flagged, a closed-won deal whose Salesforce record drifts from the contract, and a billing error traced back a quarter late.
Legal, provisioning, RevOps and engineering all inherit the same gap, so one unflagged exception becomes everyone's cleanup problem, not just Deal Desk's.
The fix is not one more review step. It is someone who diagnoses whether the gap is an SFDC field mapping issue, an Ironclad template gap, or a rep habit, and drives the fix upstream so it stops recurring.

What I have already run, at smaller scale

At PriceKeel, I review every in-flight discount exception for non-standard terms before it reaches Legal, then built the audit trail that reconciles each approved exception against the contract it actually shipped.
At Sensata, I audited closed deals against a $627M target, diagnosed root cause deal by deal, and recovered $700K+ in margin in 2 quarters by fixing the pattern, not the one deal.
Both mean I have already sat on the diagnosis side of a reconciliation gap, not just the routing side of one.
A Deal Desk Analyst who reviews order forms, audits closed-won deals against Salesforce, and drives fixes upstream is describing this exact loop: catch it, diagnose it, fix the system underneath it.
