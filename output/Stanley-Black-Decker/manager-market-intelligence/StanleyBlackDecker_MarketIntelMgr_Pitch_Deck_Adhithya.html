MARKET INTELLIGENCE PITCH
01
Why this deck exists

The job is not "watch the market." It is turn trends from three regions into one recommendation leadership can act on this quarter.

What that actually requires

Trends, competitive moves, consumer behavior and channel dynamics have to be tracked across the US, Europe and other priority markets at the same time, not region by region in isolation.
The data never arrives as one clean feed. It has to be synthesized from several sources into a single clear takeaway before a category or brand leader can use it.
None of it moves the business if it stays a spreadsheet. It has to become a dashboard or a deck that a non-technical stakeholder can act on in the room.

What I have already run

At Sensata, I ran competitor and customer analysis across EMEA and the US at once, turning purchasing patterns and competitor market share into revenue-opportunity calls for category leadership.
At Sensata again, I built the FY2026 Power BI dashboard that synthesized pricing, channel and competitive data into one view across 3 regions, 4 product families and 20 product lines.
At Applied Materials today, I size the semiconductor manufacturing market with Sales and Engineering, the same cross-functional intelligence loop Tools & Outdoor runs on.
The through-line: every market read I have shipped was pulled from more than one region and more than one data source, then handed to a leader as a clear call, not a raw export.
