Sales enablement, decks, battlecards, FAQs, talk tracks, that a rep can pick up cold and use correctly on a call the same day.
Competitive and market research that stays current enough to inform the next launch, not a quarterly snapshot nobody opens.

First 30 days

What the first month supporting launches and enablement at Nscale looks like.

Week 1
Audit the current messaging and enablement set
Read every live battlecard, one-pager and FAQ against the current product roadmap, and sit in on Sales and SME conversations to find where the technical story and the sales story have already drifted apart.
Why first: the same first move behind every Sensata launch, find where the message breaks before rewriting it.
Week 2-3
Rebuild one launch's positioning and enablement
Pick the next major launch or roadmap update, translate its technical capabilities into a value proposition and use cases with Product and Engineering, then ship the sales deck, one-pager and talk track together with Sales.
Why now: the same cross-functional build that moved Sensata's win rate 30% instead of a deck Sales never opened.
Week 4
Stand up the competitive-intelligence cadence
Institute the direct-research discipline behind Sensata's 30+ customer interviews, applied to Nscale's buyers and competitors, then automate the repeat tracking so the intelligence stays current every cycle, not once a quarter.
Why this fast: the same Claude-agent habit that keeps my own market research moving at Applied Materials today, not a plan for someday.
