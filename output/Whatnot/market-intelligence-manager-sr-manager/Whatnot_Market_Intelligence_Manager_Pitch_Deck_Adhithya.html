Why this deck exists

Understanding a market that changes weekly is really two jobs held by one person, not a report that ships once a quarter.

What losing that connection runs into

Market intelligence that is not tied to a trusted data pipeline becomes a deck nobody double-checks before the next launch decision.
Vendor and data-source relationships that are not actively quality-managed quietly go stale, and the dashboard built on top of them looks fine right up until it is wrong.
The fix is not another market report. It is one person who owns the vendor relationship, the data quality and the point of view as a single connected loop.

What I have already run

At Sensata, I built the FY2026 Power BI system myself: pipeline funnel, regional scorecards and win rate, all tied back to a $627M target, not handed to me already built.
At Pixis, I owned the vendor relationships and data quality for every retail-media and attribution channel an 8-client portfolio ran on, not just the dashboard sitting on top.
At Applied Materials, I turn noisy field input from three different functions into one market view leadership can act on without re-checking it first.
A Market Intelligence Manager who owns vendor relationships through to data quality, then turns that into dashboards leadership trusts, is describing this exact loop, already run at two different companies.
