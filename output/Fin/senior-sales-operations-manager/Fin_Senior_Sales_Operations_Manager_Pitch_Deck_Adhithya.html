Why this deck exists

Owning sales operations end to end is not a reporting job. It is a design job: get the data model and the operating cadence wrong and the system becomes the bottleneck it was built to remove.

What a scaling GTM engine runs into

Forecasting, pipeline, territory and quota data live in different systems, so one source of truth has to be built before leadership can trust any dashboard.
Without consistent metric definitions and governance, every business review becomes a debate about whose number is right instead of what to do next.
Manual reporting does not scale with headcount flat, so the operating cadence has to be designed to run itself as the sales org grows.
