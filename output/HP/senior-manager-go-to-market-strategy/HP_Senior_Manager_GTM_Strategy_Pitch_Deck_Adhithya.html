Why this deck exists

A three-year growth plan does not fail at the launch review. It fails when the revenue and margin model behind it goes stale by quarter three.

What a multi-year commercial plan runs into

Revenue and margin models get built once for the kickoff deck, then country teams and commercial functions keep operating off a baseline that nobody updates when channel mix, pricing or demand shift.
An in-year target inside a three-year goal needs a model that can be re-run mid-cycle, not re-derived from scratch when an operating review asks a hard question.
The fix is not another one-time model. It is someone who builds the model, tracks the gap it finds, and closes the loop by acting on it before the next review.
