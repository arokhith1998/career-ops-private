Why this deck exists

This posting is really four jobs held by one person: deal support, order-form drafting, governance, and quarter-close reconciliation, on every transaction at once.

What losing that discipline runs into

A Deal Desk without a deal-by-deal read does not fail loudly. It fails as a non-standard order form approved on last quarter's assumptions, a clause nobody logged in the library, and a Salesforce record that does not match the signed work order.
Sales, Legal, Finance and Accounting all touch the same deal, so a gap in escalation or data alignment becomes everyone's guessing game at the next quarter close, not just one team's problem.
The fix is not another spreadsheet. It is someone who treats quotation review, order-form drafting and system hygiene as one connected discipline instead of three separate jobs.

What I have already run

At PriceKeel, I set up and run the CRM, CPQ configuration and the audit trail myself, the same system-ownership and governance loop this role holds for SFDC and its clause library.
At Sensata, I owned deal-by-deal review of prior deal terms inside the Bleeders and Leakers program and built the Power BI dashboard that made 1,200 pricing opportunities auditable by segment before a quarter closed.
Both mean I have sat on the quote-review side and the system-configuration side of the same gate, not just one or the other.
A Senior Deal Desk Analyst who drafts order forms, maintains a clause library, escalates non-standard deals and keeps Salesforce aligned after sign-off is describing this exact loop: review the deal, document the exception, keep the system honest.
