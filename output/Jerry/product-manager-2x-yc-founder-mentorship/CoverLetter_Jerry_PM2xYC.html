Hiring Team, Jerry

Direct mentorship from a founder who has built two YC companies is worth more than a title. It is the fastest way to compress years of product judgment into months, and it only works if the person on the other side of the table can already own a problem without being told how. That is the project I keep coming back to: SwipeHire, an AI job-matching app I took from a blank page to 50 early users by myself, writing the PRD, scoping the MVP, and shipping auto-tailored resumes and personalized matching with no cofounder and no team to hand anything off to.

I have run that same loop inside a company with more at stake. At Sensata I owned the product roadmap for sensor solutions in automotive and industrial segments, carrying a 100+ feature backlog through PRDs with engineering across three regions, which cut time-to-market 20%. I got there by running 30+ customer interviews with OEMs and distributors myself rather than waiting for research to hand me a summary, and I used that same instinct to launch GTM for a new sensing product line that drove $2M+ in first-year revenue. A founder-led program that hands a new hire a vertical and expects them to move fast is describing exactly that kind of week.

Right now at Applied Materials I build the forecasting and market-sizing models that decide whether an opportunity is worth engineering time, and I automate the repetitive parts of that work with AI agents built on Claude so more hours go to the calls that actually need a human. I can be in Palo Alto for the first six months, and I want the kind of compressed, high-ownership exposure to product, growth and operations that learning directly from a repeat founder gives you, not a rotation that stays theoretical.
