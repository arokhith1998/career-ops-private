First 30 days

What the first month owning Perplexity's revenue funnel processes looks like.

Week 1
Map the current funnel
Sit with Sales, Marketing, Finance and Analytics to trace how a lead currently becomes a routed, worked opportunity, and where attribution, routing rules or stage hygiene already break down.
Why first: the same move that started the Bleeders and Leakers program: find where the case for action is strongest before touching anything.
Week 2-3
Fix the highest-friction breaks
Rebuild the Salesforce reports, flows and validation rules behind the worst-behaved routing or attribution gaps, using Computer and other AI tools to automate the research and QA behind each fix.
Why now: fixing the underlying logic, not overriding the symptom, is what made the 53% to 61% distributor gain stick.
Week 4
Document and hand off
Turn the fixes into documented process and train reps and front-line leaders on the new rules, so the improvement survives without me in the room.
Why this fast: a process only counts once someone else can run it unsupervised.
