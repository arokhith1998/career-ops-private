Why this deck exists

The job is not "watch the market." It is turn a competitor's new move into a battlecard update and a talking point before the next deal needs it.

What that actually requires

Competitors in AI analytics ship fast. A battlecard or quick-reference guide that was accurate last quarter can cost a deal this quarter if nobody refreshes it.
The refresh has to turn into messaging, launch and pricing insights sales can use cold, not a research memo that sits in a shared drive.
None of it counts until reps are trained on the new positioning and the win/loss story reaches executives in a form they can act on.

What I have already run

At Sensata, I benchmarked 10+ competitors across channel, pricing and market share and turned the read into a pricing realignment that drove +12% YoY revenue and +8% market share.
At Sensata again, I built sales enablement materials (decks, case studies) for a 50-rep team and briefed senior executives with competitive talking points on live deals.
At Applied Materials today, I track competitive and demand inflection points with Sales and Engineering, the same monitor-and-brief loop this role runs on.
The through-line: every competitive read I have shipped turned into a sales-facing artifact and an executive-facing number, not a research note that stayed mine.
