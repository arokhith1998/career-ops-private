The discipline that drove the 3x ROAS - cut weak spend, double down on what works - is the same muscle that protects spend when blended numbers look fine but the engine is leaking under the hood.

Slide 05 | First Thirty Days at SyntheticFi

Three experiments I would run in the first thirty days on the RIA channel.

Concrete and scoped. Each ships inside a month and produces a measurable AUM-retention outcome - not a CPL one.
Experiment 1 - RIA Channel Holdout
Matched holdout on the largest RIA reengagement audience
Pick the biggest RIA-reengagement audience currently active. Carve a matched holdout cell. Measure incremental AUM-retention + box-spread-issuance lift over 3 weeks against the exposed cell. Cut over-credited spend, reallocate to true lift.
Why first. On a partner-channel product, reengagement spend always over-attributes. This is the Pixis 3x ROAS muscle applied to the RIA channel - protects AUM-retention spend before it scales.
Experiment 2 - Box-Spread Content Engine
Three content assets that scale the explanation of box spreads
(a) Long-form RIA-partner playbook on box spreads vs bank lines (3,000 words + visuals + comparison tables); (b) 4-minute explainer video on box-spread mechanics for the RIA-facing microsite; (c) cohort-specific landing pages for top 3 RIA segments with case studies + AUM-retention proof.
Why first. Same translation muscle that turned Sensata's technical sensor features into 15% conversion lift and 50-rep sales enablement that drove 30% win-rate lift. Box spreads need the same explainer cadence.
Experiment 3 - Sales + CX Feedback Loop
Closed-loop attribution + RIA-cohort dashboard in week 4
Build UTM spec + GTM containers + Salesforce attribution + RIA-cohort segments mirroring the Plug Power rebuild. Ship a 1-page weekly dashboard tracking pipeline by RIA AUM tier + acquisition source + content-asset attribution. The artifact Sales + CX opens every Monday.
