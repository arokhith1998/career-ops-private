Why this deck exists

A Deal Strategy & Operations seat is three jobs in one: deal architecture, approval ownership, and a win/loss discipline Sales can actually use.

What losing that discipline runs into

A quote-to-cash process without a clear escalation owner does not fail at the signature. It fails earlier, as a creative deal structure nobody stress-tested, a CPQ configuration Sales routes around, and a win/loss pattern nobody wrote down until it repeats next quarter.
Sales needs training on pricing and deal construction they will actually use mid-negotiation, not a policy doc they read once and forget.
The fix is not another approval gate. It is someone who treats deal architecture, approval ownership and win/loss retrospective as one connected loop instead of three separate jobs.
