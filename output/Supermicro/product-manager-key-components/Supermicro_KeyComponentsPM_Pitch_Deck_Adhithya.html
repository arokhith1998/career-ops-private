Why this deck exists

The mandate is not "write a spec sheet." It is requirements from the field, a vendor negotiation, and a lifecycle plan someone else can sell.

What that actually requires

Requirements have to come from three places at once, customer input, market and competitive forces, and company capabilities, reconciled into one spec before Engineering ever opens a ticket.
The lifecycle does not stop at launch. A component manager owns it through growth, maturity and decline, which means pricing, positioning and vendor terms all have to move together over years, not a single release.
None of it lands if the sales team cannot explain it. The PM has to be the subject matter expert standing in front of reps, internal and external, making the requirements sellable.

Week 1
Pull the current requirements sources together
Sit with Sales, Engineering and the key external vendors to map where customer input, market research and competitive teardowns already live, and where the gaps are between what customers ask for and what the current roadmap covers.

Week 2-3
