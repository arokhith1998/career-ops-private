The proof, in depth

A 3-layer operating-cadence framework for a growing Founder's Office, built from what already works.

A starting framework for institutionalizing execution discipline, not a biography: three layers that keep a strategic initiative from stalling between the decision and the dashboard.
Layer 1
Decision log
Every strategic initiative gets one page before work starts: the question, the decision rights, the milestone and the metric that proves it worked.
Built before: defined objectives and decision rights for PriceKeel's own build-versus-pilot calls every month.
Layer 2
Review dashboard
A living scorecard, by initiative and owner, that turns weekly updates into a single view of where the plan and the number have diverged.
Built before: the Sensata dashboard did this at $627M scale: 1,200 opportunities, regional scorecards and stage distribution in one view.
Layer 3
Escalation path
A standing rule for when a variance gets flagged to the Founder versus resolved inside the team, so risk surfaces early instead of at the quarterly review.
Built before: the same dashboard surfaced $433M in margin gaps by salesperson and product line, routed to leadership to prioritize.
Same instinct as PriceKeel: build the decision trail first, so execution does not stall waiting on the next review.

First 30 days

What the first month inside Storm Finance's Founder's Office would look like.

Week 1
Map the current decision trail
Sit with the Founder and functional leads to find where today's strategic initiatives actually lose time: an undefined decision right, a missing metric, or a review that happens too late, the same audit that grounded the Bleeders and Leakers program before a dashboard existed.
Why first: a mechanism built on the wrong bottleneck just adds more reporting.
Week 2-3
Ship one operating mechanism
Turn one live initiative into the first review cadence, dashboard or issue log the team actually uses, modeled on the Bleeders and Leakers build at Sensata.
Why now: one mechanism that moves one real decision beats a week-one framework deck.
Week 4
Turn it into a cadence
Set the recurring rhythm, a scorecard update, a cross-functional review, or an escalation checkpoint, that keeps surfacing risk without being rebuilt from scratch each cycle.
Why this fast: a cadence only earns the Founder's trust once it has already moved one real decision.
