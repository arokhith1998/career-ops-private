Why this deck exists

Paid social and mobile UA are one acquisition motion, not two. Measured wrong, they compete for budget instead of compounding.

What running them apart runs into

A user acquired on Meta and a user acquired through mobile display look identical in a blended CAC number, but they convert differently and need different measurement, SKAN for one, MMP event data for the other.
Without one shared incrementality and mobile-measurement framework, paid social and mobile UA end up optimizing against each other's attribution instead of the same business outcome.
The fix is not more spend. It is a testing agenda that treats creative, audience and bidding tests as one funnel, with a measurement partner stack that survives privacy changes.

What I have already run, at smaller scale

At Pixis, I ran iOS app user acquisition on Appsflyer and Adjust while also running paid social and retail media (Amazon Ads, Criteo) for the same 8-client, $15K-$90K/month portfolio, one measurement view across channels.
I used Amplitude and Tableau across 30+ campaigns to connect channel-level testing to downstream ROAS, lifting it 40%, the same testing-to-outcome loop this role runs on paid social and mobile UA.
At Plug Power, I ran Paid Search and Paid Social across Google, Microsoft, Meta and LinkedIn under one CPA target, cutting it 25% through bid optimization and structured A/B testing.
A Performance Marketing Manager who owns paid social and mobile UA end to end, runs a rigorous testing agenda, and partners with Data Science on incrementality and mobile measurement, is describing this exact loop: one funnel, one measurement stack, tested constantly.
