Base Pricing's hardest problem isn't the model, it's getting a market manager to trust the price change it recommends enough to actually ship it. I have already closed that exact trust gap, just with a different model underneath. At Sensata I led the Bleeders and Leakers program, evaluating deal-by-deal pricing against margin thresholds and competitive gaps across 1,200 opportunities and a $627M target. The dashboard surfaced $433M in margin gaps, but the harder part was getting market and sales leadership to act on each one, which is how we recovered $700K in margin in two quarters and lifted distributor margins from 53% to 61%. That is the same job this role does at the ride level: turn a model's output into a number a market manager will actually sign off on.

I also know the build side of that trust problem. PriceKeel, the company I founded, reads a live discount exception and recommends an explainable guardrail before a deal closes, then keeps an audit trail Finance can use instead of a black box. Getting that logic to a point where a non-technical reviewer trusts it on sight is the same design problem as getting a base-price change past a market manager who has to defend it upward, or past an executive who wants the one-line version of why a price moved.

What drew me to this req specifically is the portfolio framing: jointly optimizing standard, alternative-mode and scheduled rides as one book instead of three separate levers. That is the same granularity call I made building the FY2026 pricing dashboard across 3 regions, 4 product families and 20 product lines, deciding where the line between one price and many should sit before the model stops being useful. Lyft's version swaps regions and product lines for time, location and ride type, but the underlying decision does not change, and neither does the discipline of running A/B tests and benchmarking the competitive set before calling a result real, which is how I drove the pricing realignment behind a 12% revenue lift and an 8% market share gain at Sensata.
