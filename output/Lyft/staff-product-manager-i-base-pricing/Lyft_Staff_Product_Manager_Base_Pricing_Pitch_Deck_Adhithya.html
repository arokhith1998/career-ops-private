01
Why this deck exists

The mandate is not "tune a pricing model." It is getting that model's output trusted enough to ship, across a portfolio of ride types, with no playbook to copy.

What that actually requires

Translating the cost to fulfill a ride into a price at whatever granularity the market needs, by time, location or trip type, then defending that call upward.
Jointly optimizing standard, alternative-mode and scheduled rides as one portfolio instead of three separate levers pulled in different directions.
Making impact-estimation output accurate and trusted by market management teams, not just correct on a slide.

What I have already run

At Sensata, I evaluated deal-by-deal pricing against margin thresholds across 1,200 opportunities and a $627M target, the same unit-to-portfolio translation this role runs across ride types.
At Sensata again, I drew the granularity line across 3 regions, 4 product families and 20 product lines in the FY2026 dashboard, the by-time, by-location, by-type call Base Pricing makes every week.
At PriceKeel, I built the trust layer itself: an explainable guardrail plus an audit trail, so a recommendation survives the question of why.
The through-line: every pricing system I have touched started with a model nobody trusted yet, and ended with a number leadership could act on.

First 30 days

What the first month on Base Pricing's roadmap and vision looks like.

Week 1
Map the granularity decision end to end
Pull the existing base-price data by time, location and trip type alongside Engineering, Data Science and Market Management, so the first granularity call is grounded in what the data already shows, not a guess.
Why first: the same first move behind drawing the pricing line across 3 regions and 20 product lines at Sensata.
Week 2-3
Ship the first portfolio-level price view
Work directly with the downstream dynamic-pricing team to frame standard, alternative-mode and scheduled rides as one priced portfolio, versioned once instead of three levers pulled separately.
Why now: the same one-book framing that treated 1,200 pricing opportunities as a single $627M target at Sensata.
Week 4
Stand up the trust layer market managers need
Build the first impact-estimation explainer market managers can act on without re-deriving the model, then automate the recurring reporting step with an AI agent so the explainer updates every cycle.
Why this fast: the same explainable-guardrail design behind PriceKeel's audit trail, and the same AI-agent habit running my reporting at Applied Materials today.
