I've run the first two pieces, just against a different kind of deal. The deal was an industrial part instead of an ad impression, but the math underneath it, a floor, a threshold, a defensible reason to hold or move it, is the same math this role runs against CPM floors.

I built the muscle to systematize that judgment with PriceKeel, the company I founded. Running PriceKeel also means I set up and run my own CRM and CPQ configuration end to end, so the systems coordination piece of this role, sitting between pricing, revenue recognition and Accounting, isn't new ground.
