Hiring Team, TransferX

At PriceKeel, I built the audit trail that lets Finance trust a pricing decision it did not make itself: every discount exception documented, every override explainable. TransferX is solving a version of that same problem at a different scale, making it clear how money moves across borders so people can trust the process. That is the kind of decision-integrity work I want to keep doing.

Right now at Applied Materials, I turn scattered input from Sales, Engineering and Accounts into one market view, then build the leadership decks and run the OKR reviews that keep a decision from getting lost after the meeting ends. Gather the input, structure it, document the call, follow up on it: that loop is most of what a Founders Office Associate does, and it's the part of my current role I'd want to do full time.

Before that, at Sensata I built a 1,200-opportunity pricing dashboard against a $627M target and used it to surface $433M in margin gaps for leadership to prioritize, then partnered directly with senior executives on the revenue models and site-transition strategy that followed. The research only mattered because someone tracked what leadership decided with it, which is the follow-through half of the job I liked best.

I've run this same cross-functional thread before: a 3-region product roadmap with Engineering at Sensata, an 8-person team and its leadership decks at Pixis, and PriceKeel's own CRM, pipeline reviews and deal approvals today. A remote, fast-moving founding team solving a real cross-border money problem is exactly the kind of place I want that thread to keep running.
