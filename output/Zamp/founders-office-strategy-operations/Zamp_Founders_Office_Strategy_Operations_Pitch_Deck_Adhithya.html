First 30 days

What the first month inside Zamp's Founder's Office would look like.

Week 1
Sit inside the real signal
Talk to the Founder, partnerships, finance and customer-facing teams to find where strategy, fundraising or customer experience work is stalling today, the same audit that grounded the Bleeders and Leakers program before a dashboard existed.
Why first: a cross-domain mandate built on assumptions instead of real blockers is the fastest way to miss the mark.
Week 2-3
Ship the first partnership or brief
Take one cross-functional problem, whether a partnership to close, an investor update to draft or a market-entry question, and deliver a decision-ready recommendation, the same move that turned 1,200 opportunities into one shortlist at Sensata.
Why now: one measured recommendation that moves a real decision beats a week-one list of ideas.
Week 4
Turn the first win into the cadence
Use what the first deliverable proved to set the recurring rhythm, be it a partnership pipeline, an investor-update cadence or a customer-feedback loop, that keeps producing results without rebuilding it from scratch every cycle.
Why this fast: a cadence only earns trust once it has already moved one real decision.
