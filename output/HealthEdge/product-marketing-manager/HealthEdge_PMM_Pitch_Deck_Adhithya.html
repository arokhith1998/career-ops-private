First 30 days

What the first month on the HealthEdge Source Product Marketing team would look like.

Week 1
Sit with Sales and Product, not the deck library
Pull the last quarter of lost competitive deals and talk to the reps who ran them, the same move that grounded every Sensata battlecard in a real conversation instead of a guess.
Why first: a battlecard built on assumptions is the fastest way to lose a rep's trust before it ever ships.
Week 2-3
Ship the battlecard or persona reps are missing most
Build the single asset most likely to move a live competitive deal this quarter, benchmarked the same way the Sensata library was, the one that lifted win rates 30%.
Why now: one asset in active use beats a full library nobody has opened yet.
Week 4
Turn the first win into the maintenance system
Set the cadence that keeps battlecards, personas and case studies current as the competitive landscape moves, so the library never goes stale between launches again.
Why this fast: an enablement library only earns its maintenance budget by proving it moved a deal first.
