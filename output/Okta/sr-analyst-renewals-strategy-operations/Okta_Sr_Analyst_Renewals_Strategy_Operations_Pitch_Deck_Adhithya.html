Why this deck exists

A renewals org this size runs on one analytical backbone, not four separate jobs. A gap in any one of them shows up everywhere else.

What breaks without that backbone

A forecast without accuracy tracking does not fail loudly. It fails as a number that was wrong last quarter too, and nobody can say by how much or why, so the next forecast inherits the same blind spot.
Capacity and book-of-business visibility that lives in someone's head instead of a system means territory and account health get re-discovered every quarter instead of tracked continuously.
AI-generated analysis without someone directing the sources and owning the judgment on top just moves the risk from a slow spreadsheet to a fast, confidently wrong one.

What I have already run

At Sensata, I built the dashboard that made a $627M target and 1,200 pricing opportunities auditable by region, product line and salesperson, the same accuracy-tracking instinct a forecast process needs.
At PriceKeel, I set up and run the CRM, CPQ configuration and forecast and pipeline reviews myself, so the forecasting backbone and the accountability trail are never two different stories.
At Applied Materials, I direct AI agents built on Claude to generate the first pass on repetitive analysis and own the judgment before it reaches a decision.
Forecasting, capacity and retention planning, accountability and measurement, and analysis and automation are describing one connected operating system, not four separate hires.
