Slide 05 | First Thirty Days

Three concrete experiments to ship inside thirty days.

Each one ships in a month and produces a measurable economic outcome - CAC, LTV, payback period, or attribution coverage.
Week 1-2 - Attribution Audit
End-to-end attribution audit and gap teardown
Map current state: GA4 event coverage, GTM container hygiene, Meta CAPI / server-side parity, Salesforce / CRM lead-routing, UTM spec compliance. Output a 2-page teardown ranking gaps by revenue impact and a sequenced fix-it roadmap.
Why first. Same shape as the Plug Power rebuild. Without trustworthy data, every paid decision later is theater. Fix the contract first.
Week 2-4 - Holdout Test
Holdout-incrementality slice on the largest spend channel
Identify the channel currently getting the most credit on last-click or last-touch. Carve a matched holdout cell. Measure incremental conversion lift over 3 weeks. Cut over-credited spend, reallocate to true lift. Defend the call with finance + leadership.
Why first. The Pixis muscle. On regulated / high-consideration buyers, last-click attribution under-credits long consideration cycles and over-credits closing-window channels. Holdout incrementality is the only way to see the true picture.
Week 3-4 - Economics Dashboard
CAC / LTV / Payback dashboard for weekly + monthly cadence
Build a one-page dashboard tracking CAC, LTV, ROAS, payback period, and cost per conversion sliced by channel + cohort. Looker Studio or Tableau on the data layer. Becomes the artifact that anchors the weekly performance update and the monthly business review.
Why first. Same shape as the $627M margin-recovery executive dashboard at Sensata (1,200 opportunities, 3 regions, 4 product families). Executive artifact, built for decisions.
