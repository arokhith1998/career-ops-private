First 30 days

What the first month on Cursor's product marketing team looks like.

Week 1
Learn the product like a power user
Sit with Product, Engineering and Developer Relations to understand the current feature set, the competitive landscape, and where today's positioning already resonates with developers versus where it reads generic.
Why first: the same move that made the Sensata launches land, talk to the people closest to the product before writing anything.
Week 2-3
Ship the first narrative
Pair with Developer Relations on one piece of high-signal technical content, a guide or comparison, and test it against real developer feedback before the next release.
Why now: content tested with real users, not written in a vacuum, is what made the SwipeHire activation gain hold.
Week 4
Run the first growth experiment
Pick one channel or onboarding step and run a structured test, the same experiment discipline that grew SwipeHire's activation 25%, now applied to a live Cursor funnel.
Why this fast: a PMM earns product and GTM trust by shipping something measurable, not by presenting a plan.
