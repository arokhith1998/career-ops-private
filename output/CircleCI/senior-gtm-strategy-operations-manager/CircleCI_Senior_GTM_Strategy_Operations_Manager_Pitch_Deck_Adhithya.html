Why this deck exists

The hard part of a GTM analytics suite is not the first dashboard. It is who keeps the refresh, the forecast and the metric definitions aligned every single cycle.

What a GTM Strategy and Operations team runs into

Bookings, pipeline, expansion and churn views get built for one quarterly business review, then the next cycle turns into a manual refresh because nobody owns the pipeline that feeds them.
Finance, Sales and Marketing each keep their own version of a metric, so a bottoms-up forecast quietly drifts from the number Sales is actually carrying.
The fix is not another one-off report. It is one owner who builds the dashboard, automates the refresh with AI agents, and closes the process gaps between teams before they show up in a business review.

What I have already run, hands on

At Sensata, I built the FY2026 Bleeders and Leakers Power BI dashboard myself: 1,200 opportunities against a $627M target, with its own win-rate and pipeline-stage reporting leadership could trust cycle over cycle.
At Applied Materials, I automate the recurring part of that analysis with AI agents built on Claude, so a refresh is a review, not a rebuild.
As founder of PriceKeel, I run the CRM, CPQ, forecast and pipeline reviews myself, so I know where a quote-to-close process gap actually lives.
Own the revenue planning process, build the dashboards, automate the recurring workflows, and keep data integrity across the lifecycle: this is the exact loop I already run, not a step I would need to learn on the job.
