First 30 days

What the first month owning Agiloft's pricing and packaging model looks like.

Week 1
Learn the current packaging and the committee's cadence
Sit with Product GTM, Finance, Sales and RevOps to map how a pricing change moves from proposal to committee approval today, and pull the competitive and win/loss data every recommendation should be checked against.
Why first: a recommendation is only as strong as the deal evidence and committee process it is built to survive.
Week 2-3
Draft the first hybrid-pricing model and bundle logic
Model one usage-based or outcome-based packaging option against the current seat-based baseline, define the entitlement and upgrade-trigger logic behind it, and run the willingness-to-pay check before it goes to committee.
Why now: building the model on clean competitive and willingness-to-pay data is what made the 53% to 61% distributor gain hold at Sensata.
Week 4
Ship the first pricing metrics dashboard and playbook
Roll up pricing metrics (win rate, deal size, discount depth) into one dashboard, write the first deal desk playbook from it, and present both to the pricing committee.
Why this fast: the same habit that keeps Claude agents running recurring pricing analysis at Applied Materials today.
