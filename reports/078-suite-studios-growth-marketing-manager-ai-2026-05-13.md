## C) Strategy
The JD's headline ask is "establish AI as an operational foundation across the marketing function." Almost no Growth Marketing Manager candidate can claim this credibly - they have AI tool *experience*, not AI tool *ownership*. The candidate has the rare combination:

1. **Two shipped AI products solo.** SwipeHire (OpenAI API + GA4 + GTM + 8 A/B tests + +25% activation) and NSE Bot (Claude API agentic reasoning over rule-based scoring). These aren't side experiments; they're production builds with instrumented loops.
2. **Four Anthropic Claude certifications + Cursor + Claude daily workflow.** Not credential-only - the workflow is the actual operating loop.
3. **Pixis 8-marketer AI training program.** Already done the "make AI usable for non-engineers in marketing" work, with 8 enterprise marketers as the audience.

Lead the resume + CL + deck on this AI-ops + AI-builder convergence. Frame Sensata for the messaging/launch criterion. Frame Plug Power for the website + content + lifecycle stack. Frame GenY for the D2C content + creative refresh discipline.

The "5+ YoE" stretch is the only friction; the AI substance defends the year count.
