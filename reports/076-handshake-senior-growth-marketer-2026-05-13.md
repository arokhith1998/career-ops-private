## C) Strategy
This role lands inside the candidate's strongest possible positioning. Three threads to weave:

1. **The convergence headline: SwipeHire is structurally what Handshake AI is doing.** SwipeHire is a solo-built AI career platform on Python + OpenAI + React, targeting early-career and international students with specialized skill profiles. Handshake AI is the same problem space, $1B scale, with PhDs and researchers as the network. Few candidates can credibly claim they have already been operating in this product space - even at small scale, solo, end-to-end. This is the slide-1 hook.

2. **The literal must-have: Pixis $1.4M, 6 channels.** The JD says "efficiently managed at least a 7-figure budget across a minimum of 2 paid marketing channels." Pixis aggregate book was $1.4M across Google, Meta, LinkedIn, Criteo, Amazon Ads, and programmatic - six channels, three years ago, with measured outcomes (3x ROAS via attribution restructure). This is the literal proof.

3. **Plug Power for SEO + lifecycle + multi-channel attribution.** Closed-loop attribution rebuild + SEO + content + HubSpot lifecycle architected from zero. Where the JD says "leverage SEO best practices" and "manage and optimize lifecycle marketing flows," Plug Power is the proof.

The 6+ YoE ask is the only stretch. Counter-frame: actual hands-on years aren't the test; the budget scope, channel mix, and shipped AI work are. Most 6-YoE growth marketers have managed budgets at this scale once; few have shipped two AI products solo on top of that.
