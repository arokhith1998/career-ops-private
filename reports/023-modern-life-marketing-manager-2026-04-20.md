## G) Draft Application Answers

### Why this role?
Modern Life is the right scale for a marketing generalist who actually wants to own the loop. $35M Series A, Thrive-backed, ~50 people means one marketer owns campaigns, events, enablement, and MarTech ops. I have worn all those hats: Plug Power (MarTech rebuild + paid media), Pixis (AI SaaS budget ops), Sensata (GTM + enablement for 50+ reps), SwipeHire (entire loop solo). AI fluency is a requirement here; it is how I work.

### Why Modern Life?
AI-driven brokerage is the kind of technical repositioning I find interesting. Life insurance is a $175B market that has barely changed in 30 years; a Thrive-backed team with actual AI in the underwriting path has a real shot at moving the cost curve. The marketing job at this stage is to make advisors trust the platform. I have been the CSM that earned trust for an AI adtech platform at Pixis; the motion translates.

### Relevant experience
Plug Power: built the full MarTech stack from scratch (Salesforce + Adobe + GA + GTM) and ran paid media across Google, Meta, LinkedIn. -25% CPA, +20% conversion. Pixis: managed $1.4M across 8 enterprise AI adtech clients; 3x ROAS, -50% churn. Sensata: 100+ feature Agile backlog, 30+ customer interviews, $2M+ first-year launches, enablement for 50+ reps. SwipeHire: built AI product on OpenAI API + React; 50+ users, 8+ A/B tests, +25% activation.

### AI fluency example
SwipeHire uses Claude for draft-and-critique on tailored landing copy: one Claude call drafts, a second scores against the conversion signal, a human gate approves before ship. Separating draft from judge is the non-obvious design move. I run Claude + Zapier + custom GPT workflows daily for ops work at Sensata.
