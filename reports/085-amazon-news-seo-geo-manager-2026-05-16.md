## A) Role summary

Amazon is hiring an SEO/GEO Manager to own organic search and AI-visibility strategy for AboutAmazon.com - Amazon's editorial newsroom that publishes corporate-story content, product launches, and policy/communications material for journalists, customers, and search. The seat sits inside Teodora Zivkovic's Insights & Analytics team for Amazon News, working cross-functionally with editorial, comms, and engineering. The role is unusual because it pairs traditional SEO (keyword strategy, technical audits, structured data, site architecture, SEMrush + GSC reporting) with **GEO** (Generative Engine Optimization) - i.e., shaping how AboutAmazon.com content surfaces and is cited by ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, and Google's AI Overviews. NYC onsite/hybrid (Amazon RTO policy is 5 days for most NYC seats; confirm at recruiter screen). Posted band $130K-$185K.

The role is genuinely interesting: organic search is being reshaped by LLMs, and AboutAmazon.com is one of the highest-leverage corporate newsrooms in the world to learn that shift on.
