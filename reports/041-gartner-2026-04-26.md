## G) Draft Application Answers

**"Why this role at Gartner?" (~100 words)**
> Gartner is rebuilding 3P syndication as a governance and lead-quality program, not a media buy. That is the part of paid demand I am best at. At Plug Power I built the closed-loop side that 3P vendors deliver into: UTMs, lead routing, suppression, click-to-SQL attribution. -25% CPA, +20% conversion. At Pixis I held vendors and clients to weekly delivery, suppression, and quality standards across an 8-client, $1.4M book. The work here is the same operating model with different vendor names. I want to do it at Gartner's scale, against VP+ buyers, where governance actually matters.

**"Walk me through how you would approach a vendor not hitting lead-quality targets." (~150 words)**
> Step one is the data, not the vendor. Pull last 30 days at the lead-record level. Are the leads disqualified by suppression, by title, by company-size match, or by funnel-stage drop-off? That tells me if it is a targeting problem, a delivery problem, or a downstream problem. Step two is the contract. What did we agree to on suppression, formatting, tagging, replacement, and escalation? Most "vendor problems" are scope problems we never wrote down. Step three is the conversation. Walk the vendor through the data, name the standard from the contract, ask for their root-cause read in writing, and set a 30-day plan with explicit checkpoints. If they hit, we continue. If they miss, the 30-day plan was the escalation memo. Procurement and Legal are already aligned because the standards were written down before there was a problem.
