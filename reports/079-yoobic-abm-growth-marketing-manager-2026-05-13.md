## C) Strategy
This is the first role I've evaluated this batch where the JD's must-have (3+ years enterprise ABM) doesn't have a clean answer on the CV. The honest framing:

1. **Lead the resume on the account-level work that *does* exist.** Plug Power Adobe AAM account-level segmentation + LinkedIn ABM + closed-loop attribution rebuild. Pixis 8-client B2B SaaS portfolio with weekly QBRs. HubSpot account-level behavioral lifecycle stages.
2. **Don't apologize in candidate-facing content.** Per memory, no honest-gap paragraphs in CL/outreach. The resume + CL + deck frame the work positively; the gap is left to the recruiter's judgment.
3. **The "builder, test-and-learn" bonus criterion is the differentiator.** Two shipped AI products solo + 4 Claude certs + Cursor + Claude daily = an unusually strong proof of the bonus trait. Lean into this on the deck.
4. **The comp ($135-145K, top of target) + remote (zero relo) make this worth the stretch.** If the recruiter is open-minded on "ABM Manager title" specifically vs "account-level work substance," this lands. If they auto-filter on title, it doesn't.
