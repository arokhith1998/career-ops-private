## A) Role Summary

Reevo is a Santa Clara / San Francisco startup founded in 2024 by David Zhu, Cindy Hao, Curtis Tan, and Clement Fang (ex-DoorDash, Affirm, Airbnb, Salesforce). It raised $80M across seed and Series A co-led by Khosla Ventures and Kleiner Perkins, at roughly a $500M valuation. Around 50 employees. The company describes itself as "50+ builders with 30 exits," and prefixes every job title with "Builder -" as a hiring convention.

The product is an AI-native revenue operating system that unifies marketing, sales, ops, and customer success. Its central claim against the incumbent CRM: it generates its own clean first-party activity data rather than depending on reps to log it, and it proactively guides reps to a next best action instead of storing a record after the fact.

The PMM Lead owns six areas:

- **Narrative and positioning.** Turn complex AI-driven features into sharp customer value; set the bar for clarity and storytelling company-wide.
- **End-to-end product marketing strategy.** GTM from ideation through adoption; "market moments," not launches.
- **Product influence and customer intelligence.** Synthesize customer conversations, usage data, and competitive intel into roadmap influence.
- **Revenue and sales enablement.** Pitches, demo narratives, competitive battlecards. Measured on win rates and deal velocity.
- **Growth and web strategy.** Own product storytelling on the website and growth surfaces; optimize for conversion and clarity.
- **Scalable systems and AI mastery.** Repeatable GTM playbooks that survive team doubling; AI tooling (including Reevo itself) and Notion to automate routine work.

**Asks:** 6+ years product marketing in high-growth AI or modern B2B SaaS, preferably sales/revenue startups; product partnership on messaging and positioning; executive presence and stakeholder influence; strong grasp of B2B buying and sales process plus revenue-funnel diagnosis; high agency and bias for action; high bar on messaging precision and visual presentation; comfort with data; Notion and AI tooling; exceptional writing across audiences.

**Location:** San Francisco or Santa Clara, in-office 5 days a week (explicitly non-negotiable in the JD). **Comp:** not posted. Third-party aggregators estimate $80K-$130K base plus bonus and early-stage equity.

## F) Red Flags and Watch Items

1. **Posting is ~91 days old.** Senior PMM searches routinely run a full quarter, so this is not disqualifying, but it is old enough to ask "is this still open and what has the search turned up so far" in the first call.
2. **A parallel "Builder - Head of Product Marketing" req is open** at the same company, in the same function. Two readings: the company is building a PMM function with a Head plus a Lead under them (normal at 50 people scaling fast), or the two reqs are competing versions of the same seat and the Lead req is stale. Ask directly which one is live and who the Lead would report to.
3. **6+ years asked, 5 years held.** Real, and the reason the score is 3.9 rather than 4.3.
4. **Comp not posted** and the aggregator band is wide. California pay-transparency norms make an unposted band worth asking about immediately.
5. **Relocation is required, not optional.** Boston to SF, 5 days on-site.

## G) Posting Legitimacy

**Proceed with Caution.**

Positive signals: active Ashby posting on a well-maintained board with 21 open reqs across engineering, GTM, and ops; verifiable $80M raise from two top-tier funds; a specific, non-generic JD with real ownership areas and a clearly opinionated point of view; named founders with traceable backgrounds; an accessible product and public blog.

Signals worth noting: the 91-day posting age, and the overlapping Head of Product Marketing req. Legitimate explanations exist for both.
