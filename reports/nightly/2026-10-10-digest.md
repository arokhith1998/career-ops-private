**A real bug found and worked around, not fixed:** `check-jd-experience.mjs`
takes the *lowest* years-figure it finds near a requirement-cue word. Several
extraction sub-agents wrote their own "flag for Agent 3" commentary directly
into the JD files (e.g. "...exceeds the **6**-year mid-level band") instead of
under a stripped `## Extraction notes` heading, and the gate's parser picked
up that **6** as if it were the JD's own ask -- wrongly passing Workday (true
ask: 8y), Samsung Semiconductor (7-12y tiered), and Google (8y). Found by
manually re-reading three JDs whose visa-evaluator report flagged a years
mismatch the automated gate had missed, then confirmed by stripping the notes
and re-running the gate clean. All three (plus Salesforce, caught separately:
its "8+ years" bullet has no requirement-cue word on its own line) are
dropped. **Recommend fixing two things in `check-jd-experience.mjs` next
system-layer pass:** don't let agent commentary outside a stripped section
carry a years figure, and loosen the requirement-cue match for a bare
"`N+ years in X, Y, or Z`" bullet under a "Required" header.
