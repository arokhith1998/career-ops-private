### WebSearch sweep (fallback for the blocked boolean search)

Ran the full sweep per the instructions: 8 India-track searches + 6 US-track searches (14 total, above the 6-per-track minimum). Found 35 candidate URLs (19 US, 16 India).

**Liveness check:** `check-liveness.mjs` (Playwright) could not be used — every URL came back `ERR_CERT_AUTHORITY_INVALID`. Root cause confirmed, not a config typo: this sandboxed container's outbound HTTPS appears to pass through a transparent intercept using the local agent-proxy's CA, which curl/Node trust via `SSL_CERT_FILE`/`NODE_EXTRA_CA_CERTS`, but Chromium's own cert verifier does not honor those env vars and has no trusted copy of that CA. Explicitly passing the proxy to Playwright's `chromium.launch({proxy})` made no difference (same error), and importing the CA into Chromium's NSS trust store (`certutil -A`) was denied by the session's own auto-mode safety classifier as a "TLS/Auth Weaken" action — correctly, since that's a real trust-store change, not something a nightly job should be routing around. **This blocks Playwright-based liveness/extraction for every run in this environment, not just tonight** — worth a maintainer decision on whether to special-case this sandbox the same way the headless-batch exception already does for `check-liveness.mjs`/`browser-extract.mjs`.

Fell back to `WebFetch` per the batch-mode exception. Confirmed 6 dead (4 US 404s: Anzen, Wealthfront, Freed, Leadbank; 1 India 404: Krishna Consultancy; 1 India LinkedIn `expired_jd_redirect`: Glance) — dropped before entering the feed.
