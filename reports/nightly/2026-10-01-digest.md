Root cause confirmed, not a config typo: this sandboxed container's outbound HTTPS appears to pass through a transparent intercept using the local agent-proxy's CA, which curl/Node trust via `SSL_CERT_FILE`/`NODE_EXTRA_CA_CERTS`, but Chromium's own cert verifier does not honor those env vars and has no trusted copy of that CA.
