### Experience gate — a real bug caught and worked around
`check-jd-experience.mjs --over-only` only caught 2 of this run's over-cap rows (the two Databricks Industry Marketing Manager reqs, correctly dropped at 7y > 6y cap). Manual review of every extracted JD's own "Flag for Agent 3" note caught **5 more that the script silently passed**: Meta (10+ yrs), OpenAI (10+ yrs), Legion Technologies (8+ yrs), and both Intuitive reqs (12+ yrs preferred, 8+ yrs required). Root cause: when an extraction agent's own flag note says "...exceeds the US-track 6-year seniority ceiling," the script's zero-token regex picks up that incidental "6-year" as a *lower* candidate figure and takes the minimum across all candidate lines on the page — masking the real, higher requirement stated elsewhere in the same JD. All 7 over-cap rows were correctly dropped before the visa gate. **Worth a fix:** `check-jd-experience.mjs` should exclude its own extractor commentary lines (anything containing "seniority ceiling" or "Flag for Agent") from the years-required scan.
