## A) Role Summary

Sarvam AI is Bengaluru's flagship sovereign-AI startup. Founded 2023 by Vivek Raghavan and Pratyush Kumar (ex-AI4Bharat, IIT). $41M seed/Series A in Dec 2023 led by Lightspeed with Peak XV and Khosla; total raised $54M+. First startup selected under India's IndiaAI Mission to build indigenous foundation models. Shipped Sarvam-1 and the 105B-parameter sovereign model. Product surface spans consumer AI, enterprise platforms, developer tools, voice/speech for Indian languages, and edge deployments.

The PM Growth seat owns the top-of-funnel and activation engine across Sarvam's product portfolio:

- **Onboarding/signup:** charter "from first touchpoint to activated user" including flow design and activation moments across products.
- **Activation metrics:** define per product, identify drop-offs, run experiments to lift conversion.
- **Marketing and attribution:** channel strategy across paid, organic, referral, partnerships; build attribution tracking to measure channel ROI.
- **User quality:** segment by source, kill low-quality channels, focus on repeat-user acquisition.
- **Virality and referral:** design in-product referral mechanics and social sharing.
- **Lifecycle:** email, WhatsApp, push nudges driving repeat sessions and habit formation.

**Asks:** 5+ years PM with 2-3 in growth/activation/top-of-funnel; demonstrated activation lift with specific examples; Mixpanel/PostHog/Amplitude/SQL; CAC, activation, D1/D7/D30, cohort fluency; attribution measurement. **Bonus:** referral/viral loop builder, dev/AI/India-consumer background, mobile growth + store optimization.

**Location:** Bengaluru, full-time, on-site. **Comp:** not posted; market band for senior PM at well-funded Bengaluru AI startup is Rs 50-100L base + ESOPs (Glassdoor/Levels Bangalore, May 2026).
